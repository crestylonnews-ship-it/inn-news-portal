  # Generative AI Disrupts Education Models: New Talent-Training Developments in Taiwan and South Korea

  > The wave of generative AI is globally disrupting traditional education and career paths. Higher education institutions in Taiwan are actively advancing AI transformation, while in South Korea youths are turning to vocational education; both places show new trends in talent cultivation.

  The rapid development of generative artificial intelligence (AI) technology is profoundly transforming global higher education models and traditional career trajectories. Multiple reports indicate that, faced with AI’s redefinition of knowledge production and the mission of talent cultivation, national education systems and younger generations are actively exploring new response strategies.

  In Taiwan, National Taipei University and the Taiwan Artificial Intelligence Academy Foundation (AIA) recently signed a memorandum of understanding to jointly promote “AI Technology Empowering Innovation and Higher Education Transformation.” According to Yahoo Entertainment, the two will focus on three main pillars: teacher technology empowerment, campus-level AI development strategy, and cross-domain talent cultivation, with the aim of establishing a replicable and scalable model for higher education transformation. National Taipei University President Lin Dao-tong said generative AI has redefined modes of knowledge production and the mission of talent cultivation; universities must do more than introduce new tools—they must rethink educational models.

  At the same time, Techbang.com reports that in South Korea, a society that places high value on traditional diplomas, a disruptive wave of educational transformation is rising. Increasing numbers of young people are choosing to forgo preparing for traditional university entrance exams and instead enter semiconductor vocational high schools to learn professional practical skills. This phenomenon shows that, against the backdrop of generative AI rapidly upending white-collar career ladders, the conventional equation "getting into a prestigious university = securing a high-paying stable job" is being questioned, and the semiconductor industry’s strong demand for technical talent is prompting students to target high-paying positions earlier.

# 生成式AI衝擊教育模式 臺韓兩地現人才培育新局

> 生成式AI浪潮正全球性地衝擊傳統教育與職涯路徑。臺灣高等教育機構積極推動AI轉型，南韓則見青少年轉向技職教育，兩地皆展現人才培育新趨勢。

生成式人工智慧（AI）科技的快速發展，正對全球高等教育模式與傳統職涯路徑帶來深刻變革。多方報導指出，面對AI重新定義知識生產與人才培育使命的趨勢，各國教育體系與年輕世代正積極探索新的應對策略。

在臺灣，國立臺北大學與財團法人臺灣人工智慧學校基金會（AIA）於日前簽署合作備忘錄，共同推動「人工智慧科技賦能創新與高教轉型」。據Yahoo Entertainment報導，雙方將聚焦教師科技賦能、校級AI發展策略及跨域人才培育三大主軸，旨在建立可複製、可擴散的高教轉型示範模式。臺北大學校長林道通表示，生成式AI已重新定義知識生產模式與人才培育使命，大學不能僅是匯入新工具，更需重新思考教育模式。

與此同時，Techbang.com報導指出，在高度重視傳統文憑的南韓社會，一股顛覆性的教育轉型浪潮正興起。越來越多的青少年選擇放棄備戰傳統大學入學考試，轉而進入半導體職業高中學習專業實務技能。此現象顯示，在生成式AI快速顛覆白領職涯階梯的背景下，傳統「考取名校大學＝保障高薪穩定」的職涯路徑正受到質疑，半導體產業對技術人才的強大需求也促使學子提早鎖定高薪職位。
