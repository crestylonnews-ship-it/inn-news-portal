  # Food Safety Alert: Carcinogen Detected in Camellia Oil

  > Two camellia oil products tested above the limit for the carcinogen benzo[a]pyrene, the Taipei City Department of Health announced.

  The Taipei City Department of Health announced on the evening of the 7th that two camellia oil products tested above the limit for the Group 1 carcinogen benzo[a]pyrene.
  "Premium Camellia Oil" tested at 2.8 ppb; it was produced under contract by Yuan Chun Oil Mill for Wei Jia International.
  Separately, Hanlin Trading voluntarily reported one camellia oil product containing 2.1 ppb. Both products have been removed from shelves.

# 食安警訊：苦茶油驗致癌物

> 兩款苦茶油驗出致癌物苯駢芘超標，北市衛生局公佈。

臺北市衛生局7日晚間公佈，兩款苦茶油檢出1級致癌物苯駢芘超標。
「極品苦茶油」驗出2.8ppb，由威加國際委託源春製油廠代工。
另翰霖貿易自主通報一款苦茶油含2.1ppb，兩款皆已下架。
