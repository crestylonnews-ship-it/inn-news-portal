  The Third Elderly Health Industry Expo took place August 7–9 at Taipei World Trade Center Hall 1, with an opening ceremony attended by the President, the Minister of Health and Welfare, and officials from relevant central government agencies. The Ministry of Health and Welfare's Taoyuan Hospital participated under the theme of "Integration of Medical and Long-Term Care Services," with exhibits jointly presented by its Department of Geriatric Medicine, Community and Home Nursing, Department of Traditional Chinese Medicine, and Assistive Devices Center. The displays covered health promotion, functional assessment, chronic disease management, home medical care, home-based emergency services, home hospice care, and smart assistive devices, showcasing a new model of home-based care.
  
  The Ministry of Economic Affairs' Technology Division simultaneously organized three exhibition zones—"Health Management," "Caring Protection," and "Rehabilitation and Empowerment"—at its dedicated pavilion, bringing together four affiliated institutions: the Industrial Technology Research Institute, the Metal Industries Research and Development Center, the Textile and Fiber Research Institute, and the Food Industry Research and Development Institute. A total of 30 elderly health technology achievements were exhibited. Technology Division Director Guo Zhaozhong stated that the showcased innovations have been successively introduced into home care, community centers, and medical long-term care institutions. On the opening day, the Industrial Technology Research Institute and Taisheng Company signed a memorandum of understanding for cooperation. Taoyuan Mayor Chang Shan-cheng also visited the Taoyuan pavilion on August 8, meeting with representatives from Tiansheng Medical System, Linkou Chang Gung Memorial Hospital, Minsheng Medical System, and Yiren Hospital.
  
  According to綜合 media reports, the expo focused on elderly health care and technology applications. Taoyuan Hospital emphasized integrated clinical and community services, while the Technology Division's pavilion centered on research achievements. TSG Financial Holdings also launched a financial product combining AI-powered fraud prevention and genetic testing at the venue. Exhibition content and target audiences varied across organizations. Specific cooperation outcomes and follow-up progress remain subject to further announcements by the organizing committee or relevant authorities.

# 第三屆高齡健康博覽會登場 部桃展在宅照護、技術司推30項成果

> 第三屆高齡健康產業博覽會8月7日至9日於臺北世貿一館舉行，衛生福利部桃園醫院展示在宅醫療新模式，經濟部技術司則展出30項高齡健康科技成果。

第三屆高齡健康產業博覽會於8月7日至9日在臺北世貿一館舉行，開幕典禮由總統、衛生福利部部長及中央相關部會長官共同出席。衛生福利部桃園醫院以「醫療與長照整合」為主軸參展，由老年醫學科、社羣居家護理、中醫科及輔具中心共同展示，內容涵蓋健康促進、功能評估、慢性病管理、居家醫療、在宅急診、居家安寧與智慧輔具等服務成果，呈現在宅照護新模式。

經濟部產業技術司同期於技術司專館規劃「健康管理」、「暖心守護」及「復健賦能」三大展區，集結工研院、金屬中心、紡織所及食品所等4家法人，共展出30項高齡健康科技成果。技術司司長郭肇中表示，展出成果已陸續匯入居家照護、社羣據點及醫療長照機構；開幕當天並促成工研院與泰陞公司簽署合作意向書。桃園市長張善政亦於8日蒞臨參觀桃園館，與天成醫療體系、聯新國際醫院、敏盛醫療體系、怡仁醫院等機構代表共同會面。

綜合各媒體報導，本次博覽會聚焦高齡健康照護與科技應用，部桃側重臨牀與社羣整合服務，技術司專館則以科研成果展示為主；臺新金控亦於會場推出結合AI智慧防詐與基因檢測的理財方案。各單位展區內容與參展物件有所不同，具體合作成果與後續推動進度，仍需依主辦單位或相關機關進一步發布資訊確認。
