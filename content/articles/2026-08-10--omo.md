  # New Retail Wave Sweeps In: E-commerce Platforms Drive OMO Virtual-Physical Integration Transformation
  
  > As digital transformation accelerates, OMO (Online-Merge-Offline) has become a core strategy for the retail industry. Companies are actively integrating consumer journey data to enhance customer experience and brand loyalty, while the role of e-commerce platforms has shifted from tools to full business ecosystems.
  
  The new retail concept has continued to gain momentum in recent years, with OMO (Online Merge Offline) and O2O (Online to Offline) becoming focal points of industry discussion. According to Amazon Business, while OMO and O2O both fall under new retail strategies, they emphasize different aspects. OMO stresses the deep integration of online and offline channels, aiming to strengthen the bond between brands and consumers through optimized service and consumer experience, thereby enhancing loyalty. The report further explains that the core of new retail lies in "deepening the connection with consumers," rather than merely switching sales channels.
  
  On the practice of digital transformation, different media present diverse perspectives. Cloud Interactive analyzes that the evolution from O2O to an O2O2O model is an important part of post-pandemic operational solutions, but also a significant challenge, emphasizing that hypermarket retailers need to get ahead in their佈局 to improve customer experience. Cyberbiz approaches the topic from a data-driven angle, pointing out that the core of OMO new retail lies in "comprehensively collecting consumers' online and offline purchase trajectories and effectively integrating and applying them." This capability is crucial for enterprises or brands during digital transformation.
  
  However, some voices have called for reflection on the essence of e-commerce. Bearboss argues that "e-commerce is simply a different sales channel; the essence of retail has never changed." It emphasizes that a brand's understanding of market characteristics and consumer thinking, along with meeting needs through appropriate products, pricing, and service, remains the primary threshold for retail brands, and suggests reinterpreting B2C as "Brand to Customers." This perspective highlights the caution of some industry figures toward overemphasizing technological concepts, advocating a return to the essence of retail.
  
  Looking ahead to 2025, Vocus analysis indicates that the role of e-commerce platforms has extended far beyond mere tools—they are now complete business ecosystems. OMO and D2C (Direct to Consumer) have become the core drivers of industry development, with e-commerce platforms playing a key supporting role. Tiandiren Academy also adds an educational perspective, explaining that after progressing through stages of street-side grocery stores, shopping malls, supermarkets, and hypermarket channels, traditional physical retail is now moving toward the OMO new model of virtual-physical integration.
  
  Synthesizing observations from various sources, while new retail transformation is a consensus direction, different media interpret implementation priorities differently: some emphasize data integration and technological layout, while others remind us to return to the essence of brand and consumer needs. Currently, the industry lacks a unified standard defining the specific differences between OMO and O2O. Enterprises driving transformation still need to choose a virtual-physical integration path suited to their own industry characteristics and resource conditions.

# 新零售浪潮席捲 電商平臺推動OMO虛實融合轉型

> 隨著數位轉型加速，OMO（線上線下虛實整合）成為零售業核心策略，企業積極整合消費軌跡資料以提升顧客體驗與品牌忠誠度，電商平臺角色亦從工具轉為商業生態系統。

新零售概念近年持續發酵，OMO（Online Merge Offline）與O2O（Online to Offline）成為業界討論焦點。根據亞馬遜企業服務團隊（Amazon Business）指出，OMO與O2O雖同屬新零售策略，但兩者側重不同；OMO強調線上與線下的深度融合，旨在透過服務與消費者體驗的最佳化，深化品牌與消費者的連結，進而提升忠誠度。該報導進一步說明，新零售的核心在於「深化與消費者的連結」，而非僅止於銷售管道的轉換。
在數位轉型實踐方面，不同媒體呈現多元觀點。Cloud Interactive 分析指出，零售業從O2O進化至O2O2O模式，是疫情後營運解決方案的重要一環，但同時亦是一大挑戰，強調量販業需搶先佈局以提升顧客體驗。Cyberbiz 則從資料驅動角度切入，指出OMO新零售的核心在於「完整蒐集消費者線上與線下的消費軌跡，並加以有效整合應用」，企業或品牌在推動數位轉型過程中，此項能力至關重要。
然而，亦有觀點對電商本質提出反思。Bearboss 認為，「電商，不過是換了一種銷售管道，而零售本質始終沒改變」，強調品牌對市場特徵、消費者思維的理解，以及用合適的產品、價格與服務滿足需求，纔是零售品牌的首要門檻，並建議將B2C重新詮釋為「Brand to Customers」。此說法凸顯部分業界人士對過度聚焦技術概念的警惕，主張回歸零售本質。
展望2025年，Vocus 分析指出，電商平臺角色已遠不止於工具，而是完整的商業生態系統；OMO與D2C（Direct to Consumer，直銷對消費者）成為推動行業發展的核心動力，電商平臺在其中扮演關鍵支撐角色。天地人學堂亦從教育面向補充，說明傳統實體零售歷經街邊雜貨店、購物商場、超市、量販通路等階段後，當前正邁向虛實融合的OMO新形態。
綜合各來源觀察，新零售轉型雖為共識方向，但各媒體對實施重點的詮釋仍有差異：部分強調資料整合與技術佈局，部分則提醒回歸品牌與消費者需求之本質。目前業界尚無統一標準界定OMO與O2O的具體差異，企業在推動轉型時，仍需依自身產業特性與資源條件，選擇適合的虛實融合路徑。
