  # 2025 Global Sustainability Governance Turning Point: Dual Upgrade of Carbon Pricing and GRI Standards
  
  > 2025 marks a pivotal year for global sustainability governance, as the International Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism, GRI disclosure standard updates, and Asia's low-carbon race advance simultaneously, placing stricter climate accountability and transparency demands on enterprises.
  
  The Industrial Development Bureau of the Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA) launched the 2025 Green Industry Trend Promotion Program, holding "Green Finance Industry Application Briefing Sessions" in Hsinchu, Taichung, and Tainan. The initiative encourages enterprises to adopt sustainable green finance strategies and promotes gender equality across industries. The Bureau also published the latest issue of its *Sustainable Industry Development Journal*, which covers the trends of the International Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM), the definition and implementation of carbon pricing, and self-assessment tools and carbon offset systems available to enterprises—aimed at building corporate readiness for the evolving carbon regulatory landscape.
  
  On the international front, ESG Business Daily reports that 2025 has become a turning point for global sustainability governance. From Brazil's COP30 to the launch of the EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD), and Asia's accelerating competition in carbon markets and sustainable finance, countries are facing stricter climate accountability, regulations, and transparency requirements. The publication analyzes four key shifts in 2025 sustainability, including climate accountability and Asia's low-carbon race, reflecting a global shift toward a more stringent compliance era.
  
  Regarding disclosure standards, SustaiHub reports that the GRI Standards released their latest provisions in June 2025, including GRI 102 *Climate Change 2025* and GRI 103 *Energy 2025*, which will take effect in 2027. Enterprises must now meet more detailed disclosure requirements—spanning just transition, climate adaptation, and enhanced Scope 3 emissions transparency. This update represents not merely a revision of provisions, but a shift from compliance-driven reporting toward substantive governance management.
  
  ESG Vision offers an industry-action perspective, emphasizing that nature underpins every dimension of human society, well-being, and economic activity—providing development, equality, economic value, and security, while strengthening resilience against climate change. The report outlines action frameworks for 12 major industries to address sustainable development and biodiversity, offering concrete sustainability implementation pathways across sectors.
  
  Synthesizing these sources, the 2025 sustainability landscape is undergoing a multi-layered upgrade in regulations, carbon pricing mechanisms, and corporate disclosure requirements. The MOEA's briefing sessions and journal publication aim to help domestic enterprises keep pace with international trends, while the GRI standard updates and EU CSRD launch further raise the bar for corporate transparency. Industry observers expect that enterprises will need to strengthen carbon management, Scope 3 emissions accounting, and climate adaptation capabilities to remain competitive in an increasingly rigorous sustainability governance environment.

# 2025全球永續治理轉折 碳定價與GRI準則雙重升級

> 2025年成為全球永續治理關鍵轉折年，國際碳邊境調整機制、GRI揭露準則更新與亞洲低碳競局同步推進，企業面臨更嚴格氣候問責與透明度要求。

經濟部產業發展署於114年度推動產業綠色趨勢推廣計畫，分別於新竹、臺中及臺南辦理「綠色金融產業應用宣導說明會」，鼓勵企業採取永續綠色金融相關營運策略，並配合推動性別平等，促進產業全面落實性別平等。該署同步發行《永續產業發展期刊》，本期規劃介紹國際碳邊境調整機制（CBAM）的推動趨勢、碳定價的定義與施行方式，並介紹企業可加以利用之自我檢視工具、碳抵換制度等資訊，提供企業參考借鑑，培養因應碳制度浪潮的應變能力。
國際層面，ESG商業日報指出，2025年成為全球永續治理的轉折點，從巴西COP30到歐盟CSRD啟動，以及亞洲在碳市場與永續金融的競速，各國面臨更嚴格的氣候問責、法規與透明度要求。該報導分析2025永續關鍵四大變局，涵蓋氣候問責、亞洲低碳競局等議題，反映全球永續治理正邁向更嚴格的合規時代。
在揭露標準方面，SustaiHub報導指出，GRI Standards於2025年6月釋出最新條文，包含GRI 102《氣候變遷2025》及GRI 103《能源2025》，該準則將於2027年生效。企業必須面對更細緻的揭露要求，從公正轉型、氣候調適、到範疇三排放資訊透明度的提升，這場更新不只是條文上的調整，更代表永續報告揭露從合規走向實質治理管理。
ESG遠見則從產業行動角度解析，大自然支撐著人類社會、福祉與經濟之各個層面，其提供人類發展與平等、經濟價值及安全，同時也強化我們對抗氣候變遷之韌性。該報導解析12大產業應對永續發展、生物多樣性的行動方針，為不同產業提供具體的永續實踐路徑。
綜合各來源觀察，2025年永續產業正經歷法規標準、碳定價機制與企業揭露要求的多重升級。經濟部產業發展署透過說明會與期刊出版，協助國內企業掌握國際趨勢；國際間GRI準則更新與歐盟CSRD啟動，則進一步提高企業透明度門檻。業界預期，未來企業需強化碳管理、範疇三排放盤查及氣候調適能力，方能在日益嚴格的永續治理環境中保持競爭力。
