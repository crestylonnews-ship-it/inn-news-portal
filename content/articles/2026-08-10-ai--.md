  # AI Pilot Applications in News Media: Industry Pragmatically Explores, Journalists Urge Protection of Professional Autonomy
  
  > Artificial intelligence technology is gradually entering news media workflows, with multiple outlets launching pilot applications in video editing and synthesized voice. The trend has sparked discussion on structural impacts to media, while journalists continue to monitor issues of media monopoly and professional autonomy.
  
  According to Yam News (蕃新聞), the structural impact of artificial intelligence on news media has gone beyond mere news practice and management processes. The report notes that newspaper companies are actively investing in niche markets within the media sector, such as JP/Politikens Hus purchasing medical and design publications, and Schibsted sweeping through Norway's podcast market, indicating that capital deployment and technology application are proceeding in parallel. Wazaiii News (哇塞新聞網) further explained that current AI pilot work across media outlets includes testing AI to reduce the video editing burden on editorial teams, experimenting with synthesized voice to create audio content, and integrating dictation and translation functions, reflecting the industry's pragmatic exploration of concrete pathways for technology integration.
  
  Both reports confirm that AI technology has entered the pilot stage in news media, with application directions focused on automating and improving efficiency in content production workflows. However, there are differing views on the depth and breadth of AI's impact. Yam News emphasizes that its "structural impact" has transcended news practice itself, implying potential deeper transformations touching media business models and industry structures. Wazaiii News, on the other hand, focuses on describing specific current "pilot projects," with a tone more偏向 to recording the present state of technology application, without making clear judgments on long-term structural impacts. This difference highlights varying perspectives within the industry on AI's development trajectory—some viewpoints may see AI as an auxiliary tool, while others worry about its potential disruptive consequences. Related impacts remain to be further confirmed by subsequent developments and research.
  
  Beyond technological change, the internal tensions facing media operations also warrant attention. An article on the UDN Blog notes that newcomers to the broadcast media field often enter with a sense of public service and conscience, hoping to follow journalistic codes of conduct, uphold social responsibility, and fulfill their duties in an autonomous, neutral, and precise manner. Yet the contradiction between media ideals and profit concerns has always existed in reality, meaning that when facing the introduction of technologies like AI, journalists must not only consider efficiency gains but also examine whether technology application will further compress editorial space aligned with journalistic ethics, or exacerbate the impact of profit-first priorities on professional autonomy. This issue intertwines with technical discussions, forming the complex dimensions of media's current situation.
  
  Journalists' concerns about professional autonomy and the media environment also have specific social actions as historical markers. A Thinking Taiwan (想想論壇) article reviewed the "Hello Big, I'm Not Afraid, 901 Anti-Media Monopoly" march initiated by the Taiwan News Reporters Association, regarded as an important milestone in Taiwan's history of fighting for freedom of speech and professional autonomy in news media. The action highlighted the deep concerns of journalism professionals about media monopoly potentially threatening diverse voices and professional judgment. Although this march took place in an earlier period, its core demands—defending journalistic professional autonomy and opposing monopoly—remain relevant against the backdrop of AI technology rapidly permeating the media industry. Technological change and market structural shifts may bring new concentration risks or autonomy challenges, and how journalist groups respond to the new situation while continuing to safeguard professional values remains a noteworthy follow-up issue.
  
  Overall, pilot applications of AI in news media have become a visible trend, but interpretations and levels of emphasis regarding its impact vary across sectors. As media pursue technological efficiency and operational benefits, how to balance journalistic ideals, professional autonomy, and social responsibility while remaining vigilant against potential monopolistic tendencies in market structure is a challenge共同 faced by the industry and its practitioners. The future evolution of AI technology, adjustments to media business models, and the direction of collective action by journalists will all influence the development trajectory of Taiwan's news media, and related developments continue to attract attention.

# AI試點應用於新聞媒體 業者務實探索 新聞工作者籲守護專業自主

> 人工智慧技術正逐步進入新聞媒體工作流程，多家業者展開影片編輯、合成語音等試點應用。此趨勢引發對媒體結構性影響的討論，同時新聞工作者亦持續關注媒體壟斷與專業自主議題。

根據蕃新聞報導，人工智慧對新聞媒體的結構性影響，已超越單純的新聞實踐與管理過程。報導指出，報業公司正積極投資媒體領域中的利基市場，例如JP/Politikens Hus購買醫療與設計出版物，以及Schibsted橫掃挪威播客市場，顯示資本佈局與技術應用並行。哇塞新聞網則進一步說明，目前各家媒體的AI試點工作，包括測試人工智慧減輕編輯團隊的影片編輯負擔、實驗使用合成語音建立音訊內容，以及整合聽寫、翻譯等功能，反映業界正務實探索技術整合的具體路徑。
兩則報導共同確認了人工智慧技術已進入新聞媒體的試點階段，且應用方向聚焦於內容製作流程的自動化與效率提升。然而，對於AI影響的深度與廣度，說法存在差異。蕃新聞強調其「結構性影響」已超越新聞實踐本身，暗示可能觸及媒體商業模式與產業結構的深層變革；哇塞新聞網則側重於描述當前「試點工作」的具體專案，語氣較偏向技術應用的現況記錄，尚未對長期結構性衝擊作出明確判斷。此種差異凸顯了業界對AI發展軌跡的觀察角度不同，部分觀點可能視AI為輔助工具，另一部分則擔憂其潛在的顛覆性後果，相關影響仍有待後續發展與研究進一步確認。
在技術變遷之外，媒體營運面臨的內在張力同樣引人關注。UDN部落格文章提及，初投入傳播媒體領域的工作者，往往懷抱公益與良知，希望依循新聞工作守則，秉持社會責任理念，以自主、中立、精確的方式盡責。然而，現實中媒體理想與營利問題之間的矛盾始終存在，這使得新聞工作者在面對AI等技術匯入時，不僅需考量效率提升，也必須審視技術應用是否會進一步壓縮符合新聞倫理的編輯空間，或加劇營利優先對專業自主的衝擊。此議題與技術層面的討論相互交織，構成媒體當前處境的複雜面向。
新聞工作者對專業自主與媒體環境的關注，亦有具體的社會行動作為歷史印記。想想論壇文章回顧，由臺灣新聞記者協會發起的「你好大、我不怕，901反媒體壟斷」大遊行，在臺灣爭取言論自由、新聞媒體專業自主的歷史上，被視為一個重要的裡程碑。該行動凸顯了新聞從業人員對媒體壟斷可能威脅多元聲音與專業判斷的深切憂慮。儘管此遊行發生於較早時期，但其核心訴求——捍衛新聞專業自主與對抗壟斷——在當前AI技術快速滲透媒體產業的背景下，仍具參考意義。技術變革與市場結構變化可能帶來新的集中風險或自主性挑戰，新聞工作者團體如何因應新形勢，持續守護專業價值，成為值得觀察的後續課題。
整體而言，人工智慧在新聞媒體的試點應用已成為可見趨勢，但各界對其影響的解讀與重視程度不一。媒體在追求技術效率與營運效益的同時，如何兼顧新聞理想、專業自主與社會責任，並警惕市場結構可能出現的壟斷傾向，是產業與從業人員共同面臨的課題。未來AI技術的演進、媒體商業模式的調整，以及新聞工作者集體行動的走向，都將影響臺灣新聞媒體的發展軌跡，相關發展持續受到關注。
