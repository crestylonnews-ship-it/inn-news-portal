  # Global Logistics Supply Chains Face Triple Challenge: AI Warehouse Upgrades, Inflation Pressures, and Geopolitical Risks
  
  > Global logistics supply chains are confronting a dual transformation of technological upgrades and risk challenges. Amazon is making massive investments in AI warehouse technology, supply chain pressures are fueling inflation concerns, and the attack on a Russian e-commerce warehouse has further highlighted geopolitical risks.
  
  Against the backdrop of global supply chain restructuring, the logistics industry is facing a dual transformation of technological upgrades and risk challenges. According to JENJAN, smart warehousing is rapidly evolving from a traditional logistics cost center into core infrastructure for enterprise competitiveness and investment value. North America has become a key battleground for this wave of industrial upgrading, covering diverse application scenarios from California e-commerce to Texas healthcare.
  
  Amazon's layout in the AI warehouse sector has been particularly noteworthy. LINE TODAY reported that Amazon has invested $25 billion in AI warehouse and logistics automation technology, attempting to redefine the e-commerce competitive landscape. The report also noted that since acquiring robotics startup Kiva Systems in 2012, Amazon has deployed over 750,000 mobile devices, demonstrating its long-term commitment to smart warehousing. However, the scale and specific benefits of this investment have only been reported by a single media outlet and await cross-verification from other sources.
  
  Supply chain pressures are posing a potential threat to inflation. The Economic Daily, citing the Logistics Manager Index (LMI) compiled by Associate Professor Zac Rogers of the University of Colorado, showed that the monthly survey covers costs and capacity across three key areas: transportation, inventory, and warehousing. Recent data reflects persistently rising supply chain pressures. This indicator serves as an important leading signal for observing inflation pressures, but experts caution that a single index cannot fully reflect overall supply chain conditions and must be combined with other data for comprehensive judgment.
  
  Geopolitical risks are also impacting logistics supply chains. The Economic Times reported that Russian e-commerce giant Wildberries' warehouse facilities were attacked on July 29, 2026, sparking market concerns about the fragility of e-commerce supply chains. This incident highlights the potential threats facing logistics infrastructure operating in conflict zones and has prompted enterprises to reassess the geopolitical risk distribution of their supply chains.
  
  Facing multiple challenges, logistics providers are actively adjusting their strategies. one.O pointed out that developing more flexible logistics strategies does not mean simply outsourcing warehousing and transportation; enterprises can grasp supply chain dynamics and inventory flows through innovative models such as cross-docking. JENJAN also analyzed that the North American smart warehouse market is expanding from traditional e-commerce to diverse industries such as healthcare, indicating that the application scope of technological upgrades continues to broaden.
  
  Synthesizing reports from various media, global logistics supply chains are facing a triple test of technological upgrades, inflation pressures, and geopolitical risks. Amazon's large-scale investment reflects tech giants' emphasis on smart warehousing, but the investment benefits and industry impact still need time to verify; the supply chain pressures shown by the Logistics Manager Index may become a potential variable for inflation; and the Russian warehouse attack incident reminds enterprises that geopolitical risks have become an indispensable link in supply chain planning. Future supply chain strategies will trend more toward diversification and flexibility, and enterprises need to strike a balance between efficiency and risk.

# 全球物流供應鏈迎變局：AI 倉儲升級、通膨壓力與地緣風險並存

> 全球物流供應鏈正面臨技術升級與風險挑戰雙重變局，亞馬遜大舉投資 AI 倉儲技術，供應鏈壓力推升通膨疑慮，俄羅斯電商倉儲遇襲更凸顯地緣政治風險。

在全球供應鏈重組的背景下，物流產業正面臨技術升級與風險挑戰的雙重變局。根據 JENJAN 報導，智慧倉儲正在快速從傳統物流成本中心，轉變為企業競爭力與投資價值的核心基礎設施，北美地區成為此波產業升級的重要戰場，涵蓋從加州電商到德州醫療的多樣化應用場景。
亞馬遜在 AI 倉儲領域的佈局尤為引人注目。LINE TODAY 報導指出，亞馬遜已投入 250 億美元佈局 AI 倉儲與物流自動化技術，試圖重新定義電商競爭格局。該報導同時提到，亞馬遜自 2012 年收購機器人新創公司 Kiva Systems 以來，已部署超過 75 萬臺移動裝置，顯示其長期投資智慧倉儲的決心。然而，此項投資規模與具體效益，目前僅見單一媒體報導，尚待其他來源交叉驗證。
供應鏈壓力正對通膨形成潛在威脅。經濟日報引述科羅拉多大學副教授 Zac Rogers 編制的「物流經理人指數」（LMI）顯示，該月度調查涵蓋運輸、庫存與倉儲三大關鍵領域的成本與量能，近期資料反映供應鏈壓力持續升高。此指標為觀察通膨壓力的重要先行訊號，但專家提醒，單一指數無法完全反映整體供應鏈狀況，需結合其他資料綜合判斷。
地緣政治風險同樣衝擊物流供應鏈。《經濟時報》報導，俄羅斯電商巨頭 Wildberries 的倉儲設施於 2026 年 7 月 29 日遭遇襲擊，引發市場對電商供應鏈脆弱性的關注。此事件凸顯在衝突區域運營的物流基礎設施面臨的潛在威脅，也促使企業重新評估供應鏈的地緣風險分佈。
面對多重挑戰，物流業者正積極調整策略。one.O 指出，發展更具彈性的物流策略並非只能將倉儲運輸委外，企業可透過越庫作業等創新模式掌握供應鏈動態與庫存流向。JENJAN 亦分析，北美智慧倉儲市場正從傳統電商延伸至醫療等多元產業，顯示技術升級的應用範疇持續擴大。
綜合各媒體報導，全球物流供應鏈正面臨技術升級、通膨壓力與地緣風險的三重考驗。亞馬遜的大規模投資反映科技巨頭對智慧倉儲的重視，但投資效益與產業影響仍需時間驗證；物流經理人指數顯示的供應鏈壓力，可能成為通膨的潛在變數；而俄羅斯倉儲遇襲事件則提醒企業，地緣政治風險已成為供應鏈規劃不可忽視的環節。未來供應鏈策略將更趨向多元化與彈性化，企業需在效率與風險之間取得平衡。
