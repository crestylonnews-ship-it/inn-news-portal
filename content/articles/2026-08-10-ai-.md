  The Ministry of Education's "2026 Digital Learning International Forum" was held on August 6–7 at the Kaohsiung Exhibition Hall. This year's theme, "New Horizons in Digital Learning under Human-Machine Collaboration," invited experts and scholars from Singapore, South Korea, Estonia, Finland, the United Kingdom, Hong Kong, the Netherlands, and Japan to exchange ideas with Taiwan's industry, government, and academic communities.
  
  Liu Guowei, Deputy Minister of Education, stated that the forum focuses on the Talent Ark Initiative, aiming to build four core competencies among teachers and students through self-developed educational AI: the ability to learn with AI, the responsibility to use AI, the ability to solve problems with AI, and the ability to collaborate with machines.
  
  Meanwhile, on August 7, the Taoyuan City Council held a session for the mayor's policy report and inquiries. City councilor Liu Shengquan raised concerns about youth unemployment, noting that although Taoyuan has 240,000 job openings, the unemployment rate for those aged 15 to 24 has remained above 11% in recent years, indicating structural problems in the labor market. Deputy Mayor Su Junbin responded that the city government has convened the Youth Affairs Bureau, Labor Bureau, and Education Bureau for cross-departmental coordination and will propose concrete improvement measures.

# 青年就業與AI教育並重 論壇聚焦人才培育

> 教育部於高雄舉辦數位學習國際論壇，聚焦AI人才培育；桃園市議員同步質疑青年失業率高企，促跨局處協調解決結構性問題。

教育部主辦的「2026數位學習國際論壇」於 8 月 6 日至 7 日在高雄展覽館舉行，今年主題為「人機協作下數位學習新視野」，邀請新加坡、韓國、愛沙尼亞、芬蘭、英國、香港、荷蘭及日本等國專家學者，與國內產官學界進行交流。
教育部政務次長劉國偉表示，論壇聚焦於人才方舟計畫，期望透過自主建構的教育型 AI，建立師生四項核心能力：用 AI 學習的能力、有責任使用 AI 的能力、用 AI 解決問題的能力，以及人機協作的能力。
與此同時，桃園市議會 8 月 7 日進行市長施政報告與質詢。市議員劉勝全針對青年失業問題提出質疑，指出桃園市雖有 24 萬個職缺，但 15 至 24 歲青年失業率近年持續卡在 11% 以上，顯示勞動市場存在結構性問題。副市長蘇俊賓回應表示，市府已召集青年局、勞動局及教育局進行跨部門協調，將提出具體改善方案。
