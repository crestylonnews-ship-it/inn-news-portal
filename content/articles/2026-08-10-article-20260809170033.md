  # South Korea's Education Trends and Taiwan-Korea Academic Exchange Developments
  
  > Representative of the Korean Representative Office in Taipei, Hwang Jae-ho, recently returned to his alma mater, Chinese Culture University, to look ahead to future Taiwan-Korea educational cooperation; meanwhile, South Korean youth, influenced by the AI wave, are shifting toward semiconductor vocational education, overturning traditional academic advancement concepts.
  
  Representative Hwang Jae-ho of the Korean Representative Office in Taipei recently led his office colleagues back to his alma mater, Chinese Culture University. According to Yahoo Entertainment, Hwang's visit aimed to express gratitude for his educational upbringing, reconnect with former mentors and classmates, and look forward to future Taiwan-Korea cooperation in education, culture, and academia. Hwang graduated from the Russian Language Program of the Department of Oriental Languages and Literatures at Culture University. He noted that this was his first return to his alma mater in 38 years since graduation and one of the most important external activities since assuming his post in Taiwan. Accompanying him were representatives from the office, including Researcher Zhang Taixuan of the Documents Section and Researcher Park Dong-jun of the Consular Section's Visa Division, both of whom are also alumni of Culture University's Korean Language Department and Tourism and Hospitality Management Department, respectively. During the visit, Hwang joyfully reunited with his former university classmate, now Dean of the College of International Studies and Foreign Languages, Wang Yijun.
  
  Meanwhile, South Korea is undergoing a transformation in its education and career pathways. Techbang.com reports that in the context of generative AI rapidly disrupting white-collar careers, the traditional career path of "entering a prestigious university equals guaranteed high salary and stability" is being strongly questioned by the younger generation. More South Korean youth are choosing to abandon preparation for traditional university entrance exams and instead enter semiconductor vocational high schools to learn professional practical skills.
  
  Techbang.com further explains that this trend reflects the strong demand for technical talent in the semiconductor industry, while also allowing students to sidestep the devaluation of academic credentials and redefine their career paths through practical abilities, securing high salaries earlier. Given South Korean society's high regard for traditional diplomas, this transformation is seen as a disruptive wave of educational change.

# 韓國教育趨勢與臺韓學術交流動態

> 駐臺北韓國代表處代表黃載皓日前重返母校中國文化大學，展望未來臺韓教育合作；與此同時，南韓青少年受AI浪潮影響，轉向半導體技職教育，顛覆傳統升學觀念。

駐臺北韓國代表處代表黃載皓日前率領代表處同仁，重返其母校中國文化大學。據Yahoo Entertainment報導，黃載皓此行旨在感念培育之恩，並與昔日師長、同窗敘舊，同時展望未來臺韓在教育、文化與學術等領域的合作。黃載皓畢業於文化大學東方語文學系俄文組，他表示這是他畢業三十八年來首次重返母校，也是抵臺履新後最重要的對外活動之一。隨行參訪的代表處同仁，包括文書組研究員張泰瑄與領事科簽證組研究員樸東俊，兩人亦分別為文大韓文系與觀光事業學系校友。此行黃載皓與大學同班同學、現任國際暨外語學院院長王怡君歡喜重逢。

在南韓國內，教育與職涯路徑正經歷一波轉型。Techbang.com報導指出，在生成式人工智慧（AI）快速顛覆白領職涯的背景下，傳統「考取名校大學＝保障高薪穩定」的職涯路徑正受到年輕一代的強烈質疑。南韓青少年越來越多地選擇放棄備戰傳統大學入學考試，轉而進入半導體職業高中學習專業實務技能。

Techbang.com進一步說明，這股趨勢顯示半導體產業對技術人才的強大需求，同時也讓學子能避開學歷貶值，透過實務能力重新定義職涯路徑，並提早鎖定高薪。南韓社會高度重視傳統文憑，這場變革被視為顛覆性的教育轉型浪潮。
