  The Third Senior Health Industry Expo was held from August 7 to 9 at Taipei World Trade Center Hall 1. The opening ceremony was attended by the President, the Minister of Health and Welfare, and officials from relevant central government agencies, demonstrating the government's commitment to advancing senior health policies (Source: Yahoo Entertainment, 2026-08-07).
  
  The Department of Industrial Technology under the Ministry of Economic Affairs organized three exhibition zones—"Health Management," "Warm Guardianship," and "Rehabilitation Empowerment"—in its dedicated pavilion. The displays brought together four affiliated institutions: the Industrial Technology Research Institute, the Metal Industries Research & Development Centre, the Textile Research Institute, and the Food Industry Research and Development Institute, showcasing a total of 30 senior health technology achievements. Guo Zhao-zhong, director of the Department of Industrial Technology, stated that the exhibited成果 have been successively integrated into home care, community hubs, and medical long-term care institutions. On opening day, the institute facilitated the signing of a letter of intent for cooperation between the Industrial Technology Research Institute and Taisheng Company (Source: Yahoo Entertainment, 2026-08-07).
  
  The Ministry of Health and Welfare's Taoyuan Hospital participated under the theme of "Integration of Medical Care and Long-term Care," with its geriatric medicine department, community and home nursing, traditional Chinese medicine department, and assistive devices center jointly showcasing achievements in home-based medical care, home hospice, and smart assistive devices (Source: Yahoo Entertainment, 2026-08-07). Taoyuan Mayor Chang Shan-zheng attended the expo on August 8, stating that this year's Taoyuan pavilion brings together top medical institutions and innovative startups, demonstrating Taoyuan's smart healthcare capabilities, and expressing hope that Taoyuan will become a livable city where seniors can live with peace of mind (Source: Yahoo Entertainment, 2026-08-08).
  
  Tai Shin Financial Holdings, together with Tai Shin Bank,新光人壽 (Sino Guardian Life Insurance), and Tai Shin Securities, launched an integrated program titled "Smart Financial Planning for Health, AI Protection for Enjoyable Living." The initiative combines an AI-powered fraud prevention "War God Model," pre-arranged trust planning, and innovative services pairing genetic testing with critical illness insurance, creating a financial protection network covering medical prevention and asset succession (Source: Yahoo Entertainment, 2026-08-07).

# 第三屆高齡健康博覽會登場 政學研產共推智慧照護

> 第三屆高齡健康產業博覽會8月7日至9日於臺北世貿一館舉行，經濟部展出30項科技成果，衛福部桃園醫院展示在宅醫療新模式，桃園市長張善政8日出席並強調打造宜居城市。

第三屆高齡健康產業博覽會於8月7日至9日在臺北世貿一館舉行，開幕典禮由總統、衛生福利部部長及中央相關部會長官共同出席，展現政府推動高齡健康政策的決心（來源：Yahoo Entertainment，2026-08-07）。

經濟部產業技術司於技術司專館規劃「健康管理」、「暖心守護」及「復健賦能」三大展區，集結工研院、金屬中心、紡織所及食品所等4家法人，共展出30項高齡健康科技成果。技術司司長郭肇中表示，展出成果已陸續匯入居家照護、社羣據點及醫療長照機構；開幕當天促成工研院與泰陞公司簽署合作意向書（來源：Yahoo Entertainment，2026-08-07）。

衛生福利部桃園醫院以「醫療與長照整合」為主軸參展，由老年醫學科、社羣居家護理、中醫科及輔具中心共同展示在宅醫療、居家安寧與智慧輔具等服務成果（來源：Yahoo Entertainment，2026-08-07）。桃園市長張善政8日出席博覽會，表示本屆桃園館匯集頂尖醫療機構與新創團隊，展現桃園智慧醫療實力，期盼讓桃園成為長輩安心生活的宜居城市（來源：Yahoo Entertainment，2026-08-08）。

臺新金控則攜手臺新銀行、新光人壽與臺新證券推出「聰明理財存健康，AI守護享樂活」整合方案，結合AI智慧防詐「戰神模型」、預立信託規劃及「基因檢測×重大傷病險」創新服務，打造涵蓋醫療預防與資產傳承的金融防護網（來源：Yahoo Entertainment，2026-08-07）。
