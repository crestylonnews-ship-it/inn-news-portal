  The Third Senior Health Industry Expo was held from August 7 to 9, 2026, at Taipei World Trade Center Hall 1. The opening ceremony was attended by the President, the Minister of Health and Welfare, and senior officials from relevant central government agencies, demonstrating the government's commitment to promoting a healthy Taiwan and senior health policies.
  
  The Department of Industrial Technology of the Ministry of Economic Affairs brought together four affiliated institutions—Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI), Metal Industries Research & Development Centre (MIRDC), Textile Research and Development Center (TRDC), and Food Industry Research and Development Institute (FIRDI)—to organize three exhibition zones: "Health Management," "Heartfelt Protection," and "Rehabilitation Empowerment," showcasing a total of 30 senior health technology achievements. Guo Zhao-zhong, Director of the Department of Industrial Technology, stated that these achievements have been successively introduced into home care, community centers, and medical long-term care institutions. On the opening day, ITRI and Taisheng Company signed a memorandum of understanding for cooperation.
  
  Taoyuan General Hospital of the Ministry of Health and Welfare participated with the theme of "Integration of Medical Care and Long-Term Care." Exhibits were jointly presented by the Geriatric Medicine Department, Community and Home Nursing, Traditional Chinese Medicine Department, and Assistive Devices Center, covering services such as health promotion, chronic disease management, home medical care, home emergency care, and home hospice care, presenting a new model for home-based care.
  
  Taoyuan Mayor Chang Shan-zheng visited the expo on August 8, noting that this year's Taoyuan pavilion brings together top medical institutions and innovative startups, showcasing diverse senior health care services and smart medical capabilities. He expressed hope to continue collaborating with all sectors to make Taoyuan a livable city where seniors can live with peace of mind. Taiwan Shin Kong Commercial Bank also launched an integrated financial solution at the event, combining an AI-powered anti-fraud model with innovative gene testing services to safeguard the health and financial assets of the silver-haired population.

# 第三屆高齡健康產業博覽會登場 跨域展示銀髮照護新科技

> 第三屆高齡健康產業博覽會 8 月 7 日至 9 日於臺北世貿一館舉行，經濟部技術司展出 30 項科技成果，部桃展示在宅照護新模式，桃園市長張善政出席期許打造宜居城市。

第三屆高齡健康產業博覽會於 2026 年 8 月 7 日至 9 日在臺北世貿一館舉行，開幕典禮由總統、衛生福利部部長及中央相關部會長官共同出席，展現政府推動健康臺灣與高齡健康政策的決心。

經濟部產業技術司此次集結工研院、金屬中心、紡織所及食品所等 4 家法人，規劃「健康管理」、「暖心守護」及「復健賦能」三大展區，共展出 30 項高齡健康科技成果。技術司司長郭肇中表示，相關成果已陸續匯入居家照護、社羣據點及醫療長照機構，開幕當天更促成工研院與泰陞公司簽署合作意向書。

衛生福利部桃園醫院以「醫療與長照整合」為主軸參展，由老年醫學科、社羣居家護理、中醫科及輔具中心共同展示，涵蓋健康促進、慢性病管理、居家醫療、在宅急診與居家安寧等服務，呈現在宅照護新模式。

桃園市長張善政 8 日前往與會，指出本屆桃園館匯集頂尖醫療機構與新創團隊，展現長者健康多元照護服務與智慧醫療實力，期盼持續與各界攜手，讓桃園成為長輩安心生活的宜居城市。臺新金控則於會中推出整合金融方案，結合 AI 智慧防詐模型與基因檢測創新服務，守護銀髮族健康與財富資產。
