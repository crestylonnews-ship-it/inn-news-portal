  The Third Senior Health Industry Expo was held from August 7 to 9 at Taipei World Trade Center Hall 1. According to the Ministry of Economic Affairs' Technology Division, the division's dedicated exhibition area brought together four affiliated institutions—Industrial Technology Research Institute, Metal Industries Research & Development Centre, Textile Research Centre, and Food Industry Research and Development Institute—organizing three exhibition zones: "Health Management," "Caring Guardianship," and "Rehabilitation and Empowerment," displaying a total of 30 senior health technology achievements. Guo Zhao-zhong, director of the Technology Division, stated that the showcased innovations have been successively integrated into home care, community centers, and medical long-term care institutions.
  
  The Department of Health Taoyuan General Hospital participated under the theme of "Integration of Medical Care and Long-Term Care," with its geriatric medicine department, community and home nursing, Chinese medicine department, and assistive devices center jointly presenting achievements in health promotion, chronic disease management, home medical care, home emergency services, and home palliative care. Additionally, the office of Taoyuan Mayor Zhang Shan-zheng announced that Zhang visited the venue on August 8, stating that the Taoyuan pavilion brought together top medical institutions and innovative startups, showcasing diverse care services for seniors' health.
  
  Financial institutions also participated in the expo. E.Sun Financial Holding, in partnership with E.Sun Bank,新光人壽 (Sino United Life Insurance), and E.Sun Securities, launched an integrated solution combining AI-powered anti-fraud technology with pre-established trust planning, while promoting innovative services that combine genetic testing with critical illness insurance. The team provided on-site professional consultations and interactive experiences.

# 第三屆高齡健康博覽會開幕 科技與照護整合成亮點

> 第三屆高齡健康產業博覽會8月7日至9日於臺北世貿一館舉行，經濟部技術司展出30項科技成果，部桃展示在宅照護新模式，臺新金控推出AI防詐理財方案。

第三屆高齡健康產業博覽會於8月7日至9日在臺北世貿一館舉行。根據經濟部產業技術司說明，本次技術司專館集結工研院、金屬中心、紡織所及食品所等4家法人，規劃「健康管理」、「暖心守護」及「復健賦能」三大展區，共展出30項高齡健康科技成果。技術司司長郭肇中表示，展出成果已陸續匯入居家照護、社羣據點及醫療長照機構。
衛生福利部桃園醫院以「醫療與長照整合」為主軸參展，由老年醫學科、社羣居家護理、中醫科及輔具中心共同展示健康促進、慢性病管理、居家醫療、在宅急診與居家安寧等服務成果。另據桃園市長張善政辦公室指出，張善政於8日前往會場出席，表示桃園館匯集頂尖醫療機構與新創團隊，展現長者健康多元照護服務。
金融業者也參與此次博覽會。臺新金控攜手臺新銀行、新光人壽與臺新證券推出整合方案，結合AI智慧防詐技術與預立信託規劃，並推動基因檢測與重大傷病險結合之創新服務，現場提供專業諮詢與互動體驗。
