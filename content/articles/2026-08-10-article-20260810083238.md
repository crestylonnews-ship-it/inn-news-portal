  # Taiwan's International Education Exchange Thrives: Legal Cooperation and Digital Forum Advance in Tandem
  
  > Taiwan has recently promoted a range of international education exchanges, including legal talent training programs for Taiwan-Vietnam partnerships, overseas study tours for high school students, and an international forum on digital learning, showcasing diverse efforts to deepen international education.
  
  National Kaohsiung University recently partnered with Ho Chi Minh City University of Law in Vietnam to co-host the "115th International Legal Leadership Training Camp," combining all-English courses with practical visits to judicial, administrative, and legal service institutions. The program allows faculty and students from both universities to gain in-depth exposure to Taiwan's rule-of-law practices, thereby deepening Taiwan-Vietnam legal education cooperation and cultivating legal professionals equipped with an international perspective, cross-cultural communication skills, and cross-border practical capabilities.
  
  Wu Xinghao, Vice President for Administration at Kaohsiung University, delivered a lecture titled "Overview of Taiwanese Law" at the opening ceremony, introducing Taiwan's legal system and judicial institutions in English. He stated that in the face of globalization and increasingly complex cross-border legal issues, Kaohsiung University continues to deepen its international legal education by collaborating with sister universities abroad, combining theoretical coursework with practical exchanges to cultivate legal professionals who possess both professional competence and an international outlook.
  
  Meanwhile, the Ministry of Education's Youth Development Administration provided financial support to help Keelung Nuanli Senior High School apply for the "Youth Billion-Dollar Overseas Dream Fund Program — Soaring Overseas Track" on behalf of Keelung City. The school was selected with its proposal titled "Mirroring a Century, Creating the Future: Japan Kinki Region Study Tour," and carried out a 14-day overseas study program from July 30 to August 12. The initiative aims to broaden young people's international horizons and meaningfully connect international education with Keelung City's urban development issues, allowing high school students to learn about international urban development experiences through overseas visits.
  
  Additionally, the "2026 Digital Learning International Forum," hosted by the Ministry of Education, was held at the Kaohsiung Exhibition Hall on August 6–7. This year's forum, themed "New Horizons in Digital Learning under Human-Machine Collaboration," focused on the ministry's Talent Ark Program and invited scholars and experts from Singapore, South Korea, Estonia, Finland, the United Kingdom, Hong Kong, the Netherlands, and Japan, alongside domestic industry, government, and academic stakeholders. Liu Guowei, Political Deputy Minister of Education, stated that through the Talent Ark Program, the ministry hopes to build four key competencies among teachers and students: the ability to learn using AI, the responsibility to use AI ethically, the ability to solve problems with AI, and a related fourth competency. The forum drew participants from local and county governments, primary and secondary school teachers, and teacher-training universities for experience-sharing discussions.
  
  In summary, Taiwan's recent international education exchanges reflect a diversified development trajectory, spanning higher-education legal cooperation, overseas study tours for senior high schools, and digital learning and international education policy exchanges across different levels and dimensions. The Taiwan-Vietnam legal talent training camp emphasizes cross-border legal practical skills, the Nuanli High School Japan Kinki study tour focuses on expanding youth international perspectives and connecting them to local development, and the digital learning international forum centers on educational transformation and human-machine collaboration in the AI era. These activities reflect Taiwan's sustained investment in educational internationalization and talent development, while also demonstrating that different educational stages have distinct priorities and plans for international exchange.

# 臺國際教育交流熱絡 法學合作與數位論壇並舉

> 臺灣近期推動多項國際教育交流，包括臺越法學人才培訓、高中海外見學及數位學習國際論壇，展現深化國際教育的多元努力。

國立高雄大學近期與越南胡志明市法律大學合作，共同舉辦「115年度國際法律領導人才培訓營」，結合全英文課程與司法、行政及法律服務等實務參訪，讓兩校師生深入臺灣法治現場，瞭解臺灣法治運作，藉此深化臺越法學教育合作，培育兼具國際視野、跨文化溝通與跨境實務能力的法律人才。高雄大學行政副校長吳行浩於開幕時講授「臺灣法概說」，以英文介紹臺灣法律體系及司法制度。他表示，面對全球化發展與跨境法律議題日益增加，高雄大學持續深化國際法學教育，透過與海外姊妹校合作，結合理論課程與實務交流，培養兼具專業素養、國際視野及跨文化溝通能力的法律人才。
另一方面，教育部青年發展署提供金援，協助基隆市立暖暖高階中學代表基隆市申請「青年百億海外圓夢基金計劃—海外翱翔組」，以「映象百年・創生未來：日本近畿見學計劃」獲選，並於7月30日至8月12日展開為期14天的海外見學行程。此計劃旨在拓展青年國際視野，並將國際教育基隆市城市發議題深度連結，讓高中生透過海外參訪認識國際城市發展經驗。
此外，由教育部主辦的「2026數位學習國際論壇」於8月6日至7日在高雄展覽館舉行，今年以「人機協作下數位學習新視野」為主題，聚焦於教育部推動的人才方舟計劃，邀請新加坡、韓國、愛沙尼亞、芬蘭、英國、香港、荷蘭及日本等國學專家學者與國內產官學交流。教育部政務次長劉國偉表示，希望透過人才方舟計劃，建立師生四項能力，包括用AI學習的能力、有責任使用AI的能力、用AI解決問題的能力，以及另一項相關能力。論壇吸引各縣市政府、中小學教師在及師資培育大學等參與，進行經驗交流。
綜合觀察，臺灣近期在國際教育交流上呈現多元發展態勢，涵蓋高等教育法學合作、高中職海外見學，以及數位學習與國際教育政策交流等不同層級與面向。臺越法學人才培訓營強調跨境法律實務能力培育，暖暖高中日本近畿見學計劃則聚焦青年國際視野拓展與地方發展連結，數位學習國際論壇則聚焦AI時代教育轉型與人機協作議題。這些活動反映臺灣在教育國際化與人才培養上的持續投入，同時也顯示不同教育階段對國際交流的需求與規劃各有側重。
