  # Senior Financial Opportunities Boil Over as Banking Groups Race to Develop Wellness Villages and Asset Protection
  
  > As Taiwan enters a super-aged society, the third Senior Health Industry Expo opened in Taipei, with major banking groups including Cathay, CITIC, and Taishin-Sinlight actively developing senior financial products such as wellness villages, asset health checks, and fraud prevention services, demonstrating the financial sector's commitment to the silver economy.
  
  Taiwan is rapidly approaching a super-aged society, and financial institutions are competing to capture the blue ocean of senior economics. The third Senior Health Industry Expo opened in Taipei on the 7th of this month, with representatives from Cathay Financial Holdings, CITIC Financial Holdings, and Taishin-Sinlight Financial Holdings all participating to showcase financial and insurance services tailored for the elderly. CNA reporters Lü Yanci and Su Siyun reported that life insurance companies are actively planning wellness villages and senior care residences, while banks are developing integrated "full asset health check" services combining real estate and insurance, aiming to meet clients' diverse needs across different life stages through comprehensive financial services.
  
  Cathay Financial Holdings has assembled its five major subsidiaries to launch a "one-stop senior service." Liu Mingda, deputy general manager of Cathay Life Insurance, stated that expatriate policies will undergo significant transformation in the second half of the year, expected to shift toward a points-based system and deepen preventive medicine through "degenerative medical accounts" and expatriate policies. Chuang Hsiu-chu, deputy general manager of Cathay United Bank, explained that to combat senior financial fraud and dementia risks, the bank is prioritizing "elderly care trust" services, having already assisted over 46,000 people in establishing asset protection. Additionally, Cathay General Insurance and Cathay Securities are addressing senior daily mobility safety and retirement asset allocation respectively, offering insurance gap coverage and fraud-prevention financial education; Cathay Investment Trust is pursuing a "dual-core" strategy, combining popular high-dividend ETFs to capture retirement dividend opportunities.
  
  CITIC Financial Holdings, themed "Let Love Continue, Inherit the Family's Future," partnered with CITIC Bank and Taiwan Life Insurance to introduce a "three books, one retention" strategy, assisting the public in retirement and asset inheritance planning. CITIC Bank, recognized with the Senior-Friendly Award for two consecutive years for its "comprehensive wealth management planning," showcased AI-powered smart ATMs and teller systems utilizing image and behavioral recognition technology to prevent financial fraud. Taiwan Life Insurance launched "Healthy Living Manager 2.0" and unveiled plans for Taiwan's first campus wellness village, integrating a senior care ecosystem with digital financial tools and demonstrating its commitment to cross-sector development.
  
  Lin Wei-chun, general manager of Taishin-Sinlight Financial Holdings, announced that the Financial Supervisory Commission has approved the merger of Taishin Bank and Sinlight Bank, with Taishin Bank as the surviving entity and a tentative merger effective date of January 1 next year. Lin noted that Taiwan's economic outlook for the second half of the year is quite optimistic, with active capital markets and continued growth in financial sector lending. As a comprehensive financial services group spanning banking, insurance, securities, and investment trust, Taishin-Sinlight Financial Holdings also participated in the Senior Health Expo, demonstrating its commitment to the senior market.
  
  A review of each group's strategies reveals that senior financial services are shifting from single products toward integrated solutions. Cathay Financial Holdings emphasizes the integration of financial and insurance mechanisms, transforming professional services into interactive experiences; CITIC Financial Holdings focuses on combining digital technology with financial services; and Taishin-Sinlight Financial Holdings leverages post-merger scale advantages to expand its service range. However, the specific details and effectiveness of each group's services remain to be seen—particularly the progress of wellness village construction, the adoption of elderly care trusts, and the real-world performance of AI fraud prevention technology—all of which require ongoing monitoring.

# 高齡金融商機熱 金控搶佈局養生村與資產防護

> 臺灣邁入超高齡社會，第三屆高齡健康產業博覽會於臺北登場，國泰、中信、臺新新光等金控積極佈局高齡金融商機，推出養生村、資產健診與防詐服務，展現金融業對熟齡經濟的重視。

臺灣正快速邁向超高齡社會，金融業競相搶攻熟齡經濟藍海。第三屆高齡健康產業博覽會於本月7日在臺北登場，國泰金控、中信金控與臺新新光金控等集團皆派員參與，展示針對高齡族羣的金融與保險服務。中央社記者呂晏慈、蘇思雲報導指出，壽險業積極規劃養生村與康養住宅，銀行則規劃整合不動產、保險等「全資產健診」服務，盼透過完整金融服務滿足客戶在人生不同階段的各種需求。
國泰金控集結五大子公司推出「一站式樂齡服務」。國泰人壽副總劉明達表示，下半年外溢保單將進行重大轉型，預計朝點數化發展，並以「退化醫療帳戶」及外溢保單深耕預防醫學領域。國泰世華銀行副總莊秀珠則說明，為防範高齡金融詐騙與失智風險，銀行主力推廣「安養信託」服務，目前已協助逾4.6萬人建立資產防護。此外，國泰產險與證券分別針對高齡日常移動安全及退休資產配置，提供保險缺口補足與防詐理財教育；國泰投信則以「雙核心」策略，結合熱門高股息ETF搶攻退休除息商機。
中信金控則以「讓愛延續 傳承家的未來」為主軸，攜手中信銀行與臺灣人壽推出「三本一留」策略，協助民眾規劃退休與資產傳承。中信銀行憑藉「全財富理財規劃」連續兩年獲頒高齡友善大獎，並展示AI智慧ATM與櫃檯，運用影像與行為辨識技術防範金融詐騙。臺灣人壽則推出「健康樂活管家2.0」及全臺首座校園養生村藍圖，整合康養生態圈與數位理財工具，展現跨界佈局的決心。
臺新新光金控總經理林維俊表示，金管會已同意臺新銀行與新光銀行合併案，合併後存續主體為臺新銀行，暫定合併基準日為明年1月1日。林維俊指出，下半年臺灣經濟發展相當樂觀，資本市場活絡，金融業放款將持續成長。臺新新光金控為涵蓋銀行、保險、證券、投信等完整金融服務集團，此次亦參與高齡健康博覽會，展現對熟齡市場的重視。
綜合各家金控策略可見，高齡金融服務正從單一產品轉向整合型解決方案。國泰金控強調金融與保險機制的整合，將專業服務轉化為互動體驗；中信金控著重數位科技與金融服務的結合；臺新新光金控則以合併後規模優勢拓展服務範圍。然而，各集團具體服務細節與成效仍有待觀察，尤其是養生村建設進度、安養信託推廣成果，以及AI防詐技術的實際應用表現，皆需後續追蹤確認。
