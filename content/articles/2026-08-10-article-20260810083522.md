  # Multiple Countries Deepen Educational Exchanges as Taiwan-US, Taiwan-Vietnam, and Taiwan-Japan Collaborations Advance
  
  > The Ministry of Education's Youth Development Administration is funding Keelung High School's educational trip to Japan, while National Kaohsiung University co-hosts a training camp with Vietnam's Ho Chi Minh City University of Law. Meanwhile, US state legislative leaders visited Taiwan to explore educational cooperation, highlighting vibrant international educational exchanges.
  
  National Kaohsiung University announced on August 7, 2026, that it would partner with its sister institution, Ho Chi Minh City University of Law in Vietnam, to host the "115th International Legal Leadership Talent Training Camp." The program combines all-English courses with practical field visits to judicial, administrative, and legal service institutions, allowing faculty and students from both universities to experience Taiwan's rule-of-law environment firsthand and gain a deeper understanding of how Taiwan's legal system operates.
  
  Wu Hsing-hao, Vice President for Administration at Kaohsiung University, delivered the opening lecture on "An Overview of Taiwanese Law," presenting Taiwan's legal system and judicial institutions in English. He noted that as globalization advances and cross-border legal issues grow increasingly complex, Kaohsiung University continues to deepen its international legal education by collaborating with overseas sister institutions, integrating theoretical coursework with practical exchanges to cultivate legal professionals equipped with professional expertise, global perspectives, and cross-cultural communication skills.
  
  Meanwhile, a delegation of state legislative leaders from the southeastern United States visited Taiwan. Lawmakers at the leadership level from the state senates and houses of representatives of Alabama, Georgia, Kentucky, North Carolina, South Carolina, and Tennessee came to Taiwan, where Wu Chih-chung, Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs, hosted a welcoming dinner. Wu stated that Taiwan and the United States share deep historical, educational, and cultural ties, particularly noting that US aid following World War II helped Taiwan and other partners develop, demonstrating the strong friendship between the two sides. The two sides exchanged views on Taiwan's democratic progress, international participation, regional developments, and economic, trade, educational cooperation, and high-tech industry development between Taiwan and various US states. Wu expressed hope and welcomed more US state governments establishing offices in Taiwan to continuously expand the mutually beneficial partnership between both sides.
  
  Additionally, Keelung Nuan-nan Senior High School applied on behalf of Keelung City to the Ministry of Education's Youth Development Administration's "Youth Billion-Dollar Overseas Dream Fund Program — Soaring Overseas Track," with its project titled "Mirroring a Century, Creating the Future: Japan Kinki Educational Study Tour." The application was selected, and a 14-day overseas study tour was conducted from July 30 to August 12, aimed at broadening youth international perspectives and deeply connecting international education with Keelung's urban development issues.
  
  The Ministry of Education also hosted the "2026 Digital Learning International Forum" at the Kaohsiung Exhibition Center on August 6–7, 2026, under the theme "New Horizons in Digital Learning through Human-Machine Collaboration." Experts and scholars from Singapore, South Korea, Estonia, Finland, the United Kingdom, Hong Kong, the Netherlands, and Japan were invited to exchange ideas with Taiwan's industry, government, and academic sectors. Liu Kuo-wei, Deputy Minister of Education, stated that through the Talent Ark Program, the ministry aims to use self-developed educational AI to build four key competencies among teachers and students, including the ability to learn with AI, the responsibility to use AI ethically, and the ability to solve problems using AI.
  
  Taken together, these multiple international educational exchange activities demonstrate that Taiwan's educational cooperation with the United States, Vietnam, Japan, and other countries continues to deepen across multiple levels, including higher education, secondary education, and digital learning. US state legislative leaders' visits to Taiwan focused on economic, trade, and educational cooperation; Taiwan-Vietnam legal education cooperation emphasizes cultivating cross-border legal talent; Keelung High School's educational trip to Japan broadens youth international perspectives; and the Digital Learning International Forum focuses on educational transformation in the AI era. These activities complement one another, showcasing the diverse landscape of Taiwan's international educational exchanges.

# 多國教育交流熱絡 臺美臺越臺日教育合作並進

> 教育部青發署金援基隆高中赴日見學、高雄大學與越南法律大學合辦培訓營，另美州議會領袖訪臺探討教育合作，國際教育交流熱絡。

國立高雄大學於2026年8月7日宣佈，攜手姊妹校越南胡志明市法律大學舉辦「115年度國際法律領導人才培訓營」，結合全英文課程與司法、行政及法律服務等實務參訪，讓兩校師生走進臺灣法治現場，深入瞭解臺灣法治運作。高雄大學行政副校長吳行浩於開幕講授「臺灣法概說」，以英文介紹我國法律體系及司法制度，並表示面對全球化發展與跨境法律議題日益增加，高雄大學持續深化國際法學教育，透過與海外姊妹校合作，結合理論課程與實務交流，培養兼具專業素養、國際視野及跨文化溝通能力的法律人才。
另一方面，美國東南地區州議會領袖訪問團來訪，來自阿拉巴馬州、喬治亞州、肯塔基州、北卡羅萊納州、南卡羅萊納州、田納西州等州的州參、眾議會領導級議員訪臺，由外交部政務次長吳志中設宴款待。吳志中表示，臺灣與美國在歷史、教育及文化上都有深刻連結，尤以二次大戰後美國提供援助協助臺灣等夥伴發展，彰顯雙方深厚情誼；雙方就臺灣民主程式、國際參與、區域情勢，以及臺灣與美國各州的經貿、教育合作跟高科技產業發展等議題交換意見。吳志中並期待並歡迎更多美國州政府來臺設處，持續擴大雙方互利共好的夥伴關係。
此外，基隆市立暖暖高階中學代表基隆市申請教育部青年發展署「青年百億海外圓夢基金計畫—海外翱翔組」，以「映象百年‧創生未來：日本近畿見學計畫」獲選，並於7月30日至8月12日展開為期14天的海外見學行程，旨在拓展青年國際視野，並將國際教育與基隆城市發展議題深度連結。教育部亦於2026年8月6日至7日在高雄展覽館主辦「2026數位學習國際論壇」，以「人機協作下數位學習新視野」為主題，邀請新加坡、韓國、愛沙尼亞、芬蘭、英國、香港、荷蘭及日本等國專家學者與國內產官學交流；教育部政務次長劉國偉表示，希望藉由人才方舟計畫，用自建構的教育型AI，建立師生四個能力，包括用AI學習的能力、有責任使用AI的能力、用AI解決問題的能力等。
綜合上述多項國際教育交流活動，可見臺灣與美國、越南、日本等國的教育合作持續深化，涵蓋高等教育、中等教育及數位學習等多個層面。美國州議會領袖訪臺聚焦經貿與教育合作，臺越法學教育合作著重跨境法律人才培育，基隆高中赴日見學拓展青年國際視野，數位學習國際論壇則聚焦AI時代的教育轉型，各項活動互為補充，展現臺灣國際教育交流的多元面貌。
