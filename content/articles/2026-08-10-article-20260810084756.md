  # Taiwan Schools Advance International Education Exchange, Focusing on Digital Learning and Legal Talent Development
  
  > Several schools across Taiwan have recently promoted international educational exchanges, including a digital learning international forum, Taiwan-Vietnam legal cooperation, and a high school student study tour to Japan, showcasing diverse developments in international education.
  
  National Kaohsiung University recently partnered with Ho Chi Minh City University of Law in Vietnam to host the "115th International Legal Leadership Talent Training Camp," combining all-English courses with practical visits to judicial, administrative, and legal service institutions. The program enabled faculty and students from both universities to experience Taiwan's rule-of-law environment firsthand, deepening their understanding of Taiwan's legal operations and strengthening Taiwan-Vietnam legal education cooperation to cultivate legal professionals with international perspectives, cross-cultural communication skills, and cross-border practical capabilities.
  
  Wu Hsing-hao, Vice President for Administration at Kaohsiung University, delivered a lecture on "Introduction to Taiwanese Law" at the opening ceremony, presenting Taiwan's legal system and judicial institutions in English. He stated that in the face of globalization and increasingly complex cross-border legal issues, Kaohsiung University continues to deepen international legal education through collaboration with overseas sister universities, combining theoretical coursework with practical exchanges to cultivate legal professionals possessing professional competence, international vision, and cross-cultural communication abilities. This cooperation was reported by Yahoo Entertainment on August 7, 2026.
  
  Meanwhile, the "2026 Digital Learning International Forum," hosted by the Ministry of Education, was held at the Kaohsiung Exhibition Hall on August 5-6. The event focused on the Ministry's Talent Ark Initiative and invited experts and scholars from Singapore, South Korea, Estonia, Finland, the United Kingdom, Hong Kong, the Netherlands, and Japan to exchange experiences with Taiwan's county and city governments, primary and secondary school teachers, and teacher-training universities. Under the theme "New Horizons in Digital Learning through Human-Machine Collaboration," Liu Kuo-wei, Deputy Minister of Education, expressed hope that the Talent Ark Initiative would establish educational AI and cultivate four key competencies among teachers and students: the ability to learn with AI, the responsibility to use AI ethically, the ability to solve problems using AI, and the ability to collaborate with AI. This forum was reported by Yahoo Entertainment on August 6, 2026.

The school launched a 14-day overseas study tour from July 30 to August 12 under the theme "Mirroring a Century, Creating the Future: Japan Kinki Region Study Tour," deeply connecting international education with Keelung's urban development issues and expanding young people's international horizons. This program was reported by Yahoo Entertainment on August 7, 2026.

# 臺灣多所學校推動國際教育交流 聚焦數位學習與法學人才培育

> 臺灣多所學校近期推動國際教育交流，包含數位學習國際論壇、臺越法學合作及高中生日本見學，展現國際教育多元發展。

國立高雄大學近期與越南胡志明市法律大學攜手舉辦「115年度國際法律領導人才培訓營」，結合全英文課程與司法、行政及法律服務等實務參訪，讓兩校師生走進臺灣法治現場，深入瞭解臺灣法治運作，深化臺越法學教育合作，培育兼具國際視野、跨文化溝通與跨境實務能力的法律人才。高雄大學行政副校長吳行浩於開幕時講授「臺灣法概說」，以英文介紹臺灣法律體系及司法制度。他表示，面對全球化發展與跨境法律議題日益增加，高雄大學持續深化國際法學教育，透過與海外姊妹校合作，結合理論課程與實務交流，培養兼具專業素養、國際視野及跨文化溝通能力的法律人才。此項合作由Yahoo Entertainment於2026年8月7日報導。
另一方面，由教育部主辦的「2026數位學習國際論壇」於8月5日至6日在高雄展覽館舉行，聚焦教育部推動的人才方舟計畫，並邀請新加坡、韓國、愛沙尼亞、芬蘭、英國、香港、荷蘭及日本等國專家學者，與臺灣縣市政府、中小學教師及師培大學等進行經驗交流。論壇以「人機協作下數位學習新視野」為主題，教育部政務次長劉國偉表示，希望藉由人才方舟計畫，建立教育型AI，培養師生四大能力，包括用AI學習的能力、有責任使用AI的能力、用AI解決問題的能力，以及與AI協作的能力。此項論壇資訊由Yahoo Entertainment於2026年8月6日報導。
此外，基隆市立暖暖高階中學獲得教育部青年發展署「青年百億海外圓夢基金計畫—海外翱翔組」補助，以「映象百年・創生未來：日本近畿見學計畫」為主題，於7月30日至8月12日展開為期14天的海外見學行程，將國際教育與基隆城市發展議題深度連結，拓展青年國際視野。此項計畫由Yahoo Entertainment於2026年8月7日報導。
綜合各媒體報導，臺灣各級教育機構近期持續拓展國際教育合作，涵蓋高等教育法學交流、數位學習政策對話及中等學校海外見學等多個層面。然而，各報導側重不同面向，尚無單一來源完整涵蓋上述所有活動，後續發展有待進一步觀察與追蹤。
