  # Taiwan-Vietnam Legal Cooperation, High School Overseas Study Tours, and Digital Forum Showcase Diverse Dimensions of International Educational Exchange
  
  > Taiwan has recently promoted a range of international educational exchange activities, covering legal talent training, overseas study tours for high school students, and digital learning forums, expanding young people's global perspectives.
  
  National Kaohsiung University announced on August 7 that it would partner with Ho Chi Minh City University of Law in Vietnam to host the "115th International Legal Leadership Training Camp." The program combines all-English courses with practical visits to judicial, administrative, and legal service institutions, aiming to help faculty and students from both universities gain a deeper understanding of Taiwan's rule of law and deepen legal education cooperation between Taiwan and Vietnam. Vice President for Administration Wu Hsing-hao delivered a lecture on "Overview of the Taiwanese Legal System" at the opening ceremony, introducing Taiwan's legal system and judicial institutions in English, and emphasized that in the face of increasing globalization and cross-border legal issues, the university continues to cultivate legal professionals with expertise, international vision, and cross-cultural communication skills through collaboration with overseas sister institutions, integrating theory and practice. This training camp focuses on nurturing international legal talent, reflecting Taiwan's higher education efforts toward internationalization in the field of legal studies.
  
  Meanwhile, Keelung Nuan-Nan Senior High School received funding from the Ministry of Education's Youth Development Administration under the "Youth Billion-Dollar Overseas Dream Fund Program — Soaring Overseas Track" for its "Mirroring a Century, Creating the Future: Japan Kinki Study Tour Program," a 14-day overseas study tour held from July 30 to August 12. The program, applied for by Nuan-Nan High School on behalf of Keelung City, aims to broaden young people's international horizons and deeply connect international education with Keelung's urban development issues. Through on-site visits and cultural exchanges, students were exposed to the industrial and social dynamics of Japan's Kinki region. This overseas study tour highlights the importance placed on international experience in Taiwan's secondary education, helping students build a global perspective and cross-cultural understanding.
  
  Additionally, the Ministry of Education's "2026 Digital Learning International Forum" was held on August 6–7 at the Kaohsiung Exhibition Hall, themed "New Horizons in Digital Learning under Human-Machine Collaboration," focusing on the promotion of the Talent Ark Program. The forum invited experts and scholars from Singapore, South Korea, Estonia, Finland, the United Kingdom, Hong Kong, the Netherlands, and Japan to exchange experiences with local governments, primary and secondary school teachers, and teacher-training universities across Taiwan. Deputy Minister of Education Liu Kuo-wei stated that through the Talent Ark Program, the ministry hopes to build educational AI and establish four key competencies for teachers and students: the ability to learn with AI, the responsibility to use AI, the ability to solve problems with AI, and the ability to collaborate with AI. This forum underscores Taiwan's international cooperation trends in digital learning and emphasizes the impact of AI technology on the future of education.
  
  Taken together, these three activities reveal that Taiwan's international educational exchange spans multiple dimensions: legal education focuses on cross-border legal talent training, secondary education emphasizes overseas study tours linked to urban development, and the digital learning forum centers on the integration of AI technology and education. Although organized by different institutions, these activities share the common goal of expanding young people's international horizons and promoting cross-cultural communication and professional skill development. Reports from various media indicate that schools and government agencies across Taiwan are actively advancing international educational cooperation to address the challenges of globalization. However, the specific effectiveness and subsequent impact of these activities still require further observation and evaluation.

# 臺越法學合作、高中海外見學與數位論壇 展現國際教育交流多元面向

> 臺灣近期推動多項國際教育交流活動，涵蓋法學人才培訓、高中海外見學及數位學習論壇，拓展青年國際視野。

國立高雄大學於8月7日宣佈與越南胡志明市法律大學攜手舉辦「115年度國際法律領導人才培訓營」，活動結合全英文課程與司法、行政及法律服務等實務參訪，旨在讓兩校師生深入瞭解臺灣法治運作，深化臺越法學教育合作。高雄大學行政副校長吳行浩於開幕式中講授「臺灣法概說」，以英文介紹我國法律體系及司法制度，並強調面對全球化與跨境法律議題增加，該校持續透過與海外姊妹校合作，結合理論與實務，培養兼具專業素養、國際視野及跨文化溝通能力的法律人才。此項培訓營聚焦於培育國際法律人才，反映臺灣高等教育在法學領域的國際化努力。
另一方面，基隆市立暖暖高階中學獲教育部青年發展署「青年百億海外圓夢基金計畫—海外翱翔組」金援，以「映象百年‧創生未來：日本近畿見學計畫」為主題，於7月30日至8月12日展開為期14天的海外見學行程。該計畫由暖暖高中代表基隆市申請，旨在拓展青年國際視野，並將國際教育與基隆城市發展議題深度連結，透過實地參訪與文化交流，讓學生接觸日本近畿地區的產業與社會脈動。此項海外見學活動凸顯臺灣中等教育階段對國際體驗的重視，有助於學生建立全球觀與跨文化理解。
此外，教育部主辦的「2026數位學習國際論壇」於8月6日至7日在高雄展覽館舉行，以「人機協作下數位學習新視野」為主題，聚焦於人才方舟計畫的推動。論壇邀請新加坡、韓國、愛沙尼亞、芬蘭、英國、香港、荷蘭及日本等國專家學者，與國內縣市政府、中小學教師及師培大學進行經驗交流。教育部政務次長劉國偉表示，希望藉由人才方舟計畫，建構教育型AI，建立師生四大能力：用AI學習的能力、有責任使用AI的能力、用AI解決問題的能力，以及與AI協作的能力。此論壇彰顯臺灣在數位學習領域的國際合作趨勢，並強調AI技術對教育未來的影響。
綜合上述三項活動，可觀察到臺灣在教育領域的國際交流呈現多元面向：法學教育著重跨境法律人才培訓，高中教育強調海外見學與城市發展連結，而數位學習論壇則聚焦AI技術與教育整合。這些活動雖由不同機構主辦，但共同目標在於拓展青年國際視野，促進跨文化溝通與專業能力培養。不同媒體報導顯示，臺灣各級學校與政府部門正積極推動國際教育合作，以因應全球化挑戰。然而，各活動之具體成效與後續影響，仍需進一步觀察與評估。
