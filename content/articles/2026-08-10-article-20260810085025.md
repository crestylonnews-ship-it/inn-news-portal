  # Senior Finance Expo Debuts as Financial Holdings Roll Out Elderly Services; Taishin-Singuang Merger Approved
  
  > The third Senior Health Industry Expo opened in Taipei, with Cathay, CITIC, Taishin-Singuang and other financial holdings launching senior-friendly financial services. The Financial Supervisory Commission has approved the merger between Taishin Bank and Singuang Bank, with a tentative merger effective date set for January 1 next year.
  
  Taiwan has entered a super-aged society, and the financial industry is actively pursuing opportunities in senior finance. The third Senior Health Industry Expo opened in Taipei this week, with financial institutions including Cathay Financial Holding, CITIC Financial Holding, and Taishin-Singuang Financial Holding gathering to exhibit integrated one-stop elderly services spanning banking, insurance, and securities. These services assist seniors in planning their assets, preventing financial fraud, and building long-term care ecosystems such as senior housing and wellness villages. This reflects the financial industry's response to Taiwan's shifting demographic structure, aiming to meet clients' diverse needs across different life stages through comprehensive financial services.
  
  Cathay Financial Holding assembled five major subsidiaries under the theme of "5 Moves to Protect the Senior Fortress," launching degenerative medical accounts, the point-based transformation of excess insurance policies, elderly care trusts, property and casualty insurance combined with securities fraud-prevention financial education, and a dual-core investment trust strategy to capture retirement dividend opportunities. CITIC Financial Holding, meanwhile, adopted a "three accounts plus retention" strategy to help the public plan for retirement and asset succession. CITIC Bank showcased AI-powered smart ATMs and counters that use image and behavioral recognition to prevent financial fraud, while Taiwan Life Insurance introduced Health & Wellness Manager 2.0 and Taiwan's first blueprint for a campus wellness village, integrating a senior care ecosystem with digital financial tools. Lin Wei-chun, general manager of Taishin-Singuang Financial Holding, stated that the Financial Supervisory Commission has approved the merger between Taishin Bank and Singuang Bank, with Taishin Bank as the surviving entity. The tentative merger effective date is January 1 next year. He also expressed optimism about Taiwan's economic outlook for the second half of the year, noting that capital markets are expected to remain active and bank lending to continue growing.
  
  While each financial holding's services emphasize different areas, they all focus on the health management, asset succession, and daily fraud-prevention needs of the elderly population. Liu Ming-da, deputy general manager of Cathay Life Insurance, pointed out that Cathay will undertake a major transformation of its excess insurance policies in the second half of the year, expected to shift toward a point-based model. Chuang Hsiu-chu, deputy general manager of Cathay United Bank, stressed that to prevent financial fraud and dementia-related risks among seniors, the bank is heavily promoting elderly care trust services, having already assisted over 46,000 people. CITIC Bank has been awarded the Senior-Friendly Award for two consecutive years for its comprehensive wealth management planning, indicating that the industry's attention to elderly clients has shifted from individual products to integrated financial protection. Banks are also planning to integrate "full-asset health check" services covering real estate, insurance, and other areas, hoping to satisfy clients' diverse needs at different life stages through complete financial services.
  
  Although the Taishin-Singuang Bank merger has received FSC approval, details such as the post-merger organizational structure and business integration timeline remain to be seen. While the financial industry's senior service佈局 is active, actual results and client acceptance will require time to verify. As Taiwan's demographic aging continues to deepen, how financial institutions can balance commercial interests with social responsibility to provide safe and trustworthy senior financial services will be a key challenge ahead. Meanwhile, competition in the senior finance market is expected to intensify, and each institution will need to continuously innovate its service offerings to meet the challenges and opportunities of the mature-age economy blue ocean.

# 高齡金融博覽會登場 金控推樂齡服務 臺新新光合併案確定

> 第三屆高齡健康產業博覽會於臺北登場，國泰、中信、臺新新光等金控推出樂齡金融服務，臺新新光銀合併案已獲金管會同意，合併基準日暫定明年元旦。

臺灣邁入超高齡社會，金融業積極搶攻高齡金融商機。第三屆高齡健康產業博覽會於本週在臺北登場，國泰金控、中信金控、臺新新光金等金融機構集結參展，推出整合銀行、保險、證券的一站式樂齡服務，協助長者規劃資產、防範金融詐騙，並佈局康養住宅與養生村等長期照護生態圈。此舉反映金融業對臺灣人口結構變遷的回應，試圖透過完整金融服務滿足客戶在人生不同階段的各種需求。
國泰金控集結五大子公司，以「5招」老本護城河為主題，推出退化醫療帳戶、外溢保單點數化轉型、安養信託、產險證券防詐理財教育，以及投信雙核心策略搶攻退休除息商機。中信金控則以「三本一留」策略協助民眾規劃退休與資產傳承，中信銀行展示AI智慧ATM與櫃檯，運用影像與行為辨識防範金融詐騙；臺灣人壽推出健康樂活管家2.0及全臺首座校園養生村藍圖，整合康養生態圈與數位理財工具。臺新新光金總經理林維俊表示，金管會已同意臺新銀行與新光銀行合併案，合併後存續主體為臺新銀行，暫定合併基準日為明年1月1日，並展望下半年臺灣經濟發展樂觀，資本市場活絡，金融業放款將持續成長。
各金控服務雖側重不同，但共同聚焦於高齡族羣的健康管理、資產傳承與日常防詐需求。國泰人壽副總劉明達指出，下半年外溢保單將進行重大轉型，預計朝點數化發展；國泰世華銀行副總莊秀珠強調，為防範高齡金融詐騙與失智風險，主力推廣安養信託服務，目前已協助逾4.6萬人。中信銀行憑藉全財富理財規劃連續兩年獲頒高齡友善大獎，顯示業界對高齡客戶的關注已從單一產品轉向整合性金融守護。銀行也規劃整合不動產、保險等「全資產健診」服務，盼透過完整金融服務，滿足客戶在人生不同階段的各種需求。
臺新新光銀合併案雖已獲金管會同意，但合併後組織架構、業務整合時程等細節仍待觀察。金融業高齡服務佈局雖積極，但實際成效與客戶接受度仍需時間驗證。隨著臺灣人口結構高齡化持續加深，金融機構如何兼顧商業利益與社會責任，提供安全、可信賴的樂齡金融服務，將是未來關鍵課題。同時，高齡金融市場的競爭將趨激烈，各機構需持續創新服務內容，以應對熟齡經濟藍海的挑戰與機遇。
