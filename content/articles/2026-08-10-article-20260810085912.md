  # Taiwan-US Education Exchange, Taiwan-Vietnam Legal Cooperation, International Digital Forum Focus on Talent Development
  
  > Taiwan has recently intensified its international education cooperation, including a visit from US state legislative leaders to explore education and trade ties, a law talent training camp co-hosted by National Kaohsiung University and Vietnam, and a digital learning international forum bringing together experts from multiple countries to discuss education transformation in the AI era.
  
  Taiwan has recently promoted international education exchange and talent development across multiple fronts. During a visit by a legislative delegation from the southeastern United States, both sides exchanged views on Taiwan's democratic progress, international participation, regional developments, and economic, trade, education cooperation, and high-tech industry development between Taiwan and US states. Assistant Minister of Foreign Affairs Wu Chih-chung hosted a dinner for legislative leaders from the six states of Alabama, Georgia, Kentucky, North Carolina, South Carolina, and Tennessee. Wu stated that Taiwan and the United States share deep historical, educational, and cultural connections, and expressed hope that more US state governments would establish offices in Taiwan to continue expanding the mutually beneficial partnership between both sides.
  
  At the higher education level, National Kaohsiung University partnered with its sister institution, Ho Chi Minh City University of Law in Vietnam, to recently hold the "115th International Legal Leadership Talent Training Camp." The event combined all-English courses with practical visits to judicial, administrative, and legal service institutions, allowing faculty and students from both universities to experience Taiwan's rule of law firsthand and gain a deeper understanding of its legal operations. Wu Hsing-hao, Vice President for Administration at Kaohsiung University, delivered a lecture on "Introduction to Taiwanese Law" in English at the opening ceremony. He noted that in the face of globalization and increasing cross-border legal issues, Kaohsiung University continues to deepen international legal education by collaborating with overseas sister institutions, combining theoretical coursework with practical exchange to cultivate legal professionals with expertise, international perspective, and cross-cultural communication skills.
  
  At the secondary education level, Keelung Nuan-Nan Senior High School, representing Keelung City, applied for the Ministry of Education Youth Development Administration's "Youth Billion-Dollar Overseas Dream Fund — Soaring Overseas Program" with its "Mirror a Century, Create the Future: Japan Kinki Study Tour" project. The program was selected and ran for 14 days from July 30 to August 12. The initiative aims to broaden youth international perspectives and deeply connect international education with Keelung's urban development issues.
  
  Additionally, the 2026 Digital Learning International Forum, hosted by the Ministry of Education, was held at the Kaohsiung Exhibition Hall on August 6–7 under the theme "New Horizons in Digital Learning through Human-Machine Collaboration." Experts and scholars from Singapore, South Korea, Estonia, Finland, the UK, Hong Kong, the Netherlands, and Japan were invited to exchange ideas with Taiwan's industry, government, and academic sectors. The forum focused on the Ministry's Talent Ark Program. Assistant Minister of Education Liu Kuo-wei stated that the goal is to build four key competencies for teachers and students through homegrown educational AI: the ability to learn with AI, the responsibility to use AI, the ability to solve problems with AI, and the ability to develop skills that AI cannot replace. The forum also attracted participation from county and city governments, primary and secondary school teachers, and teacher-training universities for experience sharing.
  
  Overall, Taiwan's recent international education exchanges span government-level bilateral interactions, cross-border cooperation among universities, overseas study tours for secondary schools, and international digital education forums, demonstrating Taiwan's continued expansion of its佈局 in talent development and education internationalization.

# 臺美教育交流、臺越法學合作、國際數位論壇聚焦人才培育

> 臺灣近期密集推動國際教育合作，包含美國州議會領袖訪臺探討教育經貿、高雄大學與越南合辦法律人才培訓營，以及數位學習國際論壇匯聚多國專家共議AI時代教育轉型。

臺灣近期在多個層面推動國際教育交流與人才培育。美國東南地區州議會領袖訪問團來訪期間，雙方就臺灣民主程式、國際參與、區域情勢，以及臺灣與美國各州的經貿、教育合作與高科技產業發展等議題交換意見。外交部政務次長吳志中設宴款待來自阿拉巴馬州、喬治亞州、肯塔基州、北卡羅萊納州、南卡羅萊納州、田納西州等六州的州參眾議會領導級議員。吳志中表示，臺灣與美國在歷史、教育及文化上都有深刻連結，並期待歡迎更多美國州政府來臺設處，持續擴大雙方互利共好的夥伴關係。
高等教育層面，國立高雄大學攜手姊妹校越南胡志明市法律大學，於近日舉辦「115年度國際法律領導人才培訓營」。該活動結合全英文課程與司法、行政及法律服務等實務參訪，讓兩校師生走進臺灣法治現場，深入瞭解臺灣法治運作。高雄大學行政副校長吳行浩於開幕時講授「臺灣法概說」，以英文介紹我國法律體系及司法制度。他表示，面對全球化發展與跨境法律議題日益增加，高雄大學持續深化國際法學教育，透過與海外姊妹校合作，結合理論課程與實務交流，培養兼具專業素養、國際視野及跨文化溝通能力的法律人才。
中等教育部分，基隆市立暖暖高階中學代表基隆市申請教育部青年發展署「青年百億海外圓夢基金計畫—海外翱翔組」，以「映象百年‧創生未來：日本近畿見學計畫」獲選，並於7月30日至8月12日展開為期14天的海外見學行程。該計畫旨在拓展青年國際視野，並將國際教育與基隆城市發展議題深度連結。
此外，由教育部主辦的2026數位學習國際論壇於8月6日至7日在高雄展覽館舉行，今年以「人機協作下數位學習新視野」為主題，邀請新加坡、韓國、愛沙尼亞、芬蘭、英國、香港、荷蘭及日本等國專家學者，與國內產官學界進行交流。論壇聚焦教育部推動的人才方舟計畫，教育部政務次長劉國偉表示，希望透過自建構的教育型AI，建立師生四個能力，包括用AI學習的能力、有責任使用AI的能力、用AI解決問題的能力，以及發展AI無法取代的能力。論壇亦吸引縣市政府、中小學教師及師培大學等參與，進行經驗交流。
綜合觀察，臺灣近期國際教育交流涵蓋政府層級雙邊互動、大專院校跨境合作、中學海外見學，以及國際性數位教育論壇等多個面向，顯示臺灣在人才培育與教育國際化方面持續擴張佈局。
