  > Taoyuan police hold first stand-up comedy crime prevention event; US court orders Meta to pay NT$30.4 billion for harm to youth mental health; South Korean teens flock to semiconductor vocational high schools; New Taipei Youth Bureau promotes equity education.
  
  Youth issues have recently drawn attention across multiple fronts. On August 8, the Youth Police Team of the Taoyuan City Police Department held its first "Youth Comedy Night" stand-up comedy event, inviting comedians Hua Xu, Cao Xiao Ou, and Ying Ying, and attracting over a hundred student registrations. The event integrates crime prevention into stand-up comedy, encouraging young people to reflect on interpersonal relationships, self-identity, emotional management, and online behavior. Meanwhile, a court in New Mexico recently ordered Meta to pay cumulative fines of $942 million (approximately NT$30.4 billion), ruling that its social media platforms harm the mental health of children and adolescents and failed to adequately prevent risks of sexual exploitation. Additionally, South Korean teens are driving an educational transformation wave, with more students opting out of traditional university entrance exams to enter semiconductor vocational high schools and learn practical professional skills, preparing for career challenges in the AI era. In Taiwan, the New Taipei City Government Youth Bureau partnered with Womany to hold the first session of "Inclusive Generation: Three Practical Sessions to Activate Influence" on July 25, promoting empathy and equity education.
  
  Media reports indicate that the challenges faced by youth are diverse, and societal responses are showing innovative trends. The Taoyuan police team's stand-up comedy event reflects an attempt by law enforcement to conduct crime prevention outreach in a lighthearted format, transforming serious topics into easily digestible content. Yahoo Entertainment reported that the initiative aims to help young people think about related issues from different angles and improve the effectiveness of outreach. The US court's ruling against Meta highlights the growing recognition of social media's impact on adolescent mental health. Techbang's report on the rise of semiconductor vocational high schools in South Korea shows that young people are reassessing educational and career paths in the AI wave, seeking to avoid the risk of degree devaluation. Womany's coverage of the New Taipei Youth Bureau's equity education event emphasizes crossing prejudice through empathy and bringing equity into the workplace and daily life. While these measures address different aspects, they share a common goal of promoting youth mental health and social adaptation. Overall, youth issues are receiving diverse attention from government, the judiciary, and civil society, and future developments are worth tracking. # 青少年議題多元進展：創新宣導、科技監管與技職教育崛起

> 桃園警方首辦脫口秀犯罪預防活動；美國法院判Meta賠304億元危害兒少心理健康；南韓青少年湧入半導體高中；新北青年局推動平權教育。

青少年議題近期在多個面向引發關注。桃園市政府警察局少年警察隊於8月8日首度舉辦「青春Comedy Night」脫口秀活動，邀請喜劇演員華旭、曹小歐及硬硬演出，吸引超過百名學生報名。該活動將犯罪預防融入脫口秀，引導青少年思考人際相處、自我認同、情緒管理及網路使用等議題。同時，美國新墨西哥州法院近日判決Meta支付累計9.42億美元（約新臺幣304億元）罰款，認定其社羣平臺對兒童及青少年心理健康造成危害，未妥善防範性剝削風險。此外，南韓青少年正掀起一股教育轉型浪潮，越來越多學生放棄傳統大學入學考試，轉而進入半導體職業高中學習專業實務技能，以應對AI時代的職涯挑戰。在臺灣，新北市政府青年局與女人迷合作於7月25日舉辦「共融世代：啟動影響力的3場實踐學」首場活動，推動同理心與平權教育。
各媒體報導顯示青少年面臨的挑戰多元，社會應對措施亦呈現創新趨勢。桃警少年隊的脫口秀活動反映警方嘗試以輕鬆形式進行犯罪預防宣導，將嚴肅議題轉化為易於理解的內容。Yahoo Entertainment報導指出，此舉旨在讓青少年從不同角度思考相關議題，提升宣導效果。美國法院對Meta的判決則凸顯社羣平臺對青少年心理健康的影響日益受重視。Techbang報導南韓半導體高中的興起，顯示青少年在AI浪潮下重新評估教育與職涯路徑，避開學歷貶值風險。Womany報導新北青年局的平權教育活動，強調透過同理心跨越偏見，讓平權走進工作與生活。這些措施雖針對不同面向，但共同目標是促進青少年心理健康與社會適應。不同媒體間並無衝突事實，但各報導側重不同層面：Yahoo聚焦臺灣警方創新與美國司法判決，Techbang關注南韓教育趨勢，Womany則記錄臺灣民間平權倡議。
然而，各議題的具體影響與後續發展尚待觀察。Meta判決的罰款金額創下美國州政府向科技巨頭求償的裡程碑，但平臺是否將調整青少年使用政策，仍有待確認。Techbang報導指出，南韓半導體高中的興起顯示青少年在AI時代提早鎖定高薪職涯，但傳統大學學歷的價值是否真的貶值，仍需時間驗證。桃警的脫口秀活動僅為首度舉辦，其對犯罪預防的實際效果尚未有資料支援，Yahoo Entertainment僅提及報名踴躍，未提供後續評估。新北平權教育活動為首場，後續三場實踐學將持續進行，Womany報導未提及具體成效。不同觀點方面，有評論認為社羣平臺監管應更嚴格，也有聲音擔憂過度限制可能影響青少年社交發展；技職教育雖受歡迎，但傳統學術路徑仍被視為主流。整體而言，青少年議題正受到政府、司法與民間社會的多元關注，未來進展值得持續追蹤。此外，這些措施反映社會對青少年心理健康與教育選擇的重視，但如何平衡創新與風險，仍是持續挑戰。
