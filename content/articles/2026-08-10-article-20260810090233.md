  # Financial Sector Races to Capture Elderly Market Opportunities as Cathay, CITIC, Taishin-Shin Kong Step Up Efforts
  
  > As Taiwan enters a super-aged society, financial institutions including Cathay Financial Holding, CITIC Financial Holding, and Taishin-Shin Kong Financial are actively positioning themselves in the mature economy, offering integrated services across insurance, banking, and trusts to meet the elderly population's needs in health management, asset inheritance, and fraud prevention.
  
  In response to Taiwan's transition into a super-aged society, the financial sector is competing to capture elderly financial opportunities. The 3rd Elderly Health Industry Expo recently took place in Taipei, with Cathay Financial Holding, CITIC Financial Holding, and Taishin-Shin Kong Financial gathering their subsidiaries to launch one-stop elderly services covering health management, asset inheritance, and financial fraud prevention. According to CNA reports, the life insurance industry is planning wellness villages and health-care residences, while banks are developing integrated "full asset health check" services combining real estate and insurance, aiming to meet customers' needs across different life stages through comprehensive financial services.
  
  Cathay Financial Holding brought together five subsidiaries at the expo to target the super-aged society market. Liu Mingda, deputy general manager of Cathay Life Insurance, stated that expatriate policies will undergo major transformation in the second half of the year, expected to shift toward a points-based system, while also deepening preventive medicine through "degenerative medical accounts" and expatriate policies. Chuang Hsiu-chu, deputy general manager of Cathay United Bank, highlighted the promotion of "elderly care trusts" to guard against financial fraud and dementia-related risks, having already assisted over 46,000 people in preventing financial fraud and asset loss. Additionally, Cathay General Insurance and Cathay Securities are addressing elderly daily mobility safety and retirement asset allocation respectively, offering insurance gap coverage and fraud-prevention financial education; Cathay Investment Trust is pursuing a "dual-core" strategy, combining popular high-dividend ETFs to capture retirement dividend opportunities.
  
  CITIC Financial Holding, under the theme "Let Love Continue, Inherit the Family's Future," partnered with CITIC Bank and Taiwan Life Insurance at the expo, helping the public plan for retirement and asset inheritance through a "three books plus one retention" strategy. CITIC Bank, recognized with the Elderly-Friendly Award for two consecutive years for its "comprehensive wealth management planning," showcased AI-powered smart ATMs and counters utilizing image and behavioral recognition technology to prevent financial fraud. Taiwan Life Insurance launched "Healthy Living Manager 2.0" and unveiled the blueprint for Taiwan's first campus wellness village, integrating a health and wellness ecosystem with digital wealth management tools. CITIC Financial Holding continues to fulfill its commitment to elderly-friendly services by integrating banking, insurance, and digital technology resources.
  
  Meanwhile, Lin Weijun, general manager of Taishin-Shin Kong Financial, announced that the Financial Supervisory Commission has approved the merger of Taishin Bank and Shin Kong Bank, with Taishin Bank as the surviving entity and a provisional merger effective date of January 1 next year. Lin expressed optimism about Taiwan's economic outlook for the second half of the year, noting active capital markets and continued growth in financial sector lending. The merged Taishin-Shin Kong Financial Group, encompassing banking, insurance, securities, and investment trusts, will further strengthen its service capabilities.
  
  Across the industry, financial institutions are adopting varied strategies to address the super-aged society: Cathay Financial Holding focuses on integrating financial and insurance mechanisms, transforming professional services into interactive experiences; CITIC Financial Holding emphasizes digital technology applications and asset inheritance planning; and Taishin-Shin Kong Financial is expanding through merger to enhance overall competitiveness. Industry observers note that as Taiwan's population structure ages rapidly, elderly financial services will become a key growth driver for the financial sector, making future developments worth watching closely.

# 金融業搶攻高齡商機 國泰中信臺新新光積極佈局

> 臺灣邁入超高齡社會，國泰金控、中信金控、臺新新光金等金融機構積極佈局熟齡經濟，透過保險、銀行、信託等整合服務，滿足高齡族羣健康管理、資產傳承與防詐需求。

因應臺灣邁入超高齡社會，金融業競相搶攻高齡金融商機。第三屆高齡健康產業博覽會日前於臺北登場，國泰金控、中信金控、臺新新光金等機構集結子公司參與，推出涵蓋健康管理、資產傳承、金融防詐等一站式樂齡服務。中央社記者報導指出，壽險業規劃養生村與康養住宅，銀行也規劃整合不動產、保險等「全資產健診」服務，盼透過完整金融服務滿足客戶人生不同階段需求。
國泰金控在本次博覽會集結五大子公司，鎖定超高齡社會商機。國泰人壽副總劉明達表示，下半年外溢保單將進行重大轉型，預計朝點數化方向推動；同時以「退化醫療帳戶」及外溢保單深耕預防醫學。國泰世華銀行副總莊秀珠指出，為防範高齡金融詐騙與失智風險，主力推廣「安養信託」服務，目前已協助逾4.6萬人防範金融詐騙與資產流失。此外，國泰產險與證券分別針對高齡日常移動安全及退休資產配置，提供保險缺口補足與防詐理財教育；國泰投信則以「雙核心」策略，結合熱門高股息ETF搶攻退休除息商機。
中信金控則以「讓愛延續 傳承家的未來」為主軸，攜手中信銀行與臺灣人壽參與博覽會，透過「三本一留」策略協助民眾規劃退休與資產傳承。中信銀行憑藉「全財富理財規劃」連續兩年獲頒高齡友善大獎，並展示AI智慧ATM與櫃檯，運用影像與行為辨識技術防範金融詐騙。臺灣人壽推出「健康樂活管家2.0」及全臺首座校園養生村藍圖，整合康養生態圈與數位理財工具。中信金控整合銀行、保險與數位科技資源，持續落實對高齡客戶的友善承諾。
另一方面，臺新新光金總經理林維俊表示，金管會已同意臺新銀行與新光銀行合併案，合併後存續主體為臺新銀行，暫定合併基準日為明年1月1日。林維俊展望下半年臺灣經濟發展非常樂觀，資本市場相當活絡，金融業放款將持續成長。臺新新光金為涵蓋銀行、保險、證券、投信的全方位金融集團，合併後將進一步強化服務能量。
綜合各媒體報導，金融業因應超高齡社會的佈局策略各有側重：國泰金控著重金融與保險機制的整合，將專業服務轉化為互動體驗；中信金控強調數位科技應用與資產傳承規劃；臺新新光金則透過合併擴大規模，提升整體競爭力。業界觀察指出，隨著臺灣人口結構快速老化，高齡金融服務將成為金融業重要成長動能，後續發展值得持續關注。
