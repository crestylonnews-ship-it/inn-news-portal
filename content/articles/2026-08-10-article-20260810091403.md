  # Cross-Domain Exploration of Human Resources Challenges and Policy Responses in the AI Era
  
  > Scholar Cai Fang advocates guiding technological development with an employment-first strategy; Hong Kong's Labour and Welfare Bureau released its 2027 Human Resources Projection Report; Taiwan participated in the APEC Human Resources Development Working Group meeting, with multiple parties exploring ways for the labor market to adapt.
  
  Cai Fang, a member of the Academic Division of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences, recently published an article titled "Population Dimensions: Mitigating Labor Market Challenges in the AI Era" on the Ta Kung Wen Hui Network, pointing out that addressing the employment impact of technological change and resolving structural employment contradictions share consistency in both direction and means. Cai Fang proposed multiple policy recommendations, including aligning the direction of technological development with an employment-first strategy, reshaping human capital cultivation models, improving the labor market and public employment services, and strengthening the social security system, with the aim of balancing economic development and employment stability in the AI era. The report was published by Ta Kung Wen Hui Network on April 24, 2026, and is currently the publicly available academic source.
  
  Meanwhile, Hong Kong's Labour and Welfare Bureau published the "2027 Human Resources Projection Report" on its official website. The report covers multiple dimensions, including human resource demand by economic sector, demand in pillar and selected industries, demand by occupational group, and demand by education level, and further projects the supply-demand gap in human resources. This report, released through official Hong Kong government channels, is of a policy research nature and serves as a reference for the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region government in human resource planning. The report is available as a public PDF file for download and review.
  
  On the regional cooperation front, Taiwan's Ministry of Labor website indicates Taiwan's participation in the 49th Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Human Resources Development Working Group (HRDWG) meetings, as documented in the "Dynamic Outlook" section of Issue 79 (Autumn Edition) of the Taiwan Labor Quarterly, authored by Gao Shaoying. The APEC Human Resources Development Working Group aims to promote human resources development cooperation among member economies. Taiwan's continued participation in this mechanism reflects the regional emphasis on human capital investment and labor market development.
  
  Additionally, the NABI platform under 104 Human Resources Bank previously published an article on "Strategic Human Resources Development," citing references from the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD), indicating that private employment platforms are also paying attention to strategic human resources development issues and incorporating international organizational perspectives as a basis for discussion. The Yahoo Merchant Directory also lists the service offerings of Jisheng Human Resources, covering labor dispatch and warehouse management services in Kaohsiung, Tainan, Taichung, Changhua, Yunlin, Chiayi, Miaoli, and Pingtung, reflecting the operational status of private labor dispatch companies in the regional labor market.
  
  Synthesizing the above sources, Cai Fang's academic perspective focuses on national-level policy design, Hong Kong's Labour and Welfare Bureau projection report emphasizes regional human resource supply and demand forecasting, Taiwan's Ministry of Labor participation in the APEC mechanism reflects regional cooperation dimensions, and information from private employment platforms and labor dispatch companies reflects actual market operations. Although these four sources differ in level and perspective, they all point to the same core issue: under the dual pressures of rapid technological change and demographic shifts, how to address future labor market challenges through the synergy of policy, education, and market mechanisms. Currently, no direct cross-citation or consensus conclusions have been found among the sources, and the effects of related policies and their actual impact remain to be observed and verified with data.

# 跨域探討AI時代人力資源挑戰與政策對策

> 學者蔡昉主張以就業優先戰略引導技術發展，香港勞工及福利局發布2027年人力資源推算報告，臺灣參與APEC人力資源發展工作小組會議，多方探討勞動市場因應之道。

中國社會科學院學部委員蔡昉近日於《大公文匯網》發表〈人口經緯／緩解AI時代的勞動市場挑戰〉文章，指出應對技術變革的就業影響與破解結構性就業矛盾，在方向與手段上皆具一致性。蔡昉提出多項政策建議，包括引導技術發展方向對標就業優先戰略、重塑人力資本培養模式、完善勞動力市場和公共就業服務，以及健全社會保障體系等，以期在AI時代兼顧經濟發展與就業穩定。該報導由大公文匯網於2026年4月24日刊出，為目前公開可查之學術觀點來源。
與此同時，香港勞工及福利局於官網發布《2027年人力資源推算報告》，報告內容涵蓋按經濟行業劃分的人力需求、支柱產業與選定產業的人力需求、按職業組別劃分的人力需求，以及按教育程度劃分的人力需求等多個維度，並進一步推算人力資源供求差額。此報告由香港政府官方渠道發布，屬政策研究性質，提供香港特區政府進行人力規劃之參考依據。報告連結為公開PDF檔案，供公眾下載查閱。
在區域合作層面，臺灣勞工部官網顯示，臺灣參與第49屆亞太經濟合作組織（APEC）人力資源發展工作小組（HRDWG）相關會議情形，由高韶英撰述之《臺灣勞工季刊》第79期秋季號動態瞭望欄目刊載。APEC人力資源發展工作小組旨在促進成員經濟體之間的人力資源發展合作，臺灣持續參與此機制，反映區域層面對人力資本投資與勞動市場發展之重視。
此外，104人力銀行旗下NABI平臺曾發布〈策略性人力資源發展〉相關文章，並引用經濟合作與發展組織（OECD）之參考文獻，顯示民間就業平臺亦關注策略性人力資源發展議題，並引入國際組織觀點作為討論基礎。Yahoo商家指南則收錄吉陞人力資源之服務專案，涵蓋高雄、臺南、臺中、彰化、雲林、嘉義、苗慄、屏東等地的人力派遣與倉儲管製服務，反映民間人力派遣業者在區域勞動市場之營運現況。
綜合上述來源，蔡昉之學術觀點聚焦於國家層級之政策設計，香港勞工及福利局之推算報告著重於地區性人力供需預測，臺灣勞工部參與APEC機制則體現區域合作面向，而民間就業平臺與人力派遣業者之資訊則反映市場端之實際運作。四者雖層級與視角不同，但皆指向同一核心議題：在技術快速變遷與人口結構轉變之雙重壓力下，如何透過政策、教育與市場機制之協同，應對未來勞動市場之挑戰。目前各來源之間尚未見直接交叉引用或共識性結論，相關政策效果與實際影響仍有待後續觀察與資料驗證。
