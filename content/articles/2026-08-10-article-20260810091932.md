  # Software Development Process Analysis: A Multi-Perspective Exploration from Requirements to Deployment and Maintenance
  
  > This article synthesizes reports from Wikipedia, monday.com, cloud-interactive, and other media outlets to analyze the core processes, methodologies, and key stages of software development, emphasizing the importance of requirements clarification and the applicable scenarios of different development models.
  
  According to Wikipedia, software development is a product development process that builds software systems or software components within systems based on user requirements, encompassing systems engineering activities such as requirements gathering, development planning, requirements analysis, design, programming implementation, software testing, and version control. monday.com further categorizes the process into five major stages: requirements inventory and analysis, system design, development, testing, and deployment and maintenance. It notes that the first step requires conducting interviews with internal departments and stakeholders to clarify actual pain points and desired features, which are then consolidated by product managers into detailed requirements specifications. Both sources confirm that software development must proceed through structured phases to ensure the final product meets user needs.
  
  Different media outlets vary slightly in how they divide the process stages. insightdigit emphasizes that "requirements and process clarification before construction begins" is the most frequently overlooked critical stage, proposing a six-stage standard process covering requirements clarification, process mapping, technology selection, and go-live maintenance. projectmanager, from a product development perspective, lists six steps: market research, product conceptualization, product design and development, testing and validation, market promotion, and post-sales evaluation, recommending the use of tools such as monday dev, ClickUp, and Miro to improve success rates. ares briefly states that the software development process covers requirements analysis, system design, development, testing, deployment, and maintenance, and mentions that SI (System Integration) services can assist enterprises in cross-system architecture integration. These perspectives show that while stage names and quantities vary by viewpoint, the core revolves around the complete lifecycle from requirements to maintenance.
  
  Regarding development methodologies, cloud-interactive provides a detailed comparison between waterfall and agile software development. The waterfall model is a linear process that executes sequentially from product analysis, design, development, testing, to maintenance, requiring completion of each phase before proceeding to the next, making it suitable for projects with clear and stable requirements. Agile development emphasizes iteration and flexibility, allowing requirements to be adjusted during the development process. insightdigit also provides a comparison of waterfall, agile, and iterative methodologies, noting that enterprises should select the appropriate model based on project characteristics. Different sources collectively confirm that methodology selection affects development efficiency and product adaptability, though no definitive conclusion identifies one as universally superior.
  
  In practical application, the sources highlight that tools and team roles are key to success. projectmanager recommends strengthening risk management and R&D innovation through product development team role composition and new product planning document templates. ares emphasizes that SI system integration development services can accelerate product delivery and digital transformation, helping enterprises efficiently integrate cross-system architectures. insightdigit reminds readers that the decision between in-house development and outsourcing requires comprehensive evaluation of enterprise resources and technical capabilities. These perspectives collectively indicate that software development is not merely a technical activity but also involves organizational collaboration and resource allocation.
  
  Synthesizing the various media reports, the rigor of the software development process directly impacts product quality and time-to-market, with insufficient requirements clarification often cited as the primary pitfall. Future observations may focus on the growing adoption of agile methods in environments with changing requirements, as well as the potential impact of AI-assisted development tools on process efficiency. Enterprises should thoroughly clarify requirements and select appropriate methodologies and tools before initiating projects, in order to reduce risks and improve success rates.

# 軟體開發流程解析：從需求分析到部署維運的多觀點探討

> 本文綜合維基百科、monday.com、cloud-interactive 等多家媒體報導，解析軟體開發的核心流程、方法論與關鍵階段，強調需求梳理的重要性與不同開發模式的適用情境。

根據維基百科定義，軟體開發是根據使用者要求建造軟體系統或系統中軟體部分的產品開發過程，涵蓋需求取得、開發規劃、需求分析、設計、程式設計實現、軟體測試與版本控制等系統工程活動。monday.com 進一步將流程歸納為五大階段：需求盤點與分析、系統設計、開發、測試、部署與維運，並指出第一步需透過與內部部門及利害關係人訪談，釐清實際痛點與期望功能，由產品經理統整為詳細需求規格。這兩家來源共同確認軟體開發必須經過結構化階段，以確保最終產品符合使用者需求。
不同媒體對流程階段的劃分略有差異。insightdigit 強調「動工前的需求與流程梳理」是最常被忽略的關鍵階段，並提出六階段標準流程，涵蓋需求釐清、流程梳理、技術選型到上線維運。projectmanager 則從產品開發角度，列出六步驟：市場研究、產品概念化、產品設計開發、測試驗證、市場推廣與銷售後續評估，並建議搭配 monday dev、ClickUp、Miro 等工具以提升成功率。ares 簡要指出軟體開發流程涵蓋需求分析、系統設計、開發、測試、部署與維運，並提及 SI 系統整合服務可協助企業跨系統架構整合。這些說法顯示階段名稱與數量因觀點而異，但核心皆圍繞從需求到維運的完整生命週期。
在開發方法論方面，cloud-interactive 詳細比較瀑布式與敏捷軟體開發。瀑布式模型為線性流程，從產品分析、設計、開發、測試到維運按順序執行，必須完成前一階段才能進入下一階段，適合需求明確且變動少的專案。敏捷開發則強調迭代與靈活性，允許在開發過程中調整需求。insightdigit 亦提供瀑布式、敏捷式與迭代式的方法論比較，並指出企業需根據專案特性選擇適配模式。不同來源共同確認方法論的選擇會影響開發效率與產品適應性，但尚未有定論指出何者為絕對最佳。
實務應用上，各來源提及工具與團隊角色為成功關鍵。projectmanager 建議透過產品開發團隊角色構成與新產品企劃書範例，強化風險管理與研發創新力。ares 強調 SI 系統整合開發服務能加速產品交付與數位轉型，並協助企業高效整合跨系統架構。insightdigit 則提醒自建與委外決策需綜合評估企業資源與技術能力。這些觀點共同指向軟體開發不僅是技術活動，更涉及組織協作與資源配置。
綜合各媒體報導，軟體開發流程的嚴謹性直接影響產品品質與上市時間，而需求梳理不足常被視為主要踩雷原因。後續觀察可關注敏捷方法在變動需求環境中的普及程度，以及 AI 輔助開發工具對流程效率的潛在影響。企業在啟動專案前，應充分釐清需求並選擇適配的方法論與工具，以降低風險並提升成功率。
