  # Cybersecurity Governance Takes Center Stage: Ruyang, Trend Micro, and Taichung Digital Affairs Bureau Address AI Challenges
  
  > Ruyang Information, Trend Micro, and the Taichung City Government each presented perspectives on enterprise digital resilience, AI misuse risks, and healthcare cybersecurity governance, highlighting that cybersecurity governance has become a cross-disciplinary core issue in the AI era.
  
  In the face of rapid AI technology adoption, cybersecurity and digital governance have escalated from technical concerns to central issues in enterprise governance and public policy. Ma Zhengwei, CEO of Ruyang Information's Digital Resilience Office, stated at the "Path to Digital Resilience" symposium that digital resilience is no longer solely the responsibility of IT departments, but a critical core of enterprise governance and operational strategy. This view reflects the need for enterprises to integrate cybersecurity into their overall governance framework when confronting the high uncertainty brought by AI and digitalization.
  
  Trend Micro's blog addressed a real-world case involving AI watermarks in a fraud incident. The report described beggars on the streets who used AI-generated content but were exposed because they "forgot to remove the AI watermark," revealing that fraud groups are also undergoing digital transformation. Trend Micro categorized this case under industrial cybersecurity and security education, using themes like "Don't be that guy" to warn the public about AI misuse risks. This case illustrates the specific forms of malicious AI exploitation and underscores the need for cybersecurity education to adapt to new technologies.
  
  On the public sector front, the Taichung City Government's Digital Development Bureau is actively promoting smart healthcare cybersecurity governance. Lin Gulong, Director of Taichung's Digital Development Bureau, invited 14 major hospitals in the central region to participate in AI cybersecurity governance activities, emphasizing that cybersecurity is not only the foundation of digital governance but also a core guarantee of city competitiveness. This move demonstrates local governments incorporating cybersecurity governance into smart city development strategies and strengthening protection for healthcare, a critical infrastructure domain.
  
  While the three organizations' focuses differ, they collectively point to one trend: the proliferation of AI technology is reshaping the paradigm of cybersecurity governance. Ruyang Information emphasizes digital resilience at the enterprise governance level, Trend Micro focuses on practical risks of AI misuse and public education, and the Taichung City Government concentrates on cybersecurity governance in the healthcare sector. This多元 perspective reflects that cybersecurity issues have transcended technology, enterprise governance, and public policy domains.
  
  Notably, none of the sources provided specific policy implementation timelines or quantified effectiveness data. Ruyang Information's symposium content lacked specific dates, Trend Micro's case report did not specify when the incident occurred, and Taichung City Government's activity information similarly lacked clear time markers. These missing details mean the specific impact scope and implementation progress of each party's claims remain to be further confirmed.
  
  Overall, cybersecurity governance in the AI era requires joint participation from enterprises, technology vendors, and government agencies. Enterprises must integrate digital resilience into their governance structures, technology vendors should continuously monitor emerging threats and provide educational resources, and governments need to formulate corresponding policies and promote cybersecurity protection for critical infrastructure. This multi-layered governance architecture may be an effective approach to addressing the uncertainties brought by AI.

# 資安治理成焦點 叡揚、趨勢、臺中數位局關注AI挑戰

> 叡揚資訊、趨勢科技與臺中市政府分別就企業數位韌性、AI濫用風險與醫療資安治理提出觀點，凸顯AI時代資安治理成為跨領域核心議題。

面對人工智慧（AI）技術快速普及，資安與數位治理已從技術層面升級為企業治理與公共政策的核心課題。叡揚資訊數位韌性辦公室執行長馬正維於「邁向數位韌性之路」研討會中指出，數位韌性不再僅是資訊技術（IT）部門的職責，而是企業治理與營運策略的重要核心。此觀點反映企業面對AI與數位化帶來的高度不確定性時，必須將資安納入整體治理框架。
趨勢科技部落格則從實際案例切入，報導一起涉及AI浮水印的詐騙事件。該報導提及，街頭出現使用AI生成內容的乞丐，但因「忘記刪掉AI浮水印」而暴露手法，凸顯詐騙集團也開始進行數位轉型。趨勢科技將此案例歸類於工業資安與資安教育範疇，並透過「Don't be that guy」等主題提醒公眾注意AI濫用風險。此案例顯示AI技術被惡意利用的具體形式，也反映資安教育需因應新技術發展而調整。
在公共部門方面，臺中市政府數位發展局積極推動智慧醫療資安治理。臺中市數位發展局長林谷隆邀請中部14間大醫院參與AI資安治理相關活動，強調資安不僅是數位治理的基石，更是城市競爭力的核心保障。此舉顯示地方政府正將資安治理納入智慧城市發展策略，並針對醫療這一關鍵基礎設施領域加強防護。
三家機構的關注點雖有不同，但共同指向一個趨勢：AI技術的普及正在重塑資安治理的正規化。叡揚資訊強調企業治理層面的數位韌性，趨勢科技關注AI濫用的實際風險與公眾教育，臺中市政府則聚焦醫療場域的資安治理。這種多元視角反映資安議題已跨越技術、企業治理與公共政策等多個領域。
值得注意的是，目前各來源均未提供具體的政策實施時間表或量化成效資料。叡揚資訊的研討會內容未標示具體日期，趨勢科技的案例報導亦未說明事件發生時間，臺中市政府的活動資訊同樣缺乏明確時間標記。這些細節的缺失使得各方主張的具體影響範圍與實施進度尚待進一步確認。
從整體來看，AI時代的資安治理需要企業、技術廠商與政府部門的共同參與。企業需將數位韌性納入治理結構，技術廠商應持續監測新型態威脅並提供教育資源，政府則需制定相應政策並推動關鍵基礎設施的資安防護。這種多層次的治理架構可能是應對AI帶來之不確定性的有效途徑。
