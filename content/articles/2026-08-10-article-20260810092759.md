  # International Education 2.0 in Progress: Current Status of Curriculum Design and Global Competency Development in Taiwanese Schools
  
  > Multiple schools and educational institutions across Taiwan continue to advance International Education 2.0, emphasizing global citizenship literacy and cross-cultural communication skills, while integrating three key dimensions—cultural learning, global issues, and international connections—into curriculum design.
  
  According to a statement released by Kang Chiao International School (KCIS) in November 2024, the core philosophy of international education lies in cultivating students' global perspective and cross-cultural understanding, with three primary objectives: nurturing talent with international competitiveness, promoting cross-cultural communication abilities, and establishing a sense of global citizenship. The school noted that international education extends beyond language learning, encompassing multi-layered design including issue-based inquiry, curriculum integration, and practical application, with corresponding educational content tailored to elementary, junior high, and senior high school levels.
  
  A resource handbook published by the Ministry of Education's Global Information Network for International Education in Primary and Secondary Schools further outlines three dimensions—"cultural learning," "global issues," and "international connections"—from which several curriculum plans have been developed. The handbook indicates that these curriculum plans can serve as in-depth exploration within a single subject or as cross-disciplinary broad discussions, and recommends that teachers align them with the substantive content of international education topics under the 108 Curriculum Framework. This resource handbook provides teachers with a concrete framework for curriculum design, helping to integrate international education topics into daily instruction.
  
  A December 2022 report by Flipped Education noted that with the advancement of International Education 2.0, many schools have begun implementing international education courses, aiming to broaden students' horizons. The report cited the International Education White Paper, which states that one of the important goals of international education is to cultivate global citizens and develop students' abilities in inquiry and cross-cultural communication. The report also mentioned that teachers can integrate various topics into international education courses, though specific topics and teaching materials should be determined based on each school's actual circumstances.

Commonwealth Magazine's compilation shows that international education has become one of the important thematic areas in Taiwan's 12-Year Basic Education curriculum development, with related in-depth articles continuously updated.

# 國際教育2.0推動中：臺灣學校課程設計與全球素養培育現況

> 臺灣多所學校與教育機構持續推動國際教育2.0，強調全球公民素養、跨文化溝通能力，並將文化學習、全球議題、國際關聯三大面向融入課程設計。

根據康橋雙語學校（KCIS）於2024年11月發布的說明，國際教育的核心理念在於培養學生的全球視野與跨文化理解能力，並提出三大推行目標：培育具備國際競爭力的人才、促進跨文化溝通能力，以及建立全球公民意識。該校指出，國際教育並非僅限於語言學習，而是涵蓋議題探究、課程整合與實作應用等多層面設計，並針對國小、國中、高中不同階段提供相應的教育內涵說明。
教育部中小學國際教育全球資訊網所發布的資源手冊，則進一步規劃「文化學習」、「全球議題」以及「國際關聯」三大面向，並以此發展出數篇課程方案。手冊內容顯示，這些課程方案可為單一學科的深化探究，也可為跨科整合的廣泛討論，並建議教師自行參考108課綱國際教育議題實質內涵進行對應。此資源手冊提供學校教師具體的課程設計參考框架，有助於將國際教育議題融入日常教學。
翻轉教育於2022年12月報導指出，隨著國際教育2.0的推動，許多學校開始推行國際教育課程，希望帶給學生不一樣的視野。報導援引國際教育白皮書內容說明，國際教育的重要目標之一是培育全球公民，並培養學生探究、跨文化溝通等能力。報導同時提及，教師可將多項議題融入國際教育課程，但具體議題內容與教材教案需視學校實際情況而定。
天下雜誌則在其教育議題資料庫中，將國際教育列為十二年國教19項議題融入課程之一，與性別平等、人權、環境、海洋、品德、生命、法治、科技、資訊、能源、安全、防災、家庭教育、生涯規畫、多元文化、閱讀素養、戶外教育、原住民族教育並列。天下雜誌的整理顯示，國際教育已成為臺灣十二年國教課程發展的重要議題面向之一，相關深度文章持續更新。
綜合各來源資訊，臺灣目前推動國際教育的方向主要集中在課程設計與素養培育兩個層面。康橋雙語學校提供具體的學校層級實施說明，教育部資源手冊提供課程框架參考，翻轉教育則聚焦於教師實務面的議題融入。然而，各來源對於國際教育的具體成效、學生學習成果評估，以及不同學校之間的實施差異，並未提供詳細資料或比較分析，此部分尚待進一步觀察與確認。
