  # Taiwan's Biotech and Medical Industry Driven by Policy and Global Trends
  
  > The Executive Yuan is advancing the Health Taiwan Deep Cultivation Plan, investing NT$48.9 billion over five years to integrate AI and cross-ministerial resources to promote smart biotech. Meanwhile, the global biotech and medical M&A market is focusing on AI integration and high-quality therapies, presenting both opportunities and challenges for Taiwan's industry.
  
  The Executive Yuan has designated the biomedical industry as one of 13 national strategic industries under the "Health Taiwan Deep Cultivation Plan," allocating a five-year budget of NT$48.9 billion. Through cross-ministerial coordination involving the Ministry of Health and Welfare, the National Development Council, the National Science and Technology Council, and the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the plan aims to optimize the healthcare environment and leverage AI to advance smart biotech, as reported by Yahoo News. The initiative seeks to strengthen Taiwan's competitiveness in biotech and healthcare through integrated cross-ministerial resources while addressing medical demands arising from an aging society.
  
  Yang Pan-chi, president of the National Biomedical Industry Strategic Council (NBISC), noted that Taiwan's average life expectancy is approximately 81 years, yet unhealthy years account for roughly eight of those. Facing the dual pressures of declining birthrates and a super-aged society, he urged turning this crisis into business opportunities within a "longevity new economy." Yang emphasized that cross-sector collaboration among industry, government, and academia can extend healthy lifespan, transforming elderly populations from care recipients into contributors with consumption and productive capacity, as reported by Commercial Times. In recent years, NBISC has focused on connecting cross-domain industry collaborations, advancing major biomedical policies and key regulations, national quality verification and industrial upgrading, innovative R&D and startup incubation, and international trade and cooperation strategies.
  
  From a global perspective, PwC's 2026 Global Biotech and Healthcare M&A Outlook report indicates that the global biotech and medical M&A market in 2026 will focus on high-quality therapies, AI integration, and efficiency improvements. The report suggests that facing regulatory challenges, pharmaceutical, medical device, and health service companies need to flexibly deploy capital and strengthen compliance to achieve precise and efficient growth. This trend aligns with Taiwan's policy direction of combining AI with smart biotech, indicating that Taiwan's biotech industry stands at the intersection of global trends and local policy.
  
  On the technology application front, IEK Industry Intelligence Network reported that with the rapid development of digital healthcare, related technologies are not only widely applied in clinical practice but are also becoming important tools for promoting international medical cooperation. Telemedicine, AI-powered imaging analysis, and 3D digital simulation technologies not only enhance the quality of care but also enable cross-border medical teams to transcend geographical limitations and collaboratively participate in the diagnosis and treatment of complex cases. This development is consistent with the Executive Yuan's Health Taiwan Deep Cultivation Plan direction of combining AI to promote smart biotech, demonstrating Taiwan's active push for clinical application and international cooperation in digital healthcare technologies.
  
  Synthesizing various sources, Taiwan's biotech and medical industry is currently driven by both policy initiatives and global trends. The Executive Yuan's Health Taiwan Deep Cultivation Plan provides a resource foundation, NBISC promotes cross-sector collaboration among industry, government, and academia, and the global M&A market's focus on AI integration and high-quality therapies also presents opportunities for international cooperation and expansion for Taiwan's biotech industry. However, regulatory challenges, the effectiveness of cross-ministerial coordination, and the issue of unhealthy years in an aging society remain matters for ongoing observation and further implementation.

# 臺灣生技醫療產業迎政策與全球趨勢雙輪驅動

> 行政院推動健康臺灣深耕計畫，五年投入489億元，結合AI與跨部會資源推動智慧生技；全球生技醫療併購市場亦聚焦AI整合與高品質療法，臺灣產業面臨機遇與挑戰。

行政院推動「健康臺灣深耕計畫」，將生醫產業列為13項國家戰略產業之一，並編列五年489億元預算，透過衛福部、國發基金、國科會及經濟部跨部會整合，最佳化醫療環境並結合AI力推智慧生技，此資訊由Yahoo新聞報導提及。該計畫旨在透過跨部會資源整合，強化臺灣在生技醫療領域的競爭力，同時回應高齡化社會帶來的醫療需求。
國家生技醫療產業策進會（簡稱生策會）會長楊泮池指出，臺灣平均壽命約81歲，但不健康餘命卻長達約8年，面對少子化與超高齡雙重壓力，應把危機轉化為「長壽新經濟」商機。楊泮池強調，透過產官學跨域合作延長健康壽命，讓高齡人口從被照顧者轉為消費力與生產力，此觀點由工商時報報導。生策會近年亦聚焦連結跨領域產業合作，推動國內重大生醫政策與關鍵法規、國家品質驗證與產業升級、創新研發與創業育成，以及國際商貿市場與合作佈局。
全球視角方面，普華永道（PwC）發布2026年全球生技醫療產業併購展望報告指出，全球生技醫療併購市場2026年聚焦高品質療法、AI整合與效率提升。報告認為，面對監管挑戰，製藥、醫材、健康服務業者需靈活運用資本、強化合規，實現精準高效成長。此趨勢與臺灣推動AI結合智慧生技的政策方向形成呼應，顯示臺灣生技產業正處於全球趨勢與本土政策的交會點。
在技術應用層面，IEK產業情報網報導指出，隨著數位醫療快速發展，相關技術除了廣泛應用於臨牀診療，也逐漸成為推動國際醫療合作的重要工具。遠距醫療、AI影像分析及3D數位模擬等技術，不僅提升醫療品質，也讓跨國醫療團隊突破地域限制，共同參與複雜病例的診斷與治療。此發展與行政院健康臺灣深耕計畫中結合AI力推智慧生技的方向一致，顯示臺灣正積極推動數位醫療技術的臨牀應用與國際合作。
綜合各來源觀察，臺灣生技醫療產業正面臨政策推動與全球趨勢的雙重驅動。行政院四年89億元的健康臺灣深耕計畫提供資源基礎，生策會推動產官學跨域合作，而全球併購市場對AI整合與高品質療法的關注，亦為臺灣生技產業帶來國際合作與擴張的契機。然而，監管挑戰、跨部會整合成效，以及高齡社會不健康餘命問題，仍待後續觀察與進一步落實。
