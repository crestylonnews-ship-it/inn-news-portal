  # Software Development Process Explained: Five Stages and Methodology Comparison
  
  > Multiple media outlets have compiled the software development process, covering five major stages—from requirements analysis and design to development, testing, and operations—while comparing the differences between Waterfall and Agile development methodologies.
  
  According to summaries by monday.com, cloud-interactive, insightdigit, ares, and projectmanager, the software development process is generally divided into five stages: requirements analysis, system design, development and coding, testing and validation, and deployment and operations. monday.com states that the first step involves requirements gathering and analysis, which requires conducting interviews or surveys with internal departments and upstream and downstream stakeholders to identify actual pain points and desired features, with product managers consolidating the findings into detailed requirement documents. ares also emphasizes that before building a system, enterprises should first clarify requirements analysis and process mapping—a stage most often overlooked yet directly impacting subsequent development outcomes.
  
  Regarding development methodologies, both cloud-interactive and insightdigit mention the two mainstream models: Waterfall and Agile. cloud-interactive explains that Waterfall development is the most fundamental and original process, with each stage executed sequentially; the previous stage must be completed before moving to the next, making it a linear process. insightdigit further adds that, in addition to Waterfall, iterative development is also an option, and enterprises can choose the methodology based on project characteristics and team size. projectmanager, from a product development perspective, proposes a six-step process covering market research, product conceptualization, design and development, testing and validation, market promotion, and post-sales evaluation, while recommending collaboration tools such as monday dev, ClickUp, and Miro to improve new product success rates.
  
  Different media outlets divide the process stages slightly differently. monday.com and ares both advocate the five-stage model, while projectmanager breaks product development into six steps, emphasizing market-oriented aspects. insightdigit specifically reminds readers that the most often overlooked stage in system development is not coding, but rather requirements and process mapping before construction begins, and recommends that enterprises make informed decisions between in-house development and outsourcing. nss approaches the topic from the perspective of tools and methods, introducing essential software development tools for developers and exploring the benefits of outsourcing development as well as factors to consider when selecting an outsourcing company.
  
  Synthesizing perspectives from various media, while the software development process shares a common framework, enterprises should select the appropriate methodology and tool combination based on their own needs, team size, and project characteristics. The industry currently tends to favor Agile development to respond to rapidly changing market demands, but Waterfall development remains valuable for large-scale projects with well-defined requirements. Future observations will focus on the impact of AI-assisted development tools on traditional processes and the further reshaping of software development workflows by remote team collaboration models.

# 軟體開發流程解析：五大階段與方法論比較

> 多家媒體整理軟體開發流程，涵蓋需求分析、設計、開發、測試至維運五大階段，並比較瀑布式與敏捷式開發方法論差異。

根據 monday.com、cloud-interactive、insightdigit、ares 及 projectmanager 等多家媒體整理，軟體開發流程普遍被劃分為五大階段，包含需求分析、系統設計、開發編碼、測試驗證與部署維運。monday.com 指出，第一步驟為需求盤點與分析，需透過與公司內部各部門、上下遊利害關係人的訪談或問卷，挖掘實際痛點與期望功能，並由產品經理統整為詳細需求檔案。ares 亦強調，企業在打造系統前，應先釐清需求分析與流程梳理，此階段最常被忽略，卻直接影響後續開發成效。
在開發方法論方面，cloud-interactive 與 insightdigit 均提及瀑布式（Waterfall）與敏捷式（Agile）兩大主流模式。cloud-interactive 說明，瀑布式開發為最原始的基礎流程，各階段依序執行，必須完成前一階段才能進入下一階段，屬線性流程。insightdigit 則進一步補充，除瀑布式外，亦有迭代式開發可供選擇，企業可視專案特性與團隊規模決定採用何種方法論。projectmanager 則從產品開發角度提出六步驟流程，涵蓋市場研究、產品概念化、設計開發、測試驗證、市場推廣到銷售後續評估，並推薦 monday dev、ClickUp、Miro 等協作工具以提升新品成功率。
不同媒體對流程階段的劃分略有差異。monday.com 與 ares 均主張五大階段，而 projectmanager 則將產品開發拆解為六步驟，側重市場面向。insightdigit 特別提醒，系統開發最常被忽略的階段並非寫程式，而是動工前的需求與流程梳理，並建議企業在自建與委外之間做出明智決策。nss 則從工具與方法切入，介紹開發人員必備的軟體開發工具，並探討外包開發的好處與選擇外包公司的考量因素。
綜合各媒體觀點，軟體開發流程雖有共通框架，但企業應根據自身需求、團隊規模與專案特性，選擇合適的方法論與工具組合。目前業界普遍傾向敏捷開發以應對快速變化的市場需求，但瀑布式開發在需求明確的大型專案中仍具價值。後續觀察將聚焦於 AI 輔助開發工具對傳統流程的衝擊，以及遠距團隊協作模式對軟體開發流程的進一步重塑。
