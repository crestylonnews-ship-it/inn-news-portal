  # AI and Media Transformation: Structural Challenges and Ideological Tensions in Journalism
  
  > Multiple media outlets explore the structural impact of artificial intelligence on the news industry, while reflecting on long-standing issues of media ideals, profit pressures, and professional autonomy.
  
  The rapid development of artificial intelligence technology is profoundly changing the way news media operate. According to Yam News, AI's structural impact on news media has gone beyond news practice and management processes. Newspaper companies are actively investing in niche media across the broader media landscape, such as JP/Politikens Hus acquiring medical and design publications, and Schibsted sweeping through Norway's podcast market. Wazaiii further points out that the era of coexistence between traditional media and AI has arrived. Current media AI pilot projects include testing whether AI can ease video editing work for editorial teams, experimenting with synthetic voice to help create audio content, and integrating dictation and translation functions. These technological applications show that media organizations are actively exploring the practical use of AI in content production.
  
  However, behind technological change lies a deeper industry dilemma. An article on the UDN blog examines the tension between media ideals and profit, noting that those who first enter the communications media field are likely driven by public interest and conscience, hoping to follow journalistic codes of conduct and uphold the principle of social responsibility—acting autonomously, neutrally, and accurately in fulfilling their duties as media workers. This resonates with an article by Lee Li-feng in The Initium, which emphasizes that "Who is media? Who is a journalist?" is not an abstract conceptual discussion; in the operation of various social and political systems, the definitions of media and journalists carry very concrete practical significance. From a historical perspective, the Thinking Taiwan Forum recorded the "Hello Big, I'm Not Afraid, September 1 Anti-Media Monopoly" march initiated by the Taiwan News Press Association, regarded as an important milestone in Taiwan's struggle for freedom of speech and professional autonomy in news media. These events demonstrate that media professional autonomy and monopoly issues have long persisted.
  
  A comprehensive review across sources reveals that the tension between AI technology applications and media ideals warrants attention. Yam News and Wazaiii jointly confirm that AI has entered the actual workflow of news media, involving specific applications such as video editing, voice synthesis, and dictation-translation. However, the sources have not reached consensus on AI's long-term impact on journalistic professionalism. The UDN blog stresses that news workers should still uphold the principle of social responsibility, implying that technological applications must run parallel to professional values. The Initium and the historical records from Thinking Taiwan Forum remind us that the definition of media and professional autonomy remain evolving issues. What remains to be confirmed includes: the actual effectiveness of AI pilot projects, the specific impact of technological applications on news quality, and the way traditional media ideals adapt in the digital age. The media industry is currently in a phase where technological transformation and professional reflection proceed in parallel.

# AI與媒體變革：新聞業面臨結構性挑戰與理想張力

> 多家媒體探討人工智慧對新聞產業的結構性影響，同時反思媒體理想、營利壓力與專業自主等長期議題。

人工智慧技術的快速發展正深刻改變新聞媒體的運作模式。根據蕃新聞報導，人工智慧對新聞媒體的結構性影響已超越新聞實踐和新聞管理過程，報業公司正積極投資整個媒體領域的利基媒體，例如JP/Politikens Hus購買醫療和設計出版物，以及Schibsted橫掃挪威播識市場等案例。wazaiii進一步指出，傳統媒體與人工智慧並存的時代已經來臨，目前各家媒體的AI試點工作包括測試人工智慧是否可以減輕編輯團隊的影片編輯工作，也實驗使用合成語音來幫助建立音訊內容，還有整合聽寫、翻譯等功能。這些技術應用顯示媒體機構正積極探索AI在內容生產環節的實際應用。
然而，技術變革背後也反映更深的產業困境。UDN部落格文章探討媒體理想與營利問題，指出初投入傳播媒體領域的工作者，一定也懷抱著公益、良知，想依循著新聞工作守則，秉持社會責任的理念，自主、中立、精確的去善盡媒體工作者的職責。這與端傳媒李立峯的文章形成呼應，該文強調「誰是媒體？誰是記者？」並非抽象概念討論，在各種社會和政治制度的運作中，媒體和記者的定義具有非常具體的實踐意義。歷史視角方面，想想論壇記錄了臺灣新聞記者協會發起的「你好大、我不怕，901反媒體壟斷」大遊行，被視為臺灣爭取言論自由、新聞媒體專業自主歷史上重要的裡程碑。這些事件顯示媒體專業自主與壟斷問題長期存在。
綜合各來源觀察，AI技術應用與媒體理想之間的張力值得關注。蕃新聞與wazaiii共同確認的事實是：AI已進入新聞媒體的實際工作流程，涉及影片編輯、語音合成、聽寫翻譯等具體應用。但關於AI對新聞專業性的長期影響，各來源並未達成共識。UDN部落格強調新聞工作者仍應秉持社會責任理念，這暗示技術應用需與專業價值並行。端傳媒與想想論壇的歷史記錄則提醒，媒體定義與專業自主是持續演變的議題。目前尚待確認之處包括：AI試點工作的實際成效、技術應用對新聞品質的具體影響，以及傳統媒體理想在數位時代的適應方式。媒體產業正處於技術變革與專業反思並行的階段。
