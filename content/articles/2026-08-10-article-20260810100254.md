  # Labor Ministry Regulates Employment Insurance Coverage; Academia Deepens Human Resources Professional Training
  
  > The Ministry of Labor has announced regulations on labor insurance and employment insurance coverage, clarifying that employees aged 15 to 65 who are currently employed must participate in employment-based insurance. Chung Hwa University's Department of Labor and Human Resources continues to deepen its expertise in labor-management relations and human resources education, while promoting international field teaching and occupational safety certification.
  
  According to announcements on the Ministry of Labor's global information website, labor insurance and employment insurance are employment-based insurance programs that require an employment relationship as a prerequisite. Under Article 6 of the Labor Insurance Act, government agencies and public and private school employees aged 15 and above but under 65, who are not eligible to participate in the pension insurance system for public and educational personnel, shall join labor insurance through their employers as the insuring unit. The Employment Insurance Act Article 5 contains similar provisions, clearly defining the target participants and scope of application to ensure that employed workers enjoy basic social protection.
  
  The Ministry of Labor has also issued regulations concerning workers under Article 84-1 of the Labor Standards Act, along with guiding principles for workers performing duties outside the workplace, covering working hour-related rights including work and rest periods, rest days, holidays, special leave, and various types of leave. Additionally, the Ministry has established a dedicated section for labor-employer consultations on reducing working hours, and has regulated workers' rights during natural disasters and provisions for national jurors' official leave, further strengthening the mechanism for protecting working conditions.
  
  Regarding employment services, the Labor Force Development Agency of the Ministry of Labor provides an employment service hotline at (02) 8995-6000, with fax number (02) 8995-6245. Service hours are Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 12:30 PM and 1:30 PM to 5:30 PM. To enhance service quality and browsing experience, the public is advised to use the latest versions of Edge, Chrome, or Firefox browsers, and to view the website at a resolution of 1024×768 or higher.
  
  In terms of entrepreneurship support, the Labor Force Development Agency operates the "Entrepreneurship Consulting and Counseling Service Program," offering entrepreneurship training courses that cover business preparation, opportunity selection, risk assessment, management, and marketing strategies. These programs aim to help aspiring entrepreneurs build relevant knowledge and skills, create a business-friendly environment, and promote successful entrepreneurship.
  
  In the field of academic education, the Department of Labor and Human Resources at Chung Hwa University adopted its current name in the 2019 academic year. Guided by the educational philosophy of "balancing labor humanistic care with business management," the department has become one of the most representative programs in Taiwan for cultivating expertise in labor-management relations and human resources. The department conducted visits to Renmin University of China and the Chinese Academy of Labor Relations in Beijing in 2015 and 2016, and participated in the Asian Social Forum in Tokyo in 2016, promoting international academic exchange. Additionally, 18 students from the department obtained certification as internal auditors under the ISO 45001:2018 Occupational Health and Safety Management System, demonstrating their professional practical capabilities.

# 勞動部規範就業保險投保範圍 學術界深耕人力資源專業培育

> 勞動部公告勞工保險與就業保險投保規定，明確年滿 15 歲至 65 歲在職員工應參加在職保險；中國文化大學勞動暨人力資源學系持續深化勞資關係與人力資源專業教育，並推動國際移地教學與職業安全認證。

根據勞動部全球資訊網公告，勞工保險及就業保險係屬在職保險，須以僱傭關係為前提條件。依據勞工保險條例第 6 條規定，年滿 15 歲以上、65 歲以下，依法不得參加公教人員保險之政府機關及公、私立學校員工，應以其僱主為投保單位參加勞工保險。就業保險法第 5 條亦有類似規定，明確投保物件與適用範圍，確保在職勞工享有基本社會保障。
勞動部另公佈勞動基準法第 84 條之 1 工作者相關規範，並發布勞工在事業場所外工作時間指導原則，涵蓋工時相關權益，包括工作與休息時間、例假與休息日、休假、特別休假、請假等專案。此外，勞動部亦設立勞僱協商減少工時專區，並規範天然災害勞工出勤權益及國民法官公假相關規定，以完善勞動條件保護機制。
在就業服務方面，勞動部勞動力發展署提供就業服務專線，電話代表號為 (02) 8995-6000，傳真機號碼 (02) 8995-6245，服務時間為週一至週五上午 8 時 30 分至 12 時 30 分、下午 1 時 30 分至 5 時 30 分。為提升服務品質與瀏覽體驗，建議民眾使用最新版本 Edge、Chrome 或 Firefox 瀏覽器，並以 1024×768 以上解析度瀏覽網站。
創業輔導部分，勞動力發展署運用「創業諮詢輔導服務計畫」，辦理創業研習課程，提供創業準備、商機選擇、風險評估、經營及行銷策略等課程，協助有意願創業的民眾提升創業相關知能，建構友善創業環境，促進民眾成功創業。
學術教育方面，中國文化大學勞動暨人力資源學系於 108 學年度更名為現名，秉持「勞動人文關懷與企業經營並重」教育理念，深耕勞資關係與人力資源專業培育，成為國內最具代表性的相關學系。該系曾於 2015 年、2016 年赴北京參訪中國人民大學及中國勞動關係學院，2016 年亦赴東京參與亞洲社會論壇活動，推動國際學術交流。此外，該系 18 位學生透過 ISO 45001:2018 職業安全衛生管理系統條文暨內部稽覈員訓練資格認證，展現專業實務能力。
