  The Taoyuan City Police Department's Juvenile Police Division held its inaugural "Youth Comedy Night" stand-up comedy event on August 8, 2026, featuring performances by well-known comedians Hua Xu, Cao Xiao Ou, and Ying Ying. The event aims to promote crime prevention through innovative means, integrating life education into comedy performances to encourage teenagers to reflect on interpersonal relationships, self-identity, emotional management, and online usage. After registration opened, over a hundred students signed up enthusiastically, demonstrating that stand-up comedy's growing influence among young people has become an important form of youth cultural exchange.
  
  Meanwhile, a US court in New Mexico recently delivered a landmark ruling against tech giant Meta, finding that its social media platforms, including Facebook and Instagram, harm the mental health of children and adolescents and failed to adequately prevent risks of sexual exploitation. The court ordered Meta to pay a $567 million fine, bringing the cumulative total to $942 million when combined with the $375 million first-phase ruling in March this year. This marks the first successful lawsuit by a US state government against Meta on youth safety issues, regarded as an important milestone. The ruling underscores growing global concern over the risks of social media use among teenagers and is prompting tech companies to reevaluate their content safety mechanisms.
  
  In Taiwan, the New Taipei City Government Youth Bureau partnered with Womany to host the first event of "Inclusive Generation: Three Practice Sessions to Activate Influence" on July 25, 2026. Themed "Walking in Others' Shoes to See the World," the event aims to bridge prejudice through empathy education and integrate equality concepts into work and daily life. This reflects how local governments and civil society organizations in Taiwan are actively promoting youth gender equality and social inclusion education, aligning with global youth issues that emphasize diversity and inclusion.

These developments show that youth issues have moved beyond traditional awareness campaigns into a complex field intersecting law, technology, and social education. Experts note that future policies and corporate practices need to more actively address the mental health and safety risks young people face in the digital age, while cultivating their critical thinking and empathy through educational activities.

# 桃園警方辦脫口秀宣導、Meta 遭判賠 304 億、新北推平權活動

> 桃園警方首度舉辦「青春 Comedy Night」脫口秀，吸引百名學生報名；美國新墨西哥州法院判決 Meta 需支付 9.42 億美元罰款，認定社羣平臺危害兒少心理健康；新北市政府青年局與女人迷合作舉辦首場平權倡議活動。

桃園市政府警察局少年警察隊於 2026 年 8 月 8 日首度舉辦「青春 Comedy Night」脫口秀活動，邀請知名喜劇演員華旭、曹小歐及硬硬同臺演出。該活動旨在以創新方式推動犯罪預防宣導，將生活教育融入喜劇表演，引導青少年思考人際相處、自我認同、情緒管理及網路使用等議題。活動開放報名後，吸引超過百名學生踴躍參與，顯示單口喜劇在年輕世代中的影響力已逐漸成為青年文化的重要交流方式。
另一方面，美國新墨西哥州法院近日對科技巨頭 Meta 作出歷史性判決，認定旗下 Facebook、Instagram 等社羣平臺對兒童及青少年心理健康造成危害，且未妥善防範性剝削風險。法院判處 Meta 支付 5.67 億美元（約新臺幣 183 億元）罰款，加上今年 3 月第一階段判決的 3.75 億美元，累計罰款金額高達 9.42 億美元（約新臺幣 304 億元）。此為美國州政府首次針對兒少安全議題成功向 Meta 求償，被視為重要裡程碑。判決結果凸顯全球對社羣平臺青少年使用風險的關注，也促使科技業者重新檢視內容安全機制。
在臺灣本地，新北市政府青年局與女人迷合作舉辦「共融世代：啟動影響力的 3 場實踐學」首場活動，於 2026 年 7 月 25 日正式登場。活動以「走到別人的位置看世界」為主題，旨在透過同理心教育跨越偏見界線，將平權觀念融入工作與生活實踐。此活動反映臺灣地方政府與民間團體正積極推動青少年性別平權與社會包容教育，呼應全球青年議題中對多元共融的重視。
綜合觀察，近期青少年議題呈現多元發展趨勢：警方以創新演藝形式進行犯罪預防宣導，科技巨頭因兒少安全問題面臨法律責任，而地方政府與民間組織則持續推動平權教育。這些發展顯示青少年議題已跨越傳統宣導框架，進入法律、科技與社會教育交會的複雜場域。專家指出，未來相關政策與企業實踐需更積極回應青少年在數位時代面臨的心理健康與安全風險，同時透過教育活動培養其批判思考與同理能力。
