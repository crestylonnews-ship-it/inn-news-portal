  # Multifaceted Progress on Youth Issues: Meta's Damages Ruling, South Korea's Vocational Education Boom, and Taiwan's Innovative Awareness Campaigns
  
  > The US Meta faces a $942 million (approximately NT$30.4 billion) judgment over harms to children's and adolescents' mental health; South Korean teens are flocking to semiconductor vocational high schools; Taoyuan police launch a stand-up comedy crime prevention campaign; New Taipei Youth Bureau promotes equality education.
  
  A court in New Mexico, US, recently delivered a landmark ruling against tech giant Meta, finding that its social media platforms, including Facebook and Instagram, harm the mental health of children and adolescents and failed to adequately guard against risks of sexual exploitation. Meta was ordered to pay cumulative fines of $942 million (approximately NT$30.4 billion), marking the first successful lawsuit by a US state government against Meta on child and adolescent safety issues. Yahoo Entertainment reported that the ruling signals a major shift in the judiciary's attitude toward social media platform regulation, but did not detail the specific ways and scope of court-imposed restrictions on adolescent usage, which remain to be confirmed.
  
  In Asian education trends, Techbang reported that South Korean teens are embracing a "skip college, head straight to chip fabs" movement, with more and more young people choosing to enter semiconductor vocational high schools to learn practical professional skills, avoiding the risk of degree devaluation and redefining career paths through hands-on competence. This phenomenon reflects how, under the impact of the AI wave, the traditional career path of "attending a top university equals guaranteed high salary and stability" is facing strong questioning from young people worldwide, while the semiconductor industry's strong demand for technical talent is upending East Asian societies' traditional emphasis on academic credentials.
  
  In Taiwan, the Taoyuan City Police Department's Juvenile Police Division held its first "Youth Comedy Night" stand-up comedy event, inviting comedians Hua Xu, Cao Xiao Ou, and Ying Ying to perform, integrating crime prevention work into stand-up comedy. Yahoo Entertainment reported that the event attracted over a hundred student registrations as soon as it opened, using the comedians' sharing of life stories and real experiences to guide adolescents to reflect on interpersonal relationships, self-identity, emotional management, and online usage. This represents an innovative attempt by police in awareness campaigns, but the specific effectiveness of the event and follow-up feedback from participating students currently rest on a single claim of strong registration interest and await further observation.
  
  The New Taipei City Government Youth Bureau, in collaboration with Womany, held the first session of "Inclusive Generation: Three Practical Sessions to Activate Influence" on July 25, emphasizing "crossing the boundaries of prejudice with empathy, bringing equality rights into work and life." Womany reported that the event aims to promote young people's understanding and practice of equality issues, but did not provide specific information such as detailed event content and participation scale.
  
  A comprehensive observation of the above four developments shows that youth issues are receiving attention from different angles: at the judicial level, fines are used to hold tech platforms accountable for protection; at the education level, career choices are being adjusted in response to industrial changes; law enforcement agencies are attempting to use formats familiar to younger generations for awareness campaigns; and government bodies are promoting equality education. However, specific implementation details and long-term impact assessments for each case still require more information and follow-up tracking.

# 青少年議題多元進展：Meta判賠、南韓職教熱潮與臺灣創新宣導

> 美國Meta因危害兒少心理健康遭判賠304億元；南韓青少年掀起半導體高中熱潮；桃園警方以脫口秀創新犯罪預防；新北青年局推動平權教育。

美國新墨西哥州法院近日對科技巨頭Meta作出歷史性判決，認定旗下Facebook、Instagram等社羣平臺對兒童及青少年心理健康造成危害，且未妥善防範性剝削風險，判Meta須支付累計9.42億美元（約新臺幣304億元）罰款，為美國州政府首次針對兒少安全議題成功向Meta求償。Yahoo Entertainment報導指出，此判決標誌著司法體系對社羣平臺監管態度的重大轉變，但報導未詳述法院具體限制青少年使用的方式與範圍，此部分尚待確認。
在亞洲教育趨勢方面，Techbang報導南韓青少年正掀起一股「放棄大學、直奔晶片廠」的風潮，越來越多的青少年選擇進入半導體職業高中學習專業實務技能，以避開學歷貶值風險，並透過實務能力重新定義職涯路徑。此現象反映AI浪潮衝擊下，傳統「考取名校大學＝保障高薪穩定」的職涯路徑正面臨全球年輕一代的強烈質疑，半導體產業對技術人才的強大需求正顛覆東亞社會重視傳統文憑的價值觀。
臺灣方面，桃園市政府警察局少年警察隊首度舉辦「青春Comedy Night」脫口秀活動，邀請喜劇演員華旭、曹小歐及硬硬同臺演出，將犯罪預防工作融入脫口秀演出。Yahoo Entertainment報導指出，活動一開放即吸引超過百名學生踴躍報名，藉由演員分享生活趣事與真實經驗，引導青少年思考人際相處、自我認同、情緒管理及網路使用等議題。此為警方創新宣導方式的嘗試，但活動具體成效與參與學生後續反饋，目前僅有報名踴躍之單一說法，尚待進一步觀察。
新北市政府青年局則與女人迷合作，於7月25日舉辦「共融世代：啟動影響力的 3 場實踐學」首場活動，強調「用同理跨越偏見界線，讓平權走進工作與生活」。Womany報導指出此活動旨在促進青年對平權議題的認識與實踐，但未提供活動詳細內容與參與規模等具體資訊。
綜合觀察，上述四項進展顯示青少年議題正從不同面向受到關注：司法層面透過罰款要求科技平臺負起保護責任；教育層面因應產業變遷調整職涯選擇；執法機關嘗試以年輕世代熟悉的形式進行宣導；政府單位則推動平權教育。然而，各案例在具體實施細節、長期影響評估等方面，仍有待更多資訊補充與後續追蹤。
