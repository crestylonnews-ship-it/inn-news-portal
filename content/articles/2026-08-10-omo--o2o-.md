  # Cross-Analysis of New Retail Strategies: The Evolution of OMO and O2O and Consumer Behavior Insights
  
  > This article synthesizes reports from multiple media sources to outline the core differences and developmental trajectories of OMO and O2O strategies in the new retail sector, and draws on consumer behavior data to explore the impact of online-offline integration trends on the retail industry.
  
  According to Amazon Business, OMO (Online Merge Offline) and O2O (Online to Offline) have become two major strategies in the new retail field, with their core objective being to strengthen the connection between brands and consumers and enhance loyalty through deeper service and consumer experiences. Meanwhile, CYBERBIZ e-commerce blog cited 2021 consumer shopping path data showing that 48% of consumers try multiple online shopping models, 52.9% shop equally online and offline, and 73% of consumers have experience purchasing from the same brand across multiple channels. These figures confirm that online-offline integration has become common consumer behavior. Cloud Interactive further pointed out that evolving from O2O to the O2O2O model is an important direction for digital transformation in retail, especially becoming one of the operational solutions for traditional retail service industries post-pandemic, though it also brings significant challenges.
  
  Regarding divergent perspectives, BearBoss blog emphasized that e-commerce is merely a conversion of sales channels, and the essence of retail remains unchanged. Brands should focus on understanding market characteristics and consumer thinking, meeting demands with suitable products, pricing, and services, and argued that "retail branding" is the primary threshold. Vocus, from the perspective of 2025 e-commerce platform outlooks, analyzed that e-commerce platforms have far exceeded their role as tools, evolving into complete business ecosystems where OMO and D2C (Direct-to-Consumer) are seen as core drivers of industry development, with e-commerce platforms playing a key supporting role. Additionally, materials from the Tiandi Ren Xuetang course noted that with the development of the internet, traditional physical retail has given rise to new business models at each stage—from street-side grocery stores expanding to shopping malls, supermarkets, and hypermarkets, and now moving toward the online-offline integrated OMO stage. Synthesizing all sources, the industry broadly agrees that digital transformation and online-offline integration are inevitable trends, though differences remain regarding the focus of strategic evolution (such as O2O2O versus D2C) and interpretations of the essence of retail.
  
  In terms of impact and future observations, CYBERBIZ noted that the advantage of OMO's online-offline integration lies in enabling both brand e-commerce and physical store performance to grow simultaneously, which aligns with Amazon Business's goal of deepening consumer connections. Cloud Interactive cautioned that while digital transformation serves as an operational solution, it remains a major challenge for traditional retail and requires careful planning. BearBoss's perspective reminded the industry not to over-focus on channel formats but to return to the essence of retail and consumer needs. Vocus's expectations for the role of e-commerce platforms in 2025 indicate the industry is moving toward a more ecosystem-oriented direction. Areas still awaiting confirmation include: the specific differences and applicable scenarios between the O2O2O model and OMO strategy in practice; actual cases and effectiveness of D2C as a core driver; and the differing challenges faced by retailers of various sizes during digital transformation. Future observations will focus on the continued evolution of consumer online-offline integration behavior and how the retail industry balances technology investment with essential services.

# 新零售策略交叉分析：OMO 與 O2O 演進及消費者行為洞察

> 本文綜合多家媒體報導，梳理新零售領域中 OMO 與 O2O 策略之核心差異與發展脈絡，並引用消費者行為資料，探討虛實整合趨勢對零售業之影響。

根據 Amazon Business 指出，OMO（Online Merge Offline）與 O2O（Online to Offline）已成為新零售領域中兩大重要策略，其核心目標在於透過服務與消費者體驗的深化，加強品牌與消費者的連結並提升忠誠度。同時，CYBERBIZ 電商部落格引用 2021 年消費者購物路徑資料顯示，48% 的消費者會嘗試多元網購模式，52.9% 的消費者購物頻率虛實各半，且 73% 消費者有在同一品牌多通路購物的經驗，這些資料佐證了虛實整合已成為消費者常見行為。Cloud Interactive 則進一步指出，從 O2O 進化到 O2O2O 的模式，是零售業數位轉型的重要方向，尤其在疫情後成為傳統零售服務業的營運解決方案之一，但同時也帶來顯著挑戰。
在觀點分歧方面，BearBoss 部落格強調電商僅是銷售管道的轉換，零售本質始終未變，品牌應聚焦於理解市場特徵與消費者思維，以合適的產品、價格與服務滿足需求，並主張「零售品牌」是首要門檻。Vocus 則從 2025 年電商平臺未來展望的角度分析，認為電商平臺已遠超工具角色，轉而成為完整的商業生態系統，其中 OMO 與 D2C（直銷對流）被視為推動行業發展的核心動力，電商平臺在其中扮演關鍵支撐角色。此外，天地人學堂課程資料提及，隨著網際網路發展，傳統實體零售經歷各階段皆產生新商業型態，從街邊雜貨店延伸至購物商場、超市、量販通路，現正邁向虛實融合的 OMO 階段。綜合各來源，業界普遍認同數位轉型與虛實整合為必然趨勢，但對於策略演進的重點（如 O2O2O 或 D2C）及零售本質的解讀仍存在差異。
就影響與後續觀察而言，CYBERBIZ 指出 OMO 線上線下整合的優勢在於能讓品牌電商與實體門市業績一同成長，這與 Amazon Business 所述深化消費者連結的目標相呼應。Cloud Interactive 提醒，數位轉型雖為營運解決方案，但對傳統零售業而言仍是重大挑戰，需謹慎佈局。BearBoss 的觀點則提醒業界勿過度聚焦於管道形式，而應回歸零售本質與消費者需求。Vocus 對 2025 年電商平臺角色的預期，顯示行業正朝向更生態化的方向發展。目前尚待確認之處包括：O2O2O 模式與 OMO 策略在實務上的具體差異與適用情境；D2C 作為核心動力的實際案例與成效；以及不同規模零售業者在數位轉型過程中面臨的挑戰差異。後續觀察將聚焦於消費者虛實整合行為的持續演變，以及零售業如何平衡技術投資與本質服務。
