  # OMO Virtual-Physical Integration Becomes Mainstream Under New Retail Wave, Brands Accelerate Online-Offline Layout
  
  > Analysts point out that OMO and O2O models have become core strategies of new retail; virtual-physical integration can enhance consumer experience and brand loyalty, though opinions differ on whether the essence of retail has changed, and e-commerce platforms' roles continue to evolve in 2025.
  
  The concept of new retail has continued to gain momentum in recent years, with OMO (Online Merge Offline) and O2O (Online to Offline) emerging as two key strategies discussed across the industry. According to data provided by Amazon Business, the core of new retail lies in deepening service and consumer experience to build close connections with consumers, thereby enhancing brand loyalty. Amazon Business also noted that while OMO and O2O both belong to the new retail domain, they differ in operational logic: OMO emphasizes deep integration of online and offline channels, rather than simply driving online traffic to physical stores.
  
  Different media outlets hold diverging views on the essence of new retail. BearBoss Blog argues that e-commerce is merely an alternative sales channel and that the fundamental nature of retail has not changed; brands must still understand market characteristics and consumer thinking, meeting demands with appropriate products, pricing, and services, emphasizing that building a "retail brand" is the first hurdle. In contrast, CYBERBIZ cited 2021 consumer shopping path data showing that 48% of consumers have tried diverse online shopping models, 52.9% split their shopping frequency evenly between online and offline, and 73% have shopping experience across multiple channels of the same brand—indicating that consumers have grown accustomed to cross-channel shopping, and brands that integrate online and offline performance could achieve dual-track growth.
  
  Cloud Interactive further proposed the O2O2O model, noting that digital transformation has become both an operational solution and a significant challenge for traditional retail service industries during the pandemic. The analysis suggests that the wholesale sector must get ahead of the curve by evolving from O2O to O2O2O in order to enhance customer experience. Vocus observed that with advancing technology and upgrading consumer demands, e-commerce platforms in 2025 play a role far beyond that of a mere tool—they have become complete business ecosystems, with OMO and D2C (Direct-to-Consumer) serving as the core drivers of industry development, and e-commerce platforms playing a key supporting role.
  
  Synthesizing perspectives from various sources, OMO virtual-physical integration has become a clear trend in the retail industry, though opinions remain divided on whether the essence of retail has truly changed. Some argue that digital transformation is merely an upgrade of channels, with the core still resting on brand-consumer relationships; others emphasize that platform ecosystems and data integration will reshape the retail landscape. Future observations will focus on how brands implement virtual-physical integration strategies and whether consumer behavior continues to tilt toward multi-channel shopping.

# 新零售浪潮下OMO虛實整合成主流 品牌加速佈局線上線下融合

> 分析指出OMO與O2O模式成為新零售核心策略，虛實整合可提升消費者體驗與品牌忠誠度，但零售本質是否改變仍有不同看法，2025年電商平臺角色持續演進。

新零售概念近年持續發酵，OMO（Online Merge Offline）與O2O（Online to Offline）成為業界討論的兩大重要策略。根據Amazon Business提供的資料指出，新零售的核心在於透過服務與消費者體驗的深化，建立與消費者的緊密連結，進而提升品牌忠誠度。Amazon Business同時說明，OMO與O2O雖同屬新零售領域的策略，但兩者在營運邏輯上存在差異，OMO強調線上與線下的深度融合，而非單純的線上導流至線下。
不同媒體對新零售的本質看法存在分歧。BearBoss Blog認為，電商不過是換了一種銷售管道，零售本質始終沒有改變，品牌仍須理解市場特徵與消費者思維，以合適的產品、價格與服務滿足需求，並強調「零售品牌」是第一個門檻。相對地，CYBERBIZ引用2021年消費者購物路徑資料顯示，48%的消費者會嘗試多元的網購模式，52.9%的消費者購物頻率虛實各半，73%消費者有在同一品牌多通路購物經驗，顯示消費者已習慣跨通路購物，品牌若能整合線上線下業績，可望實現雙軌成長。
Cloud Interactive則進一步提出O2O2O模式，指出數位轉型成為全球傳統零售服務業在疫情下的營運解決方案，同時也是一大挑戰。該分析認為，從O2O進化到O2O2O，量販業需搶先佈局，才能提升顧客體驗。Vocus觀察到，隨著科技進步與消費者需求升級，電商平臺在2025年扮演的角色已遠不止於工具，而是完整的商業生態系統，OMO與D2C（直銷對流）成為推動行業發展的核心動力，電商平臺在其中扮演關鍵支撐角色。
綜合各來源觀點，OMO虛實整合已成零售業明確趨勢，但零售本質是否改變仍有不同解讀。部分觀點認為數位轉型僅是管道昇級，核心仍在品牌與消費者關係；另一派則強調平臺生態系與資料整合將重塑零售格局。後續觀察將聚焦於品牌如何落實虛實整合策略，以及消費者行為是否持續向多通路購物傾斜。
