  # OMO New Retail Concept Emerges as Virtual-Physical Integration Becomes Key E-commerce Transformation Strategy
  
  > As e-commerce development enters a new phase, OMO (Online Merge Offline) and O2O concepts have become the focal point of digital transformation in the retail industry. Multiple analyses indicate that consumer shopping behavior is evolving toward a balanced integration of online and offline experiences.
  
  According to a blog post by Amazon Business, OMO (Online Merge Offline) and O2O (Online to Offline) have become two major strategies in the new retail sector. Both emphasize deepening service and consumer experience, aiming to strengthen the connection between brands and consumers while boosting loyalty. The report further explains that the core of new retail lies in creating a more complete shopping journey through the integration of online and offline channels, rather than treating e-commerce merely as an extension of sales channels.
  
  CYBERBIZ e-commerce blog cited 2021 consumer shopping path data showing that 48% of consumers try diverse online shopping models, 52.9% split their shopping frequency evenly between online and offline, and 73% have experience purchasing from the same brand across multiple channels. CYBERBIZ noted that this data reflects how modern consumers are no longer limited to a single shopping channel. Brands that effectively integrate online e-commerce with physical stores are well-positioned to achieve dual-track revenue growth. The analysis emphasized that the advantage of the OMO model lies in delivering a consistent and seamless shopping experience regardless of which channel consumers use to interact with the brand.
  
  Cloud Interactive approached the topic from the perspective of retail digital transformation, pointing out that the O2O model is evolving into O2O2O, and hypermarkets and traditional retail service providers must position themselves early. The company believes that while digital transformation was an important operational solution during the pandemic, it also represents a significant challenge for traditional retailers. The key issue lies in effectively directing online traffic to physical stores and converting in-store customers into loyal online clients.
  
  A 2025 e-commerce platform outlook article published on the vocus platform proposed that the role of e-commerce platforms has extended far beyond transactional tools to become complete business ecosystems. The article highlighted that OMO and D2C (Direct to Consumer) have become the core drivers of industry development, with e-commerce platforms playing a key supporting role in helping brands reach consumers directly and capture first-hand market data.
  
  Additionally, the bearboss blog examined the essence of retail, arguing that e-commerce is simply an alternative sales channel—the fundamental nature of retail remains unchanged. The article emphasized that a brand's understanding of market characteristics and changes, consumer thinking, and the ability to meet consumer needs with appropriate products, pricing, and service constitute the first threshold of retail brand management. It advocated replacing the traditional B2C terminology with "Brand to Customers" to underscore a consumer-centric mindset.
  
  Synthesizing perspectives from various sources, while the OMO new retail concept is not entirely new, the question of how to effectively integrate online and offline resources and deliver consistent experiences has become a strategic challenge that retailers and e-commerce operators must address, given the clear consumer shift toward balanced virtual-physical engagement. Currently, the industry lacks unified standards and long-term tracking data for evaluating the specific implementation and effectiveness of OMO, making its future development worth continued observation.

# OMO新零售概念興起 虛實整合成電商轉型關鍵策略

> 隨著電商發展進入新階段，OMO（線上線下整合）與O2O概念成為零售業數位轉型焦點，多份分析指出消費者購物行為已朝向虛實並重演變。

根據亞馬遜企業服務（Amazon Business）旗下部落格指出，OMO（Online Merge Offline）與O2O（Online to Offline）已成為新零售領域中的兩大重要策略，兩者皆著重於服務與消費者體驗的深化，旨在加強品牌與消費者的連結並提升忠誠度。該報導進一步說明，新零售的核心在於透過線上與線下的融合，創造更完整的購物旅程，而非僅將電商視為單純的銷售管道延伸。
CYBERBIZ電商部落格引用2021年消費者購物路徑數據顯示，48%的消費者會嘗試多元的網購模式，52.9%的消費者購物頻率虛實各半，更有73%消費者擁有在同一品牌多通路購物的經驗。CYBERBIZ指出，此數據反映出現代消費者已不再受限於單一購物管道，品牌若能有效整合線上電商與實體門市，有望實現業績雙軌成長。該分析強調，OMO模式的优势在於讓消費者無論透過何種管道接觸品牌，都能獲得一致且流暢的購物體驗。
Cloud Interactive則從零售業數位轉型的角度切入，指出O2O模式正在進化為O2O2O，量販業與傳統零售服務業必須搶先佈局。該公司認為，數位轉型雖是疫情期間營運解決方案的重要一環，但同時也是傳統零售業者面臨的一大挑戰，如何將線上流量有效引導至實體門市，再將實體顧客轉化為線上忠實客戶，成為關鍵課題。
vocus平台發表的2025電商平台展望文章則提出，電商平台的角色已遠不止於交易工具，而是完整的商業生態系統。該文指出，OMO與D2C（Direct to Consumer，直銷對流）成為推動行業發展的核心動力，電商平台在其中扮演關鍵支撐角色，協助品牌直接觸及消費者並掌握第一手市場數據。
此外，bearboss部落格從零售本質出發，提出電商不過是換了一種銷售管道，零售的本質始終未變。該文強調，品牌對於市場特徵與變化、消費者思維的理解，以及用合適的產品、價格與服務滿足消費者需求，才是零售品牌經營的第一個門檻，並主張以「Brand to Customers」取代傳統B2C的稱呼，凸顯以消費者為中心的思維。
綜合各來源觀點，OMO新零售概念雖非全新發明，但在消費者行為已明顯轉向虛實並重的背景下，如何有效整合線上線下資源、提供一致體驗，已成為零售與電商業者必須面對的戰略課題。目前業界對於OMO的具體實踐方式與成效評估，仍缺乏統一的標準與長期追蹤數據，後續發展值得持續觀察。
