  # Chien Hsin International Invests NT$3.2 Billion to Build Northern Taiwan's Largest Cold Chain Warehouse, Targeting AI Supply Chain Opportunities
  
  > Chien Hsin International's Xinke Smart International Logistics Center was unveiled and commenced operations on August 3, 2026, becoming Northern Taiwan's largest cold chain warehouse. It targets the AI supply chain, semiconductors, and high-value temperature-controlled goods, with July revenue continuing to set a new record for the same period.
  
  Chien Hsin International (8367) officially unveiled and commenced operations of its "Xinke Smart International Logistics Center" on August 3, 2026. Located in the Taipei Port Logistics and Warehousing Zone, the center, built with an investment exceeding NT$3.2 billion, is positioned as a one-stop international logistics platform combining bonded warehousing, multi-temperature cold chain, sea-air intermodal transport, customs declaration and cargo handling, a distribution center, and smart data services. It is regarded as a new growth engine for the group's high-standard logistics services in Northern Taiwan. Chien Hsin International Chairman Chen Yen-ming stated at the unveiling ceremony that the center will target customer needs in the AI supply chain, semiconductors, electronic materials, advanced PCB substrates, biotechnology, and high-value temperature-controlled goods. It will provide a rare "sea-air intermodal + multi-temperature + bonded" one-stop service specification to safeguard the yield rates of Taiwan's electronics industry.
  
  According to Technews.tw, Chien Hsin International General Manager Ko Sheng-chung, Keelung Customs Commissioner Wu Hui-yen, and other distinguished guests jointly attended and witnessed the unveiling ceremony. The logistics center is one of the top two international logistics center operators in the Taipei Port area, with its cold chain storage area offering a significant scale advantage. Chien Hsin International emphasized that through innovative energy-saving technology and high-efficiency load-bearing design, it can meet the strict temperature and humidity requirements of the semiconductor and biotechnology industries. The center has currently successfully attracted international tech giants such as NVIDIA, Amazon, and Gold Circuit Electronics to establish a presence, making it a core base for seizing AI supply chain and high-value logistics business opportunities. Chien Hsin International expects to achieve an occupancy rate of over 50% by the end of 2026 and will launch a phase two expansion plan.
  
  CaiXun Express reported that Chien Hsin International's consolidated revenue for July 2026 reached NT$317 million, marking a month-on-month increase of 9.38% and a year-on-year increase of 13.32%, continuing to set a new high for the same period. Cumulative consolidated revenue from January to July 2026 reached NT$1.935 billion, a 4.47% increase compared to the same period last year, also setting a new high for the period. The company stated that July's revenue growth primarily benefited from stable operations in port handling, warehousing, and customs declaration, coupled with continuous improvement in the momentum of automated handling operations. With global supply chain restructuring, expansion of high-tech industry production, and increased demand for enterprises to enhance logistics resilience, related businesses such as warehousing and customs declaration remained stable. This, along with outstanding performance in copper concentrate and various bulk cargo transportation businesses, contributed to revenue growth momentum. Institutional investors are optimistic that the Xinke Smart International Logistics Center will contribute significant revenue to the group next year, with gross profit margin potentially reaching 30%, demonstrating strong growth momentum.

# 建新國際斥資32億打造北臺灣最大冷鏈倉儲 搶攻AI供應鏈商機

> 建新國際旗下新科智慧國際物流中心於2026年8月3日揭牌啟用，為北臺灣最大冷鏈倉儲，鎖定AI供應鏈、半導體與高值化溫控貨品，7月營收續創同期新高。

建新國際（8367）斥資超過32億元打造的「新科智慧國際物流中心」於2026年8月3日正式揭牌啟用，座落於臺北港物流倉儲區。該中心定位為結合保稅倉儲、多溫層冷鏈、海空聯運、報關理貨、發貨中心及智慧資料服務的一站式國際物流平臺，被視為集團在北臺灣高規格物流服務的新成長引擎。建新國際董事長陳彥銘於揭牌典禮中表示，此中心將鎖定AI供應鏈、半導體、電子材料、先進PCB載板、生技與高值化溫控貨品等客戶需求，以罕見的「海空聯運＋多溫層＋保稅」一條龍規格，為臺灣電子業的良率保駕護航。
根據Technews.tw報導，建新國際總經理柯勝鐘與基隆關關務長吳慧燕等貴賓共同出席見證揭牌典禮。該物流中心為臺北港區內前二大國際物流中心業者之一，其中冷鏈倉儲面積具規模優勢。建新國際強調，透過創新節能技術與高效載重設計，能夠滿足半導體及生技產業對溫濕度的嚴格要求。目前該中心已成功吸引輝達（NVIDIA）、亞馬遜及金像電等國際科技大廠進駐，成為搶攻AI供應鏈與高值化物流商機的核心據點。建新國際預計於2026年年底前達成出租率過半目標，並將啟動二期擴建計畫。
財訊快報報導指出，建新國際2026年7月合併營收達3.17億元新臺幣，月增9.38%、年增13.32%，續創同期新高；累計2026年1至7月合併營收達19.35億元，較去年同期成長4.47%，亦改寫同期新高。公司表示，7月營收成長主要受惠港區裝卸、倉儲及報關等業務穩健，加上自動裝卸業務動能持續提升。隨著全球供應鏈重組、高科技產業擴產及企業提升物流韌性需求增加，倉儲、報關等相關業務保持穩健，加上銅土及多項散裝貨運業務表現亮眼，挹注營收成長動能。法人看好新科智慧國際物流中心明年將挹注集團顯著營收，毛利率上看3成，展現強勁成長動能。
