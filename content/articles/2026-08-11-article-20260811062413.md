  # Memory Price Surge Shakes Global Consumer Electronics Market, Retail E-commerce Faces Challenges
  
  > Driven by AI server demand, memory prices have surged sharply, forcing consumer electronics products such as Google Pixel and Apple to face price hikes and specification adjustments, impacting the global retail e-commerce market.
  
  The global semiconductor supply chain is undergoing its most severe cost turbulence in recent years. Memory prices have continued to climb since 2024 and evolved into a storm hitting global tech giants and hardware manufacturers between 2025 and 2026. According to a report by Techbang.com on August 5, 2026, the procurement price of 1GB of memory has skyrocketed from US$2.8 in 2025 to the current US$12, a surge of nearly 330%. This wave of price hikes is primarily driven by the explosive growth in artificial intelligence (AI) computing demand. AI servers require enormous amounts of high-performance memory, causing limited LPDDR memory production capacity to shift significantly toward the high-margin data center market.
  
  This狂飆 trend in memory costs has directly impacted the manufacturing and pricing of consumer electronics. On July 29, 2026, Techbang.com reported that Shakil Barkat, Vice President of Google's Devices and Services division, confirmed that the upcoming new generation of Pixel smartphones would face overall price increases due to rising global supply chain costs and hardware production expenses. The Google Pixel 11 flagship is expected to see a US$100 price hike and will drop the 128GB entry-level storage option, effectively raising the purchasing threshold. Market rumors also suggest its specifications may be reduced due to cost pressures. To cope with soaring memory costs, software giants such as Microsoft and Google have been forced to launch system optimizations, "slimming down" their Windows and Android operating systems to reduce hardware resource demands.
  
  Not only Google, but Techbang.com reported on August 2, 2026, that Apple, which has long held absolute bargaining power in the supply chain, is also facing severe challenges. AI giants are aggressively competing for LPDDR memory resources at astonishingly high prices, making Apple's traditional strategy of locking in production capacity through massive upfront cash payments no longer as effective. An analysis showing that the memory capacity of a single AI server rack can equal that of 170 smartphones highlighted the enormous memory demand in the AI sector, causing LPDDR production capacity to tilt toward higher-margin markets and forcing Apple to face component shortages and rising end-product prices. Apple CEO Tim Cook has also issued a price hike warning on this matter.
  
  This memory cost surge, led by AI, contrasts with the strong performance of semiconductor companies such as AMD in their data center businesses. On August 5, 2026, Techbang.com noted that AMD's data center business, driven by AI, saw its revenue double, becoming its most powerful profit engine. However, its gaming business underperformed due to rising costs, illustrating a significant shift in market resource allocation. Overall, the dramatic volatility in memory prices and the reallocation of supply chains are having a notable impact on global consumer electronics retail e-commerce and consumer services. Consumers are facing rising product prices, fewer entry-level options, and even reduced specifications, which will test retailers' pricing strategies and e-commerce platforms' supply chain management capabilities, and may affect consumer spending and product sales structures across global regions, including Europe.

# 記憶體價格飆漲衝擊全球消費電子市場 零售電商面臨挑戰

> 受AI伺服器需求帶動，記憶體價格大幅上揚，導致Google Pixel、Apple等消費電子產品面臨漲價與規格調整，全球零售電商市場受衝擊。

全球半導體供應鏈正經歷近年來最嚴峻的成本震盪，記憶體價格自2024年起持續上揚，並在2025年至2026年間演變為一場衝擊全球科技巨頭與品牌硬體廠商的風暴。根據科技媒體Techbang.com於2026年8月5日的報導指出，1GB記憶體的採購價格已從2025年的2.8美元飆升至目前的12美元，漲幅高達近330%。這波漲價潮主要受人工智慧（AI）算力需求暴增所驅動，AI伺服器對高效能記憶體的需求量極大，導致有限的LPDDR記憶體產能大幅轉向高利潤的資料中心市場。
這股記憶體成本狂飆的趨勢，已直接衝擊消費電子產品的製造與定價。Techbang.com在2026年7月29日報導，Google裝置與服務部門副總裁沙基爾·巴爾卡特（Shakil Barkat）已證實，由於全球供應鏈成本與硬體生產開支援續飆升，即將亮相的新一代Pixel系列智慧型手機將面臨整體售價上調。其中，Google Pixel 11旗艦機預計將漲價100美元，並取消128GB的入門版容量，變相提高購機門檻；市場亦傳出其規格可能因成本壓力而縮水。為因應高昂的記憶體成本，微軟（Microsoft）與Google等軟體巨頭也被迫啟動系統最佳化，對Windows與Android作業系統進行「軟體瘦身」，以降低對硬體資源的需求。
不僅是Google，Techbang.com在2026年8月2日的報導中提到，長期以來在供應鏈中握有絕對議價優勢的蘋果公司（Apple），也正遭遇嚴峻挑戰。AI巨頭正以驚人的高價搶奪LPDDR記憶體資源，使得蘋果傳統上透過預付巨額現金鎖定產能的策略不再無往不利。一篇AI伺服器單機架記憶體容量可抵170臺手機的分析，凸顯了AI領域對記憶體需求的龐大，導致LPDDR記憶體產能已向高利潤市場傾斜，迫使蘋果面臨零件短缺與終端售價上漲的壓力。蘋果執行長庫克（Tim Cook）也已對此發出漲價警告。
這波由AI引領的記憶體成本飆升，與AMD等半導體公司在資料中心業務上的強勁表現形成對比。Techbang.com於2026年8月5日指出，AMD的資料中心業務受AI推動實現營收翻倍，成為其最強勁的獲利引擎。然而，其遊戲業務卻因成本高漲而表現低迷，顯示出市場資源分配的巨大轉變。整體而言，記憶體價格的劇烈波動與供應鏈的重新分配，正對全球消費電子市場的零售電商與消費服務帶來顯著影響。消費者面臨產品價格上漲、入門款選項減少甚至規格縮水的局面，這將考驗零售商的定價策略與電商平臺的供應鏈管理能力，並可能影響全球各地區（包括歐洲市場）的消費支出與產品銷售結構。
