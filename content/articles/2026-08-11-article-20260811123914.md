**Nigeria’s 650,000sq km rangelands threatened by degradation, desertification — Minister**

Nigeria’s 650,000 square kilometers of rangelands are under threat from degradation and desertification, according to the country’s Minister of Environment. The minister stated that degradation and desertification are among the biggest environmental challenges facing Nigeria, and require the collective efforts of the government, civil society, and the international community to address. Nigeria’s rangelands cover about 70% of the country’s land area and are a crucial foundation for the country’s agriculture and livestock sectors. However, overgrazing and improper land use have led to soil erosion and reduced vegetation, posing a significant threat to Nigeria’s ecosystem and agricultural development. The Nigerian government has taken some measures to address the issue, including implementing sustainable land management practices, promoting ecotourism, and developing renewable energy. However, more efforts and resources are needed to tackle this complex problem.
