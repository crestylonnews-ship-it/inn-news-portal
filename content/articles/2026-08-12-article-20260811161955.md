# NSTDA 推動本地中小企業產品推廣

## NSTDA to boost promotion of local SMEs' products

> **摘要 / Summary**：泰國國家科學與技術發展署（NSTDA）將推動本地中小企業的產品推廣，增加其在國內和國際上的競爭力。
> *The National Science and Technology Development Agency (NSTDA) is set to promote products from local small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) to increase their competitiveness in both domestic and international markets.*
