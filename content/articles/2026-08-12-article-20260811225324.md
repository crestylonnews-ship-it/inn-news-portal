According to CNN, cybersecurity experts point out that human behavior and psychological factors are the biggest cybersecurity threats. While the development of artificial intelligence (AI) technology has made cyber attacks more complex and difficult to defend against, human negligence and lack of security awareness are the root causes of most cybersecurity incidents. For example, using weak passwords, clicking on suspicious links, and not updating software can all lead to cybersecurity vulnerabilities. Additionally, cybersecurity experts also point out that human psychological factors can also lead to cybersecurity incidents. For example, people may make inappropriate decisions due to fear or stress, leading to cybersecurity incidents. Therefore, cybersecurity experts call on people to raise their security awareness, take effective security measures, such as using strong passwords, updating software, and performing regular backups, to prevent cybersecurity incidents from occurring.
