**Asean's green growth push**

The Association of Southeast Asian Nations (Asean) has been actively promoting green growth in recent years, with the aim of reducing carbon emissions and promoting sustainable development. This effort not only helps to protect the environment, but also promotes economic growth and improves the quality of life for the people. According to a report by the United Nations, the carbon emissions of Southeast Asia account for 10% of the global total, and Asean's green growth strategy will help to reduce this number. Asean's green growth push includes the promotion of renewable energy, improvement of energy efficiency, promotion of electric vehicles, and development of green infrastructure. At the same time, Asean is also strengthening cooperation with other regions, including cooperation with the European Union, China, and Japan, to promote global sustainable development.
