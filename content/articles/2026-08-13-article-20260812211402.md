  # Songlin Elementary School's Flying Wild Goose Dance Club Performs at Japan's Yosakoi Festival, Showcasing Taiwan's Cultural Education Achievements
  
  > 
  
  Kōchi Prefecture in Japan has once again invited Hsinchu County to participate in the "Yosakoi Festival," one of Asia's most representative international events. This year, the Flying Wild Goose Dance Club from Songlin Elementary School in Hsin County, which has won first place in the National Student Dance Competition for three consecutive years, represented the performance. The club took the stage on August 9 and 10 at key venues including Kōchi Castle, the area in front of Kōchi Station, and Central Park, wowing the international audience with an original dance work blending ocean, Hakka, and Taiwanese cultural elements, showcasing Hsinchu County's outstanding achievements in arts education.
  
  Since signing an education and cultural exchange memorandum of understanding in 2013, Hsinchu County and Kōchi Prefecture have continued to deepen their cooperation through events such as the Yosakoi Festival and the Manga Koshien. As the largest international festival in Japan's Shikoku region, the Yosakoi Festival attracts over 20,000 dancers and audiences from more than a hundred countries each year, making it one of the important cultural exchange platforms in East Asia. Songlin Elementary School's invitation to perform represents international recognition of Taiwan's grassroots school arts education achievements.
  
  According to media reports, the performance piece integrated ocean imagery with Hakka traditional elements, presented through original choreography by students of the Flying Wild Goose Dance Club. The performances were held at core venues including Kōchi Castle, the plaza in front of Kōchi Station, and Central Park. The Hsinchu County Government stated that this exchange activity implements the county's long-standing policy of promoting international educational cooperation, and expressed hope that through such cultural exchanges, Taiwan-Japan local friendly relations would be further deepened.

# 松林國小飛雁舞蹈社赴日參加夜來祭 展現臺灣文化教育成果

> 新豐鄉松林國小飛雁舞蹈社代表臺灣參加日本高知縣夜來祭，以客家與海洋文化為主題的原創舞作於八月九日至十日登場演出。

日本高知縣今年再度邀請新竹縣參與亞洲最具代表性的國際盛事「YOSAKOI夜來祭」（Yosakoi Festival）。今年由連續三年榮獲全國學生舞蹈比賽特優第一名的新豐鄉松林國小「飛雁舞蹈社」代表演出，於八月九日至十日分別在高知城、高知車站前及中央公園等重要舞臺登場，以結合海洋、客家及臺灣文化特色的原創舞作驚艷國際，展現新竹縣深耕藝術教育的亮眼成果。
新竹縣與日本高知縣自二○一三年簽署教育暨文化交流備忘錄以來，持續透過夜來祭、漫畫甲子園等活動深化合作。夜來祭作為日本四國地區規模最大的國際慶典，每年吸引逾二萬名舞者與百餘國觀眾參與，是東亞地區重要的文化交流平臺之一。松林國小此次受邀演出，代表臺灣基層學校藝術教育成果獲得國際肯定。
根據媒體報導，此次演出曲目融合海洋意象與客家傳統元素，由飛雁舞蹈社學生以原創編舞呈現，演出地點涵蓋高知城、高知車站前廣場及中央公園等核心舞臺。新竹縣政府方面表示，此次交流活動係落實縣府長期推動的國際教育合作政策，並期待透過此類文化交流，進一步深化臺日地方友好關係。
