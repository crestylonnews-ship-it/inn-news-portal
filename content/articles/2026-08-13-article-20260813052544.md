  # Chiang Ch'i-ch'en Proposes 13 Parenting Policies Focused on Care, Health, and Family Mobility

  > KMT Taichung mayoral candidate Chiang Ch'i-ch'en unveiled the "Taichung Family-Friendly" platform on August 10, proposing 13 parenting measures covering childcare, health, psychological support, and family mobility.

  KMT Taichung mayoral candidate Chiang Ch'i-ch'en held a policy event on August 10 to introduce the "Taichung Family-Friendly" platform. According to the Central News Agency, the proposal is organized around economic, medical, transportation, and family-friendly environment issues, and includes the creation of a Women and Children's Development Committee.

  Reported measures include a proposal to raise parental leave benefits from 80 percent to full pay, add support for quasi-public childcare and families not using childcare services, and create an appointment system for temporary childcare. The platform also covers parental counselling, postpartum home-support services, maternal RSV vaccination, AMH testing, egg-freezing support, and child enterovirus vaccination subsidies. These are campaign proposals, not measures already in force.

  On mobility and public-space policy, the proposals include four hours of free parking for family-friendly spaces, an extension of the pregnancy transportation service through one year after childbirth, parent-friendly facilities, and shared bicycles designed for carrying children. TVBS and United Daily News likewise reported the proposed committee, full parental-leave benefit goal, and the three-category, 13-measure structure. The policy package remains subject to electoral and subsequent administrative processes.

# 江啟臣提13項育兒政策　聚焦托育、醫療與親子交通

> 國民黨臺中市長參選人江啟臣8月10日發布「臺中親子味」政策，提出三大類、13項育兒主張，涵蓋托育、醫療、心理支持與親子交通等面向。

國民黨臺中市長參選人江啟臣8月10日舉行政策發布活動，提出「臺中親子味」育兒政策。中央通訊社報導，這套主張從經濟、醫療、交通與友善環境等面向提出13項措施，並包括成立「婦幼發展委員會」的構想。

報導所列措施包括將育嬰留職停薪給付由現行八成提高至全額、加碼準公共托育與未送托家庭補助、建立臨托預約機制；另涵蓋父母心理諮商、到宅月嫂服務、孕婦RSV疫苗、AMH檢測、凍卵與兒童腸病毒疫苗等項目。這些內容屬競選政策主張，並非已經施行的政府措施。

在交通與公共空間面向，主張包含親子停車優惠、好孕專車延長至產後一年、親子友善設施及可載兒童的共享單車。TVBS與聯合新聞網同樣報導婦幼發展委員會、育嬰留停全額給付目標，以及三大類、13項的政策架構；政策能否落實，仍須經選舉與後續行政程序。
