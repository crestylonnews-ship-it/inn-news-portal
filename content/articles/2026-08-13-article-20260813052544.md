  # Chiang Ch'i-ch'en Draws on European Models for New Parenting Policies; EU Focuses on Trade Imbalance
  
  > 
  
  KMT Taichung mayoral candidate Chiang Ch'i-ch'en held a press conference on August 10, 2026, to officially launch 13 comprehensive parenting policy initiatives. Chiang pointed out that Taiwan is facing a severe national security challenge posed by low birth rates and declining population, as birth rates and population size directly affect the sustainable development of the nation and its cities.
  
  To address this trend, Chiang's team has drawn on the parenting policy experiences of multiple European countries, advocating a multi-pronged approach across economic, medical, transportation, and family-friendly environment dimensions, with the goal of transforming Taichung into the warmest flagship parenting city in all of Taiwan.
  
  According to Yahoo Entertainment, Chiang emphasized that European countries have much to offer in terms of parenting support systems, including well-developed public childcare networks, family-friendly transportation design, and cross-departmental coordination mechanisms. To this end, he proposed establishing a "Women and Children's Development Committee" if elected, with the mayor personally convening cross-departmental resources to ensure policy implementation cuts across all government units. This move aims to break down traditional administrative silos, ensuring that parenting policies are no longer confined to the Social Affairs Bureau alone but become a core urban development issue.
  
  Meanwhile, the EU recently issued a press release on trade imbalance issues, expressing concern over China's market access measures. According to World Journal, the EU urged Beijing to take action to stop retaliatory trade protectionism and called on China to lift its rare earth export controls. The EU considers that trade imbalances not only affect the competitiveness of EU industries but also pose challenges to global supply chain stability. This stance demonstrates that the EU is gradually strengthening its oversight and countermeasures regarding China's trade practices in economic policy.
  
  A comprehensive observation reveals that the trend of Taiwan's local politicians referencing European policy experience is becoming increasingly evident, especially under the pressures of demographic shifts and the declining birth rate crisis, where drawing on European models of aging society support and parenting assistance systems has become an important option. At the same time, the EU's firm stance on trade issues also reflects Europe's emphasis on economic autonomy. Although these two issues belong to different domains, both demonstrate the reference value of European experience in contemporary policy-making.

# 江啟臣借鏡歐洲推育兒新政 歐盟關注貿易失衡

> 國民黨臺中市長參選人江啟臣推出13項育兒新政，借鏡歐洲經驗涵蓋交通與友善環境；歐盟發布新聞稿關注貿易失衡，敦促中國取消稀土出口管制。

國民黨臺中市長參選人江啟臣於2026年8月10日召開「臺中親子味」政見釋出記者會，正式推出13項全方位育兒新政。江啟臣指出，臺灣正面臨低生育率與少子化的嚴重國家安全挑戰，生育率與人口多寡直接影響國家與城市的永續發展。為應對此一趨勢，江啟臣團隊借鏡歐洲多國育兒政策經驗，主張從經濟、醫療、交通與友善環境等層面多管齊下，目標將臺中打造成全臺灣最溫暖的旗艦育兒城市。
根據Yahoo Entertainment報導，江啟臣強調，歐洲國家在育兒支援體繫上有諸多值得借鏡之處，包括完善的公共托育網路、親子友善的交通設計，以及跨局處的協調機制。為此，他提出若當選將設立「婦幼發展委員會」，由市長親自召集跨局處資源，確保政策執行能貫穿各部門。此舉旨在打破傳統行政藩籬，讓育兒政策不再僅限於社會局單一單位，而是成為城市發展的核心議題。
另一方面，歐盟近期針對貿易失衡問題發布新聞稿，表達對中國市場准入措施的關注。根據World Journal報導，歐盟敦促北京採取行動，停止報復性貿易保護措施，並要求中國取消稀土出口管制。歐盟方面認為，貿易失衡不僅影響歐盟產業競爭力，也對全球供應鏈穩定構成挑戰。此項立場顯示歐盟在經濟政策上正逐步強化對中國貿易行為的監督與反制措施。
綜合觀察，臺灣地方政治人物參考歐洲政策經驗的趨勢日益明顯，尤其在人口結構變遷與少子化危機下，借鏡歐洲高齡社會與育兒支援體系成為重要選項。同時，歐盟在貿易議題上的強硬立場，也反映歐洲在經濟自主性上的重視。兩項議題雖屬不同領域，但皆顯示歐洲經驗在當代政策制定中的參考價值。
