  # Kaohsiung City Government Promotes AS9100 Aerospace Quality Training to Help Manufacturers Capture Global High-Value Business Opportunities
  
  > The Kaohsiung City Government's Economic Development Bureau partnered with the Metal Industries Research and Development Center to host AS9100 aerospace quality system training. This initiative aims to help local manufacturers upgrade, capture global aerospace, drone, and defense industry opportunities, and strengthen their international competitiveness.
  
  On August 10, 2026, the Kaohsiung City Government's Economic Development Bureau collaborated with the Metal Industries Research and Development Center (MIRDC) to host the "AS9100 Aerospace Quality System Training" course at the Ministry of Economic Affairs' Traditional Industry Innovation and Value-Added Center. This initiative aims to help Kaohsiung's local manufacturing industry overcome technical upgrade barriers and subsequently seize the vast opportunities in the global aerospace, drone, and defense industries.
  
  The training reportedly attracted representatives from numerous enterprises and industry associations, including Ching Dar Technology, Rui Hu Enterprise, Hsin Fu Shan International, and the Taiwan Screw Industry Association. The enthusiastic interaction at the event demonstrated local businesses' proactive ambition to strategically position themselves in high-value manufacturing and expand into international markets.
  
  Kaohsiung City plays a crucial role in the global aerospace industry supply chain and is one of Taiwan's important aerospace industry clusters. According to relevant data, Kaohsiung-based manufacturers are responsible for processing and producing up to 75% of global orders for aircraft engine components outsourced to Taiwan. This data highlights Kaohsiung's profound strength and international standing in precision manufacturing.
  
  The AS9100 aerospace quality system, promoted through this initiative, is an internationally recognized and widely adopted quality management standard for the aerospace industry. It is based on ISO 9001 but includes additional stringent requirements specific to the aviation, space, and defense sectors, covering all stages from design, development, and production to installation and service. Its purpose is to ensure the highest quality and reliability of products and services.
  
  By adopting this international-level quality management system, local businesses can not only meet the demanding standards of international buyers but also further enhance their overall competitiveness, laying the groundwork for securing more cooperation opportunities in the global market.
  
  The implementation of this training program is expected to bring multiple positive impacts to Kaohsiung's manufacturing industry. Firstly, it will help elevate the technical standards and management capabilities of local manufacturers, enabling their product quality to match that of leading international suppliers and thus expanding into broader international markets. Secondly, with the rapid development of the aerospace, drone, and defense industries, the demand for high-precision, high-reliability components is steadily increasing.
  
  By obtaining AS9100 certification, Kaohsiung businesses will be able to more effectively integrate into these high-value-added industrial chains, achieving industrial transformation and upgrading. This initiative will not only create more employment opportunities for Kaohsiung but also drive the collective development of related industrial chains, strengthening Taiwan's strategic position in the global high-tech manufacturing sector.
  
  In the long run, continuous promotion of technological innovation and quality standardization through close collaboration between the government and industry will help Kaohsiung's manufacturing sector maintain a leading edge in a fiercely competitive global environment and inject new momentum into Taiwan's economic development.

# 高市府推AS9100航太品質培訓 助製造業搶攻全球高值商機

> 高雄市政府經濟發展局攜手金屬中心舉辦航太品質系統AS9100培訓，協助在地製造業升級，搶攻全球航太、無人機及國防產業商機，強化國際競爭力。

2026年8月10日，高雄市政府經濟發展局與財團法人金屬工業研究發展中心（金屬中心）合作，於經濟部傳統產業創新加值中心舉辦「航太品質系統AS9100培訓」課程。此舉旨在協助高雄在地製造業突破技術升級門檻，進而搶攻全球航太、無人機及國防產業的龐大商機。據報導，此次培訓吸引了多家企業及公協會代表參與，包括慶達科技、瑞滬企業、欣富山國際以及臺灣螺絲工業同業公會，現場互動熱絡，顯示在地企業對於佈局高值製造與拓展國際市場展現出積極的企圖心。
高雄市在全球航太產業供應鏈中扮演著關鍵角色，是臺灣國內重要的航太產業聚落之一。根據相關資料，全球委託臺灣製造的飛機引擎零元件訂單中，高達75%的加工生產任務由高雄地區的廠商負責。這項資料突顯了高雄在精密製造領域的深厚實力與國際地位。
此次推動的AS9100航太品質系統，是一套國際公認且廣泛採用的航太業品質管理標準，它以ISO 9001為基礎，並額外增加了航空、太空及國防產業特有的嚴格要求，涵蓋了設計、開發、生產、安裝及服務等各環節，旨在確保產品與服務的最高品質與可靠性。透過匯入此國際級品質管理系統，不僅能協助在地業者符合國際買家的嚴苛標準，更能進一步提升企業的整體競爭力，為其在全球市場中爭取更多合作機會奠定基礎。
這項培訓課程的舉辦，預期將為高雄的製造業帶來多重正面影響。首先，它有助於提升本地廠商的技術水準與管理能力，使其產品品質能與國際一線供應商看齊，進而拓展更廣闊的國際市場。其次，隨著航太、無人機及國防產業的快速發展，對高精密、高可靠性零元件的需求日益增加，高雄業者透過取得AS9100認證，將能更有效地切入這些高附加價值的產業鏈，實現產業轉型升級。此舉不僅能為高雄創造更多就業機會，亦能帶動相關產業鏈的共同發展，強化臺灣在全球高科技製造領域的戰略地位。
長遠來看，透過政府與產業的緊密合作，持續推動技術創新與品質標準化，將有助於高雄製造業在全球競爭激烈的環境中保持領先優勢，並為臺灣的經濟發展注入新的動能。
