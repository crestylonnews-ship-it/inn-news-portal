  # Horizon3 Raises $250 Million to Enter Australian and Middle East Cybersecurity Markets
  
  > 
  
  US autonomous penetration testing platform provider Horizon3 AI announced on August 10 that it has completed a $250 million Series E funding round, valuing the company at over $2 billion. According to Ithome.com.tw, Horizon3 primarily offers NodeZero, an autonomous penetration testing platform that conducts security tests on production environments under enterprise authorization. The platform identifies misconfigurations, weak login credentials, and identity management gaps, simulates how attackers could chain multiple vulnerabilities into attack paths, and provides remediation recommendations along with retesting verification.
  
  Horizon3 explicitly stated that the proceeds from this funding round will be used for product development and global market expansion, with key targets including entering the Singapore and Australian markets and deepening business operations across Europe, the Middle East, and Africa. This move reflects the cybersecurity industry's active push to capture enterprise clients in the Middle East and Oceania, particularly as Australia's role as a regional hub gains increasing attention from international cybersecurity firms. The NodeZero platform has so far conducted millions of security tests in production environments, accumulating substantial real-world data.
  
  Meanwhile, the global cybersecurity industry is undergoing rapid transformation. Ithome.com.tw reported on the same day that a vulnerability in N-able's N-central product, related to identity authentication, is being actively exploited by attackers to access critical infrastructure such as domain controllers within victim organizations, highlighting the fragility of enterprise identity management and access controls. Horizon3's NodeZero platform is specifically designed to address such vulnerabilities, helping enterprises proactively discover and remediate potential risks through automated penetration testing.
  
  On another front, OpenAI officially announced the shutdown of its AI browser Atlas on August 9, ending its 292-day run. According to Techbang, OpenAI will pivot its AI browser technology toward an extension-based format, signaling strategic adjustments by major tech companies in AI application delivery vehicles. Meanwhile, Meta founder Mark Zuckerberg predicted during a recent earnings call that billions of people worldwide will have dedicated personal AI agents within the next five years, and identified WhatsApp as the primary interaction platform, reflecting how the AI agent market has become a focal point of competition among tech giants.
  
  In a broader observation, Horizon3's strategic push into Australia and the Middle East aligns with the rising global demand for automated penetration testing in the cybersecurity market. The Middle East has been actively advancing digital transformation and cybersecurity infrastructure in recent years, while Australia continues to strengthen protection for critical infrastructure—both representing potential growth markets for cybersecurity service providers. However, media reports directly related to the Middle East and Oceania remain limited, and further developments will require continued monitoring of cybersecurity industry dynamics in these regions.

# Horizon3募資2.5億美元 進軍澳洲與中東資安市場

> 美國資安業者Horizon3完成2.5億美元E輪募資，估值逾20億美元，資金將用於拓展新加坡、澳洲市場，並深化中東與非洲佈局。

美國自主滲透測試平臺供應商Horizon3 AI於8月10日宣佈，已完成2.5億美元E輪募資，公司估值超過20億美元。根據Ithome.com.tw報導，Horizon3主要提供名為NodeZero的自主滲透測試平臺，可在企業授權下對正式環境進行安全測試，找出錯誤設定、強度不足的登入憑證與身分管理缺口，並模擬攻擊者如何串連多項弱點形成攻擊路徑，同時提出修補建議並進行複測驗證。
Horizon3明確指出，本次募資所得將用於產品研發與全球市場拓展，其中重點目標包含進軍新加坡與澳洲市場，並深化歐洲、中東與非洲地區的業務佈局。此舉顯示資安產業正積極搶攻中東與大洋洲地區的企業客戶，特別是澳洲作為區域樞紐的地位日益受到國際資安業者重視。NodeZero平臺迄今已在正式環境執行數百萬次安全測試，累積大量實戰資料。
與此同時，全球資安產業正經歷快速變革。Ithome.com.tw同日報導指出，N-able旗下N-central產品出現身分驗證漏洞，遭攻擊者積極利用以存取受害組織的網域控制器等重要設施，凸顯企業身分管理與存取控制的脆弱性。Horizon3的NodeZero平臺正是針對此類弱點設計，透過自動化滲透測試協助企業提前發現並修補潛在風險。
另一方面，OpenAI於8月9日正式宣佈關停其AI瀏覽器Atlas，結束僅292天的營運。Techbang報導指出，OpenAI將AI瀏覽器技術轉向擴充套件形式繼續發展，顯示大型科技廠在AI應用載具上的策略調整。Meta創辦人祖克柏則在近期財報會議上預測，未來五年內全球將有數十億人口擁有專屬個人AI代理，並認為WhatsApp將成為主要互動陣地，反映AI代理市場正成為科技巨頭競逐焦點。
綜合觀察，Horizon3進軍澳洲與中東的戰略佈局，與全球資安市場對自動化滲透測試需求上升的趨勢相符。中東地區近年積極推動數位轉型與資安基礎建設，澳洲亦持續強化關鍵基礎設施防護，兩者皆為資安服務商的潛在成長市場。然而，目前可取得與中東及大洋洲直接相關的媒體報導來源有限，後續發展仍需持續關注該地區資安產業的進一步動態。
