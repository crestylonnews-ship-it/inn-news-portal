  # Cross-Border E-Commerce and Port System Digital Transformation Boost Supply Chain Efficiency
  
  > Summary: Fordeal launches an inventory-free dropshipping supply chain framework, while Hong Kong advances the computerization of its port community system, reflecting the global logistics digital transformation trend and enhancing supply chain efficiency and transparency.
  
  Cross-border e-commerce platform Fordeal recently announced the launch of a digital transformation centered on an "inventory-free dropshipping supply chain" and a smart management system. According to the platform, once merchants complete store setup and product listing, consumer orders are automatically connected by the system, with goods shipped directly from the supply chain end, significantly streamlining traditional warehousing and distribution processes. This model aims to reduce inventory pressure on businesses and accelerate product circulation through automated workflows, and is regarded as a typical practice of lightweight e-commerce supply chain operations.
  
  Fordeal emphasized that the system has integrated order management, logistics tracking, and supplier collaboration functions, and is expected to improve overall cross-border transaction efficiency.
  
  Meanwhile, under Hong Kong's Transport and Logistics Bureau's "Capital Works Reserve Fund Head 710 — Computerization Programme," the new sub-item "Port Community System" is being gradually implemented. The system aims to integrate port-related data—including container movements, warehousing status, and customs clearance information—through a digital platform to strengthen collaborative operations among port stakeholders. This aligns with Fordeal's supply chain digitalization trend, indicating that the logistics industry is moving toward information transparency and process automation.
  
  Industry observers note that computerizing the port community system is expected to reduce manual review time, lower the risk of cargo delays, and provide more stable infrastructure support for cross-border e-commerce.
  
  Regarding these two developments, different media and industry perspectives offer diverse interpretations. Fordeal's inventory-free model is seen by some analysts as capable of lowering operational barriers for small and medium-sized enterprises, while others have raised concerns that over-reliance on platform systems for supply chain management may affect businesses' control over logistics processes. Hong Kong's port community system has been praised as an important step toward improving regional logistics efficiency, though the specific timeline for system integration and the scope of data sharing remain to be further clarified by officials.
  
  Overall, the digital transformation of cross-border e-commerce and port infrastructure is gradually reshaping global supply chain operations, and its long-term impact warrants continued observation.

# 跨境電商與港口系統數位轉型 供應鏈效率提升

> 摘要：Fordeal推出無庫存代出貨供應鏈架構，香港推動港口社群系統電腦化，反映全球物流數位轉型趨勢，提升供應鏈效率與透明度。

跨境電商平臺Fordeal近日宣佈啟動數位轉型，以「無庫存代出貨供應鏈」及智慧管理系統為核心架構。據該平臺說明，經營者完成店鋪建立及商品上架後，消費者下單即可由系統自動串接訂單，再由供應鏈端直接出貨，大幅簡化傳統倉儲與配送環節。此模式旨在降低業者庫存壓力，並透過自動化流程加速商品流通，被視為電商供應鏈輕量化運作的典型實踐。Fordeal強調，該系統已整合訂單管理、物流追蹤與供應商協作功能，預期能提升跨境交易的整體效率。

與此同時，香港運輸及物流局推動的「基本工程儲備基金總目710－電腦化計劃」中，新分目「構建港口社群系統」正逐步落實。該系統旨在透過數位平臺整合港口相關資料，包括貨櫃進出、倉儲狀態與通關資訊，以強化港口社群的協同作業能力。此舉與Fordeal的供應鏈數位化趨勢相呼應，顯示物流產業正朝向資訊透明化與流程自動化方向發展。業界觀察指出，港口社群系統的電腦化有望減少人工審核時間，降低貨物滯留風險，並為跨境電商提供更穩定的基礎設施支援。

針對這兩項進展，不同媒體與業界觀點呈現多元解讀。Fordeal的無庫存模式被部分分析認為能降低中小企業的營運門檻，但亦有聲音擔憂供應鏈過度依賴平臺系統，可能影響業者對物流環節的控制力。香港港口社群系統則獲評為提升區域物流效率的重要一步，不過系統整合的具體時程與資料共享範圍，目前仍待官方進一步說明。整體而言，跨境電商與港口基礎設施的數位轉型，正逐步重塑全球供應鏈的運作模式，其長期影響值得持續觀察。
