  # Student Affairs Division Launches High School Autonomy Empowerment Training Camp to Deepen Campus Democracy and Diversified Public Participation
  
  > The Student Affairs Division of the Ministry of Education held student autonomy empowerment training camps in northern, central, and southern Taiwan, focusing on campus governance and deliberative democracy while integrating diverse issues to enhance youth public participation literacy.
  
  To deepen the democratic literacy, public participation awareness, and campus governance capabilities of senior high school students nationwide, the Student Affairs Division of the Ministry of Education's National and Preschool Education Department officially held the "115th National Senior High School Student Autonomy and Participation Empowerment Training Camp" across three regions—northern, central, and southern Taiwan—during August. According to publicly reported information, the training camp brought together nearly 300 student autonomy organization representatives from across Taiwan.
  
  The overall curriculum was structured around the practical aspects of student autonomy, fully incorporating deliberative democracy mechanisms, participatory budgeting concepts, and substantive discussions on public issues. Through diversified course modules and practical exercises, the program guided young students to start from their own campus life experiences, learning how to keenly identify campus problems, build peer consensus, and propose concrete, feasible solutions—thereby substantively enhancing the operational effectiveness and public participation horizons of student autonomy organizations.
  
  Regarding course themes and training priorities, the Student Affairs Division further explained that the training camp adopted a segmented regional format, with the core theme centered on "cultivating democratic participation abilities." The scope of the training content was extensive, covering human rights education promotion, understanding of diverse ethnic groups and anti-discrimination principles,多元 gender identity and gender equality practice, student rights protection, and the spirit of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, among other dimensions. The training camp aims, through systematic training, to help high school representatives establish a solid foundation for campus public discussion, enabling students to balance diverse values and procedural justice when addressing campus governance matters.
  
  At the same time, various domestic educational organizations and cultural institutions continue to enrich youth learning resources from different angles. For example, the Taiwan Ecological Education Promotion Association, in collaboration with Zhiran Cultural and Educational Foundation, Ziranxing Cultural and Educational Foundation, and travel agencies, has promoted diversified camp education, demonstrating the parallel development of out-of-school educational practices in cultivating adolescents'多元 cognition.
  
  Beyond the active promotion of campus autonomy and student participation within the domestic education system, international public issues and regional perspectives remain an indispensable part of public affairs discussions. Current affairs commentary platforms such as ETNet's special programs have pointed out in their analysis of international situations that global geopolitical developments—including the Middle East situation and the strategic competition among major powers—profoundly affect the trajectory of the international order and regional cooperation. Meanwhile, major public information platforms continue to present the natural and cultural characteristics of different regions, including Oceania, in their educational sections and discussions on international geography and cultural contexts.

# 國教署推高中自治培力研習營 跨界深化校園民主與多元公共參與

> 教育部國教署於北中南辦理學生自治培力研習營，聚焦校園治理與審議民主，結合多元議題提升青年公共參與素養。

教育部國民及學前教育署為深化全國高階中等學校學生的民主素養、公共參與意識以及校園治理能力，於八月期間分別在北部、中部與南部三區正式辦理「一一五年度全國高階中等學校學生自治及參與培力研習營」。根據公開報導資料顯示，本次研習活動共邀集了近三百名來自全臺各地的學生自治組織代表齊聚一堂。
整體課程規劃以學生自治實務為核心架構，全面融入審議民主機制、參與式預算概念以及公共議題實質討論等豐富內容，透過多元化的課程模組與實務演練環節，引導青年學子從切身的校園生活經驗出發，學習如何敏銳發掘校園問題、凝聚同儕共識並進一步提出具體可行的解決方案，藉此實質提升學生自治組織的運作效能與公共參與視野。
在課程主題與培育重點方面，國教署進一步說明，本次研習採取分割槽辦理模式，核心主軸定調為「培養民主參與能力」，研習內容所涵蓋的範疇極為廣泛，包括人權教育推廣、多元族群理解與反歧視理念、多元性別認同與性別平等實踐、學生權利維護以及兒童權利公約相關精神等多元面向。研習營期望透過系統性的培訓，協助高中代表建立完善的校園公共討論基礎，讓學子在面對校園治理事務時能兼顧多元價值與程序正義。
與此同時，國內各類教育團體與文教機構亦持續透過不同面向充實青年學習資源，例如臺灣生態教育推廣協會協同智然文教、自然醒文教及旅行社等單位推展多元營隊教育，展示出校外教育實踐在培養青少年多元認知上的並行發展。
除了國內教育體系積極推動校園自治與學生參與外，國際公共議題與區域視野亦是公共事務討論中不可或缺的一環。相關時事評論平臺如經濟通專題節目在分析國際局勢時指出，全球地緣政治板塊如中東情勢及國際大國博弈發展，皆深刻影響著國際秩序與區域合作的走向；而各大公眾資訊平臺在教育板塊與國際地理、人文脈絡的討論中，也持續呈現大洋洲等不同區域的自然與文化特色。
綜合各方資訊觀察，現代教育不僅著重於校園內自治實務與學生權益保障的落實，亦持續透過多方參與式學習，引導學生在關注在地自治治理的同時，建立起對人權、多元平權與國際重大脈動的全面理解。
