  # Taiwan's Grassroots Sports Education Boom: Baseball and Volleyball Tournaments Advance in Parallel
  
  > Chiayi County youth baseball tournament attracts MLB scouts, while Pingtung hosts its inaugural Indigenous Cup volleyball championship, showcasing Taiwan's trend of equally prioritizing grassroots sports and cultural education.
  
  The Chiayi County Education Outlook Association, in response to the first "Community Sports Club Promotion Program" by the Ministry of Education's National Fitness Administration, held the "Chiayi County Education Outlook Association Cup National Youth Baseball Invitational Tournament" from August 9 to 12 at Puzi Baseball Field, attracting 12 youth baseball teams from across Taiwan. This year's tournament specially invited a Cincinnati Reds MLB scout to attend in person and cheer on the young grassroots players, bringing greater attention to local baseball dreams. Dongshi Junior High School ultimately claimed the championship, demonstrating Chiayi County's solid achievements in youth baseball training.
  
  Meanwhile, Pingtung County hosted the "2026 1st National Indigenous Cup Volleyball Championship" on August 8 to 9 at National Pingtung University, co-organized by the Pingtung County Government and the Social Education and Cultural Foundation, inviting teams from indigenous townships and districts across the nation to compete. The Pingtung County Government Sports Development Center noted that volleyball has long been one of the most popular sports among indigenous communities, and this tournament aims to promote grassroots sports participation among indigenous peoples, strengthen emotional bonds within the community, and preserve indigenous volleyball culture, creating the highest-level volleyball competition stage for indigenous communities.
  
  Although the two tournaments involve different sports and regions, both reflect Taiwan's direction of integrating grassroots sports with education. The Chiayi County youth baseball tournament introduces international scouting resources, opening more possibilities for young athletes, while the Pingtung Indigenous Cup focuses on cultural preservation and community cohesion, showcasing the special significance of sports within indigenous communities. Education observers believe that such tournaments not only provide competitive platforms for athletes but also serve the functions of community connection and cultural preservation, representing a concrete practice of diverse development in Taiwan's sports education.

# 臺灣基層體育教育熱潮 棒球排球賽事並行推動

> 嘉義縣青少棒賽吸引MLB球探觀戰，屏東首屆原鄉杯排球賽同期登場，展現臺灣基層體育與文化教育並重發展趨勢。

嘉義縣教育展望協會響應運動部全民運動署首屆「推動社群運動俱樂部計畫」，於8月9日至12日在朴子棒球場舉辦「嘉義縣教育展望協會盃全國青少棒邀請賽」，吸引全臺12支青少棒隊伍參賽。本屆賽事特別邀請美國職棒大聯盟紅人隊球探親臨現場觀戰，為基層小球員加油，讓地方棒球夢想獲得更多關注。最終東石國中奪得冠軍，展現嘉義縣在青少棒培訓上的扎實成果。
與此同時，屏東縣於8月8日至9日在國立屏東大學舉辦「2026年第1屆全國原鄉盃排球錦標賽」，由屏東縣政府與財團法人社教文化基金會共同主辦，邀集全國原住民族鄉（區）隊伍齊聚參賽。屏東縣政府體育發展中心指出，排球長期是原住民族部落最受歡迎的運動之一，此項賽事旨在推廣原住民族全民運動風氣，凝聚族人情感交流，並傳承原鄉排球文化，打造原住民族最高層級的排球競技舞臺。
兩項賽事雖屬不同運動專案與地區，但都體現臺灣基層體育與教育結合的發展方向。嘉義縣青少棒賽引入國際球探資源，為年輕選手開啟更多可能性；屏東原鄉杯則著重文化傳承與部落凝聚，展現體育在原住民社群中的特殊意義。教育觀察人士認為，此類賽事不僅提供選手競技平臺，更承擔社群連結與文化儲存的功能，是臺灣體育教育多元發展的具體實踐。
