  # Africa Business Forum Focuses on Regional Integration; France-Africa Cooperation Deepens West Africa Investments
  
  > Biashara Afrika 2026 and the France-Africa innovation cooperation framework focus on regional economic integration, with the West African market attracting multinational investments, the AfCFTA Secretariat participating in dialogue, and French enterprises continuing to deepen their footprint in West Africa.
  
  Biashara Afrika 2026 was held recently, focusing on African regional economic integration and cross-border trade development. During the event, Stephen Jennings, Founder and CEO of Rendeavour, Yomi Ademola, West Africa Chair of Rendeavour, and H.E. Wamkele Mene, Secretary-General of the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) Secretariat, attended together to engage in dialogue regarding market opportunities in West Africa and the promotion of intra-African trade. Reported by the Economic Daily News, this forum demonstrates that African commercial activities are progressively strengthening regional connectivity and attracting international corporate attention to West Africa's economic potential.
  
  Meanwhile, economic cooperation between France and Africa continues to deepen. According to a report by Initium Media, under the "Africa-France Partnerships for Innovation and Growth" framework, French enterprises such as Orange, TotalEnergies, CMA CGM, Veolia, and Canal+ remain key investment pillars for France in the African economy, especially in the West African region. These companies span telecommunications, energy, logistics, environmental services, and media, demonstrating that France-West Africa economic and trade relations have a diversified industrial foundation rather than relying on a single sector.
  
  From a historical perspective, StoryStudio notes that Africa is not "a" country, but a diverse continent composed of multiple nations. The historical partition during the colonial era has shaped modern Africa's economic structure and trade patterns. Today, the promotion of the AfCFTA is attempting to break past fragmented patterns, promoting cross-border trade and industrial integration. The focus on the West African market by companies such as Rendeavour also reflects the region's importance in Africa's economic landscape.
  
  Taken together, African commercial activities are exhibiting a multi-track development trend: on one hand, Africa is strengthening regional connectivity internally through the free trade area mechanism; on the other hand, external actors like France continue to invest in West Africa, forming an economic and trade landscape of internal and external interaction. However, different media outlets place different emphasis on Africa's economic development: the Economic Daily News focuses on business forums and corporate participation, Initium Media looks closely at the investment layout of French companies, and StoryStudio provides historical and structural context. The three complement each other, presenting the diverse facets of Africa's economic development.

At the same time, whether economic and trade relations between France and Africa will adjust in response to global geopolitical shifts also warrants attention.

# 非洲商貿論壇聚焦區域整合 法非合作深化西非投資

> 非洲商貿論壇2026與法非創新合作論壇聚焦區域經濟整合，西非市場吸引跨國企業投資，非洲大陸自由貿易區秘書處參與對話，法國企業持續深化西非佈局。

非洲商貿論壇2026（Biashara Afrika 2026）近日舉行，聚焦非洲區域經濟整合與跨國貿易發展。活動中，Rendeavour 創辦人兼行政總裁 Stephen Jennings、Rendeavour 西非主席 Yomi Ademola，與非洲大陸自由貿易區秘書處秘書長 Wamkele Mene 閣下共同出席，就西非市場機會與非洲內部貿易推動進行對話。此論壇由經濟日報報導，呈現非洲商貿活動正逐步強化區域連結，並吸引國際企業關注西非經濟潛力。
與此同時，法國與非洲的經濟合作亦持續深化。端傳媒報導指出，在「Africa-France Partnerships for Innovation and Growth」框架下，Orange、TotalEnergies、CMA CGM、Veolia、Canal+ 等法國企業，仍是法國在非洲經濟中，尤其是西非地區的重要投資支點。這些企業涵蓋電信、能源、物流、環境服務與媒體等領域，顯示法國與西非的經貿關係具有多元產業基礎，並非單一領域依賴。
從歷史脈絡觀察，故事 StoryStudio 指出，非洲並非「一個」國家，而是由多個國家組成的多元大陸。過去殖民時期的瓜分歷史，影響了當代非洲的經濟結構與貿易模式。如今，非洲大陸自由貿易區的推動，正試圖打破過往分割格局，促進跨國貿易與產業整合。Rendeavour 等企業聚焦西非市場，亦反映該區域在非洲經濟版圖中的重要性。
綜合來看，非洲商貿活動正呈現多軌並行的發展態勢：一方面，非洲內部透過自由貿易區機制強化區域連結；另一方面，法國等外部勢力持續在西非投資，形成內外互動的經貿格局。然而，不同媒體對非洲經濟發展的側重點有所不同：經濟日報聚焦商貿論壇與企業參與，端傳媒關注法國企業的投資佈局，而故事 StoryStudio 則提供歷史與結構性背景。三者互補，呈現非洲經濟發展的多元面向。
後續觀察將聚焦於非洲大陸自由貿易區的具體落實進度，以及跨國企業在西非市場的投資決策是否隨區域整合而擴大。同時，法國與非洲的經貿關係是否隨全球地緣政治變化而調整，亦值得關注。
