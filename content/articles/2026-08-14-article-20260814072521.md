  # Climate Change Impacts the Americas: Banana-Producing Regions and the World Cup Face Threats
  
  > Banana-producing regions in Latin America are threatened by climate change, and the USA-Mexico-Canada World Cup has been rescheduled due to extreme heat, drawing international attention to climate impacts across the Americas.
  
  According to a report titled *Going Bananas: How Climate Change Threatens the World's Favourite Fruit*, released by the international nonprofit organization Christian Aid in May 2025, the world's primary banana-producing regions—Latin America and the Caribbean—will face severe threats by 2080.
  
  The report reveals the profound impact of climate change on agriculture in the Americas. As a key economic crop, banana production is already confronting challenges such as rising temperatures and increasingly frequent extreme weather, which could lead to significant declines in yield and subsequently affect local farmers' livelihoods and the global banana supply chain.
  
  The impact of climate change on the Americas extends beyond agriculture. According to reports, the 2026 FIFA World Cup co-hosted by the United States, Canada, and Mexico has also drawn attention due to high-temperature concerns. *twreporter* noted that extreme heat has already altered the world's most-watched sporting event, as the World Cup faces the challenge of excessively high temperatures. This reflects how climate change has permeated every aspect of life in the Americas, from agricultural production to major international sporting events, all of which require reassessment of response strategies.
  
  Furthermore, a report titled *Beyond Borders: The Role of Climate Change in Conflict and Displacement*, published by the Environmental Justice Foundation (EJF), points out that climate change is already triggering social conflicts and population displacement in the Americas and other regions. Citing the perspective of retired U.S. Marine Corps Brigadier General Stephen A. Cheney, the report emphasizes that climate change has become a security issue, and some areas in the Americas may see heightened social tensions due to resource shortages.
  
  KGI Futures also noted in its August 2026 market analysis that investment market volatility risks are rising in the face of uncertainties surrounding the U.S. election and global climate anomalies. This demonstrates that climate change is not merely an environmental issue but has become a significant factor affecting the economy and financial markets in the Americas. Experts recommend that countries in the Americas strengthen their climate adaptation capacity and invest in green energy transitions to address increasingly severe climate challenges.

# 氣候變遷衝擊美洲 香蕉產區與世界盃面臨威脅

> 拉丁美洲香蕉產區受氣候變遷威脅、美加墨世界盃因高溫改期，國際社會關注美洲氣候衝擊。

根據國際公益組織 Christian Aid 於 2025 年 5 月發布的報告《Going Bananas: How Climate Change Threatens the World's Favourite Fruit》指出，全球最主要的香蕉產區——拉丁美洲與加勒比海地區，到 2080 年將面臨嚴重威脅。
該報告揭示氣候變遷對美洲農業的深遠影響，香蕉作為當地重要經濟作物，其生產正面臨氣溫上升、極端天氣頻繁等挑戰，可能導致產量大幅下降，進而影響當地農民生計與全球香蕉供應鏈。
氣候變遷對美洲的影響不僅限於農業。根據報導，2026 年美加墨足球世界盃也因高溫問題受到關注。英國《報導者》（twreporter）指出，高溫已改變全球關注程度最高的運動賽事，美加墨世界盃的舉辦面臨氣溫過高的挑戰。這反映出氣候變遷已滲透至美洲各個層面，從農業生產到大型國際賽事均需重新評估應對策略。
此外，環境正義基金會（EJF）發布的報告《超越疆界——氣候變遷在衝突與流離中扮演的角色》進一步指出，氣候變遷正在美洲等地引發社會衝突與人口流離失所問題。報告引用美國海軍陸戰隊退役準將 Stephen A. Cheney 的觀點，強調氣候變遷已成為安全議題，美洲部分地區可能因資源短缺而加劇社會緊張。
凱基期貨在 2026 年 8 月的市場分析中也提到，面對美國大選變數與全球氣候異常，投資市場波動風險升溫。這顯示氣候變遷不僅是環境議題，更已成為影響美洲經濟與金融市場的重要因素。專家建議，美洲各國需加強氣候調適能力，並投資綠色能源轉型，以應對日益嚴峻的氣候挑戰。
