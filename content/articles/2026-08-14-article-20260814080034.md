  # EU Promotes Global Education Framework, Combining Sustainable Design to Cultivate Innovative Talent
  
  > The EU North-South Centre emphasizes four dimensions—knowledge, skills, values, and attitudes—while Taiwan's National United University practices sustainable design education through the SD4SD model, showcasing an innovative pathway for higher education to address global challenges.
  
  In the face of global sustainability issues such as climate change, resource depletion, and the circular economy, higher education bears the important mission of cultivating future talent. The North-South Centre of the Council of Europe proposed a global education framework in 2010, emphasizing four core dimensions—knowledge, skills, values, and attitudes—aiming to promote cross-cultural understanding and awareness of sustainable development through education. Promoted by an institution under the Council of Europe, the framework seeks to ensure that learners not only master professional knowledge but also develop the values and practical abilities needed to confront global challenges.
  
  Meanwhile, Taiwan's National United University (NUUID) recently showcased its sustainable design education model centered on "SD4SD (Sustainable Design for Sustainable Development)" at the 2026 Asia-Pacific Sustainability Expo. The university actively integrates sustainability concepts into teaching, guiding students to start from real-world social issues and combine creativity, technology, and design thinking to propose innovative products that balance market value with sustainability principles. The university stated that it hopes to demonstrate the practical outcomes of sustainable design education to all sectors, enabling more people to see how education can serve as a vital force driving sustainable development.
  
  Euronews, the European Union's leading international news channel, has long adhered to cross-party and independent principles, broadcasting global news in 19 languages and continuously monitoring Europe's policy progress in education and sustainable development. Analysis indicates that the EU has recently been actively promoting parallel green and digital transitions, with the education system regarded as a key pillar for achieving the 2030 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). The four-dimension framework proposed by the EU North-South Centre aligns with the education reform directions of member states, emphasizing that learners must possess core competencies such as critical thinking, cross-cultural communication, and sustainability awareness.
  
  However, different education systems still have differences in their practical approaches. Taiwan's National United University's SD4SD model focuses on design thinking and industry integration, enabling students to solve real problems through hands-on practice, while the EU framework places greater emphasis on cultivating values and attitudes, highlighting civic awareness and global responsibility. The two approaches have different emphases, yet they demonstrate that global higher education is moving toward interdisciplinary, practice-oriented, and value-driven directions when addressing sustainability issues.
  
  Observing subsequent developments, exchanges between the EU and Asian academia in the field of sustainability education are becoming increasingly frequent. The 2026 Asia-Pacific Sustainability Expo has become an important platform for showcasing regional educational innovation achievements, while continued reporting by media such as Euronews on related topics also helps raise public awareness of sustainability education issues. In the future, how to further integrate knowledge transmission, skill development, and value shaping will be a common challenge facing higher education institutions worldwide.

# 歐盟推動全球教育框架 結合永續設計培育創新人才

> 歐盟南北中心強調知識、技能、價值與態度四大面向，臺灣聯合大學以 SD4SD 模式實踐永續設計教育，展現高等教育因應全球議題的創新路徑。

面對氣候變遷、資源耗竭與迴圈經濟等全球永續議題，高等教育正肩負培育未來人才的重要使命。歐盟南北中心（North-South Centre of the Council of Europe）於 2010 年提出全球教育框架，強調知識、技能、價值與態度四個核心層面，旨在透過教育促進跨文化理解與永續發展意識。該框架由歐洲委員會（Council of Europe）所屬機構推動，目標是讓學習者不僅掌握專業知識，更能培養面對全球挑戰所需的價值觀與實踐能力。
與此同時，臺灣國立聯合大學（NUUID）近期於 2026 亞太永續博覽會中，展示其以「SD4SD（Sustainable Design for Sustainable Development）」為核心的永續設計教育模式。該校積極將永續理念融入教學，引導學生從真實社會問題出發，結合創意、科技與設計思維，提出兼具市場價值與永續理念的創新產品。校方表示，希望向各界展示永續設計教育的實踐成果，讓更多人看見教育如何成為推動永續發展的重要力量。
Euronews 作為歐盟首屈一指的國際新聞頻道，長期以跨黨派與獨立經營為原則，透過 19 種語言播報全球新聞，並持續關注歐洲在教育與永續發展領域的政策進展。分析指出，歐盟近年積極推動綠色轉型與數位轉型雙軌並行，教育體系被視為實現 2030 年永續發展目標（SDGs）的關鍵支柱。歐盟南北中心所提出的四大面向框架，正與各會員國的教育改革方向相互呼應，強調學習者需具備批判思考、跨文化溝通與永續意識等核心能力。
然而，不同教育體系在實踐路徑上仍存有差異。臺灣聯合大學的 SD4SD 模式著重於設計思維與產業接軌，讓學生透過實作解決真實問題；而歐盟框架則更側重價值觀與態度的培養，強調公民意識與全球責任。兩種路徑各有側重，但也顯示出全球高等教育在面對永續議題時，正朝向跨領域、重實作、強價值的方向發展。
觀察後續發展，歐盟與亞洲學界在永續教育領域的交流日益頻繁。2026 亞太永續博覽會成為展示區域教育創新成果的重要平臺，而 Euronews 等媒體對相關議題的持續報導，也有助於提升公眾對永續教育議題的關注。未來，如何進一步整合知識傳授、技能培養與價值塑造，將是各國高等教育面臨的共同課題。
