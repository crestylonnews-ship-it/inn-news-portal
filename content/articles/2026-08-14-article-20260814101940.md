  # Taiwan's Biomedical Therapies Advance Toward Multinational Clinical Trials as Local Public Health Governance and National Tech Budgets Move Forward Simultaneously
  
  > Taiwan's new biotech drug development has been published in an international journal with plans for multinational Phase III trials underway, while local public health governance achievements have garnered international smart city recognition, and the central government has allocated a record-high science and technology budget to accelerate the implementation of smart applications.
  
  In the fields of public health and biomedical research and development, biotech R&D institutions continue to advance clinical progress in cancer treatments. According to public biotech industry disclosures and media reports, PTS-100, a new human liver cancer drug developed by G-Med Biotechnology—a Taiwan subsidiary of Gongwin Biopharm—has achieved a critical breakthrough. Following the completion of patient enrollment for its Phase II clinical trial in June 2026, specific case study results regarding its application in human liver tumor treatments were officially published in the renowned international medical journal, the *Journal of Hepatocellular Carcinoma* (JHC).
  
  Gongwin Biopharm noted that PTS-100 serves as the core product of the company's "Intratumoral Drug Ablation (IDA)" platform. As a novel tumor ablation agent, its mechanism of action primarily induces tumor necrosis. Regarding subsequent R&D and regulatory milestones, the company plans to complete the data collection for primary efficacy endpoints in the third quarter and expects to launch multinational, multicenter Phase III clinical trials across Taiwan, Japan, Thailand, and the United States, thereby accelerating the global rollout of the new drug.
  
  In the areas of grassroots public health and local governance practices, local governments have also demonstrated concrete results in combining digital technology with health governance. The Yunlin County Government held an executive and county affairs meeting on August 6, where Deputy Magistrate Hsieh Shu-ya led 23 department heads to present the administration's achievements for the first half of 2026. The county team earned domestic and international recognition across multiple public sectors, including public health, smart governance, social welfare, environmental sustainability, and education. During the meeting, the Deputy Magistrate and department heads jointly presented two major international honors: the "Full Smart Community Certification" and the "2026 Global Top 7 Smart Communities" certificate awarded by the Intelligent Community Forum (ICF).
  
  These achievements indicate that the local administration continues to closely link digital technology with public health and industrial transformation through cross-departmental collaboration, striving to respond to the diverse needs of grassroots residents in healthcare and public services.
  
  Concurrently, the central government has expanded the injection of public resources to provide policy support for upgrading biomedical sciences, forward-looking technologies, and public health systems. The Executive Yuan has finalized the compilation of the 2027 central government general budget proposal. The National Science and Technology Council announced that next year's science and technology budget (excluding national defense) reaches NT$182.3 billion. Compared to NT$166.5 billion in fiscal year 2026, this represents an increase of approximately NT$15.8 billion—an annual growth rate of 9.47 percent—setting a new historical high for the total science and technology budget.

# 臺灣生醫療法邁向跨國臨床 地方公衛治理與國家科技預算同步推進

> 臺灣生技新藥研發獲國際期刊刊登並規畫跨國三期試驗，地方公衛治理成果獲國際智慧城市肯定，中央亦編列創新高科技預算加速智慧應用落地。

在公共衛生與生醫技術研發領域，生技研發機構持續推進癌症治療的臨床進展。根據生技產業公開資訊與媒體報導，共信-KY 旗下臺灣子公司共信醫藥科技研發之人體肝癌新藥「PTS 100」取得關鍵突破。該新藥繼二零二六年六月完成第二期臨床試驗收案後，其應用於人體肝癌腫瘤治療的具體病例研究成果，正式獲得國際知名醫學期刊《肝細胞癌期刊》（Journal of Hepatocellular Carcinoma，JHC）刊登發表。
共信醫藥指出，PTS 100 為該公司「瘤內藥物消融（IDA）」平臺的核心產品，屬於新型腫瘤消融劑，其作用機制主要為誘導腫瘤壞死。針對後續研發與法規進度，該公司規畫於第三季完成主要療效指標的資料收集作業，並預計啟動涵蓋臺灣、日本、泰國及美國等地的多國多中心第三期臨床試驗，以加速推動新藥的全球佈局程式。
在基層公共衛生與地方治理實踐方面，地方政府亦展現將數位科技與健康治理結合的具體成效。雲林縣政府於八月六日舉行主管會報暨縣務會議，副縣長謝淑亞率領二十三局處首長呈現二零二六上半年度的施政成果。縣府團隊在公共衛生、智慧治理、社會福利、環境永續及教育等多項公共領域獲得國內外評比肯定。在會議中，副縣長與局處首長共同獻上兩項重要國際認證，分別為國際智慧城市論壇（ICF）頒發的「完整智慧城市社群認證」以及「二零二六全球 Top 7 智慧城市」證書。
相關成果顯示地方行政團隊持續透過跨局處協作，將數位科技與公共衛生、產業轉型緊密連結，致力於回應基層民眾在健康照護與公共服務上的多元需求。
與此同時，中央政府層級亦擴大公共資源挹注，為生醫、前瞻科技與公衛體系升級提供政策支撐。行政院已完成編列二零二七年中央政府總預算案，國家科學及技術委員會宣佈，明年編列之科技預算（不含國防）達一千八百二十三億元，相較於二零二六年度的一千六百六十五億元，增加約一百五十八億元，年成長幅度達百分之九點四七，科技預算總額創下歷年新高
