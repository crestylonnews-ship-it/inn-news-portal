  # Taiwan Pharmaceutical Industry and Public Health Issues: Recent Developments
  
  > Pu Yuan Biotech's inhalation device receives TFDA approval, supporting L606's global rollout; psychiatrist warns Alzheimer's early warning signs extend beyond memory loss, with depression also a risk indicator.
  
  Pu Yuan Biotech, a subsidiary recently brought under the Guoyi (6875) Group, was reported on August 4, 2026, to have obtained a Class II medical device license from the Taiwan Food and Drug Administration (TFDA). The company's self-developed smart inhalation device (project code: PN105) was approved under its own brand "Yi Pu Neng," marking the first regulatory milestone for the Guoyi Group since acquiring Pu Yuan Biotech. With Guoyi focusing on "drug formulations" and Pu Yuan Biotech on "precision drug delivery devices," the two have jointly built an integrated pharmaceutical-device dual platform; this approval signifies the transition from strategic planning to the commercialization phase.
  
  Guoyi further stated that "Yi Pu Neng" has completed the device preparations required for L606's future launch in Taiwan and provides a comprehensive product solution for L606's global commercialization, with continued promotion of related applications expected. This progress demonstrates that Taiwan's pharmaceutical industry continues to achieve breakthroughs in the integration of innovative devices and pharmaceuticals, injecting new momentum into the regional public health medical device supply chain.
  
  Meanwhile, as Taiwan's population continues to age, dementia has become a significant global public health issue. On August 4, 2026, a psychiatrist noted that early warning signs of Alzheimer's disease extend beyond memory loss; depressive symptoms may also be an early indicator of dementia and depression risk. The physician recommended four strategies to reduce the risk of dementia and depression: regular exercise, social interaction, cognitive training, and management of cardiovascular risk factors. This reminder underscores the importance of strengthening early screening and preventive education within public health systems as Asia confronts its aging society.
  
  As one of the faster-aging societies in East Asia, Taiwan stands to benefit from the wider dissemination of relevant health education, which can raise public awareness of neurodegenerative diseases and promote the rational allocation of community healthcare resources.
  
  Taken together, these two developments reflect positive progress in both pharmaceutical industry innovation and public health education in Taiwan. Pu Yuan Biotech's TFDA approval not only strengthens the domestic medical device supply chain but also provides potential drug delivery device support for new drug development targeting conditions such as Alzheimer's; meanwhile, the psychiatrist's clarification of early warning signs reflects growing societal attention to aging-related health issues. Going forward, the global commercialization progress of L606 and the Taiwan Ministry of Health and Welfare's clinical application regulations for new inhalation devices are developments to watch, as they will influence the interaction between public health policy and the pharmaceutical industry across Asia.

# 臺灣醫藥產業與公共衛生議題近期動態

> 普元生技吸入裝置獲TFDA許可，助攻L606全球佈局；精神科醫提醒阿茲海默症早期警訊不止健忘，憂鬱亦為風險徵兆。

甫納入國邑（6875）集團的子公司普元生技，於2026年8月4日傳出取得衛生福利部食品藥物管理署（TFDA）第二等級醫療器材許可證的訊息。該公司自主開發的智慧型呼吸引動吸入裝置（專案程式碼：PN105），以自有品牌「邑普能」透過核可，成為國邑集團納入普元生技後的首項法規成果。國邑以「藥物劑型」為核心，普元生技則以「精準給藥器械」為核心，雙方共同建構藥械合一雙平臺，此次取證代表雙平臺由策略佈局正式進入商業化階段。
國邑進一步表示，「邑普能」已完成L606未來在臺灣上市所需的器械準備，也為L606的全球商業化提供完整的產品方案，後續亦將持續推動相關應用。此項進展顯示臺灣醫藥產業在創新器械與藥物整合領域持續取得突破，並為區域公共衛生醫療器材供應鏈注入新動能。
另一方面，隨著臺灣人口老化趨勢持續，失智症已成為全球重要公共衛生議題。2026年8月4日有精神科醫師指出，阿茲海默症（Alzheimer's disease）的早期警訊不僅限於健忘，憂鬱症狀同樣可能是失智與憂鬱風險的早期徵兆。醫師建議民眾可透過四大策略降低失智與憂鬱風險，包括規律運動、社互動動、認知訓練及控制心血管危險因子。此項提醒呼應了亞洲地區面對高齡化社會時，公共衛生體系需強化早期篩檢與預防教育的重要性。
臺灣作為東亞高齡化進展較快的社會之一，相關衛教資訊的普及有助於提升民眾對神經退化性疾病的認識，並促進社群醫療資源的合理配置。
綜合兩項動態，臺灣在醫藥產業創新與公共衛生教育方面均呈現積極進展。普元生技取得TFDA許可，不僅強化本土醫療器材產業鏈，亦為阿茲海默症等新藥開發提供潛在的給藥裝置支援；而精神科醫對早期警訊的說明，則反映社會對高齡健康議題的關注度提升。未來可觀察L606全球商業化進度，以及臺灣衛福部對新型吸入裝置的臨床應用規範，這些發展將影響亞洲地區公共衛生政策與醫藥產業的互動模式。
