  # UK, France, Germany Compete for Influence in Africa; Trade, Migration, Security Take Center Stage
  
  > This week, the German Chancellor and British Foreign Secretary separately visited Africa, as European nations seek to expand their engagement on trade, migration, and security issues, contrasting with competition from China and the US.
  
  The German Chancellor and British Foreign Secretary visited multiple African countries separately this week, signaling that major European nations are actively strengthening their diplomatic and economic footprint on the African continent. Reports from VOA Cantonese indicate that the UK, post-Brexit, is seeking new partnerships beyond the EU, while Germany and France are pursuing high-level visits to boost their engagement on trade, migration control, and regional security. This move is seen as a key strategy for Europe in responding to the global realignment of power, particularly as China and the US continue to expand their investments and influence in Africa, with Europe hoping to maintain its traditional advantages through diversified cooperation models.
  
  The report also notes that the African continent is facing economic competition from both China and the United States. VOA Cantonese data shows that China has long deepened its ties with Africa through infrastructure investment and trade agreements, while the US focuses on security cooperation and democratic governance. European nations'密集 visits this time aim to offer differentiated approaches, such as emphasizing sustainable development financing, trade facilitation for small and medium-sized enterprises, and addressing migration issues at their source. The UK's Financial Times previously analyzed that Africa's rapidly growing young population and digital economy potential are attracting countries to compete for positioning, and Europe's advantage lies in the linguistic, educational, and legal system connections established through its colonial history.
  
  Analysts observe that African governments take a pragmatic approach to competition among external powers, tending to maximize their benefits within multilateral frameworks. Regional powers such as South Africa and Nigeria have actively negotiated trade agreements with China, the US, and Europe simultaneously, avoiding over-reliance on any single partner. Economists warn that Africa's infrastructure gap remains significant; the World Bank estimates that approximately USD 130 billion in investment is needed annually, which cannot be met by Europe or any single country alone. Additionally, climate change, food security, and debt sustainability have become new focal points for transnational cooperation. The IMF has recommended that African countries strengthen fiscal reforms and industrial diversification to attract long-term investment.
  
  The implementation progress of the African Union and EU's "Post-2022 Addis Ababa Agenda" will affect the effectiveness of cooperation among all parties.

# 英法德競逐非洲影響力 貿易移民安全成焦點

> 德國總理與英國外交大臣本週相繼訪問非洲，歐洲國家試圖擴大在貿易、移民與安全議題上的參與度，與中美競爭形成對比。

德國總理與英國外交大臣本週分別前往非洲多國訪問，顯示歐洲主要國家正積極強化在非洲大陸的外交與經濟佈局。美國之音粵語部報導指出，英國在脫歐後正尋求歐盟以外的新夥伴關係，而德國與法國亦透過高層訪問，試圖在貿易、移民管控及區域安全等議題上提升參與度。此舉被視為歐洲應對全球權力重組的關鍵策略，尤其在中國與美國持續擴大對非投資與影響力的背景下，歐洲希望以多元合作模式維持其傳統優勢。
報導同時提到，非洲大陸正面臨來自中美兩國的經濟競爭。美國之音粵部資料顯示，中國長期透過基礎建設投資與貿易協定深化與非洲的聯絡，美國則側重於安全合作與民主治理議題。歐洲國家此次密集出訪，意在提出差異化方案，例如強調可持續發展融資、中小企業貿易便利化，以及移民問題的原地解決。英國《金融時報》此前分析亦指出，非洲年輕人口快速成長與數位經濟潛力，正吸引各國爭相佈局，而歐洲的優勢在於既有殖民歷史所建立的語言、教育與法律體系連結。
分析人士觀察，非洲各國政府對外部勢力競爭持務實態度，傾向在多邊框架中爭取最大利益。南非、奈及利亞等區域大國已積極與中美歐同時洽談貿易協定，避免過度依賴單一夥伴。經濟學者提醒，非洲基礎建設缺口仍大，世界銀行估算每年需約1,300億美元投資，僅靠歐洲或單一國家難以滿足需求。此外，氣候變遷、糧食安全與債務可持續性成為跨國合作新焦點，國際貨幣基金組織建議非洲國家加強財政改革與產業多元化，以吸引長期投資。
未來非洲聯盟與歐盟的「2022年後亞的斯亞貝巴議程」落實進度，將影響各方合作成效。
