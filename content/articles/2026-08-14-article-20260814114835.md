  # Goldman Sachs: RMB Internationalization Requires Deeper Markets; US Tariffs Could Add $82.3 Billion in Costs
  
  > Goldman Sachs report notes RMB still needs deeper market conditions to become an international financing currency; JPMorgan analysis finds Trump's tariff plan would impose $82.3 billion in costs on US employers, while business community questions whether Taiwan would be the biggest beneficiary of a trade war.
  
  Goldman Sachs released a report in August 2026 stating that for the RMB to become a mainstream global financing channel, the cost advantage of panda bonds and dim sum bonds alone is insufficient; sustained economic growth and deeper market conditions are still required. Report analyst Chen Xinquan noted that benefiting from lower RMB financing costs and policy support, the issuance volume of panda bonds and dim sum bonds grew over 60% year-on-year in the first half of 2026, but in terms of both outstanding size and market depth, they remain limited compared to China's domestic bond market and mature G3 currency bond markets. Goldman Sachs emphasized that without deeper liquidity and broader investment options, RMB internationalization would struggle to make lasting progress.
  
  JPMorgan analysts, meanwhile, issued a warning regarding Trump's tariff plan, stating that if tariff measures are fully implemented, US employers would face direct cost burdens of up to $82.3 billion, not only driving up manufacturing and import costs but also potentially passing them on to consumers, further exacerbating inflationary pressures. This analysis aligns with Goldman Sachs' cautious outlook on the RMB internationalization process, indicating that the global economy is facing dual shifts in trade policy and monetary landscape. Taiwan's business community holds differing interpretations on the matter; some industry players believe the US-China trade war may not necessarily make Taiwan the biggest beneficiary—if global economic growth stalls due to trade friction, enterprises will reduce investment and factory expansion, and demand for Taiwan's machinery industry could be impacted, meaning Taiwan may not fully benefit in the long run.
  
  Synthesizing various perspectives, the RMB internationalization process still needs to overcome the bottleneck of insufficient market depth, and if US tariff policies are implemented, they will impose significant cost pressures on US enterprises and global supply chains. The business community remains cautious about Taiwan's role in the trade war, emphasizing the need to monitor the cascading effects of potential stalled global economic growth. Future observations will focus on the development of RMB bond market liquidity, the specific implementation details of US tariff policies, and Taiwan's industrial structural adjustment measures.

# 高盛：人民幣國際化需更深市場 美關稅或增823億美元成本

> 高盛報告指出人民幣成為國際融資貨幣仍需更深市場條件；摩根大通分析川普關稅計畫將使美國僱主承擔823億美元成本，工商界質疑臺灣未必是貿易戰最大受益者。

高盛集團於2026年8月發布報告指出，人民幣若要成為全球主流融資管道，僅憑熊貓債與點心債的融資成本優勢尚不足夠，仍需依賴經濟持續成長及更深層次的市場條件。報告分析師陳鑫泉表示，受惠於人民幣融資成本較低與政策支援，2026年上半年熊貓債與點心債發行規模年增逾60%，但無論存量規模或市場深度，與中國境內債券市場及成熟G3貨幣債券市場相比仍顯有限。高盛強調，若缺乏更深厚的流動性與更廣闊的投資選擇，人民幣國際化難以取得持久進展。
摩根大通分析師則針對川普關稅計畫提出警告，指出若關稅措施全面實施，美國僱主將面臨高達823億美元的直接成本負擔，不僅推高製造與進口成本，更可能轉嫁至消費者端，進一步加劇通膨壓力。此分析與高盛對人民幣國際化程式的審慎看法形成呼應，顯示全球經濟正面臨貿易政策與貨幣格局的雙重變動。臺灣工商界對此亦有不同解讀，有業者認為美陸貿易戰未必使臺灣成為最大受益者，若全球經濟成長因貿易摩擦停滯，企業將減少投資與擴廠，臺灣機械產業需求恐受衝擊，長遠而言臺灣未必全然有利。
綜合各方觀點，人民幣國際化程式仍須克服市場深度不足的瓶頸，而美國關稅政策若落實，將對美國企業與全球供應鏈產生顯著成本壓力。工商界對臺灣在貿易戰中的角色持保留態度，強調需關注全球經濟成長停滯可能帶來的連鎖影響。後續觀察將聚焦於人民幣債券市場流動性發展、美國關稅政策具體實施細節，以及臺灣產業結構調整的應對措施。
