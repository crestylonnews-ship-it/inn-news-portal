  # Middle East Conflict Disrupts Energy Supply Chains, Countries Accelerate Energy Transition Plans
  
  > Escalating geopolitical tensions in the Middle East have triggered global energy supply chain volatility. The International Energy Agency and Goldman Sachs note that nations are reassessing energy security strategies and accelerating renewable energy transitions, with China's early preparations demonstrating a clear advantage.
  
  Geopolitical conflicts in the Middle East continue to intensify, exerting a profound impact on global energy supply chains. On May 28, the International Energy Agency (IEA) released its "2026 World Energy Investment Report," highlighting that with shipping disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz, governments and enterprises worldwide are reassessing energy investment strategies, with energy security and the reliability of global trade flows emerging as focal points. The report emphasizes that the impact of Middle East conflicts extends beyond short-term supply interruptions, and will long-term influence the direction of energy policies across nations.
  
  Goldman Sachs' latest analysis identifies three key trends highlighted by the Middle East energy shock. First, solar energy enjoys structural bullishness; the 2022 energy crisis already accelerated global solar installations, with China having shifted its transition pathway from "coal to gas" to "coal to renewables" well before the crisis, and Europe similarly accelerating photovoltaic deployment. The Middle East conflict will drive economies further toward localized energy, aligning closely with the structural growth of solar and the bullish outlook on global electricity demand. Second, coal demand sees reinforcement as some nations temporarily resume coal-fired power generation to ensure energy supply stability. Third, developing nations face mounting pressure, as the funding and technological thresholds for energy transition pose greater challenges to weaker economies.
  
  China has demonstrated foresight in its energy security planning. The Columbia University Global Energy Policy Center estimates China's strategic oil reserves at approximately 1.4 billion barrels; while the exact scale remains undisclosed, it is sufficient to withstand short-term supply shocks. Data from the UK energy think tank Ember shows that in 2024, wind, solar, and hydroelectric projects accounted for approximately 31% of China's total power generation, marking significant progress in energy transition. Taiwan's Ministry of Economic Affairs also stated that it has immediately activated an energy emergency response team, preparing three major plans to stabilize energy supply and address potential impacts from the Middle East conflict.
  
  Wood Mackenzie's analysis focuses on local energy transition targets in the Middle East, noting that clean energy plans across Middle Eastern countries encompass emissions reduction, renewable energy deployment, carbon capture, and hydrogen development, with interrelated target designs. Some clean energy goals depend on the commercialization of low-carbon exports to be achieved. This perspective indicates that while Middle Eastern nations face geopolitical risks, they are also actively promoting energy structure transformation, though technological scaling and commercialization remain key challenges.
  
  A comprehensive review of various sources reveals that the Middle East conflict has become a catalyst for shifts in the global energy landscape. Nations are seeking to balance energy security with green transition, and future energy policy directions will continue to be influenced by geopolitics.

# 中東衝突衝擊能源供應鏈 各國加速能源轉型佈局

> 中東地緣衝突持續升溫，引發全球能源供應鏈動盪。國際能源署與高盛等機構指出，各國正重新審視能源安全戰略，加速推動可再生能源轉型，中國提前佈局展現優勢。

中東地區地緣衝突持續加劇，對全球能源供應鏈造成深遠影響。國際能源署（IEA）於5月28日發布《2026年世界能源投資報告》，指出在霍爾木茲海峽運輸受阻背景下，各國政府與企業正重新審視能源投資戰略，能源安全與全球貿易流通可靠性成為焦點。報告強調，中東衝突帶來的衝擊不僅限於短期供應中斷，更將長期影響各國能源政策走向。
高盛在最新分析中指出，中東能源衝擊凸顯三大趨勢。首先，太陽能獲結構性看漲，2022年能源危機已加速全球太陽能裝機，中國早於危機前便將轉型路徑從「煤轉氣」調整為「煤轉可再生能源」，歐洲同樣加速光伏部署。中東衝突將驅使各經濟體進一步向本地化能源傾斜，與太陽能結構性增長及全球電力需求看多判斷高度一致。其次，煤炭需求再強化，部分國家為確保能源供應穩定，暫時恢復燃煤發電。第三，發展中國家承壓，能源轉型資金與技術門檻使弱勢經濟體面臨更大挑戰。
中國在能源安全佈局上展現前瞻性。哥倫比亞大學全球能源政策中心估計，中國戰略儲備油約14億桶，雖未公開具體規模，但足以抵禦短期供應衝擊。英國能源智庫Ember資料顯示，2024年中國風能、太陽能與水電工程佔總發電量約31%，能源轉型進展顯著。臺灣經濟部亦表示，已即刻啟動能源應變小組，備妥三大方案穩固能源供應，應對中東戰事可能帶來的衝擊。
Wood Mackenzie分析則聚焦中東本地能源轉型目標，指出中東各國清潔能源計畫涵蓋減排、再生能源部署、碳捕捉與氫能發展，且目標設計相互關聯，部分清潔能源目標依賴低碳出口商業化才能達成。此觀點顯示中東國家在面對地緣風險同時，亦積極推動能源結構轉型，但技術規模化與商業化仍是關鍵挑戰。
綜合各來源觀察，中東衝突已成為全球能源格局變遷的催化劑，各國在確保能源安全與推動綠色轉型之間尋求平衡，未來能源政策走向將持續受地緣政治影響。
