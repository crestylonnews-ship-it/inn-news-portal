  # EU Advances Artificial Intelligence Policy, Emphasizing Excellence and Trust
  
  > The EU continues to promote its AI development strategy, emphasizing the need to ensure safety and fundamental rights while fostering research and industrial competitiveness. IEEE has also expressed its position on AI issues, and the European Political Community is paying attention to related matters.
  
  The European Commission clarified on its official website that artificial intelligence (AI) is transforming the way businesses operate, reshaping public services, and revolutionizing scientific development. As AI technology continues to evolve, the EU expects it to bring greater benefits to European citizens and enterprises. To maintain the EU's leadership position in the global AI landscape, the EU is advancing various initiatives to unlock its potential for development. The European Commission emphasized that its AI approach aims to promote excellence and trust by strengthening research and industrial capabilities while ensuring that safety and fundamental rights are safeguarded.
  
  According to relevant data from Taiwan's National Development Council, the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) has also expressed its position on AI-related issues. The data indicates that the EU plans to establish a representative body, specifically a European Agency for robots and artificial intelligence, to handle related technological development and regulatory matters. This move reflects the EU's desire to build a more comprehensive dedicated mechanism for AI governance to address rapidly evolving technological challenges.
  
  The European Political Community (EPC) has also incorporated AI-related issues into its discussion agenda. This political forum, composed of EU member states and other European countries, aims to strengthen coordination and cooperation across Europe on key issues. The development and regulatory framework of AI technology have become a shared focus among European nations, which hope to establish unified technical standards and ethical norms through regional cooperation mechanisms.
  
  The European Commission further explained that the core of its AI strategy lies in balancing innovation and risk management. On one hand, the EU is committed to maintaining its leading position in global AI competition by investing in research and development and supporting industrial innovation, ensuring that European enterprises can secure their place in the AI era. On the other hand, the EU stresses the importance of building trustworthy AI systems that protect citizens' fundamental rights, including principles of privacy, non-discrimination, and transparency. This approach of emphasizing both "excellence and trust" is regarded as a distinctive feature that sets the EU apart from other major AI-developing regions.
  
  Analysts point out that the EU's stringent stance on AI governance could have a profound impact on the global technology industry. Many multinational tech companies must comply with the EU's AI regulatory requirements when entering the European market. If this regulatory framework gains widespread adoption, it could become a reference standard for global AI governance. However, some argue that overly strict regulation may hinder the pace of innovation, and a careful balance must be struck.

# 歐盟推進人工智慧政策 強調卓越與信任並重

> 歐盟持續推動人工智慧發展策略，強調在促進研究與產業競爭力的同時，確保安全與基本權利。IEEE亦對AI議題表達立場，歐洲政治共同體關注相關議題。

歐盟委員會於官方網站闡明，人工智慧（AI）正在改變企業營運方式、重塑公共服務，並革命性地推動科學發展。隨著AI技術持續演進，歐盟預期其能為歐洲公民與企業帶來更多效益。為維持歐盟在全球AI領域的領導地位，歐盟正推動各項倡議，以釋放潛在發展動能。歐盟委員會強調，其AI方法旨在促進卓越與信任，透過強化研究與產業能力，同時確保安全與基本權利得到保障。
根據臺灣國家發展委員會相關資料顯示，國際電機電子工程師學會（IEEE）亦針對人工智慧相關議題表達立場。資料指出，歐盟規劃建立代表機構，應為機器人和人工智慧設定歐洲代表機構（European Agency），以專門處理相關技術發展與監管事務。此舉反映歐盟希望在AI治理架構上建立更完善的專責機制，以應對快速發展的技術挑戰。
歐洲政治共同體（European Political Community, EPC）亦將AI相關議題納入討論範圍。該政治論壇由歐盟成員國及歐洲其他國家組成，旨在加強歐洲地區在關鍵議題上的協調合作。AI技術發展與監管框架已成為歐洲各國共同關注的重點，各國期望透過區域合作機制，建立統一的技術標準與倫理規範。
歐盟委員會進一步說明，其AI策略的核心在於平衡創新與風險管理。一方面，歐盟致力於維持其在全球AI競爭中的領先地位，透過投資研究與開發、支援產業創新，確保歐洲企業能在AI時代佔有一席之地。另一方面，歐盟強調必須建立可信賴的AI系統，保護公民基本權利，包括隱私、非歧視與透明度等原則。這種「卓越與信任」並重的方法，被視為歐盟區別於其他主要AI發展地區的重要特色。
分析人士指出，歐盟在AI治理上的嚴格立場，可能對全球科技產業產生深遠影響。許多跨國科技企業在進入歐洲市場時，必須符合歐盟的AI規範要求。這種監管框架若獲得廣泛採用，可能成為全球AI治理的參考標準。然而，也有觀點認為，過於嚴格的監管可能影響創新速度，需要謹慎拿捏平衡點。
