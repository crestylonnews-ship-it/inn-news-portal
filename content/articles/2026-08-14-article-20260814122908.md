  # New Publication on Education Systems in the Americas; Cross-Border Education Information Platform Updated Simultaneously
  
  > Springer publishes the handbook "The Education Systems of the Americas," covering the education systems of countries in North, Central, and South America; EducationUSA and Taiwan's Ministry of Education simultaneously update international comparative statistics, providing the latest study abroad and policy information.
  
  Research on education systems in the Americas has seen recent developments. Springer Nature Link has published the handbook *The Education Systems of the Americas*, focusing on the education systems of North America, Central America, and South America, and including chapters on most countries within the region. Each chapter follows a unified structure, detailing the institutional development, organizational forms, and current trends of each country's education system from preschool through higher education, providing a systematic reference for academics and policymakers.
  
  Meanwhile, the EducationUSA network, under the U.S. Department of State, continues to provide information on American higher education. The network spans 175 countries and regions worldwide, with over 430 international student advising centers. The Taiwan site (EducationUSA Taiwan) clearly states that, as an official U.S. information source, it aims to provide accurate, comprehensive, objective, and up-to-date information on studying in the United States, helping Taiwanese students find suitable schools. The platform emphasizes the objectivity and timeliness of its information, serving as an important channel for Taiwanese students to learn about American higher education.
  
  Taiwan's Ministry of Education Statistics Division has also simultaneously released the *International Comparison of Education Statistical Indicators (2025 Edition)*. This publication collects and compiles domestic and international education statistical data, enabling various sectors to understand the educational development and differences among major countries, and providing the Ministry of Education and other organizations with references for formulating education policies and conducting research and analysis. The statistical data covers education indicators across multiple countries, facilitating cross-national education policy comparisons and academic research.
  
  The geographic scope of the Americas encompasses a vast region stretching from Coffee Island near the Arctic Circle to South Georgia Island and the South Sandwich Islands. The terrain, characterized by broad plains flanked by mountain ranges on both sides, also reflects the geographic context underlying education development across countries in the region. Research on education systems involves not only curricula and institutions but also requires consideration of regional geography, population distribution, and economic development among other multifaceted factors. In summary, while education systems across the Americas each have their own distinctive features, regional educational exchange and research are gradually deepening through academic publications, government information platforms, and international statistical comparisons. In the future, further cooperation among American countries in education policy, academic research, and student exchange is expected.

# 美洲教育系統研究新出版 跨國教育資訊平臺同步更新

> Springer出版《美洲教育系統》手冊，涵蓋北美、中美及南美各國教育體系；EducationUSA與臺灣教育部同步更新國際比較統計，提供最新留學與政策資訊。

關於美洲教育系統的研究近日有新進展。Springer Nature Link出版《美洲教育系統》（The Education Systems of the Americas）手冊，聚焦北美洲、中美洲及南美洲三區域的教育體系，收錄區域內多數國家的教育章節。各章節採用統一架構，詳述各國從學前至高等教育的制度發展、組織形態與現行趨勢，為學術界與政策制定者提供系統性參考資料。
與此同時，美國國務院所屬EducationUSA網路持續提供美國高等教育資訊。該網路遍佈全球175個國家與地區，設有逾430個國際學生諮商中心。臺灣站點（EducationUSA Taiwan）明確指出，其作為美國官方資訊來源，旨在提供準確、全面、客觀且最新的美國留學資訊，協助臺灣學生尋找適合的學校。此平臺強調資訊的客觀性與即時性，成為臺灣學生了解美國高等教育的重要管道。
臺灣教育部統計處亦同步推出《教育統計指標之國際比較（2025年版）》。該專刊蒐集國內外相關教育統計資料，彙編成冊，供各界瞭解主要國家教育發展及其差異情形，並提供教育部制訂教育政策及其他單位作為研究分析之參考。此統計資料涵蓋多國教育指標，有助於跨國教育政策比較與學術研究。
美洲地理範圍涵蓋從北極圈附近的咖啡館島至南喬治亞島與南桑威奇群島的廣闊區域，兩岸山脈夾持廣闊平原的地形特徵，亦反映區域內各國教育發展的地理背景。教育系統的研究不僅涉及課程與制度，亦需考量區域地理、人口分佈與經濟發展等多元因素。綜合觀察，美洲各國教育體系雖各有特色，但透過學術出版、政府資訊平臺與國際統計比較，區域教育交流與研究正逐步深化。未來可望進一步促進美洲各國在教育政策、學術研究及學生交流等方面的合作。
