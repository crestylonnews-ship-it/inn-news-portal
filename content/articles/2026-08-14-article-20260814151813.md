  # Pacific Islands Forum Concludes, South Pacific Strategic Position Draws Attention
  
  > The 54th Pacific Islands Forum concluded in the Solomon Islands, with member nations focusing on regional security and economic cooperation, as the strategic value of Pacific island nations draws international attention.
  
  According to UDN News, the 54th Pacific Islands Forum (Pacific Islands Forum), hosted by the Solomon Islands, concluded today. The forum comprises 18 member nations, including major members such as Australia and New Zealand, along with several Pacific island countries. During the closing session, leaders held closed-door meetings, with discussions primarily centered on the status of "dialogue partners" barred from participating in the forum this year, reflecting the region's sensitivity toward external power involvement.
  
  The geopolitical value of Pacific island nations is increasingly attracting international attention. According to an analysis by the Shiqi Qiqi blog, the impoverished South Pacific island nations are becoming a new battleground in international politics, emerging as a frontline region in the power competition between the United States and China. Though these islands are small in scale, they hold irreplaceable importance in terms of marine resources, shipping route security, and strategic positioning, attracting major powers to actively expand diplomatic and economic ties.
  
  Japan's diplomatic strategy in the region also warrants attention. Academic research indicates that after 2004, under the leadership of the Prime Minister's Office, Japan successively signed free trade agreements (FTAs) with ASEAN countries, Australia, and others, demonstrating Japan's active efforts to deepen relations with Pacific region nations through economic cooperation in response to regional shifts brought about by China's rise. This FTA diplomatic strategy has become a key means for Japan to maintain its influence in the Asia-Pacific.
  
  As the most important intergovernmental organization in the region, the Pacific Islands Forum's closed-door discussions reflect member nations' emphasis on safeguarding regional autonomy. The mechanism barring certain "dialogue partners" from participation reveals the islands' cautious stance toward excessive external interference. Australia and New Zealand, as major members, play a pivotal role in regional security and economic development, while also being key targets of competition among major powers seeking cooperation.
  
  Experts observe that Pacific island nations are at the center of shifting international politics. As issues such as climate change, marine resource development, and shipping route security grow increasingly important, the strategic value of these islands will continue to rise. How nations balance their relationships with major powers while preserving regional autonomy will be a critical challenge for the Pacific Islands Forum in the years ahead. Regional countries must also work through multilateral cooperation mechanisms to jointly address multiple challenges spanning security, economic, and environmental domains.

# 太平洋島國論壇閉幕 南太戰略地位受矚目

> 第54屆太平洋島國論壇於索羅門群島閉幕，成員國聚焦區域安全與經濟合作，太平洋島國戰略價值引發國際關注。

根據聯合新聞網報導，索羅門群島主辦的第54屆太平洋島國論壇（Pacific Islands Forum）於今日閉幕。該論壇擁有18個成員國，除澳洲和紐西蘭等主要成員國外，通常還有數個太平洋島國參與。閉幕期間各國領導人舉行閉門會議，主要討論議題聚焦於今年被禁止參加論壇的「對話夥伴」相關事宜，顯示區域內對於外部勢力參與的敏感態度。
太平洋島國的地緣政治價值日益受到國際社會矚目。根據志祺七七部落格分析，貧窮的南太平洋島國正掀起國際政治新戰場，成為美中國力競爭的前線區域。這些島國雖規模不大，但在海洋資源、航線安全及戰略位置上具有不可替代的重要性，吸引各大國積極拓展外交與經濟關係。
日本在該區域的外交策略亦值得關注。學術研究指出，2004年以後，在官邸主導下，日本與東協、澳洲等國相繼簽訂自由貿易協定（FTA），顯示日本積極透過經濟合作深化與太平洋區域國家的關係，以應對中國崛起帶來的區域格局變化。這種FTA外交策略成為日本在亞太地區維持影響力的重要手段。
太平洋島國論壇作為區域最重要的政府間組織，其閉門會議的討論內容反映成員國對於維護區域自主性的重視。被禁止參加的「對話夥伴」機制，顯示島國對於外部勢力過度介入的防範心態。澳洲和紐西蘭作為主要成員國，在區域安全與經濟發展中扮演關鍵角色，同時也是各大國爭取合作的物件。
專家觀察認為，太平洋島國正處於國際政治變局的中心位置。隨著氣候變遷、海洋資源開發及航線安全等議題日益重要，這些島國的戰略價值將持續提升。各國如何平衡與主要大國的關係，同時維護區域自主性，將是太平洋島國論壇未來面臨的重要課題。區域國家也需透過多邊合作機制，共同應對安全、經濟與環境等多重挑戰。
