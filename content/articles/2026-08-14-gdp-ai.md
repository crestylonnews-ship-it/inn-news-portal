  # Singapore Raises GDP Forecast for Second Time as AI Boom Offsets Middle East Conflict Drag
  
  > Singapore's Ministry of Trade and Industry has raised its 2026 GDP growth forecast to 4.5%–5.5%, marking the second upward revision this year. The AI investment surge is driving trade growth, partially offsetting economic pressures from ongoing Middle East conflicts. The IMF report notes that the global economy still faces slowdown and inflation challenges.
  
  Singapore's Ministry of Trade and Industry announced on Tuesday, August 11, 2026, that it is raising its 2026 gross domestic product (GDP) growth forecast to the 4.5%–5.5% range, the second upward revision of the economic growth outlook this year. The adjustment is higher than the 2%–4% forecast range the ministry provided in February 2026 before the outbreak of the Iran war. According to Yahoo Entertainment, the upward revision is primarily based on the artificial intelligence (AI) investment boom exceeding expectations, driving significant growth in trade activity and partially offsetting the economic drag from ongoing conflicts in the Middle East.
  
  Singapore's second-quarter GDP grew 5.9% year-on-year, above the government's initial estimate of 5.7% and slightly higher than the 5.8% median forecast of economic experts. However, this pace of growth slowed compared to the 6.3% recorded in the first quarter. In its announcement, the Ministry of Trade and Industry emphasized that the global AI investment boom continues to gain momentum, providing strong support for Singapore's trade sector. Nevertheless, instability in the Middle East remains a source of uncertainty for the regional economy, with factors such as energy price volatility and disruptions to shipping routes continuing to affect trade prospects.
  
  The International Monetary Fund (IMF) stated in its April 2026 World Economic Outlook report that the global economy is facing a dual challenge of slowing growth and rising inflation pressures. The IMF recommended that policymakers in all countries maintain flexibility, carefully manage the trade-offs involved in increasing defense spending, and lay the foundation for sustainable recovery. This report aligns with Singapore's assessment of the impact of Middle East conflicts, indicating that regional geopolitical risks remain a significant variable for the global economy.
  
  From the perspective of regional economic integration, analysis by polsci.institute indicates that regional economic groups such as ASEAN continue to influence the cross-border flow of goods, services, capital, and personnel by reducing trade barriers, opening markets, and coordinating economic policies. As an important ASEAN member, Singapore's economic performance is closely linked to regional integration processes. Meanwhile, trade agreement data from trade.gov.tw shows that Middle Eastern countries are deepening economic ties through multilateral agreements, including the COMESA (Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa) agreement and partnership agreements with the European Union, reflecting the ongoing evolution of the region's economic structure.
  
  Synthesizing information from various sources, the upward revision of Singapore's economic growth forecast reflects the momentum of the AI industry and the resilience of regional trade, but risks from the Middle East situation remain to be watched. The IMF's global economic assessment and the development trends of regional economic groups provide a broader framework for understanding the drivers of this growth. Subsequent economic trends will depend on the sustainability of AI investment, the evolution of the Middle East conflict, and the direction of global trade policy.

# 新加坡二次上調GDP預測 AI熱潮抵銷中東衝突拖累

> 新加坡貿工部將今年GDP成長預測上調至4.5%至5.5%，為第二次上調。AI投資熱潮帶動貿易成長，部分抵消中東持續衝突帶來的經濟壓力。IMF報告指出全球經濟仍面臨放緩與通膨挑戰。

新加坡貿易與工業部於2026年8月11日（星期二）宣佈，將2026年國內生產毛額（GDP）成長預測上調至4.5%至5.5%區間，這是官方今年第二次上調經濟成長展望。此次調整高於該部今年2月在伊朗戰爭爆發前所給出的2%至4%預測區間。根據Yahoo Entertainment報導，此次上調主要基於人工智慧（AI）投資熱潮強於預期，帶動貿易活動顯著成長，部分抵銷了中東地區持續衝突所帶來的經濟拖累效應。
新加坡第二季GDP年比值成長5.9%，高於政府初步估計的5.7%，也略高於經濟專家5.8%的預估中值。不過，這一增速較今年第一季的6.3%有所放緩。貿工部在宣告中強調，全球AI投資熱潮持續延燒，對新加坡貿易部門形成強勁支撐。然而，中東局勢動盪仍對區域經濟構成不確定性，特別是能源價格波動與航運路線受擾等因素，持續影響貿易前景。
國際貨幣基金組織（IMF）於2026年4月發布的《世界經濟展望》報告指出，全球經濟正面臨成長放緩與通膨壓力回升的雙重挑戰。IMF建議各國政策制定者需保持靈活性，謹慎管理國防支出增加所涉及的權衡取捨，並為永續復甦奠定基礎。此報告與新加坡當局對中東衝突影響的評估相互呼應，顯示區域地緣政治風險仍為全球經濟的重要變數。
從區域經濟整合角度觀察，polsci.institute的分析指出，東協（ASEAN）等區域經濟集團透過降低貿易障礙、開放市場與協調經濟政策，持續影響商品、服務、資本與人員的跨境流動。新加坡作為東協重要成員，其經濟表現與區域整合程式密切相關。同時，trade.gov.tw所載的埃及貿易協定資料顯示，中東地區國家正透過多邊協議深化經濟連結，包括COMESA（東非和南部非洲共同市場）協定及與歐盟的夥伴關係協定等機制，反映該區域經濟結構的持續演變。
綜合各來源資訊，新加坡經濟成長預測的上調反映AI產業動能與區域貿易韌性，但中東局勢風險仍待觀察。IMF的全球經濟評估與區域經濟集團的發展趨勢，提供理解此輪成長動力的更廣闊框架。後續經濟走勢將取決於AI投資持續性、中東衝突演變，以及全球貿易政策走向。
