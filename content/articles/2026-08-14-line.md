  # Taiwan's Digital Anti-Fraud Reports Exceed One Million, LINE Service Termination, and New Asia-Pacific Diplomatic Progress
  
  > The Ministry of Digital Affairs announced that the anti-fraud reporting network has exceeded one million cases, LINE announced the termination of its VOOM service at the end of September, and diplomatically, Taiwan has also confirmed its attendance at the Pacific Islands Forum.
  
  In the area of digital security and public governance, the Ministry of Digital Affairs recently released the latest operational results of its "Online Fraud Reporting and Inquiry Network," nearly two years after its launch. According to reports from Ithome.com.tw, the platform's public reporting volume has officially surpassed one million cases, and through close cross-ministerial collaboration and joint defense mechanisms, over 500,000 fraudulent messages of various types have been successfully taken down from major online channels.
  
  Minister of Digital Affairs Lin Yi-jing stated that behind every record in the reporting and inquiry network is a citizen willing to step forward—enthusiastic netizens, corporate partners, and individuals from all sectors of society have all joined hands in the fight against fraud. Statistical data further shows that eight dedicated citizens have each accumulated over 10,000 reports, demonstrating the tangible results of public-private cooperation. The Ministry of Digital Affairs indicated that the focus of future prevention efforts will further concentrate on cross-sector joint defense within the advertising ecosystem, continuously reducing the risk of citizens being deceived.
  
  In the realm of digital communications and social platform services, significant operational policy adjustments have been announced for the instant messaging application widely used in Taiwan. According to reports from Techbang.com, LINE has officially announced that its short video and social post feature, "LINE VOOM," will be fully discontinued in the Taiwan market starting September 30, 2026. The report indicates that the feature, launched several years ago, has maintained consistently low overall usage rates in the market, prompting the official decision to withdraw. After the service termination, users will no longer be able to browse any previously published posts and updates through the application.
  
  The company specifically reminded users that the system will only allow one backup request, urging users who wish to preserve personal text and multimedia records to complete their downloads and backups as soon as possible before the service officially ceases operations, to avoid the loss of precious data.
  
  In the area of Asia-Pacific regional diplomacy and international multilateral participation, Taiwan has recently achieved important diplomatic progress. According to Yahoo Entertainment's citation of foreign media reports, the Pacific Islands Forum (PIF) released its latest announcement, formally confirming that Taiwan will be invited to attend the annual Leaders' Summit held in Palau from August 30 to September 4, 2026. PIF Secretary-General Baron Waqa explicitly stated at a press conference that Taiwan is a very important development partner in the region; although not an official dialogue partner, it has maintained close and positive interactive relations with forum member countries for many years.
  
  Prior to this resolution announcement, ministers from the forum's eighteen member countries had completed preparatory meetings in Fiji. This formal invitation progress enables Taiwan to return to an important multilateral high-level dialogue platform in the Pacific region.
  
  Viewing recent developments comprehensively, from the formation of public-private cooperation mechanisms in digital anti-fraud efforts, to mainstream communication software adjusting service features in response to market viability, to Taiwan's substantive participation in Asia-Pacific regional multilateral summits, all reflect the latest dynamics in Taiwan's public digital governance, communication ecosystem changes, and international multilateral relations.
  
  The concrete implementation results of relevant competent authorities and enterprises in digital protection and service transformation, as well as Taiwan's substantive exchange outcomes at the Pacific Islands Forum going forward, will continue to receive attention and scrutiny from all sectors.

# 臺灣數位防詐通報破百萬、LINE服務終止與亞太外交新進展

> 數發部公佈防詐查詢網成效破百萬件，LINE宣佈九月底終止VOOM服務，外交方面臺灣亦確認出席太平洋島國論壇。

在數位安全與公共治理方面，數位發展部日前公佈了「網路詐騙通報查詢網」上線近兩年來的最新營運成果。根據《Ithome.com.tw》報導，該平臺的公眾通報件數已正式突破一百萬大關，並藉由跨部會的密切協作與聯防機制，成功自各大網路管道下架逾五十萬件各類詐騙訊息。數位發展部部長林宜敬表示，該通報查詢網的每一筆資料背後，都代表著一位願意挺身而出的民眾，從熱心網友、企業夥伴到社會各界人士皆齊心投入防詐行列。

統計資料更顯示，目前已有八位熱心民眾個人的累計通報量達到一萬件以上，展現了公私協力的具體成效；數發部指出，接下來的防治重點將進一步聚焦於廣告生態系的跨界聯防，持續降低民眾受騙風險。

在數位通訊與社群平臺服務動態方面，臺灣廣泛使用的即時通訊軟體營運政策出現重大調整。根據《Techbang.com》報導，LINE官方已正式發布公告，旗下的短影音與社群貼文功能「LINE VOOM」將於2026年9月30日起在臺灣市場全面停止提供服務。報導指出，該項功能推出多年以來，在市場上的整體使用率持續偏低，因而促使官方做出退場決定。服務終止之後，使用者將無法再透過應用程式瀏覽過去發布的任何貼文與動態。

官方特別提醒廣大使用者，系統僅開放申請一次備份作業，呼籲有儲存個人圖文與影音紀錄需求的使用者，務必在服務正式停止營運前儘速完成下載及備份，以免珍貴資料遺失。

在亞太區域外交與國際多邊參與方面，臺灣近期亦取得重要外交進展。根據《Yahoo Entertainment》引述外電報導，太平洋島國論壇（PIF）發布最新訊息，正式確認臺灣將受邀出席2026年8月30日至9月4日於帛琉舉行的年度領袖峰會。太平洋島國論壇秘書長瓦卡（Baron Waqa）在記者會中明確表示，臺灣是該區域非常重要的發展夥伴，雖然並非正式對話夥伴，但長年與論壇成員國維持著緊密且良好的互動關係。

在此項決議公佈前，該論壇的十八個成員國部長已於斐濟完成籌備會議，這項正式受邀的進展，使臺灣得以重返太平洋區域的重要多邊高層對話平臺。

綜觀近期各項發展，從數位防詐的公私協作機製成型、主流通訊軟體因應市場效益調整功能服務，到亞太區域多邊峰會的實質參與，皆反映出臺灣在公共數位治理、通訊生態變遷與國際多邊關係等層面的最新脈動。相關主管機關與企業在數位防護及服務轉型上的具體落實成效，以及後續臺灣在太平洋島國論壇上的實質交流成果，將持續受到各界關注與檢視。
