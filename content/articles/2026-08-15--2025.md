  # Africa's Political Landscape Overall Stable, Multiple Countries See Smooth Transitions in 2025 Elections
  
  > In 2025, most African nations achieved smooth political transitions, while the African Union deepened its participation in G20 global governance; experts warn that circumvention of term limits remains a concern, and regional security dynamics are complex.
  
  Africa's political landscape in 2025 was characterized by "overall stability with regional volatility." According to NetEase reports, nine African countries held elections throughout the year, with most achieving smooth transitions of power. Gabon and Guinea also completed political transitions through electoral processes. Overall, most African countries maintained political stability, with economic growth rebounding against the odds and key sectors showing strong performance, suggesting that growth momentum is likely to continue into 2026. However, security hotspot regions still face challenges, and the complexity of the political security situation has reached a ten-year high.
  
  At the international governance level, Sohu reports indicate that Africa has clearly demonstrated its pathway for participating in global governance through the African Union on the G20 platform. The AU's engagement in G20 cooperation has enabled African countries to more effectively articulate their development aspirations and policy needs, build extensive international cooperation networks, and strengthen communication and coordination with G20 partners. Scholars believe this marks the arrival of an "African moment" in global governance, with Africa participating in international decision-making with a more unified voice.
  
  Nevertheless, concerns persist beneath the surface of political stability. Research from the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences indicates that some African countries have found ways to circumvent presidential term limits, undermining governance quality. Studies by scholars such as Michar Wiebusch and Christina Murray show that the circumvention of presidential term limits has become a persistent governance challenge in Africa. Additionally, conflicts in African security hotspot regions continued throughout 2024, with regional countries experiencing shifting alliances and divisions. Security dynamics and election outcomes have been mutually influential, creating a complex political security landscape.
  
  Africa is a vast continent covering approximately 30.2 million square kilometers with a population of about 1.286 billion, encompassing 55 sovereign states and territories. According to Info Coffee reports, Africa is broadly divided into five major regions—North Africa, West Africa, Central Africa, Southern Africa, and East Africa—each displaying distinct characteristics in political, economic, and security issues. Although Africa is regarded as one of the world's less developed continents, primarily reliant on mining and pastoral industries, 2025 political and economic data shows the continent is gradually moving toward a more stable development trajectory.

# 非洲政局整體穩定 2025年多國大選平穩過渡

> 2025年非洲多數國家實現政權平穩交接，非盟深化G20參與全球治理；專家提醒任期限制規避仍為隱憂，地區安全域性勢複雜。

2025年非洲政治格局呈現「整體穩定、區域性動盪」的特點。根據網易報導，全年共有9個非洲國家舉行大選，多數實現政權平穩交接，加彭與幾內亞亦透過選舉程式完成政治過渡。整體而言，非洲多數國家政局穩定，經濟增速逆勢回升，重點行業表現亮眼，2026年有望延續增長態勢。然而，安全熱點地區仍面臨挑戰，政治安全形勢的複雜程度達到近十年新高。
在國際治理層面，搜狐報導指出，非洲透過非盟力量在G20平臺清晰展現參與全球治理的路徑。非盟參與G20合作的渠道，使非洲國家得以更有效表達自身發展訴求與政策需求，構建廣泛的國際合作網路，並加強與G20合作夥伴的溝通協調。學者認為，這標誌著全球治理中「非洲時刻」的到來，非洲正以更統一的聲音參與國際事務決策。
不過，政治穩定背後仍存在隱憂。中國社會科學院相關研究指出，部分非洲國家出現規避總統任期限制的情況，削弱治理品質。Michar Wiebusch與Christina Murray等學者研究顯示，總統任期限制的規避已成為非洲治理的持續性課題。此外，2024年非洲安全熱點地區衝突持續，地區國家間關係分化組合，安全域性勢動盪與大選結果相互影響，形成複雜的政治安全圖景。
非洲地域遼闊，面積約3020萬平方公里，人口約12.86億，涵蓋55個主權國家與地區。依據資訊咖報導，非洲大致可分為北非、西非、中非、南非與東非五大區域，各區域在政治、經濟與安全議題上呈現不同特徵。儘管非洲被視為世界發展程度較低的大洲，主要依賴採礦與農牧產業，但2025年的政治經濟資料顯示，該大陸正逐步邁向更穩定的發展軌道。
