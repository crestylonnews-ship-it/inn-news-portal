  # Energy Transition and Climate Challenges Run Parallel in the Americas: 2025 Regional Development Analysis
  
  > The Americas are facing dual challenges of energy transition and climate change. The WMO report indicates that Latin America and the Caribbean experienced record-high temperatures and extreme weather in 2025, while Wood Mackenzie and Energy Global analyses show countries balancing traditional energy and renewable energy development.
  
  According to the World Meteorological Organization's (WMO) "2025 Climate Report for Latin America and the Caribbean," the region is experiencing significant and irreversible impacts of climate change. The report notes that signs such as accelerating glacier melt, sea-level rise, extreme heat, floods, and droughts are clearly visible across the region. In 2025, the region experienced record-breaking temperatures, prolonged droughts, and destructive tropical cyclones, highlighting the severe challenges the climate crisis poses to developing countries in the Americas.
  
  Regarding energy transition, Wood Mackenzie's "Americas Energy Transition Outlook" report covers nine major markets in North and Latin America, noting that as North America enters a new decade, it is re-emphasizing domestic resource development and energy security. Energy Global's analysis further indicates that the Americas' energy sector is undergoing a major transition, characterized by a dual-track approach with traditional hydrocarbon development running parallel to rapid renewable energy initiatives in some countries. In North America, Canada continues to seek a balance between traditional and clean energy, while the United States and Latin American countries adopt different energy development paths based on their respective resource endowments and policy orientations.
  
  The International Energy Agency's (IEA) "2025 Global Energy Review," released in March 2025, provides a broader perspective. The report comprehensively reviews global energy supply and demand, technological development, and carbon emission trends for 2024, noting that the interplay of extreme climate, moderating high inflation, and geopolitical uncertainty creates multiple challenges and transition pressures for energy markets. Despite energy-related carbon emissions reaching new highs, the development of renewable energy and clean technologies continues to advance, indicating that the global energy transition is in a critical transitional period.
  
  Different media outlets present diverse perspectives on energy transition in the Americas. Wood Mackenzie's quarterly update report focusing on the United States, Canada, and Latin America specifically addresses structural issues facing each market, such as whether California will tighten its carbon credit market regulatory framework, reflecting how regional policy differences affect transition progress. Energy Global emphasizes the duality of the transition process, where traditional energy investment and renewable energy expansion occur simultaneously, highlighting the trade-offs countries face between energy security and emission reduction goals.
  
  Synthesizing analysis from various sources, the energy and climate issues in the Americas present three main characteristics: first, climate change impacts are particularly pronounced in Latin America and the Caribbean, with the frequency and intensity of extreme weather events continuing to increase; second, energy transition pathways vary across countries, with North America leaning toward domestic resources and security considerations, while Latin America seeks balance between traditional energy dependence and renewable energy potential; third, the global energy market faces both transition pressures and opportunities amid the interplay of economic and geopolitical factors. Future developments will depend on multiple factors including policy coordination among countries, technological breakthroughs, and international financial support.

# 美洲能源轉型與氣候挑戰並行 2025年區域發展現況分析

> 美洲地區正面臨能源轉型與氣候變遷雙重挑戰，WMO報告指出拉美與加勒比海地區2025年出現破紀錄高溫與極端天氣，Wood Mackenzie與Energy Global分析顯示各國正平衡傳統能源與再生能源發展。

根據世界氣象組織（WMO）發布的《2025年拉丁美洲與加勒比海氣候狀況報告》，該地區正經歷顯著且不可逆轉的氣候變遷影響。報告指出，從冰川加速消融、海平面上升，到極端高溫、洪水與乾旱，這些跡象在整個區域清晰可見。2025年該地區經歷了破紀錄的高溫、持續性乾旱以及破壞性的熱帶氣旋，凸顯氣候危機對美洲發展中國家的嚴峻挑戰。
能源轉型方面，Wood Mackenzie在其《美洲能源轉型展望》報告中涵蓋北美與拉丁美洲九個主要市場，指出北美地區進入新十年之際，正重新強調國內資源開發與能源安全。Energy Global的報導進一步分析指出，美洲能源部門正面臨重大轉型，呈現傳統碳氫化合物開發與部分國家快速推動再生能源計畫並行的雙軌特徵。在北美地區，加拿大持續在傳統能源與清潔能源之間尋求平衡，而美國與拉丁美洲各國則根據各自資源稟賦與政策取向，採取不同的能源發展路徑。
國際能源總署（IEA）於2025年3月發布的《2025全球能源回顧》提供了更廣闊的視角。該報告全面回顧2024年度全球能源供需、技術發展與碳排放趨勢，指出極端氣候、高通膨趨緩與地緣政治不確定性交織，使能源市場面臨多重挑戰與轉型壓力。儘管能源相關碳排放再創新高，但再生能源與潔淨技術的發展仍持續推進，顯示全球能源轉型處於關鍵過渡期。
不同媒體對美洲能源轉型的解讀呈現多元觀點。Wood Mackenzie在針對美國、加拿大與拉丁美洲的季度更新報告中，特別關注各市場面臨的結構性問題，例如加州是否收緊其碳信用市場的監管框架，反映出區域政策差異對轉型進度的影響。Energy Global則強調轉型過程中的雙重性，即傳統能源投資與再生能源擴張同時發生，凸顯各國在能源安全與減排目標之間的權衡。
綜合各來源分析，美洲地區的能源與氣候議題呈現三個主要特徵：其一，氣候變遷影響在拉丁美洲與加勒比海地區尤為顯著，極端天氣事件頻率與強度持續增加；其二，能源轉型程式在不同國家呈現差異化路徑，北美偏向國內資源與安全考量，拉丁美洲則在傳統能源依賴與再生能源潛力之間尋找平衡；其三，全球能源市場在經濟與地緣政治多重因素交織下，轉型壓力與機遇並存。後續發展將取決於各國政策協調、技術突破與國際資金支援等多重因素。
