  # Africa's Economy Shows Steady Growth, Multiple Institutions Optimistic About 2026 Trade Opportunities
  
  > Multiple studies indicate that Africa's economy demonstrated robust growth in 2025, with regional integration and industrial upgrading driving trade potential. China-Africa economic and trade cooperation continues to deepen, and international institutions maintain an optimistic outlook on Africa's long-term development prospects.
  
  Africa's economy is at a strategic window of critical transformation. The African Export-Import Bank (Afreximbank) released its *2026 African Trade Report*, themed "Leveraging Geopolitics to Drive Trade and Industrialization in a Global Africa," which points out that the current global geopolitical landscape is being reshaped. Supply chain restructuring, technological變革, and changes in trade and investment patterns are redefining the international economic map. The report emphasizes that if African countries can capitalize on opportunities arising from geopolitical shifts, it will help advance regional industrialization and trade diversification.
  
  The EIU 2025 African Market Forecast Report, cited by the Taipei Economic and Trade Office in Nigeria under Taiwan's Ministry of Economic Affairs, indicates that although African countries have faced political pressures and economic challenges in recent years, the overall economy has demonstrated strong resilience. The report outlines five key trends in Africa's economic and political outlook for 2025, covering energy transition, advancement of regional trade agreements, digital economy development, infrastructure investment, and improved political stability, providing important reference for international companies planning to enter the African market.
  
  The Hong Kong Trade Development Council's economic and trade research report analysis shows that in 2024, Africa's merchandise trade amounted to 48% of the continent's total GDP, reaching US$1.4 trillion, demonstrating Africa's enormous internal trade potential. The report further examines Africa's existing trade relations with mainland China and Hong Kong, noting that Africa's unique economic structure and growth trajectory are shaping its trade patterns, providing background information and strategic direction for enterprises seeking to expand into the African market.
  
  China-Africa economic and trade cooperation continues to deepen. The *China-Africa Economic Bulletin 2026 Edition*, jointly published by the Center for Global Development Policy at Boston University and the Consortium of African Economic Research Associations, tracks China-Africa cooperation dynamics and focuses on analyzing three areas: trade, investment, and loans and development finance. The bulletin points out that since the previous edition (data up to 2022), China-Africa economic and trade relations have seen multiple new trends and changes, including trade structure adjustments, expansion of investment fields, and innovation in financing models, providing important data support for understanding the latest developments in China-Africa economic cooperation.
  
  The International Monetary Fund (IMF) latest report analysis indicates that against the backdrop of rising global economic uncertainty, African countries have proactively advanced economic institutional reforms, focusing on promoting industrial upgrading and regional integration, and systematically cultivating endogenous growth momentum. Africa's economy as a whole showed steady growth in 2025, demonstrating strong resilience, and is regarded as the region with the highest concentration of high-growth economies worldwide. The Ministry of Commerce's African Union website reposted this analysis, emphasizing that Africa's economy is gathering momentum and progressing steadily.
  
  Synthesizing multiple studies and institutional reports, Africa's economy between 2025 and 2026 has exhibited characteristics of steady growth alongside structural transformation. Geopolitical shifts, supply chain restructuring, and technological變革 present strategic opportunities for Africa, while regional integration and industrial upgrading have become the core drivers of growth. China-Africa economic and trade cooperation continues to deepen across trade, investment, and financing, providing broad market space for enterprises on both sides. International institutions generally maintain an optimistic outlook on Africa's long-term development prospects, believing that Africa is poised to play a more significant role in the global economic landscape.

# 非洲經濟穩健增長 多機構看好2026貿易機遇

> 多項研究顯示，非洲經濟2025年呈現穩健增長態勢，區域一體化與產業升級帶動貿易潛力，中國與非洲經貿合作持續深化，國際機構對非洲長期發展前景持樂觀態度。

非洲經濟正處於關鍵轉型的戰略視窗期。非洲出口進口銀行（Afreximbank）發布的《2026年非洲貿易報告》，以「在全球非洲利用地緣政治推動貿易與工業化」為主題，指出當前全球地緣政治格局正在重塑，供應鏈結構調整、技術變革以及貿易與投資模式的改變，正在重新定義國際經濟版圖。報告強調，非洲國家若能善用地緣政治變動帶來的契機，將有助於推動區域工業化程式與貿易多元化發展。
臺灣經濟部國際貿易署駐奈及利亞代表處引用的經濟學人智庫（EIU）2025年非洲市場預測報告指出，非洲國家近年雖面臨政治壓力及經濟挑戰，但整體經濟仍展現出強勁韌性。該報告梳理出2025年非洲經濟與政治展望的五大趨勢，涵蓋能源轉型、區域貿易協定推進、數位經濟發展、基礎建設投資以及政治穩定性改善等面向，為國際企業佈局非洲市場提供重要參考依據。
香港貿易發展局經貿研究報告分析顯示，2024年非洲商品貿易額相當於整個非洲國內生產總值（GDP）的48%，總值達1.4萬億美元，顯示非洲內部貿易潛力龐大。報告進一步檢視非洲與中國內地及香港的既有貿易關係，指出非洲獨特的經濟結構與成長軌跡正在塑造其貿易模式，為有意拓展非洲市場的企業提供背景資訊與戰略方向。
中國與非洲的經貿合作持續深化。波士頓大學全球發展政策研究中心與非洲經濟研究聯合會共同發布的《中國—非洲經濟公報2026年版》，追蹤中國與非洲合作動態，重點分析貿易、投資、貸款與開發金融三個領域。公報指出，自上一期（資料截至2022年）以來，中非經貿關係出現多項新趨勢與變化，包括貿易結構調整、投資領域擴充套件以及融資模式創新等，為理解中非經濟合作最新發展提供重要資料支撐。
國際貨幣基金組織（IMF）最新報告分析指出，在全球經濟不確定性持續攀升的背景下，非洲各國主動推進經濟體制改革，著重促進產業迭代升級與區域一體化程式，系統培育內生增長動能。2025年非洲經濟整體呈現穩健增長態勢，展現出強勁韌性，被視為全球高增長經濟體最集中的區域。商務部非洲聯盟網站轉發此項分析，強調非洲經濟正蓄勢待發、穩步前行。
綜合多項研究與機構報告，非洲經濟在2025至2026年間呈現穩健增長與結構轉型並行的特徵。地緣政治變動、供應鏈重組與技術變革為非洲帶來戰略契機，區域一體化程式與產業升級成為推動增長的核心動力。中國與非洲經貿合作在貿易、投資與融資領域持續深化，為雙方企業提供廣闊市場空間。國際機構普遍對非洲長期發展前景持樂觀態度，認為非洲有望在全球經濟版圖中扮演更重要的角色。
