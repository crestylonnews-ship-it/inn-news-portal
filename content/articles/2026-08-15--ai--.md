  # White House Issues "America's AI Action Plan" — Three Pillars Reshape Global Tech Order
  
  > The Trump administration officially released the "America's AI Action Plan" on July 23, 2025, advancing over 90 federal policy initiatives through three core pillars—accelerating innovation, building infrastructure, and leading international cooperation—to reestablish American dominance in artificial intelligence.
  
  The White House issued the "America's AI Action Plan" on July 23, 2025, implementing the executive order signed by President Trump in January, which aimed to "remove barriers to American leadership in AI." According to TechNews (科技新報), the plan envisions the United States winning the AI race and ushering in a new golden age of prosperity, economic competitiveness, and national security for the American people. Led by the Trump administration, the plan encompasses more than 90 federal policy actions, structured around three core pillars: accelerating innovation, building infrastructure, and leading international cooperation.
  
  TechOrange (科技報橘) notes that this report is not merely a domestic policy document but a concrete manifestation of America's global AI strategy. For Taiwan, which sits at the core of the global tech supply chain and maintains close ties with the United States, the report's content is of paramount importance, helping to decode America's AI layout and understand how it will shape international cooperation, geopolitics, and the trajectory of global industries. tenten.co's analysis suggests the plan's core philosophy is straightforward: innovate faster and more comprehensively than competitors while dismantling unnecessary regulatory barriers that hinder the private sector.
  
  Ernest Chiang further points out that the plan, through its three-pillar strategy, aims to reshape America's global AI dominance, and he analyzes the policy shift, differences from the previous administration, and the impact on the international AI competitive landscape.
  
  From a macroeconomic perspective, artificial intelligence is becoming a new dividing line in global industries. INSIDE reports that according to the latest "2025 Science, Technology and Innovation Report" released by the United Nations Conference on Trade and Development (UNCTAD), the cutting-edge technology market centered on AI will expand to $16.4 trillion by 2033—six times its size a decade ago. This figure represents not only the pace of technological breakthroughs but also reveals that the global economic structure is being fundamentally reconfigured, with the competitive landscape between a handful of tech giants and emerging economies being reshaped.
  
  Different media outlets emphasize different aspects in their interpretations of the plan. TechNews highlights the plan's continuity with Trump's executive order, focusing on the dual goals of national security and economic competitiveness; TechOrange approaches the plan from Taiwan's perspective, examining its impact on global supply chains and geopolitics; INSIDE centers on the expansion of global market size and economic structural transformation; while tenten.co and Ernest Chiang's analyses place greater emphasis on interpreting the policy content and competitive strategy.
  
  Several questions remain to be confirmed, including the plan's specific policy implementation details, the division of responsibilities and resource allocation among federal departments, and the concrete responses from international cooperation partners. Furthermore, the plan's actual impact on Taiwan's tech industry remains to be seen, pending observation of the U.S. government's subsequent enforcement measures and the evolution of the international landscape.

# 白宮頒布《美國 AI 行動計畫》 三大支柱重塑全球科技秩序

> 川普政府於2025年7月23日正式發布《美國 AI 行動計畫》，透過加速創新、建設基礎設施、領導國際合作三大支柱，推動超過90項聯邦政策行動，目標是重塑美國在人工智慧領域的全球主導地位。

白宮於2025年7月23日頒布《美國 AI 行動計畫》（America's AI Action Plan），落實總統川普今年1月簽署的行政命令，該命令旨在「消除美國在 AI 領導地位的障礙」。根據 TechNews 科技新報報導，這份行動計畫期許美國贏得 AI 競賽，為美國人民迎來一個繁榮昌盛、具經濟競爭力兼顧國家安全的新黃金時代。該計畫由川普政府主導，包含超過90項聯邦政策行動，核心策略架構為三大支柱：加速創新、建設基礎設施、領導國際合作。
TechOrange 科技報橘指出，這份報告不只是一份美國國內的政策檔案，更是其全球 AI 戰略的具體展現。對於身處全球科技供應鏈核心、並與美國有著緊密連結的臺灣而言，這份報告的內容至關重要，能幫助解讀美國在 AI 上的佈局，看懂其如何影響國際合作、地緣政治，以及全球產業的走向。tenten.co 分析認為，該計畫的核心理念很簡單：創新速度要比競爭對手更快、更全面，同時拆除阻礙私人部門發展的不必要監管障礙。
Ernest Chiang 進一步指出，這份計畫透過三大支柱策略，旨在重塑美國 AI 全球主導地位，並分析了政策轉向、與前政府差異，以及對國際 AI 競爭格局的影響。
從宏觀經濟角度觀察，人工智慧正成為全球產業的新分水嶺。INSIDE 報導指出，根據聯合國貿易與發展會議（UNCTAD）最新發布的《2025 年科技與創新報告》，以人工智慧為核心的尖端技術市場，將在2033年擴大至16.4兆美元，是十年前的六倍。這項數字並非僅代表技術突破的速度，而是揭示了全球經濟結構正在被重新編碼，少數科技巨頭與新興經濟體之間的競爭格局正在重塑。
不同媒體對該計畫的解讀側重點有所不同。TechNews 強調該計畫與川普行政命令的連貫性，著重於國家安全與經濟競爭力的雙重目標；TechOrange 則從臺灣視角出發，關注該計畫對全球供應鏈與地緣政治的影響；INSIDE 則將焦點放在全球市場規模的擴張與經濟結構的轉變；tenten.co 與 Ernest Chiang 的分析則更側重於政策內容與競爭策略的解讀。
目前尚待確認之處包括：該計畫具體的政策執行細節、各聯邦部門的分工與資源配置，以及國際合作夥伴的具體回應。此外，該計畫對臺灣科技產業的實際影響，仍有待觀察美國政府後續的執行措施與國際形勢的演變。
