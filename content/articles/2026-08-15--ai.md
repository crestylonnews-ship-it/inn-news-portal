  # NUK Scholars Focus on Middle East and Africa Governance; Seminar Explores AI and Red Sea Crisis
  
  > The Department of Political and Legal Studies at National Kaohsiung University hosted the Third International Seminar on the Middle East and Africa on the 8th, focusing on AI governance, the Red Sea crisis, and shifts in global order, examining how regional conflicts impact international governance.
  
  The Department of Political and Legal Studies at National Kaohsiung University held the "Third International Seminar on the Middle East and Africa" on May 8, themed "Embedded Crises and Reconstruction: Governance in the Middle East and Africa under Global Order," attracting attention from academia and policy researchers. According to PChome Online News, the seminar focused on topics such as artificial intelligence (AI) governance, the Red Sea crisis, and shifts in global order, reflecting the rapid evolution of the current international landscape. The National Kaohsiung University Sustainability Report also noted that the seminar aimed to explore how Middle East conflicts and the Red Sea crisis affect global supply chains and semiconductor diplomacy, demonstrating that the effects of regional conflicts have transcended geographical boundaries.
  
  Reports from Fire News and life.tw are consistent with the aforementioned media, all emphasizing the academic dialogue value of the seminar on governance in the Middle East and Africa.
  
  The seminar is situated within the context of global order restructuring, with the Middle East region continuing to face security and economic challenges, while Africa involves development and governance issues. PChome Online News pointed out that AI governance became a key discussion project, highlighting the connection between technology policy and regional stability. The National Kaohsiung University Sustainability Report further explained the relationship between global supply chains and semiconductor diplomacy.

# 高大學者聚焦中東非洲治理 研討會探討AI與紅海危機

> 國立高雄大學政治法律學系8日舉辦第三屆中東與非洲國際研討會，聚焦AI治理、紅海危機與全球秩序變動，探討區域衝突對國際治理的影響。

國立高雄大學政治法律學系於5月8日舉辦「第三屆中東與非洲國際研討會」，以「鑲嵌的危機與重建：全球秩序下的中東與非洲治理」為主題，吸引學術界與政策研究者關注。根據PChome Online新聞報導，此次研討會聚焦人工智慧（AI）治理、紅海危機與全球秩序變動等議題，反映當前國際局勢的快速演變。國立高雄大學永續報告亦指出，研討會旨在探討中東衝突、紅海危機如何影響全球供應鏈與半導體外交，顯示區域衝突的效應已跨越地理疆界。
火報與life.tw的報導內容與前述媒體一致，均強調研討會對中東與非洲治理的學術對話價值。
