  # US Largest Grid Proposes Power Restrictions for Data Centers as AI Computing Surge Strains Infrastructure
  
  > North America's PJM grid plans to implement mandatory power restrictions for large data centers starting in 2027, reflecting the growing tension between AI computing demand and power infrastructure, and drawing widespread attention from the tech industry and regulators.
  
  PJM Interconnection, the operator of North America's largest regional grid, recently proposed a mandatory power restriction policy targeting large data centers, set to take effect in 2027. The plan requires data centers with electricity consumption of 50 MW or more to adjust their load to guard against the risk of large-scale blackouts. The policy covers a service area serving 67 million customers across the United States and represents a key regulatory measure to address the power shortfall triggered by the AI computing boom. While PJM is working to increase generation capacity, it faces the reality that infrastructure expansion cannot keep pace with computing demand. The draft restriction policy has already sparked discussion within the tech industry and among local governments.
  
  The explosive growth of AI and cloud computing has driven the rapid construction of large data centers worldwide, generating unprecedented electricity demand. However, the growth rate of power infrastructure supply has far lagged behind the pace of computing expansion, and the power gap is gradually becoming the greatest bottleneck to technological development. Techbang reports that PJM's restriction policy aims to prevent large-scale blackouts by requiring data centers to adjust their load during peak hours. If formally implemented, this measure will directly impact AI training and inference operations that depend on stable power supply.
  
  Meanwhile, the AI industry itself faces governance and safety alignment challenges. Techbang reports that OpenAI ethics lead Chloë Bakalar resigned in July of this year, reflecting the tension within the company between safety alignment and commercialization. Bakalar had been responsible for the ethics framework on a full-time basis since August last year, promoting ethical approaches to model development and discussions on machine consciousness. Her resignation has drawn industry attention to AI corporate governance. FTV News also reported that prospects of a US-Iran peace deal lifted pre-market US stocks, with technology and individual stocks becoming market focal points, underscoring capital markets' attention to the AI industry's development outlook.
  
  Different media outlets observe AI industry developments from varying angles. Techbang focuses on power infrastructure and regulatory policy, emphasizing the impact of computing expansion on traditional energy grids; the same outlet also covers changes in OpenAI's internal ethics framework, reflecting the governance challenges that accompany rapid industry growth; FTV News takes a market perspective, recording capital markets' reactions to geopolitics and the tech industry. Together, these reports outline the multiple dimensions facing the AI industry's development in the United States: infrastructure pressure, corporate governance issues, and shifting capital market expectations.
  
  Industry observers note that if PJM's power restriction policy is formally implemented, it may prompt data center operators to reassess their site selection strategies or accelerate the deployment of backup power generation and energy storage systems. At the same time, ethics and governance issues within AI companies continue to attract attention. Striking a balance between commercial expansion and safety alignment has become an issue the industry cannot ignore. Future developments await further confirmation of policy details by PJM and responses from relevant industry players.

# 美最大電網擬限電資料中心 AI算力熱潮衝擊基礎設施

> 北美PJM電網擬於2027年起對大型資料中心實施強制限電，反映AI算力需求與電力基礎設施之間的緊張關係，引發科技業與監管單位的廣泛關注。

北美最大區域電網營運商PJM Interconnection（PJM）近期提出針對大型資料中心的強制限電政策，計畫於2027年上路，要求用電量達50MW以上的資料中心配合負載調整，以防備大規模停電風險。這項政策涵蓋全美6,700萬使用者的供電區域，是美國應對AI算力熱潮所引發電力缺口的重要監管舉措。PJM在增加發電能力的同時，也面臨基礎設施擴張速度趕不上算力需求的現實困境，這項限電草案已引發科技業與地方政府的討論。
AI與雲端運算的爆發性成長推動大型資料中心在全球各地快速興建，推升前所未有的龐大電力需求。然而，電力基礎設施的供電成長速度遠不及算力擴張步伐，電力缺口正逐漸成為科技發展的最大瓶頸。Techbang報導指出，PJM的限電政策旨在防止大規模停電，要求資料中心在尖峰時段配合負載調整，這項措施若正式實施，將對依賴穩定供電的AI訓練與推論作業產生直接影響。
與此同時，AI產業內部也面臨治理與安全對齊的挑戰。Techbang報導，OpenAI倫理負責人巴卡拉爾（Chloë Bakalar）於今年7月離職，反映出該公司在安全對齊與商業化之間的張力。巴卡拉爾自去年8月起專職負責倫理架構，推動模型開發的倫理方法與機器意識議題討論，其離職引發業界對AI企業治理的重視。FTV新聞網亦報導，美伊傳和平協議曙光帶動美股盤前走高，科技與個股成為市場焦點，顯示資本市場對AI產業發展前景的關注。
不同媒體對AI產業發展的觀察角度各異。Techbang聚焦於電力基礎設施與監管政策，強調算力擴張對傳統能源網的衝擊；同一媒體亦關注OpenAI內部倫理架構的變動，反映產業高速成長伴隨的治理挑戰；FTV新聞網則從市場面切入，記錄資本市場對地緣政治與科技產業的反應。這些報導共同勾勒出AI產業在美國發展所面臨的多重面向：基礎設施壓力、企業治理課題，以及資本市場的預期變化。
業界觀察指出，PJM的限電政策若正式實施，可能促使資料中心業者重新評估設點策略，或加速部署備用發電與儲能系統。同時，AI企業的倫理治理議題也持續受到關注，如何在商業化擴張與安全對齊之間取得平衡，已成為產業不可忽視的課題。後續發展有待PJM官方進一步確認政策細節，以及相關業者的回應。
