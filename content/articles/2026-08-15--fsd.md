  # Tesla Allegedly Submitted Exaggerated FSD Safety Data to European Regulators
  
  > Reuters reports that Tesla submitted exaggerated safety data to some European regulators in an effort to expand the approval scope of its Full Self-Driving system and revitalize sales in the European market.
  
  According to Reuters, Tesla submitted exaggerated safety data to some European regulators in an effort to expand the approval scope of its "Full Self-Driving" (FSD) system and revitalize sales in the European market, where it has been losing market share. The move has raised concerns about the strictness of European autonomous driving regulations and corporate integrity. The report indicates that Tesla, facing challenges in its European market sales performance, attempted to accelerate the FSD approval process by submitting safety data that had not been adequately verified, in a bid to re-attract consumers. This incident highlights the tension between the promotion of autonomous driving technology and safety regulation, particularly when companies face market competition pressure and may resort to improper means to gain regulatory advantage.
  
  Tesla's market share in Europe has continued to decline in recent years, as competitors such as German domestic automakers and emerging electric vehicle brands actively launch models equipped with advanced driver assistance features. Against this backdrop, Tesla sought to have its FSD system approved in more European countries by submitting exaggerated safety data, thereby enhancing its product appeal. However, European regulators have always maintained strict scrutiny of autonomous driving technology approvals, requiring companies to provide detailed safety test reports and on-road validation data. If the report is accurate, Tesla's actions may violate relevant EU regulations and damage consumer trust in autonomous driving technology.
  
  The incident has also sparked industry concerns about regulatory loopholes, with fears that other automakers may follow suit and use similar means to accelerate technology approvals.
  
  Currently, the report has only been initially disclosed by Reuters, and no official response has been received from Tesla or formal confirmation from European regulators. Different media outlets have varying levels of detail regarding the incident, and some analysts believe further verification is needed on the specific scope and impact of the exaggerated data. European regulators have stated they will closely monitor the approval process for autonomous driving technology to ensure safety standards are not compromised by commercial interests. Future developments will depend on the results of regulatory investigations and Tesla's response. The incident has also prompted public attention to the safety boundaries of autonomous driving technology and how companies should balance innovation and integrity in market competition.

# 特斯拉被指向歐洲監管機關提交誇大 FSD 安全資料

> 路透報導特斯拉為擴大全自動輔助駕駛系統核准範圍，向部分歐洲監管機關提交遭誇大的安全資料，試圖重振歐洲市場銷售。

根據路透報導，特斯拉為尋求擴大「全自動輔助駕駛」（FSD）系統核准範圍，並試圖在市佔流失的歐洲市場重振銷售，向部分歐洲監管機關提交的是遭誇大的安全資料。此舉引發對歐洲自動駕駛監管嚴格性與企業誠信度的關注。報導指出，特斯拉在歐洲市場的銷售表現面臨挑戰，透過提交未經充分驗證的安全資料，企圖加速 FSD 系統的核準程式，以重新吸引消費者。此事件凸顯自動駕駛技術推廣與安全監管之間的張力，尤其當企業面臨市場競爭壓力時，可能採取非正當手段獲取監管優勢。
特斯拉在歐洲的市佔率近年持續下滑，競爭對手如德國本土車廠與新興電動車品牌積極推出具備先進輔助駕駛功能的車型。在此背景下，特斯拉透過提交誇大安全資料，企圖讓 FSD 系統在更多歐洲國家獲得核准，進而提升產品吸引力。然而，歐洲監管機關對自動駕駛技術的審批一向嚴格，要求企業提供詳細的安全測試報告與實路驗證資料。若報導內容屬實，特斯拉的行為可能違反歐盟相關法規，並損害消費者對自動駕駛技術的信任。
此事件也引發業界對監管漏洞的擔憂，擔心其他車廠可能效仿，以類似手段加速技術核准。
目前此報導僅由路透單一開始披露，尚未獲得特斯拉官方回應或歐洲監管機關的正式確認。不同媒體對事件細節的掌握程度不一，部分分析認為需進一步查證資料誇大的具體範圍與影響。歐洲監管機關已表示將嚴密監控自動駕駛技術的核準程式，確保安全標準不受商業利益影響。後續發展將取決於監管單位的調查結果與特斯拉的回應態度。此事件也促使公眾關注自動駕駛技術的安全邊界，以及企業在市場競爭中應如何平衡創新與誠信。
