  # EU First: Portugal Joins HealthAI Network to Strengthen Medical AI Regulation
  
  > Portugal becomes the first EU member to join the HealthAI network, strengthening the medical AI regulatory framework. HealthAI's CEO revealed ongoing discussions with other EU member states, emphasizing that EU regulatory decisions will have spillover effects on global AI governance.
  
  Portugal has become the first EU member state to join the HealthAI network, marking a significant step forward in the regulation of medical artificial intelligence within the European Union. According to FTV News, Portugal's accession to the HealthAI network aims to strengthen regulatory mechanisms for medical AI, ensuring the safe and compliant application of related technologies within the EU. Baptista Leite, CEO of HealthAI, told Reuters that the organization is currently in discussions with other EU member states to expand the network's coverage. He emphasized that European regulatory decisions will have spillover effects on global AI governance, making it crucial how the Brussels-based EU authorities formulate their regulations.
  
  The European Union is a political and economic alliance established under the Maastricht Treaty, which took effect in 1993, and currently comprises 27 member states as a supranational political, economic, and judicial entity formed by multiple European countries. In the field of AI governance, the EU has been actively advancing relevant regulatory frameworks in recent years, striving to strike a balance between promoting innovation and safeguarding citizens' rights. Portugal's accession to the HealthAI network is regarded as an important milestone in the EU's medical AI regulatory landscape. Baptista Leite further noted that the EU's regulatory standards in AI governance will have a demonstration effect globally, with other regions likely referencing the EU's regulatory model.
  
  Analysts believe that Portugal's move to join the HealthAI network reflects the growing importance EU member states place on medical AI regulation. As AI technology applications continue to expand across the healthcare sector, ensuring the safety, efficacy, and ethical compliance of related technologies has become a focal point of concern for governments and international organizations worldwide. The expansion of the HealthAI network is expected to provide EU member states with a platform for coordinating regulatory standards and promoting the development of cross-border medical AI cooperation. However, differing positions and priorities among member states on AI regulation remain a challenge, and achieving consensus will require further observation.
  
  The EU official website states that the organization is committed to promoting coordination and cooperation among member states in key policy areas, and the expansion of the HealthAI network is a concrete manifestation of this objective. In the future, as more member states join, the EU's influence on medical AI governance is expected to further expand, exerting a profound impact on the global AI regulatory landscape.

# 歐盟首例 葡萄牙加入HealthAI網路強化醫療AI監管

> 葡萄牙成為歐盟首個加入HealthAI網路的成員國，強化醫療AI監管框架。HealthAI執行長透露正與其他歐盟成員國洽談，強調歐盟監管決策將對全球AI治理產生外溢效應。

葡萄牙成為歐盟首例加入HealthAI網路的成員國，此舉標誌著歐盟在醫療人工智慧監管領域邁出重要一步。民視新聞網報導指出，葡萄牙此次加入HealthAI網路，旨在強化醫療AI的監管機制，確保相關技術在歐盟境內的安全與合規應用。HealthAI執行長Baptista Leite向路透社表示，目前正與其他歐盟成員國進行洽談，期望擴大該網路的覆蓋範圍。他強調，歐洲的監管決策將對全球AI治理產生外溢效應，因此歐盟總部布魯塞爾當局如何正確制定規範，顯得至關重要。
歐盟作為根據1993年生效的《馬斯垂克條約》所建立的政治經濟聯盟，現擁有27個成員國，是歐洲多國共同建立的政治、經濟及司法超主權實體。在AI治理方面，歐盟近年積極推動相關法規框架，力求在促進創新與保障公民權益之間取得平衡。葡萄牙此次加入HealthAI網路，被視為歐盟在醫療AI監管領域的重要里程碑。Baptista Leite進一步指出，歐盟在AI治理方面的規範制定，將對全球產生示範效應，其他地區可能參考歐盟的監管模式。
分析認為，葡萄牙加入HealthAI網路的舉措，反映歐盟成員國對醫療AI監管的重視程度日益提升。隨著人工智慧技術在醫療領域的應用不斷擴充套件，如何確保相關技術的安全性、有效性與倫理合規，成為各國政府與國際組織關注的焦點。HealthAI網路的擴充，有望為歐盟成員國提供一個協調監管標準的平臺，促進跨境醫療AI合作的發展。然而，不同成員國在AI監管方面的立場與優先事項可能存在差異，如何達成共識仍待觀察。
歐盟官方網站指出，該組織致力於推動成員國在關鍵政策領域的協調與合作，HealthAI網路的擴充套件正是這一目標的具體體現。未來，隨著更多成員國加入，歐盟在醫療AI治理方面的影響力有望進一步擴大，對全球AI監管格局產生深遠影響。
