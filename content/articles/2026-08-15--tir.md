  According to reports from Australian media outlet News.com.au and others, Australia and Fiji have signed the "Ocean Peace Alliance" agreement. This marks the fourth time Australia has entered into a similar pact with a Pacific island nation, demonstrating Australia's continued effort to strengthen its maritime security and economic and trade cooperation network with Oceania countries. Taiwan also concurrently donated US$2 million to assist the Pacific Islands Forum (PIF) in advancing its "Blue Pacific Continent 2050 Strategy," further deepening regional marine governance and sustainable development cooperation.
  
  The Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region remains a weak link in the global trade landscape. Analysis by the International Monetary Fund (IMF) indicates that the region accounted for only 7.4% of global trade in 2022, with exports heavily concentrated in a narrow range of products and trading partners, limited economic complexity, and relatively insufficient integration into global value chains. However, with Saudi Arabia's announcement of a large gold deposit valued at up to US$50 trillion and multiple countries actively embracing the "post-oil era," the MENA region is attempting to reshape its economic structure by positioning itself as the "New Middle East" and connecting manufacturing corridors between India and Europe and America.
  
  In terms of trade facilitation, the Egyptian Customs Authority and the General Federation of Egyptian Chambers of Commerce signed a guarantee agreement on July 13, 2026, formally launching the TIR (International Road Transport) customs transit system under the International Road Transport Convention. This move will reduce regional freight clearance costs and improve land transport efficiency between the MENA region and Europe and Asia. Meanwhile, research from the Hong Kong Trade Development Council indicates that emerging industry cooperation in artificial intelligence and new energy will become a new growth driver for China-Middle East economic and trade relations. Cooperation in the digital economy between the two sides is projected to exceed US$50 billion by 2030, and enterprises need to plan ahead in setting technical standards and building industrial ecosystems to seize the commanding heights of regional economic prosperity.

The Australia-Fiji Ocean Peace Alliance focuses on regional security and sustainable development, Egypt's launch of the TIR system aims to optimize land trade corridors, and China-Middle East digital economy cooperation demonstrates that emerging industries will become the core driving force for regional economic and trade growth. In the future, whether these cross-regional cooperation mechanisms can be effectively implemented remains to be seen based on policy advancement and actual execution by each country.

# 澳洲斐濟簽海洋和平聯盟 埃及啟動TIR體制 中東經貿格局重塑

> 澳洲與斐濟簽署「海洋和平聯盟」協議，埃及啟動國際公路運輸公關約TIR海關過境體制；中東北非貿易佔全球7.4%，中國與中東數字經濟合作預計2030年突破500億美元。

澳洲新聞網（News.com.au）等多家媒體報導指出，澳洲與斐濟已簽署「海洋和平聯盟」協議，此為澳洲第四度與太平洋島國簽訂類似協議，顯示澳洲持續強化與大洋洲國家的海洋安全與經貿合作網路。臺灣亦同步捐出200萬美元，協助太平洋島國論壇（PIF）推動「2050藍色太平洋大陸戰略」，進一步深化區域海洋治理與永續發展合作。
中東北非地區在全球貿易版圖中仍屬薄弱環節。國際貨幣基金組織（IMF）分析指出，該地區在2022年全球貿易總額中僅佔7.4%，出口高度集中於少數產品與貿易夥伴，經濟複雜性有限，參與全球價值鏈的程度相對不足。然而，隨著沙烏地阿拉伯宣佈發現價值高達50兆美元的大型金礦，以及多國積極迎向「後石油時代」，中東北非正以「新中東」之姿，嘗試銜接印度與歐美的製造鏈廊道，重塑區域經濟結構。
在貿易便利化方面，埃及海關總署與埃及商會總聯合會於2026年7月13日簽署擔保協議，正式啟動《國際公路運輸公約》（TIR）海關過境體制，此舉將降低區域貨運通關成本，提升中東北非與歐洲、亞洲之間的陸路運輸效率。同時，香港貿易發展局研究指出，人工智慧與新能源等新興產業合作將成為中國與中東經貿關係的新增長點，預計至2030年，雙方在數字經濟領域的合作規模將突破500億美元，企業需提前佈局技術標準制定與產業生態構建，以搶佔區域經濟繁榮的制高點。
綜合觀察，大洋洲與中東北非雖地理上相隔遙遠，但兩地正透過多邊協議、基礎建設投資與數字經濟合作，逐步融入全球貿易新格局。澳洲與斐濟的海洋和平聯盟聚焦區域安全與永續發展，埃及啟動TIR體制則著眼於陸路貿易通道最佳化，而中國與中東的數字經濟合作更顯示新興產業將成為區域經貿增長的核心動力。未來，這些跨區域合作機制能否有效落實，仍待各國政策推進與實際執行成效觀察。
