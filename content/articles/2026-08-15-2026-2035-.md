  # African Union Adopts 2026-2035 Education Strategy; UNESCO Releases Transformation Report
  
  > The African Union formally adopted the Continental Education Strategy for Africa (2026-2035) in February 2025, while UNESCO and other organizations simultaneously released a monitoring report focusing on the transformation of learning and skills development.
  
  In February 2025, the African Union (AU) formally adopted the Continental Education Strategy for Africa (CESA 2026-2035) through its heads of state and government. The full text of the strategy was published on the African Union's official website on June 16, 2025. This strategy serves as the core guiding document for education development in Africa over the next decade, aiming to address the continent's long-term challenges in education access, quality improvement, and skills training.
  
  According to official African Union materials, the CESA 2026-2035 strategy consists of four main components: an education investment brief, analysis of key challenges, an action framework, and a monitoring and evaluation mechanism. The strategy explicitly incorporates consideration of core elements such as human rights, peace, sustainable development, gender equality, equity, and inclusion. The African Union also noted that this strategy complements the African Science, Technology and Innovation Strategy for 2034 (STISA-2034), jointly advancing Africa's future development agenda.
  
  Meanwhile, the Transforming Learning and Skills Development in Africa: 2nd Continental Report, jointly authored by UNESCO, the African Union, and the United Nations Children's Fund (UNICEF), was officially released during the African Regional Forum on Sustainable Development.
  
  The report provides a detailed analysis of the current state of learning outcomes and skills development in Africa, emphasizing the need to make learning and skills development more effective, efficient, equitable, and responsive to Africa's economic and social development needs by leveraging existing strengths and addressing weaknesses.
  
  UNESCO stated in related coverage that the purpose of releasing this monitoring report is to provide practical resources for national ministries of education, teacher service commissions, teacher councils, and other education stakeholders to help advance the implementation of CESA 2026-2035. The report's content complements the AU's education strategy by providing both a data and analytical foundation and specific transformation pathways, offering reference for African countries in formulating education policies and budget allocations.
  
  Education analysts point out that the African continent has long faced structural problems such as unequal distribution of educational resources, teacher shortages, and inconsistent learning quality. The adoption of CESA 2026-2035 marks an important step forward in regional education integration and coordination in Africa. However, translating the strategy from text to practice still requires overcoming multiple challenges including funding mobilization, cross-border coordination, and local implementation, and subsequent progress warrants continued observation.

# 非盟透過2026-2035教育戰略 教科文組織發布轉型報告

> 非洲聯盟於2025年2月正式透過《非洲大陸教育戰略（2026-2035年）》，聯合國教科文組織等機構同步發布監測報告，聚焦學習與技能發展轉型。

非洲聯盟（African Union）於2025年2月由各國元首與政府首腦正式透過《非洲大陸教育戰略》（Continental Education Strategy for Africa，簡稱CESA 2026-2035），戰略全文已於2025年6月16日在非洲聯盟官方網站上線公佈。此戰略為非洲未來十年教育發展的核心指導檔案，旨在回應非洲大陸在教育普及、品質提升與技能培養等方面的長期挑戰。
根據非洲聯盟官方資料，CESA 2026-2035戰略由四個主要部分構成，涵蓋教育投資簡案、關鍵挑戰分析、行動框架與監測評估機制。戰略內容明確納入對人權、和平、可持續發展，以及性別平等、公平與包容等核心要素的考量。非洲聯盟同時指出，此戰略與《非洲科學、技術與創新戰略2034》（STISA-2034）相互呼應，共同推動非洲未來發展議程。
與此同時，聯合國教科文組織（UNESCO）、非洲聯盟與聯合國兒童基金會（UNICEF）聯合撰寫的《非洲學習和技能發展轉型：第2次大陸報告》（Transforming Learning and Skills Development in Africa: 2nd Continental Report）亦於非洲可持續發展區域論壇期間正式發布。
該報告詳細分析非洲在學習成果與技能培養方面的現狀，強調透過發揮現有優勢並補強弱項，使學習與技能發展更為有效、高效、公平，並更能回應非洲經濟與社會發展需求。
教科文組織在相關報導中表示，此監測報告的發布旨在為各國教育部、教師服務委員會、教師理事會及其他教育利益相關者提供實用資源，協助推動CESA 2026-2035的落實。報告內容與非盟教育戰略形成互補，一方面提供資料與分析基礎，另一方面提出具體轉型路徑，為非洲各國制定教育政策與預算編列提供參考依據。
教育分析人士指出，非洲大陸長期面臨教育資源分配不均、師資短缺、學習品質參差等結構性問題，CESA 2026-2035的透過標誌著非洲在區域教育整合與協調方面邁出重要一步。然而，戰略從文字到實際執行仍須克服資金籌措、跨國協調與地方落實等多重挑戰，後續進展值得持續觀察。
