  # Forced Displacement in the Americas Reaches 22.9 Million; US "Americas First" Policy and "Shield of the Americas" Draw Attention
  
  > The UNHCR report indicates that the Americas hosted 22.9 million forcibly displaced persons in 2025, accounting for 18% of the global total. The US is simultaneously advancing its "Americas First" foreign policy and the multilateral security initiative "Shield of the Americas," while Doctors Without Borders criticizes US cuts to humanitarian aid and their impact on global health.
  
  The regional overview of the Americas in the UNHCR's *2025 Global Report* highlights that the region has experienced high levels of forced displacement due to violence, persecution, crime, insecurity, and climate-related disasters. In 2025, the Americas hosted 22.9 million forcibly displaced and stateless persons, representing 18% of the global total, underscoring the ongoing severity of the regional humanitarian crisis.
  
  In January 2025, US Secretary of State Marco Rubio published an article in *The Wall Street Journal* outlining the "Americas First" foreign policy direction, emphasizing that the United States should pay greater attention to the Western Hemisphere. Rubio stated that his first overseas trip as Secretary of State was to Central America, reflecting this policy focus. The Human Security Centre, which has long tracked US foreign policy and issues related to security, development, and human rights in Latin America, notes that research from the organization shows the US policy shift will have far-reaching implications for the region.
  
  Meanwhile, in March 2026, President Trump hosted a summit at the Doral Golf Resort in Florida and formally announced the launch of "Shield of the Americas," a multilateral security and military cooperation initiative. The move is seen as a concrete step by the United States to strengthen its security architecture in the Western Hemisphere, but Amnesty International continues to monitor human rights conditions across the Americas, reminding stakeholders that security policies must also safeguard human rights.
  
  In a January 2026 retrospective article, Doctors Without Borders Taiwan Branch pointed out that the Trump administration issued a series of executive orders a year prior that upended global health and humanitarian assistance programs, severely undermining international cooperation and solidarity. The organization observed that throughout 2025, aid grounded in public health principles was gradually abandoned, clinics closed, life-saving medicines remained stranded in ports, and healthcare workers lost their jobs, exacting a heavy humanitarian toll. This criticism stands in tension with the US "Americas First" policy, demonstrating that the shift in American foreign aid policy is already producing tangible effects.
  
  Taken together, the Americas region faces a dual challenge of forced displacement crisis and policy realignment. UNHCR data provides a quantitative foundation for the region's humanitarian situation, US official documents reveal a westward policy pivot, and international organizations have raised concerns about human rights and aid reductions. Future developments will depend on the specific content and implementation effectiveness of the "Shield of the Americas" initiative, as well as the long-term impact of US policy adjustments on regional humanitarian assistance.

# 美洲被迫流離失所達2,290萬人 美推「美洲優先」與「美洲之盾」引關注

> 聯合國難民署報告指出，2025年美洲地區承載2,290萬被迫流離失所者，佔全球18%。美國同時推動「美洲優先」外交政策與多國安全倡議「美洲之盾」，無國界醫生批評美國削減人道援助影響全球健康。

聯合國難民署（UNHCR）發布之《2025年全球報告》美洲區域概述指出，該地區因暴力、迫害、犯罪、不安全狀況及氣候相關災害，經歷高度被迫流離失所現象。2025年美洲地區共收容2,290萬被迫流離失所與無國籍人口，佔全球被迫流離失所者總數之18%，凸顯區域人道危機持續嚴峻。
美國政府於2025年1月由國務卿馬可·盧比奧於《華爾街日報》發表文章，明確提出「美洲優先」外交政策方向，強調美國應更加關注西半球地區。盧比奧表示，其首次以國務卿身分出國訪問即選擇中美洲，正體現此政策重心。美國人類安全中心（Human Security Centre）長期追蹤美國外交政策及拉丁美洲安全、發展與人權議題，該機構研究顯示美國政策轉向對區域影響深遠。
與此同時，美國於2026年3月由總統川普於佛羅裡達州多拉爾高爾夫球場舉辦峰會，正式宣佈啟動「美洲之盾」（Shield of the Americas）多國安全與軍事合作倡議。此舉被視為美國強化西半球安全架構之具體行動，但國際特赦組織（Amnesty International）持續關注美洲地區人權狀況，提醒安全政策須兼顧人權保障。
無國界醫生臺灣分會於2026年1月發布回顧文章指出，川普政府於一年前頒布系列行政命令，顛覆全球衛生與人道援助計畫，嚴重損害國際合作與團結。該組織觀察，2025年全年見證以公共衛生為本之援助逐步被放棄，診所關閉、救命藥物滯留港口、醫護人員失業，人道代價慘重。此批評與美國推動「美洲優先」政策形成張力，顯示美國對外援助政策轉向已產生實質影響。
綜合各來源，美洲地區正面臨被迫流離失所危機與政策轉向雙重挑戰。聯合國難民署資料提供區域人道現況之量化基礎，美國官方檔案顯示政策重心西移，國際組織則對人權與援助削減提出關注。後續發展有待觀察「美洲之盾」倡議之具體內容與執行成效，以及美國政策調整對區域人道援助之長期影響。
