  # Over 414 Migrants Die in Americas as UN Calls for Attention to Humanitarian Funding Gap
  
  > At least 414 migrants died or went missing in the Americas in 2025. The UN reports that humanitarian aid has received only 19% of the funding needed, with Haiti identified as one of the crisis hotspots.
  
  According to the annual regional overview published by the International Organization for Migration (IOM) and ReliefWeb, at least 414 migrants died or went missing during their migration journey in the Americas in 2025. The Caribbean region had the highest death toll with 134 fatalities, followed by North America with 131, Central America with 92, and South America accounting for the remainder. This data highlights the continued danger of transnational migration routes in the Americas, with the Caribbean and the U.S.-Mexico border emerging as the most lethal risk areas.
  
  Tom Fletcher, head of the UN Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (OCHA), stated in September at the New York headquarters that the international community has currently raised only 19% of the funding required for humanitarian aid, reflecting a severe shortfall. He also noted that conflicts and crises in multiple regions worldwide are worsening the humanitarian situation, including the Democratic Republic of Congo, Gaza, Sudan, Syria, Ukraine, and Yemen, with Haiti in the Americas also listed as one of the crisis hotspots. World Vision Taiwan, reviewing global emergency humanitarian crises in 2024, similarly noted that UN statistics show conflict, extreme climate, and economic turmoil intersecting to leave nearly 300 million people worldwide in need of assistance and protection, with poverty and hunger issues continuing to deepen.
  
  Human Rights Watch's "World Report 2026" issued harsh criticism of the human rights situation in the United States, pointing out that Trump's second term has shown blatant disregard and serious violations of human rights from the outset of his presidency. The report covers multiple areas including migrant rights, health, environment, labor, disabilities, gender equality, criminal justice, and freedom of speech, concluding that the rollback of human rights protections has become a defining feature of his term. This report echoes the data on migrant deaths in the Americas, indicating that the region faces multiple challenges on migration and human rights issues.
  
  Analysts point out that the causes of the humanitarian crisis in the Americas are complex, involving factors such as economic inequality, violent crime, climate disasters, and political turmoil. Haiti has long been in a state of gang violence and government paralysis, making it the most severe humanitarian emergency in the Caribbean region. Meanwhile, residents of the Central American "Northern Triangle" countries (Guatemala, Honduras, and El Salvador) continue to migrate northward along dangerous routes, facing human trafficking, violent coercion, and environmental threats along the way. The UN and various NGOs are calling on the international community to increase funding and promote root-cause governance to reduce the drivers of forced migration.

# 美洲移民死亡逾414人 聯合國籲關注人道資金缺口

> 2025年美洲至少414名移民死亡或失蹤，聯合國指出人道援助僅獲所需資金19%，海地列為危機熱點之一。

根據國際移徙組織（IOM）與 ReliefWeb 發布的年度區域概覽，2025年美洲地區至少有414名移民在遷徙途中死亡或失蹤。其中加勒比地區死亡人數最高，達134人；北美地區131人；中美洲92人；南美洲則佔剩餘數額。此資料凸顯美洲跨國遷徙路線的危險性持續未減，尤其加勒比海與美墨邊境成為致命風險最高的區域。
聯合國人道主義事務協調廳（OCHA）主管湯姆·弗萊徹（Tom Fletcher）於9月在紐約總部表示，當前國際社會僅籌得所需人道援助資金的19%，資金嚴重不足。他同時指出，全球多處衝突與危機正在加劇人道局勢，包括剛果民主共和國、加沙、蘇丹、敘利亞、烏克蘭與葉門，而美洲地區的海地亦被列為危機熱點之一。臺灣世界展望會回顧2024年全球緊急人道危機時亦提到，聯合國統計顯示衝突、極端氣候與經濟動盪交織，導致全球近3億人需要援助與保護，貧窮與飢餓問題持續深化。
人權觀察（Human Rights Watch）發布的《2026世界人權報告》針對美國人權狀況提出嚴厲批評，指出特朗普第二任期自上任之初即顯現對人權的公然漠視與嚴重侵犯。報告涵蓋移民權利、健康、環境、勞動、身心障礙、性別平等、刑事司法與言論自由等多個面向，認為美國在人權保障方面的倒退已成為其任期的顯著特徵。此報告與美洲移民死亡資料相互呼應，顯示美洲地區在移民與人權議題上正面臨多重挑戰。
分析人士指出，美洲人道危機的成因複雜，包含經濟不平等、暴力犯罪、氣候災害與政治動盪等因素。海地長期處於幫派暴力與政府功能癱瘓狀態，成為加勒比地區最嚴重的人道緊急情況；而中美洲「北三角」國家（瓜地馬拉、宏都拉斯、薩爾瓦多）居民持續透過危險路線向北遷徙，途中面臨人口販運、暴力脅迫與自然環境威脅。聯合國與各國非政府組織呼籲國際社會加強資金投入，並推動源頭治理，以減少被迫遷徙的動因。
