  # AI Computing Power Surge Sparks Infrastructure Crisis; Power and Cooling Become Development Bottlenecks
  
  > Surging AI computing demand prompts North America's largest grid operator PJM to consider mandatory power restrictions for major data centers, while Europe faces a strategic decision on digital sovereignty and cooling technology emerges as a critical challenge.
  
  The AI computing power boom is triggering a global infrastructure crisis. PJM Interconnection, the largest regional grid operator in North America, recently proposed mandatory power restriction policies targeting large data centers with electricity consumption exceeding 50MW, with the policy expected to take effect in 2027. PJM, which serves 67 million users across the United States, noted that AI and cloud computing have experienced explosive growth in recent years, with large data centers being built rapidly worldwide, driving unprecedented power demand. However, the growth rate of infrastructure power supply is far lagging behind the pace of computing power expansion, and the power gap is gradually becoming the biggest bottleneck for technological development.
  
  Meanwhile, the AI field is undergoing a profound transformation known as the "inference revolution." According to the American Enterprise Institute, the inference revolution refers to the process by which AI systems perform real-time translation, document synthesis, and scientific simulations—all of which heavily rely on massive and reliable distributed computing capabilities. At this critical juncture, Europe faces an important strategic choice that will determine its future positioning in the AI ecosystem. U.S. tech and AI companies are expected to invest billions in data center infrastructure over the coming years, and if Europe fails to keep pace, it risks losing competitiveness in the AI era.
  
  Cooling technology has similarly become a critical bottleneck for AI development. According to Bnext, industry projections indicate that a single AI chip's power consumption will exceed 1000W by 2026, prompting the global tech industry to actively seek next-generation cooling solutions. The Taoyuan accelerator team is developing a second-generation Trahvo cooling technology to address increasingly severe thermal challenges. This demonstrates that AI computing power expansion involves not only power supply but also requires simultaneous upgrades to cooling infrastructure.
  
  Across various media reports, the infrastructure challenges brought by AI development exhibit multifaceted characteristics. PJM's power restriction policy highlights the bottleneck in power infrastructure, Europe's digital sovereignty strategy reflects geopolitical and industrial competition considerations, and cooling technology innovation represents a key breakthrough at the technical level. These challenges are interconnected, collectively forming the core issues of infrastructure development in the AI era. Experts point out that if power, cooling, and other infrastructure cannot be upgraded in sync, AI computing power expansion will face severe limitations, thereby affecting the global technology competition landscape.

# AI算力爆發引發基礎設施危機 電力散熱成發展瓶頸

> 人工智慧算力需求激增，北美最大電網PJM擬對大型資料中心實施強制限電，歐洲面臨數位主權戰略抉擇，散熱技術亦成關鍵挑戰。

人工智慧（AI）算力熱潮正引發全球基礎設施危機。北美最大區域電網營運商PJM Interconnection近期提出強制限電政策，針對用電量達50MW以上的大型資料中心實施負載調整，該政策預計於2027年上路。涵蓋全美6,700萬使用者的PJM指出，近年來人工智慧與雲端運算迎來爆發性成長，大型資料中心在全球各地如雨後春筍般興建，推升了前所未有的龐大電力需求，但基礎設施的供電成長速度遠趕不及算力擴張步伐，電力缺口正逐漸成為科技發展的最大瓶頸。
與此同時，AI領域正經歷被稱為「推論革命」的深刻轉變。根據《American Enterprise Institute》報導，推論革命指的是AI系統執行即時翻譯、檔案合成與科學模擬等功能的過程，這些都高度仰賴龐大且可靠的分散式運算能力。歐洲在此關鍵時刻面臨重要的戰略選擇，決定其在AI生態系中的未來定位。美國科技與AI公司預計未來幾年將在資料中心基礎設施上投入鉅額資金，歐洲若未能跟上腳步，恐在AI時代失去競爭力。
散熱技術同樣成為AI發展的重要瓶頸。根據Bnext報導，產業預估2026年單顆AI晶片功耗將突破1000W，全球科技產業正積極尋找下一代散熱解方。桃園加速器團隊正在開發第二代Trahvo散熱技術，以應對日益嚴峻的散熱挑戰。這顯示AI算力擴張不僅涉及電力供應，散熱基礎設施亦需同步升級。
綜合各家媒體報導，AI發展帶來的基礎設施挑戰呈現多面向特徵。PJM的限電政策凸顯了電力基礎設施的瓶頸，歐洲的數位主權戰略反映了地緣政治與產業競爭的考量，而散熱技術的創新則是技術層面的關鍵突破。這些挑戰相互關聯，共同構成了AI時代基礎設施發展的核心議題。專家指出，若電力、散熱等基礎設施無法同步升級，AI算力擴張將面臨嚴重限制，進而影響全球科技競爭格局。
