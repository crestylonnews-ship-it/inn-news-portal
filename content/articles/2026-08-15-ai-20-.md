  # Asia's AI Boom Heats Up as Taiwan Surges into Global Top 20; ITRI Ranks AI as Leading Key Technology of the Future
  
  > Microsoft's report shows Asia has become the main driver of AI growth, with Taiwan's AI diffusion rate reaching 31.8% in Q1 2026; ITRI released its list of Asia's top ten key future technologies with AI ranked first; cloud AI investment exceeded $160 billion in 2026.
  
  Microsoft's AI Economic Research Institute recently released its updated global AI adoption report, revealing that Asia has become the primary engine of recent artificial intelligence growth. The report notes that since mid-2025, 12 of the world's 15 fastest-growing markets are located in Asia, covering both developed and emerging economies. Notably, Taiwan's AI diffusion rate rose to 31.8% in the first quarter of 2026, climbing three positions from the second half of last year and successfully breaking into the global top 20. This data indicates that Taiwan is accelerating its efforts to catch up with international standards in AI technology implementation and industrial applications.
  
  To keep pace with forward-looking technology development trends in Asia, ITRI recently published its annual special report, identifying ten key emerging technologies for Asia's future from among 150 new technologies, with artificial intelligence ranking at the top. The report further points out that the pace of AI adoption in Asia is significantly higher than the global average, particularly in East and Southeast Asia, where government policy initiatives and private investment enthusiasm reinforce each other, creating strong industrial development momentum. ITRI's analysis suggests that AI has become not merely a technological trend but a core strategy for Asian countries to enhance their international competitiveness.
  
  In terms of investment scale, technology giants continue to shift toward Asia, with cloud AI investment surpassing the $160 billion mark in 2026. According to relevant analysis, Western tech giants are rooting their operations in Asia to expand their global footprint, a trend that not only drives infrastructure investment but also accelerates local talent development and ecosystem building. However, the International Energy Agency's "Energy and AI 2025" report also warns that the capital expenditure boom is likely to persist amid rising energy prices, and AI investment will need to contend with the challenge of increasing energy costs, requiring investors to carefully select their targets.
  
  The overall scale of Asia's AI market has reached $102 billion, encompassing diverse areas such as sovereign large language models, chip supply chains, and national policy frameworks. All ten member states of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) are partner countries under Taiwan's New Southbound Policy, and countries within the region are actively formulating AI governance and development policies in an effort to secure a favorable position in technological competition. Studies such as Deloitte's "Asia-Pacific 2026 Report" and PwC's "Asia-Pacific Clean Energy Gap" also indicate that Asia's AI development must balance energy sustainability with technological innovation to maintain its competitive edge in the long term.
  
  A comprehensive review of multiple reports reveals that Asia's AI development exhibits three key characteristics: strong growth momentum, active policy promotion, and expanding investment scale. However, it simultaneously faces challenges related to energy costs, semiconductor supply chain stability, and talent shortages. Taiwan, leveraging its semiconductor industry foundation and digital transformation policies, has delivered impressive performance in AI diffusion rates. How Taiwan strengthens cross-disciplinary integration and international cooperation in the future will be the key factor in determining whether it can sustain its leading position.

# 亞洲AI熱潮升溫 臺灣躍居全球前20 工研院列AI為未來十大關鍵技術之首

> 微軟報告顯示亞洲已成AI成長主要動能，臺灣2026年首季AI擴散率達31.8%；工研院發布亞洲未來十大關鍵技術，人工智慧居首；2026年雲端AI投資突破1600億美元。

微軟AI經濟研究院近期發布全球AI採用更新報告，揭示亞洲已成為近期人工智慧成長的主要動能。報告指出，自2025年中以來，全球成長最快的15個市場中，有12個位於亞洲，涵蓋已開發與新興經濟體。值得注意的是，臺灣在2026年第一季的AI擴散率已成長至31.8%，較去年下半年排名提升三位，成功躋身全球前20名。此資料顯示臺灣在AI技術落地與產業應用方面，正加速追趕國際步伐。
工研院為掌握亞洲未來前瞻科技發展趨勢，於近日發表年度專刊，從150項新技術中盤點出未來亞洲十大關鍵前瞻技術，其中又以人工智慧位居榜首。該報告進一步指出，AI技術在亞洲的普及速度顯著高於全球平均水平，尤其在東亞與東南亞地區，政府政策推動與民間投資熱忱相互疊加，形成強勁的產業發展動能。工研院分析認為，AI已不僅是技術趨勢，更成為亞洲各國提升國際競爭力的核心戰略。
在投資規模方面，科技巨頭持續向亞洲遷徙，2026年雲端AI投資金額已突破1600億美元大關。根據相關分析，西方科技巨頭正將業務根植亞洲，以擴大全球足跡，此趨勢不僅帶動基礎設施投資，也促使當地人才培育與生態系建構加速。然而，國際能源署《能源與人工智慧2025》報告亦提醒，資本開支熱潮料將在能源價格升勢下持續，AI投資需面對能源成本上升的挑戰，投資者須精挑細選標的。
亞洲AI市場整體規模已達1020億美元，涵蓋主權大型語言模型、晶片供應鏈、各國政策框架等多元面向。東南亞國家協會（東協）十個成員國皆為臺灣新南向政策夥伴國，區域內各國正積極制定AI治理與發展政策，以期在技術競爭中佔據有利地位。德勤《亞太區2026年報告》與羅兵鹹永道《亞太潔淨能源缺口》等研究亦指出，亞洲AI發展需兼顧能源永續與技術創新，方能在長期競爭中維持優勢。
綜合多項報告觀察，亞洲AI發展呈現「成長動能強勁、政策積極推動、投資規模擴大」三大特徵，但同時面臨能源成本、晶片供應鏈穩定性與人才短缺等挑戰。臺灣憑藉半導體產業基礎與數位轉型政策，在AI擴散率上表現亮眼，未來如何強化跨領域整合與國際合作，將是決定能否持續領先的關鍵。
