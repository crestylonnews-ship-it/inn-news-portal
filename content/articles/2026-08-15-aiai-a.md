  # Samsung Develops AI Chip; Sun Daqian Publishes Book on AI Agents; Asia Policy and Investment Trends
  
  > Samsung is secretly developing the Gaia AI accelerator chip, expected to enter mass production next year; Sun Daqian publishes "The New Era of AI Agents" exploring the direction of AI development; Asian countries actively formulate AI policies, and investment institutions are optimistic about the region's tech potential.
  
  South Korean chip giant Samsung Electronics is secretly developing a PC-specific AI accelerator chip codenamed "Gaia," and has already delivered initial samples to select key customers for functional verification. According to Techbang, the chip uses an advanced 4-nanometer process, targets the edge AI and physical robotics markets, offers excellent energy efficiency, and supports running large language models (LLMs) locally. It is expected to enter mass production next year. Samsung's move aims to fill its absence in the personal computer processor and edge AI markets, leading the new generation of smart computers with ultimate performance.
  
  Former Taiwanese legislator and current professor at the Institute of Technology and Finance, Chin Shu College, Sun Daqian, published his fifth AI book, "The New Era of AI Agents," on August 14, available simultaneously in physical and online bookstores. Sun Daqian noted that artificial intelligence is advancing rapidly, and the global focus is shifting from generative AI to AI agents. The new book aims to further explore the direction of AI evolution in the coming years and how enterprises and individuals should respond. This publication reflects the high attention paid by Taiwan's academic and industrial circles to AI Agent technology trends.
  
  On the policy front, Digital Asia (digitalinasia.com) released its Q2 2026 update of the Asia AI Policy Tracker, covering AI legislation and national strategies in China, Japan, South Korea, India, Vietnam, Singapore, Malaysia, and Thailand.
  
  The World Bank also released a report titled "Future Jobs: Robots, Artificial Intelligence, and Digital Platforms in East Asia and the Pacific," examining how industrial robots, AI, and digital platforms are reshaping employment, productivity, and labor markets in the East Asia and Pacific region.
  
  Institutional investors such as Allianz Global Investors and Eastspring Investments have also released analyses indicating that Asia's tech growth story remains compelling. The region's tech sector sits at the forefront of the global AI revolution, and despite the need for careful assessment of geopolitical and currency risks, Asia continues to offer diverse and promising corporate investment opportunities.
  
  A comprehensive observation reveals that Samsung's entry into the PC AI chip market, Taiwan scholars' focus on AI Agent research, Asian countries accelerating AI policy formulation, and international investment institutions' optimism about Asia's tech potential all indicate that Asia is forming a complete ecosystem across four dimensions: AI hardware, software, policy, and capital. Over the coming months, the mass production timeline and customer response for Samsung's Gaia chip will serve as key indicators for observing the competitive landscape of Asia's AI hardware sector.

# 三星研發AI晶片、孫大千出書解析AI Agent、亞洲政策與投資動向

> 三星秘密研發Gaia AI加速晶片預計明年量產；孫大千出版《AI Agent新紀元》探討AI發展方向；亞洲各國積極制定AI政策，投資機構看好區域科技潛力。

南韓晶片巨擘三星電子正秘密研發一款代號為「Gaia」的PC專用AI加速晶片，目前已向部分重要客戶交付初期樣品進行功能驗證。根據Techbang報導，這款晶片採用先進4奈米製程，鎖定邊緣AI與實體機器人市場，具備優異能效，支援在本地執行大型語言模型（LLM），預計明年量產。三星此舉旨在填補其在個人電腦處理器與邊緣AI市場的缺席，以極致效能引領智慧電腦新世代。
臺灣前立法委員、現任中信金融管理學院科技金融研究所教授孫大千於8月14日出版個人第5本AI著作《AI Agent新紀元》，並於實體及線上書店同步開賣。孫大千指出，人工智慧發展速度快速，全球關注焦點正從生成式AI轉向AI代理人（AI Agent），新書希望進一步探討人工智慧未來幾年的演進方向，以及企業與個人應如何因應。此出版動態反映臺灣學界與產業界對AI Agent技術趨勢的高度關注。
在政策層面，數位亞洲網站（digitalinasia.com）發布2026年第二季更新的亞洲AI政策追蹤器，涵蓋中國、日本、南韓、印度、越南、新加坡、馬來西亞與泰國等國的AI立法與國家戰略。
世界銀行同期發布報告《Future Jobs: Robots, Artificial Intelligence, and Digital Platforms in East Asia and the Pacific》，探討工業機器人、AI與數位平臺如何改變東亞與太平洋地區的就業、生產力與勞動市場。
安聯投資與Eastspring投資等機構亦發布分析指出，亞洲科技成長故事依然引人入勝，區域科技部門位居全球AI革命前沿，儘管地緣政治與匯率風險須謹慎評估，亞洲仍提供多元具潛力的企業投資機會。
綜合觀察，三星進軍PC AI晶片市場、臺灣學者聚焦AI Agent研究、亞洲各國加速AI政策制定，以及國際投資機構看好亞洲科技潛力，顯示亞洲在AI硬體、軟體、政策與資本四大面向正形成完整生態系。未來幾個月，三星Gaia晶片的量產時程與客戶反應，將是觀察亞洲AI硬體競爭格局的重要指標。
