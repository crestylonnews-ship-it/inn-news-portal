  # Turkey Strengthens Eurasian Trade Hub Status as APEC Continues to Advance Asia-Pacific Free Trade
  
  > Turkey is actively leveraging connectivity as a tool for strategic autonomy, reinforcing its hub role in Eurasian trade, transport, and energy; APEC continues to promote free trade cooperation across the Asia-Pacific region.
  
  The Turkish government is accelerating its regional strategic autonomy policy by strengthening connectivity in trade, transport, and energy infrastructure, positioning itself as a stable hub between Europe and Asia. According to an analysis shared on LinkedIn by the Stuttgart Institute for Security Affairs and International Affairs (SWP Berlin), Ankara is actively capitalizing on its geographic advantages, using connectivity as a core tool to enhance regional influence, particularly amid rising regional instability such as disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz, as Turkey swiftly responds and consolidates its strategic position.
  
  The Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC), as an intergovernmental forum of 21 member economies along the Pacific Rim, continues to promote free trade and economic integration across the Asia-Pacific region. Since its establishment, APEC has served as a vital platform for advancing regional economic cooperation, working to reduce trade barriers, promote investment liberalization, and strengthen policy coordination among members. APEC's operational model emphasizes consensus-based decision-making and non-binding commitments, enabling member economies to advance regional economic integration while respecting differences in their respective stages of development.
  
  Turkey's strategic pivot and APEC's regional cooperation mechanism present an interesting contrast. Turkey is seeking to play a more proactive role between Europe and Asia, transforming itself into a key node for energy and cargo transport through infrastructure investment and diplomatic mediation. This strategy not only reflects Turkey's response to geopolitical shifts but also demonstrates how middle powers can elevate their international standing through economic connectivity.
  
  Analyses indicate that Turkey's path toward strategic autonomy faces multiple challenges, including regional security conditions, economic stability, and the need to balance relations with major powers. Meanwhile, APEC member economies must also address global issues such as rising protectionism and supply chain restructuring in their pursuit of free trade. Although geographically distant, both regions share the common challenge of maintaining competitiveness and stability amid global economic transformations.
  
  Future observations will focus on the actual progress of Turkey's infrastructure projects and the concrete outcomes of APEC in promoting regional economic integration. The experiences of both regions may offer reference cases for other areas on how to enhance strategic autonomy through economic connectivity.

# 土耳其強化歐亞貿易樞紐地位，APEC持續推動亞太自由貿易

> 土耳其積極以連線性作為戰略自主工具，強化其在歐亞貿易、運輸與能源領域的樞紐角色；APEC持續推動亞太地區自由貿易合作。

土耳其政府正加速推進其區域戰略自主政策，透過強化貿易、運輸與能源基礎設施的連線性，將自身定位為歐亞之間穩定的樞紐。根據LinkedIn上分享的斯圖加特世界事務與安全研究所（SWP Berlin）分析指出，安卡拉正積極利用地緣位置優勢，以連線性作為提升區域影響力的核心工具，特別是在霍爾木茲海峽中斷等區域不穩定因素增加的背景下，土耳其正迅速回應並鞏固其戰略地位。
亞太經濟合作組織（APEC）作為太平洋沿岸21個成員經濟體的政府間論壇，持續推動亞太地區的自由貿易與經濟整合。該組織自成立以來，一直是促進區域經濟合作的重要平臺，致力於降低貿易壁壘、促進投資自由化，並加強成員間的政策協調。APEC的運作模式強調共識決策與非約束性承諾，使各成員經濟體能在尊重彼此發展階段差異的前提下，共同推動區域經濟一體化程式。
土耳其的戰略轉向與APEC的區域合作機制形成有趣對比。土耳其正試圖在歐亞之間扮演更主動的角色，透過基礎建設投資與外交斡旋，將自身打造為能源與貨物運輸的關鍵節點。這種策略不僅反映土耳其對地緣政治變局的回應，也顯示中等強國如何透過經濟連線性來提升國際地位。
分析指出，土耳其的戰略自主路徑面臨多項挑戰，包括區域安全形勢、經濟穩定性以及與主要大國的關係平衡。同時，APEC成員經濟體在推動自由貿易過程中，也需應對保護主義抬頭、供應鏈重組等全球性課題。兩地雖地理上相隔遙遠，但都面臨如何在全球經濟變局中維持競爭力與穩定性的共同挑戰。
未來觀察將聚焦於土耳其基礎建設專案的實際進展，以及APEC在促進區域經濟整合方面的具體成果。兩地經驗或可為其他區域提供關於如何透過經濟連線性提升戰略自主性的參考案例。
