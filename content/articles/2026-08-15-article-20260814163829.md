  # Shadows of US-China Trade War Linger, Scholars Warn of Economic Recession, Think Tanks Focus on Industrial Security
  
  > Scholars point out that the US economy may face recession starting in 2021 due to the impact of the trade war, while the White House insists the economy remains sound; think tanks and industry reports focus on tariff restructuring and the development of industrial clusters in the Americas.
  
  According to a report by Housefun News (好房網News), the ongoing impact of the US-China trade war has drawn attention from the economics community. The media cited scholars' views indicating that the question is not whether the economy will recession, but when; despite the White House's insistence that the US economy remains robust and healthy, global economic prospects are growing increasingly weak, and economists and investors are increasingly questioning whether the US economy can withstand a prolonged trade war with China without suffering serious damage. This view reflects market concerns over the long-term consequences of the trade war, but a clear divergence exists between the White House's stance and scholars' predictions, awaiting further economic data for verification.
  
  The Chinese Institute of Economic Research (CIER) released its "2026 Global Economic Outlook" special publication, themed "Global Turbulence Rises, Economic Security Takes Priority," presenting researchers' analyses of the 2025 economic review and key issues across regions in 2026. The think tank report emphasizes the impact of tariff restructuring and the "America First" policy on the global economic landscape, and points out that the Americas face new economic security challenges amid trade policy adjustments. The report focuses on trend analysis at the policy level, echoing the scholars' views cited by Housefun News and jointly highlighting the potential impact of the trade war on the Americas economy.
  
  IEK Industry Intelligence Network (IEK產業情報網) provides detailed information on key US industrial clusters and business opportunities in 2025, covering hot topics such as opportunities for Taiwan-US technology and industry cooperation under the 31 Tech Hubs promoted by the US through the CHIPS Act, business opportunities for Taiwan entrepreneurs driven by US-Canada hydrogen energy industry development, and incentives, clusters, and development opportunities for Taiwan entrepreneurs in Mexico's electric vehicle industry under the USMCA framework. The report analyzes the reshaping of the Americas supply chain by US policies from an industrial practice perspective, complementing the aforementioned economic outlook report and demonstrating that the trade war not only affects the macroeconomy but also profoundly changes regional industrial layouts.
  
  Synthesizing various sources, scholars' concerns about economic recession, think tanks' analyses of tariff restructuring, and the industry intelligence network's observations on cluster business opportunities collectively outline the multi-dimensional impact of the US trade war on the Americas economy; subsequent developments will require continued attention to policy implementation and market responses.

# 美貿易戰陰霾未散 學者憂經濟衰退 智庫聚焦產業安全

> 學者指出美國經濟或於2021年起受貿易戰衝擊而衰退，白宮則堅持經濟健全；智庫與產業報告聚焦關稅重構與美洲產業聚落發展。

根據好房網News報導，美國與中國貿易戰的持續影響引發經濟學界關注。該媒體引述學者觀點指出，問題不在於經濟是否會衰退，而在於何時衰退；儘管白宮堅持美國經濟仍健全強勁，但全球經濟展望日益疲弱，經濟學家與投資者日益懷疑美國經濟能否承受與中國長期貿易戰而不受嚴重損害。此說法反映市場對貿易戰長期後果的擔憂，但白宮立場與學者預測之間存在明顯分歧，尚待後續經濟資料驗證。
中華經濟研究院發布之《2026年全球經濟展望》專書以「全球局勢風雲起 經濟安全優先」為主題，呈現研究人員針對2025年經濟回顧與2026年各地區重要議題之分析。該智庫報告強調關稅重構與美國優先政策對全球經濟格局之影響，並指出美洲地區在貿易政策調整下面臨新的經濟安全挑戰。報告內容聚焦於政策層面之趨勢分析，與好房網News所載學者觀點相互呼應，共同凸顯貿易戰對美洲經濟之潛在衝擊。
IEK產業情報網則提供2025年美國重點產業聚落與商機之詳細資訊，涵蓋晶片法案下美國推動31個Tech Hubs對臺美科技與產業合作之機會、美加氫能產業發展推動臺商商機，以及USMCA框架下墨西哥電動車產業之誘因、聚落與臺商發展機會等熱門議題。該報告從產業實務角度分析美國政策對美洲供應鏈之重塑，與前述經濟展望報告形成互補，顯示貿易戰不僅影響宏觀經濟，更深刻改變區域產業佈局。
綜合各來源，學者對經濟衰退之擔憂、智庫對關稅重構之分析，以及產業情報網對聚落商機之觀察，共同勾勒出美國貿易戰對美洲經濟之多層面影響，後續發展仍需關注政策落實與市場反應。
