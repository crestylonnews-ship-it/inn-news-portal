  # UNESCO Deepens Education Cooperation in Africa; Taiwan's Representative to South Africa Attends the Second Africa Region Scout Camp
  
  > UNESCO continues to advance its "Africa Priority" education policy, as Taiwan's Representative to South Africa, Liao Wenzhe, attended the second Africa Region Scout Camp, demonstrating Taiwan's intent to strengthen exchanges with Africa in education and youth affairs.
  
  The United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) has long promoted education development in Africa. Since the launch of its "Africa Priority" policy in 1985, a systematic cooperation framework has been established. The organization formally created its Africa Department in 1996 to build long-term and stable partnerships with African nations, laying the foundation for subsequent policy consultation, educational assistance, and resource support. According to UNESCO's official website, the organization is leading various efforts to promote inclusive, equitable, and quality education across African regions, with related initiatives covering multiple dimensions such as increasing educational access, improving teaching quality, and promoting lifelong learning opportunities.
  
  Chinese media outlet The Paper reported that UNESCO is implementing the values of its education policies across the African continent through institutions such as the Africa International Institute for Capacity Building, emphasizing that this reflects the international community's high regard for education development in Africa and that strengthened educational cooperation is expected to drive the comprehensive socioeconomic development of African nations. The report further explained that the Africa International Institute for Capacity Building, a subsidiary of UNESCO, plays a key role in advancing the "Africa Priority" initiative by assisting African nations in strengthening their education systems.
  
  Meanwhile, Taiwan's grassroots exchanges with Africa continue. According to United Daily News, Ambassador Liao Wenzhe, Taipei Liaison Representative to the Republic of South Africa, led a delegation of overseas Chinese representatives and staff of the Representative Office to attend the cultural day event of the second Africa Region Scout Camp at Arrowe Park Scout Centre in South Africa on August 7 local time. They exchanged ideas with Scout representatives from Africa and around the world and extended an invitation for the 2029 World Scout event to further deepen bilateral relations. This move shows that Taiwan's diplomatic and grassroots exchanges in Africa continue to focus on youth development and international cooperation.
  
  In the religious sphere, the Chinese-language website of The Gospel Coalition reported that East Africa is emerging as an important face of the global church's future development, with Western churches actively leveraging resources to walk alongside African churches toward a vibrant future. The report emphasized that sub-Saharan Africa, particularly the East African region, is demonstrating distinctive characteristics in church development and has become an important center of global Christian faith.
  
  Synthesizing information from various sources, UNESCO's education policies in Africa and Taiwan's grassroots exchanges through its Representative to South Africa reflect the international community's attention to Africa's development from different angles. However, the specific progress of Taiwan's official relations with Africa, as well as the actual implementation effectiveness of UNESCO's policies across African countries, remain to be further observed and confirmed.

# 聯合國教科文組織深化非洲教育合作 臺灣駐南非代表參與羅浮大會

> 聯合國教科文組織持續推動「非洲優先」教育政策，駐南非代表廖文哲出席第二屆非洲區羅浮大會，展現臺灣與非洲在教育與青年事務上的交流意圖。

聯合國教科文組織（UNESCO）長期推動非洲教育發展，其「非洲優先」政策自1985年啟動以來，已建立系統性的合作框架。該組織於1996年正式成立非洲部，旨在與非洲國家建立長期穩定的夥伴關係，並為後續提供政策諮詢、教育援助與資源扶持奠定基礎。根據UNESCO官方網站指出，該組織正領導各項努力，推動非洲各地區實現包容、公平且優質的教育目標，相關倡議涵蓋提升教育普及率、改善教學品質，以及促進終身學習機會等多個面向。
中國媒體《澎湃新聞》報導指出，聯合國教科文組織透過非洲國際能力建設研究所等機構，將教育政策的價值落實於非洲大地，強調此舉體現國際社會對非洲教育發展的高度重視，期望透過加強教育合作，推動非洲國家經濟社會的全面發展。該報導進一步說明，非洲國際能力建設研究所作為UNESCO下屬機構，在推動「非洲優先」計畫中扮演關鍵角色，協助非洲國家提升教育體系的能力建設。
另一方面，臺灣與非洲的民間交流亦持續進行。據《聯合新聞網》報導，駐南非共和國臺北聯絡代表處大使廖文哲於南非當地時間8月7日，率領僑界代表及代表處同仁，前往Arrowe Park Scout Centre出席第二屆非洲區羅浮大會的文化日活動，與來自非洲及世界各地的童軍代表進行交流，邀請世界童軍2029年活動進一步深化雙邊關係。此舉顯示臺灣在非洲的外交與民間交流活動，仍持續關注青年發展與國際合作議題。
宗教領域方面，《福音聯盟》中文網站報導指出，東非地區正成為全球教會未來發展的重要面貌，西方教會正積極善用資源，與非洲教會攜手同行，共同邁向充滿活力的未來。該報導強調，撒哈拉以南非洲，尤其是東非地區，正展現鮮明的教會發展特色，成為全球基督教信仰的重要中心。
綜合各來源資訊，UNESCO在非洲的教育政策與臺灣駐南非代表的民間交流，分別從不同面向呈現國際社會對非洲發展的關注。然而，臺灣與非洲官方關係的具體進展，以及UNESCO政策在非洲各國的實際落實成效，仍有待進一步觀察與確認。
