  # Asia's Trade Rise Becomes Global Engine: Multi-Source Analysis of Regional Challenges and Opportunities
  
  > Asia accounts for over a third of global import and export volume, with strong prospects for intra-regional trade; however, WTO stagnation and geopolitical shifts present challenges, while the evolving trade dynamics between BRICS and G7 will shape the regional economic landscape.
  
  Asian economies stand at a critical juncture in the transformation of the global trade system. According to a report released by Thomson Reuters titled "State of Asia: Position as a Global Trade Leader," the Asia region accounts for over a third of the world's import and export volume and has been the primary engine of global trade growth over the past decade. The report, recently released, provides a comprehensive analysis of the challenges and opportunities Asia faces amid a rapidly changing geopolitical and economic environment, emphasizing that the drivers of Asia's trade performance stem from intra-regional supply chain integration and investment development prospects.
  
  The foundational scale of the Asian economy is vast. Wikipedia data indicates that the Asian economy refers to the economic entity composed of 46 countries and over 4 billion people residing in Asia, accounting for 60% of the world's population. In addition, six other countries have partial territory located in Asia but are considered politically or economically part of other regions. This massive population base and market size provide a solid foundation for intra-regional trade in Asia.
  
  Intra-regional trade prospects have drawn attention. An Online Magazine report indicates that if a trade war erupts, it would benefit the prospects of the intra-Asian shipping market, with China's eastern maritime trade representing the shortest intra-Asian route and carrying the highest cargo value. The report also notes that investment development prospects within Asia remain positive, and robust intra-regional trade can be expected in the future, with development in the Greater Mekong Subregion set to drive the prosperity of intra-Asian trade. This perspective focuses on cross-border trade dynamics across East Asia, South Asia, and Southeast Asia.
  
  The global trade system is facing multiple challenges. Data from the Ministry of Economic Affairs indicates that the world is at a critical moment in determining the future of a rules-based trade system. In recent years, global trade has faced challenges including the stagnation of WTO functions, certain countries treating trade as a weapon to coerce others, and trade rules failing to keep pace with changes in technology, climate, economic inclusion, and supply chain issues. This viewpoint underscores the structural dilemmas of the international trade rule system.
  
  The trajectory of trade relations between BRICS and G7 is also under scrutiny. A BEA Wise report analyzes that if alliance cohesion weakens, it is expected to help balance international trade, and the currently tense atmosphere in regions such as Europe or South Asia may see trade relations with countries like China warm up, easing their trade pressures. The report further points out that China could also enhance trade exchanges with BRICS member countries; as long as BRICS reduces trade with G7, the impact on the less productive G7 economies would be evident. This is a single-source analytical perspective and awaits further verification.
  
  Synthesizing multi-source information, Asia's intra-regional trade network is undergoing structural adjustment. Intra-regional shipping markets, investment flows, and supply chain reorganization will continue to influence the regional economic landscape. WTO reform processes, geopolitical shifts, and BRICS expansion are all key variables. Subsequent observation will focus on the progress of intra-regional trade agreement signings, changes in shipping cargo values, and cross-border investment flows.

# 亞洲貿易崛起成全球引擎 多源分析區內挑戰與機遇

> 亞洲佔全球進出口額逾三分之一，區內貿易前景看好；惟WTO功能停滯、地緣政治變局帶來挑戰，BRICS與G7貿易關係走向影響區域經濟格局。

亞洲經濟體正處於全球貿易體系變革的關鍵節點。根據湯森路透釋出的《亞洲作為全球貿易領導者的現狀調查》報告指出，亞洲地區佔世界進出口額的三分之一以上，過去十年來一直是全球貿易成長的主要引擎。該報告於近期釋出，全面分析了亞洲在急遽變化的地緣政治和經濟環境中面臨的挑戰與機會，強調亞洲貿易表現的驅動力來自區內產業鏈整合與投資發展前景。
亞洲經濟的基礎規模龐大。維基百科資料顯示，亞洲經濟指由居住在亞洲的46個國家、超過40億人口所組成的經濟體，佔世界人口的60%。除此之外，另有6個國家部分領土位於亞洲，但在政治上或經濟上被認為屬於其他地區。這一龐大的人口基礎與市場規模，為亞洲區內貿易提供了堅實根基。
區內貿易前景受到關注。《Online Magazine》報導指出，貿易戰若爆發將有利於亞洲區內航運市場前景，中國東部海上貿易屬亞洲區內最短航線，運輸貨值最高。報導同時提到，亞洲內部投資發展前景看好，區內貿易未來興旺可期，大湄公河次區域發展將推動亞洲區內貿易繁榮。此說法聚焦於東亞、南亞與東南亞的跨國貿易動向。
全球貿易體系正面臨多重挑戰。經濟部資料顯示，世界正處於決定規則為基礎的貿易體系未來的關鍵時刻，近年全球貿易面臨挑戰，包括WTO功能停滯、若干國家將貿易作為脅迫他國之武器，且貿易規則跟不上科技、氣候、經濟包容以及供應鏈議題的變化。此觀點強調國際貿易規則體系的結構性困境。
BRICS與G7的貿易關係走向亦受矚目。《BEA Wise》報導分析，若聯盟向心力下降，料有利平衡國際貿易，上劍拔弩張的氛圍歐洲或南亞等地區與中國等國家的貿易關係料將回暖，減輕其貿易上的壓力。報導進一步指出，中國亦可增進與BRICS成員國的貿易往來，只要BRICS減少與G7的貿易往來，相信對缺乏生產力的G7經濟衝擊是顯而易見。此為單一來源的分析觀點，尚待進一步驗證。
綜合多源資訊，亞洲區內貿易網路正經歷結構性調整。區內航運市場、投資流動與產業鏈重組，將持續影響區域經濟格局。WTO改革程式、地緣政治變局與BRICS擴充套件，均為關鍵變數。後續觀察將聚焦於區內貿易協定簽署進度、航運貨值變化與跨國投資流向。
