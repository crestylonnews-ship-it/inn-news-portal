  # EU Strengthens Energy and Climate Diplomacy, Advances Global Net-Zero Transition
  
  > Through its "EU Energy and Climate Diplomacy" document, the EU Council emphasizes enhancing sovereignty through strengthened diplomatic action and deepening partnerships with third countries to drive the global clean transition.
  
  The European Environment Agency (EEA) annually publishes the "Trends and Projections in Europe" report, which updates assessments of progress toward climate mitigation and energy goals across the EU and individual European countries, while also outlining the EU's targets for 2030 and 2050 in reducing greenhouse gas emissions, increasing renewable energy generation, and improving energy efficiency. The report serves as a key reference for EU climate policy, continuously tracking each country's carbon reduction progress and future projections.
  
  The EU Council recently adopted the document "EU Energy and Climate Diplomacy – Strengthening Sovereignty and Advancing the Global Clean Transition," which notes that amid geopolitical turmoil and immense pressure on the international order, with rising energy security and climate risks, the EU will enhance its sovereignty through strengthened diplomatic action and promote the global net-zero transition. Meanwhile, the EU Council has invited the EU High Representative, the European Commission, and member states to advance cross-sectoral cooperation through the "Team Europe" approach, deepen partnerships with third countries, and promote global stability and sustainable development. This move demonstrates that the EU is placing energy security and climate issues at the core of its overall diplomatic strategy.
  
  Since launching the European Green Deal in 2019, the EU has incorporated climate targets into the European Climate Law, giving them legal binding force and requiring the EU to place climate issues at the center of decision-making, actively adjusting existing regulations and policies to ensure the achievement of carbon reduction goals. The European Green Deal not only affects the EU's internal industrial structure but also extends to international trade through mechanisms such as carbon border taxes, profoundly impacting global supply chains. CNA reported that, facing challenges such as changing geopolitical circumstances, AI-driven electricity demand, and climate targets, many European countries are re-planning their energy layouts, unveiling an "energy transformation" crucial to European industrial development and economic security.
  
  A synthesis of various sources indicates that the EU is reinforcing its leadership position in global climate governance through a three-pronged approach of policy documents, legal binding force, and diplomatic action. However, the green energy transition is accompanied by tensions between industrial adjustment and energy security, and subsequent developments warrant continued attention.

# 歐盟強化能源氣候外交 推進全球淨零轉型

> 歐盟理事會透過《歐盟能源與氣候外交》檔案，強調透過強化外交行動提升主權，並與第三國深化夥伴關係，推動全球清潔轉型。

歐洲環境署（EEA）每年發布《歐洲趨勢與展望報告》（Trends and Projections in Europe），更新歐盟及各歐洲國家朝向氣候減緩與能源目標的進展評估，並展望歐盟2030年與2050年在減少溫室氣體排放、增加再生能源發電及提升能源效率等方面的目標。該報告為歐盟氣候政策的重要參考依據，持續追蹤各國減碳進度與未來投影。
歐盟理事會近期透過《歐盟能源與氣候外交－強化主權並推進全球清潔轉型》檔案，指出在地緣政治動盪與國際秩序承受巨大壓力、能源安全與氣候風險升高的背景下，歐盟將透過強化外交行動提升主權，並推動全球淨零轉型。同時，歐盟理事會邀請歐盟高階代表、歐盟執委會與會員國，以「歐洲團隊」（Team Europe）模式推動跨部門合作，深化與第三國的夥伴關係，促進全球穩定與永續發展。此舉顯示歐盟正將能源安全與氣候議題納入整體外交戰略核心。
歐盟自2019年推出歐洲綠色新政，將氣候目標納入《歐洲氣候法》並賦予法律約束力，規範歐盟須將氣候議題置於決策核心，積極調整現行法規及政策以確保達成減碳目標。歐洲綠色新政不僅影響歐盟內部產業結構，更透過碳關稅等機制擴充套件至國際貿易層面，對全球供應鏈產生深遠影響。中央社報導指出，面臨地緣政治情勢改變、AI帶動耗電需求與氣候目標等挑戰，歐洲多國正重新謀劃能源佈局，揭開攸關歐洲工業發展與經濟安全的「能源變局」。
綜合各來源觀察，歐盟正透過政策檔案、法律約束與外交行動三管齊下，強化其在全球氣候治理中的領導地位。然而，綠能轉型過程亦伴隨產業調整與能源安全之間的張力，後續進展值得持續關注。
