  # U.S. Releases "America First Global Health Strategy" Focused on Infectious Disease Prevention and Control in the Americas
  
  > The U.S. State Department released the "America First Global Health Strategy," proposing a three-pillar framework that emphasizes preventing infectious diseases from spreading to U.S. soil while enhancing global health security.
  
  The U.S. State Department recently released the "America First Global Health Strategy," which presents a comprehensive vision aimed at saving millions of lives worldwide and preventing infectious disease outbreaks from spreading to U.S. territory, while making the United States safer, stronger, and more prosperous. According to official U.S. State Department documents, this strategy is built on three pillars, with the first pillar being "Safer," emphasizing the protection of American public health through strengthened global health defense mechanisms.
  
  In releasing the strategy, the U.S. State Department noted that global infectious disease threats continue to rise, particularly in the Americas region, where closer regional cooperation is needed for cross-border disease prevention and control. As a neighboring region to the United States, the public health situation in the Americas directly affects U.S. homeland security. According to long-term monitoring data from the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO/WHO), the health status and trends in the Americas and Caribbean region have been the core focus of the organization since its founding in 1954. PAHO continues to collect and disseminate health information from countries and regions across the Americas through its flagship publication, *Health in the Americas*, providing an important reference for regional health policy.

The article noted that President Lai Ching-te is Taiwan's first physician-president in history, holding a Master's degree in Public Health from Harvard University. His "Healthy Taiwan" campaign platform emphasizes "making citizens healthy, making the nation healthy, and letting the world embrace Taiwan," and advocates that all future initiatives or policies proposed by the government should prioritize promoting the health of its citizens. The Far East Foundation believes that achieving the goal of "Health Right for All" can begin with the U.S. global health strategy, indicating an interactive relationship between Taiwan's health policy and the U.S. global health strategy.

This demonstrates that global public health emergencies continue to persist and that countries need to strengthen cooperation in addressing them.

The release of the U.S. "America First Global Health Strategy" marks a policy shift in the United States in the field of public health, and how it will cooperate with countries in the Americas and international organizations in the future will be a key area of observation.

# 美國發布《美國優先全球衛生戰略》聚焦美洲傳染病防控

> 美國國務院發布《美國優先全球衛生戰略》，提出三大支柱架構，強調防止傳染病蔓延至美國本土，同時提升全球衛生安全。

美國國務院於近日發布《美國優先全球衛生戰略》（America First Global Health Strategy），該戰略提出全面願景，旨在拯救全球數百萬生命並防止傳染病疫情蔓延至美國本土，同時使美國更安全、更強大、更繁榮。根據美國國務院官方檔案顯示，此戰略建立在三大支柱之上，其中首要支柱為「更安全」，強調透過強化全球衛生防禦機制來保護美國民眾健康。
美國國務院在發布該戰略時指出，全球傳染病威脅持續上升，特別是在美洲地區，跨國傳染病防控需要更緊密的區域合作。美洲地區作為美國鄰近區域，其公共衛生狀況直接影響美國本土安全。根據泛美衛生組織（PAHO/WHO）長期監測資料顯示，美洲及加勒比海地區的健康狀況與趨勢一直是該組織自1954年創立以來的核心關注領域。PAHO透過其旗艦出版物《Health in the Americas》持續收集並傳播美洲各國及地區的健康資訊，為區域衛生政策提供重要參考依據。
遠景基金會研究分享文章指出，臺灣新政府衛生政策與美國全球衛生戰略存在一定關聯性。該文章提到，賴清德總統為臺灣史上首位醫師總統，曾獲美國哈佛大學公共衛生碩士學位，其發表的「健康臺灣」政見強調「讓國人健康、讓國家健康、讓世界擁抱臺灣」，並主張國家未來提出的建設或政策都應以促進國人健康為最高原則。遠景基金會認為，實現「Health Right for All」的目標，可從美國全球衛生策略談起，顯示臺灣衛生政策與美國全球衛生戰略之間存在互動關係。
世衛組織（WHO）在近期發布的報告中，介紹了2024年1月1日至12月31日期間所有被世衛組織定級的緊急突發事件和長期突發事件的情況。報告指出，執行委員會在其第156屆會議上注意到該報告的較早版本，並提及需要啟動聯合國機構間常設委員會人道主義全系統擴大方案的突發事件，以及需要世衛組織應對的各項衛生緊急情況。這顯示全球公共衛生緊急狀況仍持續存在，各國需加強合作應對。
《Global Public Health》期刊2026年第21卷第1期當前issue亦關注全球公共衛生議題，反映國際學術界對公共衛生政策的持續關注。美洲地區作為全球公共衛生的重要區域，其跨國衛生合作、傳染病防控策略及衛生政策制定，持續受到國際社會關注。美國《美國優先全球衛生戰略》的發布，標誌著美國在公共衛生領域的政策轉向，未來如何與美洲各國及國際組織合作，將是觀察重點。
