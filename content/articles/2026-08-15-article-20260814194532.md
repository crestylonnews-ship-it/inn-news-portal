  # East Asian Education Transformation Accelerates: Digitalization and Policy Learning Take Center Stage
  
  > East Asian countries lead the world in education digitalization transformation, with China, Japan, and South Korea taking the lead in applying advanced technologies. In March 2026, a scholars' roundtable conference explored the development of Asian education hubs, focusing on topics such as generative AI and liberal arts education.
  
  The East Asian region is undergoing profound structural transformation, with demographic shifts, economic and social system restructuring, rapid scientific and technological development, and changes in international relations collectively forming the core driving forces of this historic stage. According to the East Asian Education column, these factors interweave and influence one another, reshaping the education systems and practices of East Asian countries. The UNESCO *2025 Global Education Monitoring Report* regional edition also confirms that East Asian countries occupy a leading position in the global digitalization transformation of economic, social, and education systems, with China, Japan, and South Korea regarded as representative nations that have taken the lead in applying advanced technologies in classrooms.
  
  In terms of specific policy initiatives, China released the *Education Informatization 2.0 Action Plan* in 2018, proposing a vision for smart education and emphasizing pathways to achieve personalized learning. Japan launched the "GIGA School Program" in 2019, the implementation of which was significantly accelerated by the COVID-19 pandemic, further promoting the普及 of digital learning environments. These policy measures reflect the active commitment of East Asian governments toward education modernization and demonstrate that digital technology has become a key vehicle for educational reform.
  
  The academic community maintains close attention to this trend, with Routledge's Education and Society in Asia series and the Asia Pacific Education Journal consistently publishing related analyses, focusing on the developmental trajectories of Asian education systems within social, cultural, political, and economic contexts, and exploring their uniqueness and comparability.
  
  On March 5, 2026, the Graduate School of the Hang Seng University and the University of Manchester in the United Kingdom, supported by the Alliance for Research on Post-Secondary Education in Asia, jointly hosted the international roundtable conference "Exploring Asian Education Hubs: Comparative Perspectives and Policy Learning," bringing together international scholars to discuss issues related to the development of Asian education hubs. The conference covered a range of topics including impact-oriented research, international education as cultural diplomacy, digital technology, generative artificial intelligence, and liberal arts education. Meanwhile, relevant academic activities at the Institute of Social Sciences Research, Academia Sinica, deepened understanding of Taiwan's neighboring countries by comparing the historical development of education across different societies and promoted the advancement of comparative research on Asian education history.
  
  Scholars point out that although East Asian countries have achieved significant progress in digitalization, how to balance technological application with humanistic literacy and how to maintain cultural subjectivity in international education competition remain issues that policymakers and education practitioners need to continuously reflect upon.

# 東亞教育轉型加速 數位化與政策學習成焦點

> 東亞國家於教育數位化轉型居全球領先，中國、日本、韓國率先應用先進技術；2026年3月學者圓桌會議探討亞洲教育樞紐發展，聚焦生成式AI與博雅教育等議題。

東亞地區正經歷深刻的結構性轉型，人口結構變化、經濟與社會體制重組、科學技術快速發展，以及國際關係轉變，共同構成這一歷史性階段的核心動力。根據東亞教育專欄指出，這些因素交織影響，正在重塑東亞各國的教育體系與實踐模式。聯合國教科文組織（UNESCO）《2025年全球教育監測報告》區域版亦確認，東亞國家在全球經濟、社會及教育體系的數位化轉型程式中居於領先地位，其中中國、日本與韓國被視為率先在課堂中應用先進技術的代表性國家。
在具體政策推動方面，中國於2018年發布《教育資訊化2.0行動計劃》，提出智慧教育願景並強調個性化學習的實現路徑。日本則於2019年啟動「GIGA學校計劃」，該計劃的實施程式因新冠肺炎疫癘而顯著加速，進一步推動了數位學習環境的普及。這些政策舉措反映出東亞各國政府對於教育現代化的積極投入，也顯示出數位技術已成為教育改革的關鍵載體。
學術界對此趨勢保持高度關注，Routledge教育與社會亞洲研究系列以及亞太教育期刊均持續刊載相關分析，聚焦亞洲教育體系在社會、文化、政治與經濟脈絡中的發展軌跡，並探討其獨特性和可比性。
2026年3月5日，恆生大學研究生院與英國曼徹斯特大學在亞太高等教育研究聯盟支援下，聯合舉辦「亞洲教育樞紐的探索——比較視野與政策學習」國際圓桌會議，匯聚國際學者共同探討亞洲教育樞紐的發展議題。會議討論範圍涵蓋以影響力為導向的研究、作為文化外交的國際教育、數碼技術、生成式人工智慧，以及博雅教育等多個面向。與此同時，中央研究院相關學術活動亦透過比較不同社會的教育歷史發展，深化對臺灣鄰近國家的理解，並促進亞洲教育歷史比較研究的推進。
學者們指出，儘管東亞各國在數位化程式中取得顯著進展，但如何平衡技術應用與人文素養、如何在國際教育競爭中維持文化主體性，仍是政策制定者與教育實踐者需要持續反思的課題。
