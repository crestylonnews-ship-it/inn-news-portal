  # EU Monitoring Report Reveals Gap in Youth Sustainability Action; European Education Moves Toward Diversity and AI Regulation
  
  > The EU's 2024 Education Monitoring Report indicates that while young people hold strong認同 toward sustainable development, the proportion translating knowledge into action remains insufficient; meanwhile, the OECD's 2025 Trends Outlook emphasizes that European education must transcend standardized models and actively address the normalization shifts brought by generative AI.
  
  The European Commission released the "European Education and Training Monitoring Report" in 2024, conducting a comprehensive review across areas including sustainability learning, preschool education, school education, vocational education, and higher education. The report shows that EU youth generally hold high認同 of sustainable development concepts, yet the rate of translating認知 into daily action is noticeably low, highlighting the gap between "knowing" and "doing" in the education process. The European Commission's Global Education Project, based on recommendations from Resolution 1318 (2003), is also committed to promoting enhanced public awareness of sustainable development among member states and advancing critical action by empowering citizens with the knowledge and skills to understand a globalized society.
  
  Both documents underscore the important position of sustainability education on Europe's policy agenda, yet effectively bridging認知 and practice remains to be further explored by individual countries.
  
  Different media and institutions have offered diverse observations on the current state and future trends of European education. *European Times* cited the OECD's 2025 Education Trends Outlook report, which指出 that dramatic changes in society, technology, economy, and the environment are forcing education systems to adjust rapidly. The report emphasizes that education must better meet learners' individual needs and aspirations, a viewpoint that aligns with the EU's long-term policy direction of coordinating national diversity with common standards.
  
  On the other hand, scholars Pei-Ying Chen and Yi-Rong Fu from the Department of Education at National Taiwan Normal University published an analysis on the *International Connection of Educational New Insights* platform, stating that since the rapid development of generative AI (GAI) in late 2022, the global education system has faced an unprecedented normalization shift, with countries successively establishing regulatory frameworks to address AI's impact on education. Meanwhile, the Sui Xu Think Tank, in its article "Record of European Education Reform Turbulence," reviewed Europe's long-standing and globally influential education system, noting that it currently faces reform challenges brought by rapid socioeconomic changes, and pointed out that educational diversity is an important characteristic of Europe.
  
  Synthesizing various sources, it is evident that European education is simultaneously confronting multiple challenges including deepening sustainability, AI technology integration, and systemic diversification and adaptation. While different institutions emphasize different aspects, all agree that education policy must possess flexibility and forward-looking vision.
  
  Experts and policy documents generally believe that Europe's future education development must balance standardization with individualized needs. The OECD report explicitly recommends moving beyond traditional standardized models to better accommodate learner differences; the EU monitoring report calls for strengthening teacher training and curriculum coverage to improve the efficiency of translating sustainability education from knowledge to action. The European Commission's Global Education Project continues to promote citizen empowerment, emphasizing critical action and participation in a globalized society. Regarding AI issues, while countries have begun formulating regulatory frameworks, how to balance technological innovation with the essence of education remains an area yet to be determined.
  
  Overall, European education policy is evolving toward diversity, flexibility, and sustainability. Future development will depend on how countries integrate resources, strengthen teacher professionalism, and continuously monitor policy implementation effectiveness.

# 歐盟監測報告揭青少年永續行動落差 歐洲教育邁向多元與AI規範

> 歐盟2024年教育監測報告指出，青少年對永續發展理念認同度高，但將知識轉化為實際行動的比例不足；同時經合組織2025年趨勢展望強調，歐洲教育需超越標準化模式，並積極應對生成式AI帶來的正規化轉移。

歐盟委員會於2024年發布《歐洲教育與培訓監測報告》，針對永續發展學習、學前教育、學校教育、職業教育與高等教育等領域進行全面檢視。報告顯示，歐盟青少年對永續發展理念的認同度普遍較高，然而將認知轉化為日常行動的比率卻明顯偏低，凸顯教育過程中「知」與「行」之間的落差。歐洲委員會全球教育專案亦基於1318號決議（2003）的建議，致力於促進成員國加強公眾永續發展意識，並透過賦能公民掌握理解全球化社會的知識與技能，以推動批判性行動。
兩項檔案共同指向永續教育在歐洲政策議程中的重要地位，但如何有效 bridging 認知與實踐，仍待各國進一步探索。
不同媒體與機構對歐洲教育現況與未來趨勢提出多元觀察。《歐洲時報》引述經合組織（OECD）2025年教育趨勢展望報告指出，社會、技術、經濟與環境的劇變正迫使教育系統快速調整，報告強調教育需更好地滿足學習者的個別需求與願望，此觀點與歐盟長期協調國家多樣性與共同標準的政策方向相呼應。
另一方面，臺灣師範大學教育系學者陳佩英、傅奕榮在《教育新知國際串聯》平臺發表文章分析，自2022年底生成式人工智慧（GAI）迅速發展以來，全球教育體系正面臨前所未有的正規化轉移，各國相繼制定規範框架以應對AI對教育的衝擊。歲序智庫於《歐洲教育變革風雲錄》一文中則回顧歐洲教育體系歷史悠久且具全球影響力，近年正面臨社會經濟快速變遷帶來的變革挑戰，並指出教育體系多元化為歐洲重要特徵。
綜合各來源可見，歐洲教育正同時面對永續發展深化、AI技術整合與體系多元調適等多重課題，不同機構雖側重點各異，但均認同教育政策需具備彈性與前瞻性。
專家與政策檔案普遍認為，歐洲教育未來發展須兼顧標準化與個別化需求。經合組織報告明確建議超越傳統標準化模式，以更貼近學習者差異；歐盟監測報告則呼籲加強教師培訓與課程覆蓋，以改善永續發展教育從知識到行動的轉化效率。歐洲委員會的全球教育專案持續推動公民賦能，強調批判性行動與全球化社會參與。在AI議題方面，各國雖已開始制定規範框架，但如何平衡技術創新與教育本質，仍屬尚待確認的領域。
整體而言，歐洲教育政策正朝多元、彈性與永續方向演進，後續發展將取決於各國如何整合資源、強化教師專業，並持續監測政策實施成效。
