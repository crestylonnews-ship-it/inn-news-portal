  # Europe's Heatwave Causes Massive Losses, STIs Hit Record — A Dual Health Crisis
  
  > Europe's heatwaves have caused hundreds of billions of euros in economic losses, with hundreds of millions facing water shortages; meanwhile, STI infections in 2024 reached a historic high, with gonorrhea and syphilis surging, posing a dual challenge to European public health.
  
  Europe is facing a dual public health crisis of climate change and infectious diseases. According to an analysis by Maximilian Kotz, a researcher at the Barcelona Supercomputing Center, extreme heat waves that have frequently struck Europe in recent years have caused hundreds of billions of euros in economic losses and left hundreds of millions of people facing water shortages. Kotz noted that the impact of extreme heat on food prices is more pronounced in hotter regions, particularly in Southern Europe, which will challenge the European Central Bank's inflation targets. This study highlights that climate change is no longer just an environmental issue—it directly threatens food security and economic stability for Europeans.
  
  Meanwhile, new data released by the European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (ECDC) shows that STI infections in Europe reached a record high in 2024. The data indicates a sharp rise in gonorrhea and syphilis infection rates, while the gap between testing and prevention measures continues to widen. This trend has raised concerns among European public health experts, who warn that if screening and public education are not strengthened in time, the spread of STIs will place an even greater burden on Europe's healthcare systems. The ECDC emphasized that there are significant disparities among member states in testing accessibility and the allocation of prevention resources, with outbreak data from some Eastern and Southern European countries being particularly concerning.
  
  The European Commission has listed the "European Health Union" as a priority policy initiative, with the "European Health Data Space" regarded as one of its core components. The plan aims to improve healthcare quality within the EU, allowing citizens to manage their personal health data in their home country or other EU nations, and to provide a consistent, secure, trustworthy, and efficient framework for health data use. However, analysts point out that cross-border coordination mechanisms remain lacking for climate-related health risks and infectious disease prevention. Countries show vastly different capacities in scheduling resources to respond to extreme heat and disease outbreaks, making the integration of data and policy a key challenge facing the EU.

# 歐洲熱浪釀巨額損失 性傳染病創紀錄雙重健康危機

> 歐洲熱浪造成數千億歐元經濟損失，數億人面臨缺水衝擊；同時2024年性傳染病感染數達歷史新高，淋病與梅毒急升，歐洲公共衛生面臨雙重挑戰。

歐洲正面臨氣候變遷與傳染病雙重公共衛生危機。根據西班牙巴塞隆納超級計算中心研究員 Maximilian Kotz 的分析，近年來頻襲歐洲的極端高溫已釀成數千億歐元經濟損失，並使數億人面臨缺水衝擊。Kotz 指出，極端高溫對糧食價格的影響在較熱的地區更為顯著，尤其在南歐地區，這將挑戰歐洲央行（European Central Bank）的通膨目標。此項研究凸顯氣候變遷已不僅是環境議題，更直接衝擊歐洲民眾的糧食安全與經濟穩定。
與此同時，歐洲疾病預防與控制中心（ECDC）發布的新資料顯示，2024年歐洲性傳染病感染數達到歷史創紀錄水平。資料指出，淋病與梅毒的感染率出現急劇上升，同時檢測與預防措施之間的差距持續擴大。這一趨勢引發歐洲公共衛生專家擔憂，認為若不及時加強篩檢與教育宣導，性傳染病的蔓延將對歐洲醫療體系造成更大負擔。ECDC 強調，不同成員國在檢測可及性與預防資源分配上存在顯著差異，部分東歐與南歐國家的疫情資料尤其令人關注。
歐盟委員會將「歐洲健康聯盟」列為優先政策專案，其中「歐洲健康資料空間」被視為核心建設之一。該計畫旨在改善歐盟境內的醫療照護品質，讓民眾能在祖國或其他歐盟國家掌控個人健康資料，並提供一致、安全、可信且高效的健康資料使用框架。然而，分析人士指出，氣候相關健康風險與傳染病防控仍缺乏跨國協調機制，各國在應對極端高溫與疾病爆發時的資源排程能力差異懸殊，如何整合資料與政策成為歐盟面臨的關鍵挑戰。
