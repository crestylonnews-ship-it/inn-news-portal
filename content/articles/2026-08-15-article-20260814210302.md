  # African Diplomacy and Artistic Development Draw Multidimensional Attention
  
  > Diplomatic developments and artistic progress across multiple African nations have drawn growing interest, as South Africa's diplomatic tensions escalate and the art world actively reclaims African narratives.
  
  Recently, significant developments have emerged in both the diplomatic and cultural-artistic spheres across Africa. On the diplomatic front, Taiwan's relations with African countries continue to face challenges. According to Yahoo News, following Morocco in North Africa's surprise suspension of visa confirmation applications for Taiwanese citizens in late July, Uganda in East Africa has also reported incidents of Taiwanese passport holders being stopped and questioned by local law enforcement. Taiwan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs stated that these unfriendly actions by African nations toward Taiwan, occurring one after another, may be linked to Taiwan's prior retaliatory measures, indicating that the regional diplomatic situation is in a sensitive phase.
  
  Meanwhile, Africa's art and cultural sectors are undergoing important transformation. According to Taiwan's Ministry of Culture's Museum Island, Zeitz MOCAA (Museum of Contemporary Art Africa) in Cape Town, South Africa, is engaged in curatorial practices that reconstruct African art. Through diverse curatorial perspectives, the museum seeks to break the established Western frameworks around African art, enabling African artists to narrate their own stories in their own way. The report also notes that Ghanaian curator Koyo Kouoh has been selected as curator for the 2026 Venice Biennale, signaling that the visibility of contemporary African art continues to rise.
  
  Furthermore, the academic and cultural communities continue to focus on development issues in Africa. The "AfricaLounge" program on the Xiaoyuzhou podcast platform once explored the meaning of "studying something other than what one uses" within the African context, inviting scholar Liu Ye to share his analysis on "the concept of Africa and the transhistorical." The program also recommended Liu Ye's research on the postcolonial construction of Chinese architecture in Africa during the 1960s and 1970s, as well as related discussions on asymmetric giving relationships, reflecting the academic world's multidimensional attention to African issues.
  
  A comprehensive observation reveals that Africa is simultaneously experiencing adjustments in diplomatic relations and a process of cultural and artistic self-determination. On the diplomatic front, Taiwan's relations with certain African nations face challenges; on the cultural front, African art institutions and scholars are actively constructing narrative frameworks that belong to Africa itself. These two threads together outline the increasingly complex and diverse face of contemporary Africa on the international stage, warranting continued attention to subsequent developments.

# 非洲外交與藝術發展 多面向動態引關注

> 非洲多國外交動態與藝術發展引發關注，南非外交戰持續升溫，同時藝術界亦積極重構非洲敘事。

近日非洲地區在外交與文化藝術領域均出現重要動態。在外交層面，臺灣與非洲國家的關係持續面臨挑戰。根據Yahoo News報導，繼北非摩洛哥於7月下旬無預警叫停臺灣人申請簽證確認函後，東非烏幹達也傳出有持臺灣護照的國民遭當地執法單位攔查的事件。臺灣外交部表示，這些非洲國家對臺灣不友善的事件一樁接一樁，恐與臺灣先前採取的對等措施有關，顯示區域外交局勢正處於敏感階段。
與此同時，非洲的藝術與文化領域正經歷重要的轉型。根據臺灣文化部博物之島的報導，南非開普敦的Zeitz MOCAA（非洲當代藝術博物館）正在進行重構非洲藝術的策展實踐。該館透過不同的策展觀點，試圖打破西方對非洲藝術的既定框架，讓非洲藝術家能夠以自己的方式敘述自身的故事。報導中亦提及，迦納策展人Koyo Kouoh已獲選為2026年威尼斯雙年展策展人，顯示非洲當代藝術的能見度正持續提升。
此外，學術與文化界也持續關注非洲的發展議題。小宇宙播客平臺的「AfricaLounge」節目曾探討「學非所用」在非洲脈絡下的意義，並邀請學者劉燁分享關於「非洲的概念與超歷史之物」的分析。該節目亦推薦劉燁關於1960至70年代中國在非洲建築的後殖民建構研究，以及非對稱給予關係的相關論述，反映學術界對非洲議題的多維度關注。
綜合觀察，非洲地區正同時經歷外交關係的調整與文化藝術的自主化程式。外交層面，臺灣與部分非洲國家的關係面臨挑戰；文化層面，非洲藝術機構與學者正積極建構屬於非洲自身的敘事框架。這兩條線索共同勾勒出當代非洲在國際舞臺上日益複雜且多元的面貌，值得持續關注後續發展。
