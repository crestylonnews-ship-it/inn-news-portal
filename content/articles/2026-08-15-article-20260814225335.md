  # AIIB Criticizes Trade Barriers; China's Foreign Trade Resilience Draws Attention
  
  > AIIB president states that free trade no longer exists globally, with developed nations erecting trade barriers; DBS Bank Singapore raises China's foreign trade growth forecast, while Malaysian media express confidence in China's industrial resilience.
  
  Recent developments in Asian trade have drawn international attention. The president of the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB) recently publicly criticized the current state of global trade, stating that free trade no longer exists and that developed countries are erecting trade barriers, highlighting the tense cross-border trade environment. Meanwhile, China's foreign trade data has exceeded expectations, attracting the attention of regional economists. Economists at DBS Bank Singapore noted that the AI industry chain is driving a boom in two-way technology product trade, with semiconductors and computing devices simultaneously boosting both imports and exports; the bank has raised its full-year China foreign trade growth forecast. A report in Malaysia's Sin Chew Daily also emphasized that China's export performance highlights its economic and industrial resilience.
  
  The trend of industrial restructuring is also reshaping regional trade patterns. Research by Taiwanese academic institutions indicates that the shift of industries toward Southeast Asia continues, with trade and investment ties between Taiwan and ASEAN growing increasingly close—a phenomenon linked to Taiwan's Southward Policy and enterprises' internationalization strategies. The Hong Kong Trade Development Council also led a delegation of business representatives to participate in the "Go Asia, First Choice Hong Kong" event, conducting inspections in Silicon Valley, demonstrating Asian enterprises' active efforts to expand cross-border cooperation and technology applications.
  
  Synthesizing media reports, the AIIB's criticism reflects rising trade protectionism in developed nations, which may pressure global supply chains. China's foreign trade data, however, shows that its manufacturing and technology sectors remain competitive, particularly in AI-related product trade. Southeast Asia, as a destination for industrial relocation, sees its economic role further strengthened. Experts observe that regional trade agreements and infrastructure investment will be key factors shaping future economic trajectories.

# 亞投行批貿易壁壘 中國外貿韌性受矚目

> 亞投行行長指全球已無自由貿易，已開發國家設貿易壁壘；新加坡大華銀行上調中國外貿預期，馬來西亞媒體看好中國產業韌性。

亞洲貿易局勢近期引發國際關注。亞洲基礎設施投資銀行（亞投行）行長近日公開批評，指全球已無自由貿易，已開發國家設立貿易壁壘，此言論凸顯跨國貿易環境的緊張局勢。與此同時，中國外貿資料表現超預期，吸引區域經濟學家關注。新加坡大華銀行經濟學家指出，AI產業鏈帶動科技產品雙向貿易繁榮，半導體與計算機裝置同時拉動進出口上行，該行已上調全年中國外貿增長預期。馬來西亞《星洲日報》報道亦強調，中國出口表現凸顯經濟與產業韌性。
產業結構轉移趨勢同樣影響區域貿易格局。臺灣學術單位研究指出，產業移向東南亞的趨勢持續，臺灣與東協之間的貿易投資聯絡日益緊密，此現象與南向政策及企業國際化策略相關。香港貿發局亦率領企業代表參與「邁向亞洲 首選香港」活動，考察美國矽谷地區，顯示亞洲企業積極拓展跨國合作與技術應用。
綜合各媒體報導，亞投行的批評反映已開發國家貿易保護主義抬頭，可能對全球供應鏈造成壓力。中國外貿資料則顯示製造業與科技產業仍具競爭力，尤其AI相關產品貿易活躍。東南亞作為產業轉移目的地，其經濟角色進一步強化。專家觀察，區域貿易協定與基礎建設投資將成為影響未來經濟走向的關鍵因素。
