The report, with data as of October 1, 2022, shows that multiple European countries face increasingly severe forest fire threats, and the impacts of climate change on ecosystems and public safety are gradually coming to light.
  
  Meanwhile, Germany is actively promoting legislation and grassroots action on the "right to repair." According to TAISE, the Repair Café movement, which has gained widespread momentum across Germany, is gradually coalescing into a Europe-wide demand for the "right to repair." This initiative aims to extend product lifespans, reduce electronic waste, and promote the development of a circular economy, aligning with the EU's broader decarbonization goals.
  
  In Taiwan, Presidential Office documents indicate that the government is comprehensively assisting industries in enhancing green competitiveness and strengthening climate policy and carbon governance leadership. The report notes active implementation of the Climate Change Response Act, steady progress toward the 2050 net-zero goal, promotion of circular economy transformation and industrial internationalization, and enhanced international participation and policy alignment.

From the increasing frequency of wildfires to the promotion of circular economy measures such as the right to repair, green transition has emerged as a cross-border policy consensus. Taiwan is simultaneously strengthening relevant policy instruments, seeking to seize opportunities in regional and global climate governance.

# 歐盟野火季節延長、德國推動維修權 綠色轉型成歐洲焦點

> 歐盟聯合研究中心報告顯示野火已不分季節，德國同步推動維修權立法，臺灣亦積極對接綠色轉型政策，各國相繼強化氣候行動。

歐盟聯合研究中心（JRC）與歐洲森林火災資訊系統（EFFIS）針對歐洲地區氣候與災害發布觀測報告，指出火災已不再受限於傳統季節，野火發生時間延長且範圍擴大。報告資料截至2022年10月1日，顯示歐洲多國面臨日益嚴峻的森林火災威脅，氣候變遷對生態與公共安全的影響逐漸浮現。
與此同時，德國正積極推動「維修權」相關立法與民間行動。根據TAISE報導，德國境內廣泛興起的Repair Café（維修咖啡館）運動，已逐漸匯聚成歐洲範圍內的「維修權」（right to repair）訴求。此舉旨在延長產品使用壽命、減少電子廢棄物，並促進迴圈經濟發展，與歐盟整體減碳目標形成呼應。
臺灣方面，總統府檔案顯示政府正全面協助產業提升綠色競爭力，並強化氣候政策與碳治理主導力。報告提及積極落實《氣候變遷因應法》，穩定推動2050淨零目標，同時推動迴圈經濟轉型與產業國際化，並加強國際參與及政策對接。
綜合觀察，歐洲各國正面臨氣候變遷帶來的實質挑戰，從野火頻率增加至推動維修權等迴圈經濟措施，顯示綠色轉型已成為跨國政策共識。臺灣亦同步強化相關政策工具，試圖在區域與全球氣候治理中掌握契機。
