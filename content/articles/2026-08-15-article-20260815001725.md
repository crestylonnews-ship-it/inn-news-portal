  # Africa Seeks Strategic Autonomy in a Multipolar World; Regional Leadership Role Rises
  
  > Between 2025 and 2026, Africa faces challenges including US strategic retrenchment, strained EU relations, and intensifying great power competition, while actively reshaping its international role through regional integration and growing strategic autonomy awareness.
  
  The African continent entered a critical transition period in 2025, as profound shifts in the global geopolitical landscape are reshaping its international positioning. According to Strategy International, Africa is transforming from a passive object in international relations into an active strategic actor by leveraging geopolitical competition, integrating continental markets, and reshaping the politics of humanitarian aid. This transformation is reflected in multiple dimensions, including the strengthened stance of African nations in negotiations over critical mineral resources and their more coordinated voices in multilateral diplomacy.
  
  Africa faces a mix of challenges and opportunities in 2025. Afripoli notes that US strategic retrenchment, strained relations between the African Union and the EU, the first G20 summit held in Africa, intensifying competition over critical minerals, and escalating tensions in the Horn of Africa collectively shape the geopolitical landscape of the year. Chatham House further predicts that Africa's political and security trajectory in 2026 will be influenced simultaneously by local dynamics and an unpredictable international order. The institution emphasizes that the weakening of multilateral norms, divisions in international leadership, and intensifying competition among great and middle powers have created a more permissive global environment, where external accountability has taken a back seat to transactional interests.
  
  Different media outlets present diverse perspectives on Africa's autonomy. Guoming Wang, looking ahead to the 2025 situation in Africa, pointed out that despite uncertainties, Africa continues to exhibit characteristics of stability amid turbulence, resilience amid chaos, recovery and development, and an overall positive trend, as the continent undergoes profound awakening and transformation. CSSN, analyzing from the perspective of Western engagement in governance, noted that Africa's sense of autonomy continues to strengthen, and that the continent is accelerating internal unity, cooperation, and regional integration efforts in line with the goals of the UN 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development and the AU Agenda 2063.
  
  RFI focused on China's role in Africa, noting that in 2024 China established the "Global Security Initiative Research Center" under the Chinese Academy of International Affairs and Trade at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs to coordinate the implementation of the initiative.
  
  The advancement of Africa's strategic autonomy faces dual tests from both internal and external fronts. On one hand, the regional integration process is accelerating, with the African Union playing an increasingly prominent role in mediating regional conflicts and coordinating external policies; on the other hand, intensifying great power competition requires African nations to seek balance among multiple partnerships. Experts observe that African countries are shifting from passively receiving aid to proactively setting agendas, demonstrating stronger negotiating capabilities in areas such as climate financing, debt restructuring, and infrastructure development. However, uneven regional development, persistent security threats, and governance challenges remain to be addressed.

# 非洲在多極世界中尋求戰略自主 區域領導角色崛起

> 2025至2026年間，非洲面臨美國戰略收縮、歐盟關係緊張、大國競爭加劇等挑戰，同時透過區域一體化與戰略自主意識提升，積極重塑國際參與角色。

非洲大陸在2025年進入關鍵轉型期，全球地緣政治格局的深刻變動正重塑其國際定位。根據Strategy International分析，非洲正透過利用地緣政治競爭、整合大陸市場以及重塑人道援助政治，從國際關係中的被動物件轉變為積極的戰略主體。這一轉變反映在多個層面，包括非洲國家在關鍵礦產資源談判中的立場強化，以及在多邊外交場閤中協調一致的聲音。
2025年非洲面臨的多重挑戰與機遇並存。Afripoli指出，美國戰略收縮、非洲聯盟與歐盟關係緊張、首屆G20峰會在非洲舉行、關鍵礦產競爭加劇以及非洲之角緊張局勢升溫，共同塑造了這一年份的地緣政治圖景。Chatham House進一步預測，2026年非洲的政治與安全軌跡將同時受到本地動態與不可預測國際秩序的影響。該機構強調，多邊規範弱化、國際領導分裂以及大國與中等強國競爭加劇，創造了更為寬鬆的全球環境，外部問責已次於交易性利益。
不同媒體對非洲自主性的評估呈現多元視角。光明網展望2025年非洲形勢時指出，儘管存在不確定性，非洲仍呈現穩中有亂、亂中有韌、復蘇發展和整體向好的特徵，大陸正經歷深刻覺醒和變革。CSSN則從西方參與治理的角度分析，指出非洲自主意識持續增強，並根據聯合國《2030年可持續發展議程》及非盟《2063年議程》目標，加快內部團結合作與區域一體化建設。
RFI則關注中國在非洲的角色，提及2024年中國在外交部下屬中國國際問題研究院設立「全球安全倡議研究中心」，統籌該倡議的實施。
非洲戰略自主的推進面臨內外雙重考驗。一方面，區域一體化程式加速，非盟在調解地區衝突、協調對外政策方面的作用日益顯著；另一方面，大國競爭加劇使非洲國家需在多重夥伴關係中尋求平衡。專家觀察指出，非洲國家正從被動接受援助轉向主動設定議程，在氣候融資、債務重組、基礎設施建設等領域展現更強談判能力。然而，區域發展不平衡、安全威脅持續以及治理挑戰仍待解決。
