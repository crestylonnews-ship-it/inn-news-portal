  # Froman Warns of China's Overcapacity; International Organizations Strengthen Transparency Standards
  
  > Council on Foreign Relations President Michael Froman wrote in Foreign Affairs magazine that China's reliance on subsidies and overcapacity to drive exports is approaching the global absorption limit, while the United Nations, World Bank, OECD and other international organizations have made government transparency a core governance indicator.
  
  Michael Froman, president of the Council on Foreign Relations, recently published an opinion piece in the authoritative journal Foreign Affairs, raising warnings about China's long-standing economic model of supporting export growth through government subsidies and massive industrial capacity.
  
  Froman pointed out that China has continued to expand production capacity in key areas such as steel, solar panels, and electric vehicles through large-scale fiscal subsidies, low-interest loans, and industrial policies. However, the global market's demand for absorbing China's excess capacity is gradually approaching its limit, and this unsustainable export-driven model has drawn widespread concern from the international community.
  
  Froman's analysis highlights the structural challenges facing global trade governance. China's overcapacity not only affects trade balances but also produces spillover effects on the industrial chains and employment markets of other economies. Multiple countries have successively launched anti-subsidy investigations and trade protection measures in an attempt to mitigate the impact of China's excess products on local markets. This issue has also become an important agenda item in multilateral trade negotiations and bilateral economic and trade dialogues, reflecting the governance gap in the current global economic governance system in addressing non-market policies and overcapacity issues.
  
  At the same time, international organizations are strengthening regulatory frameworks from the perspectives of transparency and governance. According to publicly available data from the Taipei City Government, important international organizations such as the United Nations (UN), the World Bank (WB), and the Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development (OECD) have listed government transparency as a core governance indicator and continue to promote related standards and evaluation mechanisms.
  
  Liao Hsing-chung, associate professor of the Department of Public Administration at National Chengchi University and deputy executive director of Transparency International Taiwan, who participated in the relevant initiative, pointed out that this trend reflects the international community's emphasis on openness and accountability in the public sector, and also aligns with the long-term global goals of anti-corruption and promoting good governance.
  
  In summary, the issues of China's overcapacity and the advancement of international transparency standards present a dual aspect of current global governance: on one hand, the spillover effects of trade and industrial policies require more effective multilateral coordination mechanisms; on the other hand, the strengthening of government transparency and accountability systems also provides a governance foundation for addressing structural problems such as overcapacity and subsidy disputes. How to establish a fair, transparent, and sustainable international trade order in the future without compromising the legitimate development rights of developing countries will be a long-term challenge faced by all countries and international organizations.

# 傅洛曼警示中國產能過剩 國際組織同步強化透明度規範

> 外交關係協會會長傅洛曼於《外交事務》雜誌撰文，指出中國依賴補貼與過剩產能推動出口，全球吸收能力逼近極限；同時聯合國、世界銀行與 OECD 等國際組織已將政府透明度列為核心治理指標。

美國外交關係協會（Council on Foreign Relations）會長傅洛曼（Michael Froman）近期於權威期刊《外交事務》（Foreign Affairs）發表投書，針對中國長期以政府補貼與龐大工業產能支撐出口增長的經濟模式提出警示。
傅洛曼指出，中國透過大規模財政補貼、低息貸款與產業政策，持續擴張鋼鐵、太陽能板、電動車等關鍵領域的生產能力，然而全球市場吸收中國過剩產能的需求正逐步逼近極限，此種不可持續的出口驅動模式已引發國際社會廣泛關注。
傅洛曼的分析凸顯出全球貿易治理面臨的結構性挑戰。中國產能過剩不僅影響貿易平衡，更對其他經濟體的產業鏈與就業市場產生外溢效應。多個國家已相繼啟動反補貼調查與貿易防護措施，試圖緩解中國過剩產品對本地市場的衝擊。此議題亦成為多邊貿易談判與雙邊經貿對話中的重要討論專案，反映出現階段全球經濟治理在應對非市場政策與產能過剩問題上的治理缺口。
與此同時，國際組織正從透明度與治理面強化規範框架。根據臺北市政府公開資料顯示，聯合國（United Nations, UN）、世界銀行（World Bank, WB）以及經濟合作暨發展組織（Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development, OECD）等重要國際組織，已將政府透明度列為核心治理指標，並持續推動相關規範與評鑑機制。
政治大學公共行政學系副教授、臺灣透明組織協會副執行長廖興中參與相關計畫，指出此趨勢反映國際社會對公共部門開透明與問責制的重視，亦與全球反貪腐、促進良治的長期目標相呼應。
綜合來看，中國產能過剩議題與國際透明度規範的推進，呈現出當前全球治理的雙重面向：一方面，貿易與產業政策的外溢效應需要更有效的多邊協調機制；另一方面，政府透明度與問責制度的強化，亦為解決產能過剩、補貼爭議等結構性問題提供治理基礎。未來如何在不損害發展中國家正當發展權益的前提下，建立公平、透明且可持續的國際貿易秩序，將是各國與國際組織共同面臨的長期課題。
