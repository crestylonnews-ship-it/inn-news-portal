  # EU Monitor: May Was Second Hottest Month Globally; Europe Faces Dual Challenges of Heatwave and Green Energy Transition
  
  > The EU's climate monitoring agency reported that global May was the second hottest on record, while Europe experienced an unusually early heatwave. Meanwhile, Solar Power Europe warned that the EU's goal of boosting local green energy manufacturing faces significant challenges, with solar components heavily reliant on China.
  
  The Copernicus Climate Change Service recently released data indicating that the just-concluded May was the second hottest month on record globally, surpassed only by May 2023. The agency also emphasized that Europe has been hit by an unusually early heatwave, with high temperatures increasingly becoming Europe's "new normal." As extreme climate events grow more frequent, European countries are facing direct impacts from climate change, and meteorological experts warn that this trend may continue to intensify.
  
  At the same time, the EU faces structural challenges on its energy transition path. Solar Power Europe recently released a report stating that while the EU has set a target of sourcing at least 40% of its clean technology needs from domestic manufacturing by 2030, achieving this goal may prove difficult. The report's analysis shows that European domestic suppliers can currently meet less than 20% of the European market's demand for solar products, primarily because approximately 90% of solar components still rely on supply from mainland China. This heavy dependence on imports is viewed by the industry as a significant concern for the EU's green energy autonomy.
  
  The climate monitoring data and the current state of the green energy industry form a contrast that highlights Europe's dual pressures in addressing climate change and energy transition. On one hand, extreme heat has become a real threat requiring accelerated decarbonization efforts; on the other hand, insufficient domestic manufacturing capacity for green energy installations could affect the timeline and autonomy of the energy transition. EU policymakers are grappling with the complex challenge of balancing climate goals with supply chain security.
  
  Experts note that Europe's earlier and more intense heatwaves are closely linked to the global warming trend, underscoring the urgency of accelerating the energy transition and reducing greenhouse gas emissions. However, Solar Power Europe's report cautions that if the EU cannot boost its domestic clean technology manufacturing capacity in the short term, it may struggle to achieve both climate goals and industrial autonomy simultaneously. The EU is currently discussing relevant policy tools, including subsidies for domestic manufacturing and supply chain diversification, but specific implementation details and their effectiveness remain to be seen.

# 歐盟監測指5月全球第二熱 歐洲迎熱浪與綠電轉型雙重挑戰

> 歐盟氣候監測機構指出，全球5月為有紀錄以來第二炎熱月份，歐洲遭遇異常提早熱浪。同時Solar Power Europe警告，歐盟綠能本土製造目標面臨挑戰，太陽能零元件高度依賴中國。

歐盟氣候監測機構（Copernicus Climate Change Service）近日發布資料指出，剛過去的5月是全球有紀錄以來第二炎熱的月份，僅次於2023年5月。該機構同時強調，歐洲地區遭遇異常提早來襲的熱浪侵襲，高溫現象正逐漸成為歐洲的「新常態」。隨著極端氣候事件日益頻繁，歐洲各國正面臨氣候變遷帶來的直接衝擊，氣象專家警告此趨勢可能持續加劇。
與此同時，歐盟在能源轉型道路上亦面臨結構性挑戰。Solar Power Europe於近日發布報告指出，歐盟雖訂定2030年前至少40%潔淨技術需求由本土製造的目標，但達成此目標可能面臨困難。報告分析顯示，目前歐洲本土供應商僅能滿足不到20%的歐洲市場對太陽能產品的需求，主要原因在於約90%的太陽能零元件仍依賴中國大陸供應。此種高度依賴進口的情況，被業界視為歐盟綠能自主性的重要隱憂。
氣候監測資料與綠能產業現況形成對比，凸顯歐洲在應對氣候變遷與能源轉型上的雙重壓力。一方面，極端高溫已成為現實威脅，需要加速減碳行動；另一方面，綠能裝置的本土製造能力不足，可能影響能源過渡的時程與自主性。歐盟政策制定者正面臨如何平衡氣候目標與產業鏈安全的複雜課題。
專家指出，歐洲熱浪提早且強度增加，與全球暖化趨勢密切相關，這也凸顯加速能源轉型、減少溫室氣體排放的迫切性。然而，Solar Power Europe的報告提醒，若無法在短期內提升本土潔淨技術製造能力，歐盟可能難以同時達成氣候目標與產業自主。目前歐盟內部正討論相關政策工具，包括補貼本土製造業、多元化供應鏈來源等方案，但具體實施細節與成效仍有待觀察。
