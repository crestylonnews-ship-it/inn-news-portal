  # UN Declares Middle East Crisis a Major Humanitarian Emergency
  
  > UNHCR states that the situation deteriorated rapidly after US-Israeli strikes on Iran, leaving nearly 25 million people displaced, and calls on all parties to ensure safe passage for civilians.
  
  The UN Refugee Agency (UNHCR) announced on March 6 that the situation in the Middle East had deteriorated rapidly into a "major humanitarian emergency" due to military strikes launched by the United States and Israel against Iran. Ayumi Ito, Director of Emergencies and Cross-Border Refugee Coordinator at UNHCR, stated at a regular press briefing in Geneva that the recent escalation of hostilities and ongoing attacks have triggered large-scale population displacement, with the number of refugees in affected areas reaching 25 million, constituting a severe humanitarian crisis. Ito called on all parties to the conflict to exercise restraint, seek de-escalation, and provide safe passage for displaced civilians.
  
  Data from the Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) of the International Organization for Migration (IOM) shows that millions of people across Iran and much of the Middle East have been forced to flee their homes due to conflict, violence, and disasters. Citing Ayumi Ito, Wen Wei Po reported that in the first two days after the US and Israel launched strikes on Iran, official Iranian figures showed approximately 100,000 people had left their homes, but this number is likely significantly underestimated. UN News simultaneously emphasized that the United Nations is working with regional and international partners to ease tensions, improve conditions on the ground, and advance a two-state solution to the Israel-Palestine conflict through political negotiations, in order to achieve comprehensive, just, and lasting peace in the Middle East.
  
  Different media outlets have focused on slightly different aspects of the crisis. Wen Wei Po and hk01 both concentrated on UNHCR's emergency declaration and refugee figures, while the IOM official page places greater emphasis on tracking overall regional displacement trends. Ito clearly pointed out that existing data may not fully reflect the actual scale of the affected population, highlighting the severity of the humanitarian situation and challenges surrounding information transparency. The UN continues to call on relevant parties to comply with international humanitarian law, ensure civilian safety, and provide necessary guarantees for the passage of humanitarian aid supplies and personnel.
  
  The continued heating up of the Middle East situation not only exacerbates local livelihood difficulties but also has far-reaching implications for regional stability and the international security order. The involvement and appeals of relevant UN agencies reflect the international community's high concern over the humanitarian disaster in the region. Subsequent developments will depend on whether the parties to the conflict respond to de-escalation calls, whether humanitarian corridors can be implemented, and whether international coordination mechanisms can effectively alleviate the crisis. Analysts point out that if the situation fails to ease, the displaced population could rise further, placing greater pressure on neighboring countries and global resource allocation.

# 聯合國宣佈中東危機為重大人道緊急狀態

> 聯合國難民署指美以襲擊伊朗後局勢急遽惡化，近2,500萬人流離失所，籲各方保障平民安全通道。

聯合國難民署（UNHCR）於3月6日宣佈，因美國與以色列對伊朗發動軍事打擊，中東地區局勢已急遽惡化為「重大人道主義緊急狀態」。聯合國難民署緊急情況主任兼跨區域難民協調員伊藤綾木在日內瓦例行記者會上表示，近期敵意升級與持續襲擊已引發大規模人口流動，受影響地區難民人數高達2,500萬人，構成嚴重人道危機。伊藤綾木呼籲衝突各方保持冷靜、尋求降級，並為流離失所的平民提供安全通道。
國際移民組織（IOM）的流離失所追蹤矩陣（DTM）資料顯示，伊朗及中東多地已有數百萬民眾因衝突、暴力與災害被迫遷離家園。香港文匯報引述伊藤綾木指出，美國與以色列對伊朗發動打擊後的最初兩天，伊朗官方數字顯示約10萬人離開家園，但這一數字很可能被極大程度低估。聯合國新聞部（UN News）同時強調，聯合國正與區域及國際夥伴合作，致力於緩和緊張局勢、改善地面情況，並推動透過政治談判尋求以色列-巴勒斯坦衝突的兩國解決方案，以實現中東地區全面、公正且持久的和平。
不同媒體對危機規模的關注點略有差異。文匯報與hk01均聚焦於聯合國難民署的緊急宣告與難民數字，而IOM官方頁面則更側重於整體區域性流離失所趨勢的追蹤。伊藤綾木明確指出，現有資料可能未能完全反映實際受衝擊人口規模，突顯了人道狀況的嚴峻性與資訊透明度挑戰。聯合國方面持續呼籲相關方遵守國際人道法，確保平民安全，並為人道援助物資與人員通行提供必要保障。
中東局勢的持續升溫不僅加劇了當地民生困境，亦對區域穩定與國際安全秩序構成深遠影響。聯合國相關機構的介入與呼籲，反映國際社會對該地區人道災難的高度關注。後續發展將取決於衝突各方是否回應降級呼籲、人道通道能否落實，以及國際協調機制能否有效緩解危機。分析人士指出，若局勢未能緩和，流離失所人口可能進一步攀升，對鄰國及全球資源分配帶來更大壓力。
