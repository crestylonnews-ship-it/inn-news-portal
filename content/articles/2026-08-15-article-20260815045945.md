  # WHO Calls on Africa to Break Aid Dependence, Move Toward New Era of Health Self-Reliance
  
  > The WHO African Region urges countries to accelerate implementation of the Lusaka Agenda, promoting nationally led health system development, while China-Africa health cooperation enters a new phase through a new action plan.
  
  The WHO African Region recently released a report indicating that the region faces the heaviest burden of public health emergencies globally. According to an academic study published in BMJ Global Health, the research team conducted an exploratory analysis of public health event data collected across the African Region from 2001 to 2022, finding that over the past twenty-two years, the number and scope of reported public health emergencies in the African Region ranked highest worldwide. The research team developed an exploratory data analysis method aimed at providing a basis for public health strategy and policy adjustments, as well as improving disease surveillance, preparedness, and emergency response capabilities.
  
  The core objective of the WHO African Region's Emergency Risk and Crisis Management Programme is to mitigate the health impacts of public health emergencies, natural disasters, and conflicts through effective risk reduction, preparedness, early warning, response, and recovery actions, while minimizing their social and economic consequences. The programme emphasizes coordination and cooperation at both national and international levels to build more resilient public health systems.
  
  Against the backdrop of gradually declining aid, the WHO African Region is calling on countries to accelerate implementation of the Lusaka Agenda. Proposed by African nations in 2023, the agenda advocates building nationally led, investment-oriented, and sustainable health systems to achieve genuine health sovereignty. The related summit aims to encourage African countries to strengthen their domestically led health investment and governance mechanisms.
  
  Meanwhile, China-Africa health cooperation is advancing to a new stage. According to reports from International Online, the China-Africa Cooperation Forum Beijing Summit held in 2024 adopted the "FOCAC Beijing Action Plan (2025–2027)," which clearly emphasizes the significance of health cooperation for China-Africa friendly relations and for building a high-standard China-Africa community with a shared future. African media have noted that China's experience and technical support in public health will help Africa move toward a more secure future.
  
  Analysis shows that the development of Africa's public health system faces multiple overlapping challenges. On one hand, there is a need to reduce dependence on foreign aid; on the other, domestic health system capacity must be strengthened. Under the China-Africa cooperation framework, health collaboration offers African countries an alternative development path, helping to establish more comprehensive disease surveillance and emergency response mechanisms through technology transfer and experience sharing.
  
  Subsequent observation will focus on the specific implementation progress of the Lusaka Agenda and the actual execution effectiveness of the China-Africa health cooperation action plan. Whether African countries can effectively integrate international cooperation resources while reducing aid dependence will be a key factor in determining the pace of their health self-reliance.

# 世衛組織呼籲非洲擺脫援助依賴 邁向衛生自立新時代

> 世衛組織非洲區域呼籲各國加快落實《盧薩卡議程》，推動以國家為主導的衛生體系建設，同時中非衛生合作透過新行動計劃邁向新階段。

世衛組織非洲區域近期發布報告指出，該區域面臨全球最沉重的突發公共衛生事件負擔。根據《BMJ Global Health》刊載的學術研究，研究團隊對2001年至2022年間非洲區域收集的公共衛生事件資料進行探索性分析，發現過去二十二年期間，非洲區域報告的突發公共衛生事件數量與影響範圍均居全球之首。研究團隊開發了一套探索性資料分析方法，旨在為公共衛生戰略與政策調整提供依據，並改善疾病監測、準備工作與緊急應變能力。
世衛組織非洲區域緊急風險與危機管理計畫的核心目標，是透過有效的風險降低、準備、警報、應對與復原行動，減輕公共衛生緊急事件、自然災害與衝突對健康的衝擊，同時最小化其社會與經濟影響。該計畫強調國家與國際層面的協調合作，以建立更具韌性的公共衛生體系。
在援助逐漸減少的背景下，世衛組織非洲區域呼籲各國加快落實《盧薩卡議程》。這項由非洲國家於2023年共同提出的議程，主張建構以國家為主導、投資導向且可持續的衛生體系，實現真正的衛生主權。相關峰會的召開，旨在推動非洲國家加強本國主導的衛生投資與治理機制。
與此同時，中非衛生合作正邁向新階段。根據國際線上報導，2024年舉行的中非合作論壇北京峰會透過了《中非合作論壇－北京行動計劃（2025－2027）》，明確強調衛生健康合作對中非友好合作與構建高水準中非命運共同體的重要意義。非媒指出，中國在公共衛生領域的經驗與技術支援，將幫助非洲邁向更有保障的未來。
分析顯示，非洲公共衛生體系的發展面臨多重重疊挑戰。一方面需要減少對外來援助的依賴，另一方面則需強化本國衛生系統的自主能力。中非合作框架下的衛生協作，為非洲國家提供了另一種發展途徑，透過技術轉移與經驗交流，協助當地建立更完善的疾病監測與應急機制。
後續觀察將聚焦於《盧薩卡議程》的具體落實進度，以及中非衛生合作行動計劃的實際執行成效。非洲各國能否在減少援助依賴的同時，有效整合國際合作資源，將是決定其衛生自立程式的關鍵因素。
