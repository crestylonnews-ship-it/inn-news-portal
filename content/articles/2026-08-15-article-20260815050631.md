  # Global Education Trends Report Released, Middle East Studies Degree Program Established
  
  > OECD releases 2025 global education trends report exploring the impact of inequality and AI technology on education; National Chengchi University establishes a master's degree program in Middle East and Central Asian studies to address regional research needs.
  
  The Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) recently released its triennial report, *Trends Shaping Education 2025*, providing an in-depth analysis of how social, technological, economic, environmental, and political forces are reshaping global education systems. The report highlights that growing inequality and polarization will profoundly influence the direction of educational development, while advances in technologies such as artificial intelligence and virtual reality may fundamentally transform teaching and learning models. The report emphasizes that its trend analyses are grounded in a robust academic foundation, offering important reference points for education policy formulation across countries.
  
  In terms of institutional developments, National Chengchi University has recently established a master's degree program in Middle East and Central Asian Studies, located at Daofan Building, 64 Section 2, Zhinan Road, Wenshan District, Taipei City, with contact telephone and email information published, demonstrating that Taiwan's higher education institutions are actively responding to the demand for talent in Middle East regional studies. This move aligns with themes raised in the global education trends report, such as the importance of cross-cultural understanding and regional research, reflecting how higher education institutions are adjusting their academic strategies in response to global geopolitical and social changes.
  
  The United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) previously released the report *Reimagining Our Futures Together: A New Social Contract for Education*, in which Professor Noah W. Sobel, as one of the key drafters, shared his unique insights based on his academic research and experience participating in the report's drafting. The report is built upon theoretical foundations including the three-horizon framework, theory of change, future literacy, the capability to desire, and emergent thinking, providing theoretical guidance for the future development of global education. The report's content was also published in the journal *Global Education Review* (全球教育展望), sparking extensive discussion within the academic community.
  
  Under the influence of historical backgrounds, cultural traditions, and development needs, educational objectives across countries exhibit significant differences and diverse pursuits. While education systems in all countries serve social and economic development to some extent, different regions place varying emphasis on educational goals. OECD statistics show that the ratio of higher education students to total population differs among major countries, reflecting different strategies in educational accessibility and resource allocation.
  
  Education experts point out that global education is at a critical turning point, with technological change and social transformation simultaneously driving the reshaping of education systems. Education development in the Middle East is particularly influenced by multiple factors including geopolitics, resource distribution, and cultural traditions, making the cultivation of regional research talent especially important. Future education trends will continue to be shaped by the interplay of inequality, technological progress, and global cooperation, requiring countries' education policies to balance local needs with a global perspective.

# 全球教育趨勢報告出爐 中東研究學位學程設立

> OECD 發布 2025 年全球教育趨勢報告，探討不平等與 AI 技術對教育影響；國立政治大學設立中東與中亞研究碩士學位學程，回應區域研究需求。

經濟合作與發展組織（OECD）近日發布《2025 年塑造教育的趨勢》（Trends Shaping Education 2025）三年期報告，深入分析社會、科技、經濟、環境與政治力量如何重塑全球教育體系。報告指出，日益嚴重的不平等與極化現象將深刻影響教育發展走向，而人工智慧、虛擬實境等技術進步，亦可能徹底轉變教學與學習模式。該報告強調，相關趨勢分析具備穩健的學術基礎，為各國教育政策制定提供重要參考依據。
在教育機構設定方面，國立政治大學近期設立中東與中亞研究碩士學位學程，地址位於臺北市文山區指南路二段 64 號道藩樓，並公佈聯絡電話與電子郵件信箱，顯示臺灣高等教育機構正積極回應中東區域研究的人才需求。此舉與全球教育趨勢報告中所提及的跨文化理解、區域研究重要性等議題形成呼應，反映高等教育機構正根據全球地緣政治與社會變遷調整學術佈局。
聯合國教科文組織此前發布《一起重新構想我們的未來：為教育打造新的社會契約》報告，諾亞·W. 索貝教授作為重要起草者之一，基於學術研究與參與報告起草的經歷，分享其獨特見解。該報告建立在三地平線框架、變化理論、未來素養、渴望能力、湧現思想等學理基礎之上，為全球教育未來發展提供理論指引。報告內容亦被收錄於《全球教育展望》期刊，引發學術界廣泛討論。
世界各國教育目的在歷史背景、文化傳統與發展需求影響下，呈現顯著差異與多元追求。雖然所有國家的教育制度都在一定程度上服務社會與經濟發展，但不同地區對教育目標的設定各有側重。OECD 統計資料顯示，主要國家高等教育學生佔總人口比率存在差異，反映各國在教育普及程度與資源分配上的不同策略。
教育專家指出，全球教育正處於關鍵轉折點，技術變革與社會變遷同時推動教育體系重塑。中東地區的教育發展更受到地緣政治、資源分配與文化傳統等多重因素影響，區域研究人才的培養顯得尤為重要。未來教育趨勢將持續受到不平等現象、技術進步與全球合作等因素交織影響，各國教育政策需兼顧本土需求與全球視野。
