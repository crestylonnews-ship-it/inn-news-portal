  # Asia's Space Race Heats Up: Taiwan and China Compete for New Industrial Blue Ocean
  
  > Multiple Asian nations are vying for influence in the space sector. Taiwan is actively building its low-Earth-orbit satellite supply chain, while China continues to advance space science research and public science education, shaping a regional space competition landscape.
  
  According to the Wikipedia article "Asian Space Race," as Asian countries experience economic growth and seek to expand their regional influence, the scope of competition has expanded from the economic sphere to include technology and military dimensions. The People's Republic of China, Japan, India, and other nations view space as a competitive arena due to historical factors of opposition, and their development models differ significantly from Europe's integration- and cooperation-oriented approach, with each country tending to pursue its own space program.   On Taiwan's side, the National Space Organization (TASA) emphasizes that space technology development requires systems integration and high-tech talent. Taiwan enjoys worldwide recognition in manufacturing and technology sectors, giving it unique advantages. Through the establishment of a national-level dedicated agency, resources and assistance needed by industry and academia can be provided, enabling domestic industries to upgrade once again. PanSci further reported that Taiwan's space center is actively positioning itself within the low-Earth-orbit satellite supply chain and is monitoring the environmental challenges faced by commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) electronic products as they transition from ground to space.
  
  Satellite technology is developing rapidly. From the vibration and acoustic shocks of rocket launch environments to the thermal vacuum and radiation challenges in space, COTS electronic components must all undergo rigorous testing, demonstrating Taiwan's attempt to occupy a key role in the space supply chain.

# 亞洲太空競賽升溫 臺海中岸競逐產業新藍海

> 亞洲多國競逐太空領域影響力，臺灣積極佈局低軌衛星供應鏈，中國持續推動太空科學研究與科普教育，區域太空競爭格局成形。

根據維基百科〈亞洲太空競賽〉條目指出，亞洲國家隨著經濟成長，欲增加各自的區域影響力，競爭範疇已從經濟領域擴及科技、軍事各面向。中華人民共和國、日本、印度等國間因歷史上存在對立因素，將太空視為競爭場域，發展模式與歐洲以整合、合作為主的途徑相當不同，各國傾向各自發展太空計劃。此趨勢反映亞洲太空領域正朝向多極競爭格局發展，各國相繼投入資源以爭取區域主導地位。
臺灣方面，國家太空中心（TASA）強調，太空科技發展需要系統整合及高科技人才，臺灣在製造業、科技業等領域享譽全球，具備得天獨厚的優勢。透過國家級專責機構的設立，可提供產學界所需的資源及協助，讓國內產業再次升級。PanSci泛科學進一步報導，臺灣太空中心正積極佈局低軌衛星供應鏈，並關注商用電子產品（COTS）從地面進入太空所面臨的環境挑戰。
衛星科技發展迅速，從火箭發射環境的振動與音震，到太空中的熱真空與輻射挑戰，COTS電子零元件皆須經過嚴苛測試，顯示臺灣正試圖在太空供應鏈中佔據關鍵角色。
中國方面，《太空探索》雜誌由中國科協主管、中國宇航學會主辦，為權威性航天科普月刊，已選入原新聞出版總署「中國期刊陣列」雙效期刊。2023年，在國家新聞出版署、中國科協首次組織的「優秀科普期刊」推薦活動中，《太空探索》入選20家「優秀科普期刊」推薦名單。此外，中國科學院國家空間科學中心於2026年召開黨組夏季擴大會議，顯示中國持續推動太空科學研究與政策規劃。
綜合各來源觀察，亞洲太空競賽不僅涉及國家安全與科技競爭，更延伸至產業經濟與科普教育層面。臺灣與中國在太空領域的發展路徑各有側重：臺灣著重供應鏈整合與產業升級，中國則兼顧科學研究與科普推廣。未來亞洲太空領域的競爭與合作動態，值得持續關注。
