  # UN and US Drive Global Education Reform; Central and South America Face Development Challenges
  
  > The UN calls for urgent reform of education systems, while the US releases its 2024-2029 International Basic Education Strategy; several Central and South American nations are transforming their economic structures, facing both challenges and opportunities.
  
  UN Deputy Secretary-General Aminah Mohammed warned on April 10, 2023, during her opening address at the 56th session of the Population and Development Commission that the world urgently needs to make commitments and achieve progress on education. The UN pointed out that global education goals are currently facing multiple challenges and called on countries to reform their education systems to address an increasingly complex international landscape. This move highlights that education has become one of the core issues in global governance, and for developing countries in particular, the quality and accessibility of education directly affect long-term development potential.
  
  Meanwhile, the US Agency for International Development (USAID) released the "US Government International Basic Education Strategy 2024-2029" on April 15, 2024, focusing on three key areas: quality, equity, and cooperation. The strategy emphasizes that in the context of deepening globalization, education is not only the foundation for individual growth and social progress but also a powerful force for driving sustainable development, eliminating poverty, and promoting peace and stability. Through this strategy, the US demonstrates its commitment to international basic education and expects to improve education quality through international cooperation, with particular attention to learning opportunities in resource-scarce regions.
  
  At the regional level in the Americas, Central and South American countries are facing dual challenges of economic transformation and social development. According to curriculum resources from Taiwan's Gongguan High School Singapore, a common misconception about Central and South America is that the region's economy relies solely on agricultural and mineral resources and is difficult to upgrade; however, in reality, many countries are actively integrating automotive parts, aerospace, information technology services, and creative industries, and expanding markets through regional integration. Additionally, rainforest conservation issues have drawn attention, with sustainable forestry, non-timber forest products, low-carbon tourism, and ecological compensation being viewed as possible approaches that can balance conservation and economic development.
  
  In summary, the actions of the UN and the US demonstrate that the international community is promoting education reform and regional development from different levels. The UN focuses on overall reform of the global education system, the US strengthens international basic education cooperation through a concrete strategy, and the development experience of Central and South America provides important reference for regional transformation. All sectors expect that the interaction between future education policies and regional development strategies will continue to influence the evolution of the global socioeconomic landscape.

# 聯合國與美國推動全球教育變革 中南美洲面臨發展挑戰

> 聯合國呼籲教育體系緊急變革，美國發布2024-2029國際基礎教育戰略；中南美洲多國正轉型經濟結構，挑戰與契機並存。

聯合國常務副秘書長阿明娜·穆罕默德於2023年4月10日在人口與發展委員會第56屆會議發表開幕致詞時警告，全球在教育問題上亟需盡快作出承諾並取得進展。聯合國指出，當前全球教育目標正面臨多重挑戰，呼籲各國變革教育體系，以應對日益複雜的國際形勢。此舉凸顯教育議題已成為全球治理的核心議題之一，尤其對發展中國家而言，教育品質與普及程度直接影響長期發展潛力。
與此同時，美國國際開發署（USAID）於2024年4月15日發布《美國政府國際基礎教育戰略2024-2029》，聚焦質量、公平與合作三大面向。該戰略強調，在全球化日益加深的背景下，教育不僅是個人成長與社會進步的基石，更是推動可持續發展、消除貧困、促進和平與穩定的強大動力。美國透過此戰略，展現其對國際基礎教育的重視，並預期透過國際合作提升教育品質，特別關注資源匱乏地區的學習機會。
在美洲區域層級，中南美洲各國正面臨經濟轉型與社會發展的雙重挑戰。根據臺灣公館中學新加課程資源顯示，中南美洲常見迷思認為該地區經濟僅依賴農礦資源，難以升級；然而事實上，多國正積極匯入汽車零元件、航太、資訊科技服務與創意產業，並透過區域整合拓展市場。此外，關於雨林保護的議題亦引發關注，永續林業、非木質林產品、低碳旅遊與生態補償等工具，被視為可兼顧保育與經濟發展的可能途徑。
綜合觀察，聯合國與美國的行動顯示，國際社會正從不同層面推動教育變革與區域發展。聯合國著眼於全球教育體系的整體變革，美國則透過具體戰略強化國際基礎教育合作，而中南美洲的發展經驗則提供區域轉型的重要參考。各界預期，未來教育政策與區域發展策略的互動，將持續影響全球社會經濟格局的演進。
