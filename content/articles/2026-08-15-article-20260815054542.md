  # Climate Change Impacts Intensify in Africa as Energy Transition Explores Dual-Track Path
  
  > The World Meteorological Organization report indicates that climate change impacts across Africa are comprehensively affecting socioeconomic development, exacerbating hunger and displacement; meanwhile, the "2026 Africa Energy Outlook Report" reveals the continent is exploring a dual-track path combining oil and gas with green energy.
  
  The World Meteorological Organization's (WMO) "State of Africa's Climate 2024" report shows that extreme weather and climate change impacts are comprehensively affecting Africa's socioeconomic development, exacerbating hunger, insecurity, and displacement. The report notes that since the pre-industrial era (1850–1900), Africa's warming has exceeded the global average, while sea-level rise along Africa's coastline is occurring faster than the global average, leading to increased frequency and severity of coastal flooding and erosion, with low-lying cities facing threats from rising salinity. UN news sources corroborate this trend, emphasizing that Africa's warming幅度 exceeds the global average, with water resource pressures and related hazards becoming increasingly severe.
  
  Africa is classified by some sources as one of "the most vulnerable continents on Earth," drawing international attention due to its high sensitivity to climate change. On the policy front, Africa has a long history of participation in international climate change efforts, dating back to its attendance at the 1972 Stockholm Conference. The African Union has also established the African Climate Change Committee of Heads of State and Government (CAHOSCC) and the African Ministerial Conference on Climate Change, actively promoting regional response actions. However, climate security issues still face multiple challenges, including resource scarcity, weak infrastructure, and difficulties in cross-border coordination.
  
  In terms of energy transition, Africa is forging a unique path. Wood Mackenzie's "Energy Transition Outlook: 2024–2025" analysis identifies four distinct development pathways for Africa, ranging from a catastrophic 3°C delayed transition to an ambitious 1.5°C net-zero emissions scenario. Meanwhile, the "2026 Africa Energy Outlook Report," jointly published by the African Energy Chamber and S&P Global Commodity Insights, notes that Africa has approximately 600 million people without electricity access yet possesses 23% of the world's renewable energy potential, exploring a "dual-track" development model combining oil and gas with green energy.
  
  The report argues that while global energy transition debates have fallen into a "black-and-white" path爭論, Africa offers a pragmatic alternative that maintains traditional energy industry operations while accelerating green energy investment and infrastructure development.
  
  Synthesizing analysis from various sources, Africa's climate change issues present a dual nature: on one hand, it faces severe challenges from extreme weather and environmental degradation; on the other, it holds potential opportunities for energy transition and sustainable development. International and regional organizations' attention and resource investment will be key factors in determining whether Africa can achieve balance between climate adaptation and energy transition.

# 非洲氣候變遷衝擊加劇 能源轉型探索雙軌路徑

> 世界氣象組織報告指出，非洲氣候變遷影響正全面衝擊社會經濟，加劇飢餓與流離失所；同時《2026年非洲能源狀況展望報告》揭示該大陸探索油氣與綠電雙軌並行之路。

世界氣象組織（WMO）發布的《2024年非洲氣候狀況》報告顯示，極端天氣與氣候變遷的影響正全方位衝擊非洲社會經濟發展，加劇飢餓、不安全與流離失所問題。該報告指出，自工業化前時代（1850至1900年）以來，非洲的氣候變暖程度已超過全球平均水準，同時沿非洲海岸線的海平面上升速度也快於全球平均，導致沿海洪水與侵蝕的頻率與嚴重程度增加，低窪城市面臨鹽度上升威脅。聯合國新聞資料亦佐證此趨勢，強調非洲氣候變暖幅度高於全球平均，水資源壓力與相關危害日益嚴峻。
非洲被部分訊息來源歸類為「地球上最脆弱的大陸」之一，其對氣候變遷的高度敏感性引發國際關注。在政策層面，非洲參與國際氣候變遷工作歷史悠久，最早可追溯至1972年參加斯德哥爾摩會議。非洲聯盟亦設立非洲氣候變遷國家元首和政府首腦委員會（CAHOSCC），以及非洲氣候問題部長級會議，積極推動區域應對行動。然而，氣候安全議題仍面臨多重重重挑戰，包括資源匱乏、基礎設施薄弱與跨國協調困難等問題。
在能源轉型方面，非洲正走出一條獨特道路。伍德麥肯錫（Wood Mackenzie）《能源轉型展望：2024至2025》分析指出，非洲存在四條截然不同的發展路徑，涵蓋從災難性的3℃延遲轉型到雄心勃勃的1.5℃淨零排放情景。另一方面，由非洲能源商會與S&P Global Commodity Insights聯合發布的《2026年非洲能源狀況展望報告》提出，非洲擁有約6億無電人口，卻手握全球23%的可再生能源潛力，正探索「油氣與綠電雙軌並行」的發展模式。
此報告認為，在全球能源轉型陷入「非黑即白」路徑爭論之際，非洲提供了一種務實的替代方案，既維持傳統能源產業運作，同時加速綠電投資與基礎設施建設。
綜合各來源分析，非洲氣候變遷議題呈現兩面性：一方面面臨極端天氣與環境惡化的嚴峻挑戰，另一方面也蘊含能源轉型與可持續發展的潛在機遇。國際社會與區域組織的關注與資源投入，將是影響非洲能否在氣候適應與能源過渡之間取得平衡的關鍵因素。
