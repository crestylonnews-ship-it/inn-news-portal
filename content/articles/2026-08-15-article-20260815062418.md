  # African Space Agency Unveiled in Cairo, Marking New Chapter for Regional Space Development
  
  > The headquarters of the African Space Agency was officially inaugurated in Cairo, Egypt, with the goal of achieving continent-wide space data coverage by 2030, while simultaneously advancing satellite manufacturing capabilities and policy integration. The European Space Agency has also opened a research fellowship program for African scholars.
  
  The headquarters building of the African Space Agency was officially inaugurated in Cairo, the capital of Egypt, with a unveiling ceremony held at the Cairo Space City. The establishment of the agency aims to coordinate space development initiatives across African nations, promote economic and social development through advancements in space technology, and foster scientific research cooperation within and beyond Africa, as well as the exploration of outer space. Sherif Sidky, Director-General of the Egyptian Space Agency, publicly stated that the founding of the African Space Agency is a significant milestone in the history of Africa's aerospace industry, symbolizing a new phase of autonomous development for the African continent in the space domain.
  
  According to relevant reports, the African Space Agency has set clear development goals, planning to provide space data access services covering the entire African continent for African nations by 2030, and to establish a regional satellite manufacturing base, formally integrating space solutions into the national policy frameworks of member states. In recent years, African nations have taken multiple measures to accelerate the development of their space programs, including strengthening regional cooperation, cultivating professional talent, and promoting the application of science and technology in livelihood sectors such as agriculture, meteorology, and communications.
  
  The eo4society platform under the European Space Agency (ESA) has also simultaneously announced its 2026 African Research Fellowship Program, providing specialized training, scientific tools, and expert knowledge through the African Space Academy and Digital Campus, while building a professional network that promotes open research practices and reproducible science, further supporting the development of space application projects by African researchers.
  
  The African Academy of Sciences (AAS), as the only academic institution on the African continent with the support and recognition of the African Union, has long been dedicated to transforming the quality of life across Africa through science. Headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya, the organization adheres to principles of non-alignment, non-political affiliation, and non-profit operation, recognizing outstanding talent through the conferral of its highly prestigious fellowship qualifications.
  
  In addition, the African Initiative for Planetary and Space Sciences was formally proposed during a poster session on the theme of diversity, equity, inclusion, and accessibility in March 2024, demonstrating the continued expansion of Africa in the field of fundamental space science research. Analysts note that as the African Space Agency's operations gradually take shape, combined with the complementary fellowship and training programs from European and African academic institutions, the cultivation of African space science talent and the development of technological self-reliance are expected to see significant improvement in the coming years, injecting new scientific and technological momentum into the sustainable development of the entire continent.

# 非洲航天局開羅揭牌 區域太空發展邁新頁

> 非洲航天局總部近日於埃及開羅正式落成，目標2030年實現全非太空資料覆蓋，同步推動衛星製造基地與政策整合，歐洲太空總署亦同步開放非洲研究獎學金計畫。

非洲航天局（African Space Agency）總部建築於近日在埃及首都開羅正式落成，並於開羅航天城舉行揭幕儀式。該機構的成立旨在協調非洲各國的航天發展計畫，透過航天科技進步促進經濟社會發展，同時推動非洲域內外的科研合作與外太空探索。埃及航天局執行長謝裡夫·西德基公開表示，非洲航天局的成立是非洲國家航空業發展史上的重要里程碑，象徵著非洲大陸在太空領域邁向自主發展的新階段。
根據相關報導，非洲航天局訂定了明確的發展目標，計畫在2030年前為非洲各國提供覆蓋整個非洲大陸的太空資料訪問服務，並建立區域衛星製造基地，將太空解決方案正式納入各成員國的國家政策規劃。近年來，非洲國家已採取多項措施加快推動航天事業發展，包括加強區域合作、培育專業人才、以及推動科學技術應用於農業、氣象、通訊等民生領域。
歐洲太空總署（ESA）旗下eo4society平臺亦同步公佈2026年非洲研究獎學金計畫，透過非洲太空學院與數位校園提供專門培訓、科學工具與專業知識，並建立促進開放研究實踐與可重複科學的專業網路，進一步支援非洲科研人員的太空應用專案發展。
非洲科學院（African Academy of Sciences，AAS）作為非洲大陸唯一獲得非洲聯盟夥伴支援與認可的學術機構，長期致力於透過科學變革非洲大陸的生活品質。該組織總部設於肯亞內羅畢，秉持非對立、非政治、非營利的原則，透過頒發具高度聲望的院士資格來認可優秀人才。
此外，非洲行星與太空科學倡議（African Initiative for Planetary and Space Sciences）亦於2024年3月於多元、公平、包容與可及性主題海報環節中正式提出，顯示非洲在基礎太空科學研究領域的持續擴張。分析指出，隨著非洲航天局運作逐步上軌道，加上歐洲與非洲學術機構的獎學金與培訓計畫相互配合，非洲太空科學人才培育與技術自主能力有望在未來數年內顯著提升，為整個大陸的永續發展注入新的科技動能。
