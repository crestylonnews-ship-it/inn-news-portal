  # Heatwave Threats and Healthspan: Europe's Public Health Faces a Dual Challenge
  
  > Europe's heatwaves have caused hundreds of billions in losses, with hundreds of millions facing water scarcity; meanwhile, "healthspan" has emerged as a new trend, bringing structural transformation to European public health and industry.
  
  According to Maximilian Kotz, a researcher at the Barcelona Supercomputing Center in Spain, Europe has recently experienced frequent extreme heat events, causing hundreds of billions of euros in economic losses and exposing hundreds of millions of people to water scarcity. The study further found that the impact of extreme heat on food prices is more pronounced in hotter regions, particularly in Southern Europe, where rising food prices will directly challenge the European Central Bank's inflation targets. These findings highlight that climate change is no longer merely an environmental issue, but has become a core risk affecting European public health and food security.
  
  In terms of health trends, "healthspan" is becoming a new focus of attention for Europe's health industry and daily wellness. According to Cool Health, as the global population ages, European society is gradually shifting from pursuing "living longer" to "living better," emphasizing not only extending lifespan but also maintaining health and vitality. The NutraIngredients Europe Awards 2026 has also listed this trend as a key industry observation, indicating that the nutritional supplements, functional foods, and related industries are actively adjusting their product strategies to meet European consumers' demand for high-quality healthy living.
  
  A comprehensive view reveals that European public health is facing a dual test of climate change and population aging. Heatwave-driven food price increases and water scarcity may further exacerbate nutritional security risks in Southern Europe, thereby affecting the healthspan of vulnerable populations. Conversely, the growing popularity of the healthspan concept is prompting Europe's medical and wellness industries to rethink the integration of preventive medicine and nutritional intervention. Future developments worth watching include how European governments will coordinate climate adaptation policies with public health resources, and whether the industry can create sustainable business models within the healthspan trend.

# 熱浪威脅與健康壽命：歐洲公共衛生面臨雙重挑戰

> 歐洲熱浪釀數千億歐元損失，數億人面臨缺水；同時「健康壽命」成新趨勢，歐洲公共衛生與產業迎來結構性轉變。

根據西班牙巴塞羅那超級計算中心研究員 Maximilian Kotz 指出，歐洲近年頻傳極端高溫事件，不僅造成數千億歐元經濟損失，更讓數億人面臨缺水衝擊。該研究進一步分析顯示，極端高溫對糧食價格的影響在較熱的地區更為顯著，尤其在南歐地區，糧價上漲壓力將直接挑戰歐洲央行（European Central Bank）的通膨目標。此項發現凸顯氣候變遷已不僅是環境議題，更成為影響歐洲公共衛生與糧食安全的核心風險。
在健康趨勢方面，「健康壽命」（healthspan）正成為歐洲健康產業與日常保健關注的新焦點。根據潮健康報導，隨著全球人口高齡化，歐洲社會逐漸從追求「活得久」轉向「活得好」，強調不僅延長壽命，更要維持健康與活力。NutraIngredients Europe Awards 2026 亦將此趨勢列為產業觀察重點，顯示營養保健、功能性食品等相關產業正積極調整產品策略，以因應歐洲消費者對高品質健康生活的需求。
綜合觀察，歐洲公共衛生正面臨氣候變遷與人口高齡化的雙重考驗。熱浪導致的糧價上漲與缺水問題，可能進一步加劇南歐地區的營養安全風險，進而影響弱勢族群的健康壽命；相對地，健康壽命概念的普及，也促使歐洲醫療與保健產業重新思考預防醫學與營養幹預的整合模式。後續發展值得關注歐洲各國政府如何協調氣候適應政策與公共衛生資源，以及產業界能否在健康壽命趨勢中創造可持續的商業模式。
