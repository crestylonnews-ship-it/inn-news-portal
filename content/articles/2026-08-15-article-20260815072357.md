  # Middle East Geopolitical Conflict Reshapes Regional Political Order; Great Power Competition and Regime Change Pose Governance Challenges
  
  > The Middle East has recently experienced military confrontation between Israel and Iran and sudden political changes in Syria, prompting international think tanks and scholars to focus on regional governance restructuring, great power policy maneuvering, and the development of future security orders.
  
  The Middle East's geopolitical landscape has recently undergone significant turbulence, with military confrontations and regime changes occurring in succession, profoundly impacting regional governance and international security order. According to reports from a foreign exchange and geopolitical analysis platform, Israel recently launched a large-scale preemptive air strike on over 100 targets within Iranian territory, including nuclear facilities and military positions, reportedly causing casualties among Iranian military commanders and nuclear scientists. In retaliation, Iran subsequently fired hundreds of ballistic missiles and drones at major Israeli cities in a counterattack, and the conflict further escalated to Israel's sustained strikes on Iranian administrative facilities and infrastructure. This direct military confrontation not only heightened the risk of a broader Middle Eastern conflict but also drew intense attention from global financial and energy markets.
  
  In response to the volatile situation, an Australian international affairs research institution released an assessment stating that the core issue of the current crisis lies in whether the present geopolitical turmoil will transform into an opportunity to rethink governance structures and ease regional tensions, or merely become another entry on the long list of protracted conflicts that have defined the Middle East norm over the past decades. The institution's analysis suggests that the ultimate evolution of the crisis will not only directly shape Iran's future domestic and foreign policy directions but also have a decisive impact on broader regional stability across the Middle East. The Australian think tank's research highlights that the international community remains cautiously watchful regarding whether the Middle East can escape its security dilemma and establish a sustainable security architecture.
  
  Meanwhile, significant shifts are also occurring within the region's power structure. A special research article published by the Far East Foundation, authored by Associate Professor and Director Cui Jinkui of the Institute of International Politics at Chung Hsing University, points out that the Gaza conflict, which erupted in October 2023 and has persisted for over 15 months, has had far-reaching impacts on overall geopolitical security. Additionally, the overthrow of former Syrian President Bashar al-Assad by the rebel group Hay'at Tahrir al-Sham in December 2024 has added considerable uncertainty to the already complex and volatile Middle Eastern landscape. Under the convergence of multiple crises, the rise of regional non-state actors and emerging regimes is subjecting the existing regional balance to comprehensive reshaping.
  
  Beyond regional military conflicts and regime changes, the policies of international great powers also play a pivotal role in the realignment of the Middle Eastern landscape. The China International Cooperation Center (ICC) has conducted a series of geopolitical strategy studies examining the drivers and manifestations of the new round of Middle Eastern realignment, with a focus on analyzing the evolution, strategic objectives, and spillover effects of U.S. Middle East security policy. The study indicates that against the backdrop of great power competition, China and the United States have pursued different approaches in terms of policy drivers, narrative contestation, and regional cooperation initiatives in the Middle East, and great power interactions will continue to shape the future development of Middle Eastern security architecture and economic cooperation.
  
  Synthesizing various analyses and publicly available information, the Middle East is currently at the intersection of normalized military confrontation, reorganization of traditional regimes, and great power maneuvering. Whether it is the threat posed by escalating military conflicts to critical infrastructure and nuclear security, or the power vacuums created by political changes in Syria and adjustments in great power policies, all are testing the diplomatic mediation and crisis management capabilities of all parties. Whether the region can construct a new political order through multilateral consultation or fall into deeper and more prolonged turmoil remains to be seen, pending continued observation of subsequent policy moves and military deployments by various countries.

# 中東地緣衝突重構區域政治秩序　大國競爭與政權更迭引發治理挑戰

> 中東地區近期歷經以伊軍事對決與敘利亞政局突變，國際智庫與學者聚焦區域治理重組、大國政策博弈及未來安全秩序發展。

中東地緣政治格局近期出現顯著震盪，軍事對抗與政權更迭接連發生，深刻衝擊區域治理與國際安全秩序。根據外匯與地緣政治分析平臺報導，以色列日前對伊朗境內的核子設施及軍事據點等一百多處目標發動大規模先制空襲，據悉造成伊朗軍方指揮官與核子科學家傷亡；作為反制，伊朗隨後向以色列主要城市發射數百枚彈道飛彈與無人機進行報復，衝突更進一步擴大至以色列對伊朗行政設施及基礎建設的持續打擊。這場直接軍事交鋒不僅加劇了中東全面爆發衝突的風險，也引發全球金融與能源市場的高度關注。
面對劇烈變動的局勢，澳洲國際事務研究機構發布評估指出，當前危機的核心問題在於，現階段的地緣動盪究竟會轉化為重新思考治理架構與緩解區域緊張局勢的契機，抑或只是再度淪為過去數十年來定義中東常態的長期衝突清單之一。該機構分析認為，危機的最終演變不僅直接左右伊朗未來的國內與外交走向，亦將對更廣泛的中東區域穩定產生決定性影響。澳洲智庫的研究凸顯出，國際社會對中東能否走出安全困境、建立可持續的安全架構仍抱持審慎觀望態度。
與此同時，區域內部的權力結構亦發生重大變化。遠景基金會刊登中興大學國際政治研究所副教授兼所長崔進揆的專題研究指出，加薩戰火自2023年10月爆發並持續超過15個月，對整體地緣安全造成深遠影響；此外，敘利亞前總統阿薩德於2024年12月遭反政府勢力「沙姆解放組織」推翻，敘利亞未來的政局走向更使複雜多變的中東局勢增添高度不確定性。多重危機交織下，區域非國家行為者與新興政權的崛起，使既有的區域平衡面臨全面重塑。
除了區域內部的軍事衝突與政權變更，國際大國政策在中東格局調整中亦扮演關鍵角色。中國國際合作中心（ICC）的地緣戰略研究系列探討了新一輪中東格局調整的動因與表徵，並重點分析美國中東安全政策的演變、戰略目標與外溢效應。該研究指出，在大國競爭背景下，美中兩國在中東政策驅動力、敘事博弈及地區合作倡議上呈現不同途徑，大國互動將持續牽動中東安全架構與經濟合作的未來發展。
綜合各方分析與公開資料，中東當前正處於軍事對峙常態化、傳統政權重組以及外部大國博弈的多重交會點。無論是軍事衝突升級對關鍵基礎設施與核安全構成的威脅，抑或是敘利亞政局演變與大國政策調整帶來的權力真空，均考驗著各方外交調解與危機管控的能力。未來該地區是否能透過多邊協商建構新的政治秩序，抑或陷入更深層次的長期動盪，仍有待持續追蹤各國後續的政策動向與軍事部署。
