  # EU Advances European Education Area Plan, Combining Digital and Green Transitions to Reduce Inequality
  
  > The European Commission is promoting the "European Education Area 2025 Action Plan," combining post-pandemic recovery with the strengths of higher education to improve educational equity for disadvantaged groups through digital and green dual transitions.
  
  In response to the pressures on the global economy and social structure in recent years, the European Commission is actively planning a blueprint for cross-border educational integration. According to a report by the overseas academic exchange platform ScholarsUpdate.com, to mitigate the negative impacts of the COVID-19 pandemic on Europe's economy and society, the European Commission, after extensive consultation with all sectors, combined the post-pandemic European economic recovery strategy and officially launched the "European Education Area 2025 Action Plan," clearly setting the policy goal of fully building a European Education Area by 2025 and establishing a new direction for deeper cooperation among European countries in the field of education.
  
  Regarding higher education and academic mobility, the official European education portal "European Education Area" (education.ec.europa.eu) notes that Europe continues to leverage the strengths of its higher education system by promoting the "Study in Europe" initiative. The platform emphasizes that Europe offers world-class higher education environments, encompassing a wide range of diverse bachelor's, master's, and doctoral degree programs, and is committed to creating an open, high-quality, and cross-border mobile educational space for students within and outside Europe to promote academic exchange and the cultivation of professional talent.
  
  Beyond the advancement of higher academic systems, education also plays a central role in promoting social equity and global sustainable development. A policy guideline issued by the European Commission's Directorate-General for International Partnerships (international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu) states that education is a key force in addressing social inequality and poverty, capable of effectively unlocking human development potential and providing a fair and sustainable foundation for the successful implementation of digital and green transitions.
  
  The European Commission specifically emphasizes that the policy implementation process must ensure that girls, refugees, ethnic minorities, and children and youth in vulnerable environments or crisis situations can substantially benefit from high-quality education and vocational training resources.
  
  Synthesizing publicly available information and policy developments, the EU's approach to educational reform has extended from traditional academic integration to multiple dimensions including economic recovery, sustainable development, and social inclusion. Through the framework of the "European Education Area 2025 Action Plan," the EU hopes to balance the cultivation of high-level talent with the educational rights of disadvantaged groups, and to use education as a cornerstone for driving digital and green transitions. However, differences in the implementation progress and effectiveness among member states regarding educational resource integration, support mechanisms for disadvantaged education, and digital infrastructure still require further observation and evaluation before the 2025 target deadline.

# 歐盟推動歐洲教育區計畫 結合數位與綠色轉型弭平不平等

> 歐盟委員會推動「歐洲教育區2025行動計畫」，結合疫後復甦與高等教育優勢，透過數位與綠色雙轉型改善弱勢教育權益。

面對近年全球經濟與社會結構所承受的衝擊，歐盟委員會積極規劃跨國教育整合藍圖。根據海外學術交流平臺《美國華裔教授專家網》（ScholarsUpdate.com）報導，為減緩新冠疫情對歐洲經濟與社會造成的負面影響，歐盟委員會在廣泛徵詢各界意見後，結合疫後歐洲經濟復甦戰略，正式出臺「歐洲教育區2025行動計畫」，明確訂定於2025年全面建成歐洲教育區的政策目標，確立了歐洲各國在教育領域深化合作的新方向。
在高等教育與學術流動性方面，歐盟官方教育入口平臺「歐洲教育區」（education.ec.europa.eu）指出，歐洲持續發揮其高等教育體系之優勢，推動「留學歐洲」（Study in Europe）計畫。該平臺強調，歐洲提供世界級的高等教育環境，涵蓋廣泛多樣的學士、碩士及博士學位研究學程，致力為歐洲境內及國際學生打造開放、高品質且具備跨國流動性的教育空間，以促進學術交流與專業人才培育。
除高等學術體系之推展外，教育在促進社會公平與全球永續發展中亦扮演核心角色。歐盟委員會國際夥伴關係總署（international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu）發布的政策方針指出，教育是應對社會不平等與貧窮問題的關鍵力量，不僅能有效激發人類發展潛能，更能為數位轉型與綠色轉型的成功落實提供公平且可持續的基礎。
歐盟官方特別強調，政策推動過程必須確保女童、難民、少數族群，以及身處脆弱環境或面臨危機情境中的兒童與青年，均能實質受惠於高品質的教育與職業培訓資源。
綜合各方公開資訊與政策進展，歐盟推動教育改革的方針已從傳統的學術整合，延伸至經濟復甦、永續發展與社會包容等多重面向。透過「歐洲教育區2025行動計畫」的架構，歐盟期望兼顧高階人才培育與弱勢群體教育權益，並以教育作為推動數位與綠色轉型的基石。然而，針對各成員國在教育資源整合、弱勢教育支援機制以及數位基礎建設上的落實進度與成效差異，仍有待2025年目標期程前進一步觀察與檢驗。
