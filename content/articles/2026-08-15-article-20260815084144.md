  # Africa's Energy Transition Accelerates as Renewables and Oil & Gas Development Advance in Parallel
  
  > The World Economic Forum's 2026 Energy Transition Index highlights Namibia, Tunisia, and Gabon as leaders in Africa's energy transition; meanwhile, Africa's renewable energy potential is substantial, with solar photovoltaic capacity projected to reach 650 gigawatts by 2050.
  
  According to the World Economic Forum's (WEF) 2026 Energy Transition Index, several African countries have made significant progress in sustainable energy, with Namibia, Tunisia, and Gabon rated as the top performers on the continent. The index shows that these three countries have become regional models for energy transition through efficient development and operation of their domestic energy systems, providing a reference blueprint for sustainable infrastructure development across Africa. The report, released in late June 2026, focuses on specific achievements and policy effectiveness in countries' energy transition efforts.
  
  In terms of renewable energy development, Africa's overall potential has attracted considerable international attention. At the 2023 Abu Dhabi Sustainability Week, participants jointly launched an initiative for the development of Africa's renewable energy industry, emphasizing the continent's superior renewable energy resource endowment and vast development potential. According to projections from the initiative, Africa's solar photovoltaic installed capacity will grow to 650 gigawatts by 2050, demonstrating a long-term vision for industry expansion. This data comes from a China Daily report published in January 2024, which noted that Africa is accelerating its push for renewable energy development to address growing energy demand and climate change challenges.
  
  Running parallel to renewable energy development, Africa's traditional oil and gas industry remains important. According to a research paper published in January 2026 in the journal Earth Science, based on commercial databases such as S&P Global and the International Energy Agency, as well as public information from international oil companies like BP, the research team conducted a systematic analysis of the distribution characteristics, discoveries, future exploration and development potential, and current investment status of Africa's oil and gas resources. The study found that Africa is rich in oil and gas resources, but exploration intensity and discoveries are uneven, with North Africa and West Africa being particularly prominent geographically. The research clearly outlines Africa's future exploration and development prospects and oil and gas investment directions, providing a reference for industry decision-making.
  
  Synthesizing information from various sources, Africa's energy development presents a diversified and parallel advancement: on one hand, countries such as Namibia, Tunisia, and Gabon lead in energy transition indicators, demonstrating tangible results in renewable energy development; on the other hand, Africa's overall renewable energy potential is expected to be substantial, with a 2050 solar photovoltaic installed capacity target set at 650 gigawatts. Meanwhile, oil and gas exploration and development remain an important component of Africa's energy landscape, with North Africa and West Africa holding significant resource advantages. Observers note that Africa's energy transition must strike a balance between renewable energy expansion and traditional oil and gas development to meet regional economic development needs while兼顧ing sustainability goals.

# 非洲能源轉型加速 可再生能源與油氣開發並行發展

> 世界經濟論壇發布《2026年能源轉型指數》指出，奈米比亞、突尼西亞與加彭引領非洲能源轉型；同時非洲可再生能源潛力巨大，預計2050年太陽能光伏裝機量將達650吉瓦。

根據世界經濟論壇（WEF）發布的《2026年能源轉型指數》，非洲多國在永續能源領域取得顯著進展，其中奈米比亞、突尼西亞與加彭被評為非洲大陸表現最出色的國家。該指數顯示，這三個國家透過高效開發與運營國內能源系統，已成為區域能源轉型的典範，為整個非洲大陸的基礎建設可持續發展提供參考藍圖。此報告於2026年6月底發布，聚焦於各國在能源轉型方面的具體成果與政策成效。
在可再生能源發展方面，非洲整體潛力備受國際關注。2023年阿布扎比可持續發展周活動上，與會者共同發布了非洲可再生能源產業發展倡議，強調非洲擁有優越的可再生能源資源稟賦與巨大發展潛力。根據該倡議的預測資料，到2050年非洲太陽能光伏裝機量將增長至650吉瓦，顯示出長遠的產業擴張願景。此資料來自中國日報網於2024年1月刊載的報導，指出非洲正加速推動可再生能源發展，以應對能源需求增長與氣候變遷挑戰。
與可再生能源發展並行的是，非洲傳統油氣產業仍具重要性。根據《地球科學》期刊於2026年1月刊載的研究論文，基於標普全球、國際能源署等商業資料庫及BP等國際油公司公開資訊，研究團隊對非洲油氣資源分佈特徵、發現情況、未來勘探開發潛力及投資現狀進行了系統分析。研究指出，非洲油氣資源豐富，但勘探程度與油氣發現呈現不均狀態，地理上以北非與西非地區較為突出。該研究明確提出非洲未來勘探開發前景與油氣投資方向，為相關產業決策提供參考依據。
綜合各來源資訊，非洲能源發展呈現多元並進格局：一方面，奈米比亞、突尼西亞與加彭等國在能源轉型指標上領先，展現可再生能源發展的實際成果；另一方面，非洲整體可再生能源潛力預期龐大，2050年太陽能光伏裝機量目標設定為650吉瓦。同時，油氣勘探開發仍為非洲能源版圖的重要組成部分，北非與西非地區具備顯著資源優勢。各界觀察指出，非洲能源轉型需在可再生能源擴張與傳統油氣開發之間取得平衡，以滿足區域經濟發展需求並兼顧永續目標。
