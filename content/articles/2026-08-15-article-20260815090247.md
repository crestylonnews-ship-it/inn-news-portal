  # EU Launches Billion-Euro Defense Fund and Humanitarian Aid Reform, Accelerating Policy Layout Amid Geopolitical Challenges
  
  > Facing geopolitical and humanitarian crises, the EU has finalized the billion-euro SAFE defense fund to advance European prioritization, while issuing a humanitarian declaration to drive reforms and sustain aid capacity amid resource shortfalls.
  
  Against a backdrop where regional security dynamics and supply chain autonomy are under intense scrutiny, the European Commission has officially finalized the "Security Action for Europe" (SAFE) major defense industry financing fund. According to industry data center reports, the fund's total scale reaches 150 billion euros (approximately 170 billion US dollars), and will primarily be provided in loan form to support cross-border joint defense R&D and equipment projects.
  
  To strengthen Europe's domestic defense resilience, this policy strictly adheres to the core principle of "European prioritization," explicitly requiring that up to 65% of procurement份額 must come from Europe's own supply chain system, striving to comprehensively enhance Europe's domestic defense industry capacity and autonomous supply capabilities. Meanwhile, reports also indicate that the UK is expected to participate in related cooperation frameworks, showing that multiple European countries are accelerating the integration of their defense resources and industrial capabilities to address increasingly complex regional defense needs.
  
  Beyond strengthening hard power in defense and security, the EU has also adopted new strategic adjustments in facing global humanitarian affairs. According to EU Reporter, the EU has passed the "Joint Declaration on Humanitarian Aid," which elaborates in depth on how the EU can continue to play the role of a principled and trustworthy international donor in the face of the current immense pressure on the global aid system.
  
  Relevant public data shows that global humanitarian needs are at an unprecedented high level. Affected by wars, natural disasters, and various crises, there are currently as many as 239 million people worldwide urgently needing external assistance. However, existing global humanitarian aid funding faces severe resource shortages, with actual raised funds only able to cover and help less than half of the affected population. To address this growing supply-demand imbalance, the EU has established new measures through this joint declaration, declaring that while defending basic values, it will fully promote internal reforms of the aid system, hoping to create substantial influence and rescue synergy under limited funding conditions.
  
  From the autonomous integration of European regional defense to the governance of global crisis assistance, the EU is demonstrating policy restructuring trends across multiple dimensions. Research literature collected by the Taiwan Association for European Studies indicates that under the long-term impact of geopolitical security shocks such as the Russia-Ukraine war, EU policy is moving toward new development trends and challenges.
  
  Synthesizing facts confirmed across various sources, Europe is actively seeking autonomy in the defense industry chain, including locking procurement focus within Europe through the billion-euro SAFE fund and evaluating the inclusion of important external partners like the UK for joint collaboration. In non-traditional security and humanitarian affairs, the EU also recognizes the harsh reality that existing international funding cannot meet the massive 239 million aid needs globally, thus turning toward mechanism reform to maintain policy influence. Although the specific implementation progress of defense autonomous procurement standards and humanitarian resource integration still needs follow-up tracking and verification, these measures clearly outline Europe's comprehensive efforts to strengthen its own security and defense while restructuring humanitarian aid in a turbulent international environment.

# 歐盟啟動千億國防基金與人道援助改革 面對地緣挑戰加速政策佈局

> 面對地緣與人道危機，歐盟敲定千億SAFE國防基金以落實歐洲優先，並發布人道公報推動改革，力求在資源缺口下維持援助能量。

在區域安全域性勢與供應鏈自主性備受考驗的背景下，歐洲聯盟執委會正式敲定名為「歐洲安全行動計畫」（Security Action for Europe, SAFE）的重大軍工融資基金。根據產業資料中心報導，該基金的整體規模高達1,500億歐元（約摺合1,700億美元），未來主要將以貸款形式提供支援，用於推動跨國聯合國防研發與裝備專案。
為了強化歐洲本土的防衛韌性，此項政策嚴格秉持「歐洲優先」的核心原則，明文要求計畫中高達65%的採購份額必須來自歐洲自身的供應鏈體系，力求全面提升歐洲本土國防工業的產能與自主供應能力；與此同時，報導亦指出英國也傳出即將參與相關合作架構，顯示歐洲多國正在加速整合彼此的防務資源與工業實力，以因應日益複雜的區域防衛需求。
除了在防務與安全領域強化硬實力外，歐盟在面對全球人道事務時亦採取了新的戰略調整。依據《歐盟記者》報導，歐盟透過了《關於人道援助的聯合公報》，深入闡述歐盟如何在當前面臨巨大壓力的全球援助體系中，持續扮演具備原則性且值得信賴的國際捐助者角色。
相關公開資料顯示，全球人道需求正處於前所未有的空前高漲狀態，受戰亂、天災與各類危機影響，目前全球有多達2.39億人亟需外界伸出援手；然而，國際間現有的全球人道援助資金卻面臨嚴重的資源短缺，實際籌措到的經費僅能覆蓋並幫助不到半數的受難人口。為瞭解決這項日益擴大的供給失衡問題，歐盟透過該項聯合公報確立了新措施，宣示將在捍衛基本價值觀的同時，全力推動援助體系內部改革，期望能在資金有限的條件下創造實質影響力與救援綜效。
從歐洲區域防務的自主整合到全球危機的援助治理，歐盟在多個面向展現出政策重構的趨勢。中華民國（臺灣）歐洲研究協會所收錄之研究文獻指出，在俄烏戰爭等地緣安全衝擊的長遠影響下，歐盟政策正邁向新的發展趨勢與挑戰。
綜合各來源所共同確認的事實，歐洲正在積極尋求防務產業鏈的自主化，包括透過千億規模的SAFE基金將採購重心鎖定於歐洲內部，並評估吸納英國等外部重要夥伴共同協作；而在非傳統安全與人道事務方面，歐盟亦深知既有國際資金無法滿足全球2.39億龐大援助需求的殘酷現實，因而轉向推動機制改革以維持其政策影響力。雖然防務自主採購標準與人道資源整合的具體落實進度仍有待後續追蹤驗證，但各項措施已清楚勾勒出歐洲在動盪國際環境中，全力強化自身安全防衛並重整人道援助
