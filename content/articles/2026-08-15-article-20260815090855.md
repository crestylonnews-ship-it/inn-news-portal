  # US Tariff Policy Reshapes Americas Trade Landscape as China Loses Top Trading Partner Status
  
  > The US continues to advance tariff measures, imposing duties on major trading partners including Canada, Mexico, and China, as the global economy enters a new era. China has fallen to third place as the US's largest trading partner, with clear trends of regional supply chain restructuring across the Americas.
  
  Since beginning his second term in office, US President Donald Trump has vigorously pursued the "America First" policy, with tariff measures emerging as a key variable shaping the global economic landscape. According to analysis by the International Monetary Fund (IMF), the current global economic system is undergoing a reset process—existing rules are being challenged while new ones have yet to take shape, and the world has formally entered a new era. Starting from late January 2025, the US successively announced tariff measures targeting Canada, China, Mexico, and several key industries, and on April 2 announced tariffs on nearly all imported goods, escalating global trade tensions.
  
  The HKU SPACE School of Business and Economics pointed out that since March 2025, the US has not only imposed a 25% import tariff on global steel and aluminum products but also implemented specific tariffs on major trading partners including China, Canada, and Mexico. In the face of the US's increasingly assertive trade protectionism, economic powers such as the European Union, Canada, and China have successively taken countermeasures, and global trade friction continues to expand.
  
  The report "Global Economic Outlook for the Second Half of 2025 Under the Background of Tariff Barriers," published by Min Yin International, also noted that the isolationism and rising trade protectionism pursued by the Trump administration have once again disrupted the new equilibrium system established since 2023, with friction between the US and its traditional allies continuously escalating.
  
  In terms of the regional trade landscape in the Americas, China has ceded its position as the US's largest trading partner, dropping to third place. According to analysis by Business Today, the trade dynamics among the US and its top three trading partners—China, Canada, and Mexico—show that US trade with its neighbors Canada and Mexico has maintained steady growth. Specifically, the US-Mexico trade ratio rose from 12.3% in 2010 to 15.7% in 2024, indicating a clear trend of supply chain restructuring in the Americas. Data from the US-Taiwan Trade Council (TAITRA) also shows that economic activity in the Americas region remains consistently active, and the US continues to hold a dominant position in the regional economy.
  
  Educational materials from the Council on Foreign Relations (CFR) indicate that the Americas economic landscape is dominated by the US, which accounts for nearly one-third of the region's population of over one billion and three-quarters of its economic output. However, as US tariff policy continues to tighten, countries within the region are actively adjusting their trade strategies. China faces supply chain challenges, while Taiwan is viewed as facing both opportunities and challenges in the new landscape. Analysts believe that regional trade relations in the Americas will continue to evolve, and countries need to respond cautiously to the uncertainties brought by tariff barriers.

# 美國關稅政策重塑美洲貿易格局 中國失去最大貿易夥伴地位

> 美國持續推動關稅措施，對加拿大、墨西哥及中國等主要貿易夥伴徵收關稅，全球經濟進入新時期。中國屈居美國第三大貿易夥伴，美洲區域供應鏈重組趨勢明顯。

美國總統特朗普（Donald Trump）自二次執政以來，大力推行「美國優先」政策，關稅措施成為影響全球經濟格局的關鍵變數。根據國際貨幣基金組織（IMF）分析，當前全球經濟體系正經歷重置過程，現有規則受到挑戰，新規則尚未形成，世界正式進入一個新的時期。自2025年1月下旬起，美國陸續宣佈針對加拿大、中國、墨西哥及若干關鍵行業的關稅措施，並於4月2日宣佈對幾乎所有進口商品徵收關稅，引發全球貿易緊張局勢升溫。
香港大學專業進修學院商務及經濟學院指出，自2025年3月起，美國不僅對全球鋼鐵和鋁產品徵收25%的進口關稅，亦對中國、加拿大、墨西哥等主要貿易夥伴實施特定關稅。面對美國日益強硬的貿易保護主義政策，歐盟、加拿大和中國等經濟體相繼採取反制措施，全球貿易摩擦持續擴大。
民銀國際發布的《關稅壁壘背景下，2025年下半年全球經濟展望》報告亦指出，特朗普政府推行的孤立主義和貿易保護主義抬頭，使全球自2023年以來建立的新均衡體系再度被打破，美國與傳統盟友之間的摩擦不斷升級。
在美洲區域貿易版圖方面，中國已拱手讓出美國最大貿易夥伴地位，屈居第三。根據《今週刊》分析，美國與其前三大貿易夥伴中國、加拿大和墨西哥的貿易消長顯示，美國與加墨鄰國貿易規模保持穩定增長。其中，美國與墨西哥的貿易比例從2010年的12.3%上升至2024年的15.7%，顯示美洲區域供應鏈重組趨勢明顯。美國海外貿易促進協會（TAITRA）資料亦顯示，美洲地區經濟活動持續活躍，美國在區域經濟中仍佔主導地位。
美國海外關係委員會（CFR）教育資料指出，美洲經濟版圖由美國主導，美國佔該地區十億以上人口的近三分之一，以及該地區經濟產出的四分之三。然而，隨著美國關稅政策持續收緊，區域內各國正積極調整貿易策略。中國面臨供應鏈困局，臺灣則被視為在新格局下面臨機遇與挑戰並存。分析人士認為，未來美洲區域貿易關係將持續演變，各國需謹慎應對關稅壁壘帶來的不確定性。
