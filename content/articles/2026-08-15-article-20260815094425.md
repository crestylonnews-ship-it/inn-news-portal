  # U.S. Releases New Global Health Strategy, Pivoting to National Security and Commercial Priorities
  
  > On September 18, 2025, the U.S. Department of State released the "America First Global Health Strategy," marking a shift from humanitarian action to national security and commercial priorities in U.S. global health cooperation. The WHO simultaneously launched its 2025–2028 Global Health Strategy, while PAHO continues to coordinate regional health affairs in the Americas.
  
  On September 18, 2025, the U.S. Department of State officially released the "America First Global Health Strategy," clearly indicating that the United States is undergoing a strategic adjustment in the field of global health cooperation, shifting from its previous focus on humanitarian aid to a new direction centered on national security and commercial interests. The strategy, anchored by the theme of "making America safer, stronger, and more prosperous," reflects the U.S. government's rethinking of its positioning on global health affairs. This move has drawn attention from international health policy observers, who believe it will affect the model of U.S. health cooperation with other countries and multilateral institutions.
  
  The World Health Organization (WHO) simultaneously launched the "2025–2028 Global Health Strategy," also known as the 14th General Programme of Work (GPW 14), setting a clear agenda aimed at bringing the world back on track to achieve health-related Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), while advancing health equity and strengthening health system resilience. The WHO emphasized that in an increasingly volatile global environment, this strategy will assist countries in addressing health challenges.
  
  Regional health cooperation in the Americas is driven by the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO), founded in 1902 as the world's oldest international public health institution. It serves as both the WHO Regional Office for the Americas and the specialized health agency of the Inter-American System, continuing to cooperate with countries across the Americas to improve population health and quality of life.
  
  The U.S. domestic public health system comprises multiple tiers of institutions, including federal agencies, non-health government entities, clinical care systems, state, tribal, local, and territorial health departments (STLTs), media, community organizations, private and nonprofit associations, educational institutions, and private enterprises. Relevant materials from the Ministry of Education indicate that this decentralized structure means the U.S. public health response capacity relies on coordination across all levels. Analysis suggests that the U.S. new global health strategy's pivot may affect its resource investment and policy priorities for regional health cooperation in the Americas, and PAHO and WHO coordination efforts in the Americas will face new tests in the international environment.

# 美國發布全球衛生新戰略 轉向國安與商業優先

> 美國國務院2025年9月18日發表「美國優先全球衛生戰略」，標誌美國在全球衛生合作領域從人道行動轉向國安與商業優先；WHO同步推出2025至2028年全球衛生戰略，PAHO持續協調美洲區域衛生事務。

美國國務院於2025年9月18日正式發表《美國優先全球衛生戰略》（America First Global Health Strategy），明確指出美國在全球衛生合作領域正經歷策略性調整，從過往側重人道援助行動，轉向以國家安全與商業利益為核心的新方向。該戰略以「讓美國更安全、強大與繁榮」為主軸，反映美國政府對全球衛生事務定位的重新思考。此舉引發國際衛生政策觀察家關注，認為這將影響美國與其他國家及多邊機構的衛生合作模式。
世界衛生組織（WHO）同期推出《2025至2028年全球衛生戰略》，即第十四期總工作計劃（GPW 14），設定明確議程旨在讓世界重新回到實現衛生相關永續發展目標（SDGs）的軌道上，同時推進衛生公平並強化衛生系統韌性。WHO強調，在日益動盪的全球環境中，此戰略將協助各國應對衛生挑戰。
美洲區域衛生合作則由泛美衛生組織（PAHO）負責推動，該組織成立於1902年，為全球最古老的國際公共衛生機構，同時擔任WHO美洲區域辦公室及美洲間系統的專門衛生機構，持續與美洲各國合作改善人口健康與生活品質。
美國國內公共衛生體系由多層級機構組成，包含聯邦機構、非衛生部門的政府單位、臨床護理系統、州部落地方與地區衛生部門（STLTs）、媒體、社群組織、私營與非營利協會、教育機構及私營企業等多元主體。教育部相關資料指出，此分散式架構使美國公共衛生應對能力依賴各層級協調。分析認為，美國新全球衛生戰略的轉向，可能影響其對美洲區域衛生合作的資源投入與政策優先順序，PAHO與WHO在美洲的協調工作將面臨新的國際環境考驗。
