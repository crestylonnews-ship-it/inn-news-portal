  > Negotiations between the United States and Iran over nuclear programs have reached an impasse. While the 60-day deadline can be extended, consensus remains elusive on issues such as the Hormuz Strait, and geopolitical risks in the Middle East continue to escalate.
  
  Nuclear negotiations between the United States and Iran have recently stalled. Although the original 60-day agreement deadline can be extended, breakthroughs on key issues remain out of reach. According to the World Journal, the 60-day period mentioned in the agreement is an extendable timeframe during which Iran and the United States are expected to reach a final agreement covering more significant issues, including Iran's controversial nuclear program. However, a series of issues, including the Hormuz Strait, remain unresolved, stalling efforts to achieve lasting peace. As a vital channel for global oil transportation, the Hormuz Strait has long been a focal point of dispute between the US and Iran over control and freedom of navigation. The significant divergence in positions between the two sides on this issue has slowed the pace of negotiations.
  
  The complexity of the Middle East situation is reflected not only in nuclear negotiations but also in ongoing regime changes and conflicts across the region. Cui Jinkui, a researcher at the Far East Foundation, pointed out that the Gaza War, which erupted in October 2023, has now lasted 15 months. Additionally, the overthrow of Syrian President Bashar al-Assad by the rebel group Hay'at Tahrir al-Sham in December 2024 has had a major impact on the already complex and rapidly evolving Middle East geopolitical landscape. This regime change has further intensified uncertainty surrounding the reshuffling of regional powers, with neighboring countries and the international community closely monitoring the security and governance trajectory during Syria's transitional period.
  
  Geopolitical analysts emphasize that the complexity and challenges of the Middle East stem from multi-layered intersecting interests, including great power competition, regional power struggles, and the rise of non-state actors, making it difficult to resolve any single issue without considering broader structural shifts.
  
  Views within the international community on the prospects of US-Iran negotiations are divided. Some observers believe that extending the negotiation deadline may leave room for a diplomatic resolution, while others worry that a stalemate could trigger an escalation of regional military tensions. The safety of navigation in the Hormuz Strait is directly linked to the stability of global energy supplies; if negotiations break down and friction arises in the region, it could impact international oil prices and supply chains. Meanwhile, developments in Syria and the prolonged Gaza War further highlight the long-accumulated security dilemmas in the Middle East. Experts recommend that relevant parties reduce the risk of miscalculation through multilateral dialogue mechanisms and pay attention to humanitarian aid and reconstruction issues to alleviate the suffering borne by civilians.

# 美伊核協議談判陷僵局 荷莫茲海峽議題成關鍵障礙

> 美國與伊朗就核計畫談判陷入僵局，60天期限可延長但荷莫茲海峽等議題難以達成共識，中東地緣政治風險持續升溫。

美國與伊朗之間的核計畫談判近期陷入僵局，雙方原定60天的協議期限雖可延長，但關鍵議題仍無法取得突破。根據《世界日報》報導，協議所稱的60天是一個可以延長的時間範圍，在此期間內，預期伊朗和美國將達成內容涵蓋伊朗備受爭議的核計畫等更重大議題的最終協議。然而，包括荷莫茲海峽在內的一系列議題談不攏，導致尋求持久和平的努力陷入僵局。荷莫茲海峽作為全球石油運輸的重要通道，其管控權與航行自由問題一直是美伊爭議焦點，雙方在此議題上的立場差異顯著，使得談判進展緩慢。
中東局勢的複雜性不僅體現在核談判，更反映在區域政權更迭與衝突持續上。遠景基金會研究員崔進揆指出，加薩戰爭自2023年10月爆發迄今已15個月，而敘利亞總統阿薩德在2024年12月被反政府勢力沙姆解放組織推翻後，敘利亞未來政局發展對本就複雜與變化快速的中東地緣政治產生重大影響。這一政權更迭事件進一步加劇了區域勢力重新洗牌的不確定性，周邊國家與國際社會正密切關注敘利亞過渡時期的安全與治理走向。
地緣政治分析人士強調，中東局勢的複雜性與挑戰在於多層次的利益交織，包括大國競爭、區域強權角力以及非國家行為體的崛起，使得任何單一議題的解決都難以脫離整體格局的變動。
國際社會對美伊談判前景看法分歧，部分觀察家認為延長談判期限可能為外交解決留下空間，但亦有聲音擔憂僵局可能引發區域軍事緊張升級。荷莫茲海峽的航行安全直接關乎全球能源供應穩定，若談判破裂導致該區域出現摩擦，將對國際油價與供應鏈造成衝擊。同時，敘利亞局勢演變與加薩戰爭延燒，進一步凸顯中東地區長期積累的安全困境。專家建議，相關方應透過多邊對話機制降低誤判風險，並關注人道援助與重建議題，以緩解平民所承受的苦難。
未來數週內美伊談判進展、敘利亞過渡安排以及加薩停火協商，將成為影響區域穩定的關鍵指標。
