  The European Space Agency (ESA) has recently launched a research and development project called HOBI-WAN, focused on developing sustainable food sources for deep space missions. The project falls under the ESA's "Earth New Stars Exploration Programme," with its main objective being the testing of a powdered protein called Solein. According to publicly available information, Solein is produced in a unique manner, synthesized from microbes, air, and electricity, and is expected to become an important nutritional source for future human missions to the Moon and Mars.
  
  This move reflects the European space agency's active efforts to address the technical challenges of food supply during long-duration space missions and to break through the limitations of traditional space food.
  
  The European Space Agency, founded in 1975, currently has 22 member states and operates both crewed and uncrewed space missions. At the EU level, space programs are also closely integrated with a number of civilian and scientific projects. The EU's space program integrates the Galileo satellite navigation system, the Copernicus Earth observation program, and the European Geostationary Navigation Overlay Service (EGNOS), aiming to provide world-class space services to European citizens, businesses, and governments.
  
  The European Space Policy Institute (ESPI), a think tank in the European space sector supported by 23 member organizations, is committed to promoting European space policy globally and providing independent analysis, recommendations, and policy proposals to decision-makers and institutions, while raising public awareness of strategic space issues.
  
  The core innovation of the HOBI-WAN project lies in the production technology of Solein. This protein powder does not rely on traditional agriculture or animal husbandry; instead, it is synthesized through a microbial fermentation process using carbon sources from the air, electricity, and water as raw materials. If this technology can be successfully validated in a space environment, it will significantly reduce deep space missions' dependence on Earth resupply, while also decreasing the weight and volume of food transportation. For planned lunar bases and Mars missions, a sustainable food source is one of the key factors determining mission feasibility. Through this project, the European Space Agency demonstrates its research capabilities in the field of space life support systems and lays a technological foundation for future international space cooperation.

The HOBI-WAN project is a concrete practice of this strategic direction, reflecting Europe's long-term planning in space science and exploration.

Different media and research institutions hold varying assessments regarding the timeline and application prospects of deep space food technology. Some experts believe that microbial protein technology still needs to overcome challenges such as cost and stability. However, the continued investment from the European Space Agency and relevant policy institutions indicates that this field has become an important component of Europe's space strategy. # 歐洲太空總署啟動深空食物計畫 以微生物蛋白供應月球與火星任務

> 歐洲太空總署（ESA）啟動 HOBI-WAN 專案，測試以微生物、空氣和電力製成的 Solein 蛋白質粉末，作為未來月球與火星任務的可持續食物來源，彰顯歐洲太空探索對長期深空任務的創新投入。

歐洲太空總署（ESA）近日啟動一項名為 HOBI-WAN 的研發專案，專注於為深空任務開發可持續的食物來源。該專案隸屬於歐空局「地球新星探索計劃」（Earth New Stars Exploration Programme），主要目標是測試一種稱為 Solein 的粉末狀蛋白質。根據公開資料，Solein 的生產方式獨特，係利用微生物、空氣與電力合成而成，有望成為未來人類前往月球與火星任務中的重要營養來源。
此舉反映歐洲太空機構正積極應對長期太空任務中食物供應的技術挑戰，並尋求突破傳統太空食品的限制。
歐洲太空總署成立於一九七五年，目前擁有二十二個成員國，同時營運載人與無人太空任務。在歐盟層面，太空計畫亦與多項民用與科學專案緊密結合。歐盟太空計畫整合了伽利略（Galileo）衛星導航系統、哥白尼（Copernicus）地球觀測計畫以及歐洲地理資訊導航系統（EGNOS），旨在為歐洲公民、企業與政府提供世界級的太空服務。
歐洲太空政策研究所（ESPI）作為歐洲太空領域的智庫，由二十三個成員組織支援，致力於在全球範圍內推廣歐洲太空政策，並向決策者與機構提供獨立分析、建議與政策方案，同時提升公眾對戰略太空議題的認知。
HOBI-WAN 專案的核心創新在於 Solein 的生產技術。這種蛋白質粉末不依賴傳統農業或動物飼養，而是透過微生物發酵過程，以空氣中的碳源、電力與水為原料合成。此技術若能在太空環境中成功驗證，將大幅降低深空任務對地球補給的依賴，同時減少食物運輸的重量與體積。對於計劃中的月球基地與火星任務而言，可持續的食物來源是決定任務可行性的關鍵因素之一。歐洲太空總署透過此專案，展示其在太空生命支援系統領域的研發實力，並為未來國際太空合作奠定技術基礎。
歐盟委員會亦持續推動太空政策，強調衛星導航等太空技術對歐洲公民的實際益處。伽利略與哥白尼計畫已廣泛應用於導航、災害監測與環境保護等領域，而深空食物研發則是歐洲太空探索從近地軌道延伸至月球與火星的重要延伸。歐洲太空政策研究所指出，歐洲需在太空科技領域保持競爭力，同時確保太空活動符合永續發展與公共利益。HOBI-WAN 專案正是這一戰略方向的具體實踐，體現歐洲在太空科學與探索方面的長期規劃。
儘管 HOBI-WAN 專案目前處於測試階段，其技術可行性與規模化生產仍需進一步驗證。不同媒體與研究機構對深空食物技術的進展時程與應用前景存在不同評估，部分專家認為微生物蛋白質技術仍需克服成本與穩定性等挑戰。然而，歐洲太空總署與相關政策機構的持續投入，顯示該領域已成為歐洲太空戰略的重要一環。未來隨著月球與火星任務的推進，此類創新食物技術有望成為深空探索的關鍵支撐。
