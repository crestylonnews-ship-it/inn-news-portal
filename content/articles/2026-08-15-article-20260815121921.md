  # Rubio Advocates "Americas First" Diplomacy, Sets Central America as First Foreign Visit Destination
  
  > U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio outlined an "Americas First" foreign policy in a Wall Street Journal op-ed, emphasizing focus on the Western Hemisphere; his first overseas trip to Central America has drawn regional attention.
  
  U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio formally outlined the new administration's foreign policy approach in a column published in The Wall Street Journal on January 30, 2025, advocating "Americas First" as its core strategy. The article noted that after Donald Trump won a decisive victory in the previous November, he was charged with a mission to pay greater attention in diplomacy to the region where the United States is located—the Western Hemisphere. Rubio emphasized that this policy shift was not accidental, but a strategic adjustment based on geopolitical realities and regional security considerations.
  
  According to information published on the official U.S. State Department website, Rubio mentioned at the end of the article that his first overseas trip upon assuming the role of Secretary of State would be to Central America. The choice of this visit route was seen as a concrete implementation of the "Americas First" policy, demonstrating that the U.S. government is refocusing resources and diplomatic attention on Western Hemisphere nations. Analysts believe this move aims to strengthen U.S. diplomatic ties with Central America and the Caribbean region and to address increasingly complex security and immigration issues within the area.
  
  Insight Post noted that Latin America is undergoing significant political changes, ranging from political polarization in Brazil to constitutional reform in Chile, from Colombia's peace process to Argentina's economic crisis, with each country's political development displaying complex and diverse characteristics. These changes not only affect domestic affairs but also have far-reaching impacts on regional stability and international relations. Against this backdrop, the U.S. adjustment of its Latin America policy is viewed as a response to shifting regional dynamics.
  
  Global Voices' Traditional Chinese site also reported that developments across Latin America, North America, and the Caribbean continue to attract international attention, with digital rights and regional governance issues interwoven, making the region an important focal point for global political observation.
  
  Synthesizing information from various sources, Rubio's "Americas First" policy has not yet had a detailed implementation blueprint released to the public, and its specific measures and budget allocations remain to be observed. Interpretations of the policy also differ across media: some analyses suggest it could help strengthen U.S. relations with traditional allies, while others express concern that an overemphasis on the Western Hemisphere may affect U.S. engagement in other international affairs. Currently, the U.S. government has not issued further details on the specific agenda or agreements to be signed during the Central America visit.

# 盧比奧主張「美洲優先」外交 首訪鎖定中美洲

> 美國國務卿盧比奧於《華爾街日報》撰文提出「美洲優先」外交政策，強調聚焦西半球；其首次外訪目的地選定中美洲，引發區域關注。

美國國務卿馬可·盧比奧（Marco Rubio）於2025年1月30日刊載於《華爾街日報》的專欄文章中，正式闡述新政府的外交方針，主張「美洲優先」（Americas First）為核心戰略。文章指出，當唐納·川普（Donald Trump）於前一年11月贏得壓倒性勝選後，被賦予一項使命，即在外交領域更加關注美國所處的地區——西半球。盧比奧強調，這項政策轉向並非偶然，而是基於地緣政治現實與區域安全考量所做出的戰略調整。
根據美國國務院官方網站發布的資訊，盧比奧於文章末尾提及，其就任國務卿後的首次出國訪問將以中美洲為目的地。該訪問路線的選擇被視為「美洲優先」政策的具體實踐，顯示美國政府將資源與外交注意力重新聚焦於西半球國家。分析認為，此舉意在強化美國與中美洲及加勒比海地區的外交聯絡，並回應區域內日益複雜的安全與移民議題。
《洞見國際事務評論網》指出，拉丁美洲正經歷一波重要的政治變遷，從巴西的政治極化到智利的憲政改革，從哥倫比亞的和平程式到阿根廷的經濟危機，各國政治發展呈現複雜多樣的特徵。這些變化不僅影響各國內政，也對區域穩定和國際關係產生深遠影響。在此背景下，美國調整對拉美政策，被視為對區域局勢變化的回應。
Global Voices 繁體中文站點亦報導，拉丁美洲、北美洲與加勒比海地區的多國動態持續受到國際社會關注，數位人權與區域治理議題交織，使該地區成為全球政治觀察的重要焦點。
綜合各方資訊，盧比奧的「美洲優先」政策尚未有詳細的執行藍圖對外公佈，其具體措施與預算配置仍待後續觀察。不同媒體對該政策的解讀亦存在差異：部分分析認為此舉有助於強化美國與傳統盟友的關係；亦有觀點擔憂，過度聚焦西半球可能影響美國在其他國際事務上的投入。目前，美國政府尚未就首訪中美洲的具體議程與簽署協議內容發布進一步說明。
