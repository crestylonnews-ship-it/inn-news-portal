  # EU Unveils New Space Policy to Strengthen Autonomy Amid Space Race
  ...
  
  > The European Commission has proposed new measures for its space program, combining the EU Space Act with a space economy vision, aiming to capture a share of the global $1.6 trillion space economy by 2035 and reduce dependence on the United States.
  ...
  
  The EU's space policy is undergoing a major turning point. According to Wikipedia, the European Union Space Programme officially launched on June 22, 2021, led by the European Space Agency (ESA), with goals covering all 27 EU member states and the United Kingdom. Investment areas include satellite navigation, Earth observation, space situational awareness, and secure communications—key technologies for the bloc. To align with the program's development, ESA also established the EU Space Programme Agency to ensure policy implementation and resource integration. ...
  
  The European Commission has recently proposed further ambitious measures aimed at making Europe's space industry cleaner, safer, and more competitive. The EU official website states that current European space regulations are fragmented, hindering innovation, eroding Europe's market share, and generating additional costs. By establishing a unified EU regulatory framework, the bloc can ensure safety, resilience, and environmental responsibility while helping businesses grow and scale up. ...
  
  This move reflects the EU's emphasis on autonomy in the space domain and echoes a trend highlighted by Deutsche Welle (DW): over the past few decades, Europe has been heavily dependent on the United States for space security, but the Trump administration's "America First" policy and the rapid advancement of commercial spaceflight have prompted Europe to reassess its own space strategy. ...
  
  In terms of funding and policy support, Horizon Europe serves as a key EU research and innovation funding program, running from 2021 to 2027. In March 2024, the European Commission released the "Horizon Europe 2025–2027 Strategic Plan," the program's second strategic iteration, further adjusting resource allocation to strengthen investment in key areas such as space. ...
  
  Notably, the EU has issued the EU Space Act and the EU Space Economy Vision, regarded as landmark measures marking a shift toward the formalization of European space policy. According to an analysis by the Shanghai Institute of Scientific and Technical Information (istis.sh.cn), this approach treats Europe's space economy as a comprehensive ecosystem for the first time, encompassing not only the industrial base but also orbital and extra-orbital space, including emerging areas such as space mining and space resource utilization. The report estimates that the global space economy will reach €1.6 trillion by 2035, and the EU intends to secure a larger share of the rapidly expanding market through policy integration and regulatory harmonization. ...
  
  In summary, the EU is gradually reducing its dependence on U.S. space technology and services through regulatory integration, funding investment, and strategic vision, while actively positioning itself in emerging areas of the space economy. However, how to coordinate the interests of 27 member states and the United Kingdom, and maintain innovative momentum under the competitive pressure of rapidly developing U.S. commercial spaceflight, remains a long-term challenge for EU space policy. ...

# 歐盟推太空新政 強化自主能力應對太空競賽

> 歐盟委員會提出太空計畫新措施，結合《歐盟太空法案》與空間經濟願景，目標2035年掌握全球太空經濟1.6兆歐元市場，降低對美依賴。

歐盟太空政策迎來重大轉折。根據維基百科資料顯示，歐盟太空計畫（European Union Space Programme）於2021年6月22日正式啟動，由歐洲太空總署（ESA）主導，目標涵蓋歐盟27個成員國與英國，投資領域包含衛星導航、地球觀測、空間態勢感知及安全通訊等關鍵技術。為銜接此計畫發展，歐空局亦設立「歐盟太空計畫機構」，確保政策執行與資源整合。
歐盟委員會近期進一步提出雄心勃勃的新措施，旨在讓歐洲太空產業更清潔、更安全且更具競爭力。歐盟官方網站指出，現行歐洲太空法規分散，阻礙創新、削弱歐洲市場份額，並產生額外成本；透過建立歐盟統一的規範框架，可確保安全、韌性與環境責任，同時協助企業成長與擴張規模。
此舉反映歐盟對太空領域自主性的重視，亦呼應德國之聲（DW）報導所指出的趨勢：過去幾十年來，歐洲在太空安全方面高度依賴美國，但川普政府的「美國優先」政策與航天商業化的快速推進，已促使歐洲重新審視自身太空戰略。
在資金與政策配套方面，「展望歐洲」（Horizon Europe）作為歐盟研究與創新領域的重要資助計畫，執行期間為2021年至2027年。歐盟執委會於2024年3月公佈《展望歐洲2025年至2027年戰略計畫》，為該計畫的第二個戰略版本，進一步調整資源配置以強化太空等關鍵領域的投資。
值得注意的是，歐盟已發布《歐盟太空法案》與《歐盟空間經濟願景》，被視為歐洲太空政策正規化轉變的標誌性舉措。根據上海科學技術情報研究所（istis.sh.cn）的分析，此舉首次將歐洲太空經濟視為綜合生態系統，不僅涵蓋工業基礎，亦延伸至軌道與軌道外空間，包含太空採礦與太空資源利用等新興領域。報告預估，至2035年全球太空經濟規模將達1.6兆歐元，歐盟意圖透過政策整合與法規統一，在快速擴張的市場中爭取更大份額。
綜合觀察，歐盟正透過法規整合、資金投入與戰略願景，逐步降低對美國太空技術與服務的依賴，同時積極佈局太空經濟新興領域。然而，如何協調27個成員國與英國的利益，並在美國商業航太快速發展的競爭壓力下保持創新動能，仍是歐盟太空政策面臨的長期挑戰。
