  # Taiwan Universities Hold Higher Education Exhibition in Indonesia; Oceania Education Tour Launches Simultaneously
  
  > The 2026 Indonesia Taiwan Higher Education Exhibition opened on August 1 in Jakarta with 65 Taiwanese universities participating; simultaneously, the OEGlobal Tour launched educational outreach activities across the Middle East, Asia, and Oceania.
  
  The Department of International and Cross-Strait Education of the Ministry of Education announced that the "2026 Indonesia Taiwan Higher Education Exhibition" grandly opened on August 1 in Jakarta, the capital of Indonesia, running from August 1 to 9. A total of 65 Taiwanese universities participated, sending 165 representatives in person to introduce Taiwan's higher education resources and study opportunities to Indonesian students and parents. This event serves as one of the important platforms for educational exchange between Taiwan and Southeast Asian nations, and also reflects the emphasis placed by Taiwanese universities on continuously expanding international inter-university cooperation.
  
  At the same time, the Open Education organization "OEGlobal Tour" also kicked off its first day of touring activities recently, with stops covering the Middle East, Asia, and Oceania. The event aims to visit educational landmarks and attractions in each region, introducing the development status and local characteristics of open education across different cultural and geographical contexts. Activity records on YouTube show that the first day's itinerary focused on presenting open education resources and local flavors in the Middle East and Oceania, highlighting the significance of educational exchange transcending geographical boundaries.
  
  Oceania is the smallest of the world's six continents, with land area accounting for approximately 6% of the global total. The Australian continent alone makes up 86% of this area, and together with New Guinea Island and New Zealand, the three account for 98% of Oceania's total landmass. The remainder consists of scattered island groups across the Pacific Ocean—a geographical feature that also gives "Oceania" its name. According to the definition by the UN Economic and Social Commission for Asia and the Pacific (ESCAP), the Asia-Pacific region encompasses the western Pacific, the Indian Ocean coastline, and the vast sea areas adjacent to both Asia and Oceania, with its boundaries varying depending on context.
  
  Educational institutions and international organizations are actively leveraging the geographic and cultural diversity of this region to promote cross-border educational cooperation. Taiwanese universities are deepening their presence in the Southeast Asian market through the Indonesia higher education exhibition, while the OEGlobal Tour extends its reach to the Middle East and Oceania, demonstrating that international exchange in open education and higher education is developing in a multi-pronged direction. The specific outcomes and scale of participation for future activities remain to be further disclosed by the respective organizing bodies.

# 臺灣大學赴印尼辦高教展 大洋洲教育巡展同步展開

> 2026印尼臺灣高等教育展8月1日於雅加達開幕，65所臺灣大學參與；同期OEGlobal Tour展開中東、亞洲與大洋洲教育巡迴活動。

教育部國際及兩岸教育司指出，「2026印尼臺灣高等教育展」於8月1日在印尼首都雅加達盛大開幕，展期自8月1日至9日。本次展覽共匯集65所臺灣大學，派出165位各校代表親赴現場，向印尼學生與家長介紹臺灣高等教育資源與就學機會。此活動為臺灣與東南亞國家教育交流的重要平臺之一，亦反映臺灣大學持續拓展國際校際合作的重視。
與此同時，開放教育相關組織「OEGlobal Tour」亦於近日展開首日巡迴活動，行程涵蓋中東、亞洲與大洋洲地區。該活動旨在走訪各地教育地標與景點，介紹開放教育（open education）在不同文化與地理環境中的發展現況與在地特色。YouTube平臺上的活動紀錄顯示，首日行程著重於呈現中東與大洋洲地區的開放教育資源與地方風味，凸顯教育交流跨越地理界限的意涵。
大洋洲為全球六大洲中面積最小的一洲，陸地面積約佔全球6%，其中澳洲大陸佔總面積86%，加上新幾內亞島與紐西蘭後，合計佔大洋洲陸地總面積的98%。其餘為太平洋上零星分佈的島嶼群，此地理特徵亦為「大洋洲」名稱的由來。根據聯合國經濟及社會理事會亞洲及太平洋區域委員會（ESCAP）的定義，亞太區域涵蓋西太平洋、印度洋沿岸，以及亞洲與大洋洲相鄰的廣大海域，其邊界依不同脈絡有所差異。
教育機構與國際組織正積極利用此區域的地理與文化多樣性，推動跨國教育合作。臺灣大學透過印尼高教展深耕東南亞市場，而OEGlobal Tour則將視野延伸至中東與大洋洲，顯示開放教育與高等教育的國際交流正朝向多點並進的格局發展。未來相關活動的具體成果與參與規模，仍有待各主辦單位進一步公開說明。
