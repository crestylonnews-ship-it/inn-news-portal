The EU faces dual challenges in both security order and economic competition.
  
  On November 1, 2025, the School of European Languages and Cultures at Beijing Foreign Studies University hosted an academic symposium titled "EU Strategic Autonomy and the Future of the European Security Order Amid Order Transition." More than 20 scholars from over 10 universities and research institutions across Beijing, Shanghai, Shaanxi and other regions participated, focusing on the theoretical logic, historical evolution and practical motivations behind EU strategic autonomy, as well as the future direction of the European security order. The conference was organized by the School of European Languages and Cultures at Beijing Foreign Studies University, and relevant content was published on the university's official website.
  
  Meanwhile, the Institute of International Studies at Fudan University released a 36-page report titled "The EU's Great Power and Regional Policies" in 2024, focusing on the EU's policy interactions with major powers and regions and revealing its strategic adjustments and challenges in the geopolitical and economic landscape. The report pointed out that the EU's policy toward the United States in 2024 centered on "strengthening partnerships and advancing a balance of strategic autonomy," reflecting the EU's continued efforts to seek autonomy within transatlantic relations. The report's abstract was reposted and published by Sohu.com.
  
  On the economic front, HK01 cited former French diplomat Jérémie Gallon, who warned: "Europe has shaped history, but the risk today is that we will become bystanders to history's course in the years to come." Data from Deutsche Bank shows that the European economy has languished in a state of near-stagnation for approximately 15 years, potentially the longest period of economic stagnation since the Industrial Revolution. The report identified policy errors and bureaucracy as key factors hindering development.

All three point to the challenges of strategic autonomy facing the EU amid order transition, but none of the sources reached a consistent narrative on specific policy recommendations or progress in the EU's internal decision-making. # 歐盟戰略自主困境：經濟停滯15年與安全秩序重塑之爭

> 北京外語大學舉辦學術研討會探討歐盟戰略自主，學者指出歐洲經濟停滯15年，政策失誤與官僚主義成發展阻礙，歐盟面臨安全秩序與經濟競爭雙重挑戰。

2025年11月1日，北京外國語大學歐洲語言文化學院舉辦「秩序變遷下的歐盟戰略自主與歐洲安全秩序走向」學術研討會，來自北京、上海、陝西等地10餘所高校與研究機構的20餘名學者參與，聚焦歐盟戰略自主的理論邏輯、歷史演進與現實動因，以及歐洲安全秩序未來走向等議題。該會議由北京外國語大學歐洲語言文化學院主辦，相關內容刊載於該校官網。
與此同時，復旦大學國際問題研究院於2024年發布《歐盟的大國和地區政策》報告，共36頁，聚焦歐盟與主要大國及地區的 policy 互動，揭示其在地緣政治與經濟格局中的戰略調整與挑戰。報告指出，2024年歐盟對美政策以「強化夥伴關係與推進戰略自主平衡」為核心，反映歐盟在跨大西洋關係中尋求自主性的持續努力。該報告摘要由搜狐網轉載發布。
經濟層面，香港01引述法國前外交官熱雷米·加隆（Jérémie Gallon）警告稱：「歐洲塑造過歷史，但如今的風險是，我們日後會淪為歷史程式的旁觀者。」德意志銀行資料顯示，歐洲經濟已在基本停滯的狀態中蹉跎了15年左右，這可能是工業革命以來最長的經濟停滯期。報告將政策失誤和官僚主義列為阻礙發展的關鍵因素。
綜合多方來源觀察，學術界與政策研究機構對歐盟戰略自主的討論呈現多維度特徵：北京外國語大學研討會側重理論與安全秩序層面，復旦研究院報告聚焦歐盟大國政策互動，而香港01引述的經濟資料則揭示結構性困境。三者共同指向歐盟在秩序變遷中面臨的戰略自主挑戰，但各來源並未就具體政策建議或歐盟內部決策進展達成一致敘述，相關影響與後續發展仍有待進一步觀察與驗證。
