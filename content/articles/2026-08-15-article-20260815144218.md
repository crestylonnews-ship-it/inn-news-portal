  > On the 20th, the US Supreme Court ruled 6-3 that the White House's emergency tariff measures on imported goods under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA) were unlawful, and that President Trump's unilateral imposition of tariffs without congressional authorization constituted an illegal act.
  
  On the 20th, the US Supreme Court issued a landmark ruling, overturning the White House's emergency tariff measures on imported goods under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA) by a 6-3 vote. The ruling determined that President Trump's unilateral imposition of tariffs without congressional authorization was unlawful, a decision with far-reaching implications for US trade policy and the global economy.
  
  The core controversy of this ruling centers on the boundary between executive and legislative powers. According to reports, the Trump administration previously invoked the broad economic powers granted to the president under IEEPA during a national emergency to impose tariffs on imported goods from multiple countries. However, the majority opinion of the Supreme Court held that the authority to levy tariffs belongs exclusively to Congress, and the president may not exercise it unilaterally through executive order. This ruling not only affects current tariff policies but may also constrain how future administrations invoke emergency powers for trade control.
  
  From an economic perspective, as the world's largest economy, any shift in US tariff policy directly affects global trade chains. According to data provided by the Taiwan External Trade Development Council (TAITRA), the United States is a major trade partner of Taiwan, with close economic and trade relations between the two. If the tariff measures are overturned, it may create adjustment pressures on the cost of US imported goods, corporate supply chain arrangements, and cross-border trade flows. Meanwhile, US economic history shows that since the colonial era, import and export trade has been a vital pillar of its economic development, and tariff policy has long shaped the pattern of interaction between the US and the global economy.
  
  The ruling has drawn divergent interpretations. Supporters argue that the Supreme Court upheld the constitutional principle of separation of powers, ensuring that trade policy requires congressional deliberation and authorization and preventing excessive expansion of executive authority. Opponents, however, worry that if the president cannot swiftly implement trade measures during emergencies, it may undermine the US's flexibility in responding to international economic crises. # 美最高法院推翻川普緊急關稅 裁定未經國會授權屬違法

> 美國最高法院20日以6比3裁定，白宮依據《國際緊急經濟權力法》對進口商品加徵的緊急關稅措施違法，總統川普未經國會授權單方面徵收關稅屬違法行為。

美國最高法院於20日作出重要裁決，以6比3的票數裁定推翻白宮依據《國際緊急經濟權力法》（IEEPA）對進口商品加徵的緊急關稅措施。該項裁決認定，總統川普未經國會授權單方面徵收關稅的行為屬違法，此舉對美國貿易政策與全球經濟產生深遠影響。
這項裁決的核心爭議在於行政權與立法權的界線。根據報導，川普政府過去援引IEEPA賦予總統在緊急狀態下的廣泛經濟權力，對多國進口商品加徵關稅。然而，最高法院多數意見認為，關稅許可權屬於國會專屬權責，總統不得僅憑行政命令單方面行使。此裁決不僅影響現行關稅政策，也可能對未來政府援引緊急狀態法進行貿易管制的方式產生制約效果。
從經濟面向觀察，美國作為全球最大經濟體，其關稅政策變動直接牽動國際貿易鏈。根據臺灣貿協（TAITRA）提供的資料顯示，美國為臺灣重要貿易夥伴，兩國間經貿關係密切。若關稅措施被推翻，可能對美國進口商品成本、企業供應鏈安排及跨國貿易流向產生調整壓力。同時，美國經濟史顯示，自殖民地時期以來，進出口貿易即為其經濟發展重要支柱，關稅政策長期影響美國與全球經濟互動模式。
此項裁決引發不同解讀。支援方認為，最高法院維護了憲法分權原則，確保貿易政策需經國會討論與授權，避免行政權過度擴張。反對方則擔憂，若總統無法在緊急狀態下迅速採取貿易措施，可能影響美國應對國際經濟危機的彈性。目前相關政策細節與後續影響仍有待觀察，各界關注白宮將如何調整貿易策略，以及國會是否會制定新的關稅授權法律框架。
