  # Africa Advances Green Energy Transition with Hydrogen Projects and Clean Energy Education
  
  > Several African nations are actively developing a hydrogen economy while partnering with the United Nations through private enterprises to promote clean energy education, addressing climate change challenges.
  
  According to Tencent News, statistics show that at least twelve African countries are currently carrying out hydrogen projects, while nineteen nations have established dedicated regulatory frameworks or national strategies for this emerging industry, indicating that the trend toward green hydrogen economies in Africa is accelerating. Meanwhile, Sohu News cited a relevant declaration stating that Africa's primary demand at COP28 is to be treated fairly on the global climate agenda, and to seek financial and technical support, urging the fulfillment of aid commitments. The declaration emphasized that Africa should not bear historical responsibility for global warming, and that its share of global carbon emissions is relatively low, yet Africa's economy and livelihoods are disproportionately affected by climate change.
  
  On the other hand, PRNAsia reported that BLUETTI signed a memorandum of understanding with the United Nations Human Settlements Programme (UN-Habitat), with key initiatives including training electricians and conducting clean energy education activities through BLUETTI's "Lighting African Families" (LAAF) program. Huang Liting (Liting Huang), manager of the LAAF program, stated that establishing cooperation with UN-Habitat is an important milestone for the company in advancing sustainability in Africa. This move demonstrates that private enterprises are actively participating in Africa's clean energy transition, helping local communities improve energy access through technical training and educational outreach.
  
  A synthesis of media reports reveals that Africa is pursuing a dual-track strategy on climate change: on one hand, actively developing green energy industries such as hydrogen, and on the other, promoting grassroots energy education through international cooperation and private sector efforts. Information from different sources indicates that African nations continue to advocate for fair treatment and substantive support in the international climate agenda, while domestic green energy transition actions are gradually unfolding. Experts note that although Africa accounts for a small share of global carbon emissions, it bears the brunt of climate change impacts; therefore, ensuring the delivery of financial and technical assistance will be a critical factor in determining the success of Africa's green transition.

# 非洲推動綠色能源轉型 氫能專案與清潔教育並行

> 非洲多國積極發展氫能經濟，同時透過民間企業與聯合國合作推動清潔能源教育，應對氣候變遷挑戰。

據騰訊新聞報導，統計資料顯示目前至少有十二個非洲國家正在開展氫能專案，十九個國家針對這一新興行業制定了專門的監管框架或國家戰略，顯示非洲國家發展綠氫經濟的趨勢正加速推進。與此同時，搜狐新聞引述相關宣言指出，非洲對 COP28 的主要訴求是在全球氣候變化議程中被公平對待，並尋求相關財政與技術支援，敦促落實援助承諾。該宣言強調，非洲不應對全球暖化承擔歷史責任，且其在全球碳排放中所佔比例較低，但非洲的經濟與生計卻首當其衝地受到氣候變遷的影響。
另一方面，PRNAsia 報導指出，BLUETTI 與聯合國人居署簽署了諒解備忘錄，主要舉措包括培訓電工，以及透過 BLUETTI 的「點亮非洲家庭」(LAAF) 計劃開展清潔能源教育活動。LAAF 計劃經理黃麗婷 (Liting Huang) 表示，與聯合國人居署建立合作是該公司在非洲推進可持續發展的重要里程碑。此舉顯示民間企業正積極參與非洲清潔能源轉型，透過技術培訓與教育推廣，協助當地社群提升能源可及性。
綜合各媒體報導可見，非洲在氣候變遷議題上採取雙軌並行的策略：一方面積極發展氫能等綠色能源產業，另一方面透過國際合作與民間力量推動基層能源教育。不同來源的資訊顯示，非洲國家在國際氣候議程中持續爭取公平對待與實質支援，同時國內的綠色能源轉型行動也逐步展開。專家觀察指出，非洲在全球碳排放中佔比較低，卻承受氣候變遷的主要衝擊，因此如何確保資金與技術援助落實，將是影響非洲綠色轉型成敗的關鍵因素。
後續需關注各國氫能專案的實際進展，以及國際社會對非洲氣候援助承諾的履行情況。
