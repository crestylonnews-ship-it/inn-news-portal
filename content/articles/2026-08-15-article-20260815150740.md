  # On the Eve of the Munich Security Conference, the EU Faces New Challenges in Security Strategy
  
  > The Munich Security Conference is approaching, and the EU is grappling with renewed scrutiny of its security strategy after US Vice President JD Vance's criticism of European immigration and free speech policies from a year ago. The EU continues to advance its Common Foreign and Security Policy while confronting multiple threats including terrorism and the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction.
  
  The Munich Security Conference is about to convene, bringing European security issues back into the international spotlight. According to a BBC report, US Vice President JD Vance delivered a striking speech at last year's Munich Security Conference, condemning Europe's policies on immigration and freedom of expression and asserting that the greatest threat to Europe stems from within. His remarks sparked widespread discussion across European political circles and prompted the EU to re-examine its security strategy direction.
  
  The EU's security strategy framework traces back to the European Security Strategy, adopted in 2003. According to Wikipedia, the document was commissioned by Javier Solana, the then-High Representative for the Common Foreign and Security Policy, and was adopted at the EU summit in Brussels on December 13, 2003. The document clearly stated that the EU's goal is "a secure Europe in a better world," identifying terrorism, the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction, regional conflicts, and failed states as the primary security threats. This strategic document laid the foundation for the EU's subsequent security and defence policies.
  
  At the policy implementation level, the EU guides its defence and military operations through the Common Security and Defence Policy (CSDP). According to Wikipedia, the policy was formerly known as the European Security and Defence Policy (ESDP) and forms an important component of the EU's Common Foreign and Security Policy, inheriting relevant mechanisms from NATO's European Security and Defence Identity. The European External Action Service (EEAS) is responsible for the concrete execution of the EU's foreign and security policy, operating more than 140 delegations and offices worldwide. The EU website further explains that the Council of the EU, composed of member state heads of state and government, serves as the ultimate decision-making body for foreign policy, with most foreign and security policy decisions requiring the agreement of all EU member states.
  
  Analysts note that Vance's criticism at the Munich Security Conference highlighted tensions within the transatlantic relationship. Europe faces not only external security threats but also internal divisions over immigration policy and freedom of expression standards. The EU's High Representative for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy and member states are working to coordinate their positions in response to an increasingly complex international security environment. The Munich Security Conference will serve as a key forum for countries to review and adjust their security strategies, and how Europe balances great power competition with its own values will remain a focal point of future attention.

# 慕尼黑安全會議前夕 歐盟面臨安全戰略新挑戰

> 慕尼黑安全會議即將舉行，美國副總統萬斯一年前批評歐洲移民與言論自由政策，引發歐洲安全戰略反思。歐盟持續推動共同外交與安全政策，面對恐怖主義、大規模殺傷性武器擴散等多重威脅。

慕尼黑安全會議即將召開，歐洲安全議題再度成為國際焦點。根據英國廣播公司（BBC）報導，美國副總統JD·萬斯（JD Vance）一年前在慕尼黑安全會議上發表震撼演說，痛斥歐洲在移民與言論自由上的政策，並聲稱歐洲面臨的最大威脅來自於此。此番言論在歐洲政壇引發廣泛討論，也促使歐盟重新檢視其安全戰略方向。
歐盟的安全戰略框架可追溯至2003年透過的《歐洲安全戰略》。根據維基百科資料，該檔案由當時共同外交與安全政策高階代表哈維爾·索拉納（Javier Solana）授權制定，於2003年12月13日在布魯塞爾歐盟高峰會上透過。檔案明確指出，歐盟的目標是「在更美好的世界中實現安全的歐洲」，並將恐怖主義、大規模殺傷性武器擴散、區域衝突及失敗國家等列為主要安全威脅。這項戰略檔案為歐盟後續的安全與防務政策奠定了基礎。
在政策執行層面，歐盟透過共同安全與防務政策（CSDP）來指導其國防與軍事行動。根據維基百科資料，該政策前稱歐洲安全與國防政策（ESDP），是歐盟共同外交與安全政策的重要組成部分，並繼承了北約歐洲安全與防務個體的相關機制。歐盟對外行動署（EEAS）則負責具體執行歐盟的外交與安全政策，該機構在全球擁有超過140個代表團與辦事處。歐盟官網進一步說明，歐盟理事會作為外交政策的最終決策機構，由各國元首與政府首腦組成，大多數外交與安全政策決策需獲得所有歐盟成員國同意。
分析指出，萬斯在慕尼黑安全會議上的批評，凸顯了跨大西洋關係中的張力。歐洲方面面臨的挑戰不僅來自外部安全威脅，也包括內部在移民政策與言論自由標準上的分歧。歐盟外交與安全政策的高階代表與各成員國正致力於協調立場，以應對日益複雜的國際安全環境。慕尼黑安全會議將成為各國檢視並調整安全戰略的重要場合，歐洲如何在大國競爭與自身價值觀之間取得平衡，將是未來關注的焦點。
