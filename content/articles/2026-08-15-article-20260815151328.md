  # Global Trade Resilience Evident as Americas Economic Outlook Diverges
  
  > The World Bank released its Latin America economic outlook, while DHL and KPMG noted that global trade remains resilient amid shifting U.S. policy, and Mexico's energy transition faces challenges due to insufficient government support.
  
  The World Bank released its economic outlook report for Latin America and the Caribbean in April 2026, emphasizing that opportunities driven by competition are crucial for promoting regional growth, and noting that only by maintaining sufficient growth rates can the goals of poverty reduction and quality job creation be achieved. The report also provides relevant data and statistics for all parties to reference regarding regional development opportunities and directions for governance improvement.
  
  The DHL Trade Atlas 2025 report, published by international logistics provider DHL, analyzed from a global trade perspective, pointing out that despite the U.S. trade policy becoming more stringent, most countries still view trade as an important economic opportunity, and U.S. trade barriers may instead promote trade ties among other countries, leading to an optimistic outlook for global trade. KPMG's 2026 Trade: A Hard Battle Requiring Great Perseverance and Wisdom report also noted that even affected by the tariff turmoil that began in April 2025, global trade flows overall have maintained significant resilience, with trade policies primarily centered on the protection and development of key industries and continuing to evolve.
  
  At the regional level, the Hong Kong Trade Development Council (HKTDC) cited the views of exhibitors at the Solar + Storage Mexico exhibition, indicating that Mexico's energy industry lacks government support and may fall behind in the race toward sustainable energy transition. This perspective contrasts with the World Bank report's emphasis on growth and poverty reduction goals, highlighting differences among countries in the Americas regarding energy transition and economic development issues.
  
  Synthesizing the various sources, the World Bank report focuses on overall economic growth and poverty reduction goals in Latin America, DHL and KPMG analyze the impact of U.S. policy changes from a global trade perspective, and HKTDC's report focuses on Mexico's energy transition challenges as a single country. The collective information demonstrates that global trade continues to show resilience amid policy uncertainty, but there remain significant gaps in industrial development and policy support across different countries within the region, making subsequent developments worth continued observation.

# 全球貿易韌性顯現 美洲經濟展望分化

> 世界銀行發布拉丁美洲經濟展望，DHL與KPMG指出全球貿易於美國政策轉變下仍具韌性，墨西哥能源轉型則面臨政府支援不足的挑戰。

世界銀行（World Bank）於2026年4月發布拉丁美洲與加勒比海地區經濟展望報告，強調競爭帶來的機會對於推動區域成長至關重要，並指出唯有維持足夠的成長速度，方能實現減貧與創造優質就業的目標。該報告同時提供相關資料、統計資料，供各界參考區域發展機遇與治理改善方向。
國際物流業者DHL發布的《DHL Trade Atlas 2025》報告則從全球貿易角度分析，指出儘管美國貿易政策轉趨嚴謹，但多數國家仍將貿易視為重要經濟機遇，美國的貿易壁壘反而可能促進其他國家之間的貿易聯絡，因此對全球貿易未來持樂觀態度。KPMG於《2026年貿易：一場極需毅力與智慧的硬仗》報告中亦提到，即便受到2025年4月開始的關稅風波影響，全球貿易流通情形整體仍保有顯著韌性，貿易政策主要圍繞關鍵產業的保護與發展，並不斷演化中。
在區域層面，香港貿易發展局（HKTDC）引述墨西哥太陽能及儲能展覽會（Solar + Storage Mexico）參展商觀點指出，墨西哥能源產業缺乏政府支援，在可持續發展能源轉型的賽道上可能有落後之虞。此說法與世界銀行報告中強調成長與減貧目標的語境形成對比，顯示美洲不同國家在能源轉型與經濟發展議題上存在差異。
綜合各來源，世界銀行報告聚焦拉丁美洲整體經濟成長與減貧目標，DHL與KPMG則從全球貿易視角分析美國政策變化的影響，而HKTDC的報導則聚焦墨西哥單一國家的能源轉型困境。各方資料共同顯示，全球貿易在政策不確定性中仍展現韌性，但區域內不同國家的產業發展與政策支援程度仍存在明顯落差，後續發展值得持續觀察。
