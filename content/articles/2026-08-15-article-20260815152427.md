  # Africa's Humanitarian Security Challenges: Multinational Cooperation to Address Them
  
  > The 2025 African Peace and Security Annual Conference focused on power restructuring and autonomous empowerment, with China and international organizations strengthening cooperation to address humanitarian challenges, while the EU calls on social media platforms to prevent disinformation from inciting migration crises.
  
  The 2025 Ninth African Peace and Security Annual Conference was recently held. The conference review noted that "power restructuring" and "autonomous empowerment" have become key terms in Africa's current security landscape. Expert participants analyzed that the African continent is undergoing profound adjustments in its geopolitical landscape, with traditional major powers' influence being reshuffled, while African countries and regional organizations continue to enhance their autonomy in security affairs. This conference focused on how to address increasingly complex security threats and humanitarian crises through multi-party collaboration.
  
  China's engagement in African humanitarian and security affairs is increasingly significant. The International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) stated that as Beijing has become one of the world's important diplomatic hubs, the organization is actively leveraging its dual advantages to promote related dialogues. Discussions were held under the framework of the "Forum on China-Africa Cooperation Beijing Action Plan (2025–2027)," which highlights China-Africa security cooperation, covering multiple dimensions including military training, cybersecurity, and joint security. The ICRC emphasized that in the face of Africa's complex humanitarian and security challenges, an all-encompassing approach is needed, integrating various tools such as diplomacy, development, and humanitarian assistance.
  
  Meanwhile, the EU is strengthening regulation of social media platforms. The EU has required Meta and TikTok to cooperate with fact-checking mechanisms, primarily to prevent online disinformation and misleading videos from inciting migrants to undertake dangerous illegal crossings, thereby avoiding a recurrence of humanitarian tragedies like those seen in Ceuta, where large-scale influxes have caused deaths. The influx of illegal migrants into Ceuta and Melilla, Spanish enclaves in North Africa, could undermine the credibility of Europe's open-border policies. EU officials believe that social media platforms play a key role in disseminating migration-related information and must take responsibility for preventing the spread of misleading content.
  
  Regarding conflict risks, analysis indicates that in 2025, conflict and security risks across the African continent are presenting themselves in a multi-dimensional and intertwined manner. Potential tensions exist in North Africa, West Africa, East Africa, Central Africa, and Southern Africa, affecting not only stability and development on the African continent but also having a direct impact on overseas Chinese residing in Africa. Relevant insurance and security service providers have advised that overseas Chinese should fully understand the risks in each region and make preventive preparations in advance to cope with the complex and ever-changing security environment.
  
  Synthesizing observations from various sources, the evolution of Africa's humanitarian and security landscape involves multiple factors, including geopolitical maneuvering, the influence of social media, pressure from migration flows, and the development of local security capabilities. How to coordinate cooperation between the international community and African local forces in the future, and to establish more effective crisis prevention and response mechanisms, will be an important issue facing all countries.

# 非洲人道安全挑戰 多國協力應對

> 2025年非洲和平與安全年度會議聚焦勢力重構與自主賦能，中國與國際組織加強合作應對人道挑戰，歐盟要求社群平臺防範假訊息煽動移民危機。

2025年第九屆非洲和平與安全年度會議近日舉行，會議評述指出，「勢力重構」與「自主賦能」成為當前非洲安全域性勢的關鍵詞彙。與會專家分析，非洲大陸正經歷地緣政治版圖的深刻調整，傳統大國影響力重新洗牌，同時非洲本土國家與區域組織在安全事務上的自主性持續提升。此次會議聚焦於如何透過多方協作，應對日益複雜的安全威脅與人道危機。
中國對非洲人道與安全事務的參與度日益提高。紅十字國際委員會（ICRC）表示，隨著北京成為全球重要外交樞紐之一，該組織正積極發揮雙重優勢，推動相關對話。討論在《中非合作論壇北京行動計劃（2025–2027）》框架下展開，該計劃重點突出中非安全合作，涵蓋軍事培訓、網路安全及聯合安全等多個層面。ICRC強調，面對非洲複雜的人道與安全挑戰，需要採取全方位的工作方式，整合外交、發展與人道援助等多種工具。
與此同時，歐盟正針對社群平臺加強監管。歐盟要求Meta與TikTok配合事實查核機制，主要目的是防止網路假訊息與誤導影片煽動移民冒險偷渡，避免再度發生類似休達（Ceuta）那類大量湧入、甚至造成死亡的人道悲劇。非法移民湧入西班牙在北非的飛地休達與梅利利亞，可能衝擊歐洲開放邊界政策的可信度。歐盟官員認為，社群平臺在傳播移民相關資訊方面扮演關鍵角色，必須承擔起防止誤導性內容擴散的責任。
在衝突風險方面，分析指出2025年非洲大陸的衝突與安全風險正以多維交織的態勢呈現。北非、西非、東非、中非與南部非洲多地均存在潛在緊張局勢，不僅關係非洲本土的穩定與發展，也對身在非洲的海外華人產生直接影響。相關保險與安全服務業者建議，海外華人應充分了解各地風險，提前做好防範準備，以應對複雜多變的安全環境。
綜合各方觀察，非洲人道與安全域性勢的演變涉及多重因素，包括地緣政治博弈、社群媒體影響、移民潮壓力以及本土安全能力建設等。未來如何協調國際社會與非洲本土力量的合作，建立更有效的危機預防與應對機制，將是各國面臨的重要課題。
