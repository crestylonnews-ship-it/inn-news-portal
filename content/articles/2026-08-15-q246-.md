  # Evergreen Line Q2 Profit Surges 46% Year-on-Year, Asia-Pacific Economic Resilience Evident
  
  > Evergreen Marine's Q2 after-tax net profit reached NT$16.034 billion, up 46.33% year-on-year, benefiting from resilient global trade demand; both the IMF and Oxford Economics noted that while the Asian economy faces geopolitical risks and energy shocks, it has overall demonstrated resilience and is expected to continue leading global growth.
  
  Evergreen Marine (2603) released its Q2 2026 financial results on August 13, reporting consolidated revenue of NT$105.161 billion for the quarter, a 21.60% increase year-on-year, with after-tax net profit of NT$16.034 billion, up 46.33% year-on-year. Earnings per share (EPS) stood at NT$7.41, and the operating profit margin reached 22.80%. For the first half of 2026, consolidated revenue totaled NT$191.672 billion, down 2.43% year-on-year, while after-tax net profit came in at NT$24.337 billion, a decline of 36.48% year-on-year, with EPS of NT$11.24.
  
  Evergreen stated that benefiting from resilient global trade demand and an early peak shipping season, cargo demand and freight rates on major routes from Asia to North America and Europe all saw increases, which in turn drove the Q2 profit performance.
  
  International institutions maintain an overall positive outlook on Asia-Pacific economic prospects. The IMF's April 2026 "Asia-Pacific Economic Outlook" noted that after last year's trade tensions, the Asian economy showed resilience in 2026 and is expected to continue leading global growth; however, energy shocks from the Middle East conflict are testing this resilience, with rising oil and gas prices fueling inflation and widening trade deficits, particularly pressuring economies heavily dependent on fuel imports.
  
  Oxford Economics' concurrent 2026 Asia-Pacific Economic Key Themes report emphasized that while current monetary and fiscal policies lean overall accommodative, individual economies differ in their capacity to absorb future policy adjustments, making the degree of policy easing and its transmission to the real economy the central question affecting regional economic prospects.
  
  On regional trade integration, a Thomson Reuters survey found that Asia-Pacific trade practitioners demonstrate resilience, adaptability, and forward-looking perspectives, with supply chain strategy adjustments across 17 industries including pharmaceuticals, life sciences, consumer goods, and e-commerce gradually strengthening Asia's leadership in global trade. Additionally, HKBEA market information indicates that multiple Asian economies continue reaching trade agreements with the United States, further deepening regional trade networks. Although Taiwan has not joined RCEP, as an important Asia-Pacific economy it can still deeply participate in regional economic integration through bilateral cooperation and industrial chain integration.
  
  Overall, Evergreen Marine's strong financial results align with international institutions' recognition of Asian economic resilience, demonstrating that the Asia-Pacific trade and shipping industries retain development momentum despite multiple challenges, though geopolitics and energy price volatility remain the primary focus for the second half of the year.

# 長榮Q2獲利年增46% 亞太經濟韌性顯現

> 長榮海運第二季稅後淨利達160.34億元，年增46.33%，受惠全球貿易需求韌性；IMF與牛津經濟研究院均指出，亞洲經濟雖面臨地緣風險與能源衝擊，整體仍展現韌性並有望繼續領跑全球。

長榮海運（2603）於2026年8月13日公佈第二季財報，單季合併營收達1,051.61億元，年增21.60%；稅後淨利160.34億元，年增46.33%，每股盈餘（EPS）7.41元，營業毛利率達22.80%。累計上半年合併營收1,916.72億元，年減2.43%，稅後淨利243.37億元，年減36.48%，EPS為11.24元。
長榮表示，受惠於全球貿易需求韌性及提前發酵的出貨旺季，亞洲至北美及歐洲等主航線的貨載需求及運費水準均見提升，進而帶動第二季獲利表現。
國際機構對亞太經濟前景看法整體偏正面。國際貨幣基金組織（IMF）於2026年4月發布的《亞太地區經濟展望》指出，亞洲經濟在經歷去年貿易緊張局勢後，2026年開局展現韌性，仍有望領跑全球經濟；然而，中東戰事引發的能源衝擊正在考驗這種韌性，石油與天然氣價格走高推升通膨、擴大貿易缺口，對高度依賴燃料進口的經濟體尤其構成壓力。
牛津經濟研究院同期發布的2026年亞太經濟關鍵主題報告則強調，當前貨幣與財政政策整體偏向寬鬆，但各經濟體對未來政策調整的風險承受能力存在差異，政策寬鬆力度及其向實體經濟的傳導效果，將是影響區域經濟前景的核心問題。
在區域貿易整合方面，Thomson Reuters調查顯示，亞洲貿易從業人員具備韌性、適應能力與前瞻性眼光，涵蓋製藥、生命科學、消費品及電子商務等17個產業的供應鏈策略調整，正逐步強化亞洲在全球貿易中的領導地位。此外，香港商業銀行（HKBEA）市場資訊指出，亞洲多個經濟體持續與美國達成貿易協議，進一步推動區域貿易網路深化。臺灣雖未參與RCEP，但作為亞太重要經濟體，仍可透過雙邊合作與產業鏈整合，深度參與區域經濟整合程式。
整體而言，長榮海運的亮眼財報與國際機構對亞洲經濟韌性的肯定相互呼應，顯示亞太貿易與航運產業在多重挑戰下仍具發展動能，惟地緣政治與能源價格波動仍是下半年主要觀察重點。
