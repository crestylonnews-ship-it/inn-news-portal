  # UN and UNESCO Focus on African Peace and Development, North Africa Regional Challenges in Spotlight
  
  > UN Secretary-General Guterres emphasized elevating Africa's position in global peace and security affairs, while UNESCO has listed Africa as a priority area. North Africa faces dual development and security challenges due to its geographic and demographic characteristics.
  
  UN Secretary-General António Guterres recently stated at a press conference that strengthening Africa's position and voice in global peace and security affairs is crucial. He praised initiatives such as the African Union's Agenda 2063 and the African Continental Free Trade Area, noting that Africa is working collectively to build a modernized, diversified, and innovative economy that benefits all African people. This remark reflects the UN's emphasis on the overall development of the African continent, particularly focusing on peace, security, and economic transformation.
  
  UNESCO's Executive Board documents list "Africa" as an overarching priority, covering North African countries including Egypt, Libya, Algeria, Tunisia, and Morocco, as well as a broad range of other regions such as Benin, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Burundi, Cabo Verde, Cameroon, Chad, Comoros, Côte d'Ivoire, Djibouti, Ethiopia, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Ghana, Guinea, and Kenya. The documents note that the African continent faces multiple challenges including climate change, resource management, and regional conflicts, and that UNESCO supports countries in enhancing governance capacity and social resilience through education, science, and cultural programs.
  
  The North African region is dominated by the Sahara Desert's arid climate, with populations concentrated along the lower Nile River and Mediterranean coastal plains. The majority are Arab, sharing the Arab world with West Asia. These geographic and demographic characteristics make food security and cross-border migration issues particularly prominent in the region.
  
  Different media outlets offer nuanced interpretations of Africa's peace and security programs. UN News focuses on political participation, emphasizing Africa's advocacy role in reforming global financial structures. UNESCO documents emphasize development aspects, prioritizing education and cultural infrastructure. Geographic media such as KKNEWS provide North African regional context, pointing out how terrain and population distribution affect regional stability. North African countries like Libya and Sudan continue to face internal conflicts, while parts of West and East Africa need to address extremist threats.
  
  Experts observe that the advancement of the African Union's Agenda 2063 and the African Continental Free Trade Area provides a framework for economic diversification, but peace and security affairs still require sustained international support to ensure effective resource allocation and deeper regional cooperation.

# 聯合國與UNESCO聚焦非洲和平發展，北非區域挑戰受矚目

> 聯合國秘書長古特雷斯強調提升非洲在全球和平安全事務中的地位，UNESCO將非洲列為優先事項，北非地區因地理與人口特徵面臨發展與安全雙重挑戰。

聯合國秘書長古特雷斯近日在新聞發布會中表示，加強非洲在全球和平與安全事務中的地位與聲音至關重要，並讚揚非洲聯盟《2063年議程》與非洲大陸自由貿易區等倡議，顯示非洲正共同努力建設現代化、多樣化且具創新力的經濟，以造福所有非洲人民。此言論呼應聯合國對非洲大陸整體發展的重視，尤其聚焦於和平安全與經濟轉型議題。
UNESCO執行情局檔案將「非洲」列為總體優先事項，涵蓋埃及、利比亞、阿爾及利亞、突尼西亞、摩洛哥等北非國家，以及貝南、波札那、布吉納法索、蒲隆地、維德角、喀麥隆、查德、葛摩、象牙海岸、吉布提、衣索比亞、赤道幾內亞、加彭、迦納、幾內亞、肯亞等廣泛區域。檔案指出，非洲大陸正面臨氣候變化、資源管理與區域衝突等多重挑戰，UNESCO透過教育、科學與文化專案支援各國提升治理能力與社會韌性。
北非地區因撒哈拉沙漠主導乾旱氣候，人口集中於尼羅河下游與地中海沿岸平原，多為阿拉伯人，與西亞同屬阿拉伯世界，此地理與人口特徵使該區在糧食安全與跨境移民議題上尤為突出。
不同媒體對非洲和平安全程式的解讀存在細微差異。聯合國新聞側重政治參與，強調非洲在全球金融架構改革中的倡議角色；UNESCO檔案則著重發展層面，將優先事項置於教育與文化基礎建設；地理媒體如KKNEWS則提供北非區域背景，指出其地貌與人口分佈如何影響區域穩定。北非國家如利比亞與蘇丹仍面臨內部衝突，而西非與東非部分地區則需應對極端主義威脅。
專家觀察認為，非洲聯盟《2063年議程》與非洲大陸自由貿易區的推進，為經濟多元化提供框架，但和平安全事務仍需國際社會持續支援，以確保資源有效分配與區域合作深化。
