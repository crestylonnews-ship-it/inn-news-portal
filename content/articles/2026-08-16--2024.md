  # European Universities Association Calls for Updated Social Contract to Address 2024 Key Year Challenges
  
  > The European Universities Association (EUA) released a policy opinion and forward-looking report calling on the EU and universities to update their social contract, emphasizing transnational cooperation and the role of industry to address the multiple challenges Europe faces in 2024.
  
  The European Association of Universities (EUA) released its latest policy opinion in January 2024, titled "Universities and the Future of Europe," clearly calling on the EU and its university system to "update the social contract." Released on the eve of the European Parliament elections, this document aims to highlight the key role of higher education institutions in industrial development and social transformation, and to explore the future direction of transnational university cooperation.
  
  According to the EUA's policy opinion, 2024 is a pivotal year for the future of Europe and its university system. The association points out that Europe is facing multiple challenges, including geopolitical shifts, economic transition pressures, and social structural adjustments. As core institutions for knowledge production and talent cultivation, universities must play a more active role in these transformations. The policy opinion is accompanied by a forward-looking report that uses scenario analysis through "hypothesis" frameworks to consider various possible future development paths for Europe, providing a reference for policymakers and the academic community.
  
  Information on the EU from the Taiwan Ministry of Foreign Affairs global information gateway shows that since French Foreign Minister Robert Schuman's declaration in 1950 proposing the establishment of a coal and steel community between France and Germany, the EU has, over half a century of effort, gradually achieved階段性 goals in economic integration, including a free trade area, a customs union, and a single market. Education and talent cultivation, as an important dimension of EU integration, have in recent years continued to promote cross-border academic exchange and research cooperation programs, such as the Erasmus Programme, becoming an important cornerstone of European higher education integration. The EUA's call to update the social contract can be seen as a further deepening and expansion of the EU's existing education integration policies.
  
  The European education system itself is highly diverse. According to relevant educational research materials, while education systems across European countries each have their own characteristics, they generally emphasize the integration of theory and practice. Germany, for example, is known for its vocational education and scientific research, while also making significant progress in life education and sex education; French education emphasizes comprehensive quality development, valuing students' all-around growth beyond the transmission of basic knowledge. This pluralistic educational landscape means that European universities must balance national institutional differences and common goals when addressing transnational cooperation issues.
  
  The EUA's policy opinion and forward-looking report reflect deep thinking within the European higher education community about future development directions. The association emphasizes that universities should not only be institutions of knowledge production but also play a leading role in social transformation and industrial innovation. Raising this call on the eve of the European Parliament elections also demonstrates that the academic community hopes to influence the direction of the EU's future education and research policy through policy advocacy.

# 歐洲大學協會籲更新社會契約 應對2024關鍵年挑戰

> 歐洲大學協會（EUA）釋出政策意見與前瞻報告，呼籲歐盟與大學更新社會契約，強調跨國合作與產業角色，以應對2024年歐洲面臨的多重挑戰。

歐洲大學協會（European Association of Universities，EUA）於2024年1月發布最新政策意見，標題為「大學與歐洲的未來」，明確呼籲歐盟及其大學體系應「更新社會契約」。該協會在歐洲議會選舉前夕發布此份檔案，旨在強調高等教育機構在產業發展與社會轉型中的關鍵角色，並探討跨國大學合作的未來方向。
根據EUA發布的政策意見，2024年對歐洲及其大學體系的未來而言是關鍵的一年。協會指出，歐洲正面臨地緣政治變動、經濟轉型壓力以及社會結構調整等多重挑戰，大學作為知識生產與人才培育的核心機構，必須在這些變革中發揮更積極的作用。政策意見同時附上前瞻報告，透過「假設」情境分析，思考歐洲可能面臨的多種未來發展路徑，為決策者與學術界提供參考依據。
中華民國外交部全球資訊閘道器於歐盟的介紹資料顯示，歐盟自1950年法國外長舒曼發表宣告、建議法德兩國建立煤鋼共同體以來，歷經半世紀努力，在經濟整合方面已逐步完成自由貿易區、關稅同盟及單一市場等階段性目標。教育與人才培育作為歐盟整合的重要面向，近年來亦持續推動跨境學術交流與研究合作計畫，如「伊拉斯謨計畫」（Erasmus Programme）等，成為歐洲高等教育一體化的重要基石。EUA此次呼籲更新社會契約，可視為對歐盟既有教育整合政策的進一步深化與擴充。
歐洲教育體系本身具有高度多樣性。根據相關教育研究資料，歐洲各國教育體系雖各有特色，但整體而言注重理論與實踐的結合。以德國為例，其教育體系以職業教育與科研為主要特點，同時在生命教育與性教育方面亦有顯著發展；法國教育則強調綜合素質培養，除基礎知識傳授外，亦重視學生全面發展。這種多元並存的教育格局，使得歐洲大學在面對跨國合作議題時，需兼顧各國制度差異與共同目標。
EUA此次發布的政策意見與前瞻報告，反映了歐洲高等教育界對未來發展方向的深度思考。協會強調，大學不僅是知識生產的機構，更應在社會轉型與產業創新中扮演引領角色。在歐洲議會選舉前夕提出此項呼籲，亦顯示學術界希望透過政策倡議，影響歐盟未來教育與研究政策的制定方向。
