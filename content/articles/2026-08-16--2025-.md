  # EU Outlines Vision for European Education Area, 2025 Goals Focus on Sustainable Transition
  
  > The European Commission has proposed establishing a "European Education Area" by 2025, closely linking education with environmental sustainability and green transition; the European University Association calls for a new social contract to strengthen transnational university cooperation.
  
  The Education Section of the Representation of the European Union to Belgium and the European Union pointed out that in mid-November, the European Commission put forward the policy goal of establishing a "European Education Area" by 2025 to shape the future vision of education, aiming to promote social integration and unity in Europe. This move indicates that the EU will place education and cultural affairs on its priority agenda in the future.
  
  According to NAER data, the EU has designated 2024 to 2029 as the core target period for its agenda, and the construction of the European Education Area is regarded as an important task during this phase. The EU hopes to strengthen educational cooperation mechanisms among member states and narrow the gap in education quality and opportunities across member countries through the integration of education policies.
  
  Sohu reported further that the EU's education reform simultaneously focuses on the relationship between education and environmental sustainability, closely linking it with the "Climate Education Alliance" and the "New European Bauhaus Initiative." The report emphasized that education and training play a key role in supporting the development of the knowledge, skills, attitudes, and values needed for green transition; the New European Bauhaus Initiative and the Climate Education Alliance are also seen as important policy tools for driving the EU's sustainable transition.
  
  The European University Association (EUA) released a policy opinion before the 2024 European elections, calling for "a new social contract for Europe and its universities," accompanied by a forward-looking report exploring possible future "assumptions" for Europe. The EUA stressed the critical role of industry in Europe's future development and examined the future direction of transnational university cooperation. The report pointed out that 2024 is a pivotal year for the future of Europe and its universities, and universities need to respond to multiple challenges posed by the EU in green transition, digital transformation, and social integration within the framework of a new social contract.
  
  A synthesis of various sources reveals that EU education policy is advancing along three directions: transnational integration, sustainable development, and the reconstruction of a social contract. The establishment of the European Education Area is closely linked to the 2024–2029 agenda, and the connection between education and green transition has also become an important highlight of EU policy. The EUA's policy opinion, from an academic perspective, proposes that universities should play a more active role in industry cooperation and transnational alliances. However, the balance between member states' education sovereignty and EU integration remains a topic worth observing in subsequent policy implementation.

# 歐盟擘畫歐洲教育區願景 2025 年目標聚焦永續轉型

> 歐盟執委會提出於 2025 年前建立「歐洲教育區」政策目標，並將教育與環境永續、綠色轉型緊密連結；歐洲大學協會呼籲制定新社會契約，強化跨國大學合作。

駐歐盟兼駐比利時代表處教育組指出，歐盟執委會於 11 月中旬為擘劃未來教育願景，提出將於 2025 年之前建立「歐洲教育區（European Education Area）」的政策目標，旨在促進歐洲社會融合及一體化，此舉顯示歐盟未來將把教育及文化事務列為優先議程之一。
根據 NAER 資料顯示，歐盟將 2024 至 2029 年訂為歐盟議程核心目標期，歐洲教育區的建構被視為此階段的重要任務。歐盟希望透過教育政策統合，強化成員國之間的教育合作機制，並縮小各會員國在教育品質與機會上的落差。
搜狐報導進一步指出，歐盟教育變革同時關注教育與環境永續性，並將其與「氣候教育聯盟」及「新歐洲包浩斯倡議（New European Bauhaus Initiative）」緊密連結。報導強調，教育與培訓在支援發展綠色轉型所需之知識、技能、態度與價值觀方面，扮演關鍵角色；新歐洲包浩斯倡議與氣候教育聯盟亦被視為推動歐盟永續轉型的重要政策工具。
歐洲大學協會（EUA）則在 2024 年歐洲選舉前釋出政策意見，呼籲「為歐洲及其大學制定新的社會契約」，並附上前瞻報告，思考歐洲可能的未來「假設」。EUA 強調產業在歐洲未來發展中的關鍵作用，並探討跨國大學合作的未來方向。該報告指出，2024 年對於歐洲及其大學的未來而言是關鍵一年，大學需在新的社會契約框架下，回應歐盟在綠色轉型、數位轉型與社會融合等多重挑戰。
綜合各來源觀察，歐盟教育政策正朝向跨國整合、永續發展與社會契約重建三個方向推進。歐洲教育區的建立目標與 2024 至 2029 年議程緊密相連，而教育與綠色轉型的連結亦成為歐盟政策的重要亮點。歐洲大學協會的政策意見則從學術界角度，提出大學應在產業合作與跨國聯盟上扮演更積極的角色。然而，各會員國在教育主權與歐盟統合之間的平衡，仍是後續政策推動上值得觀察的課題。
