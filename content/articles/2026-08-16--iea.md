  # Middle East Conflict Disrupts Global Energy Supply Chains; IEA Urges Accelerated Energy Transition
  
  > The International Energy Agency released a report stating that the Middle East conflict has triggered an energy crisis, prompting countries to reassess their energy investment strategies; the United Nations has called for accelerating the shift to renewable energy to reduce dependence on fossil fuels.
  
  According to Reuters, the International Gas Union (IGU) released a report indicating that global liquefied natural gas (LNG) trade volume reached a record high in 2025, with strong growth in U.S. exports and increased European imports partially offsetting weakened demand in Asia. However, the turmoil in the Middle East has cast a shadow over the global energy outlook, and despite record trade volumes, future trends remain highly uncertain.
  
  A United Nations press release pointed out that the Middle East crisis has exposed a core vulnerability of the global economy: dependence on fossil fuels that flow through conflict-affected regions. The UN has used this to reinforce its call for countries to accelerate the transition to cheaper, more resilient renewable energy sources to reduce the impact of geopolitical risks on energy supply. The report emphasized that the current situation highlights that energy transition is not merely an environmental issue, but a key factor for economic security and stability.
  
  The IEA's 2026 World Energy Investment Report further analyzed that the current energy crisis, triggered by the de facto closure of the Strait of Hormuz, is driving governments and enterprises worldwide to reassess their energy investment strategies. The report predicts that this crisis will have long-term effects on the focus of global energy investment, prompting countries to seek diversification of energy and transportation routes and reduce dependence on single shipping corridors.
  
  ESG Vision reported that the IEA's latest report reviewed global response strategies, ranging from remote work in Indonesia and price caps in Europe to QR code fuel rationing in Asia. This crisis, described as the "largest-scale energy disruption in history," is forcing governments to adopt extraordinary measures to address energy supply chain disruptions. The report shows that countries' response measures reflect the fact that energy security has become a policy priority, and traditional market mechanisms face challenges in extreme scenarios.
  
  A special report issued by the Legislative Yuan Research Service Department focused on Taiwan's energy security, analyzing industrial development layouts under energy transition policies and the impact of the Middle East conflict and global energy supply chain restructuring on Taiwan's energy security, along with response strategies. The report pointed out that as a region with an extremely high dependence on energy imports, Taiwan's natural gas supply stability is directly impacted by Middle East turmoil. It is imperative to strengthen diversification of energy sources and reserve mechanisms, and to accelerate the development of renewable energy to enhance energy self-sufficiency.
  
  A synthesis of analysis from various sources indicates that the Middle East conflict not only affects short-term energy supply but also highlights the urgency of global energy transition. Both the IEA and the United Nations emphasize that accelerating the development of renewable energy and reducing dependence on single energy sources are key pathways to enhancing energy resilience. Future global energy investment will trend more toward diversification and greening to address geopolitical risks. Governments and enterprises worldwide need to reassess their energy strategies, ensuring supply stability while promoting energy structure transformation to cope with similar crises that may arise in the future.

# 中東衝突衝擊全球能源供應鏈 IEA籲加速能源轉型

> 國際能源署發布報告指出，中東衝突引發能源危機，促使各國重新審視能源投資戰略；聯合國呼籲加速轉向可再生能源，以降低對化石燃料依賴。

根據路透社報導，國際天然氣聯盟（IGU）發布報告指出，2025年全球液化天然氣（LNG）貿易量創下歷史新高，美國出口強勁增長，歐洲進口上升，部分抵消了亞洲需求減弱的影響。然而，中東局勢動盪為全球能源前景蒙上陰影，貿易量雖創新高，但未來走勢仍充滿不確定性。
聯合國新聞稿指出，中東危機暴露了全球經濟的核心弱點：對經過衝突地區流動的化石燃料的依賴。聯合國藉此強化其呼籲，要求各國加速轉向更便宜、更具韌性的可再生能源，以降低地緣政治風險對能源供應的衝擊。報告強調，當前局勢凸顯了能源轉型不僅是環境議題，更是經濟安全與穩定的關鍵。
國際能源署（IEA）發布的2026年世界能源投資報告進一步分析，由霍爾木茲海峽事實上關閉引發的當前能源危機，正推動各國政府和企業重新審視能源投資戰略。報告預計，這場危機將對未來全球能源投資重點產生長期影響，促使各國尋求能源和運輸路線多元化，減少對單一航道的依賴。
ESG遠見報導指出，IEA最新報告盤點全球因應策略，從印尼的遠距辦公、歐洲的價格封頂，到亞洲的QR Code燃料配給，這場被形容為「史上最大規模能源中斷」的危機，正迫使各國政府採取非常手段應對能源斷鏈。報告顯示，各國應對措施反映能源安全已成為政策優先事項，傳統市場機制在極端情境下面臨挑戰。
立法院研究服務部發布的專題報告則聚焦臺灣能源安全，分析能源轉型政策下產業發展布局，以及中東衝突與全球能源供應鏈重組對臺灣能源安全之影響與因應策略。報告指出，臺灣作為能源進口依賴度極高的地區，中東局勢動盪直接衝擊天然氣供應穩定性，亟需強化能源來源多元化與儲備機制，並加速可再生能源發展以提升能源自主能力。
綜合各來源分析，中東衝突不僅影響短期能源供應，更凸顯全球能源轉型迫切性。IEA與聯合國均強調，加速發展可再生能源、降低對單一能源來源依賴，是提升能源韌性的關鍵途徑。未來全球能源投資將更趨向分散化與綠色化，以應對地緣政治風險。各國政府與企業需重新評估能源戰略，在確保供應穩定的同時，推動能源結構轉型，以應對未來可能出現的類似危機。
