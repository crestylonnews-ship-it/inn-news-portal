  # EU Advances Education Area 2025 Action Plan to Strengthen European Educational Competitiveness
  
  > The EU is promoting educational reform through the Open Coordination Mechanism and has released the "European Education Area 2025 Action Plan," while member states are also adjusting policies to address post-pandemic educational challenges and enhance global competitiveness.
  
  The European Commission has recently initiated and promoted a series of reform initiatives and coordination actions in the field of education through the "Open Coordination Mechanism" established with member states, aiming to improve the quality of European education and its global competitiveness. According to overseas reports, the EU plays an important role in guiding educational concepts, setting standards, international cooperation, and funding educational research. It has also established a new direction for educational reform through the "European Education Area 2025 Action Plan." The promotion of this action plan partly stems from the need to reduce the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on European society and the economy. The new European Commission launched related reform measures in 2020, hoping to strengthen the resilience and adaptability of the European education system through cross-border coordination and policy integration.
  
  From an international comparative perspective, the education systems of European countries show diverse developmental characteristics. According to an analysis by Sociology Institute, Belgium faces the challenge of language-segmented education, Germany maintains its dual-track education model, and the UK is adjusting its education policies after Brexit. These differences reflect the complexity and diversity of educational development in Europe, and also show that the EU-level coordination mechanism must take into account the characteristics and differences of each country's education system when promoting educational reform. The different directions of education policies in various countries have also become important indicators for observing the development of European education.
  
  The "International Comparison of Education Statistical Indicators (2024 Edition)" compiled by the Statistics Office of Taiwan's Ministry of Education also shows that countries continue to reference international data in formulating education policies to strengthen educational reform and quality improvement. This special publication compiles domestic and international education statistical data, providing various sectors with an understanding of the differences in major countries' educational development and serving as a reference for policy formulation. The editorial explanation points out that this initiative aims to strengthen educational reform, improve education quality, and enhance international cultural and educational exchanges and integration, providing the Ministry with a reference for formulating education policies and other units for research and analysis.
  
  Synthesizing information from various sources, European educational reform is moving toward cross-border coordination and standardization, while also taking into account the characteristics and differences of each country's education system. The effectiveness of future education policies will depend on the operation of the coordination mechanism between the EU and member states, as well as each country's ability to respond to post-pandemic educational challenges. The comparison and exchange of education statistical data among countries has also become an important foundation for promoting educational reform.

# 歐盟推動教育區2025行動計劃 強化歐洲教育競爭力

> 歐盟透過開放協調機制推動教育改革，發布《歐洲教育區2025行動計劃》，各國亦針對疫情後教育挑戰進行政策調整，提升全球競爭力。

歐盟委員會近期透過與成員國建立的「開放協調機制」，在教育領域發起並推動一系列改革倡議與協調行動，旨在提升歐洲教育質量與全球競爭力。根據海外近報報導，歐盟在教育理念引領、標準制定、國際合作及教育科研資助等方面發揮重要作用，並透過《歐洲教育區2025行動計劃》確立教育改革新方向。這項行動計劃的推動，部分源於減少新冠疫情對歐洲社會經濟造成的衝擊，新屆歐盟委員會於2020年啟動相關改革措施，期望透過跨國協調與政策整合，強化歐洲教育體系的韌性與適應能力。
從國際比較的角度來看，歐洲各國教育體系呈現多元發展特徵。根據 Sociology Institute 的分析，比利時面臨語言分割槽的教育挑戰，德國維持其雙軌制教育模式，英國則在脫歐後進行教育政策調整。這些差異反映出歐洲教育發展的複雜性與多樣性，也顯示歐盟層級協調機制在推動教育改革時，必須兼顧各國教育體系的特色與差異。不同國家的教育政策走向，也成為觀察歐洲教育發展的重要指標。
臺灣教育部統計處編製的《教育統計指標之國際比較（2024年版）》也顯示，各國在教育政策制定上持續參考國際資料，以強化教育改革與品質提升。該專刊彙編國內外相關教育統計資料，供各界瞭解主要國家教育發展差異，並作為政策研擬的參考依據。編輯說明指出，此舉旨在加強教育改革、提升教育品質，並強化國際文教交流與接軌，提供本部制訂教育政策及其他單位作為研究分析之參。
綜合各來源資訊，歐洲教育改革正朝向跨國協調與標準化方向發展，同時兼顧各國教育體系的特色與差異。未來教育政策的成效，將取決於歐盟與成員國之間的協調機制運作，以及各國對疫情後教育挑戰的應對能力。各國教育統計資料的比較與交流，也成為推動教育改革的重要基礎。
