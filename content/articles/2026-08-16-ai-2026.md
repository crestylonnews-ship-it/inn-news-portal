  # US-China AI Competition Intensifies: 2026 Innovation Index Reveals Bipolar Landscape
  
  > The 2026 Global AI Innovation Index shows the United States leading with 78.44 points, while China holds second place with 60.49 points, as the US-China bipolar格局 continues to widen. The Americas region benefited from cloud services and high-performance computing demand, driving double-digit growth.
  
  The "Global Artificial Intelligence Innovation Index Report 2026" was released on July 17, 2026, at the plenary session of the Science Frontier Conference of the World AI Conference, compiled by the China Institute of Science and Technical Information. The report indicates that the United States leads the world significantly with 78.44 points, while China holds steady at second place with 60.49 points, ahead of third place by 20.5 points. The two countries belong to the first tier, forming a clear bipolar competitive格局. Since its release, the index has continuously tracked global AI innovation momentum, becoming an important indicator for observing each country's technological capabilities and policy directions.
  
  According to the "2025 Global Artificial Intelligence Innovation Pattern Analysis" report, the United States has built globally leading supercomputing capabilities and a distributed computing power network, while creating a more flexible innovation environment through regulatory policy adjustments, forming a dual guarantee of "hard power" and "soft environment." The "2025 AI Index Report" released by Stanford University's Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence Institute (HAI) points out that China has achieved leapfrog development in technological engineering, patent transformation, and scenario implementation capabilities, indicating that the global AI competition has shifted from single technological leadership to comprehensive ecosystem competition.
  
  At the industry level, the global technology sector showed strong momentum in the Americas and Asia-Pacific regions in 2024, with double-digit growth mainly driven by AI infrastructure and cloud services spending. The Americas region benefited from large cloud service providers continuously expanding data center scale, with insatiable demand for high-performance computing chips; the Asia-Pacific region, relying on its semiconductor manufacturing advantages, plays a key role in advanced process and packaging capacity. AI server shipments continue to climb, further consolidating the Americas' core position in the global AI supply chain.
  
  Synthesizing observations from various reports, the US-China competition in the AI field has extended from the technological level to multiple dimensions including the industrial chain, talent cultivation, and international cooperation. The United States maintains its leadership through basic technology research capabilities, free market mechanisms, and talent cultivation systems; China demonstrates advantages in application scenario implementation and patent transformation. Future global AI development will continue to be profoundly influenced by the policy directions, technological breakthroughs, and international cooperation models of the two major economies.

# 中美AI競爭白熱化 2026創新指數揭雙極格局

> 2026年全球AI創新指數顯示，美國以78.44分領先，中國60.49分穩居第二，中美雙極格局持續擴大。美洲地區受惠於雲端服務與高效能運算需求，帶動雙位數成長。

《全球人工智慧創新指數報告2026》於2026年7月17日世界人工智慧大會科學前沿全體會議上發布，由中國科學技術資訊研究所編製。報告指出，美國以78.44分大幅領先全球，中國以60.49分穩居第二，領先第三名達20.5分，兩國同屬第一梯隊，形成明顯的雙極競爭格局。該指數自發布以來持續追蹤全球AI創新動能，成為觀察各國技術實力與政策走向的重要指標。
根據《2025年全球人工智慧創新格局分析》報告，美國已構建起全球領先的超級計算能力與分散式算力網路，同時透過監管政策調整營造更靈活的創新環境，形成「硬實力」與「軟環境」雙重保障。斯坦福大學人類中心人工智慧研究所（HAI）發布的《2025年人工智慧指數報告》則指出，中國在技術工程化、專利轉化及場景落地能力上實現跨越式發展，顯示全球AI競爭已從單一技術領先轉向綜合生態競爭。
在產業層面，2024年全球科技產業於美洲與亞太區展現強勁動能，雙位數成長主要由AI基礎設施與雲端服務支出帶動。美洲地區受惠於大型雲端服務供應商持續擴張資料中心規模，對高效能運算晶片需求若渴；亞太區則憑藉半導體製造優勢，在先進製程與封裝產能上扮演關鍵角色。AI伺服器出貨量持續攀升，進一步鞏固美洲在全球AI供應鏈中的核心地位。
綜合各報告觀察，中美在AI領域的競爭已從技術層面延伸至產業鏈、人才培養與國際合作等多個維度。美國憑藉基礎科技研究能力、自由市場機制與人才培養體系維持領導地位；中國則在應用場景落地與專利轉化上展現優勢。未來全球AI發展將持續受到兩大經濟體政策走向、技術突破與國際合作模式的深刻影響。
