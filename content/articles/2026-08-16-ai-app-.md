  # US Senator Urges AI Research Pause, Microsoft Launches Super APP, Google Expands to Canada
  
  > US Senator sends letter calling for AI research pause, citing virus design失控 incident; Microsoft's Nadella confirms Copilot Super APP arriving this year; Google's Gemini for Home smart home assistant expands to Canadian market.
  
  US Senator Bernie Sanders recently wrote to multiple artificial intelligence development companies, calling for a pause on the research and deployment of advanced AI systems until the government establishes an effective safety regulatory framework. According to unwire.hk, the open letter cited a recent incident involving a virus design that got out of control, warning that if tech giants do not self-regulate, Congress will consider mandatory legislative intervention.
  
  The letter also cited reports from Business Insider, Reuters, The Guardian, and The Register as supporting evidence, emphasizing that AI technology without proper controls could pose serious threats to public safety and social stability. The move has sparked widespread discussion in the tech industry; supporters argue that regulation is urgently needed, while opponents worry that excessive intervention will slow America's leading position in the AI race.
  
  Running parallel to the regulatory debate, US tech giants are accelerating their AI product rollout. Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella officially confirmed during an earnings call that the company is fully developing a new Copilot "Super App." Techbang reported that this AI Agent-integrated application not only writes code but also delivers one-stop collaboration features covering work and development environments. Nadella emphasized that this move is a key step in Microsoft's strategic reshaping of its AI product line, with the goal of making Copilot an all-around smart assistant that defines future work models.
  
  The product is expected to launch later this year, but detailed feature specifications and the exact release date have not yet been fully disclosed.
  
  Meanwhile, Google is pushing its next-generation smart home assistant service into international markets. According to biggo.com.tw, Google aims to replace the classic Google Assistant with its more advanced Gemini AI, and this ambitious plan has taken its first step beyond the United States. Following a rapid but controversial US user rollout, Google is now opening its early access program to Canadian users, marking an important milestone in Gemini for Home's global deployment.
  
  Reports indicate that demand for French language support in the Canadian market prompted Google to prioritize it as the first stop outside the US, demonstrating progress in the service's language localization.
  
  In a comprehensive observation, the Americas AI industry is at a critical stage where development and regulation proceed in parallel. On one hand, tech giants such as Microsoft and Google are accelerating product innovation and market expansion; on the other hand, concerns among lawmakers and the public about AI safety are growing increasingly prominent. Senator Sanders' open letter reflects political attention to the risks of technology getting out of control, while companies continue to advance their commercialization programs. The future direction of US AI policy will depend on the balance between regulatory frameworks and industry innovation.

# 美議員籲停AI研發 微軟推超級APP 谷歌拓展加國

> 美國參議員致信呼籲暫停AI研發，引述病毒設計失控事件；微軟納德拉證實Copilot超級APP今年登場；谷歌Gemini for Home智慧家庭助理擴充套件至加拿大市場。

美國參議員伯尼·桑德斯（Bernie Sanders）近期致信多家人工智慧開發企業，呼籲暫停先進AI系統的研發與部署，直至政府建立有效的安全監管框架。根據unwire.hk報導，這封公開信引述了近期發生的病毒設計失控事件，警告科技巨頭若未主動自律，國會將考慮立法強制介入。
該信同時引用Business Insider、Reuters、The Guardian及The Register等媒體的報導作為佐證，強調AI技術若缺乏適當管控，可能對公共安全與社會穩定造成嚴重威脅。此舉引發科技界廣泛討論，支援方認為監管刻不容緩，反對方則擔憂過度幹預將延緩美國在AI競賽中的領先地位。
與監管爭議並行的是，美國科技巨頭正加速推進AI產品佈局。微軟執行長薩蒂亞·納德拉（Satya Nadella）在法說會兼財報電話會議上正式證實，公司正在全力開發一款全新的Copilot「超級APP」（Super App）。Techbang報導指出，這款整合AI Agent的應用程式不僅能編寫程式碼，更實現一站式協作功能，涵蓋工作與開發環境。納德拉強調，此舉是微軟重塑AI產品線戰略佈局的重要一步，目標是讓Copilot成為定義未來工作模式的全能智慧助手。
目前該產品預計於今年內登場，但詳細功能規格與上市時間尚未完全公開。
另一方面，谷歌（Google）正將其新一代智慧家庭助理服務推向國際市場。根據biggo.com.tw報導，谷歌欲以更先進的Gemini AI取代經典的Google Assistant，此項雄心計畫已邁出超越美國的第一步。在經歷了頗具爭議但快速的美國使用者推廣後，谷歌現正將其早期體驗計畫開放給加拿大使用者，標誌著Gemini for Home全球部署的重要里程碑。
報導指出，加拿大市場對法語支援的需求促使谷歌優先選擇當地作為美國之外的首站，顯示該服務在語言本地化方面的進展。
綜合觀察，美洲AI產業正處於發展與監管並行的關鍵階段。一方面，微軟、谷歌等科技巨頭加速產品創新與市場擴充套件；另一方面，國會議員與公眾對AI安全性的擔憂日益升高。桑德斯參議員的公開信反映了政界對技術失控風險的關注，而企業則持續推進商業化程式。未來美國AI政策的走向，將取決於監管框架與產業創新之間的平衡。
