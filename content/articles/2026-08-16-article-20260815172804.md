  # Europe Economy Sounds Alarm: Structural Dilemmas Emerge in Germany, UK, France, and Italy
  
  > Germany's manufacturing sector is stalling, the UK is trapped in high debt, and France and Italy are burdened with mounting debt. Europe's major economies face stagnation and structural challenges, while the EU is advancing economic security and trade policies to navigate these shifts.
  
  Europe's economy is facing unprecedented structural challenges. According to an analysis by the Taiwan Institute for Economic Research (IEK), in 2023, amid factors such as US-China tensions, the Russia-Ukraine war, and geopolitical developments, countries have closely linked industrial development with economic security, driving strategic policies to support industrial growth to an unprecedented peak. The United States has actively introduced multiple policy measures and courted neighboring countries to shape new regional economic markets, prompting European nations to re-examine their own industrial security and economic resilience.
  
  Europe's four largest economies—Germany, the UK, France, and Italy—are facing varying degrees of economic difficulty. Germany, the largest economy in Europe, is showing signs of manufacturing slowdown, with a surge in corporate bankruptcies indicating that its traditional industrial advantages are under severe strain. The UK is described as suffering from "affluenza," caught in a high-debt predicament. France faces a substantial fiscal deficit and is mired in political deadlock. Italy is experiencing sluggish growth and an increasingly severe aging population problem. These structural issues are interwoven, casting a shadow over Europe's economic outlook.
  
  On the EU front, the EU's official trade website states that the EU's trade policy is dedicated to serving 450 million European consumers by promoting trade agreements and defending European interests worldwide. Composed of 27 member states, the EU has progressively advanced integration among its members since its establishment in 1993 through the implementation of common policies and the creation of a single market. The EU has further deepened integration by introducing the euro, which is now the single European currency used by 19 of the 27 member states.
  
  From an industrial structure perspective, the European Economic Area (EEA) comprises EU member states along with some non-EU countries, featuring a diversified economic structure and abundant natural resources, playing a pivotal role on the global economic stage. The pillars of the European economy extend beyond German manufacturing, as the innovative industries of the Nordic countries are gradually emerging as important growth drivers.
  
  Regarding the EU's governance structure, data from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of China indicates that after the Lisbon Treaty took effect on December 1, 2009, the practice of rotating the presidency of the EU Council among member states' ministers was abolished. Instead, a permanent president is elected by the Council for a term of two and a half years, renewable once, and is directly accountable to the European Parliament. Additionally, the position of "High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy" was established to represent the EU in exercising its external functions, aiming to strengthen the EU's unified voice and decision-making efficiency in international affairs.

Whether Europe's economy can successfully transform in the future will depend on the effectiveness of policy coordination among member states and industrial upgrading.

# 歐洲經濟拉警報：德英法義結構性困境浮現

> 德國製造業熄火、英國陷高負債、法義債臺高築，歐洲大國面臨經濟停滯與結構性挑戰，歐盟推動經濟安全與貿易政策應對變局。

歐洲經濟正面臨前所未有的結構性挑戰。根據臺灣經濟研究院（IEK）分析指出，2023年在中美對峙、烏俄戰爭及地緣政治等各種因素影響下，各國將產業發展與經濟安全緊密掛鉤，針對產業發展所推出的策略性政策力道達到前所未有的高峰。其中美國積極推出多項政策法案，並拉攏鄰近國家企圖形塑新區域經濟市場，此舉也促使歐洲各國重新審視自身產業安全與經濟韌性。
歐洲四大經濟體德國、英國、法國與義大利正面臨不同程度的經濟困境。德國作為歐洲最大經濟體，製造業出現熄火跡象，企業破產數量激增，顯示其傳統工業優勢正面臨嚴峻考驗。英國則被形容罹患「富裕病」，捲進高負債困境。法國面臨高額財政赤字，更陷入政治僵局。義大利則成長低迷且人口老化問題日益嚴重。這些結構性問題相互交織，使歐洲經濟前景蒙上陰影。
歐盟方面，根據歐盟官方貿易網站指出，歐盟貿易政策致力於為4.5億歐洲消費者服務，透過推動貿易協定並捍衛歐洲利益於世界各地。歐盟由27個成員國組成，自1993年成立以來，透過實施共同政策與建立單一市場，逐步實現成員國一體化程式。歐盟更透過引入歐元進一步深化一體化，目前歐元已是27個成員國中19個國家使用的單一歐洲貨幣。
從產業結構來看，歐洲經濟區（EEA）由歐盟成員國以及一些非歐盟國家組成，擁有多樣化的經濟結構與豐富的自然資源，在全球經濟舞臺上扮演舉足輕重的角色。歐洲經濟的支柱不僅限於德國製造業，北歐國家的創新產業亦逐漸成為重要成長動能。
歐盟治理架構方面，根據中華民國外交部資料顯示，里斯本條約於2009年12月1日生效後，廢止各國部長輪值歐盟理事會主席的成例，改由理事會選出常任主席，任期兩年半，得連任一次，並直接向歐洲議會負責。另設立「歐盟外交暨安全政策高階代表」，代表歐盟對外行使職權，此舉旨在強化歐盟在國際事務上的統一聲音與決策效率。
綜合觀察，歐洲經濟正面臨多重挑戰，從德國製造業衰退到南歐國家債務問題，從英國高負債困境到法國政治僵局，結構性問題亟待解決。歐盟在推動經濟安全與貿易政策的同時，也需應對地緣政治變局與美國政策競爭帶來的壓力。未來歐洲經濟能否成功轉型，將取決於各國政策協調與產業升級的成效。
