  # UNESCO Releases Report on African Basic Education, Focusing on Out-of-School Children and Learning Crisis
  
  > The 2025 report released by UNESCO and the African Centre for School Leadership highlights Africa's high proportion of out-of-school children and the severe challenges facing foundational learning development, with education cooperation remaining a key topic in China-Africa collaboration.
  
  The United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) and the African Centre for School Leadership (ASCL) recently jointly released the "Spotlight on Basic Education Completion and Foundational Learning in Africa, 2025: Lead for Foundational Learning" report.
  
  The report points out that although Africa has made certain progress in expanding basic education access in recent years, the proportion of out-of-school children remains high and foundational learning development faces severe challenges. The report emphasizes that improving the quality of basic education and learning outcomes is a key link for Africa to achieve sustainable development.
  
  According to the report, many African countries have made efforts to expand school enrollment opportunities for school-age children, but the phenomena of "out-of-school" and "in-school but lacking basic literacy and numeracy skills" remain widespread. UNESCO regards foundational learning as a core objective of the education system and calls on governments, international organizations, and the private sector to jointly invest resources to improve teaching quality, train teachers, and enhance educational infrastructure. The report also notes that African countries need to place greater emphasis on the銜接 between early education and foundational learning in formulating and implementing education policies, to ensure that children build a solid learning foundation from the start of their schooling.
  
  In the international context of education cooperation, African education development is not only a strategic priority for the United Nations system and major international organizations, but is also regarded as an important pathway to achieving global equitable and inclusive development. According to a special report by relevant academic units at Shanghai International Studies University, the 2024 Forum on China-Africa Cooperation summit was held in Beijing from September 4 to 6, with the theme "Joining Hands to Advance Modernization and Build a High-Level China-Africa Community with a Shared Future." The report emphasizes that education development is a fundamental pathway to driving national modernization, and education cooperation has become one of the important components of China-Africa cooperation.
  
  African education issues have also been incorporated into regional development frameworks such as the African Continental Free Trade Area and the African Union's Agenda 2063, demonstrating the central position of education in Africa's overall development strategy.
  
  A special feature in the United Nations "African Renewal" magazine also points out that Africa's progress in education is closely linked to the UN Sustainable Development Goals, the Paris Climate Change Agreement, and Agenda 2063. The magazine covers themes including sustainable development, climate action, youth voices, women's empowerment, health, and economic development, with education regarded as an important thread running through all development objectives. Analysts believe that African education development not only relates to the accumulation of human capital, but also affects the continent's overall economic transformation and social progress.
  
  In the future, how to effectively integrate international resources, strengthen regional education cooperation, and formulate appropriate policies tailored to different national contexts will be the key issues for expanding basic education access and improving learning quality in Africa.

# 聯合國教科文組織發布非洲基礎教育報告 聚焦失學與學習危機

> 聯合國教科文組織與非洲學校領導力中心發布2025年報告，指出非洲失學兒童比例偏高，基礎學習發展面臨嚴峻挑戰，教育合作亦為中非合作重要議題。

聯合國教科文組織（UNESCO）與非洲學校領導力中心（African Centre for School Leadership，簡稱ASCL）近日聯合發布《2025非洲基礎教育普及與基礎學習發展報告：引領基礎學習之路》（Spotlight on basic education completion and foundational learning in Africa, 2025: Lead for foundational learning）。
該報告指出，雖然近年來非洲在基礎教育普及方面取得一定進展，但失學兒童比例仍然偏高，基礎學習發展面臨嚴峻挑戰。報告強調，提升基礎教育品質與學習成效，是非洲實現可持續發展的關鍵環節。
根據報告內容，非洲多國在擴大學齡兒童就學機會方面已付出努力，但「失學」與「在學但未掌握基礎讀寫與計算能力」的現象依然普遍存在。聯合國教科文組織將基礎學習視為教育體系的核心目標，並呼籲各國政府、國際組織與民間部門共同投入資源，改善教學品質、培訓教師，以及完善教育基礎設施。報告亦指出，非洲各國在教育政策制定與執行上，需更重視早期教育與基礎學習的銜接，以確保兒童在入學初期即能建立扎實的學習根基。
在教育合作的國際脈絡方面，非洲教育發展不僅是聯合國系統及各主要國際組織的優先戰略，亦被視為實現全球公平包容發展的重要途徑。根據上海外國語大學相關學術單位的專題報導指出，2024年中非合作論壇峰會於9月4日至6日在北京舉行，此次峰會主題為「攜手推進現代化，共築高水準中非命運共同體」。報導強調，教育發展是推動國家現代化的基礎路徑，教育合作已成為中非合作的重要內容之一。
非洲教育議題同時亦被納入非洲大陸自由貿易區、非洲2063年議程等區域發展框架之中，顯示教育在非洲整體發展戰略中的核心地位。
聯合國《非洲復興》雜誌的相關專題亦指出，非洲在教育領域的進展與聯合國可持續發展目標、巴黎氣候變化協議及非洲2063年議程緊密相連。該雜誌涵蓋的主題包括可持續發展、氣候行動、青年聲音、婦女賦權、健康、經濟發展等面向，教育議題被視為貫穿各項發展目標的重要主軸。分析人士認為，非洲教育發展不僅關係到人力資本的累積，亦影響到大陸整體的經濟轉型與社會進步。
未來，如何有效整合國際資源、強化區域教育合作，並針對不同國家的情境制定適切的政策，將是非洲基礎教育普及與學習品質提升的關鍵課題。
