  # EU Passes Artificial Intelligence Act, Setting Global Regulatory Standards
  
  > The European Parliament voted to pass the AI Act in mid-June, aiming to establish a global AI regulatory framework. The European Commission and the Council will now enter trilogue negotiations to reconcile their respective versions of the legislation.
  
  The European Parliament passed the Artificial Intelligence Act (AI Act) by a majority vote in mid-June 2024, marking a pivotal step for the EU in AI regulation and positioning it as a potential pioneer in global standards-setting. According to reports from MingNanTang, the legislation aims to establish unified rules for the development, deployment, and use of AI systems to address the risks and challenges posed by the rapid advancement of AI technology. The European Commission had previously proposed a draft version, while the Council of the EU also holds its own proposal; the three institutions will now enter negotiations to bridge differences and reach a final agreement.
  
  This legislative process reflects the EU's emphasis on technology governance, seeking to balance innovation with risk control while sending a regulatory signal to the world.
  
  The passage of the AI Act has drawn widespread attention. Its core provisions include strict scrutiny of high-risk AI systems, requirements for transparency and data governance, and bans on certain unacceptable AI applications. The Parliament's vote showed majority support for the act, though political groups remain divided on the intensity of regulation. Some lawmakers argue that overly strict rules could undermine the competitiveness of Europe's tech industry, while environmental and human rights groups stress the necessity of protective measures. Additionally, differences between the Commission's and the Council's versions have not yet been fully clarified, and negotiations are expected to focus on issues such as corporate compliance costs, innovation exemptions, and enforcement mechanisms.
  
  This legislative process also influences the direction of global tech policy, with economies such as the United States and China closely observing the EU's regulatory framework and potentially drawing on its model.
  
  From an industry impact perspective, the AI Act will have far-reaching consequences for European and multinational tech companies. Firms will need to reassess their AI product development processes to ensure compliance with risk classification and transparency requirements, which may increase R&D and compliance costs. However, in the long run, clear regulatory standards will help build consumer trust and promote responsible AI innovation. Meanwhile, other regions such as Asia and North America are also drafting similar regulations, and the EU's progress could accelerate international regulatory coordination. Experts note that the outcome of the trilogue negotiations will determine the final strength of the act; if a compromise version is reached, the EU is poised to become a global benchmark for AI governance.
  
  Subsequent observations will focus on the negotiation process, corporate feedback, and international reactions to assess the act's actual impact on the tech industry and society.

# 歐盟透過人工智慧法 擬定全球監管標準

> 歐洲議會於6月中表決透過《人工智慧法》，旨在建立全球AI監管框架，歐盟執委會與理事會將展開三方協商，以整合各自法案版本。

歐洲議會於2024年6月中旬以多數票透過《人工智慧法》（AI Act），此舉標誌著歐盟在人工智慧監管領域邁出關鍵一步，並有望成為全球標準制定的先驅。根據《鳴人堂》報導，該法案旨在為人工智慧系統的開發、部署與使用設立統一規範，以應對AI技術快速發展所帶來的風險與挑戰。歐洲執委會先前已提出草案版本，而歐盟理事會亦擁有自己的提案，三方現將進入協商階段，以整合差異並達成最終協議。
此立法程式反映歐盟對科技治理的重視，試圖在創新與風險管控之間取得平衡，同時向全球傳遞監管訊號。
《人工智慧法》的透過引發廣泛關注，其核心內容包括對高風險AI系統實施嚴格審查、要求透明度與資料治理，並禁止某些不被接受的AI應用。歐洲議會表決結果顯示多數議員支援該法案，但不同政治團體對監管強度仍有分歧。部分議員認為規範過於嚴格可能影響歐洲科技產業競爭力，而環保與人權團體則強調必要防護措施。此外，歐盟執委會與理事會的版本差異尚未完全明朗，預計協商過程將聚焦於企業合規成本、創新豁免條款及執法機制等議題。
此立法程式亦影響全球科技政策走向，美國、中國等經濟體正密切觀察歐盟監管框架，並可能借鑒其模式。
從產業影響面來看，《人工智慧法》將對歐洲及跨國科技企業產生深遠影響。企業需重新檢視AI產品開發流程，確保符合風險分級與透明度要求，這可能增加研發與合規成本。然而，長遠而言，明確的監管標準有助建立消費者信任，並促進負責任的AI創新。與此同時，全球其他地區如亞洲與北美，亦在研擬類似法規，歐盟的進展可能加速國際監管協調。專家指出，三方協商結果將決定法案最終力度，若達成妥協版本，歐盟有望成為AI治理的全球標竿。
後續觀察將聚焦於協商程式、企業反饋及國際反應，以評估該法對科技產業與社會的實際影響。
