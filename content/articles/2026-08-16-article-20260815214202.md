  # Middle East Energy Risks Escalate as Oceania Accelerates Green Energy Transition
  
  > Global natural gas demand in 2025 is driven by the United States, the European Union, and the Middle East; Oceania, led by Australia and New Zealand, pushes sustainable aviation fuel commercialization, while Middle East geopolitical tensions continue to impact energy markets.
  
  The International Energy Agency's (IEA) "2026 Global Energy Review" indicates that global natural gas demand in 2025 showed regional disparities, with macroeconomic conditions, price dynamics, and weather factors jointly driving consumption trends. The United States, the European Union, and the Middle East emerged as the primary drivers of global natural gas demand growth. The report emphasizes that the Middle East plays a key role in the structural transformation of energy demand, while also being a concentrated area of geopolitical risk.
  
  Meanwhile, MacroMicro Financial Square released an analysis on March 17, 2026, stating that the ongoing Middle East stalemate continues to affect global energy risks, with varying degrees of impact across countries. The report notes that tensions in U.S.-Iran relations have affected energy supply chains and raw material prices to different extents across economies including China, the United States, Europe, Taiwan, emerging markets, and Japan, with market concerns over energy supply stability in the Middle East remaining unabated.
  
  In Oceania, Carbon Note's 2025 report "Current Status of SAF Development in Africa and Oceania" shows that the region centers on policy advancement and research innovation. Australia is actively moving toward sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) commercialization, while New Zealand focuses on technology validation, together shaping a new landscape for low-carbon fuel systems. The report points out that Africa and Oceania demonstrate diverse momentum in the Southern Hemisphere, with Africa's industrial framework built around raw material potential and international cooperation, forming a nascent industry where hydrogen and biofuels run in parallel.
  
  In summary, the Middle East faces dual challenges of geopolitical risk and energy demand growth, while Oceania is actively positioning itself in the green energy sector amid climate transition. The IEA report and MacroMicro analysis together reflect the Middle East's strategic position in the global energy landscape, while Carbon Note's report highlights Oceania's progress in sustainable aviation fuels. All eyes are on how Middle East developments will affect global energy prices and whether Australia and New Zealand's SAF industrialization timelines can meet their decarbonization targets.

# 中東能源風險升溫 大洋洲綠能轉型加速

> 2025 年全球天然氣需求由美國、歐盟與中東驅動增長；大洋洲以澳洲、紐西蘭推動永續航空燃料商業化，中東地緣緊張持續影響能源市場。

國際能源署（IEA）發布的《2026 年全球能源回顧》指出，2025 年全球天然氣需求呈現區域性差異，宏觀經濟、價格動態與天氣因素共同驅動消費趨勢，其中美國、歐洲聯盟與中東地區成為全球天然氣需求增長的主要驅動力。該報告強調，中東地區在能源需求結構轉型中扮演關鍵角色，同時也是地緣政治風險的集中區域。
與此同時，MacroMicro 財經M平方於 2026 年 3 月 17 日發布分析指出，中東僵局持續牽動全球能源風險，各國受影響程度各異。報導提及，美伊關係緊張局勢對中國、美國、歐洲、臺灣、新興市場與日本等經濟體的能源供應鏈與原物料價格均產生不同程度的衝擊，市場對中東地區能源供應穩定性的擔憂未減。
在大洋洲方面，卡本記事於 2025 年發布的《非洲與大洋洲 SAF 發展現況》分析顯示，大洋洲地區以政策推進與研究創新為核心，澳洲積極邁向永續航空燃料（SAF）商業化階段，紐西蘭則聚焦於技術驗證，兩者共同構築低碳燃料體系的新地景。報告指出，非洲與大洋洲展現出南半球的多元動能，非洲以原料潛力與國際合作為主軸，形成氫能與生質燃料並行的產業雛形。
綜合觀察，中東地區同時面臨地緣政治風險與能源需求增長的雙重挑戰，而大洋洲則在氣候轉型議題上積極佈局綠能產業。IEA 報告與 MacroMicro 分析共同反映出中東在全球能源版圖中的戰略地位，而卡本記事的報導則呈現大洋洲在永續航空燃料領域的進展。各界關注中東局勢演變對全球能源價格的影響，以及澳洲、紐西蘭 SAF 產業化程式能否如期達成減碳目標。
