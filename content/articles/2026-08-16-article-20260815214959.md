  # Cross-Border Education Becomes Asia's Transformation Engine, East Asian Higher Education Policy Continues to Adjust
  
  > UNESCO notes that Asia's education transformation must account for regional diversity; China Social Sciences Network reports cross-border education as an innovation catalyst; National Academy for Educational Research analyzes the current state of East Asian higher education policy development.
  
  The United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) has pointed out that education transformation in the Asia-Pacific region must take into account the area's massive population and high degree of diversity. Characterized by rapid economic growth, innovative development, and a steadily increasing number of middle-income countries, the region makes the direction of education policy implementation increasingly complex. UNESCO emphasizes that countries within the region differ significantly in their stages of economic development, social structures, and cultural backgrounds, and that education reform strategies must be tailored to local conditions in order to effectively respond to the needs of different countries.
  
  China Social Sciences Network reports that cross-border education has long provided Asian students with an alternative pathway, enabling them to obtain globally recognized degrees without having to travel to traditional study-abroad destinations such as the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, and Australia. The report also notes that this educational model is continuously evolving and has gradually become a catalyst for driving national innovation and enhancing competitiveness. Furthermore, the report explains that demographic shifts, economic transitions, and accelerating technological change are reshaping perceptions and expectations of higher education, placing cross-border education in an increasingly critical role against this backdrop.
  
  The 163rd issue of the National Academy for Educational Research e-Newsletter, based on the guiding directions of UNESCO's Education 2030 Framework for Action—specifically Targets 3, 4, and 5—focuses on the higher education stage in East Asian countries, organizing and analyzing relevant developments in enrollment conditions and environmental preparedness. The publication notes that a considerable portion of recent international education information pertains to higher education reform in various countries, and therefore systematically organizes the current state of policy development at the higher education level, primarily centered on East Asian nations, to provide reference material for relevant authorities.
  
  A synthesis of the above materials reveals that Asia's education transformation is moving toward diversified pathways. On one hand, international organizations continue to monitor overall trends and policy coordination in regional education development; on the other hand, the rise of cross-border education models has provided new academic advancement options for regional students and prompted countries to re-examine the competitiveness of their own higher education systems. The policy adjustments made by East Asian countries in higher education reflect an active response to international education frameworks. The future direction of education development in Asia will still depend on observing the progress of policy implementation and the practical effectiveness of cross-border education cooperation.

# 跨國教育成亞洲轉型引擎，東亞高等教育政策持續調整

> 聯合國教科文組織指出亞洲教育轉型需考量區域多元性；中國社會科學網報導跨國教育成創新催化劑；國家教育研究院分析東亞高教政策發展現況。

聯合國教科文組織（UNESCO）指出，亞洲與太平洋地區的教育轉型必須考量該區域的龐大人口與高度多元性。該地區以快速經濟成長、創新發展以及中等收入國家數量持續增加為特徵，教育政策的推動方向因而更顯複雜。UNESCO 強調，區域內各國在經濟發展階段、社會結構與文化背景上差異顯著，教育改革的策略必須因地制宜，方能有效回應不同國家的需求。
中國社會科學網報導指出，跨國教育長期為亞洲學生提供了一條替代途徑，使他們無需遠赴英國、美國、加拿大與澳大利亞等傳統留學大國，便能獲得全球認可的學位。報導同時指出，此種教育模式正在不斷演變，已逐漸成為推動國家創新與提升競爭力的催化劑。報導進一步說明，人口結構變化、經濟轉型以及加速中的技術變革，正在重塑人們對高等教育的認知與期待，跨國教育在這樣的背景下扮演日益關鍵的角色。
國家教育研究院電子報第 163 期則以聯合國教科文組織「教育 2030 行動框架」目標 3、目標 4 與目標 5 的綱領性推動方向為基礎，聚焦東亞國家的高等教育階段，整理與分析各國的就學條件及環境整備等相關動向。該刊指出，近期國際教育訊息發布內容中，有較多篇幅與各國高等教育改革相關，因此以東亞國家為主體，針對高等教育階段的政策發展現況進行系統性整理，以提供有關部門參酌。
綜合上述資料可見，亞洲教育轉型正朝向多元路徑發展。一方面，國際組織持續關注區域教育發展的整體趨勢與政策協調；另一方面，跨國教育模式的興起為區域學生提供了新的升學選擇，也促使各國重新檢視自身高等教育體系的競爭力。東亞國家在高等教育政策上的調整，則反映出對國際教育框架的積極回應。未來亞洲教育發展走向，仍須觀察各國政策落實進度與跨國教育合作的實際成效。
