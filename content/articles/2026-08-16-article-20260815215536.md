  # France, Germany, and Italy Sign Space Agreement; EU Advances Policies to Reshape Space Economy
  
  > In November 2023, the economic ministers of France, Germany, and Italy signed a joint agreement for space exploration, while the EU simultaneously released its Space Act and Space Economy Vision, projecting the global space economy to reach 1.6 trillion euros by 2035, with Taiwan also upgrading cooperation with the UK's space industry cluster.
  
  On November 6, 2023, the economic ministers of France, Germany, and Italy jointly issued a trilateral declaration announcing that Europe will independently conduct space exploration over the next decade. This agreement aligns with related resolutions of the European Space Agency (ESA), aiming to strengthen Europe's autonomy and competitiveness in the space domain. According to data translated by the Chinese Academy of Sciences Institute of Scientific and Technical Information of China, the declaration explicitly supports future launch missions for the Ariane 6 and Vega C launch vehicles, while promoting a competitive procurement mechanism for new launch vehicles. Starting in 2026, the Ariane 6 rocket will receive an annual budget of 340 million euros in support, a move regarded as an important step toward enhancing the EU's independence in space launch capabilities.
  
  Policy adjustments at the EU level are equally noteworthy. According to official EU documents cited by the Shanghai Institute of Science and Technology Information, the EU recently released the "EU Space Act" and the "EU Space Economy Vision," which have been evaluated as a groundbreaking initiative marking a fundamental shift toward the formalization of European space policy. This policy is the first to treat Europe's space economy as a comprehensive ecosystem, encompassing not only traditional industrial foundations but also orbital and extra-orbital space, including emerging areas such as space mining and space resource utilization. The documents estimate that the global space economy will reach 1.6 trillion euros by 2035, reflecting the EU's emphasis on the economic potential of the space industry.
  
  The European Commission has also emphasized through its official space policy website that it will lay the foundation for a competitive, sovereign, and future-oriented European space industry by funding breakthrough research and ensuring access to critical technologies.
  
  In terms of cross-border cooperation, Taiwan's space industry ties with the UK have also advanced. According to CNA reports, Taiwan and "Space South Central" (SSC), the UK's largest space industry cluster, are upgrading their cooperation through an institutionalized platform to accelerate bilateral enterprise collaboration, promote knowledge exchange, and advance talent training. Initial cooperation will focus particularly on the satellite supply chain, indicating that Taiwan's role within the space industry value chain is gradually deepening. This cooperation aligns with Europe's overall direction of strengthening space industry cluster development, reflecting a global trend in the space industry toward regional cooperation and supply chain integration.
  
  Experts observe that Europe's dual-track approach—combining the France-Germany-Italy trilateral agreement with EU-level policy—aims to reduce dependence on non-European launch technologies, while Taiwan-UK industry cooperation also illustrates the coexistence of globalization and regionalization in the space economy.

# 法德意簽太空協議 歐盟推政策重塑太空經濟

> 法國、德國、義大利三國經濟部長於2023年11月簽署聯合探索太空協議，歐盟同步發布太空法案與空間經濟願景，預計2035年全球太空經濟達1.6兆歐元，臺灣亦與英國太空產業聚落升級合作。

法國、德國與義大利三國經濟部長於2023年11月6日共同發表三方宣告，宣佈歐洲將在下一個十年獨立進行太空探索。這項協議與歐洲太空總署（ESA）的相關決議相互呼應，旨在強化歐洲在太空領域的自主能力與競爭力。根據中國科學院科技戰略諮詢研究院轉譯的資料顯示，該宣告明確支援阿麗亞娜6型與織女星C型運載火箭未來的發射任務，同時推動新型運載火箭的競爭性採購機制。自2026年起，阿麗亞娜6型火箭每年將獲得3.4億歐元預算支援，此舉被視為歐盟提升太空發射自主性的重要一步。
歐盟層級的政策調整同樣引人注目。根據上海科學技術情報研究所引用的歐盟官方檔案，歐盟近期發布《歐盟太空法案》與《歐盟空間經濟願景》，被評價為一項開創性舉措，標誌著歐洲太空政策正規化的根本轉變。該政策首次將歐洲太空經濟視為一個綜合生態系統，不僅涵蓋傳統工業基礎，更將軌道與軌道外空間納入範疇，包含太空採礦與太空資源利用等新興領域。檔案預估，到2035年全球太空經濟規模將達到1.6兆歐元，顯示歐盟對太空產業經濟潛力的重視。
歐盟委員會亦透過官方太空政策網站強調，將透過資助突破性研究與確保關鍵技術取得，奠定具競爭力、主權獨立且面向未來的歐洲太空產業基礎。
在跨國合作方面，臺灣與英國的太空產業連結亦有所進展。根據中央社記者報導，臺灣與英國規模最大的太空產業聚落「Space South Central」（SSC）合作升級，將透過制度化平臺加速雙邊企業合作、推升知識交流與人才訓練。初期合作將特別聚焦於衛星供應鏈領域，顯示臺灣在太空產業鏈中的角色正逐步深化。此項合作與歐洲整體強化太空產業聚落發展的方向不謀而合，反映全球太空產業正朝向區域合作與供應鏈整合的方向發展。
專家觀察指出，歐洲透過法德意三邊協議與歐盟層級政策雙軌並行，旨在減少對非歐洲發射技術的依賴，同時臺灣與英國的產業合作也體現了太空經濟全球化與區域化並存的趨勢。
