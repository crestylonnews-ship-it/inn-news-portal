  # Middle East Energy Transition Accelerates: Green Hydrogen and Energy Storage Present Opportunities
  
  > While maintaining oil supplies, the Middle East is actively promoting green hydrogen and energy storage technologies. Geopolitical tensions are accelerating energy diversification and impacting global supply chains.
  
  The Middle East and North Africa region plays a central role in the global economy thanks to its abundant hydrocarbon resources, but in recent years it has been gradually moving toward energy transition. The International Energy Agency's (IEA) "World Energy Outlook 2024" notes that while the region supplies oil abroad, it also faces multiple challenges including energy security, emissions, and economic development, with geopolitical risks continuing to affect global energy markets.
  
  According to the "Middle East and North Africa 2024 Energy Industry Outlook" report, Middle Eastern countries are seeking to reduce their dependence on traditional fossil fuels by developing new energy industries such as green hydrogen, in order to address future changes in energy demand.
  
  Green hydrogen has become an important direction for the Middle East's energy transition. Reports from Popular Science indicate that Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates plan to use low-cost renewable energy electricity to split water into hydrogen and oxygen, producing green hydrogen fuel. Saudi Arabia has set ambitious goals, hoping to become a leading global hydrogen producer and exporter in the 2030s. Analysis from SmartPropel Bess emphasizes that 2026 is a critical moment for the Middle East energy industry, with geopolitical tensions and global oil price volatility highlighting the importance of energy diversification and security, thereby accelerating the widespread adoption of solar, photovoltaic, and energy storage solutions.
  
  Lithium batteries, as the core of modern energy storage, play a key role in driving this transition.
  
  The impact of geopolitical conflicts on global energy supply chains has also drawn attention. Business Today cites Dun & Bradstreet's "Middle East Crisis White Paper," which points out that escalating tensions in the Middle East could disrupt the globally critical energy corridor of the Strait of Hormuz, thereby affecting energy markets, logistics, and trade. Although Taiwan's export share to the Middle East is low, its imports are structurally dependent on energy and key chemical raw materials. If the conflict continues to deteriorate, Taiwan will face rising energy and manufacturing cost risks. The IEA's outlook report also warns that escalating risks in the Middle East could have cascading effects on global energy supply and economic development.
  
  Synthesizing reports from various media, the Middle East region is actively positioning itself in green hydrogen and energy storage technologies while maintaining traditional oil supplies, in order to address energy transition and geopolitical risks. However, different sources vary on the specific timeline and scale of the transition: Popular Science focuses on Saudi Arabia's hydrogen goals, while SmartPropel Bess emphasizes 2026 as a key turning point and highlights opportunities for lithium battery energy storage. Additionally, Dun & Bradstreet's white paper primarily analyzes risks from Taiwan's perspective, while the IEA report focuses on global energy trends and security assessments.
  
  Currently, the actual development progress of the green hydrogen industry, the level of energy storage technology adoption, and the long-term impact of geopolitical conflicts on energy supply chains remain to be further observed and confirmed.

# 中東能源轉型加速 綠色氫能與儲能迎機遇

> 中東地區在維持石油供應同時，積極推動綠色氫能與儲能技術，地緣政治緊張加速能源多元化程式，並對全球供應鏈產生影響。

中東與北非地區憑藉豐富的碳氫化合物資源，在全球經濟中扮演核心角色，但近年來正逐步朝向能源轉型邁進。國際能源總署（IEA）發布的《2024年世界能源展望》指出，該地區在向外供應石油的同時，也面臨能源安全、排放與經濟發展等多重挑戰，地緣政治風險持續影響全球能源市場。
根據《Middle East and North Africa 2024 Energy Industry Outlook》報告，中東國家正尋求透過發展綠色氫能等新能源產業，降低對傳統化石燃料的依賴，以應對未來能源需求變化。
綠色氫能成為中東能源轉型的重要方向。《大眾科學》報導指出，沙烏地阿拉伯與阿聯酋計劃利用低成本可再生能源電力，將水分解為氫氣與氧氣，生產綠色氫能燃料。沙烏地阿拉伯更設定宏偉目標，希望在2030年代成為全球領先的氫氣生產國與出口國。SmartPropel Bess 分析則強調，2026年對中東能源產業而言是關鍵時刻，地緣政治緊張局勢與全球石油價格波動，凸顯能源多元化與安全的重要性，進而加速太陽能、光電與儲能解決方案的普及應用。
鋰電池作為現代儲能核心，在推動這項轉型過程中發揮關鍵作用。
地緣政治衝突對全球能源供應鏈的衝擊亦引發關注。《今週刊》引述鄧白氏發布的《中東危機白皮書》指出，中東局勢升溫可能導致全球能源核心航道「荷莫茲海峽」受阻，進而影響能源市場、物流與貿易業務。臺灣對中東出口佔比雖低，但進口對能源與關鍵化學原料存在結構性依賴，若衝突持續惡化，將使臺灣面臨能源與製造成本風險升高的挑戰。IEA 的展望報告亦提醒，中東地區風險升級可能對全球能源供應與經濟發展產生連鎖影響。
綜合各媒體報導，中東地區在維持傳統石油供應的同時，正積極佈局綠色氫能與儲能技術，以應對能源轉型與地緣政治風險。然而，不同來源對轉型程式的具體時程與規模仍有差異：《大眾科學》聚焦沙烏地阿拉伯的氫能目標，而 SmartPropel Bess 則強調2026年為關鍵轉折點，並突出鋰電池儲能的機會。此外，鄧白氏的白皮書主要從臺灣風險角度分析，IEA 報告則著重全球能源趨勢與安全評估。
目前綠色氫能產業的實際發展進度、儲能技術的普及程度，以及地緣政治衝突對能源供應鏈的長期影響，仍有待進一步觀察與確認。
