  # Africa's Economy Shows Steady Growth; China-Africa Cooperation Forum Focuses on Trade Development
  
  > Africa's economy demonstrated steady growth in 2025, the China-Africa Cooperation Forum was held in Beijing, the Taiwan External Trade Development Council (TAITRA) plans a 2026 trade promotion delegation to multiple countries, and international institutions emphasize the potential of regional trade.
  
  According to the Chinese Ministry of Commerce's African Union website, citing the latest report from the International Monetary Fund (IMF), amid rising global economic uncertainty, African countries are proactively advancing economic institutional reforms, promoting industrial upgrading and regional integration processes, and the overall economy is steadily progressing. Africa's economy in 2025 showed a steady growth trend, demonstrating strong resilience and regarded as the region with the highest concentration of high-growth economies worldwide.
  
  The United Nations Conference on Trade and Development (UNCTAD) also focused on regional trade issues in its *2024 Africa Economic Development Report*. The report proposed a comprehensive framework tool to assist African countries in analyzing their vulnerability to shocks, particularly the nature of trade and investment risks, and through evidence-based analysis, discussed how regional trade can enhance the resilience of the African continent. The Faculty of Liberal Arts at the HKU Space College of Professional and Continuing Education cited the view that Africa must promote trade to develop its economy, and mentioned that the 2024 China-Africa Cooperation Forum summit opened in Beijing, marking the fourth time in 24 years that the forum has been held in summit form.
  
  Regarding macroeconomic prospects, Wikipedia data shows that Africa's population is approximately 1.3 billion, accounting for about 15% of the global total (2019 data). Instability during the decolonization process intensified Cold War conflicts, and corruption and authoritarian rule during the Cold War era also plunged Africa's economy into a downturn. However, looking ahead to 2050, the combined GDP of West Africa, East Africa, Central Africa, and Southern Africa is projected to reach $29 trillion, indicating significant long-term growth potential.
  
  On the Taiwan front, the External Trade Development Council (TAITRA) announced that it will organize the "Africa Economic and Trade Promotion Delegation" from October 10 to 25, 2026, leading Taiwanese businesses to Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, and Ethiopia to explore local business opportunities in depth. The association pointed out that Africa, as one of the most promising emerging markets globally, boasts rapidly growing economies and a diverse industrial structure. This trade promotion delegation aims to help Taiwanese enterprises grasp the pulse of the African market and expand their international market footprint.
  
  In addition, research data from the Hong Kong Trade Development Council shows that Egypt has established a *National Smart City Strategy*, aiming to house 40 million people in smart cities by 2050. To achieve this grand vision, Egypt will actively recruit international talent, reflecting African countries' proactive佈局 in infrastructure and urban development.
  
  A comprehensive review of various sources reveals that Africa's economy is at a critical stage of transformation and growth, with regional trade integration, infrastructure investment, and industrial upgrading becoming focal points of concern for all countries. The continued holding of the China-Africa Cooperation Forum, along with Taiwanese enterprises' active expansion into the African market, demonstrates the international community's attention to Africa's economic prospects. However, different institutions still hold varying views on specific growth data and risk assessments, and subsequent developments remain to be observed.

# 非洲經濟穩健增長 中非合作論壇聚焦貿易發展

> 非洲經濟2025年展現穩健增長，中非合作論壇於北京舉行，臺灣外貿協會規劃2026年拓銷團赴多國考察，國際機構強調區域貿易潛力。

根據中國商務部非洲聯盟網站引述國際貨幣基金組織（IMF）最新報告指出，在全球經濟不確定性持續攀升的背景下，非洲各國主動推進經濟體制改革，促進產業迭代升級與區域一體化程式，整體經濟正穩步前行。2025年非洲經濟呈現穩健增長態勢，展現強勁韌性，被視為全球高增長經濟體最集中的區域。
國際組織聯合國貿易及發展會議（UNCTAD）發布的《2024年非洲經濟發展報告》同樣關注區域貿易議題。報告提出綜合框架工具，協助非洲國家分析自身受衝擊風險，特別是貿易與投資風險的性質，並透過循證分析論述區域貿易如何增強非洲大陸的韌性。香港大學專業進修學院博雅學院轉引觀點指出，非洲須促進貿易以發展經濟，並提及2024年中非合作論壇峰會於北京啟幕，為該論壇24年來第四次以峰會形式舉行。
在宏觀經濟前景方面，維基百科資料顯示，非洲人口約13億，佔全球總人口約15%（2019年資料）。非殖民化過程中的不穩定加劇了冷戰衝突，冷戰時期的貪汙與專制統治也曾令非洲經濟陷入景氣低迷。然而展望2050年，預計西非、東非、中非及南部非洲的GDP總量將達到29兆美元，顯示長期增長潛力。
臺灣方面，外貿協會宣佈將於2026年10月10日至25日籌組「非洲經貿拓銷團」，率領臺灣業者前往象牙海岸、迦納、奈及利亞及衣索比亞，深入探索當地商機。該協會指出，非洲作為全球最具潛力的新興市場之一，擁有快速成長的經濟體與多元的產業結構，此次拓銷團旨在協助臺灣企業掌握非洲市場脈動，拓展國際市場版圖。
此外，香港貿易發展局經貿研究資料顯示，埃及已訂定《國家智慧城市策略》，目標在2050年讓4,000萬人安居於智慧都市，為此將廣納國際人才以實現此宏願，反映非洲國家在基礎建設與城市發展方面的積極佈局。
綜合各來源觀察，非洲經濟正處於轉型與成長的關鍵階段，區域貿易整合、基礎建設投資與產業升級成為各國共同關注的焦點。中非合作論壇的持續舉辦，以及臺灣企業積極拓展非洲市場，顯示國際社會對非洲經濟前景的重視。然而，不同機構對具體增長資料與風險評估仍存在差異，後續發展仍有待觀察。
