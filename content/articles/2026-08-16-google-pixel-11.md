  # Google Pixel 11 Launches in Taiwan Using TSMC 3nm Process; Taiwan AI Budget Controversy Draws Attention
  
  > The Google Pixel 11 series was unveiled in Taiwan, with chips manufactured using TSMC's 3nm process; meanwhile, Taiwan's Ministry of Science and Technology AI budget of only NT$1.365 billion has sparked discussion, while Hong Kong pushes forward AI applications in the financial sector.
  
  The Google Pixel 11 series officially launched in Taiwan recently. According to CNA reports, the series' smartphone chips utilize TSMC's 3nm process technology, highlighting Taiwan's pivotal role in the semiconductor supply chain. Google executives also specifically emphasized the importance of the Taiwan team during the launch event, stating that Taiwan is one of the few locations where an engineering team could be invited to share the stage, underscoring the continued deepening of Taiwan-U.S. technological cooperation.
  
  Meanwhile, Taiwan's artificial intelligence development budget has drawn attention. BusinessNext reported that Taiwan's Ministry of Science and Technology allocated only NT$1.365 billion to AI, described as "pitifully small," with the article's headline directly calling it "an important piece missing from Asia's Silicon Valley." The report argues that as various Asian countries actively position themselves in the AI industry, Taiwan risks falling behind in regional technological competition if it fails to increase related budget investments.
  
  Hong Kong's Government Information Technology Department (gia.info.gov.hk), on the other hand, is promoting AI applications from the perspective of the financial services industry. Official documents indicate that the financial services sector is inherently data-driven, and AI can effectively assist with data analysis, significantly enhancing industry efficiency and competitiveness. The documents also caution that AI has dual effects: while it brings enormous potential, improper use may give rise to risks, and financial institutions should take comprehensive precautions against related issues.
  
  Synthesizing reports from the three media outlets, Taiwan holds advantages in hardware manufacturing and the semiconductor sector, as evidenced by the Google Pixel 11's adoption of TSMC's 3nm chips; however, Taiwan's budget investment in software and AI applications remains relatively limited, contrasting with Hong Kong's proactive strategy of promoting financial AI applications. Experts observe that Taiwan must balance its hardware manufacturing strengths with the development of a software ecosystem in order to maintain a leading position in Asia's AI competition.

# Google Pixel 11登臺採臺積電3奈米 臺灣AI預算爭議引關注

> Google Pixel 11系列在臺發布，晶片由臺積電3奈米製程生產；同期臺灣科技部AI預算僅13.65億引發討論，香港則推動金融業AI應用。

Google Pixel 11系列於近日在臺灣正式登臺，中央社報導指出，該系列手機晶片採用臺積電3奈米製程技術，凸顯臺灣在半導體供應鏈中的關鍵地位。Google高層在發表會中亦特別提及臺灣團隊的重要性，表示臺灣是少數能邀請工程團隊共同分享的發表會地點，顯示臺美科技合作關係持續深化。
與此同時，臺灣人工智慧發展預算議題引發關注。數位時代（BusinessNext）報導指出，臺灣科技部人工智慧領域預算僅有13.65億新臺幣，被形容為「少得可憐」，文章標題直指此為「亞洲矽谷遺失的重要板塊」。報導認為，在亞洲各國積極佈局AI產業的背景下，臺灣若未能提升相關預算投入，恐將影響其在區域科技競爭中的地位。
香港政府資訊科技總監辦公室（gia.info.gov.hk）則從金融服務業角度推動AI應用。官方檔案指出，金融服務業本質上為資料導向產業，人工智慧可有效協助資料分析，顯著提升行業效率與競爭力。檔案亦提醒AI具有雙面效果，雖帶來龐大潛能，但不當使用可能衍生風險，金融機構應全面防範相關問題。
綜合三家媒體報導，臺灣在硬體製造與半導體領域具備優勢，Google Pixel 11採用臺積電3奈米晶片即為明證；然而，在軟體與AI應用層面，臺灣預算投入相對有限，與香港積極推動金融AI應用的策略形成對比。專家觀察指出，臺灣需兼顧硬體製造優勢與軟體生態系發展，方能在亞洲AI競爭中保持領先。
