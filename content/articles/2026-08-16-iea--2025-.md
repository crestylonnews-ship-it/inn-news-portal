  # IEA Releases 2025 Energy and Climate Model Report, Americas Energy Transition Pathways Draw Attention
  
  > The International Energy Agency released its 2025 Global Energy and Climate Model Research Report, outlining pathways to net-zero emissions and clean energy adoption; the U.S. Energy Information Administration continues tracking regional data while CNN Americas News reports on related developments.
  
  The International Energy Agency (IEA) recently released the *2025 Global Energy and Climate Model Research Report*, presenting multiple development scenarios for the world's energy systems in English. Based on varying policy assumptions, technological development speeds, and climate targets, the report constructs a range of model scenarios. The "NZE Scenario" (Net Zero Emissions pathway) outlines a concrete route for the energy sector to achieve net-zero emissions by 2050 and limit warming to 1.5°C above pre-industrial levels by 2100; the "ACCESS Scenario" proposes a blueprint for universal electricity access by 2035 and universal clean cooking by 2040, focusing on a development direction that balances energy access with the energy transition.
  
  This report provides an important reference for countries formulating energy policies and has also prompted the Americas region to re-examine its own transition pathways.
  
  The U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), as the official U.S. energy data and analysis agency, continuously provides international energy information, rankings, data, and analytical reports covering energy production, consumption, and trade across the Americas. The EIA's data foundation complements the IEA's model projections, helping to clarify the actual progress and challenges of the Americas region in the energy transition process. CNN Americas News Channel continues to track real-time news and in-depth reporting across South America, Canada, Mexico, and other regions, covering policy changes, industry developments, and public issues from multiple dimensions, providing the public with diverse informational perspectives on energy and climate topics in the Americas.
  
  Notably, the IEA's regional cooperation center layout reflects positive developments in global energy governance. According to data from Taiwan's Ministry of Economic Affairs Energy Information Platform, Singapore established the first IEA regional cooperation center in 2024, aimed at promoting energy transition and information sharing in the Asia-Pacific region. Although the center is located in the Asia-Pacific, its experience and model also hold reference value for the Americas. As one of the world's most economically developed regions, the Americas include the United States and Canada, both developed nations with high human development indices and deep economic integration. Their energy consumption structure and carbon emission scale hold significant importance globally.
  
  How to construct energy transition strategies that meet the actual needs of the Americas within the framework of IEA model scenarios, combined with EIA regional data and CNN news tracking, has become an important current challenge. In the future, while the potential for direct application of renewable energy in the Americas will vary due to differences in local resources, the policy role in areas such as international carbon markets, energy storage technology, and smart grids is expected to become increasingly critical.

# IEA 發布 2025 能源氣候模型報告 美洲能源轉型路徑受矚目

> 國際能源署發布 2025 年全球能源與氣候模型研究報告，勾勒淨零排放與清潔能源普及路徑；美國能源資訊管理局持續追蹤區域資料，CNN 美洲新聞同步報導相關進展。

國際能源署（IEA）近日發布《2025 年全球能源和氣候模型研究報告》，以英文版呈現全球能源系統多元發展情景。該報告基於不同政策假設、技術發展速度與氣候目標，建構多組模型場景。其中「NZE 情景」（淨零排放路徑）勾勒出能源部門於 2050 年實現淨零排放、將 2100 年溫升控制在 1.5℃以內的具體路線；另一「ACCESS 情景」則提出 2035 年普及電力、2040 年普及清潔烹飪的行動藍圖，聚焦能源可及性與轉型並重的發展方向。
此報告為各國制定能源政策提供重要參考依據，亦引發美洲地區對自身轉型路徑的重新檢視。
美國能源資訊管理局（EIA）作為美國官方能源資料與分析機構，持續提供國際能源資訊、排名、資料與分析報告，涵蓋美洲各國能源生產、消費與貿易等面向。EIA 的資料基礎與 IEA 的模型預測形成互補，有助於釐清美洲地區在能源轉型過程中的實際進度與挑戰。CNN 美洲新聞頻道則持續追蹤南美洲、加拿大、墨西哥等地區的即時新聞與深度報導，涵蓋政策變動、產業發展與公共議題等多個層面，為公眾提供美洲能源與氣候議題的多元資訊視角。
值得注意的是，IEA 區域合作中心的佈局顯示全球能源治理的正向發展。根據臺灣經濟部能源資訊平臺資料，新加坡於 2024 年設立首座 IEA 區域合作中心，旨在促進亞太地區能源轉型與資訊共享。儘管該中心位於亞太，但其經驗與模式對美洲地區亦具參考價值。美洲作為全球經濟最發達地區之一，美國與加拿大均為已開發國家，人類發展指數高，經濟一體化程度深，其能源消費結構與碳排放規模在全球佔有重要地位。
如何在 IEA 模型情景的框架下，結合 EIA 的區域資料與 CNN 的新聞追蹤，建構符合美洲實際需求的能源轉型策略，成為當前重要課題。未來，再生能源在美洲直接應用的潛力雖因各地資源差異而有所不同，但在國際碳市場、儲能技術與智慧電網等領域的政策角色，預期將愈發關鍵。
