  # NASA's Spot The Station Program Continues to Promote International Space Station Observations
  
  > The U.S. National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) continues to promote the visibility and educational value of the International Space Station (ISS) to the public through the Spot The Station platform, welcoming public participation in observation activities.
  
  According to NASA's official website spotthestation.nasa.gov, the agency continues to promote the "Spot The Station" program, broadcasting ISS-related updates to the public through platforms such as Ustream, allowing people worldwide to follow the station's orbital position and observation opportunities in real time.
  
  The program aims to enhance public engagement with space exploration and provide educational and science resources, enabling students and astronomy enthusiasts to observe the International Space Station passing across the sky at specific times and locations.
  
  NASA states that the International Space Station continuously orbits the Earth, and its orbital altitude and position can be checked through the platform. Users simply enter their location, and the system provides forecasted observation times and directions, helping the public locate this artificial object during nighttime or dawn hours. This program is not merely an observation activity; it also integrates science education and public participation, enabling more people to understand the progress of space exploration and the importance of international cooperation.
  
  According to NASA's public information, the Spot The Station program has become one of the key channels for global space science outreach since its launch. Through live broadcasts and orbital forecasts, the public can participate in ISS observation activities and learn about the latest developments in space science. The program also collaborates with educational institutions across multiple countries, providing teachers and students with relevant teaching resources to further promote space science education.

# NASA Spot The Station 計畫持續推廣國際太空站觀測

> 美國國家航空暨太空總署（NASA）透過 Spot The Station 平臺，持續向公眾推廣國際太空站（ISS）的可見度與教育意義，歡迎民眾參與觀測活動。

美國國家航空暨太空總署（NASA）官方網站 spotthestation.nasa.gov 顯示，該署持續推動「Spot The Station」（尋找太空站）計畫，透過 Ustream 等平臺向公眾直播國際太空站（International Space Station, ISS）的相關動態，讓全球民眾得以即時關注太空站的軌道位置與觀測時機。
此計畫旨在提升公眾對太空探索的參與感，並提供教育與科普資源，讓學生與天文愛好者能夠在特定時間與地點觀賞國際太空站掠過天際的實況。
NASA 表示，國際太空站持續繞行地球，其軌道高度與位置可透過該平臺查詢。民眾只需輸入所在地區，系統即會提供預報的觀測時間與方向，協助公眾在夜間或黎明時分尋找這個人造天體。此計畫不僅是觀測活動，也結合了科學教育與公眾參與，讓更多人瞭解太空探索的進展與國際合作的重要性。
根據 NASA 的公開資訊，Spot The Station 計畫自啟動以來，已成為全球太空科普的重要管道之一。透過即時直播與軌道預報，公眾得以參與國際太空站的觀測活動，並瞭解太空科學的最新發展。此計畫也與全球多國的教育機構合作，提供教師與學生相關的教學資源，進一步推廣太空科學教育。
