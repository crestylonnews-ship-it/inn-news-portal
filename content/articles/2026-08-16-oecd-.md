  # OECD Education Report Focuses on Middle East and Oceania; Australian Education System Reform Draws Attention
  
  > The OECD released its 2025 Education Report while EducationUSA launched a global recruitment guide, with analysis of Australia's education system showing deepening Sino-Australian educational cooperation and growing international attention on Pacific education development.
  
  The Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) recently released the *Education at a Glance 2025* report, one of the most authoritative sources in the global education field, providing detailed insights into the school structures, funding allocation, and educational performance of OECD member countries and partner economies. The report contains over a hundred charts and offers in-depth analysis of institutional outputs and learning outcomes, with particular attention to education development trends in the Middle East and Oceania. According to the report, Oceania countries such as Australia stand out in higher education internationalization indicators, while some Middle Eastern countries continue to increase education budgets to address the challenges posed by growing youth populations.
  
  The OECD emphasizes that this report serves as a key reference for policymakers, helping countries compare the strengths and weaknesses of their education systems and formulate improvement strategies.
  
  EducationUSA, an organization under the U.S. Department of State, simultaneously released the *2024 Global Guide*, its annual flagship publication designed to guide U.S. colleges and universities in planning international student recruitment strategies. The guide clearly delineates regional sections covering six major areas, including the Middle East and North Africa, East Asia and the Pacific, and Europe and Inner Asia, and provides education market analyses for each region. According to the guide, the number of students from the Middle East pursuing education in the United States has risen steadily in recent years, with a significant increase in the proportion of students in engineering and business fields, particularly as Gulf countries promote education diversification policies.
  
  Regarding Oceania, the guide notes that Australia and New Zealand have become important partners for U.S. institutions in the Pacific region, with ongoing deepening of academic exchanges and student swap programs. EducationUSA emphasizes that the guide is based on frontline data from its overseas offices, helping U.S. institutions more precisely reach their target student populations.
  
  As an OECD member and a participant in the Pacific Islands Forum, Australia's education system has attracted significant international attention. According to official information from the Australian government, the Education and Research Section of the Australian Embassy in China has maintained long-term exchanges with China's Ministry of Education and various universities, promoting bilateral educational cooperation. Meanwhile, the educational institution jris.com recently published the *2026 Complete Analysis of the Australian Education System*, which details the academic pathways from preschool to doctoral levels in Australia and compares the differences between the Taiwanese and Australian education systems. The analysis points out that Australia's Foundation preparatory programs and university diploma courses provide diverse pathways for international students, particularly attracting students from Southeast Asia and the Middle East.
  
  Wikipedia data shows that Australia consistently ranks among the top in global education indices, and its education export industry makes significant contributions to the national economy. However, different sources present varying data on the specific flow of Middle Eastern students to Oceania. The OECD report focuses on overall trend analysis, while the EducationUSA guide offers more specific recruitment recommendations, with the two complementing each other.
  
  A comprehensive review of various sources reveals that educational cooperation between the Middle East and Oceania is gaining new momentum. OECD data shows that Oceania countries outperform the global average in the proportion of education spending relative to GDP, while some Middle Eastern countries are actively introducing international education institutions to improve the quality of their domestic education. Australia's education system reforms and the deepening of Sino-Australian educational exchanges reflect increasingly frequent regional educational interactions. Future development will depend on policy coordination and data transparency among countries, and continued monitoring by international organizations and official institutions will help clarify the actual impact of regional education trends.

# OECD教育報告聚焦中東與大洋洲 澳洲學制改革引關注

> 經濟合作暨發展組織發布2025年教育報告，EducationUSA同步推出全球招生指南，澳洲學制解析顯示中澳教育合作持續深化，太平洋地區教育發展受國際矚目。

經濟合作暨發展組織（OECD）於近日發布《Education at a Glance 2025》報告，此為全球教育領域最具權威性的資料來源之一，詳細呈現OECD會員國及夥伴經濟體的學校結構、經費配置與教育表現。報告內含逾百項圖表，深入分析教育機構的產出成效與學習影響，特別關注中東與大洋洲地區的教育發展趨勢。根據該報告，大洋洲國家如澳洲在高等教育國際化指標上表現突出，而中東部分國家則持續增加教育預算以應對青年人口成長的挑戰。
OECD強調，此份報告為政策制定者提供關鍵參考，有助於各國比較教育體系優劣並擬定改進策略。
美國國務院旗下EducationUSA組織同步發布《2024 Global Guide》，此為該機構年度旗艦出版物，主要指導美國大專院校如何規劃國際學生招募策略。指南中明確劃分割槽域，包含中東與北非、東亞與太平洋、歐洲與內亞等六大區域，並提供各區域的教育市場分析。根據該指南，中東地區學生赴美留學人數近年穩步上升，尤其在海灣國家推動教育多元化政策下，工程與商業領域的學生比例顯著增加。
大洋洲方面，指南指出澳洲與紐西蘭成為美國院校在太平洋區域的重要合作夥伴，雙方在學術交流與學生互換計畫上持續深化。EducationUSA強調，此指南基於各駐外館處的一線資料，有助於美國院校更精準地接觸目標生源。
澳洲作為OECD會員國及太平洋島國論壇成員，其教育體系深受國際關注。根據澳洲政府官方資訊，澳洲駐華大使館教育與研究處長期與中國教育部及各大學保持交流，推動雙邊教育合作。同時，教育機構jris.com於近期發布《2026澳洲學制完整解析》，詳細說明澳洲從學前教育至博士階段的升學途徑，並比較臺灣與澳洲學制差異。該解析指出，澳洲的Foundation先修班與大學文憑課程為國際學生提供多元升學管道，尤其吸引東南亞與中東地區學生。
維基百科資料顯示，澳洲在全球教育指數排名中持續位居前列，其教育出口產業對國家經濟貢獻顯著。不過，不同來源對中東學生流向大洋洲的具體資料尚存差異，OECD報告側重整體趨勢分析，而EducationUSA指南則提供更具體的招募建議，兩者互為補充。
綜合各來源觀察，中東與大洋洲的教育合作正呈現新動能。OECD資料顯示大洋洲國家在教育經費佔GDP比重上優於全球平均，而中東部分國家則積極引進國際教育機構以提升本土教育品質。澳洲學制改革與中澳教育交流的深化，反映區域間教育互動日益頻繁。未來發展將取決於各國政策協調與資料透明度，國際組織與官方機構的持續監測將有助於釐清區域教育趨勢的實際影響。
