  # Iran Threatens Attacks on US Tech Firms as Middle East AI Investment Faces Geopolitical Risks
  
  > The Iranian Revolutionary Guard has designated 18 US technology companies as attack targets, creating a stark contrast with the accelerating Middle East AI investments by Microsoft, OpenAI, and others, as the region's AI infrastructure investments face geopolitical risks.
  
  The Middle East is becoming a new focal point for the global artificial intelligence industry, but geopolitical tensions are also bringing uncertainty to related investments. According to unwire.pro, the Iranian Revolutionary Guard (IRGC) issued a declaration on March 31 designating 18 US technology and defense-related companies with operations in the Middle East—including Apple, Microsoft, Google, and Nvidia—as "legitimate military targets," and threatened to launch retaliatory attacks starting at 8 PM Tehran time on April 1, while also demanding that employees of these companies and residents within a 1-kilometer radius of their facilities evacuate immediately.
  
  This move has sparked international concern over the security of Middle East AI infrastructure.
  
  In contrast to Iran's warning, US tech giants continue to expand their AI footprint in the Middle East. According to forecastock.tw, Microsoft (MSFT) recently announced it will increase its total investment in the United Arab Emirates (UAE) to US$15 billion by the end of 2029, with the majority of funds focused on AI data center construction—a move seen as a dual breakthrough in US technology and geopolitics in the region. Microsoft Vice Chairman and President Brad also issued a statement regarding the investment plan.
  
  Meanwhile, wantgoo.com reported that in early 2025, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman met with AI fund MGX in Abu Dhabi, revealing that global tech capital is flowing rapidly toward the Middle East and the AI sector, as the region accelerates its佈局 of the artificial intelligence industry and becomes a new core for global tech investment.
  
  Synthesizing reports from multiple media outlets, Middle East AI development presents a clear contradiction. On one hand, Gulf sovereign wealth funds and international tech giants are actively investing to drive local AI infrastructure construction; on the other hand, the IRGC's threat declaration highlights the region's potential security risks. unwire.pro notes that the Middle East's AI infrastructure investments, worth hundreds of billions of dollars, face geopolitical risks. Although companies like Microsoft and OpenAI continue to expand their investments, Iran's designation of 18 US tech firms as attack targets could affect the stability of the future investment environment.

# 伊朗威脅攻擊美科技企業 中東 AI 投資面臨地緣風險

> 伊朗革命衛隊將 18 間美國科技企業列為攻擊目標，與微軟、OpenAI 等加速中東 AI 投資形成強烈對比，中東 AI 基建投資面臨地緣政治風險。

中東地區正成為全球人工智慧產業的新焦點，但地緣政治緊張局勢也為相關投資帶來不確定性。根據 unwire.pro 報導，伊朗革命衛隊（IRGC）於 3 月 31 日發表宣告，將 Apple、Microsoft、Google、Nvidia 等 18 間在中東設有業務的美國科技及國防相關企業列為「合法軍事目標」，並揚言自 4 月 1 日德黑蘭時間晚上 8 時起展開報復攻擊，同時要求相關公司員工及設施方圓 1 公里範圍內的居民即時撤離。
此舉引發國際社會對中東 AI 基建安全的關注。
與伊朗的警告形成對比的是，美國科技巨頭持續擴大在中東的 AI 版圖。根據 forecastock.tw 報導，微軟（Microsoft，MSFT）近日宣佈至 2029 年底前將把阿拉伯聯合大公國（UAE）總投資提高至 150 億美元，其中大部分資金聚焦於 AI 資料中心建設，被視為美國在中東新興科技與地緣政治的雙重突破。微軟副董事長兼總裁 Brad 亦對此投資計畫發表相關說明。
另一方面，wantgoo.com 報導指出，2025 年初 OpenAI 首席執行長奧特曼（Sam Altman）在阿布達比與 AI 基金 MGX 會面，揭示全球科技資本正快速流向中東及 AI 領域，中東正加速佈局人工智慧產業，成為全球科技投資的新核心。
綜合多家媒體報導，中東 AI 發展呈現明顯的矛盾現象。一方面，海灣主權基金與國際科技巨頭積極投資，推動當地 AI 基礎設施建設；另一方面，伊朗革命衛隊的威脅宣告凸顯該地區潛在的安全風險。unwire.pro 指出，中東 AI 基建數千億美元投資正面臨地緣政治風險。雖然微軟、OpenAI 等企業持續擴大投資，但伊朗將 18 間美國科技企業列為攻擊目標的舉動，可能影響未來投資環境的穩定性。
目前尚待確認的是，伊朗革命衛隊的威脅是否會具體執行，以及相關企業是否已啟動應變措施。
