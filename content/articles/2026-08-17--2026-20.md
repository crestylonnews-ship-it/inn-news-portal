  # EU Strengthens Global Health Leadership Role, 2026-2030 European Work Programme Launched
  
  > The EU coordinates its global health strategy through a "Team Europe approach," while WHO Europe launches its second work programme for 2026-2030, with multiple health organizations and civil society groups supporting the integration of health at the core of European resilience.
  
  The EU's role in global health governance continues to attract attention. According to Policy Brief No. 57 published by the WHO European Observatory on Health Systems and Policies, the EU, as one of the world's largest markets, a major aid donor, a leader in healthcare innovation, and a trade powerhouse, exerts significant direct and indirect influence on global health and health policy. The brief emphasizes that the EU continues to drive improvements in the global health system through its clear global health policies and its market and regulatory influence.
  
  An article published in the British Medical Journal (BMJ) analyzes how the US decision to withdraw from the WHO constitutes a major setback for global health security and governance, but also presents an opportunity for other countries to expand their leadership roles. Authors Óscar Fernández and Hylke Dijkstra argue that the EU's multilateral nature can serve as an advantage, enabling it to seek a more active leadership position in global health security and governance. The official website of the European Commission also states that EU health policy aims to protect Europeans from cross-border health threats and ensure access to quality healthcare and affordable medical supplies.
  
  At the regional level, non-state actors such as the World Federation of Surgical Associations (WFSA) and the European Public Health Association (EUPHA) issued a joint statement at the 75th session of the WHO European Regional Committee regarding the second European work programme for 2026-2030. The programme, themed "Acting Together for Health," has been warmly welcomed by the signatories. The statement notes that the new programme correctly places health at the core of European resilience and underscores the importance of cross-sectoral collaboration.
  
  Analysis indicates that the EU adopts a "Team Europe approach" when formulating its global health strategy to coordinate actions across European countries and the EU level, while placing emphasis on cooperation with stakeholders such as the private sector and civil society organizations. This strategy also stresses alignment with the EU's own values, comparative advantages, and diplomatic geopolitical objectives. Taiwan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs has previously introduced the EU Health Policy Information Platform, which aims to support the Directorate-General for Health and Food Safety in developing evidence-based policies, provide information for national policy-making to enhance the quality and sustainability of health systems, and promote cooperation and the exchange of best practices at the EU level.

# 歐盟強化全球衛生領導角色 2026-2030歐洲工作計畫啟動

> 歐盟透過「團隊歐洲方法」協調全球衛生戰略，WHO歐洲區啟動2026-2030年第二份工作計畫，多國衛生組織與民間團體共同支援將健康置於歐洲韌性核心。

歐盟在全球衛生治理中的角色持續受到關注。根據世界衛生組織歐洲健康系統與政策觀察站發布的第57號政策簡報指出，歐盟作為世界最大市場之一、主要援助捐贈國、醫療照護創新者與貿易強權，對全球衛生與衛生政策具有深遠的直接與間接影響。該簡報強調，歐盟透過明確的全球衛生政策以及其市場與監管影響力，持續推動全球衛生體系的改善。
英國醫學期刊《BMJ》刊載文章分析，美國決定退出世界衛生組織對全球衛生安全與治理構成重大挫折，但也為其他國家擴大領導角色提供契機。作者Óscar Fernández與Hylke Dijkstra認為，歐盟的多邊性質可成為優勢，使其在全球衛生安全與治理中尋求更積極的領導地位。歐盟委員會官方網站亦說明，歐盟衛生政策旨在保護歐洲民眾免受跨境健康威脅，並確保取得優質醫療照護與負擔得起的醫療用品。
在區域層面，世界外科協會（WFSA）與歐洲公共衛生協會（EUPHA）等非國家行為體，於WHO歐洲委員會第75屆會議上就2026-2030年第二份歐洲工作計畫發表聯合宣告。該計畫以「攜手行動，共促健康」為主題，獲得簽署方熱烈歡迎。宣告指出，新計畫正確地將健康置於歐洲韌性的核心位置，並強調跨部門合作的重要性。
分析指出，歐盟在制定全球衛生戰略時，採用「團隊歐洲方法」以協調歐洲各國與歐盟層級的行動，並重視與私營部門、公民社會組織等利害關係人的合作。此策略亦強調與歐盟自身理念、比較優勢與外交地緣目標的結合。臺灣外交部駐外館處亦曾介紹歐盟衛生政策資訊平臺，該平臺旨在支援衛生及食品安全總署制定基於證據的政策，為提升衛生系統質量與永續性的國家政策制定提供資訊，並促進歐盟層面的合作與最佳實踐交流。
