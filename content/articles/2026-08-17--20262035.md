  # African Union Advances Education Strategy, 2026–2035 Plan Runs Parallel to Learning Crisis Response
  
  > The African Union continues to advance the Continental Education for Africa Strategy, launching consultations on the implementation plan for the 2026–2035 edition in May 2025; at the same year's triennial conference in Accra, Ghana, ministers and civil society organizations from across the continent initiated three flagship programs to address the foundational learning crisis.
  
  The African Union continues to place education at the core of regional development. According to AU documents translated by the Shanghai Normal University International Education Cooperation website, the AU Commission formulated the Continental Education for Africa Strategy (2016–2025) in 2015, with the goal of establishing a quality education and training system that cultivates human resources grounded in African core values and capable of realizing the vision of the AU's Agenda 2063, thereby supporting the sustainable development objectives of the African continent. The strategy spans a ten-year period, laying the framework for subsequent policy alignment.
  
  Entering a new decade, the AU has initiated a strategic transition. According to a report on the Shanghai Normal University China-Africa Cooperation Research Center website, on May 15, 2025, the AU Commission convened a two-day technical working group meeting in Nairobi, Kenya, to deliberate on the implementation plan for the Continental Education for Africa Strategy (2026–2035), abbreviated as CESA 26-35. The meeting aimed to summarize the outcomes of the previous phase of implementation and to develop concrete pathways for the new decade, signaling that the AU is moving from a policy-making stage toward an implementation stage.
  
  Education issues continue to gain momentum at the AU's senior-level meetings. A report translated by Sohu.com indicates that on February 17, 2024, the AU held its 37th Summit of Heads of State and Government at its headquarters in Ethiopia, with the theme "Education for Africa in the 21st Century: Building Resilient Education Systems to Increase Access to Inclusive, Lifelong, Quality and Relevant Learning for All." The summit placed equal emphasis on education resilience and learning quality, reflecting African countries' focus on the resilience of their education systems.
  
  Running parallel to strategic planning are concrete actions addressing the learning crisis. According to a report on the Shanghai Normal University Shenzhou Scholars website, on October 29, 2025, the Association for the Development of Education in Africa (ADEA) hosted its triennial conference in Accra, Ghana, where education ministers, philanthropists, and civil society organizations from across Africa jointly launched three flagship programs aimed at accelerating the resolution of the continent's learning crisis and transforming the demographic dividend into economic development momentum. The report emphasizes that these three initiatives are Africa-led and focus on key dimensions of the education sector.
  
  UNESCO also maintains a long-term presence in education cooperation in Africa. According to The Paper, UNESCO launched its Africa Priority Programme in 1985 and established its Africa Division in 1996 to build long-standing and stable partnerships, providing policy advisory services, education assistance, and resource support. The involvement of this international organization complements the AU's autonomously driven strategy.
  
  Synthesizing the various sources, the AU's education strategy has transitioned from 2016–2025 to 2026–2035, with clear policy continuity; meanwhile, urgent measures to address the learning crisis and support from international organizations are advancing in parallel, indicating that Africa's education agenda is progressing along multiple tracks, with the effectiveness of subsequent implementation yet to be seen.

# 非盟接力推進教育戰略 2026–2035規劃與學習危機對策並行

> 非洲聯盟接續推動《非洲大陸教育戰略》，2025年5月啟動2026–2035年版實施規劃磋商；同年10月迦納阿克拉三年展上，各國部長與民間組織啟動三項旗艦計畫，以破解基礎學習危機。

非洲聯盟持續將教育議題列為區域發展核心。根據上海師範大學國際教育合作網站轉譯的非盟檔案顯示，非盟委員會於2015年制定《非洲大陸教育戰略（2016–2025年）》，目標是建立有品質的教育與培訓系統，培養具備非洲核心價值觀、能實現非盟《2063年議程》願景的人力資源，以配合非洲大陸的永續發展目標。該戰略涵蓋十年期，為後續政策接軌奠定框架。
進入新十年，非盟已展開戰略更迭。上海師範大學中非合作研究中心網站報導，2025年5月15日，非盟委員會在肯亞內羅畢召開技術工作組會議，針對《非洲大陸教育戰略（2026–2035年）》（簡稱CESA 26-35）的實施規劃進行兩日磋商。會議旨在總結前一階段執行成果，並研擬新十年度的具體推動路徑，顯示非盟正由政策制定階段邁向落實階段。
教育議題亦在非盟高層會議中持續發酵。搜狐網轉譯的報導指出，2024年2月17日，非盟於衣索比亞總部召開第37屆國家與政府首腦會議，主題聚焦「提供二十一世紀的非洲教育：建設具復原力的教育系統，增加非洲全納、終身、優質且相關學習的取得機會」。該次峰會將教育復原力與學習品質並重，反映非洲各國對教育體系韌性的重視。
與戰略規劃並進的是針對學習危機的具體行動。上海師範大學神州學人網站報導，2025年10月29日，非洲教育發展協會（ADEA）於迦納阿克拉舉辦三年展，非洲各國教育部長、慈善家及民間社會組織共同啟動三項旗艦計畫，目標是加速解決非洲大陸的學習危機，將人口紅利轉化為經濟發展動力。該報導強調此三項舉措由非洲主導，分別聚焦教育領域的關鍵面向。
聯合國教科文組織在非洲教育合作上亦有長期佈局。澎湃新聞網指出，教科文組織自1985年啟動非洲優先計畫，並於1996年成立非洲部，以建立長期穩定的夥伴關係，提供政策諮詢、教育援助與資源扶持。此國際組織的介入，與非盟自主推動的戰略形成互補。
綜合各來源，非盟教育戰略由2016–2025年過渡至2026–2035年，政策延續性明確；同時，學習危機的緊急對策與國際組織支援同步進行，顯示非洲教育議題正朝多軌並行方向推進，後續落實成效有待觀察。
