  # Middle East AI Market Grows 35% Annually as Hundred-Billion-Dollar Blue Ocean Sees Fierce Competition
  
  > Reports from China Research and Consulting (CRR) and GII show the Middle East AI market exceeded $20 billion in 2025 and is projected to reach $100 billion by 2030, as OpenAI competitors pursue technological breakthroughs.
  
  According to the "2025-2030 Middle East Artificial Intelligence Industry Development Deep Research and Future Trend Forecast Report" released by China Research and Consulting (CRR) Industry Research Institute, as of 2025, the Middle East AI market size has surpassed $20 billion and is projected to expand at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 35% over the next five years, breaking through the $100 billion mark by 2030. The report notes that the Middle East region is treating AI as a core national strategy, with Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, and Qatar continuously driving large-scale digital infrastructure development, attracting global technology companies to establish regional headquarters and R&D centers.
  
  Meanwhile, a market analysis released in 2026 by Japanese firm Global Information Inc. (GII) shows that the Middle East digital transformation market was valued at $58.3 billion in 2025 and is projected to grow from $71.94 billion in 2026 to $205.83 billion in 2031, with a CAGR of 23.4% during the forecast period (2026-2031). The two reports differ in market size definitions and statistical benchmarks: CRR focuses on the "artificial intelligence" niche segment, while GII covers the broader "digital transformation" market, including integrated applications such as cloud computing, big data, IoT, and AI.
  
  Analysts believe the two sets of data are not contradictory but rather reflect different research institutions' approaches to defining market boundaries.
  
  Reuters reported in November 2024 that OpenAI's competitors are seeking to develop smarter AI systems through new approaches, as current mainstream methods have encountered technical bottlenecks. This trend resonates with the rapid expansion of the Middle East market: the region is not only a hotspot for consumer-side demand but also an important battleground for AI technology R&D and commercial applications. Middle Eastern sovereign wealth funds and tech giants are actively investing in local AI enterprises and cross-border collaborative projects, attempting to gain the upper hand in the hundred-billion-dollar blue ocean.
  
  Experts observe that the Middle East AI industry faces challenges such as uneven infrastructure, talent shortages, and immature regulatory frameworks, but government-driven transformation plans like "Vision 2030" have attracted over a hundred international technology companies to establish regional footholds. Over the next five years, whether the region's AI market can sustain annual growth above 30% will depend on the effectiveness of technology implementation, the depth of cross-border cooperation, and global supply chain shifts.

# 中東 AI 市場規模年增 35% 千億美元藍海現爭奪戰

> 中研普華與 GII 報告顯示，中東 AI 市場 2025 年規模已突破 200 億美元，預計 2030 年將達千億美元；OpenAI 競爭對手正尋求技術突破。

根據中研普華產業研究院發布的《2025-2030 年中東人工智慧行業發展深度調研與未來趨勢預測報告》，截至 2025 年，中東人工智慧市場規模已突破 200 億美元，預計未來五年將以年均 35% 的複合增長率（CAGR）擴張，到 2030 年將突破 1000 億美元大關。該報告指出，中東地區正將 AI 視為國家戰略核心，沙烏地阿拉伯、阿拉伯聯合大公國與卡達等國持續推動大規模數位基礎建設，吸引全球科技企業設立區域總部與研發中心。
與此同時，日商環球訊息有限公司（GII）於 2026 年發布的市場分析則顯示，中東數位轉型市場在 2025 年估值為 583 億美元，預估從 2026 年的 719.4 億美元成長至 2031 年的 2058.3 億美元，預測期間（2026-2031 年）複合年增長率為 23.4%。兩份報告在市場規模定義與統計基準上存在差異：中研普華聚焦於「人工智慧」細分領域，而 GII 涵蓋更廣泛的「數位轉型」市場，包括雲端、大資料、物聯網與 AI 等整合應用。
分析認為，兩者資料並不相悖，而是反映不同研究機構對市場邊界的界定方式不同。
路透社（Reuters）於 2024 年 11 月報導指出，OpenAI 的競爭對手正尋求透過新途徑開發更智慧的 AI 系統，因當前主流方法已遭遇技術瓶頸。此趨勢與中東市場的快速擴張形成呼應：該地區不僅是消費端需求熱區，也成為 AI 技術研發與商業化應用的重要戰場。中東主權基金與科技巨頭正積極投資本土 AI 企業與跨國合作專案，試圖在千億美元藍海中取得先機。
專家觀察指出，中東 AI 產業面臨基礎建設不均衡、人才短缺與監管框架尚未成熟等挑戰，但政府強力推動的「Vision 2030」等轉型計畫，已吸引超過百家國際科技公司設立區域據點。未來五年，該地區 AI 市場能否持續維持 30% 以上年增長率，將取決於技術落地成效、跨國合作深度與全球供應鏈變動。
