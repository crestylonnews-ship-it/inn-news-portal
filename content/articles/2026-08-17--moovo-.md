Although the Changhua station accounts for a very small portion of MOOVO's overall revenue, it serves as an important foundation for Yunding Technology's current position as Europe's largest B2B "last-mile delivery" electric bicycle rental subscription platform. This development demonstrates that Taiwan's green transportation technology is gradually expanding into the European market, carving out a place in Europe's last-mile logistics sector.
  
  Yunding Technology is building on its Taiwan-based operational experience, transforming the practical results from the Changhua region into a competitive advantage in the European market. In recent years, European countries have actively promoted carbon reduction policies, and urban logistics last-mile delivery faces environmental regulation pressures. The electric bicycle rental subscription model aligns well with this trend. MOOVO's docking station system technology and operational data have become key assets for entering the European market.
  
  Global Views Monthly explored Taiwan's path forward on the international stage in its October 2021 issue, noting that Taiwanese enterprises need to find new avenues. Yunding Technology's expansion into the European market is a concrete practice of this trend. Through technology export and business model replication, the company is forging a new cross-border operational path despite the limited scale of Taiwan's domestic market. This case reflects the strategic thinking of Taiwan's new enterprises as they move from local to international markets.

# 臺灣電動腳踏車品牌 MOOVO 拓展歐洲市場，運點科技佈局最後一哩外送

> 臺灣慧樁系統滿意度高達 97.7%，運點科技以彰化據點為基礎，拓展歐洲 B2B 最後一哩外送電動腳踏車租賃訂閱平臺。

根據《BNext 商業趨勢》報導，臺灣電動腳踏車共享系統 MOOVO 的慧樁系統獲得民眾高度評價，滿意度高達 97.7％。雖然彰化據點在 MOOVO 整體營收中佔比很低，卻是運點科技如今成為歐洲最大 B2B「最後一哩外送（last-mile delivery）」電動腳踏車租賃訂閱平臺的重要基礎。這項發展顯示臺灣綠色運具技術正逐步拓展歐洲市場，在歐洲最後一哩物流領域佔有一席之地。
運點科技以臺灣本土營運經驗為根基，將彰化地區的實際運作成果轉化為歐洲市場的競爭優勢。歐洲各國近年積極推動減碳政策，城市物流最後一哩配送面臨環保法規壓力，電動腳踏車租賃訂閱模式正好符合此趨勢。MOOVO 的慧樁系統技術與營運資料，成為進入歐洲市場的重要籌碼。
遠見雜誌曾於 2021 年 10 月號探討臺灣在國際間的自處之道，指出臺灣企業需尋找新出路。運點科技拓展歐洲市場正是此趨勢的具體實踐，透過技術輸出與商業模式複製，在臺灣原市場規模有限的情況下，開拓跨國營運新軌道。此案例反映臺灣新創企業從本土走向國際的戰略思維。
