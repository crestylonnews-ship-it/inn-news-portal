  A research team led by Associate Professor Liang Li-lin from the Institute of Public Health at Yang Ming Jiao Tong University recently published a study revealing a significant association between physical fitness and longevity in older adults. The study found that older adults in the top 20% of physical fitness had a 61% lower risk of death compared to other groups. Liang further explained that even older adults with initially poor physical condition can still derive substantial health benefits by gradually increasing their activity levels and progressively improving muscle strength, balance, and mobility. This research provides concrete exercise intervention guidelines for the field of public health and aligns with the global direction of promoting healthy aging.
  
  At the World Health Organization (WHO), the Strategic Partnership for Health Security and Emergency Response (SPH) portal system continues to operate, providing comprehensive national profiles to member states. The portal covers key information on countries' health security and emergency response preparedness capabilities. For member states in the Middle East, this platform helps assess their current status and gaps in areas such as pandemic response, disease surveillance, and healthcare system resilience.
  
  Through this mechanism, the WHO strengthens regional information sharing and technical cooperation to address cross-border public health threats.
  
  In the Oceania region, Australia's higher education system is also actively responding to the demand for public health professionals. Educational institutions have launched the Master of Nutrition and Population Health program, which spans the disciplines of nutrition science and public health nutrition, aiming to cultivate health professionals with interdisciplinary capabilities. Graduates can pursue careers in health policy development, community health promotion, chronic disease prevention, and the food industry, reflecting Australia's ongoing investment and strategic planning in public health education.

# 陽明交大研究揭體適能與長壽關聯 WHO強化中東健康安全合作

> 陽明交大研究指出長者體適能前20%死亡風險降61%；WHO透過SPH門戶強化成員國健康安全能力；澳洲多校開設營養與公共衛生碩士學位，培育跨領域人才。

陽明交通大學公共衛生研究所副教授梁立霖團隊近期發表研究指出，體適能與長者壽命存在顯著關聯。該研究發現，體適能位居前20%的長者，其死亡風險較其他群體大幅降低61%。梁立霖進一步說明，即使原本體能狀況不佳的長者，只要開始增加活動量，逐步改善肌力、平衡及行動能力，仍能獲得實質的健康益處。此項研究為公共衛生領域提供了具體的運動介入指引，也呼應了全球促進高齡健康的重要方向。
在世界衛生組織（WHO）方面，其「健康安全與緊急應變戰略夥伴關係」（Strategic Partnership for Health Security and Emergency Response）門戶系統持續運作，為各會員國提供綜合性國家檔案。該門戶系統涵蓋各國在健康安全與緊急應變準備能力方面的關鍵資訊，對於中東地區成員國而言，此平臺有助於檢視自身在疫情應變、疾病監測及醫療體系韌性等方面的現況與不足。
WHO透過此機制，強化區域間的資訊共享與技術合作，以應對跨國公共衛生威脅。
在大洋洲地區，澳洲高等教育體系亦積極回應公共衛生人才需求。相關教育機構推出「營養與人口健康碩士」（Master of Nutrition and Population Health）學位課程，該課程跨越營養科學與公共衛生營養兩個專業領域，旨在培育具備跨領域能力的健康人才。畢業生可投入健康政策制定、社群健康促進、慢性疾病預防及食品產業等方向，反映澳洲在公共衛生教育上的持續投資與佈局。
綜合上述資訊，體適能研究、WHO健康安全機制與澳洲公共衛生教育，分別從個人健康促進、國際衛生治理及專業人才培育三個層面，呈現當前全球公共衛生領域的重要進展。不同媒體與機構的資訊相互呼應，顯示跨國合作與科學實證在應對公共衛生挑戰中的關鍵角色。後續觀察將聚焦於各國政策落實情形及相關研究的長期追蹤結果。
