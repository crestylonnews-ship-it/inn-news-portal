  # Africa's 2024 Political and Security Landscape Complex, Multilateral Cooperation Continues to Deepen
  
  > In 2024, Africa's political and security situation reached its highest complexity in nearly a decade, with elections reshaping regional dynamics; China-Africa cooperation frameworks expanded, and Africa demonstrated autonomous development space within a multipolar order.
  
  In 2024, the political and security landscape across the African continent underwent significant changes. According to Tencent News, security hotspots in Africa that year exhibited a resonant pattern, with relations among countries showing divergence and reorganization, and overall political security complexity rising to a near-decade high. Elections emerged as a key catalyst in reshaping regional dynamics, while countries displayed varying approaches to cooperation in addressing security challenges.
  
  On the international cooperation front, China-Africa relations continued to deepen. According to Radio France Internationale (RFI), China established the "Global Security Initiative Research Center" under the Chinese Academy of International Studies affiliated with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and released its first assessment report in July 2024, indicating China's active promotion of implementing its Global Security Initiative in Africa. The China-Africa Cooperation Forum — Beijing Action Plan (2025–2027), published by China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, further clarified cooperation directions, including encouraging African media to join the Belt and Road News Cooperation Alliance, supporting capacity building for African broadcasting and media outlets, and promoting exchanges and cooperation among new media practitioners in China and Africa.
  
  Africa has demonstrated autonomy amid a changing world order. An article from the Peking University China-Foreign Humanities Exchange Research Base noted that Africa possesses the Agenda 2063 and other subregional agendas, and is fully capable of participating in formulating next-step cooperation plans with countries such as the United States, rather than passively accepting external arrangements. On January 15, 2026, the Brookings Institution released its report "Africa Outlook 2026: Priorities and Action Recommendations," systematically outlining the multiple challenges Africa faces — including the contraction of global development assistance, debt pressures, and dramatic demographic shifts — and proposing an action framework across six chapters spanning 128 pages.
  
  A comprehensive review of various sources reveals that in 2024, Africa faced both the challenges of deteriorating security conditions and shrinking external assistance, while also expanding its development space through multilateral cooperation mechanisms. As the international order undergoes restructuring, African nations are progressively strengthening their capacity to formulate autonomous development agendas.

# 非洲2024年政治安全形勢複雜 多邊合作持續深化

> 2024年非洲政治安全形勢達近十年新高，大選重塑區域格局；中非合作框架擴充套件，非洲在多極化秩序中展現自主發展空間。

2024年，非洲大陸的政治安全格局出現顯著變動。根據騰訊新聞報導，該年度非洲安全熱點呈現共振態勢，各國間關係產生分化與重組，整體政治安全複雜度攀升至近十年來的新高峰。大選成為重塑區域格局的重要催化劑，同時各國在應對安全挑戰時展現出不同的合作取向。
在國際合作層面，中國與非洲的關係持續深化。法國國際廣播電臺（RFI）報導指出，中國在外交部下屬的中國國際問題研究院內設立了「全球安全倡議研究中心」，並於2024年7月發布首份評估報告，顯示中國正積極推動其全球安全倡議在非洲的落實。中國外交部公佈的《中非合作論壇—北京行動計劃（2025-2027）》進一步明確了合作方向，包括鼓勵非洲媒體加入「一帶一路」新聞合作聯盟，支援非洲廣電媒體能力建設，以及推動中非新媒體從業人員交流合作。
非洲在變動的世界秩序中展現出自主性。北京大學中外人文交流研究基地文章指出，非洲擁有《2063年議程》及其他次區域議程，完全有能力參與制定與美國等國家的下一步合作計劃，而非被動接受外部安排。美國布魯金斯學會於2026年1月15日發布《非洲展望2026：優先事項與行動建議》報告，系統闡述非洲面臨全球發展援助收縮、債務壓力及人口結構劇變等多重挑戰，提出六章128頁的行動框架建議。
綜合各來源觀察，2024年非洲既面臨安全形勢惡化與外部援助收縮的挑戰，也透過多邊合作機制拓展發展空間。非洲國家在國際秩序重組過程中，正逐步強化自主發展議程的制定能力。
