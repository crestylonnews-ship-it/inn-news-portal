  # Regional Integration in the Americas and U.S. Trade Policy Become 2025 Economic Focus
  
  > The global economy in 2025 faces challenges from the new U.S. administration's trade policies, geopolitics, and regional integration. Experts note that progress toward American integration has been slow, and while the U.S. retains the ability to stabilize the global economy, China's trade surplus now exceeds the combined surpluses of Germany and Japan in the 1980s.
  
  The January issue (Volume 48, No. 1) of the *Taiwan Economic Journal* published a special economic report by Fang Chun-te, indicating that the global economy in 2025 is expected to maintain a steady trajectory, though it must still contend with multiple challenges including the policy direction of the new U.S. administration, ongoing geopolitical conflicts, and financial market instability. The report emphasizes that the new U.S. administration is expected to pursue tougher trade and immigration policies, which could trigger a new round of trade wars and increase market uncertainty. Additionally, the report notes that a ceasefire in the Middle East and the Russia-Ukraine war would help alleviate some economic pressures, though the overall outlook remains highly uncertain.
  
  Meanwhile, an article reposted by Peking University's School of International Studies, titled "Advancing Regional Integration in the Americas to Revive Global Economic Leadership," points out that the North American Free Trade Agreement (NAFTA) once helped North America maintain its global economic leadership, yet no broader regional economic integration initiative has emerged across the Americas. The article analyzes that as Asia and the European Union continue to rise as regional economic blocs, North America's global economic leadership is facing challenges. Authored by Karina Fernández-Stark and Penny Bamber, the piece explores the reasons behind declining North American competitiveness and the potential and obstacles to regional integration in Central and South America, calling on countries across the Americas to accelerate the pursuit of a broader economic cooperation framework.
  
  The UDN Money Daily offers a different perspective on the global economic landscape, noting that while the United States is not immune to global economic fluctuations, it will remain the only country possessing both the economic and institutional capacity to stabilize the global economy. The article further analyzes that, measured by its share of global GDP, China's current trade surplus in manufactured goods already exceeds the combined surpluses of Germany and Japan at any point during the 1980s, and emphasizes that this is the result of deliberate policy choices. This viewpoint highlights the profound shifts in Sino-U.S. trade structures and the unique role the United States plays as a stabilizer in the global economy.
  
  Synthesizing the above sources, economic development in the Americas in 2025 will face multiple tests. On one hand, the trade policy direction of the new U.S. administration will directly affect the cross-border trade and investment environment, potentially triggering trade friction and market volatility. On the other hand, progress toward regional integration in the Americas has been slow, and North America's competitiveness is under challenge compared to the integration processes in Asia and the European Union. At the same time, the expansion of China's trade surplus is having a far-reaching impact on the global economic landscape. While the United States possesses the capacity to stabilize the global economy, how to respond to changes in trade structures will be a challenge policymakers must confront.
  
  Future observation will focus on the specific content of the new U.S. administration's trade policies, the progress of regional integration negotiations in the Americas, and the evolution of Sino-U.S. trade relations.

# 美洲區域整合與美國貿易政策成2025經濟焦點

> 2025年全球經濟面臨美國新政府貿易政策、地緣政治與區域整合挑戰。專家指出美洲一體化進展緩慢，美國雖具穩定全球經濟能力，但中國貿易順差已超越德日1980年代總和。

臺灣經濟研究院《臺經月刊》第48卷第1期刊載方俊德之景氣特別報導指出，2025年全球經濟預計將維持平穩走勢，惟仍須應對美國新政府政策走向、地緣政治衝突持續以及金融局勢不穩等多重挑戰。報導強調，美國新政府預計將推行更為強硬的貿易政策與移民政策，此舉可能引發新一輪貿易戰，並增加市場不確定性。此外，報導亦提及，若中東局勢與俄烏戰爭得以停火，將有助於緩解部分經濟壓力，惟整體前景仍充滿變數。
另一方面，北京大學國際關係學院轉載之文章〈推進美洲區域一體化，重振全球經濟領導地位〉指出，北美自由貿易協定（NAFTA）曾助力北美地區維持全球經濟領導地位，然而整個美洲並未出現更廣泛的區域經濟一體化倡議。文章分析，隨著亞洲與歐盟作為區域經濟集團持續崛起，北美的全球經濟領導地位正面臨挑戰。該文由卡琳娜·費爾南德斯-斯塔克與佩妮·班博撰寫，探討北美競爭力減弱之原因，以及中南美洲區域整合之潛力與障礙，呼籲美洲各國應加速推動更廣泛的經濟合作架構。
經濟日報網路報則從另一個角度分析全球經濟格局，文章指出，美國同樣無法免疫於全球經濟波動，惟美國將是唯一同時具備經濟與制度能力，可以穩定全球經濟的國家。文章進一步分析，若以佔全球GDP比重衡量，中國目前的製造品貿易順差，已超過德國與日本1980年代任何時期的順差總和，並強調這是明確政策選擇的結果。此觀點凸顯中美貿易結構之深刻變化，以及美國在全球經濟中扮演穩定器角色之獨特性。
綜合上述來源，2025年美洲地區經濟發展將面臨多重考驗。一方面，美國新政府之貿易政策走向將直接影響跨國貿易與投資環境，可能引發貿易摩擦與市場波動；另一方面，美洲區域一體化進展緩慢，相較於亞洲與歐盟之區域整合程式，北美競爭力面臨挑戰。同時，中國貿易順差之擴大亦對全球經濟格局產生深遠影響，美國雖具備穩定全球經濟之能力，惟如何應對貿易結構變化，將是政策制定者必須面對之課題。
後續觀察將聚焦於美國新政府貿易政策之具體內容、美洲區域整合談判進展，以及中美貿易關係之演變。
