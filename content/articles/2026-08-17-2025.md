  # UN Report: 2025 Global Humanitarian Aid Funding Gap Exceeds $37.8 Billion
  
  > The UN Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (OCHA) released its July 2025 update report, indicating that 300 million people across 72 countries require assistance. The funding need stands at $45.48 billion, with only $7.64 billion raised so far—a gap exceeding 80 percent.
  
  The UN Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (OCHA) released the July update of the *Global Humanitarian Overview 2025* in July 2025, showing that global humanitarian aid funding needs continue to rise while fundraising progress lags severely behind. The report indicates that $45.48 billion is needed in 2025 to assist 181.2 million people among 300 million in 72 countries, yet only $7.64 billion had been raised by the end of July—a funding gap of $37.84 billion, with a fundraising rate of only about 16.8 percent.
  
  According to content published on the multilingual website of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of China in *Diplomatic Review* Issue 38, No. 2, the OCHA 2020 Global Humanitarian Aid Trends Report had already pointed out that violent conflict, forced displacement, food security crises, malnutrition, infectious diseases, and environmental impacts continue to cause severe humanitarian consequences in the West Asia region. The publication emphasized that while pursuing the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), maintaining regional stability and security remains an urgent priority. The structural regional crises revealed in this early report echo the global funding shortages reflected in the latest 2025 overview, demonstrating the long-standing structural fundraising challenges facing humanitarian aid.
  
  Amnesty International continuously monitors human rights conditions in its Asia and Pacific region專題 page, publishing related research, advocacy, and educational materials. The organization's sustained attention to human rights issues in Asia complements the perspectives raised in the UN report regarding forced displacement, conflict, and food insecurity. However, Amnesty International's public materials primarily focus on human rights advocacy and research resource guidance, and do not provide specific figures or cross-referenced data directly corresponding to the OCHA report in the available sources.
  
  Based on available public information, the 2025 global humanitarian crisis presents three key characteristics: first, the scale of affected populations is enormous, with 300 million people across 72 countries in need of assistance, of whom 181.2 million are priority beneficiaries; second, fundraising is severely insufficient, with over 80 percent of funding needs unmet; third, the Asia-Pacific region remains a focal area of humanitarian crisis, facing multiple challenges including conflict, displacement, and food security. While UN and civil society human rights reports emphasize different aspects, they collectively point to the resource scarcity and structural challenges confronting the global humanitarian system. Subsequent fundraising progress and government responses will be key indicators for assessing the effectiveness of 2025 humanitarian aid.

# 聯合國報告：2025年全球人道援助資金缺口逾378億美元

> 聯合國人道事務協調廳發布2025年7月更新報告指出，全球72國需援助人口達3億人，資金需求454.8億美元，目前僅籌得76.4億美元，缺口逾八成。

聯合國人道事務協調廳（OCHA）於2025年7月發布《2025年全球人道概覽》（Global Humanitarian Overview 2025）7月更新報告，顯示全球人道援助資金需求持續攀升，但籌募進度嚴重落後。報告指出，2025年共需454.8億美元，以協助72個國家中3億人口裡的1.812億人，然而截至7月底僅籌得76.4億美元，資金缺口高達378.4億美元，籌募率僅約16.8%。
根據中華民國外交部多語網站刊載之《外交通訊》第38期第二期內容，聯合國人道事務協調廳2020年度全球人道援助趨勢報告已指出，暴力衝突、被迫遷徙、糧食安全危機、營養不良、傳染病及環境衝擊等因素，持續對亞西區域造成嚴重人道衝擊。該期刊物強調，在追求永續發展目標（SDGs）的同時，維持區域穩定與安全為刻不容緩的工作方向。此份早期報告所揭示的區域性危機結構，與2025年最新概覽所反映的全球性資金短缺問題相互呼應，顯示人道援助長期面臨結構性籌款困境。
國際特赦組織（Amnesty International）在其亞洲及太平洋地區專題頁面中，持續追蹤該區域人權狀況，並發布相關研究、倡議與教育資料。該組織關注亞洲地區人權議題的持續性，與聯合國報告中提及的被迫遷徙、衝突與糧食不安全等議題形成互補視角。然而，國際特赦組織的公開資料主要聚焦於人權倡議與研究資源導引，並未在現有素材中提供與OCHA報告直接對應的具體數字或資料交叉比對。
綜合現有公開資料，2025年全球人道危機呈現三大特徵：其一，受助人口規模龐大，72國合計3億人處於需要援助的狀態，其中1.812億人為優先協助物件；其二，資金籌募嚴重不足，超過八成的資金需求未能獲得滿足；其三，亞洲及太平洋地區持續為人道危機重點區域，涉及衝突、遷徙與糧食安全等多重挑戰。聯合國與民間人權組織的報告雖側重不同面向，但共同指向全球人道體系面臨的資源匱乏與結構性挑戰。後續資金籌募進度與各國政府回應，將是觀察2025年人道援助成效的關鍵指標。
