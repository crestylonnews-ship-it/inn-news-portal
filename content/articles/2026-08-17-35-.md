  # Trump Threatens 35% Tariffs on Canada as North American Supply Chain Risks Mount
  
  > U.S. President Trump has threatened to impose a 35% tariff on Canadian goods, escalating risks to North American supply chains; analysts note that the U.S. has long faced trade deficits and employment pressures, with tariffs once again emerging as a policy option.
  
  U.S. President Trump recently renewed his tariff threats against Canada, stating that if the two countries cannot reach a trade agreement, a 35% tariff will be imposed on all goods from Canada. This move has drawn significant market attention and heightened North American supply chain risks. Related reports indicate that this threat was issued against the backdrop of ongoing trade agreement negotiations between the U.S. and Canada, signaling that tensions between economic cooperation and competition between the two nations are steadily intensifying.
  
  From a longer-term perspective, the U.S. dilemma of facing trade deficits and domestic employment pressures is nothing new. According to analytical materials published by Taiwan's Ministry of Economic Affairs Bureau of Foreign Trade, since 2001 the U.S. has again faced sluggish economic growth and persistently high unemployment. Under immense electoral pressure, the U.S. government needed to find a scapegoat for its massive trade deficit, blaming the rise in unemployment on the massive dumping of Chinese manufactured goods in the U.S., and arguing that China's large trade surplus with the U.S. was affecting American jobs.
  
  This historical context shows that tariff measures are a recurring tool in U.S. trade policy, and the shift of focus toward Canada reflects an expansion of U.S. trade protectionism from Asia to its North American neighbor.
  
  Economic analysis institutions have also pointed out that the global economy has taken different paths over recent quarters. Observing the matrix of quarterly GDP and annual Consumer Price Index (CPI) data reveals that the economic changes and policy directions of the world's top 20 economies vary, requiring corresponding adjustments to investment strategies. Against this backdrop of the global economy initially experiencing tailwinds, the tariff dispute between the U.S. and Canada undoubtedly casts a shadow over regional economic cooperation, particularly posing a potential impact on the highly integrated North American supply chain. Industry observers worry that if tariff measures are implemented, they will further drive up prices, affect corporate costs, and could trigger retaliatory tariffs, plunging the North American economy into a lose-lose situation.
  
  Synthesizing information from various sources, Trump's tariff threat currently remains a negotiating chip and has not yet been formally implemented, but the policy signal it sends has already affected market confidence. Analysts remind that domestic political pressure and electoral considerations in the U.S. remain key drivers of such protectionist policies. Future progress in U.S.-Canada trade negotiations will be a critical indicator for observing the trajectory of the North American economy.

# 特朗普威脅對加拿大徵35%關稅 北美供應鏈風險升溫

> 美國總統特朗普威脅對加拿大商品徵收35%關稅，北美供應鏈風險持續升溫；分析指出美國長期面臨貿易赤字與就業壓力，關稅手段再度成為政策選項。

美國總統特朗普近日再次對加拿大發出關稅威脅，表示若兩國無法達成貿易協議，將對所有來自加拿大的商品徵收35%關稅。此舉引發市場高度關注，北美供應鏈風險隨之上升。相關報導指出，此威脅是在美加兩國持續進行貿易協議談判的背景下所發出的最新警告，顯示雙方在經濟合作與競爭之間的張力正不斷擴大。
從更長遠的視角來看，美國面對貿易赤字與國內就業壓力的困境並非新鮮事。根據臺灣經濟部國際貿易局出版的分析資料，美國自2001年起再度面臨經濟成長趨緩、失業率居高不下的困境，在巨大的選票壓力下，美國政府需為龐大的貿易赤字尋找替罪羔羊，遂將失業率上升的原因歸咎於中國製造業商品大量傾銷美國，並認為中國對美國的貿易順差過大，影響了美國人的就業機會。
此番歷史脈絡顯示，關稅手段在美國貿易政策中屢見不鮮，而此次將目標轉向加拿大，反映出美國貿易保護主義的擴張趨勢正從亞洲延伸至北美鄰國。
經濟分析機構亦指出，全球經濟在過去數季呈現不同走向，透過按季GDP及按年消費物價指數（CPI）資料矩陣圖可觀察到，全球20大經濟體的經濟變化與政策方向各有差異，投資策略亦需相應調整。在這種全球經濟初嘗順風的氛圍下，美加之間的關稅爭端無疑為區域經濟合作蒙上陰影，尤其對高度整合的北美供應鏈構成潛在衝擊。業界擔憂，若關稅措施落實，將進一步推高物價、影響企業成本，並可能引發報復性關稅，使北美經濟陷入雙輸局面。
綜合各方資訊，特朗普的關稅威脅目前仍屬談判籌碼，尚未正式實施，但其釋放的政策訊號已對市場信心造成影響。分析人士提醒，美國國內政治壓力與選票考量，仍是驅動此類保護主義政策的重要因素。未來美加貿易談判的進展，將是觀察北美經濟走向的關鍵指標。
