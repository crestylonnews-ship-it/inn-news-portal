  # US-China AI Race Intensifies as Stanford Report Reveals Narrowing Gap
  
  > The Stanford University 2025 AI Index Report indicates the US still leads, but the gap between China and the US continues to narrow; the Trump administration has launched an AI national strategy, and experts believe the competition will intensify in 2026.
  
  In early April 2025, Stanford University released the Artificial Intelligence Index Report 2025, which is widely recognized by global media, governments, and leading technology companies as a credible resource, providing rigorous analysis for policymakers, business leaders, and the public on the technological progress, economic impact, and social implications of artificial intelligence. The report shows that the United States continues to maintain a strong lead in the field of AI, but the gap with China is steadily narrowing—a trend that has drawn close attention from the international community.
  
  According to BBC Chinese, the Trump administration released "Winning the AI Race: The American AI Action Plan" in late July. This is an ambitious AI national strategy launched just six months into Trump's return to the White House. The policy blueprint aims to consolidate America's leading position in AI and address competitive challenges from China. The report notes that the US-China AI race has evolved into a comprehensive contest, extending beyond the technological dimension to encompass policy, industry, education, and multiple other areas.
  
  PressReader's analysis suggests that the US-China AI competition in 2026 is expected to be more intense than in 2025. Entering his second year in office, Trump needs to consolidate his China policy and lay the foundation for his four-year term, unable to defer pressure to his third and fourth years; meanwhile, Xi Jinping must pave the way for the 21st National Congress of the Chinese Communist Party in 2027 and strive to win the competition against the United States. The report emphasizes that while Washington and Beijing compete on both political and economic fronts, the AI race may be the most critical, as AI leadership will directly affect a nation's comprehensive strength.
  
  Synthesizing reports from the three media outlets, Stanford University's data package provides quantitative evidence demonstrating that the gap between China and the US in AI development is narrowing; the Trump administration's policy actions show that the United States is actively responding to this challenge; and PressReader's analysis, from a political timeline perspective, identifies 2026 as a pivotal year. However, regarding specific technical indicators, investment figures, and policy details, each media outlet emphasizes different aspects, and further data is needed for verification. Experts generally agree that the AI race will continue to shape the global technology landscape and geopolitical trajectory.

# 中美AI競賽白熱化 史丹佛報告揭差距縮小趨勢

> 史丹佛大學2025年AI指數報告指出美國仍居領先，但中美差距持續縮小；特朗普政府推出AI國家戰略，專家認為2026年競賽將更激烈。

史丹佛大學於2025年4月初發布《人工智慧指數報告2025》（Artificial Intelligence Index Report 2025），該報告被全球媒體、政府與領先科技公司公認為可信賴的資源，為政策制定者、商業領袖與公眾提供關於人工智慧技術進步、經濟影響與社會影響的嚴謹分析。報告顯示，美國在人工智慧領域依然保持強大領先地位，但美中差距正持續縮小，這一趨勢引發國際社會高度關注。
根據BBC中文報導，特朗普政府於7月底發布《贏得AI競賽：美國人工智慧行動計劃》，這是特朗普重返白宮後上任僅六個月推出的雄心勃勃的AI國家戰略。該政策藍圖旨在鞏固美國在人工智慧領域的領先地位，應對來自中國的競爭挑戰。報導指出，美中AI競賽已走向全方位博弈，不僅限於技術層面，更涵蓋政策、產業與教育等多個領域。
PressReader報導分析認為，2026年的美中AI競賽預計將比2025年更加激烈。川普進入第二年任期，需要鞏固對中政策，為四年任期打下根基，不能將壓力延至第三年與第四年；習近平則需為2027年中共21大舖路，贏得中美競爭對抗。報導強調，華府與北京在政治與經貿層面均有競爭，但其中AI競賽可能最為重要，因為AI領先將直接影響國家綜合實力。
綜合三家媒體報導，史丹佛大學的資料包告提供了量化依據，證明中美在AI發展上的差距正在縮小；特朗普政府的政策行動則顯示美國正積極應對這一挑戰；而PressReader的分析則從政治時程角度，指出2026年將是關鍵年份。不過，關於具體的技術指標、投資金額與政策細節，各媒體報導側重點不同，尚待更多資料驗證。專家普遍認為，AI競賽將持續影響全球科技格局與地緣政治走向。
