  # EU Launches AI Action Plan, Tech Sovereignty Becomes Strategic Core
  
  > The European Commission released its AI Action Plan in October 2025, simultaneously advancing a tech sovereignty initiative to accelerate AI adoption and reduce dependence on US and Chinese technology, strengthening digital autonomy.
  
  The European Commission officially released its AI Action Plan on October 8, 2025, aimed at ensuring Europe maintains a global leading position in artificial intelligence. According to a report translated by the Taiwan Intellectual Property Office, the plan includes two major strategies: the "AI Application Strategy," which aims to accelerate the adoption and integration of AI across key industries and the public sector; and the "AI in Science Strategy," focused on strengthening AI research capabilities. This move demonstrates the EU's active policy-driven approach to AI development and its attempt to carve out a European path amid the US-China tech rivalry.
  
  Addressing the issue of technological dependence, the EU has simultaneously introduced a tech sovereignty package. iThome reports that European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen emphasized that Europe cannot rely on other countries to provide the critical technologies needed to keep hospitals running, maintain grid stability, and ensure public service security.
  
  She further explained that tech sovereignty is not merely an industrial policy but relates to Europe's ability to protect its citizens, safeguard its interests, and make autonomous decisions. creati.ai also noted that the EU has formally released an ambitious legislative package aimed at ensuring digital independence. As global competition intensifies in computing power, data governance, and artificial intelligence, the EU is taking action to narrow the technology gap with the United States and China.
  
  Talent resources are the key foundation for Europe's technological development. TechOrange cited Revelio Labs data collected from 1.6 million AI professionals, showing that the UK is currently the world's third-largest AI talent market, while Germany has the fourth-largest number of AI engineers globally. On a per-capita basis, Ireland ranks second worldwide, behind only Singapore, with Switzerland, the Netherlands, and Denmark also performing impressively. The report suggests that the development of companies ranging from Spotify to Legora indicates that Europe's tech ecosystem is forming a positive cycle, heading toward a golden inflection point.
  
  Synthesizing reports from various media outlets, although the EU's AI Action Plan and tech sovereignty package were released through different channels, their core objectives are consistent: reducing dependence on external technology and strengthening autonomous capabilities. However, how to balance open innovation with technological sovereignty remains a challenge for Europe. Key areas for future observation include legislative progress, industrial investment trends, and whether the EU can continue to maintain its advantage in AI talent competition.

# 歐盟推AI行動計畫 科技主權成戰略核心

> 歐盟執委會2025年10月發布AI行動計畫，同步推動科技主權方案，加速AI應用並減少對美中技術依賴，強化數位自主能力。

歐盟執行委員會於2025年10月8日正式發布AI行動計畫，旨在確保歐洲在人工智慧領域維持全球領先地位。根據經濟部智慧財產局轉譯報導，該計畫包含兩大戰略：一是「人工智慧應用戰略」（Apply AI Strategy），目標為加速AI在關鍵產業與公共部門的普及與整合；二是「科研領域人工智慧戰略」（AI in Science Strategy），著重強化AI研究能量。此舉顯示歐盟正以政策手段積極佈局AI發展，並試圖在美中科技競爭格局中開闢歐洲路徑。
針對科技依賴問題，歐盟同步推出科技主權方案。iThome報導指出，歐盟執委會主席烏爾蘇拉·馮德萊恩（Ursula von der Leyen）強調，歐洲不能依賴他國提供維持醫院運作、電網穩定與公共服務安全所需的關鍵技術。
她進一步說明，科技主權不只是產業政策，更關係到歐洲保護公民、維護利益及自主決策的能力。creati.ai亦指出，歐盟已正式發布一項雄心勃勃的立法方案，旨在確保數位獨立性，隨著全球在算力、資料治理與人工智慧方面的競爭白熱化，歐盟正採取行動縮小與美國和中國之間的技術差距。
人才資源是歐洲科技發展的關鍵基礎。TechOrange科技報橘引用Revelio Labs蒐集160萬名AI專業人士資料顯示，英國目前是全球第三大AI人才市場，德國則擁有全球第四多的AI工程師。若以人口比例計算，愛爾蘭更高居全球第二，僅次於新加坡，瑞士、荷蘭與丹麥也表現亮眼。該報導認為，從Spotify到Legora等企業的發展，顯示歐洲科技生態正形成正向迴圈，迎向黃金轉折點。
綜合各媒體報導，歐盟AI行動計畫與科技主權方案雖由不同管道發布，但核心目標一致：減少對外部技術依賴、強化自主能力。不過，如何平衡開放創新與技術主權，仍是歐洲面臨的挑戰。後續觀察重點將包含立法進展、產業投資動向，以及歐盟能否在AI人才競爭中持續保持優勢。
