  # EU Unveils Billion-Euro AI Strategy to Forge a Third Pole in Global Artificial Intelligence
  
  > On October 8, 2025, the European Commission released two AI strategies and announced a €1 billion investment plan, while Germany simultaneously launched an AI innovation park worth billions of euros, striving to become a leading force in the global AI competition.
  
  On October 8, 2025, the European Commission officially released two policy documents: the "AI in Industry Strategy" and the "European Science AI Strategy: Paving the Way for European AI Science Resources," aimed at fully implementing the "AI Continent Action Plan" and accelerating the application of artificial intelligence in key industries and research fields. Reuters reported that the EU simultaneously announced an investment plan worth €1 billion (approximately $1.1 billion), targeting increased AI adoption in key industries and reduced dependence on external technology. The President of the European Commission emphasized that Europe is committed to becoming a pioneer in AI innovation and governance.
  
  In terms of industrial layout, the "AI Innovation Park" in Heilbronn, Baden-Württemberg, Germany, has officially launched. Xinhua Net reported that the park involves total investments of several billion euros, making it the largest and most comprehensively planned AI industry cluster in Germany to date. According to the plan, the innovation park will focus on industrial AI, smart manufacturing, autonomous driving, smart logistics, AI ethics and safety, aiming to build Europe's largest AI industry hub. EU official documents also note that Europe's AI methodology emphasizes excellence and trust, strengthening research and industrial capabilities while ensuring safety and fundamental rights.
  
  The academic community has also conducted comparative analyses of EU AI policy. A research paper published by Airiti Library pointed out that both the EU and the UK are global leaders in AI development, with each side emphasizing different aspects of policy design. The EU strategy leans toward building a trust foundation through regulatory frameworks while promoting industrial applications; the UK tends toward a more flexible regulatory approach. Reports from the Chinese Academy of Sciences added that the two strategies launched by the EU focus respectively on industrial applications and scientific research, indicating that the EU hopes to strengthen competitiveness on both the technology implementation and basic research fronts.
  
  Observers note that the EU's current policy layout carries clear strategic intent. On one hand, the billion-euro investment plan drives the intelligent transformation of key industries; on the other, the launch of Germany's large innovation park symbolizes substantive progress in Europe's hardware infrastructure and industry clusters. However, different media outlets present slightly varying figures for the investment scale—Reuters centers on €1 billion, while Germany's innovation park investment reaches several billion euros—indicating that EU-level and member-state-level resource commitments each play distinct roles.
  
  Subsequent observation will focus on the specific implementation details of the two AI strategies and the progress of coordination between the EU and the UK on AI regulatory frameworks. Whether Europe can truly become the "third pole" of global AI development remains to be seen, depending on further industrial implementation results and the evolution of the international competitive landscape.

# 歐盟推百億歐元AI戰略 打造全球人工智慧第三極

> 歐盟委員會2025年10月8日發布兩項AI戰略，並宣佈10億歐元投資計畫，德國同時啟動規模達數十億歐元的人工智慧創新園，力爭在全球AI競爭中成為領先力量。

歐盟委員會於2025年10月8日正式推出《應用AI戰略》與《歐洲科學AI戰略：為歐洲AI科學資源鋪平道路》兩份政策檔案，旨在全面落實《AI大陸行動計劃》，加速人工智慧在關鍵產業與科研領域的應用。路透社報導指出，歐盟同時宣佈一項規模達10億歐元（約11億美元）的投資計畫，目標是提升關鍵產業的人工智慧採用率，並減少歐盟對外部技術的依賴。歐盟委員會主席強調，歐洲致力於成為人工智慧創新與治理的先行者。
在產業佈局方面，德國巴登—符騰堡州海爾布隆的「人工智慧創新園」近日正式啟動。新華網報導指出，該園區總投資達數十億歐元，是德國迄今為止規模最大、規劃最完整的人工智慧產業叢集。根據規劃，該創新園將重點佈局工業人工智慧、智慧製造、自動駕駛、智慧物流、人工智慧倫理與安全等方向，旨在打造歐洲最大的人工智慧產業聚落。歐盟官方檔案亦指出，歐洲的AI方法論強調卓越與信任，透過強化研究與產業能力，同時確保安全與基本權利。
學術界對歐盟AI政策亦展開比較分析。Airiti Library刊載的研究論文指出，歐盟與英國均為全球AI發展領導者，雙方在政策設計上各有側重。歐盟策略偏向透過法規框架建立信任基礎，同時推動產業應用；英國則傾向更為靈活的監管途徑。中科院相關報導補充，歐盟此次推出的兩項戰略分別聚焦產業應用與科學研究，顯示歐盟希望在技術落地與基礎研究兩端同時強化競爭力。
觀察人士指出，歐盟此輪政策佈局具有明確的戰略意圖。一方面，透過百億歐元投資計畫推動關鍵產業智慧化轉型；另一方面，德國大型創新園的啟動象徵歐洲在硬體基礎設施與產業聚落上的實質進展。然而，不同媒體對投資規模的表述略有差異——路透社以10億歐元為主軸，而德國創新園的投資則達數十億歐元，顯示歐盟層級與成員國層級的資源投入各有分工。
後續觀察將聚焦於兩項AI戰略的具體執行細節，以及歐盟與英國在AI監管框架上的協調進展。歐洲能否真正成為全球AI發展的「第三極」，仍有待產業落地成效與國際競爭格局的進一步演變。
