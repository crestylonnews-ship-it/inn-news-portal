  # Trump Administration Releases AI National Strategy as US-China AI Race Intensifies
  
  > The Trump administration released "Winning the AI Race: America's AI Action Plan" in July 2025 to strengthen its technological leadership; a Stanford report shows the US-China gap narrowing, marking a new phase in the global AI competition.
  
  On July 23, 2025, the Trump administration released "Winning the AI Race: America's AI Action Plan," developed under the leadership of the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy, and regarded as the core policy blueprint launched within six months of returning to the White House. BBC Chinese reported that this move elevates the AI race to the level of national strategy, aiming to consolidate technological advantages and respond to China's rise. Tencent News analysis noted that the plan emphasizes three goals—"innovation, competition, and dominance"—reflecting the US's focus on maintaining leadership in the AI field. According to Cnyes, the global AI industry chain is accelerating its restructuring, with the US-China bipolar格局 becoming the central axis of industry development.
  
  The Stanford University AI Index Report released in April 2025 showed that while the US still holds a leading position, the technological gap with China continues to narrow. Cnyes cited the report, noting that China performed impressively in indicators such as paper publications, patent applications, and AI investment, particularly making rapid progress in large model research and practical applications. An article on Zhihu further analyzed that the US once dominated AI research in 2020, but in recent years China has gradually closed the gap through policy support and resource investment, forming a "dual-power" landscape.
  
  Data from International Data Corporation (IDC) also shows that the global AI market is expanding at a remarkable pace, becoming a core driver of technological revolution and economic growth.
  
  Experts hold different interpretations of the US's new strategy. BBC Chinese believes the move aims to strengthen US technological monopoly in AI and restrict China's development through measures such as export controls and investment reviews; Tencent News pointed out that the simultaneous emphasis on "competition" and "dominance" in the strategy suggests the US may adopt a tougher tech decoupling policy. Cnyes reported that the US-China AI race has expanded from a single technological contest to a comprehensive game covering standards formulation, talent cultivation, and ecosystem building. Analysis suggests that if the US further tightens technology export restrictions, it could accelerate the fragmentation of the global AI industry chain, affecting international cooperation and the pace of innovation.

# 特朗普政府發布AI國家戰略 中美人工智慧競賽白熱化

> 特朗普政府於2025年7月發布《贏得AI競賽：美國人工智慧行動計劃》，強化技術領先地位；史丹佛報告顯示中美差距縮小，全球AI競賽進入新階段。

特朗普政府於2025年7月23日發布《贏得競賽：美國人工智慧行動計劃》，由白宮科技政策辦公室主導制定，被視為重返白宮後六個月內推出的核心政策藍圖。BBC中文網指出，此舉標誌著美國將人工智慧競賽提升至國家戰略層級，旨在鞏固技術優勢並應對中國崛起。騰訊新聞分析稱，該計劃強調「革新、競爭與霸權」三重目標，反映美國對人工智慧領域主導權的重視。鉅亨網報導則補充，全球人工智慧產業鏈正加速重組，中美雙極格局成為產業發展的主軸。
史丹佛大學2025年4月發布的《人工智慧指數報告》顯示，美國雖仍居領先地位，但與中國的技術差距持續縮小。鉅亨網援引該報告指出，中國在論文發表量、專利申請及人工智慧投資等指標上表現亮眼，尤其在大模型研發與應用落地方面進展迅速。知乎文章進一步分析，2020年美國曾獨佔人工智慧研究主導權，但近年來中國透過政策扶持與資源投入，已逐步縮小差距，形成「雙雄並立」局面。
國際資料公司（IDC）資料亦顯示，全球人工智慧市場正以驚人速度擴張，成為推動科技革命與經濟增長的核心動力。
專家對美國新戰略的意圖存在不同解讀。BBC中文網認為，此舉旨在強化美國在人工智慧領域的技術壟斷，並透過出口管制與投資審查等手段限制中國發展；騰訊新聞則指出，戰略中「競爭」與「霸權」的並提，暗示美國可能採取更強硬的科技脫鉤政策。鉅亨網報導則強調，中美人工智慧競賽已從單一技術比拼擴充套件至標準制定、人才培育與生態系建構的全方位博弈。分析認為，若美國進一步收緊技術輸出限制，可能加速全球人工智慧產業鏈分裂，影響跨國合作與創新節奏。
