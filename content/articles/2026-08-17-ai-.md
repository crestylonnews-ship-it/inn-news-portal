  # Middle East AI Race Intensifies as US-China Rivalry Takes New Shape
  
  > The Middle East is emerging as a new global hub for AI development, with Saudi Arabia and the UAE investing heavily in autonomous AI infrastructure. This has attracted US tech giants like Nvidia to compete for a market worth hundreds of billions of dollars, while maintaining close technological ties with Beijing, making the region a new battleground for US-China competition.
  
  The Middle East is rapidly rising as a new global hub for artificial intelligence (AI) development. Countries led by Saudi Arabia and the UAE are investing heavily in building autonomous AI infrastructure and ecosystems. This trend is bringing substantial business opportunities to US tech giants led by Nvidia, with cooperation projects spanning AI chips, cloud computing, and data center construction unfolding on an unprecedented scale, drawing international attention.
  
  According to the Asia-Pacific Search Engine Industry Association, the scale of AI investment across Middle Eastern countries is staggering. The UAE launched its "UAE Artificial Intelligence Strategy 2031" for the first time in 2017, dedicated to applying AI technology across government services, healthcare, transportation, and multiple other sectors. The AI investment fund established by MGX, a technology investment company with Abu Dhabi government backing, is estimated to reach $100 billion, aiming to fill the gap in Arabic language and cultural understanding in the global AI field. Saudi Arabia is also actively embracing US technology, with the venture capital arm of Saudi Aramco actively participating in related investment layouts.
  
  However, Middle Eastern countries are not leaning unidirectionally in their AI development strategies. According to Commercial Times, the United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia seek US technological support for AI development while simultaneously hoping to maintain close economic and technological ties with Beijing. Against the backdrop of great power competition, as the US seeks to contain China on chip technology, whether the Middle East will become a breakthrough point for Beijing has drawn widespread attention. Ta Kung Pao also reported that multiple Middle Eastern countries are actively promoting AI development, attempting to strike a balance between the two technological superpowers of the US and China, demonstrating a diplomatic strategy of playing both sides.
  
  Analysts believe that the rise of Middle Eastern countries in the AI field not only reflects their determination for economic transformation but also highlights subtle geopolitical shifts. The US tech industry is consolidating its leading position in AI chips and cloud computing through cooperation with Middle Eastern nations, while China is attempting to expand its influence in the Middle Eastern market through economic cooperation and technology exports. This dual-dependency landscape has made the Middle East a new battleground for US-China AI rivalry, and its developmental trajectory will have profound implications for the global technology landscape.

# 中東AI競逐白熱化 美中角力新戰場成形

> 中東地區正崛起為全球AI發展新重鎮，沙烏地阿拉伯與阿聯酋斥巨資打造自主AI基礎設施，吸引輝達等美科技巨頭搶佔千億美元商機，同時與北京保持緊密科技聯絡，成為美中角力新戰場。

中東地區正迅速崛起為全球人工智慧（AI）發展的新重鎮，以沙烏地阿拉伯和阿聯酋為首的國家，正斥巨資打造自主AI基礎設施與生態系。此趨勢為以輝達（Nvidia）為首的美國科技巨頭帶來龐大商機，包括AI晶片、雲端運算與資料中心建置等合作案正以前所未有的規模展開，引發國際關注。
根據亞太搜尋引擎產業協會報導，中東各國在AI領域的投資規模驚人，其中阿聯酋於2017年首次啟動「2031年阿聯酋人工智慧戰略」，致力於將AI技術應用於政府服務、醫療、交通等多個領域。阿布扎比政府背景技術投資公司MGX所設立的人工智慧投資基金，預估規模高達1000億美元，旨在填補全球AI領域對阿拉伯語和文化理解的空白。沙烏地阿拉伯同樣積極擁抱美國技術，沙特阿美石油公司的創投部門亦積極參與相關投資佈局。
然而，中東國家在AI發展策略上並非單向傾斜。工商時報指出，阿拉伯聯合大公國與沙烏地阿拉伯一方面在AI技術上希望獲得美國技術支援，但與此同時卻又希望與北京保持緊密的經濟與科技聯絡。在大國博弈的背景下，美國亟欲在晶片技術上圍堵中國，中東國家是否成為北京「突圍」的破口，引發外界高度關注。大公報亦報導，中東多國正積極推動AI發展，試圖在美中兩大科技強權之間取得平衡，展現左右逢源的外交策略。
分析人士認為，中東國家在AI領域的崛起，不僅反映其經濟轉型的決心，更凸顯地緣政治的微妙變化。美國科技業透過與中東國家的合作，鞏固其在AI晶片與雲端運算領域的領先地位；而中國則透過經濟合作與技術輸出，試圖在中東市場擴大影響力。這種雙重依賴的格局，使中東成為美中AI角力的新戰場，其發展走向將對全球科技格局產生深遠影響。
