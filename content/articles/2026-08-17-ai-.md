  # Stanford AI Index Report Reveals US-China Race, Environmental Costs and Industry Landscape Draw Attention
  
  > Stanford University released its AI Index Report, noting that while global AI technology is advancing rapidly, the environmental burden is heavy. US-China competition is intensifying, with the US holding definitional power and China demonstrating dominance in hardware integration.
  
  Stanford University is a leading institution in global artificial intelligence research, and its annual AI Index Report has long been regarded as a key indicator for observing global AI development trends. According to information available online, the university's Human-Centered AI Institute (HAI) released a 456-page "AI Index Report" covering the latest data and developments in areas such as US-China AI competition, open-source model development, model technical performance, large-model investment and financing, and AI for Science. The report specifically mentions the Chinese-developed DeepSeek model, which appears 45 times, highlighting the model's prominent position in global AI discussions.
  
  The report also points out that while global AI technology has achieved significant breakthroughs in scientific reasoning and practical applications, the energy and water consumption required to train models is staggering, placing a heavy burden on the environment. This finding has sparked industry concern about AI sustainability. In terms of the talent market, the employment landscape for young software developers is facing disruption from AI, reflecting the substitution effect of automation technology on entry-level programming work.
  
  Regarding the competitive landscape of the industry, the analysis indicates that in 2026, the US-China competition at the AI application layer exhibited distinct characteristics. The US occupies the top of "definitional power and ecological niches," controlling standard-setting and core technology leadership; China, meanwhile, demonstrates dominance in "penetration rate and hardware integration," building competitive barriers at the deployment level through extensive market applications and hardware supply chain advantages. This division of labor foreshadows differentiated strategies between the two nations on their AI development paths.

# 史丹佛AI指數報告揭示中美競速 環境代價與產業格局受矚目

> 史丹佛大學發布AI指數報告，指出全球AI技術躍進但環境負擔沉重，中美競爭升溫，美國佔據定義權，中國在硬體整合展現統治力。

史丹佛大學為全球人工智慧研究的重要機構，其發布的年度AI指數報告長期被視為觀察全球AI發展趨勢的關鍵指標。根據網際網路資訊顯示，該校以人為本AI研究院（HAI）發布了長達456頁的《AI指數報告》，內容涵蓋中美AI競爭態勢、開源模型發展、模型技術效能、大模型投資融資，以及AI for Science等領域的最新資料與進展。報告中特別提及中國開發的DeepSeek模型，共出現45次，顯示該模型在全球AI討論中的顯著地位。
報告同時指出，全球AI技術在科學推理與實際應用上取得重要突破，但訓練模型所需的能源與水資源消耗驚人，對環境造成沉重負擔。這項發現引發業界對AI永續發展的關注。在人才市場方面，年輕軟體開發者的就業市場正面臨AI帶來的衝擊，反映出自動化技術對初階程式設計工作的替代效應。
在產業競爭格局上，分析指出2026年美中兩國在AI應用層的競爭呈現不同特徵。美國佔據「定義權與生態位」的頂端，掌握標準制定與核心技術主導地位；中國則在「滲透率與硬體整合」上展現統治力，透過廣泛的市場應用與硬體供應鏈優勢，在實際部署層面建立競爭壁壘。這種分工格局預示著兩國在AI發展路徑上的差異化策略。
