  # Asia-Pacific AI Market Expands Rapidly as Regulatory Paths Diverge Across Nations
  
  > The Asia-Pacific AI market continues to grow, with China holding the largest share; regulatory frameworks vary across Japan, China, South Korea, India, Australia and other nations, Taiwan advances its AI New Ten Major Projects, and global digital services export competition intensifies.
  
  According to a Fortune Business Insights analysis report, the Asia-Pacific AI market is segmented by country into China, India, Japan, South Korea, ASEAN, Oceania, and other Asia-Pacific regions. China holds the largest share of the Asia-Pacific AI market, driven by substantial government budget investments, a mature technology ecosystem, and advantages in its massive digital population and data processing capabilities. The report notes that the Asia-Pacific AI market is in a phase of rapid expansion, with governments viewing AI as a cornerstone of economic development and a critical technology affecting national security, leading to increasingly strong policy support.
  
  A survey report released by the Japan Science and Technology Agency (JST) shows that AI R&D competition among major Asia-Pacific countries is intensifying. The number of related papers published in 2023 grew approximately twofold compared to 2021, and the number of patent registrations increased by about 30% compared to 2022. The report also notes that AI has become an important foundation for economic development across nations and is regarded as a key technology that shapes national security strategy, with policy-driven revitalization measures continuously strengthening.
  
  In terms of regulatory frameworks, countries are taking markedly different approaches. According to a 2025 Asia-Pacific AI regulation comparison compiled by Xenoss.io, China implements strict regulations, Japan leans toward soft guidelines, and South Korea recently passed a new AI act, forming a clear pattern of regulatory differentiation within the region. This divergence in regulatory paths will have a profound impact on how multinational corporations approach AI deployment and compliance strategies across the Asia-Pacific.
  
  A Hong Kong government economic overview report indicates that surging global demand for AI, supported by the widespread adoption of AI in business applications and consumer preference for digital services, has driven growth in computer services exports. According to World Trade Organization statistics, several Asian economies entered the top 20 global exporters of digitally deliverable services in 2024, including India (5th), mainland China (6th), Singapore (7th), Japan (12th), and South Korea (17th), demonstrating the Asia-Pacific region's significant position in AI-related services trade.
  
  In Taiwan, the National Development Council is advancing the "AI New Ten Major Projects Promotion Plan" (2025–2028), aimed at strengthening AI infrastructure and industrial applications. This policy aligns with AI development trends across Asia-Pacific nations, reflecting Taiwan's strategic intent to actively participate in regional AI competition. Observing the various sources collectively, the Asia-Pacific AI market is undergoing a phase of simultaneous scale expansion and regulatory framework maturation, and the balance each country strikes among technological R&D, commercial applications, and policy governance will become a key variable shaping the regional AI landscape.

# 亞太AI市場快速擴張 各國監管路徑分化

> 亞太人工智慧市場規模持續擴大，中國佔最大份額；中日韓印澳等國監管框架各異，臺灣推動AI新十大建設，全球數位服務出口競爭白熱化。

根據 Fortune Business Insights 分析報告，亞太地區人工智慧市場依國家區分為中國、印度、日本、韓國、東協、大洋洲及亞太其他地區。其中中國憑藉龐大的政府預算投入、成熟的科技生態系以及海量數位人口與資料處理優勢，佔據亞太人工智慧市場最大份額。該報告指出，亞太AI市場正處於快速擴張階段，各國政府將AI視為經濟發展基石與影響國家安全的重要技術，政策扶持力度持續強化。
日本科學技術振興機構（JST）發布的調查報告顯示，亞太主要國家的AI研發競爭日益激烈。2023年相關論文發表數量較2021年增長約兩倍，特許登録數較2022年增加約30%。報告同時指出，AI已成為各國經濟發展的重要基礎，並被視為左右國家安全戰略的關鍵技術，各國政策性振興措施不斷加強。
在監管框架方面，各國採取截然不同的路徑。根據 Xenoss.io 整理的2025年亞太AI法規比較，中國實施嚴格規範，日本傾向軟性指導方針，韓國則於近期透過新AI法案，形成區域內監管差異化的明顯格局。這種監管路徑的分化，將對跨國企業在亞太地區的AI部署與合規策略產生深遠影響。
香港政府經濟概況報告指出，在AI商業應用廣泛普及及消費者偏好數位服務的支援下，全球對AI的殷切需求帶動了電腦服務輸出。根據世界貿易組織統計，多個亞洲經濟體於2024年躋身全球數位化交付服務輸出地前20名，包括印度（第5位）、中國內地（第6位）、新加坡（第7位）、日本（第12位）及韓國（第17位），顯示亞太地區在AI相關服務貿易中佔據重要地位。
臺灣方面，國家發展委員會推動「AI新十大建設推動方案」（114年至117年），旨在強化AI基礎建設與產業應用。此政策與亞太各國的AI發展趨勢相互呼應，反映臺灣積極參與區域AI競爭的戰略意圖。綜合各來源觀察，亞太AI市場正經歷規模擴張與監管框架成型並行的階段，各國在技術研發、商業應用與政策治理之間的平衡，將成為影響區域AI發展格局的關鍵變數。
