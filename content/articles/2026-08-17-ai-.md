  # US State Department AI Briefing Map Error Sparks Renewed Focus on Africa's Image
  
  > A US State Department briefing map depicting Africa incorrectly has drawn criticism of "AI in diplomacy," while reports of AI's positive impact in African slums highlight the continent's multifaceted role in the AI era.
  
  A recent briefing event at the US State Department has caused a stir. According to China Times, the African map used in the briefing contained multiple serious geographic labeling errors, including the "Horn of Africa" near the Arabian Peninsula being incorrectly placed in the interior, the Ivory Coast of West Africa being misplaced in the southeastern interior of Africa, and Cameroon's label also appearing as a "stray branch" error. The incident quickly drew outside criticism, raising concerns about the risks of "AI in diplomacy" and suggesting that overreliance on AI tools for diplomatic briefings could lead to serious informational errors.
  
  In contrast to the US State Department's blunder, AI's practical applications on the African continent are delivering positive impacts. TechOrange reports that AI is making a positive difference in Kibera, Africa's largest slum. The report notes that Samasource's early-stage data labeling is a key component of Silicon Valley giants' AI projects, and Brenda and others living in Kibera are among the participants. The report emphasizes that although Kibera seems worlds away from Silicon Valley, AI is still bringing positive impact here.
  
  Japan's patent information platform (J-PlatPat) also shows that patents related to AI monitoring and care systems are being applied for, reflecting Japan's continued佈局 in AI application areas. This forms an interesting echo with the development of local AI applications in Africa, showing how AI technology is exerting influence in different ways across different regions of the world.
  
  Synthesizing reports from multiple media outlets, the US State Department's briefing map error highlights the potential risks of AI tools in professional applications. Diplomatic briefings involve national image and policy communication, and map labeling errors could affect international perception and diplomatic relations. Meanwhile, stories of local AI applications in Africa demonstrate how technology can bridge geographic and economic gaps and deliver practical benefits in resource-scarce areas. The contrast between the two reflects the multifaceted image of Africa in the AI era: both a victim of international diplomatic missteps and a genuine beneficiary of technological dividends.

# 美國務院AI簡報地圖出錯 非洲形象再引關注

> 美國務院簡報非洲地圖示示錯誤，引發「AI搞外交」批評；與此同時，人工智慧在非洲貧民窟帶來積極影響的報導，凸顯非洲在AI時代的多重面貌。

美國國務院近期一場簡報活動引發軒然大波。根據《中國時報》報導，該次簡報中使用的非洲地圖出現多處嚴重地理標示錯誤，包括靠近阿拉伯半島的「非洲之角」（Horn of Africa）被錯誤標註於內陸位置，西非象牙海岸也被錯誤放置於非洲東南部內陸，甚至喀麥隆的標示也出現「孤枝」般的錯誤。此事件迅速引發外界批評，質疑「AI搞外交」的風險，認為過度依賴人工智慧工具進行外交簡報可能導致嚴重資訊錯誤。
與美國務院的失誤形成對比的是，人工智慧在非洲大陸的實際應用正帶來正面影響。《TechOrange》報導指出，人工智慧在非洲最大的貧民窟——基貝拉（Kibera）帶來了積極影響。報導提到，Samasource 的前期資料整理是矽谷巨頭在人工智慧領域專案的重要組成部分，而居住在基貝拉的 Brenda 等人正是這項工作的參與者。報導強調，雖然基貝拉看似與矽谷無比遙遠，但人工智慧在這裡依然帶來了積極的影響。
日本專利資訊平臺（J-PlatPat）也顯示，人工知能による見守りシステム（AI照護系統）相關專利正在申請中，反映日本在AI應用領域的持續佈局。這與非洲當地AI應用的發展形成有趣的呼應，顯示人工智慧技術在全球不同地區正以不同方式發揮影響。
綜合多家媒體報導，美國務院的簡報地圖錯誤事件凸顯了AI工具在專業領域應用的潛在風險。外交簡報涉及國家形象與政策溝通，地圖示示錯誤可能影響國際認知與外交關係。與此同時，非洲當地的AI應用故事則展示了技術如何跨越地理與經濟差距，在資源匱乏的地區發揮實際效益。兩相對照，反映出AI時代非洲形象的多重面向：既是國際外交失誤的受害者，也是技術紅利的實際受益者。
