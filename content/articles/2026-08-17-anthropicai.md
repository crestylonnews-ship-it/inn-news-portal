  # US Blockade of Anthropic Triggers EU Reflection on Sovereign AI Strategy
  
  > US executive order restricting Anthropic model output raises concerns among EU officials about erosion of technological sovereignty, accelerating efforts to advance domestic AI regulatory frameworks and industry alliances.
  
  The AI executive order signed by US President Trump, which imposes export restrictions on Anthropic's Claude model, has drawn significant attention from EU policy circles. An EU Commission digital policy official, speaking at a Brussels press conference, stated that this move highlights Europe's over-reliance on US tech giants for critical AI models and will accelerate the push for a "European Sovereign AI" strategy. The official emphasized that the EU is drafting revisions to the implementation details of the AI Act, requiring high-risk AI systems to undergo validation and regulation within European territory to prevent external political interference in technological development.
  
  The US has justified the blockade decision on national security grounds. According to reports from Economic Daily News, the executive order classifies the Claude model as a controlled technology, restricting its export to specific countries and entities. Analysts believe this reflects the US's attempt to maintain its technological edge in the AI competition, but it may also intensify transatlantic digital trade friction. The EU's Digital Markets Act (DMA) has launched a compliance review of US AI service providers to assess whether they violate the EU's market openness principles.
  
  Reactions within Europe's AI industry are divided. Some companies support the US position, arguing that it is necessary to prevent sensitive technologies from reaching competitors. However, AI research institutions in France and Germany jointly issued a declaration warning that excessive regulation could stifle innovation. The declaration noted that Europe has invested billions of euros in developing domestic models, and further US tightening of technology exports would severely impact the development of Europe's AI ecosystem. The chair of the EU Parliament's Digital Committee stated that a special session will be held next month to discuss whether to impose reciprocal restrictions on US AI services.
  
  Meanwhile, Europe's education sector is also beginning to address the challenges brought by the proliferation of AI. Cases of US professors using hidden prompts to catch AI cheating have drawn attention from European academic circles. The Educational Technology Research Centre at University College London (UCL) stated that it is drafting new academic integrity guidelines, emphasizing that teachers should cultivate students' ability to use AI tools critically rather than simply banning them. The centre's report pointed out that universities across multiple European countries have seen students increasingly relying on AI to generate reports, and educational institutions need to rethink their assessment methods.
  
  The EU Commission is expected to release the "AI Sovereignty White Paper" next quarter, detailing Europe's independent path in AI development. The white paper will cover areas such as technical standards, data governance, and industry cooperation, with the goal of reducing dependence on US and Chinese AI technologies. The European Digital Industry Alliance (EDIA) has called on member states to accelerate investment in domestic AI research and development and to establish cross-border data-sharing mechanisms to strengthen Europe's voice in the global AI competition.

# 美封鎖Anthropic觸發歐盟主權AI戰略反思

> 美國行政命令限制Anthropic模型輸出，歐盟官員擔憂技術主權受侵蝕，正加速推動本土AI監管框架與產業聯盟。

美國總統川普簽署的AI行政命令中，針對Anthropic公司Claude模型實施輸出限制，引發歐盟政策圈高度關注。歐盟委員會數位政策官員在布魯塞爾記者會上表示，此舉凸顯歐洲在關鍵人工智慧模型上過度依賴美國科技巨頭的風險，將加速推動「歐洲主權AI」戰略。該官員強調，歐盟正研擬修訂《人工智慧法案》執行細節，要求高風險AI系統必須在歐洲境內進行驗證與監管，避免外部政治力量幹預技術發展。
美國方面則以國家安全為由，解釋封鎖決策。根據《經濟日報》報導，這項行政命令將Claude模型列為受管制技術，限制其向特定國家與實體輸出。分析認為，此舉反映美國試圖在AI競爭中維持技術優勢，但也可能加劇跨大西洋數位貿易摩擦。歐盟數位市場局（DMA）已啟動對美國AI服務商的合規審查，評估是否違反歐盟市場開放原則。
歐洲AI產業界反應兩極。部分企業支援美國立場，認為有必要防止敏感技術流向競爭對手；但法國與德國的AI研究機構聯合發表宣告，警告過度管制可能阻礙創新。宣告指出，歐洲已投入數十億歐元發展本土模型，若美國進一步收緊技術出口，將嚴重影響歐洲AI生態系發展。歐盟議會數位委員會主席表示，將在下月召開特別會議，討論是否對美國AI服務實施對等限制。
與此同時，歐洲教育界也開始應對AI普及帶來的挑戰。美國教授利用隱形提示詞抓AI作弊的案例，引發歐洲學界關注。英國倫敦大學學院（UCL）教育科技研究中心表示，正研擬新的學術誠信指引，強調教師應培養學生批判性使用AI工具的能力，而非單純禁止。該中心報告指出，歐洲多國大學已出現學生依賴AI生成報告的現象，教育機構需重新思考考核方式。
歐盟委員會預計於下季度發布《人工智慧主權白皮書》，詳細說明歐洲在AI發展上的獨立路徑。白皮書將涵蓋技術標準、資料治理與產業合作等面向，目標是減少對美國與中國AI技術的依賴。歐洲數位產業聯盟（EDIA）呼籲成員國加快投資本土AI研發，並建立跨國資料共享機制，以強化歐洲在全球AI競爭中的話語權。
