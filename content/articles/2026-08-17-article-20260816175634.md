  # EU Pushes for Space Sector Integration, Critical Raw Materials Policy Bolsters Space Industry
  
  > The European Commission plans to institutionalize a "European Space Team" based on European Space Agency data and invest nearly €3 billion through the RESourceEU initiative to strengthen the space industry's critical raw materials supply chain.
  
  The European Commission is actively advancing a cross-border integration strategy for the space sector, planning to further institutionalize existing cooperation models by forming a unified "European Space Team." This move aims to connect the European Commission, member states, the European Space Agency (ESA), research institutions, and industry to create a tighter space policy coordination mechanism. According to the OUTLOOK Technology Development Observatory platform, the European Commission will propose a tailored regulatory framework based on ESA data to promote the long-term development of the EU space economy.
  
  Regarding critical raw materials, the EU has already announced the RESourceEU initiative based on the Critical Raw Materials Act (CRMA) by the end of 2025 to reduce the industry's vulnerability to geopolitical disruptions. The initiative plans to invest nearly €3 billion within 12 months to support the diversification and localization of critical raw material supply chains. Yahoo News reports that this policy direction contrasts with the fragility of Taiwan's defense industrial base, highlighting Europe's strategic layout for autonomy in critical space and defense materials.
  
  The space industry heavily relies on critical raw materials such as tungsten for satellite propulsion systems, spacecraft structural materials, and precision instrument manufacturing. Through the RESourceEU initiative, the EU not only focuses on ensuring stable access to raw materials but also emphasizes building a complete supply chain from exploration and mining to processing, thereby reducing dependence on single sources. This move will have far-reaching implications for the European Space Agency's mission execution and the development of the commercial space industry.
  
  Analysts believe that the institutionalization of the "European Space Team" will help the EU consolidate a common stance in international space competition and enhance overall competitiveness in areas such as technology standards, launch services, and satellite navigation. Whether the EU can complete the legislative process for the regulatory framework by 2026 will be an important indicator for observing the effectiveness of this policy.

# 歐盟力推太空團隊整合 關鍵原物料政策助太空產業

> 歐盟委員會擬以歐洲太空總署資料為基礎，推動「歐洲太空團隊」制度化，並透過RESourceEU行動計畫投入近30億歐元，強化太空產業關鍵原物料供應鏈。

歐盟委員會正積極推動太空領域的跨國整合策略，計畫將現有的合作模式進一步制度化，組成統一的「歐洲太空團隊」（Space Team Europe）。此舉旨在串聯歐盟委員會、各會員國、歐洲太空總署（ESA）、研究機構及產業界，形成更緊密的太空政策協調機制。根據OUTLOOK科技發展觀測平臺的報導，歐盟委員會將以ESA的資料為基礎，提出量身訂做的監管框架，以推動歐盟太空經濟的長期發展。
在關鍵原物料方面，歐盟為降低產業鏈受地在地緣政治衝擊的風險，已於2025年底公佈基於《關鍵原物料法》（Critical Raw Material Act, CRMA）的RESourceEU行動計畫。該計畫規劃於12個月內投入近30億歐元，支援關鍵原物料的供應鏈多元化與本土化。Yahoo News報導指出，此政策動向與臺灣國防工業基礎的脆弱性形成對比，凸顯歐洲在太空與國防關鍵物資自主上的戰略佈局。
太空產業高度依賴鎢等關鍵原物料，用於衛星推進系統、太空器結構材料及精密儀器製造。歐盟透過RESourceEU行動計畫，不僅關注原物料的取得穩定性，更著重於建立從探勘、開採到加工的完整供應鏈，以降低對單一來源的依賴。此舉對歐洲太空總署的執行任務及商業太空產業的發展具有深遠影響。
分析人士認為，「歐洲太空團隊」的制度化將有助於歐盟在國際太空競爭中凝聚共同立場，並在技術標準、發射服務及衛星導航等領域提升整體競爭力。然而，如何平衡會員國之間的利益分配、協調ESA與歐盟委員會的職權劃分，仍是未來政策推動上需要克服的挑戰。歐盟能否在2026年內完成監管框架的立法程式，將是觀察此政策成效的重要指標。
