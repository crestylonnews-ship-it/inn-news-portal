  # Africa's Space Industry Sees Development Opportunities as Multiple Nations Join Hands to Expand Cosmic Exploration
  
  > The African Union is pushing to establish a space agency, while multiple countries accelerate their aerospace佈局. Nations such as South Africa, Egypt, and Nigeria are actively engaging in international cooperation, and the prospects for Africa's space industry development are promising.
  
  Africa's space industry is approaching a critical turning point in its development. According to a report by CUP Media, the African Union held the 2023 New Space Africa Conference this week and plans to establish the African Space Agency in Cairo, the capital of Egypt, hoping to gain greater voice on the international stage through cross-border cooperation initiatives. The report also noted that among the seven continents worldwide, Africa remains the only one without a rocket launch site, indicating that there is still enormous room for development in Africa's aerospace infrastructure.
  
  In terms of specific projects, a report from the China-Africa SPS Cooperation Information Network shows that South Africa is leading a collaboration with eight African countries to build the Square Kilometre Array radio telescope, the world's largest integrated aperture telescope, which serves as a crucial astronomical observation infrastructure in Africa. Additionally, South Africa and Egypt are tied for the most operational satellites in Africa, each possessing 13 satellites. Nigeria is one of the first African countries to formulate a national space policy, demonstrating the nation's emphasis on the aerospace sector.
  
  International cooperation has become a key driver of Africa's space development. A report from news.china.com indicates that the Africa2Moon project, Africa's first lunar exploration initiative, will launch alongside China's Chang'e-8 mission. Kara Mitchell, the Africa2Moon project's負責人 for Africa, stated that participating in the Chang'e-8 mission is a historic milestone for the African continent, with significance far exceeding that of a simple moon landing. Although Africa2Moon is Africa's first lunar exploration program, it has already set long-term scientific goals, reflecting the continent's aspiration to explore the depths of the universe.
  
  An analysis by the Institute for Global Dialogue suggests that Africa's increasingly active participation in space-related initiatives brings rich opportunities for cultural, economic, educational, and scientific cooperation. These developments not only contribute to progress within Africa but also strengthen the continent's diplomatic relations on the global stage. Drawing on reports from various media outlets, Africa's space industry is transitioning from individual national efforts toward a regional cooperation model, gradually building autonomous aerospace capabilities through pathways such as establishing regional space agencies, participating in international lunar exploration missions, and constructing large-scale astronomical facilities.

# 非洲太空事業迎發展契機 多國攜手拓展宇宙探索

> 非洲聯盟推動設立太空總署，多國加速航天佈局，南非、埃及、奈及利亞等國積極參與國際合作，非洲太空產業發展前景可期。

非洲太空事業正迎來重要發展轉捩點。根據《CUP媒體》報導，非洲聯盟於本週舉辦2023新太空非洲大會，並計劃在埃及首都開羅設立非洲太空總署，期望透過跨國合作計劃，讓非洲在國際舞臺獲得更多發言權。該報導同時指出，縱觀全球七大洲，目前只有非洲尚未擁有火箭發射場，顯示非洲在航天基礎設施方面仍有巨大發展空間。
在具體專案方面，《中國非洲國家SPS合作資訊網》報導顯示，南非正領頭與非洲八國合作建設世界最大的綜合孔徑望遠鏡——平方公里陣列射電望遠鏡，此為非洲重要的天文觀測基礎建設。此外，南非與埃及在軌衛星數量並列非洲之冠，兩國各自擁有13顆衛星。奈及利亞則是非洲最早制定國家太空政策的國家之一，展現該國對航天領域的重視。
國際合作成為非洲太空發展的重要推手。《news.china.com》報導指出，Africa2Moon計畫作為非洲首個探月專案，將隨中國嫦娥八號任務升空。該計畫非洲負責人卡拉·米切爾表示，參與嫦娥八號任務對非洲大陸而言是歷史性里程碑，其價值遠超單純登月意義。儘管Africa2Moon是非洲首個探月計劃，但已設定長遠科學目標，展現非洲大陸探索宇宙深處的渴望。
《Institute For Global Dialogue》分析認為，非洲日益積極參與太空相關倡議，為文化、經濟、教育及科學合作帶來豐富機遇。這些發展不僅有助於非洲國內進步，更能強化非洲在全球舞臺上的外交關係。綜合各媒體報導，非洲太空事業正從單一國家行動轉向區域合作模式，透過設立區域性航天機構、參與國際探月任務、建設大型天文設施等途徑，逐步建立自主航天能力。
