  > The Department of Political Science and Law at National Kaohsiung University held its third International Symposium on the Middle East and Africa on the 8th, focusing on topics such as Middle East conflicts, the Red Sea crisis, AI governance, and semiconductor diplomacy, exploring dialogue on crisis, governance, and reconstruction amid global order transformations.
  
  The Department of Political Science and Law at National Kaohsiung University (hereinafter referred to as the Department of Political Science and Law) held the "3rd International Conference on Middle East and Africa" on May 8, 2026. The conference focused on the multiple challenges brought about by the rapid changes in the current international situation. The theme of the symposium was "Embedded Governance: Dialogue on Crisis, Governance, and Reconstruction Amid Global Order Shifts," seeking to examine how regional conflicts in the Middle East and Africa transcend geographical boundaries and affect key issues such as global supply chains, artificial intelligence governance, and semiconductor diplomacy.
  
  According to information provided on the National Kaohsiung University Sustainability Report website, this symposium paid special attention to the ongoing Middle East conflicts and the Red Sea crisis, as well as how these regional conflicts produce chain reactions affecting global economic and security structures. The Department of Political Science and Law pointed out that the rapid changes in the current international situation have caused the impact of regional conflicts to extend beyond traditional geopolitical frameworks into new areas such as technology governance and industrial chain restructuring. Participating scholars believe that the turmoil in the Middle East not only affects local security but also causes substantial impacts on global trade and supply chain stability through channels such as the disruption of Red Sea shipping routes and fluctuations in energy prices.

The analysis was written by Cui Jinkui, associate professor and director of the Institute of International Politics at Chung Hsing University, emphasizing that the interweaving of multiple forces in the Middle East makes the situation even more difficult to predict.

The symposium also reflects the Taiwan academic community's attention to Middle East and Africa issues and its efforts to promote regional understanding and cooperation through international academic exchanges.

# 高雄大學辦中東非洲研討會 聚焦全球秩序變動危機治理

> 國立高雄大學政治法律學系8日舉辦第三屆中東與非洲國際研討會，聚焦中東衝突、紅海危機、AI治理與半導體外交等議題，探討全球秩序變動下的危機、治理與重建對話。

國立高雄大學政治法律學系（簡稱政法系）於2026年5月8日舉辦「第三屆中東與非洲國際研討會」（3rd International Conference on Middle East and Africa），會場聚焦當前國際局勢快速變動所帶來的多重挑戰。研討會主題為「鑲嵌的治理：全球秩序變動下的危機、治理與重建對話」，試圖從中東與非洲區域的視角，檢視區域衝突如何跨越地理疆界，影響全球供應鏈、人工智慧治理與半導體外交等關鍵議題。
根據國立高雄大學永續報告網提供的資訊，本次研討會特別關注中東衝突與紅海危機的持續延燒，以及這些區域性衝突如何對全球經濟與安全結構產生連鎖效應。政法系方面指出，當前國際局勢的快速變動已使區域衝突的影響超越傳統的地緣政治框架，延伸至科技治理、產業鏈重組等新領域。與會學者認為，中東地區的動盪不僅影響當地安全，更透過紅海航線中斷、能源價格波動等途徑，對全球貿易與供應鏈穩定造成實質衝擊。
展望基金會於2025年發布的〈2025年中東局勢發展與展望〉分析指出，加薩戰爭自2023年10月爆發迄今已持續超過一年，而敘利亞總統阿薩德於2024年12月遭反政府勢力沙姆解放組織（Hayat Tahrir al-Sham, HTS）推翻後，敘利亞未來政局發展對本就複雜與變化快速的中東地緣政治格局帶來更多不確定性。該分析由中興大學國際政治研究所副教授兼所長崔進揆撰寫，強調中東地區的多方勢力交織使局勢更加難以預測。
資訊咖平臺亦於2026年7月30日刊載〈地緣政治：中東局勢的複雜性與挑戰〉一文，進一步探討中東地緣政治的結構性難題。
本次高雄大學研討會與相關分析形成呼應，顯示學術界正積極關注中東局勢對全球治理體系的深遠影響。與會學者預期，透過跨領域對話與國際合作，有望為當前全球秩序變動下的危機處理與重建工作提供新的思維框架。研討會亦反映臺灣學術界對中東與非洲議題的重視，以及透過國際學術交流促進區域理解與合作的努力。
