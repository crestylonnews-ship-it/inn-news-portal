  # Asia's Economy Faces Tariff Uncertainty, Multiple Institutions See Resilience and Room for Policy Easing
  
  > The IMF notes Asia's economy has shown resilient performance beyond expectations, while Goldman Sachs forecasts low inflation pressure in 2025, creating room for monetary easing; experts call for boosting domestic demand rebalancing and deepening regional integration.
  
  The International Monetary Fund (IMF) recently released a report stating that Asia's economic growth is facing severe tests from tariffs and uncertainty, but benefiting from the front-loading effect of exports, technology investment, and policy support, the overall economic performance has unexpectedly demonstrated resilience. The IMF article, written by Andrea Pescatori and Krishna Srinivasan in October 2025, emphasized that to maintain strong and sustained economic growth, the Asia region must currently promote demand-driven economic rebalancing and further deepen the regional integration process.
  
  Goldman Sachs proposed analysis and forecasts on ten key questions in its 2025 Asia Economic Outlook report, which pointed out that overall inflation pressure in Asia is relatively low, creating operational space for monetary easing policies in most countries. However, the report also cautioned that inflation levels in countries such as India and the Philippines remain relatively high, which may limit the scope of their policy easing. Meanwhile, China's core CPI year-on-year growth rate continues to decline, and the 10-year government bond yield has also hit a new low, further supporting the room for implementing easing policies.
  
  At the industry and trade practice level, a survey report released by Thomson Reuters extensively covered 17 industries, including pharmaceuticals, life sciences, consumer goods, and e-commerce. The report's insights show that Asia's trade professionals have demonstrated resilience, adaptability, and forward-looking vision in supply chain management, actively adjusting strategies to respond to changes in the global trade landscape.
  
  Regarding regional economic and trade frameworks and policy trends, the APEC Research Center held its annual "Asia-Pacific Regional Situation Analysis and Outlook" symposium on December 20, 2024, inviting domestic and international experts and scholars to conduct in-depth discussions on global free trade agreement (FTA) trends and major Asia-Pacific regional economic and trade frameworks. Experts also analyzed the far-reaching impact of US-China trade policy changes on global supply chains and discussed emerging trends and future challenges in the Asia-Pacific region.
  
  In addition, the "Asia Business and Trade Regulations" column launched by the Hong Kong Trade Development Council's Economic and Trade Research continues to provide the latest developments in trade policies and regulations in South Asia and Southeast Asia, focusing on changes in trade and investment regulations related to mainland China and Hong Kong, as well as the local business environment overview. The column is compiled by Dezan Shira & Associates commissioned by the Hong Kong Trade Development Council.
  
  Synthesizing the views of various institutions, although Asia's economy faces external tariff pressure and geopolitical uncertainty in 2025, the overall inflation environment remains relatively controllable, leaving room for monetary policy easing across countries. Experts generally believe that for Asia to maintain long-term stable growth, in addition to continuously promoting technology investment and supply chain strategy adjustments, deepening regional economic and trade integration and expanding domestic demand markets will be the key directions.

# 亞洲經濟迎戰關稅不確定性 多機構看好韌性與寬鬆空間

> IMF指出亞洲經濟展現超預期韌性，高盛預測2025年通膨壓力較低，為貨幣寬鬆提供空間；專家呼籲推動內需再平衡與深化區域整合。

國際貨幣基金組織（IMF）近日發布報告指出，亞洲經濟增長正面臨關稅與不確定性的嚴峻考驗，但受惠於出口前置效應、技術投資以及政策支援，整體經濟表現出乎意料地展現韌性。IMF文章由Andrea Pescatori與Krishna Srinivasan於2025年10月撰寫，強調為維持強勁且持久的經濟增長，亞洲地區目前必須推動以內需為導向的經濟再平衡，並進一步深化區域一體化程式。
高盛在2025年亞洲經濟展望報告中提出十個關鍵問題的分析與預測，報告指出亞洲整體通膨壓力相對較低，為多數國家的貨幣寬鬆政策創造了操作空間。然而，報告也提醒，印度與菲律賓等國的通膨水準仍然偏高，可能限制其政策寬鬆的幅度。與此同時，中國的核心CPI同比增速持續下降，10年期國債收益率亦創下新低，進一步支援了寬鬆政策的實施空間。
在產業與貿易實務層面，湯姆森路透社（Thomson Reuters）發布的調查報告廣泛觸及17個產業，涵蓋製藥業、生命科學、消費品及電子商務等領域。報告洞察顯示，亞洲的貿易專業人士在供應鏈管理上展現出韌性、適應能力與前瞻性的眼光，正積極調整策略以應對全球貿易格局的變化。
針對區域經貿架構與政策趨勢，APEC研究中心於2024年12月20日舉辦年度「亞太區域情勢分析與展望」研討會，邀請國內外專家學者深入探討全球自由貿易協定（FTA）趨勢及亞太區域主要經貿架構。專家亦剖析美中貿易政策變化對全球供應鏈的深遠影響，並針對亞太區域新興趨勢及未來挑戰進行討論。
此外，香港貿易發展局經貿研究推出的《亞洲商貿法規》專欄，持續提供南亞及東南亞地區貿易政策與法規的最新動態，聚焦與中國內地及香港相關的貿易與投資法規變動，以及當地營商環境概況，該專欄由香港貿易發展局委託Dezan Shira & Associates編撰。
綜合各機構觀點，亞洲經濟在2025年雖面臨外部關稅壓力與地緣政治不確定性，但整體通膨環境相對可控，為各國貨幣政策留下寬鬆空間。專家普遍認為，亞洲若要維持長期穩定增長，除持續推動技術投資與供應鏈策略調整外，深化區域經貿整合與擴大內需市場，將是關鍵方向。
