  The European Commission published its energy policy direction on its official website, emphasizing that energy policy is crucial for Europe's competitiveness, security, and the achievement of climate neutrality goals. According to Article 194 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, energy policy is a shared competence of the EU and its member states. The EU's primary energy policy objective is to ensure that European citizens and businesses have access to reliable, affordable, and clean energy. This policy framework covers multiple dimensions, including energy efficiency, renewable energy development, energy infrastructure integration, and market mechanism design, aiming to balance energy security with emission reduction commitments.
  
  The European Environment Agency published the *2025 European Environment Report* on September 28, 2025. Its chapter on climate change mitigation and adaptation notes that the risks of climate change to energy security vary significantly across Europe. The report's analysis shows that, overall, Southern Europe faces increasingly rising risks of high temperatures, drought, and water scarcity, a trend that could affect the operation of local power generation facilities, cooling water supplies, and the stability of energy infrastructure. In contrast, other regions of Europe face different types of climate impacts, indicating that the EU needs to adopt differentiated strategies in addressing the link between climate change and energy security.
  
  The EU Energy Directorate simultaneously released a joint communication document outlining the EU's global climate and energy vision, with the goal of ensuring Europe's competitive position in global markets and accelerating the clean energy transition. The document reflects the EU's attempt to maintain influence in international energy markets while advancing its domestic energy structure toward low-carbon pathways. The European Environment Agency's website also notes that the EU has committed to achieving climate neutrality by 2050 to realize the temperature targets set by the Paris Agreement. To meet these long-term goals, the pace of greenhouse gas emission reductions must accelerate to align with the階段性 milestones set for 2030.

However, the pace of specific policy implementation, investment scales, and technology pathway choices in each country remain subjects for ongoing observation and tracking.

# 歐盟發布氣候能源願景 南歐面臨高溫乾旱風險

> 歐盟委員會與歐洲環境署近日發布氣候與能源相關報告，指出歐盟致力於2050年達成氣候中性，同時南歐地區面臨高溫、乾旱與水資源短缺風險上升，氣候變遷對能源安全影響因地區而異。

歐盟委員會於官網公佈能源政策方向，強調能源政策對於歐洲競爭力、安全以及達成氣候中性目標至關重要。根據《歐洲聯盟運作條約》第194條規定，能源政策為歐盟與成員國共享權責。歐盟主要能源政策目標為確保歐洲民眾與企業能夠取得可靠、負擔得起且清潔的能源。此項政策框架涵蓋能源效率、可再生能源發展、能源基礎建設整合以及市場機制設計等多個層面，旨在同時兼顧能源安全與減排承諾。
歐洲環境署於2025年9月28日出版《2025年歐洲環境報告》，其中氣候變遷減緩與調適章節指出，氣候變遷對能源安全的風險在歐洲各地呈現顯著差異。報告分析顯示，整體而言，南歐地區面臨高溫、乾旱與水資源短缺的風險日益上升，此種趨勢可能對當地發電設施運作、冷卻用水供應以及能源基礎建設穩定性造成影響。相對而言，歐洲其他區域面臨的氣候衝擊型別有所不同，顯示歐盟內部在應對氣候變遷與能源安全連結議題上，需採取差異化策略。
歐盟能源總署同步發布聯合通訊檔案，闡述歐盟全球氣候與能源願景，目標在於確保歐洲於世界市場的競爭地位，並加速清潔能源轉型程式。該檔案反映歐盟試圖在國際能源市場中維持影響力，同時推動國內能源結構向低碳方向邁進。歐洲環境署網站亦指出，歐盟承諾於2050年達成氣候中性，以實踐《巴黎協定》所設定之溫度目標。為達成長期目標，溫室氣體排放減少速度必須加速，方能符合2030年階段性里程碑要求。
綜合各項官方發布資訊，歐盟層級已明確將氣候目標與能源安全緊密連結，並透過政策框架與資料分析雙軌並行方式推動轉型。歐洲環境署報告所揭示的地區差異性風險，為歐盟成員國制定個別適應策略提供參考依據。然而，各國具體政策落實進度、投資規模與技術路徑選擇，仍待後續觀察與追蹤。
