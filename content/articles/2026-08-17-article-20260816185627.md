  Thomson Reuters recently released its report "State of Asia as a Global Trade Leader," analyzing the challenges and opportunities Asia faces amid a rapidly changing geopolitical and economic landscape. The report notes that the Asia region accounts for over one-third of the world's import and export volume and has been the primary engine of global trade growth over the past decade, with trade performance driven by regional supply chain integration and expanding demand from emerging markets. The report emphasizes that while Asian countries maintain their trade advantages, they must address multiple pressures including geopolitical tensions, escalating technology controls, and the green transition.
  
  The APEC Research Center held its annual "Asia-Pacific Regional Situation Analysis and Outlook" symposium on December 20, 2024, inviting domestic and international experts to conduct in-depth discussions on global free trade agreement (FTA) trends and major Asia-Pacific economic and trade frameworks. The conference focused on the impact of US-China trade policy changes on global supply chains and analyzed emerging trends in the Asia-Pacific region. Participating scholars believe that mechanisms such as the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership (RCEP) and CPTPP are reshaping the Asia-Pacific economic and trade landscape, and that countries must reduce dependence on any single market through diversified FTA networks.
  
  The Ministry of Economic Affairs' International Trade Department website also indicates that our country continues to advance bilateral trade agreement negotiations and strengthen the management of high-tech goods and trade regulation adaptation, in response to the wave of regional economic and trade integration.
  
  Regarding supply chain restructuring, both the Thomson Reuters report and the APEC symposium noted that Asian manufacturing faces dual tests of rising costs and technological competition. Some scholars pointed out that Southeast Asian countries continue to attract foreign investment through labor advantages, while advanced East Asian economies are moving toward upgrading to high value-added industries. Data from the Ministry of Economic Affairs' International Trade Department shows that our country is guiding the development of the exhibition industry and strengthening trade management mechanisms to help enterprises seize new regional economic and trade opportunities. Experts warn that Asia's future trade leadership will depend on whether countries can strike a balance between open trade and risk management, while also strengthening their capacity to align with digital trade and sustainable development rules.

# 湯森路透報告：亞洲貿易引擎地位穩固 專家籲關注供應鏈重塑

> 湯森路透發布報告指出，亞洲佔全球進出口額逾三分之一，過去十年為世界貿易成長主引擎。APEC研究中心研討會同步探討區域FTA趨勢與美中政策對供應鏈影響。

湯森路透近日發布《亞洲作為全球貿易領導者的現狀調查》報告，分析亞洲在急遽變化的地緣政治與經濟環境中所面臨的挑戰與機會。報告指出，亞洲地區佔世界進出口額的三分之一以上，過去十年來一直是全球貿易成長的主要引擎，其貿易表現的驅動力來自區域內產業鏈整合與新興市場需求擴張。該報告強調，亞洲各國在維持貿易優勢的同時，需應對地緣政治緊張、技術管制升級及綠色轉型等多重壓力。
APEC研究中心於2024年12月20日舉辦年度「亞太區域情勢分析與展望」研討會，邀請國內外專家學者深入探討全球自由貿易協定（FTA）趨勢及亞太區域主要經貿架構。會議聚焦美中貿易政策變化對全球供應鏈的影響，並剖析亞太區域新興趨勢。與會學者認為，區域全面經濟夥伴關係協定（RCEP）與CPTPP等機制正重塑亞太經貿版圖，各國須透過多元FTA網路降低對單一市場的依賴。
經濟部國際貿易署網站亦顯示，我國持續推動雙邊貿易協定談判，並加強高科技貨品管理與貿易法規調適，以因應區域經貿整合浪潮。
針對供應鏈重組議題，湯森路透報告與APEC研討會均提及亞洲製造業面臨成本上升與技術競爭雙重考驗。部分學者指出，東南亞國家憑藉勞動力優勢持續吸引外資，但東亞先進經濟體則朝向高附加值產業升級。經濟部國際貿易署資料顯示，我國正輔導會展產業發展並強化貿易管理機制，以協助企業掌握區域經貿新契機。專家提醒，未來亞洲貿易領導地位將取決於各國能否在開放經貿與風險管控間取得平衡，同時強化數位貿易與永續發展規則的接軌能力。
