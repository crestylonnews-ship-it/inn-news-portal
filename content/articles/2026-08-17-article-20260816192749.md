  # Asia-Pacific Public Health Cooperation Deepens, Global Governance Strategy Aligns with Regional Action
  
  > Between 2024 and 2025, multiple public health forums and workshops were held across the Asia-Pacific region, focusing on health sustainability, non-communicable disease prevention, and cross-border talent development, while aligning with the World Health Organization's 2025–2028 Global Health Strategy to strengthen regional cooperation and governance frameworks.
  
  Since its establishment in July 2023, the Taiwan Association for Global Health Initiatives (TAGHI) has continued to promote global health advocacy and international exchange. The association collaborates with the Ministry of Health and Welfare on the "Global Health Trend Analysis and Strategic Recommendations for Taiwan" project, analyzing trends in major global health issues, assessing their impact on Taiwan, and formulating strategic recommendations to enhance Taiwan's position in the global health arena. This initiative responds to Taiwan's role as a member of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC), calling for proficiency in global health governance affairs and the demonstration of expertise, while connecting government department policies with the development of global health governance.
  
  In terms of regional cooperation, the National Health Administration of the Ministry of Health and Welfare, from August 6 to 7, 2024, co-hosted the "Asia-Pacific Health Promotion and Non-Communicable Disease Prevention Education Workshop" with the Asia-Pacific Academic Consortium for Public Health (APACPH) Center for Health Promotion Collaboration, TAGHI, and the Institute of Population Health Sciences at the National Health Research Institutes. Experts from four countries were invited to participate, promoting cross-border public health exchange and talent development in the Asia-Pacific region.
  
  Subsequently, on June 13, 2025, the College of Public Health and the Center for Health and Welfare Policy Research at Taipei Medical University hosted the "2025 Public Health Summit Forum," themed "Building a Healthy and Sustainable Future Together." The event brought together leading scholars and experts from the United States, Japan, Thailand, Malaysia, and Indonesia to discuss health risks posed by climate change and pathways for global cooperation.
  
  The World Health Organization (WHO) released the executive summary of the "2025–2028 Global Health Strategy" in 2025, setting an ambitious agenda aimed at putting the world back on track to achieve health-related Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and promoting health equity and health system resilience. A report by Taiwan's New Society Think Tank indicated that Taiwan should actively participate in global health governance and leverage platforms such as APEC to exert influence. While assessments of regional cooperation effectiveness by different media outlets remain to be further observed, multiple sources collectively confirm that the Asia-Pacific region is strengthening cross-border public health exchange and strategic alignment.
  
  Subsequent observations indicate that the collaboration model between Taiwan's civil organizations and government agencies may serve as a reference case for regional health governance. The alignment between WHO strategies and Asia-Pacific local activities will influence future resource allocation and policy priorities. The emphasis placed by all parties on health sustainability and non-communicable disease prevention reflects a shift in global health issues from emergency response to long-term structural challenges.

# 亞太公共衛生合作深化 全球治理策略呼應區域行動

> 2024 至 2025 年間，亞太地區多場公共衛生論壇與工作坊相繼舉行，聚焦健康永續、非傳染病防治與跨國人才培育，並呼應世界衛生組織 2025–2028 年全球衛生策略，強化區域合作與治理框架。

臺灣全球衛生推動協會（TAGHI）自 2023 年 7 月成立以來，持續推動全球衛生倡議與國際交流。該協會與衛生福利部合作開展「全球衛生趨勢分析及我國策略建議」計畫，分析全球衛生重大議題趨勢，評估對臺灣影響並制定策略建議，以提升臺灣在全球衛生場域中的地位。此舉呼應臺灣作為亞太經濟合作組織（APEC）成員，應熟捻全球衛生治理事務並展現長才，同時串連政府部門政策與全球衛生治理發展。
在區域合作方面，衛生福利部國民健康署於 2024 年 8 月 6 日至 7 日，與亞太公共衛生學術聯盟健康促進協作中心（APACPH CCHP）、臺灣全球衛生推動協會及國家衛生研究院群體健康科學研究所共同主辦「亞太健康促進與非傳染病防治教育訓練工作坊」，邀請來自 4 個國家的專家參與，促進亞太地區公共衛生跨國交流及人才培育。
隨後，臺北醫學大學公共衛生學院與健康與福利政策研究中心於 2025 年 6 月 13 日舉辦「2025 公共衛生高峰論壇」，以「攜手共建健康與永續的未來」為主題，集結來自美國、日本、泰國、馬來西亞與印尼等國的頂尖學者與專家，探討氣候變遷引發的健康風險與全球合作途徑。
世界衛生組織（WHO）於 2025 年發布《2025–2028 年全球衛生策略》執行摘要，設定雄心勃勃的議程，旨在讓世界重新回到實現健康相關永續發展目標（SDGs）的軌道，並促進健康公平與衛生系統韌性。臺灣新社會智庫報告指出，臺灣應積極參與全球衛生治理，並透過 APEC 等平臺發揮影響力。不同媒體對區域合作成效的評估尚待進一步觀察，但多份資料共同確認亞太地區正加強公共衛生跨國交流與策略對接。
後續觀察顯示，臺灣民間組織與政府機構的合作模式，可能成為區域衛生治理的參考案例。WHO 策略與亞太本土活動的銜接，將影響未來資源分配與政策優先順序。各方對健康永續與非傳染病防治的重視，反映全球衛生議題正從應急應對轉向長期結構性挑戰。
