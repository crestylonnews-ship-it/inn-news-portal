  # African Governance Modernization Draws Attention; Global South Development Issues Gain Focus
  
  > The Chinese Academy of African Studies hosted its 19th Africa Forum, focusing on governance and growth in the Global South; scholars explored Africa's autonomous development and new characteristics of Western engagement, with AI governance emerging as a new focal point.
  
  On April 30, 2026, the Chinese Academy of African Studies hosted its 19th Africa Forum, inviting Professor Busani Ngcaweni, Director of the Centre for Public Policy and African Studies at the University of Johannesburg, to deliver a lecture titled "Frontiers of State and International Development: Revisiting Governance and Growth in the Global South," sparking extensive discussion among scholars and policymakers on Africa's governance pathways. The forum focused on cutting-edge issues in governance models and economic growth among Global South countries, highlighting Africa's increasingly important role in the international development agenda.
  
  According to an analysis published on the Chinese Social Sciences Net, against the backdrop of unprecedented changes in a century, Africa's sense of autonomy continues to strengthen. Guided by the governance goals set forth in the UN's 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development and the AU's Agenda 2063, Africa is accelerating internal unity and cooperation, strengthening regional integration, and cultivating and enhancing its autonomous governance capacity. The analysis also examined the new characteristics and effectiveness of Western powers' engagement in African governance against the backdrop of Africa's autonomous development, noting that the rise of Africa's autonomous consciousness is reshaping the interaction model between external forces and the African continent.
  
  The 28th issue of the *Fudan Public Administration Review*, hosted by Fudan University's School of International Relations and Public Affairs, was published under the theme "African-Style Governance Modernization: Multiple Scales." As a CSSCI collection and AMI core collection, the issue features executive editors who are leading figures across various fields of public administration. This publication focuses on the multiple dimensions of African governance modernization, reflecting the sustained attention and academic accumulation of Chinese scholars on African governance issues.
  
  Meanwhile, AI governance is emerging as a new focal point in Africa's development agenda. According to a report from Fudan University's School of International Relations and Public Affairs, artificial intelligence is reshaping global industrial division of labor and governance structures as a foundational force. As a key arena for AI development in the "Global South," Africa's formulation and implementation of industrial policies not only concern the transformative momentum of its local economy and society but also profoundly affect the fairness and inclusiveness of global AI governance. Against the backdrop of a structurally differentiated "global AI divide," how Africa can break through institutional and economic constraints to explore development pathways has become a topic of concern for the international community.
  
  Analyses suggest that African governance modernization involves not only internal institutional building but is also closely linked to the overall development agenda of the Global South. With the strengthening of Africa's autonomous consciousness and the advancement of regional integration, Africa's voice in the international governance system is expected to further increase, while governance cooperation in emerging technology fields such as AI will become an important variable influencing the future global development landscape.

# 非洲治理現代化受矚目 全球南方發展議題引關注

> 中國非洲研究院舉辦第十九屆非洲壇，聚焦全球南方治理與增長；學界探討非洲自主發展與西方參與新特點，AI治理成新興焦點。

中國非洲研究院於2026年4月30日舉辦第十九屆非洲壇，邀請南非約翰內斯堡大學公共政策與非洲研究中心主任布薩尼·恩卡韋尼（Busani Ngcaweni）教授，以「國家與國際發展前沿：重新審視全球南方的治理與增長」為題發表演講，引發學界與政策圈對非洲治理路徑的廣泛討論。此次論壇聚焦全球南方國家在治理模式與經濟增長方面的前沿議題，凸顯非洲在國際發展議程中日益重要的角色。
根據中國社會科學網刊載的分析指出，在百年未有之大變局下，非洲自主意識持續增強，並依據聯合國《2030年可持續發展議程》以及非盟《2063年議程》設定的治理目標，加快內部團結合作，加強區域一體化建設，自主治理能力得到培育和提升。該分析同時探討西方大國在非洲自主發展背景下參與非洲治理的新特點及其成效，指出非洲自主意識的崛起正在重塑外部勢力與非洲大陸的互動模式。
復旦大學國際關係與公共事務學院主辦的《復旦公共行政評論》第28輯以「非洲式治理現代化：多重尺度」為主題出版，該輯為CSSCI集刊和AMI核心集刊，由公共行政各個領域的學科帶頭人擔任執行編輯。此出版物聚焦非洲治理現代化的多重維度，反映中國學界對非洲治理議題的持續關注與學術積累。
與此同時，人工智慧治理正成為非洲發展議程中的新興焦點。據復旦大學國際關係與公共事務學院相關報導指出，人工智慧正以基礎性力量重塑全球產業分工與治理格局，非洲作為「全球南方」AI發展的關鍵陣地，其產業政策的制定與落地，不僅關乎本土經濟社會的轉型動能，更深刻影響全球AI治理的公平性與包容性。在「全球AI鴻溝」呈現結構性分化的背景下，非洲如何突破製度與經濟約束探索發展路徑，成為國際社會關注的課題。
分析認為，非洲治理現代化不僅涉及內部製度建設，也與全球南方整體發展議程密切相關。隨著非洲自主意識增強與區域一體化推進，非洲在國際治理體系中的話語權有望進一步提升，而AI等新興技術領域的治理合作，將成為影響未來全球發展格局的重要變數。
