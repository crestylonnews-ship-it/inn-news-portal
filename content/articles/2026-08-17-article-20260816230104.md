  # European Parliament Passes Foreign and Security Policy Report, Calls for EU-Taiwan Strategic Cooperation
  
  > The European Parliament, through its annual report on the Common Foreign and Security Policy, condemned China's military provocations against Taiwan and called on the EU to establish a strategic partnership with Taiwan, urging the swift launch of bilateral trade negotiations.
  
  On the 18th, the European Parliament adopted the annual implementation reports for the EU's Common Foreign and Security Policy (CFSP) and Common Security and Defence Policy (CSDP), with 407 votes in favor and 459 votes in favor, respectively. The Lens reported that the reports condemned China's continued military provocations against Taiwan and called on the European Commission to establish a strategic partnership with Taiwan and to launch bilateral trade negotiations as soon as possible. This move is seen as a further strengthening of EU-Taiwan relations on the eve of the Lunar New Year, reflecting the European Parliament's emphasis on Indo-Pacific security issues.
  
  Official EU documents show that the Common Security and Defence Policy (CSDP) is an important component of the EU's Common Foreign and Security Policy, formerly known as the European Security and Defence Policy (ESDP), and is designed to guide EU defense and military operations. The European External Action Service (EEAS) explained that the CSDP enables the EU to play a leading role in peacekeeping operations, conflict prevention, and the enhancement of international security, integrating civilian and military resources to respond to crises. Wikipedia adds that the policy is inherited from NATO's European Security and Defence Identity, but emphasizes the development of an autonomous EU defence framework.
  
  Analysts believe that the European Parliament's call through the resolution to deepen cooperation with Taiwan may influence the direction of the EU's China policy. Although the report does not specify concrete cooperation details, the proposal to launch bilateral trade negotiations has drawn attention. The official positions of the European Commission and the European External Action Service focus on security and defence mechanisms and have not directly addressed the EU-Taiwan relations issue. Future observations will focus on the reactions of EU member states to this resolution and any possible diplomatic countermeasures from China.

# 歐議會透過外交安全報告 籲臺歐建戰略合作

> 歐洲議會透過共同外交與安全政策年度報告，譴責中國對臺軍事挑釁，並呼籲歐盟與臺灣建立戰略合作關係，儘速啟動雙邊貿易談判。

歐洲議會於18日分別以407票與459票同意，透過歐盟「共同外交暨安全政策」（CFSP）與「共同安全暨防禦政策」（CSDP）年度執行報告決議案。關鍵評論網報導指出，報告中譴責中國持續對臺軍事挑釁，並呼籲歐盟執委會與臺灣建立戰略合作關係，儘速啟動雙邊貿易談判。此舉被視為歐盟在農曆春節前夕對臺關係的進一步強化，反映歐洲議會對印太區域安全議題的重視。
歐盟官方資料顯示，共同安全與防務政策（CSDP）為歐盟共同外交與安全政策的重要組成部分，前稱歐洲安全與國防政策（ESDP），旨在指導歐盟國防與軍事行動。歐洲對外行動署（EEAS）說明，CSDP使歐盟能在維和行動、衝突預防及國際安全強化方面發揮領導角色，並整合民事與軍事資源應對危機。維基百科補充，該政策繼承自北約歐洲安全與防務個體，但強調歐盟自主防務框架的發展。
分析認為，歐洲議會透過決議案呼籲與臺灣深化合作，可能影響歐盟對華政策走向。儘管報告未明確提及具體合作細節，但啟動雙邊貿易談判的倡議已引發關注。歐盟執委會與歐洲對外行動署的官方立場聚焦於安全防務機制，未直接回應臺歐關係議題。後續觀察將關注歐盟成員國對此決議的反應，以及中國可能做出的外交反制。
