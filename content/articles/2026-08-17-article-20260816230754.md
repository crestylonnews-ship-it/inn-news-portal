  # North American Economy Faces a Turning Point: Tariff Storms and Regional Integration Dynamics
  
  > The 2026 North American economic outlook shows that U.S. tariff policy and the USMCA review will reshape regional trade dynamics, with Canada facing transition pressures and businesses needing to navigate policy uncertainty with caution.
  
  The North American economy is at a critical juncture in 2026. According to North America Biz, U.S. tariff policy continues to exert a profound impact on the regional economy, and Canada, as the most pressured link in North America's economy, is undergoing a painful transition from dependence to diversification. Although U.S. tariff exemptions remain in place in the short term, the July 2026 review of the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement (USMCA) will continue to generate policy uncertainty, affecting corporate investment decisions and regional supply chain layouts.
  
  In a report titled "Global Economic Outlook for the Second Half of 2025 Against the Background of Tariff Barriers," published on Sohu.com by Minyin International, it was noted that since the Trump administration took office, it has vigorously promoted the "America First" policy, with isolationism and trade protectionism on the rise, and the new equilibrium system established globally since 2023 has been disrupted once again. Friction between the United States and its traditional allies continues to escalate, with tariff policy becoming a key variable shaping the global economic landscape. This trend is particularly pronounced in North America, given the high degree of economic interconnection among the United States, Canada, and Mexico—any changes in trade policy will trigger chain reactions.
  
  When facing policy uncertainty, businesses must carefully distinguish between short-term subsidy benefits and long-term competitiveness. North America Biz emphasized that many companies may fall into the trap of overcapacity after policy support fades, a problem especially prominent in key industries such as automobiles and energy. Canada, as the most vulnerable link in the North American economy, has long relied on the U.S. market and now faces the challenge of repositioning itself. The USMCA review involves not only tariff issues but also the restructuring of regional supply chains and the reallocation of resource competition.
  
  Lenovo Group, in an economic data analysis program, referenced U.S. economic data from April, showing that the U.S. economy is still undergoing structural adjustment. According to a report by the Hong Kong Economic Journal, Lenovo's adjusted profit exceeded US$1 billion, reflecting the adaptability of the technology sector amid tariff barriers. However, overall, North American businesses still need to cope with operational risks brought about by frequent changes in trade policy, facing greater challenges particularly in cross-border supply chain management and market diversification.

# 北美經濟迎變局：關稅風暴與區域整合博弈

> 2026年北美經濟展望顯示，美國關稅政策與USMCA複審將重塑區域貿易格局，加拿大面臨轉型壓力，企業需謹慎應對政策不確定性。

北美經濟在2026年正面臨關鍵轉折點。根據北美商業網（North America Biz）報導，美國關稅政策持續對區域經濟產生深遠影響，加拿大作為北美經濟中最受壓力的環節，正面臨從依賴到多元化的痛苦轉型過程。儘管美國關稅豁免在短期內維持，但2026年7月的《美墨加協定》（USMCA）複審將持續製造政策不確定性，影響企業投資決策與區域供應鏈佈局。
民銀國際在搜狐網發布的《關稅壁壘背景下，2025年下半年全球經濟展望》報告中指出，特朗普政府上臺後大力推行「美國優先」政策，孤立主義與貿易保護主義抬頭，全球自2023年以來建立的新均衡體系再度被打破。美國與傳統盟友的摩擦不斷升級，關稅政策成為影響全球經濟格局的關鍵變數。這一趨勢對北美地區尤為顯著，因為美國與加拿大、墨西哥的經濟聯動性極高，任何貿易政策的變動都會產生連鎖反應。
企業在面對政策不確定性時，必須謹慎區分短期補貼紅利與長期競爭力。北美商業網強調，許多企業在政策退坡後可能陷入產能過剩的困境，這在汽車、能源等關鍵產業尤為突出。加拿大作為北美經濟中最脆弱的環節，其經濟結構長期依賴美國市場，如今正面臨重新定位的挑戰。USMCA複審不僅涉及關稅問題，更關乎區域供應鏈的重組與資源競爭的重新分配。
聯想集團在經濟資料解碼節目中提及美國4月經濟資料，顯示美國經濟仍在經歷結構性調整。香港經濟日報的報導指出，聯想經調整盈利突破10億美元，反映科技產業在關稅壁壘下的適應能力。然而，整體而言，北美企業仍需應對貿易政策頻繁變動帶來的營運風險，特別是在跨國供應鏈管理與市場多元化方面面臨更大挑戰。
