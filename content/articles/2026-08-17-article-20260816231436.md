  # WHO and African Union Renew Strategic Partnership; China-Africa Public Health Aid Continues to Deepen
  
  > The World Health Organization and the African Union have renewed their strategic partnership, with the Africa CDC strengthening disease surveillance capabilities; China continues to support Africa's public health system development through vaccine aid and medical team deployments.
  
  The World Health Organization (WHO) and the African Union (AU) recently announced the renewal of their strategic partnership, aimed at advancing the strengthening of Africa's health systems and disease response capabilities. According to WHO official information, the latest agreement simplifies and strengthens cooperation mechanisms among all AU entities, building on the memorandum of understanding signed in 2019. The AU expressed that it values WHO's core and leadership role in global health and looks forward to deepening the strategic partnership to support shared goals. In its announcement, WHO emphasized that Africa needs to "shift from budgeting for survival to planning for health sovereignty," indicating that both sides will focus on enhancing Africa's autonomous health governance capacity.
  
  The Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention (Africa CDC), as a regional public health institution, continues to strengthen the capabilities and partnerships of health institutions across African countries, committed to data-driven interventions and programs that rapidly and effectively detect and respond to disease threats and outbreaks. The institution's operational model complements the cooperation framework between WHO and the AU, jointly building a public health protection network across the African continent.
  
  The academic community is also paying attention to this issue. The Journal of Public Health in Africa, as a peer-reviewed academic journal, focuses on health issues on the African continent and is committed to moving public health discussions from the background to the forefront, promoting academic and policy dialogue on Africa's fight against disease.
  
  At the international aid level, China's public health cooperation with Africa continues to deepen. China Daily reported that China has long adhered to helping African countries improve medical and health conditions and carried out major projects such as aid to the Africa CDC. Faced with outbreaks of Ebola, novel coronavirus, malaria and other diseases, China has actively provided vaccine assistance and dispatched medical teams and other public health forces, playing an important role in improving the capacity of African countries to guarantee public medical services.
  
  The Forum on China-Africa Cooperation (FOCAC) is also an important platform for China-Africa relations. The New York Times Chinese website reported that the three-day forum aims to showcase Beijing's global influence, with China hoping to strengthen trade with Africa and find new buyers for its goods, while also using the forum to defend its approach to building connections with African countries and handling debt issues.
  
  Synthesizing information from multiple sources, the renewal of the strategic partnership between WHO and the AU focuses on health system strengthening and disease response mechanisms, while the Africa CDC emphasizes regional surveillance and rapid response capabilities. China continues to support Africa's public health development through vaccine assistance, medical team deployments, and infrastructure construction. Although the focus of each party's actions differs, they all point toward enhancing the African continent's capacity to respond to public health crises. Subsequent observations will focus on the specific implementation progress of various cooperation agreements and the availability of funding.

# 世衛與非盟更新戰略合作 中非公共衛生援助持續深化

> 世界衛生組織與非洲聯盟更新戰略夥伴關係，非洲疾控中心強化疾病監測能力；中國透過疫苗援助與醫療隊派遣，持續支援非洲公共衛生體系建設。

世界衛生組織（WHO）與非洲聯盟（非盟）近日宣佈更新戰略夥伴關係，旨在推動非洲衛生系統強化與疾病應對能力。根據世衛組織官方資訊，雙方在2019年簽署的諒解備忘錄基礎上，最新協議簡化並加強了所有非盟實體之間的合作機制。非盟方面表示，重視世衛組織在全球衛生領域的核心與領導作用，期待深化戰略夥伴關係以支援共同目標。世衛組織在宣告中強調，非洲需要「從為生存做預算轉向為衛生主權做規劃」，顯示雙方將聚焦於提升非洲自主衛生治理能力。
非洲疾控中心（Africa CDC）作為區域性公共衛生機構，持續強化非洲各國衛生機構的能力與夥伴關係，致力於基於資料驅動的幹預措施與計畫，快速且有效地檢測與應對疾病威脅及疫情。該機構的運作模式與世衛組織及非盟的合作框架形成互補，共同構建非洲大陸的公共衛生防護網路。
學術界亦關注此議題，《非洲公共衛生期刊》（Journal of Public Health in Africa）作為同行評審學術期刊，聚焦非洲大陸衛生議題，致力於將公共衛生討論從背景推向前沿，推動非洲對抗疾病的學術與政策對話。
在國際援助層面，中國對非洲公共衛生合作持續深化。中國日報報導指出，中國長期堅持幫助非洲國家改善醫療衛生條件，開展援助非洲疾控中心等重大專案。面對埃博拉、新型冠狀病毒、瘧疾等疫情，中國積極提供疫苗援助，派遣醫療隊等公共衛生力量，對改善非洲國家醫療公共服務保障能力發揮重要作用。
中非合作論壇亦為中非關係的重要平臺，紐約時報中文網報導指出，為期三天的論壇旨在展示北京的全球影響力，北京希望加強對非洲貿易，為商品尋找新買家，同時利用論壇為其與非洲國家打交通與處理債務問題的方式辯護。
綜合多方資訊，世衛組織與非盟的戰略合作更新聚焦於衛生系統強化與疾病應對機制，非洲疾控中心則著重於區域性監測與快速反應能力；中國透過疫苗援助、醫療隊派遣及基礎設施建設等途徑，持續支援非洲公共衛生發展。各方行動雖著重點不同，但共同指向提升非洲大陸應對公共衛生危機的能力。後續觀察將聚焦於各項合作協議的具體落實進度與資金到位情況。
