  # Australia Promotes Global Education Policy to Strengthen International Perspective Cultivation in Primary and Secondary Schools
  
  > The Australian Department of Education released a global education framework emphasizing teachers' professional growth and students' cross-cultural exposure, while also focusing on Asia literacy and the development of education services for overseas students.
  
  In 2008, the Australian Department of Education released *Global Perspectives: A framework for global education in Australian schools*, explicitly stating that schools with effective global education should provide teachers with curriculum design and professional growth opportunities to enable them to explore and develop global dimensions within the curriculum. At the same time, schools should also provide students with opportunities to engage with other cultures or countries to foster their international perspective. This framework has become an essential policy foundation for Australia's promotion of global education, continuing to influence subsequent educational practices and assessment standards.
  
  According to policy analysis literature indexed in the ERICDATA Higher Education Knowledge Base, the development of Australia's international education policy also focuses on two major aspects: Asia literacy and education services for overseas students. Related research indicates that Australia's strategic planning in the field of international education not only focuses on academic exchange, but also incorporates regional Asia literacy into education policy considerations to address increasingly close economic and cultural connections in the Asia-Pacific region. This policy direction reflects the Australian government's emphasis on regional educational cooperation and strengthens national international competitiveness through the export of education services.
  
  Taiwan's Ministry of Education has also continued to monitor international education trends, releasing the *White Paper on International Education for Primary and Secondary Schools: Cultivating 21st Century Global Talents* in 2011 to respond to the wave of global education development. Additionally, the Department of Statistics of Taiwan's Ministry of Education regularly publishes *International Comparison of Education Statistical Indicators*, providing historical data in formats such as PDF, XLS, and ODS for public reference. In the event of revisions to the statistical data, the XLS and ODS files from the latest edition prevail to ensure data consistency and comparability.
  
  Synthesizing information from various sources, Australia's promotion of global education policy covers multiple dimensions, including curriculum design, teacher professional development, students' cross-cultural experiences, and regional literacy, forming a systematic educational framework. This policy direction not only influences domestic primary and secondary educational practices in Australia, but also serves as an important reference for regional educational cooperation and the development of international education services. Moving forward, the concrete implementation outcomes and subsequent adjustments of Australia's global education policy remain worthy of continued observation.

# 澳洲推動全球教育政策 強化中小學國際視野培育

> 澳洲教育部發布全球教育架構，強調教師專業成長與學生跨文化接觸，同時關注亞洲識讀能力與海外學生教育服務發展。

澳洲教育部於 2008 年公佈《全球觀點：澳洲中小學全球教育架構》，明確指出具備良好全球教育的學校，應提供教師課程設計與專業成長機會，使其能嘗試發展課程的全球面向。同時，學校也應提供學生接觸其他文化或國家的機會，以培養其國際視野。此架構成為澳洲推動全球教育的重要政策基礎，並持續影響後續教育實踐與評估標準。
根據ERICDATA高等教育知識庫所收錄之政策分析文獻，澳洲國際教育政策發展亦著重於亞洲識讀能力與海外學生教育服務兩大面向。相關研究指出，澳洲在國際教育領域的佈局，不僅著眼於學術交流，更將亞洲區域的識讀能力納入教育政策考量，以因應亞太地區日益緊密的經濟與文化連結。此政策方向反映澳洲政府對區域教育合作的重視，並透過教育服務出口強化國家國際競爭力。
臺灣教育部亦持續關注國際教育趨勢，並於 2011 年發布《中小學國際教育白皮書：紮根培育 21 世紀全球人才》，呼應全球教育發展浪潮。此外，臺灣教育部統計處定期出版《教育統計指標之國際比較》出版品，提供 PDF、XLS 及 ODS 等格式之歷年資料，供各界參考。該統計資料若經修正，以最後一期之 XLS 及 ODS 檔為準，確保資料之一致性與可比性。
綜合各來源資訊，澳洲在全球教育政策上的推動，涵蓋課程設計、教師專業發展、學生跨文化體驗及區域識讀能力等多個層面，形成系統性之教育架構。此政策方向不僅影響澳洲國內中小學教育實踐，亦成為區域教育合作與國際教育服務發展的重要參考。未來，澳洲全球教育政策之具體實施成效與後續調整，仍值得持續觀察。
