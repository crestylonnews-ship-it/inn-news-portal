  > Middle Eastern nations are accelerating energy transition and digital infrastructure development while Israel's tech industry continues to expand; Australia is deepening defense and economic cooperation with Pacific island nations, showcasing divergent strategic pathways for regional development.
  
  According to a Hong Kong Trade Development Council (HKTDC) research report, Israel has become one of the world's fastest-growing innovation hubs for high-tech industries, powered by its vibrant tech ecosystem. In an exclusive interview, Amir Lati, Consul General of Israel in Hong Kong, noted that the post-pandemic economic outlook remains robust, with broad prospects for the local tech industry and increasingly frequent trade and investment activities between Israel and Hong Kong, presenting significant business opportunities. This underscores how some Middle Eastern countries are reducing dependence on traditional energy through tech industry diversification and actively expanding ties with Asian economies.
  
  Research Nester's analysis further indicates that the Middle East and Africa regions continue to emerge as dynamic centers of global economic growth, leveraging their strategic geographic locations, abundant natural resources, and rapidly developing infrastructure. Gulf nations such as Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates (UAE) are driving non-oil economic expansion through investments in renewable energy, digital infrastructure, and technology.
  
  Vanzbon's Global Trends Radar report adds that the South Asia-Middle East corridor zone exhibits a "triple resonance" phenomenon: at the energy end, risk premium structures are being reshaped amid high oil price consolidation and OPEC+ compliance discipline; at the institutional and clearing end, the UAE's CBDC/mBridge and VA input allocation (SRP) are driving low-friction coupling of capital flows, bill flows, and data flows; and at the capital and industry end, momentum is supported by the recovery of Indian foreign direct investment (FDI), the normalization of Saudi regional headquarters (RHQ), and investments such as Oman's Muscat Bay Free Zone (OPAZ).

# 中東經濟轉型與澳洲太平洋合作 區域發展雙軌並行

> 中東國家加速能源轉型與數位基礎建設，以色列創科產業持續擴張；澳洲深化與太平洋島國防務及經濟合作，區域發展呈現不同戰略路徑。

根據香港貿易發展局經貿研究報導，以色列憑藉充滿活力的科技生態系統，已成為全球發展最迅速的高科技產業創新樞紐之一。以色列駐香港總領事藍天銘（Amir Lati）在接受專訪時指出，疫後經濟展望穩健，當地創科產業前景廣闊，且以色列與香港之間的貿易投資活動日益頻繁，帶來顯著商機。此說法凸顯中東部分國家正透過科技產業多元化，降低對傳統能源的依賴，並積極拓展與亞洲經濟體的連結。
Research Nester 的分析則進一步指出，中東與非洲地區憑藉優越地理位置、豐富自然資源及快速發展的基礎設施，持續崛起為全球經濟成長的活力中心。沙烏地阿拉伯與阿拉伯聯合大公國（UAE）等海灣國家，正透過投資再生能源、數位基礎設施與技術，推動非石油領域的經濟擴張。
Vanzbon 的全球趨勢雷達報告補充，南亞與中東走廊區呈現「三端共振」現象：能源端在油價高位整理與OPEC+補償紀律下，風險溢價結構重塑；制度與清算端以UAE的CBDC/mBridge與VA進項分攤（SRP）為代表，推動資金流、票據流與資料流的低摩擦耦合；資本與產業端則由印度外商直接投資（FDI）回升、沙特區域總部（RHQ）常態化，以及阿曼奧曼灣自由區（OPAZ）投資等動能支撐。
