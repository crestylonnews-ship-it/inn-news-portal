  # East Asia Leads Digital Transformation in Education, International Symposium Focuses on Reform Outlook
  
  > A UNESCO report indicates that China, Japan, and South Korea lead the world in applying advanced technology in classrooms; Taiwan's National Academy for Educational Research hosted an international symposium exploring reflections and prospects for Asian education reform.
  
  The regional edition of UNESCO's "2025 Global Education Monitoring Report" shows that East Asian countries hold a leading position in the digital transformation processes of global economic, social, and education systems. The report explicitly states that China, Japan, and South Korea are among the first nations to apply advanced technology in classrooms, setting benchmarks for regional education development.
  
  According to the report, China released its "Education Informatization 2.0 Action Plan" in 2018, proposing a vision for smart education and emphasizing the importance of personalized learning. Japan launched its "GIGA School Program" in 2019, and its implementation was significantly accelerated by the COVID-19 pandemic, demonstrating that the pandemic served as a key catalyst for educational digitalization. UNESCO also provides comparable qualitative data on national-level education policies and regulations through its Policy Evaluation for Education Reform (PEER) mechanism, covering areas such as inclusivity, equitable education financing, climate change education, regulation of non-state education providers, comprehensive sexuality education, and technology and leadership, offering reference points for countries in formulating education policies.
  
  In terms of academic exchange, Taiwan's National Academy for Educational Research (NAER) held an international academic symposium titled "Reflections and Prospects of Asian Education Reform" in 2019 to explore trends and challenges in Asian education development. The symposium introduction noted that Taiwan, situated at the intersection of ocean and continent, and of Southeast Asia and East Asia, serves as one of Asia's transportation hubs and inherently contains rich multicultural elements. This geographical and cultural advantage provides unique conditions for regional educational exchange. NAER's Taiwan Education Research Information Network also published related materials under the title "Challenges and Prospects of Asian Education," continuing to focus on regional education issues.
  
  Furthermore, the academic journal "East Asian Educational Research" (ISSN 3079-8876) focuses on educational theory, policy, and practice in the East Asian region, aiming to promote interdisciplinary research and international academic exchange. It explores the transformation, development, and global impact of East Asian education systems, providing high-quality academic outcomes for education researchers, policymakers, and practitioners. The Asia-Pacific Association for Higher Education (APAIE) is also committed to connecting higher education institutions across the Asia-Pacific region with the rest of the world, fostering regional educational cooperation.
  
  Synthesizing information from various sources, digital transformation in East Asian education has become a significant regional development issue. Policy initiatives by China and Japan carry indicative significance, while Taiwan's academic community actively participates in dialogue and research on Asian education reform. How future education policies can balance digitalization with educational equity, as well as integrate emerging issues such as climate change education, will be challenges jointly faced by countries in the region.

# 東亞教育數位轉型領先 國際研討會聚焦改革展望

> 聯合國教科文組織報告指出，中國、日本、韓國在課堂應用先進技術方面居全球領先地位；臺灣國家教育研究院舉辦國際研討會，探討亞洲教育改革的反思與展望。

聯合國教科文組織（UNESCO）發布的《2025年全球教育監測報告》區域版顯示，東亞國家在全球經濟、社會及教育體系的數位轉型程式中居於領先地位。報告明確指出，中國、日本與韓國是率先在課堂中應用先進技術的國家，為區域教育發展樹立標竿。
根據該報告內容，中國於2018年發布《教育資訊化2.0行動計劃》，提出智慧教育願景並強調個性化學習的重要性。日本則於2019年啟動「GIGA學校計劃」，其實施程式因新型冠狀病毒肺炎疫情而顯著加速，顯示疫情成為推動教育數位化的關鍵催化劑。UNESCO同時透過教育政策提升評估（PEER）機制，提供國家層級的教育政策與法規可比性定性資料，涵蓋包容性、教育公平融資、氣候變化教育、非國家教育機構的監管、全面性教育、技術與領導力等面向，為各國教育政策制定提供參考依據。
在學術交流方面，臺灣國家教育研究院曾於2019年舉辦「亞洲教育改革的反思與展望」國際學術研討會，探討亞洲教育發展的趨勢與挑戰。研討會簡介指出，臺灣位處海洋與大陸、東南亞與東亞交會，為亞洲交通樞紐之一，本身即蘊含豐富的多種文化因子，此地理與文化優勢為區域教育交流提供獨特條件。國家教育研究院旗下臺灣教育研究資訊網亦推出《亞洲教育的挑戰與展望》相關出版品，持續關注區域教育議題。
此外，學術期刊《東亞教育研究》（ISSN 3079-8876）聚焦東亞地區教育理論、政策與實踐，旨在推動跨學科研究與國際學術交流，探討東亞教育體系的變革、發展及其全球影響，面向教育研究者、政策制定者與實踐者提供高品質學術成果。亞太國際教育協會（APAIE）亦致力於連結亞太地區高等教育機構與世界其他地區，促進區域教育合作。
綜合各來源資訊，東亞教育數位轉型已成為區域發展的重要議題，中國與日本的政策推動具指標意義，而臺灣學術界亦積極參與亞洲教育改革的對話與研究。未來教育政策如何兼顧數位化與教育公平，以及氣候變化教育等新興議題的整合，將是區域各國共同面對的挑戰。
