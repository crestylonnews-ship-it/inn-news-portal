  # Multiple Reports Reveal Africa's Rising Status in Global Landscape and Development Challenges
  
  > Multiple reports indicate that Africa as a whole maintains peace and stability, but regional risks are rising and economic recovery faces pressure; major powers compete for influence, and how Africa responds to global governance and AI innovation has become a focal point.
  
  The *Africa Blue Book: Development Report on Africa (2023–2024)* was released on August 27 in Beijing, jointly published by the Institute of West Asian and African Studies of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences, the China Africa Research Institute, and Social Sciences Academic Press. The report points out that Africa generally maintained a peaceful and stable development situation in 2023, but political and security risks in certain regional areas have surfaced, and economic recovery continues to face pressure. Meanwhile, various global actors are increasing their strategic emphasis on and investment in Africa, indicating that Africa's geopolitical and economic value is increasingly attracting international attention.
  
  At the same time, the New China News Service National High-Level Think Tank released a think tank report titled *Building a "New Type of Global Leadership" — Toward a More Just and Reasonable...* at the China-Africa Partners Conference during the "Global South" Media and Think Tank High-Level Forum held on November 13 in Johannesburg, South Africa, resonating with people across Africa. The report focuses on the needs and calls of the times for global governance, exploring how to build a more inclusive international order, reflecting Africa's gradually rising role in reshaping the global governance architecture.
  
  BBC Chinese reported that various major powers in the world are currently competing for political and economic influence in Africa. The reasons why the African continent is once again attracting attention, as well as the strategies African countries are adopting to address new developments, have become issues of concern to the international community. The report analyzes how Africa can seize opportunities amid competition among various countries, highlighting the growing awareness of Africa's independent development.
  
  In addition, research from Fudan University focusing on artificial intelligence and new industrial policy transformation points out that AI is reshaping global industrial division of labor and governance patterns as a foundational force. As a key battleground for AI development in the "Global South," Africa's formulation and implementation of industrial policies not only concern the momentum of local economic and social transformation but also profoundly affect the fairness and inclusiveness of global AI governance. The research emphasizes that against the backdrop of structural differentiation in the "global AI divide," how Africa can break through institutional and economic constraints and explore development paths suited to its own conditions is an important current issue.
  
  A synthesis of multiple reports and news coverage shows that Africa is facing a situation where peace and stability coexist with development challenges, while playing an increasingly critical role in global governance, great power competition, and technological transformation. Its response strategies and development trajectory will continue to influence the evolution of the international landscape.

# 多份報告揭示非洲在全球格局中地位提升與發展挑戰

> 多份報告指出非洲整體保持和平穩定，但區域性風險上升、經濟復甦承壓；各大國爭奪影響力，非洲如何應對全球治理與AI創新成為關注焦點。

《非洲黃皮書：非洲發展報告（2023~2024）》於8月27日在北京發布，由中國社會科學院西亞非洲研究所、中國非洲研究院與社會科學文獻出版社聯合推出。報告指出，2023年非洲總體保持和平穩定發展局面，但區域性地區政治安全風險抬頭，經濟復甦持續承壓；同時，全球各方正加大對非戰略重視與投入，顯示非洲地緣政治與經濟價值日益受到國際關注。
與此同時，新華社國家高階智庫於11月13日在南非約翰內斯堡舉行的「全球南方」媒體智庫高階論壇中非夥伴大會上，發布《共建「新型全球領導力」——邁向更加公正合理……》智庫報告，引發非洲各界人士共鳴。該報告聚焦全球治理需求與時代呼聲，探討如何構建更具包容性的國際秩序，反映非洲在重塑全球治理架構中的角色正逐步提升。
BBC中文報導指出，當前世界各個主要大國都在爭奪在非洲的政治經濟影響力，非洲大地重新引起關注的原因與非洲國家應對新局面的策略，成為國際社會關注的議題。報告分析非洲如何在各國競爭中抓住機遇，呈現非洲自主發展意識的增強。
此外，復旦大學相關研究聚焦人工智慧與新產業政策轉型，指出AI正以基礎性力量重塑全球產業分工與治理格局。非洲作為「全球南方」AI發展的關鍵陣地，其產業政策的制定與落地，不僅關乎本土經濟社會轉型動能，更深刻影響全球AI治理的公平性與包容性。研究強調，在「全球AI鴻溝」呈現結構性分化的背景下，非洲如何突破制度與經濟約束，探索適合自身的發展路徑，是當前重要課題。
綜合多份報告與報導顯示，非洲正面臨和平穩定與發展挑戰並存的局面，同時在全球治理、大國競爭與科技變革中扮演日益關鍵的角色，其應對策略與發展走向將持續影響國際格局演變。
