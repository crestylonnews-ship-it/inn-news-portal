  # Asia-Pacific Climate Report Released; Green Transition and Energy Security Take Center Stage
  
  > The Asian Development Bank released its Asia-Pacific Climate Report, while the International Energy Agency launched its State of Energy Policy report, highlighting that green transition and energy security have become key policy priorities across the region.
  
  The Asian Development Bank (ADB) recently released its inaugural *Asia-Pacific Climate Report 2024*, providing a comprehensive overview of the current state of climate change in the Asia-Pacific region. The report incorporates the latest findings from public perception surveys and identifies key areas for policy response. It aims to assist governments and relevant institutions in understanding regional climate trends, serving as an important reference for developing response strategies.
  
  Meanwhile, the International Energy Agency (IEA) released its *State of Energy Policy 2026* report, reviewing global energy policy changes in 2025. The report covers 84 countries and over 6,500 policy measures, including government energy expenditures, energy efficiency and fuel-switching regulations, climate commitments, traditional and emerging energy security, energy accessibility, and policy analysis across the buildings, industry, transport, fuel, and power sectors. Taiwan's Ministry of Environment Energy Information Platform also shared the report for reference by relevant domestic agencies.
  
  In terms of regional cooperation, the Center for Asia-Pacific Resilience and Innovation (CAPRI) Foundation is advancing the "Navigating the Green Transition: Asia-Pacific Economic Development and Energy Security" initiative, focusing on green growth, regional economic partnerships, and cross-sector collaboration amid geopolitical uncertainty. The initiative will release thematic reports on green supply chains, climate finance, workforce transition, the rise of artificial intelligence (AI), and local government energy governance, aiming to promote sustainable development in the Asia-Pacific region through academic research and policy advocacy.
  
  A synthesis of the three reports reveals that the Asia-Pacific region is at a critical juncture in climate policy and energy transition. The ADB report emphasizes the link between public perception and policy response, the IEA report provides quantitative analysis of global energy policies, and the CAPRI Foundation's initiative focuses on cross-disciplinary collaboration and practical issues. The three complement each other, reflecting the region's multifaceted concerns regarding climate change and energy security.
  
  Experts note that as countries accelerate their net-zero emission targets, green supply chains and climate finance will become key areas of competition. Meanwhile, the application of AI technology may also transform energy governance models, warranting continued attention from policymakers. Future developments will depend on the implementation of specific national policies and progress in international cooperation.

# 亞太氣候報告出爐 綠色轉型與能源安全成焦點

> 亞洲開發銀行發布亞太氣候報告，國際能源總署推出能源政策現況報告，顯示區域綠色轉型與能源安全成各國政策重點。

亞洲開發銀行（ADB）近日發布首份《亞太氣候報告 2024》（Asia-Pacific Climate Report 2024），提供亞太地區氣候變遷現況的整體概覽，並納入最新公眾認知調查結果，同時提出政策回應的關鍵領域。該報告旨在協助各國政府與相關機構掌握區域氣候趨勢，作為制定應對策略的重要參考依據。
與此同時，國際能源總署（IEA）發布《2026年能源政策現況》（State of Energy Policy 2026）報告，回顧2025年全球能源政策的變化。報告涵蓋84個國家、超過6,500項政策措施，內容包括政府能源支出、能源效率與燃料轉換規範、氣候承諾、傳統與新興能源安全、能源可近性，以及建築、工業、運輸、燃料與電力部門的政策分析。臺灣環境部能源資訊平臺亦轉發此報告，供國內相關單位參考。
在區域合作方面，亞太堅韌研究基金會（CAPRI Foundation）推動「迎向綠色轉型：亞太經濟發展與能源安全」計畫，聚焦地緣政治不確定性下的綠色成長區域經濟夥伴關係與跨部門合作。該計畫將針對綠色供應鏈、氣候金融、勞動力轉型、人工智慧（AI）的興起，以及地方政府能源治理等主題發表專題報告，期望透過學術研究與政策倡議，促進亞太地區永續發展。
綜合三份報告內容可見，亞太地區正處於氣候政策與能源轉型的重要階段。亞洲開發銀行的報告強調公眾認知與政策回應的連結，IEA的報告則提供全球能源政策的量化分析，而亞太堅韌研究基金會的計畫則著重於跨領域合作與實務議題。三者互補，反映區域對氣候變遷與能源安全的多元關注。
專家指出，隨著各國加速推動淨零排放目標，綠色供應鏈與氣候金融將成為關鍵競爭領域。同時，人工智慧技術的應用亦可能改變能源治理模式，值得政策制定者持續關注。後續發展有待各國具體政策落實與國際合作進展。
