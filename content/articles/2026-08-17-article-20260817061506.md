  # New Landscape of AI Development in Asia: Patent Layout, Policy Frameworks, and Great Power Competition and Cooperation
  
  > Asia's AI landscape reaches a critical turning point as Taiwan's 12 institutions rank among the world's top 100 innovators, the World Economic Forum proposes a human-led AI framework, and China's large model rise reshapes regional competition.
  
  Asia's artificial intelligence industry is undergoing profound transformation. According to the World Economic Forum's (WEF) report *Asia's Human-Led AI Transformation: A Framework for Transition*, AI development in Asia faces the potential risk of deployment preceding design. The report proposes a "Human-Led AI Transformation Framework" and outlines three practical pathways, emphasizing that while advancing AI implementation, institutional design and technology governance must remain people-centered, avoiding blind pursuit of technological deployment that overlooks social impact and ethical norms.
  
  The report notes that AI development momentum in Asia is strong, but how to achieve a balance between innovation and governance remains a shared challenge for governments and industries across the region.
  
  In terms of patent layout, Taiwan has delivered impressive results. On the 10th, the Taiwan Intellectual Property Office (TIPO) released its 2025 list of the country's top 100 patent-holding enterprises. Among them, 12 enterprises and research institutions—including TSMC, AU Optronics, Foxconn, ITRI, Nanya Technology, Realtek, Delta Electronics, MediaTek, Wistron, ASUS, SMI, and Zhongqiang—were recognized as Taiwan's AI patent leaders. These institutions actively establish Taiwan as their first stop for patent layout before expanding into global markets, and they also appear on Clarivate's 2026 list of the world's top 100 innovative institutions, demonstrating that Taiwan's international competitiveness in core AI technologies continues to strengthen.
  
  Meanwhile, the broader Asian AI landscape is experiencing a critical turning point. According to Huxiu, 2025 is regarded as the defining year of a new era for Asian AI, with China emerging as a key regional driving force through the rise of its domestic large models. Countries are exploring pathways that combine hybrid applications with independent development, and the deep integration of AI with core operational processes will reshape economic growth models and industrial competitive dynamics. However, technological sovereignty and geopolitical factors are also bringing new challenges to Asia's AI development; as each country pursues technological self-reliance, they must navigate the delicate balance between supply chain restructuring and international cooperation.
  
  A comprehensive observation reveals that Asia's AI development exhibits multi-track characteristics: the World Economic Forum proposes a human-led development pathway from a governance framework perspective, Taiwan ranks among the global innovation forefront through its patent strength, and China has become a key regional force through breakthroughs in large model technology. Together, the three paint a diverse picture of Asia's AI ecosystem and highlight the complex development landscape shaped by the interplay of technological innovation, institutional design, and geopolitics. How Asian countries will seek collaboration amid competition in the future will be an important focus for observing the trajectory of regional AI development.

# 亞洲AI發展新局：專利佈局、政策框架與大國競合

> 亞洲人工智慧格局迎關鍵轉折，臺灣12家機構入榜全球百大創新，世界經濟論壇提出人類主導AI框架，中國大模型崛起重塑區域競爭版圖。

亞洲人工智慧產業正經歷深刻變革。根據世界經濟論壇（WEF）發布的報告《亞洲人類主導AI轉型：轉型的框架》，亞洲AI發展面臨部署先於設計的潛在風險，報告提出「人類主導AI轉型框架」（Human-led AI Transformation Framework），並規劃三條實踐路徑，強調在推動AI落地的同時，必須以人為核心進行制度設計與技術治理，避免盲目追求技術部署而忽視社會影響與倫理規範。
該報告指出，亞洲地區的AI發展動能強勁，但如何在創新與治理之間取得平衡，是各國政府與產業共同面臨的課題。
在專利佈局方面，臺灣表現亮眼。臺灣智慧財產局於10日公佈2025年我國專利百大企業名單，其中臺積電、友達、鴻海、工研院、南亞科、瑞昱、臺達電、聯發科、緯創、華碩、慧榮、中強等12家企業或研究機構，被認定為臺灣AI專利領先者。這些機構積極以臺灣作為專利佈局的首站，進而擴充套件至全球市場，同時入榜科睿唯安（Clarivate）發布的2026年全球百大創新機構名單，顯示臺灣在AI核心技術領域的國際競爭力持續提升。
與此同時，亞洲整體AI格局正經歷關鍵轉折。虎嗅網報導指出，2025年被視為亞洲AI新紀元的定義之年，中國憑藉國產大模型的崛起成為區域重要推動力量。各國正探索混合應用與自主發展並行的路徑，AI與核心運營環節的深度融合將重塑經濟增長模式與產業競爭格局。然而，技術主權與地緣政治因素也為亞洲AI發展帶來新的挑戰，各國在追求技術自主的同時，需面對供應鏈重組與國際合作之間的微妙平衡。
綜合觀察，亞洲AI發展呈現多軌並行的特徵：世界經濟論壇從治理框架角度提出人類主導的發展路徑，臺灣以專利實力躋身全球創新前列，中國則以大模型技術突破成為區域關鍵力量。三者共同勾勒出亞洲AI生態的多元面貌，也凸顯出技術創新、制度設計與地緣政治交織下的複雜發展圖景。未來亞洲各國如何在競爭中尋求協作，將是影響區域AI發展走向的重要觀察重點。
