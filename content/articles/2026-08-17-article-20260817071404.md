  # Africa Climate Report Reveals Extreme Weather Impacts, Renewable Energy Transition Emerges as Key Path
  
  > The World Meteorological Organization released its 2025 Africa Climate Report, highlighting increasingly severe extreme weather and climate change impacts from the Sahara to the Serengeti; reports from the International Energy Agency and Commercial Times indicate that Africa holds enormous potential for clean energy development, and transitioning to renewables not only benefits local development but also contributes to global net-zero emission goals.
  
  The World Meteorological Organization (WMO) recently released the "Africa Climate Report 2025," which documents in detail extreme weather events and climate change impacts across the African continent—from the Sahara Desert to the Serengeti grasslands, from coastal waters to mountain peaks. The report emphasizes that the African continent is facing increasingly frequent and intense climate-related disasters, including droughts, floods, and temperature anomalies, which are significantly affecting local ecosystems and human societies. As the UN specialized agency responsible for climate affairs, the WMO's report is regarded as an important reference for global climate conditions; this focused analysis on the African region underscores the area's particular vulnerability to climate change.
  
  In its special report "Africa Energy Outlook 2022," the International Energy Agency (IEA) explores pathways for Africa's energy systems to achieve various development goals, including universal access to modern and affordable energy services by 2030 and the implementation of countries' nationally determined contributions. The report notes that Africa's energy transition is not only crucial for local economic and livelihood development but also holds key significance for global decarbonization efforts. Commercial Times further cited the latest climate report released by the United Nations in April this year, emphasizing that from wind farms along Africa's coastlines to geothermal projects in the East African Rift Valley, the continent holds enormous potential for clean energy development.
  
  The report notes that if Africa fully realizes its renewable energy plans, it could not only mitigate the severe effects of global warming but also drive local sustainable development.
  
  Synthesizing information from all sources, the WMO report focuses on the scientific documentation and risk assessment of climate impacts, the IEA outlook concentrates on policy and technical pathways for energy system transformation, and Commercial Times integrates UN data to highlight practical application cases and dual benefits of Africa's renewable energy. Differences in emphasis include: the WMO report does not directly discuss energy solutions, the IEA outlook mentions fewer specific extreme weather event details, and Commercial Times does not deeply analyze the scientific data on climate impacts.

Key areas for future observation include the strength of national policy implementation, progress in international cooperation, and the actual effectiveness of renewable energy infrastructure development—factors that will collectively determine whether Africa can achieve sustainable economic and social development goals while mitigating climate change.

# 非洲氣候報告揭示極端天氣衝擊 再生能源轉型成關鍵路徑

> 世界氣象組織發布2025年非洲氣候報告，指出從撒哈拉到塞倫蓋蒂的極端天氣與氣候變遷影響日益嚴峻；國際能源署與工商時報報導顯示，非洲擁有巨大乾淨能源發展潛能，轉型再生能源不僅利於本地發展，亦有助全球淨零排放目標實現。

世界氣象組織（WMO）於近日發布《非洲氣候報告2025》，詳細記錄非洲全境從撒哈拉沙漠到塞倫蓋蒂草原、從沿海水域到山巔峰嶺的極端天氣事件與氣候變遷衝擊。該報告強調，非洲大陸正面臨日益頻繁且強烈的氣候相關災害，包括乾旱、洪水與溫度異常等現象，對當地生態系統與人類社會造成顯著影響。WMO作為聯合國負責氣候事務的專門機構，其報告被視為全球氣候狀況的重要參考依據，此次針對非洲區域的專項分析，凸顯該地區在氣候脆弱性上的特殊地位。
國際能源署（IEA）在《非洲能源展望2022》特別報告中，探討非洲能源系統朝向實現各項發展目標的路徑，其中包括到2030年普及現代且負擔得起的能源服務，以及落實各國自主貢獻承諾。該報告指出，非洲能源轉型不僅關係當地經濟與民生發展，亦對全球減碳程式具有關鍵意義。工商時報報導進一步援引聯合國今年4月發表的最新氣候報告內容，強調從非洲海岸線的風力發電場到東非大裂谷的地熱計畫，均顯示非洲大陸蘊藏龐大的乾淨能源發展潛力。
報導指出，若非洲能充分實現其各項再生能源計畫，不僅可減緩嚴峻的全球暖化效應，亦能推動本地可持續發展。
綜合各來源資訊，WMO報告側重氣候影響的科學記錄與風險評估，IEA展望則聚焦能源系統轉型的政策與技術路徑，而工商時報報導整合聯合國資料，突出非洲再生能源的實際應用案例與雙重效益。三家媒體共同確認的事實包括：非洲正面臨嚴峻氣候挑戰、該地區擁有豐富再生能源資源、能源轉型兼具本地與全球效益。不同說法或側重之處在於，WMO報告未直接論述能源解決方案，IEA展望較少提及具體極端天氣事件細節，而工商時報報導則未深入分析氣候影響的科學資料。
尚待確認之處包括：非洲各國再生能源計畫的具體進展時程、國際資金與技術支援的落實情況，以及氣候報告與能源展望之間的因果關聯性是否已獲充分驗證。
非洲氣候與能源議題的交叉發展，反映該地區在面對全球暖化威脅時，正尋求將挑戰轉化為轉型契機。未來觀察重點將包括各國政策執行力度、國際合作進展，以及再生能源基礎設施建設的實際成效，這些因素將共同影響非洲能否在減緩氣候變遷的同時，實現可持續的經濟與社會發展目標。
