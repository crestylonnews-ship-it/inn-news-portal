  # Africa's Economy Grows Against the Trend, Foreign Investment Inflows Hit Record High
  
  > The United Nations Conference on Trade and Development (UNCTAD) reported that amid a global decline in foreign direct investment, the Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa (COMESA) saw foreign investment inflows surge to a record $65 billion, demonstrating the region's economic resilience.
  
  A report released by the United Nations Conference on Trade and Development (UNCTAD) in December 2025 noted that, against a challenging global backdrop of an overall 11% decline in foreign direct investment (FDI), the Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa (COMESA) bucked the trend and set a new record of $65 billion in foreign investment inflows, marking a historic high. The data shows that despite rising global economic uncertainty, certain regions of Africa remain competitive in attracting international capital. RFI reported that UNCTAD analysis attributes the surge in foreign investment to African economies' continued improvement of their business environments in recent years.
  
  The International Monetary Fund (IMF) also expressed a positive outlook on Africa's economic prospects in its latest report released recently. The IMF pointed out that African countries have proactively advanced economic institutional reforms, focusing on promoting industrial upgrading and regional integration, while systematically cultivating endogenous growth momentum, resulting in robust economic growth and strong resilience. In 2025, Africa accounted for the most concentrated region among high-growth economies worldwide, with economic data from multiple countries indicating that its development momentum is gradually accumulating. The Chinese Ministry of Commerce's African Union website, citing IMF analysis, emphasized that African countries have continued to strive for economic stability despite dual pressures from changes in the global trade order and reduced aid from major development partners.
  
  The African Development Bank (AfDB) offered a more cautious perspective in its "2025 African Economic Outlook" report released in 2025. The report noted that major economies underwent significant shifts in trade policy in April 2025, profoundly impacting the global trade order; meanwhile, several key development partners have announced substantial cuts to development assistance for low-income African countries due to shifts in their domestic policy priorities. The AfDB warned that these moves would undoubtedly pose challenges to low-income African countries that heavily rely on international development assistance, potentially affecting fiscal stability and infrastructure progress in some nations.
  
  On balance, Africa's economy currently presents a complex situation characterized by increasing foreign investment inflows alongside decreasing official development assistance, requiring countries within the region to balance market-oriented approaches with policy adjustments.
  
  From a historical perspective, Baidu Baike records that Africa's economic development can generally be divided into four key stages: the slave trade period (1441–1880), the colonial period (1880–1950), the early independence period (1950–1980), and the structural adjustment and exploration period (1980 to present). Each stage profoundly shaped the economic structure and development path of the African continent. Current efforts by African countries to improve their business environments and upgrade industries can be seen as significant progress since entering the structural adjustment period, but how to address the long-term impacts of global trade policy changes and reduced foreign aid remains a key challenge for countries across the region.

# 非洲經濟逆勢成長 外資流入創歷史新高

> 聯合國貿易發展會議指出，在全球外商直接投資下滑背景下，東非與南部非洲共同市場外資流入逆勢創650億美元新高，展現區域經濟韌性。

聯合國貿易和發展會議（UNCTAD）於2025年12月發布報告指出，在全球外商直接投資（FDI）整體下滑11%的嚴峻大環境下，東非與南部非洲共同市場（COMESA）卻逆勢創下650億美元的外資流入紀錄，重新整理歷史新高。該資料顯示，儘管全球經濟不確定性持續攀升，非洲部分割槽域仍具備吸引國際資本的競爭力。RFI報導引述UNCTAD分析認為，非洲各經濟體近年來持續改善營商環境，是促成外資湧入的關鍵因素。
國際貨幣基金組織（IMF）近日發布的最新報告亦對非洲經濟前景持正面看法。IMF指出，非洲各國主動推進經濟體制改革，著重促進產業迭代升級與區域一體化程式，並系統性培育內生增長動能，使整體經濟呈現穩健增長態勢，展現強勁韌性。2025年非洲經濟在全球高增長經濟體中佔據最集中區域，多國經濟資料顯示其發展動能正逐步累積。中國商務部非洲聯盟網站轉引IMF分析時強調，非洲各國在面對全球貿易秩序變動與主要發展夥伴削減援助的雙重壓力下，仍努力維持經濟穩定前行。
非洲開發銀行（AfDB）於2025年發布的《2025年非洲經濟展望》報告則提出較謹慎的觀察視角。報告指出，2025年4月主要經濟體的貿易政策出現重大轉變，對全球貿易秩序產生深遠影響；同時，多個主要發展夥伴因國內政策重點轉移，已宣佈大幅削減對非洲的低收入國家發展援助。AfDB警告，此舉無疑將對嚴重依賴國際發展援助的非洲低收入國家構成挑戰，可能影響部分國家的財政穩定與基礎建設進度。
綜合來看，非洲經濟目前呈現「外資流入增加」與「官方發展援助減少」並存的複雜局面，區域內各國需平衡市場導向與政策調整。
從歷史脈絡觀察，百度百科記載非洲經濟發展大致可分為四個關鍵階段：奴隸貿易時期（1441年至1880年）、殖民時期（1880年至1950年）、獨立初期（1950年至1980年）以及結構調整與探索期（1980年至今）。每個階段皆深刻塑造了非洲大陸的經濟結構與發展路徑。當前非洲各國在營商環境改善與產業升級方面的努力，可視為進入結構調整期後的重要進展，但如何應對全球貿易政策變動與外援縮減的長期影響，仍是區域各國面臨的關鍵課題。
