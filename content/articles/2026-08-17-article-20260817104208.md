  # EU Reaffirms Support for WHO Universal Health Coverage Through Global Health Strategy
  
  > The EU Council approved conclusions on a global health strategy, affirming physical and mental well-being as a human right; simultaneously, it launched the fifth phase of the partnership with WHO on universal health coverage, strengthening multilateral health cooperation.
  
  The Council of the European Union recently formally approved conclusions on the EU Global Health Strategy, recognizing physical and mental well-being as a fundamental human right and noting that health is a prerequisite for sustainable development. The Council welcomed the strategic communication proposed by the European Commission, further emphasizing that the EU and its member states must play a leading role in ensuring that global health issues remain at the forefront of the international agenda. The report指出 that global health requires effective multilateralism and inclusive multi-stakeholder partnerships to address increasingly complex cross-border health challenges.
  
  Meanwhile, the World Health Organization (WHO) and the EU reaffirmed their commitment to cooperation in achieving universal health coverage and formally launched the fifth phase (2025–2028) of the Universal Health Coverage Partnership. This partnership is regarded as WHO's largest platform for strengthening health systems and advancing primary health care. The launch ceremony, attended by EU Commissioner for International Partnerships Jozef Síkela and WHO Director-General Dr. Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus, demonstrated the EU's continued key role in multilateral health governance.
  
  WHO also released its latest situation report on International Health Regulations (IHR) emergencies of 2024, covering all rated emergency and ongoing events from January to December of that year. The report noted that some events have triggered the UN Inter-Agency Standing Committee's Humanitarian Whole-System Response and require direct WHO response. Analysis indicates that through strategic approval and deepened partnerships, the EU is gradually strengthening its leadership position in global health governance while responding to the urgent need for multilateral cooperation to address cross-border health threats.

# 歐盟透過全球衛生戰略 重申支援世衛組織全民健康覆蓋

> 歐盟理事會批准全球衛生戰略結論，確認身心健康為人權；同時與世衛組織啟動全民健康覆蓋夥伴關係第五階段行動計畫，強化多邊衛生合作。

歐盟理事會近日正式批准關於歐盟全球衛生戰略的結論，承認身心健康是一項基本人權，並指出健康是永續發展的前提條件。理事會歡迎歐盟委員會提出的戰略通報，進一步強調歐盟及其成員國必須發揮主導作用，確保全球健康議題持續位居國際議程的首要位置。報告指出，全球衛生需要有效的多邊主義與包容性的多利益相關方夥伴關係，方能應對日益複雜的跨境健康挑戰。
與此同時，世界衛生組織（WHO）與歐盟重申在實現全民健康覆蓋方面的合作承諾，並正式啟動全民健康覆蓋夥伴關係第五階段（2025年至2028年）行動計畫。該夥伴關係被視為世衛組織加強衛生系統與推進初級衛生保健的最大平臺。歐盟國際夥伴關係委員約瑟夫·西凱拉（Jozef Síkela）與世衛組織總幹事譚德塞博士共同參與啟動儀式，顯示歐盟在多邊衛生治理中持續扮演關鍵角色。
世衛組織亦發布2024年度國際關注突發公共衛生事件最新情況報告，涵蓋當年1月至12月期間所有被定級的緊急與長期突發事件。報告提及，部分事件已啟動聯合國機構間常設委員會人道主義全系統擴大方案，並需要世衛組織直接應對。分析指出，歐盟透過戰略批准與夥伴關係深化，正逐步強化其在全球衛生治理中的領導地位，同時呼應多邊合作以應對跨境健康威脅的迫切需求。
