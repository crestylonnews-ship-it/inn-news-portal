  # Shield of the Americas Initiative Advances; UN Calls for Strengthened Humanitarian Aid
  
  > In March 2026, the United States announced the "Shield of the Americas" transnational security cooperation initiative to coordinate efforts among American nations against transnational criminal organizations; the United Nations simultaneously called on the international community to strengthen humanitarian aid and refugee protection support for the Americas.
  
  U.S. President Donald Trump officially announced the launch of the "Shield of the Americas" transnational security cooperation initiative at the "Shield of the Americas Summit" held in Doral, Florida on March 7, 2026. The initiative focuses on coordinating efforts among countries in the Americas to combat transnational criminal organizations, particularly drug trafficking cartels and related smuggling networks. The participation of the U.S. State Department in summit-related affairs indicates that this security cooperation mechanism has entered a substantive implementation phase. The launch of this initiative reflects the U.S. government's view that regional security and the fight against transnational crime are key priorities in its Americas policy.
  
  The United Nations has continued to monitor humanitarian aid needs in the Americas region. According to an annual report issued by the UN, despite facing challenges such as insufficient funding, staff shortages, and attacks on aid workers, the UN and its humanitarian partners have made persistent efforts to provide survival assistance and protection. The report emphasizes that humanitarian aid requires $50 billion in funding, but actual fundraising remains inadequate. The UN has called on governments and international organizations to increase resource commitments to ensure that people affected by crises can obtain basic survival needs and security guarantees.
  
  A factsheet on the Americas released by the UN Refugee Agency (UNHCR) in May 2025 indicated that Latin America and the Caribbean continue to offer meaningful opportunities to advance refugee protection and solutions. However, the factsheet also stressed that to translate this potential into lasting impact, the international community must provide stronger support and sustained investment to ensure the rights, safety, and stability of refugees, forcibly displaced persons, and stateless people. The contents of the factsheet highlight that the Americas region still faces structural challenges in humanitarian protection, requiring long-term and systematic international intervention.

This data reflects the complexity of the security situation in the Americas, with different countries facing varying types and degrees of threats.

# 美洲之盾倡議推展 聯合國籲加強人道援助

> 美國2026年3月宣佈「美洲之盾」跨國安全合作倡議，協調美洲國家打擊跨國犯罪組織；聯合國同時呼籲國際社會加強對美洲人道援助與難民保護的支援。

美國總統唐納·川普於2026年3月7日在佛羅裡達州多勒爾舉行的「美洲之盾峰會」上，正式宣佈發起「美洲之盾」（Shield of the Americas）跨國安全合作倡議。該倡議重點在於協調美洲各國打擊跨國犯罪組織，特別是販毒集團及相關走私網路。美國國務院參與峰會相關事務，顯示此項安全合作機制已進入實質推動階段。此倡議的推出，反映美國政府將區域安全與打擊跨國犯罪視為美洲政策的重要優先專案。
聯合國方面則持續關注美洲地區的人道主義援助需求。根據聯合國發布的年度報告指出，儘管面臨資金不足、人力緊張以及援助工作者遭受攻擊等挑戰，聯合國及其人道主義夥伴仍不懈努力，提供生存援助與保護。報告強調，人道主義援助所需資金達500億美元，但實際籌措情況仍顯不足。聯合國呼籲各國政府與國際組織加強資源投入，以確保受危機影響的民眾能夠獲得基本生存需求與安全保障。
聯合國難民署（UNHCR）於2025年5月發布的美洲事實簡報指出，拉丁美洲與加勒比海地區持續提供推動難民保護與解決方案進展的有意義機會。然而，簡報同時強調，要將此潛力轉化為持久影響，國際社會必須提供更強有力的支援與持續投資，以確保難民、被迫流離失所者及無國籍人士的權利、安全與穩定。簡報內容凸顯美洲地區在人道保護方面仍面臨結構性挑戰，需要長期且系統性的國際介入。
旅遊安全資訊平臺「IsItSafeToTravel」提供的資料顯示，美洲各國在衝突、犯罪、健康與環境等風險面向存在顯著差異。該平臺整合40個以上公開來源，每日更新美洲所有國家的最新旅行安全狀況與評分變化趨勢，涵蓋北美、中美、南美及加勒比地區，便於比較各國安全風險差異。此資料反映美洲地區安全情勢的複雜性，不同國家面臨的威脅型別與程度各異。
綜合各方資訊，「美洲之盾」倡議與聯合國人道援助呼籲形成互補，前者著重安全合作機制，後者關注基本人道需求，兩者共同構成美洲地區安全與人道議題的重要面向。
