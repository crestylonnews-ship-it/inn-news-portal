  # Asia Education Policy Assessment and Regional Cooperation Progress
  
  > UNESCO releases education policy monitoring report, Taiwan's Ministry of Education announces New Southbound Policy zoning principles, East Asian education cooperation continues to deepen.
  
  The United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) recently released the regional edition of the Global Education Monitoring Report, conducting a systematic assessment of education policies across Asian countries. The report indicates that the Policy Enhancement and Evaluation Review (PEER) mechanism can provide comparable qualitative data on national-level education policies and regulations, covering multiple dimensions including inclusivity, equitable education financing, climate change education, regulation of non-state education providers, comprehensive sexuality education, and technology and leadership. The report emphasizes that the Asian region still needs to strengthen policy coordination and resource allocation in promoting educational equity and quality improvement, particularly in the areas of climate change education and digital technology integration, and that countries should reference international standards to improve their domestic regulatory frameworks.
  
  To implement the New Southbound Policy, Taiwan's Ministry of Education recently announced the "Principles for Application and Use of Funds for Strengthening Exchange and Cooperation with ASEAN and South Asian Countries," clearly dividing New Southbound countries into three zones based on the number of students coming to Taiwan for study. Zone A includes Malaysia, Indonesia, Vietnam, Singapore, and Thailand; Zone B covers the Philippines, India, and Myanmar; and Zone C includes Pakistan, Nepal, Sri Lanka, and Bangladesh. This regulation aims to standardize educational exchange and cooperation with ASEAN and South Asian countries and ensure the rationality and transparency of fund usage.
  
  The Ministry of Education stated that this zoning approach helps formulate differentiated cooperation strategies tailored to the educational development needs of different countries and promotes long-term partnerships between Taiwan and Asian countries in the education sector.
  
  East Asia, as a key core of educational development in Asia, has drawn considerable attention for its geographic and cultural significance. According to Wikipedia, East Asia generally refers to the eastern part of Asia, carrying dual geographic and cultural significance. The region typically includes mainland China, the Japanese archipelago, Taiwan Island, the Korean Peninsula, Hainan Island, the Ryukyu Islands, and the Mongolian Plateau, covering an area of approximately 11.7 million square kilometers with a population of 1.6 billion, accounting for 22% of the world's population. The development of education policies in this region not only affects the local area but also holds indicative significance for the entire Asia's educational cooperation and exchange.
  
  Both UNESCO's report and Taiwan's Ministry of Education regulations reflect the ongoing efforts of Asian countries to improve education quality and promote regional cooperation. In the future, through policy assessment and experience sharing, the overall development of the Asian education system is expected to be further strengthened.

# 亞洲教育政策評估與區域合作進展

> 聯合國教科文組織釋出教育政策監測報告，臺灣教育部公佈新南向計畫分割槽原則，東亞地區教育合作持續深化。

聯合國教科文組織（UNESCO）近日發布《全球教育監測報告》區域版，針對亞洲各國教育政策進行系統性評估。該報告指出，教育政策提升評估（PEER）機制可提供國家層級的教育政策與法規可比性定性資料，涵蓋包容性、教育公平融資、氣候變化教育、非國家教育機構監管、全面性教育以及技術與領導力等多個面向。報告強調，亞洲地區在推動教育公平與品質提升方面仍需加強政策協調與資源分配，特別是針對氣候變遷教育與數位技術整合的領域，各國應參考國際標準完善國內法規架構。
臺灣教育部為落實新南向政策，近日公佈《強化與東協及南亞國家合作交流計畫申請及經費使用原則》，明確將新南向國家依來臺就學人數區分為三個區域。A區包括馬來西亞、印尼、越南、新加坡及泰國；B區涵蓋菲律賓、印度及緬甸；C區則包含巴基斯坦、尼泊爾、斯里蘭卡及孟加拉。該法規旨在規範與東協及南亞國家的教育交流合作，並確保經費使用的合理性與透明度。
教育部表示，此一分割槽方式有助於針對不同國家的教育發展需求，制定差異化的合作策略，並促進臺灣與亞洲各國在教育領域的長期夥伴關係。
東亞地區作為亞洲教育發展的重要核心，其地理與文化意義備受關注。根據維基百科資料，東亞泛指亞洲東部，具有地理及文化層面上的雙重意義，地區通常包括中國大陸、日本列島、臺灣島、朝鮮半島、海南島、琉球群島和蒙古高原等地，面積約1170萬平方公里，居住有16億人口，佔世界人口的22%。該地區的教育政策發展不僅影響本地區，也對整個亞洲的教育合作與交流具有指標性意義。
UNESCO的報告與臺灣教育部的法規，均反映出亞洲各國在提升教育品質、促進區域合作方面的持續努力，未來可望透過政策評估與經驗分享，進一步強化亞洲教育體系的整體發展。
