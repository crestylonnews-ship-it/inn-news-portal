  # Europe Heatwave Sweeps Region: Over 150 Million Face 35°C Temperatures; Chinese Home Appliances Capture 32% of Market
  
  > Multiple regions across Europe are experiencing record-breaking heat, with over 150 million people facing the threat of heatwaves exceeding 35°C; meanwhile, Chinese home appliance brands have captured 32% of the European retail market, as the competitive landscape continues to evolve.
  
  Europe has recently been hit by a large-scale heatwave affecting a wide area. According to an analysis by AFP based on forecasts from the German meteorological service and the European Union's scientific expert body, the Joint Research Centre (JRC), using its 2025 population estimates, approximately 350 million people in Europe are in regions where high temperatures will exceed 30°C, accounting for nearly three-fifths of Europe's population (excluding Turkey). Among them, over 150 million people are facing the threat of record-breaking temperatures above 35°C, and multiple countries have issued heat warnings, placing severe strain on public health and infrastructure.
  
  The impact of the heatwave extends beyond soaring temperatures. According to reports from the Economic Daily, the UK may face its hottest summer on record, putting pressure on agriculture, energy, and public health systems. The World Journal also noted that 135 million people in Europe are facing 35°C temperatures, which differs slightly from AFP's estimates, indicating that different analytical models yield varying assessments of the affected population size, pending further confirmation. Meteorological experts generally agree that climate change is driving a continued increase in the frequency and intensity of extreme heat events, and European countries need to accelerate the improvement of their heat response mechanisms.
  
  At the same time, the structure of the European market is also undergoing profound changes. Market research firm Euromonitor International reported that in 2025, three Chinese companies—Haier, Gree Electric Appliances (Zhuhai), and Midea Group—collectively accounted for approximately 32% of the European retail market, surpassing competitors such as Turkey's Beko and Japan's Daikin to become the largest force in the European home appliance market. Analysts believe that Chinese brands, leveraging price competitiveness and rapid technological iteration, continue to expand their market share in Europe, posing challenges to local traditional home appliance industries.

# 歐洲熱浪席捲 逾1.5億人面臨35度高溫 中國家電佔市場3成2

> 歐洲多地迎來破紀錄高溫，逾1.5億人面臨35度以上熱浪威脅；同時中國家電品牌於歐洲零售市場佔比達32%，市場格局持續演變。

歐洲近日遭遇大規模熱浪侵襲，影響範圍廣泛。根據法新社以德國氣象單位預報及歐盟旗下科學專家機構「聯合研究中心」（Joint Research Centre）2025年人口預估為基礎進行分析，歐洲約有3億5000萬人所處地區高溫將超過攝氏30度，相當於近五分之三的歐洲人口（不含土耳其）。其中，逾1.5億人正面臨攝氏35度以上的破錶高溫威脅，多國已發布高溫警報，民眾健康與基礎設施面臨嚴峻考驗。
熱浪影響不僅限於氣溫飆升。據《經濟日報》報導，英國恐迎來史上最熱夏季，對農業、能源及公共衛生系統皆造成壓力。《世界日報》亦指出，歐洲1.35億人正面臨35度高溫，與法新社估算資料略有出入，顯示不同分析模型對受影響人口規模的評估存在差異，尚待進一步確認。氣象專家普遍認為，氣候變遷導致極端高溫事件頻率與強度持續上升，歐洲各國需加速完善高溫應變機制。
與此同時，歐洲市場結構亦面臨深刻變化。資料分析公司歐睿國際（Euromonitor International）指出，2025年中國海爾、珠海格力、美的集團三家合佔歐洲零售市場約32%，超越土耳其Beko與日本大金等競爭對手，成為歐洲家電市場最大勢力。分析認為，中國品牌憑藉價格競爭力與快速技術迭代，持續擴大在歐洲市場份額，對當地傳統家電產業構成挑戰。
