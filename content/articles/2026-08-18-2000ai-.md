  # EU Launches €200 Billion AI Investment Plan to Forge European Artificial Intelligence Sovereignty
  
  > To break free from competitive pressure between the US and China, the EU has launched a €200 billion investment plan and accelerated the implementation of its AI Act, while promoting open-source models and supercomputing infrastructure, striving to become the third pole of global AI.
  
  The EU has recently intensively advanced its artificial intelligence development strategy, attempting to build an independent ecosystem amid US-China technological competition. According to the EU official document "AI Continent Action Plan," the EU is committed to becoming a global AI leader and is constructing an AI sovereignty system that does not rely on US or Chinese technology through channels such as the AI Act, the InvestAI fund, and open-source large models. A European Parliament briefing further noted that whether the EU can realize its AI Continent vision depends on the effectiveness of the action plan's implementation and progress in reducing dependence on foreign technology.
  
  Regarding funding scale, multiple media reports have mentioned the EU's proposed €200 billion new investment plan. Ideas-DTRI analysis pointed out that this plan aims to reverse Europe's development difficulties in the AI business environment and accelerate industrial transformation. Siuleeboss specifically explained that the funds operate in the form of the InvestAI fund, amounting to €200 billion, and are paired with MistralAI open-source large models and EuroHPC supercomputing resources to form a complete domestic AI development chain. However, the European Parliament briefing did not explicitly mention a specific amount, only emphasizing the need to advance through mechanisms such as the Savings and Investments Union.
  
  On the policy implementation front, the EU AI Act took effect in August 2024 and is expected to enter its full implementation phase in August 2026. Gui.tw reported that the act classifies and regulates AI systems according to risk levels, with some applications directly banned and high-risk applications subject to strict review, simultaneously affecting compliance strategies for Taiwanese and Chinese enterprises. The EU official action plan regards this regulatory framework as the foundation for balancing trust-building and innovation promotion.
  
  On the industry implementation side, the "AI Innovation Park" in Heilbronn, Baden-Württemberg, Germany, recently launched. Xinhua Net reported that the park has a total investment of several billion euros and is Germany's largest and most comprehensively planned AI industry cluster to date, focusing on industrial AI, smart manufacturing, autonomous driving, smart logistics, and AI ethics and safety, with the goal of creating Europe's largest AI hub. This move is seen as a concrete practice of the EU's AI sovereignty strategy.
  
  Different media outlets offer slightly varying interpretations of the EU's AI strategy. Both Siuleeboss and Ideas-DTRI emphasize the critical role of the €200 billion investment, but the former focuses on the technology ecosystem (open-source models, supercomputing), while the latter concentrates on industrial transformation benefits. The European Parliament briefing, from a governance perspective, reminds that progress is needed in other areas beyond the action plan. Gui.tw and Xinhua Net present the execution dimension of the EU strategy from the angles of regulatory compliance and industrial parks, respectively. Overall, the EU is pursuing a three-pronged approach of capital investment, regulation formulation, and infrastructure development, attempting to gradually close the gap with the US and China in AI after 2026.

# 歐盟推2000億AI投資計畫 打造歐洲人工智慧主權

> 歐盟為突破中美競爭壓力，啟動2000億歐元投資計畫並加速AI法案執行，同時推動開源模型與超算基礎建設，力求成為全球AI第三極。

歐盟近期密集推動人工智慧發展戰略，試圖在中美科技競爭的夾縫中建立獨立生態。根據歐盟官方檔案《AI Continent Action Plan》顯示，歐盟致力於成為全球人工智慧領導者，並透過《AI法案》、InvestAI基金及開源大模型等途徑，建構不依賴中美技術的AI主權體系。歐洲議會簡報進一步指出，歐盟能否實現AI大陸願景，取決於行動計畫的落實成效，以及減少對外國技術依賴的進展。
在資金規模方面，多家媒體報導提及歐盟擬推動2000億歐元的新投資計畫。Ideas-DTRI 分析指出，此計畫旨在扭轉歐洲在AI商業環境上的發展困境，加速產業轉型。Siuleeboss 則具體說明該資金以InvestAI基金形式運作，規模達2000億歐元，並搭配MistralAI開源大模型與EuroHPC超算資源，形成完整的本土AI發展鏈。不過，歐洲議會簡報未明確提及具體金額，僅強調需透過Savings and Investments Union等機制推進。
政策執行面，歐盟《AI法案》已於2024年8月生效，並預計於2026年8月進入完整執行階段。 Gui.tw 報導說明，該法案依風險等級對AI系統進行分類管制，部分應用直接禁止，高風險應用則需透過嚴格審查，同時影響臺灣與中國企業合規策略。歐盟官方行動計畫則將此監管框架視為建立信任與促進創新並重的基礎。
產業落地方面，德國巴登—符騰堡州海爾布隆的「人工智慧創新園」近日啟動。 新華網 報導指出，該園區總投資達數十億歐元，為德國迄今最大、規劃最完整的AI產業叢集，重點佈局工業AI、智慧製造、自動駕駛、智慧物流及AI倫理與安全等領域，目標打造歐洲最大AI聚落。此舉被視為歐盟落實AI主權戰略的具體實踐。
不同媒體對歐盟AI戰略的解讀略有差異。 Siuleeboss 與 Ideas-DTRI 均強調2000億歐元投資的關鍵角色，但前者側重技術生態（開源模型、超算），後者聚焦產業轉型效益。歐洲議會簡報則從治理角度提醒，除行動計畫外，還需在其他領域取得進展。 Gui.tw 與 新華網 分別從法規合規與產業園區兩面，呈現歐盟戰略的執行層面。整體而言，歐盟正透過資金投入、法規制定與基礎建設三管齊下，試圖在2026年後逐步縮小與中美在AI領域的差距。
