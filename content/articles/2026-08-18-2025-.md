  # Global Clean Energy Sees Strong Growth in 2025, U.S. Policy Shift Raises Concerns
  
  > The International Energy Agency report highlights that geopolitical tensions expose vulnerabilities in energy security, with global clean energy continuing strong growth in 2025 and renewables becoming the largest source of total energy supply growth for the first time, while the U.S. policy shift toward fossil fuels has sparked concern.
  
  The International Energy Agency (IEA) released its World Energy Outlook 2024 in October 2024, revealing that the global energy market faces significant challenges and opportunities. The report emphasizes that escalating geopolitical tensions have further exposed the fragility of energy security, while underscoring the urgency of expanding clean energy technologies and accelerating the transition. The report specifically notes that regional tensions such as the Middle East conflict continue to pressure global energy supply chains, prompting countries to reassess the balance between energy security and transition strategies.
  
  According to the Global Energy Statistical Review, jointly published by the Energy Institute, Ember, KPMG, and Kearney, global clean energy continued its strong growth momentum in 2025. The report shows that renewables became the largest source of total energy supply (TES) growth for the first time, marking an important milestone in the global energy transition. However, the report also points out that the U.S. energy policy shift toward fossil fuels casts a shadow over global decarbonization efforts, emerging as one of the key concerns in the energy sector in 2025.
  
  Different media outlets offer varying interpretations of the direction of U.S. energy policy. BBC Chinese reported that China's electricity generation is approximately 2.5 times that of the United States, providing a foundational advantage for China's development of energy-intensive industries such as AI. The report cites expert opinions suggesting that China will be able to gradually address computing power challenges over time, and while it may not catch up with the U.S., domestic AI chips and hardware systems are expected to help close the gap. Meanwhile, an article on the vocus.cc platform discusses the U.S. "Big and Beautiful Bill," questioning whether U.S. renewable energy development may come to an early end as a result.
  
  These reports indicate that the impact of the U.S. energy policy shift on global clean energy development remains to be seen, with different sources offering divergent assessments of the policy's actual effects.
  
  Changes in U.S. energy policy have a direct impact on the Americas. If the United States further tilts toward fossil fuels, it could weaken North America's leading position in clean energy investment and technological innovation. For Latin America and the Caribbean region, the U.S. policy shift may affect regional energy cooperation frameworks and clean energy financing channels. At the industry level, renewable energy supply chain companies face investment risks due to policy uncertainty, while the fossil fuel industry may gain short-term policy benefits. Educational and research institutions will also need to adjust talent development directions in relevant fields to respond to changes in the energy industry structure.
  
  Different perspectives reveal a tension between the global clean energy growth trend and the U.S. policy headwind. Some analyses suggest that despite the U.S. policy shift toward fossil fuels, the momentum of the global clean energy market remains driven by major economies such as China and the European Union, and the impact of U.S. policy is limited in scope. However, other viewpoints argue that as one of the world's largest economies, the United States' policy shift could produce a demonstration effect, influencing the energy policy directions of other countries. The AI computing power and energy constraint issues mentioned in the reports also serve as a reminder of the complex relationship between digital transformation and energy transformation, cautioning against oversimplifying the impact of any single policy on the overall energy landscape.
  
  Key areas for future observation include the specific legislative progress and implementation details of the U.S. "Big and Beautiful Bill," as well as the extent of its actual impact on the renewable energy industry. Follow-up data from the IEA's World Energy Outlook 2024 report will provide important reference for the global energy transition process. The competitive and cooperative relationship between China and the United States in AI and energy is worth continued attention, particularly how China's electricity generation advantage can be translated into industrial competitiveness. In addition, progress in regional energy integration in Latin America and the Caribbean, and the impact of U.S. policy changes on the energy strategies of these countries, will be important areas for future observation.

# 全球清潔能源2025年強勁增長 美國政策轉向成隱憂

> 國際能源總署報告指出地緣政治緊張暴露能源安全脆弱性，2025年全球清潔能源持續強勁增長，可再生能源首次成為總能源供應增長最大來源，但美國政策轉向化石燃料引發關注。

國際能源總署（IEA）於2024年10月發布《2024世界能源展望》（World Energy Outlook 2024），報告揭示當前全球能源市場面臨重大挑戰與機會。報告強調，地緣政治緊張局勢更加暴露了能源安全的脆弱性，同時突顯推動清潔能源技術擴充套件和加速轉型的緊迫性。該報告特別指出，中東衝突等區域緊張局勢對全球能源供應鏈造成持續壓力，促使各國重新審視能源安全與轉型策略的平衡點。
根據能源研究所（Energy Institute）與Ember、KPMG和Kearney聯合發布的《世界能源統計評論》，2025年全球清潔能源繼續保持強勁增長勢頭。報告顯示，可再生能源首次成為總能源供應（TES）增長的最大來源，這標誌著全球能源轉型程式的重要里程碑。然而，該報告同時指出，美國能源政策轉向化石燃料為全球脫碳努力蒙上陰影，成為2025年能源領域的主要隱憂之一。
不同媒體對美國能源政策走向的解讀存在差異。BBC中文報導指出，中國發電量約為美國的2.5倍，這為中國發展AI等能源密集型產業提供了基礎優勢。報導引用專家觀點認為，中國將能隨時間推移逐步解決算力難題，雖可能無法追平美國，但國產AI晶片和硬體系統有望彌補差距。另一方面，vocus.cc平臺文章討論美國「大而美法案」（The Big and Beautiful Bill），質疑美國再生能源發展是否將因此提前落幕。
這些報導顯示，美國能源政策轉向對全球清潔能源發展的影響仍待觀察，不同來源對政策實際效果的評估存在分歧。
美國能源政策變化對美洲地區產生直接影響。若美國進一步偏向化石燃料，可能削弱北美地區在清潔能源投資與技術創新方面的領先地位。對於拉丁美洲與加勒比海地區而言，美國政策轉向可能影響區域能源合作框架與清潔能源融資渠道。產業層面，再生能源供應鏈企業面臨政策不確定性帶來的投資風險，而化石燃料產業則可能獲得短期政策紅利。教育與研究機構也需調整相關領域的人才培養方向，以應對能源產業結構變化。
不同觀點顯示，全球清潔能源增長趨勢與美國政策逆流形成張力。部分分析認為，儘管美國政策轉向化石燃料，但全球清潔能源市場動能仍由中國、歐盟等主要經濟體驅動，美國政策影響範圍有限。然而，也有觀點指出，美國作為全球最大經濟體之一，其政策轉向可能產生示範效應，影響其他國家能源政策走向。報導中提及的AI算力與能源約束問題，也提醒人們注意數位轉型與能源轉型之間的複雜關係，不應過度簡化單一政策對整體能源格局的影響。
後續觀察重點包括美國「大而美法案」的具體立法進展與實施細節，以及該法案對再生能源產業的實際影響程度。IEA《2024世界能源展望》報告的後續追蹤資料將提供全球能源轉型程式的重要參考。中美在AI與能源領域的競爭合作關係值得持續關注，特別是中國發電量優勢如何轉化為產業競爭力。此外，拉丁美洲與加勒比海地區在區域能源整合方面的進展，以及美國政策變化對這些國家能源戰略的影響，將是未來觀察的重要面向。
