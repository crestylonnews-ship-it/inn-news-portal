  # Global Education 2030 Agenda Progress Stalled, UNESCO Calls for Accelerated Action
  
  > UNESCO notes that with only six years remaining until 2030, progress toward global education goals is severely lagging: only 17% of targets are on track, 35% are stalled or regressing, and the crisis in equitable and quality education continues to deepen.
  
  The United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) recently released its *2024 Sustainable Development Goals Report*, which indicates that global education progress is seriously off track. The report shows that with only six years remaining to achieve the *2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development*, only 17% of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals are progressing smoothly, while 35% of the targets are stagnant or even regressing. Among these, Sustainable Development Goal 4 (SDG4)—ensuring inclusive and equitable quality education—is particularly concerning. The global education crisis persists and deepens in terms of equity and quality, severely hindering education's potential as a catalyst for change and thereby affecting the realization of the entire 2030 Agenda.
  
  As the UN specialized agency responsible for education, UNESCO is mandated to lead and coordinate the Education 2030 Agenda. This agenda is an important component of the global poverty eradication movement, driving worldwide development through 17 Sustainable Development Goals. Education is regarded as a key element for achieving all goals, which is why a dedicated Goal 4 was established to "ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all." However, the report points out that the global education crisis is reflected not only in unequal resource distribution but also in regional development disparities.
  
  In some countries in the Middle East, long-standing conflicts and political instability have severely damaged education infrastructure, with children and adolescents maintaining persistently high out-of-school rates. In the Oceania region, Pacific island nations face structural challenges such as scarce educational resources and teacher shortages, issues that have become even more acute in recent years amid frequent climate change and natural disasters.
  
  While different media outlets and international organizations share a consensus on the current state of global education, discrepancies remain in specific data and attribution. UNESCO official documents emphasize that the primary causes of lagging education progress are insufficient funding, weak policy implementation, and education disruptions in conflict-affected areas. However, some analyses point out that learning losses following the pandemic have not yet been fully recovered, further exacerbating the existing educational inequalities. Taiwan's Ministry of Education Department of International and Cross-Strait Education also continues to monitor the quality of foreign universities, providing study-abroad information through its reference list section, indirectly reflecting Taiwan's emphasis on international education quality.
  
  Experts recommend that countries strengthen cross-border education cooperation, particularly educational assistance and experience exchange in the Middle East and Oceania regions, to narrow regional development gaps. UNESCO is expected to convene a global education conference in 2024 to further discuss specific strategies and action plans for accelerating the implementation of the Education 2030 Agenda.

# 全球教育2030議程進展受阻 聯合國教科文組織呼籲加速行動

> 聯合國教科文組織指出，距離2030年僅剩六年，全球教育目標進展嚴重落後，僅17%目標順利推進，35%停滯或倒退，公平與優質教育危機持續加深。

聯合國教科文組織（UNESCO）近日發布《2024年可持續發展目標報告》，指出全球教育進展正嚴重偏離軌道。報告顯示，距離實現《2030年可持續發展議程》僅剩六年，但全球17項可持續發展目標中，僅有17%的進展順利，另有35%的目標停滯不前甚至出現倒退。其中，可持續發展目標4（SDG4）——確保包容和公平的優質教育——的進展尤其令人擔憂，全球教育危機在公平與優質教育方面持續存在並不斷加深，嚴重阻礙了教育作為變革催化劑的潛力，進而影響整個2030年議程的實現。
UNESCO作為聯合國負責教育事務的專門機構，被授權領導並協調教育2030議程。該議程是全球消除貧困運動的重要組成部分，透過17項可持續發展目標推動全球發展。教育被視為實現所有目標的關鍵要素，因此設有專門的目標4，旨在「確保包容和公平的優質教育，促進終身學習機會」。然而，報告指出，全球教育危機不僅體現在資源分配不均，更反映在地區發展落差上。
中東地區部分國家因長期衝突與政治不穩定，教育基礎設施遭受嚴重破壞，兒童與青少年失學率居高不下；大洋洲地區則面臨太平洋島國教育資源匱乏、師資短缺等結構性挑戰，這些問題在近年氣候變遷與自然災害頻仍的情況下更顯嚴峻。
不同媒體與國際組織對當前全球教育局勢的評估雖有共識，但在具體資料與歸因上仍存在差異。UNESCO官方檔案強調，教育進展落後的主因在於資金投入不足、政策執行力薄弱，以及衝突地區的教育中斷問題。然而，部分分析指出，疫情後的學習損失尚未完全恢復，進一步加劇了教育不平等的現狀。臺灣教育部國際及兩岸教育司亦持續關注外國大學校院品質，透過參考名冊專區提供留學資訊，間接反映臺灣對國際教育品質的重視。
專家建議，各國應加強跨境教育合作，特別是中東與大洋洲地區的教育援助與經驗交流，以縮小區域發展差距。UNESCO預計將於2024年召開全球教育會議，進一步商討加速落實教育2030議程的具體策略與行動方案。
