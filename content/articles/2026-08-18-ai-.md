The report analyzes the current state of US investment, supply chain structures, and regulatory measures in these fields, and proposes recommendations and actions for the government, emphasizing that the US must take the lead in these three technology competitions.
  
  Stanford University's Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence Institute (HAI) had earlier released its "2025 AI Report" in April 2025, observing that AI technology is developing rapidly and gradually permeating people's daily lives. The business sector has invested heavily in AI research and development, and governments worldwide are actively formulating policies to promote technological development and application, indicating that AI has become the core focus of global technological competition.
  
  On the industry front, the career path of Palmer Luckey, founder of US defense technology company Anduril Industries, from the VR industry to defense technology has attracted attention. Luckey has been passionate about ocean exploration since childhood, and his entrepreneurial trajectory from virtual reality technology to the defense technology sector reflects the flow of US tech talent between defense and civilian applications, while also highlighting the importance of AI and advanced technologies in defense applications.

The subsequent development of how countries balance the relationship between technological development and security regulation is worth watching.

# 美智庫聚焦AI與量子科技競賽 史丹佛發布年度報告

> 美國智庫外交關係協會發布報告聚焦人工智慧、量子科技與生物技術；史丹佛大學HAI發布2025年AI報告，Anduril創辦人轉戰國防科技引發關注。

美國智庫外交關係協會（CFR）於2025年11月發布報告《美國經濟安全：贏得未來技術競賽》，聚焦確保美國在人工智慧、量子科技與生物技術三大關鍵領域的優勢地位。該報告分析美國在這些領域的投資現狀、供應鏈結構與管制措施，並提出政府應採取的建議與舉措，強調美國需在這三項技術競賽中取得領先。
史丹佛大學人本人工智慧研究院（HAI）則早在2025年4月便發布《2025年人工智慧報告》，觀察AI技術正迅速發展並逐漸深入人們日常生活。企業界已投入大量資源於AI研發，各國政府亦積極制定政策推動技術發展與應用，顯示人工智慧已成為全球科技競爭的核心焦點。
在產業實務層面，美國國防技術公司Anduril Industries創辦人Palmer Luckey從VR產業轉戰國防科技的發展路徑引發關注。Luckey自幼對海洋探索充滿興趣，其創業軌跡從虛擬實境技術轉向國防科技領域，反映美國科技人才在國防與民用技術間的流動趨勢，也凸顯AI與先進技術在國防應用上的重要性。
綜合觀察，美國在AI、量子科技與生物技術領域的政策佈局與產業動向顯示，技術競賽已成為國家經濟安全的核心議題。智庫報告與學術機構發布的資料相互呼應，指出政府政策與民間投資將持續影響美國在關鍵技術領域的競爭力。後續發展值得關注各國如何平衡技術發展與安全管制之間的關係。
