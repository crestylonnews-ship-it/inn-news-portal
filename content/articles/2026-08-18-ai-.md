  # Asia's AI Ecosystem Rises as Global Governance and Industrial Transformation Advance in Parallel
  
  > Asia's AI market reaches $102 billion; Taiwan's 15.4% adoption rate ranks it among the global top 20. The 2025 World AI Conference released a global governance action plan, and Intelligent Asia 2026 exhibition will take place in Taipei in August.
  
  According to the "AI Ecosystem Across Asia 2026" report published by Digital in Asia, Asia's artificial intelligence market has reached $102 billion, becoming the core engine of global AI growth. The report provides a detailed analysis of the current state of AI development across Asian countries, covering key issues such as sovereign large language model (LLM) initiatives and chip supply chains. The report points out that Asia is redefining global competitiveness from a springboard position, with governments and industries across the region actively building out AI ecosystems to address rapidly evolving technological landscapes and market demands.
  
  MakerPro, citing Microsoft's Global AI Adoption Report, noted that Taiwan holds a favorable position in AI development with an adoption rate of 15.4%, successfully ranking among the global top 20. However, the report also cautioned that many regions remain marginalized, and integrating these markets into the global AI growth framework will be a core challenge that global leaders must address in the coming years. This perspective aligns with Digital in Asia's observation of uneven AI development across Asian countries, highlighting significant disparities in AI adoption rates within the region.
  
  On the industry exhibition front, China Times Economic News reported that "Intelligent Asia 2026," Asia's premier annual industrial technology exhibition, will be held from August 19 to 22 at Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center Halls 1 and 2. This year's exhibition, themed "Integrate as One. Power Across Asia.," is positioned as a key strategic platform for industrial integration and transformation in Asia, showcasing advanced technologies and equipment while connecting cross-industry resources to promote cooperation and exchange among Asian countries in smart manufacturing and AI applications.
  
  At the international governance level, the Chinese Ministry of Foreign Affairs website showed that the World Artificial Intelligence Conference and the High-Level Meeting on AI Global Governance, held on July 26, 2025, released the "AI Global Governance Action Plan." The document emphasizes that AI is a new frontier for human development and a key driving force behind the next round of technological revolution and industrial transformation, while also serving as an international public good that can benefit humanity. The action plan proposes a governance framework addressing both the opportunities and challenges posed by AI, reflecting the international community's growing emphasis on AI regulation.
  
  A comprehensive observation reveals that Asia's AI development is multifaceted: market size continues to expand, adoption rates vary significantly across countries, industry exhibitions facilitate technology exchange, and international governance frameworks are gradually taking shape. While Taiwan has performed impressively in adoption rates, it must still pay attention to the integration of marginalized markets. Exhibitions such as Intelligent Asia 2026 will serve as important platforms for Asian countries to share AI industry experiences.

# 亞洲AI生態崛起 全球治理與產業轉型並進

> 亞洲AI市場規模達1020億美元，臺灣採用率15.4%躋身全球前20；2025年世界人工智慧大會發布全球治理行動計劃，Intelligent Asia 2026展覽8月於臺北登場。

根據Digital in Asia發布的《AI Ecosystem Across Asia 2026》報告，亞洲人工智慧市場規模已達1020億美元，成為全球AI成長的核心引擎。該報告詳細分析亞洲各國的AI發展現況，涵蓋主權大型語言模型（LLM）計畫與晶片供應鏈等關鍵議題。報告指出，亞洲正以跳板姿態重新定義全球競爭力，各國政府與產業界正積極佈局AI生態系，以應對快速變遷的技術環境與市場需求。
MakerPro引用微軟全球AI採用率報告指出，臺灣在AI發展程式中佔據有利位置，採用率達15.4%，成功躋身全球前20名。然而，報告同時提醒，仍有廣大地區處於邊緣化狀態，如何將這些市場納入全球AI成長版圖，將是未來幾年全球領導者必須面對的核心課題。此觀點呼應了Digital in Asia報告中對亞洲各國發展不均的觀察，凸顯區域內AI普及率仍存在顯著落差。
在產業展會方面，工商時報報導，亞洲年度工業科技指標展會「Intelligent Asia 2026」將於8月19日至22日在臺北南港展覽館一、二館盛大登場。本屆展覽以「Integrate as One. Power Across Asia.」為主題，定位為亞洲工業整合與轉型的重要策略平臺，不僅展示先進技術與裝置，更串聯跨產業資源，促進亞洲各國在智慧製造與AI應用領域的合作交流。
國際治理層面，中國外交部網站顯示，2025年7月26日舉行的世界人工智慧大會暨人工智慧全球治理高階別會議發表了《人工智慧全球治理行動計劃》。該檔案強調人工智慧是人類發展的新領域，也是新一輪科技革命和產業變革的重要驅動力量，同時可成為造福人類的國際公共產品。行動計劃針對AI帶來的機遇與挑戰提出治理框架，反映國際社會對AI規範的重視。
綜合觀察，亞洲AI發展呈現多重面向：市場規模持續擴張、各國採用率差異顯著、產業展會促進技術交流，以及國際治理框架逐步成形。臺灣雖在採用率上表現亮眼，但仍需關注邊緣化市場的整合議題；而Intelligent Asia 2026等展會將成為亞洲各國交流AI產業經驗的重要平臺。
