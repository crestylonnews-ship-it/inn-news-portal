  # US AI Strategic Expansion, Latin America Accelerates Adoption Amid Political Pressure
  
  > Latin America and the Caribbean are accelerating their artificial intelligence development while the United States exerts political influence through visa policies, leaving regional nations facing new challenges in balancing technology adoption with sovereignty.
  
  According to information released by the United Nations Economic Commission for Latin America and the Caribbean (ECLAC), the commission, together with Chile's National Artificial Intelligence Center (CENIA), published the third edition of the Latin America Artificial Intelligence Index (ILIA 2025), which systematically measures progress across 19 countries in the region in terms of AI readiness, adoption levels, and governance mechanisms. The index is regarded as a pioneering assessment tool in the region, designed to track the overall performance and policy maturity of each country in AI technology development.
  
  Meanwhile, academic research indicates that the United States is gradually forming a comprehensive global strategic system. According to an analytical article published by the Center for American Studies at Fudan University, against the backdrop of intensifying global technological competition and the reshaping of the geopolitical order, the US AI strategy has evolved into a comprehensive framework encompassing multiple dimensions—technology, security, economics, and values—strengthening its global influence in the AI field through policy mechanism design and multi-pronged advancement.
  
  Notably, political influence has already manifested concretely. According to a report on the Chinese-language website of The New York Times, the Trump administration is leveraging US visa policies as a tool to strengthen its control over Latin America. The report states that the United States is pressuring political figures in the region by threatening to revoke visas, forcing them to accept US demands or punishing those who question US policies. This move has raised concerns among regional nations regarding sovereignty and technological autonomy.
  
  In a comprehensive assessment, Latin American nations are facing political pressure from the north even as they accelerate the adoption of AI technology. ILIA 2025 shows continued progress across the region in AI governance and adoption, but the US practice of exerting influence through means such as visa policies may pose a challenge to the policy autonomy of regional countries. Experts believe that how to strike a balance between adopting US technology and maintaining political independence will be a key issue for Latin American nations in the future. What remains to be confirmed is the specific scope and impact of the US visa policy, as well as how individual countries in the region will respond to this pressure.

# 美AI戰略擴張 拉美加速採用伴隨政治壓力

> 拉丁美洲與加勒比海地區加速推動人工智慧發展，同時美國透過簽證政策施加政治影響，區域國家在科技採用與主權之間面臨新挑戰。

根據聯合國拉丁美洲和加勒比經濟委員會（CEPAL）發布的資訊，該委員會與智利國家人工智慧中心（CENIA）共同發表了第三版《拉丁美洲人工智慧指數》（ILIA 2025），系統性測量區域內19個國家在人工智慧準備度、採用程度與治理機制方面的進展。此指數被視為區域內首創的評估工具，旨在追蹤各國在AI技術發展上的整體表現與政策成熟度。
與此同時，學術研究指出美國正逐步形成一套複合型全球戰略體系。根據復旦大學美國研究中心刊載的分析文章，在全球技術競爭加劇與地緣政治秩序重塑的背景下，美國人工智慧戰略已發展出涵蓋技術、安全、經濟與價值觀等多維目標的綜合架構，透過政策機制設計與多層面推進，強化其在AI領域的全球影響力。
值得注意的是，政治層面的影響已具體顯現。根據《紐約時報》中文網報導，特朗普政府正利用美國簽證政策作為工具，加強對拉丁美洲地區的控制。報導指出，美國透過威脅撤銷簽證的方式，向該地區政治人物施壓，迫使對方接受美國要求，或懲罰那些對美國政策提出質疑的人士。此舉引發區域國家對主權與科技自主性的擔憂。
綜合觀察，拉丁美洲國家在加速採用人工智慧技術的同時，正面臨來自北方的政治壓力。ILIA 2025顯示區域各國在AI治理與採用方面持續進步，但美國透過簽證等手段施加影響的做法，可能對區域國家的政策自主性構成挑戰。專家認為，如何在引進美國技術與維持政治獨立之間取得平衡，將是拉美國家未來面臨的重要課題。目前尚待確認的是，美國簽證政策的具體實施範圍與影響程度，以及區域各國將如何回應此種壓力。
