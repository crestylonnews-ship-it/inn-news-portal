  The Russia-led Eurasian Economic Union (EEU) held a summit in Astana, Kazakhstan, where leaders warned that Armenia could face suspension of its membership over its application to join the European Union. Putin, along with the leaders of Belarus, Kazakhstan, and Kyrgyzstan, stated that Armenia's EU application poses a "significant risk" to its economic security, and called on officials to prepare a December report assessing the consequences of suspending Armenia's membership, while urging Armenia to hold a referendum on choosing between the EU and the EEU.
  
  This warning comes a week before Armenia's June 7 parliamentary election, as tensions mount between the Kremlin and Armenian Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan, with Armenia having already suspended its participation in the Collective Security Treaty Organization.
  
  Central America is being hit by a heatwave, with El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, and Nicaragua experiencing extreme temperatures, minimal rainfall, and dry conditions, prompting authorities to maintain alert status. El Salvador's meteorological stations have broken historical temperature records, with San Francisco Gotera reaching 40.6°C on August 13; Guatemala's Ministry of Agriculture is monitoring agricultural and climatic conditions across 110 municipalities where temperatures could exceed 35°C; southern Honduras is forecast to reach 44°C, while Nicaragua expects maximum temperatures of 39°C with a heat index of 41°C.
  
  Authorities are advising the public to stay hydrated and minimize sun exposure, with particular caution recommended for children, the elderly, and those with chronic illnesses.

Personal accounts describe family members who went missing after registering as refugees in Sudan, reportedly held by the military, as families struggle to secure their release. # 俄歐經盟威脅暫停亞美尼亞、中美洲熱浪襲擊、蘇丹戰亂難民危機

> 俄羅斯領導的歐亞經濟聯盟警告可能暫停亞美尼亞成員資格；中美洲多國遭遇極端高溫；蘇丹戰爭中難民被軍事拘押引發人道關注。

俄羅斯領導的歐亞經濟聯盟（Eurasian Economic Union）於哈薩克阿斯塔納舉行峰會，領袖們警告亞美尼亞可能因申請加入歐盟而面臨成員資格暫停。普京與白俄羅斯、哈薩克、吉爾吉斯斯坦領袖表示，亞美尼亞的歐盟申請對其經濟安全構成「重大風險」，並要求官員準備十二月報告評估暫停亞美尼亞成員資格的後果，同時敦促亞美尼亞就選擇歐盟或歐亞經濟聯盟舉行公投。
此警告出現在亞美尼亞六月七日國會選舉前一週，正值克里姆林宮與亞美尼亞總理尼科爾·帕什尼揚（Nikol Pashinyan）關係緊張，亞美尼亞已暫停參與集體安全條約組織。
中美洲地區正遭受熱浪侵襲，薩爾瓦多、瓜地馬拉、宏都拉斯和尼加拉瓜多國出現極端高溫、降雨稀少及乾燥條件，當局保持警戒。薩爾瓦多氣象站打破歷史最高溫紀錄，聖弗朗西斯科戈泰拉（San Francisco Gotera）八月十三日氣溫高達攝氏四十點六度；瓜地馬拉農業部監測一百一十個市鎮的農 climatic 條件，氣溫可能超過攝氏三十五度；宏都拉斯南部預報值達攝氏四十四度，尼加拉瓜預計最高溫攝氏三十九度、熱指數達攝氏四十一度。
當局建議民眾保持水分補充、減少陽光曝曬，尤其兒童、老年人及慢性病患者應特別注意。
蘇丹戰爭持續造成人道危機，BBC 透過驗證分析報導厄利垂亞難民認出親屬被蘇丹軍隊拘押的經歷。個人描述親屬在蘇丹登記為難民後失蹤，據報被蘇丹軍隊拘留，家屬尋求釋放的過程充滿挫折。此報導凸顯蘇丹衝突對區域穩定與人道狀況的深遠影響。
