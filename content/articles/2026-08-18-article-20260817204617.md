  # Education Policy Updates Across Multiple Nations: Africa's Learning and Skills Development Gains Attention
  
  > The UN Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) released a report on the transformation of learning and skills development in Africa, Tanzania launched a new version of its education policy, and the China-Africa Cooperation Forum focused on education development issues.
  
  UNESCO, the African Union, and the United Nations Children's Fund (UNICEF) recently jointly released the "Report on the Transformation of Learning and Skills Development in Africa: The Second Continental Report." The report was officially launched during the Forum on the Sustainable Development of the African Region, monitoring and evaluating the implementation progress of the "Continental Education Strategy for Africa" (CESA). This report provides important regional data and policy recommendations for Africa's education development, drawing high attention from the international community to Africa's education transformation.
  
  At the level of education policy practice, Tanzania officially released the 2023 version of its "Education and Training Policy" on January 31, 2025, marking significant progress in reshaping the country's education system. According to analysis by the Age Order Think Tank, African countries' integration of Chinese language courses into national basic education systems is primarily implemented through phased pilot programs, with varying levels of development across countries. Tanzania's new education policy is regarded as a representative case of African education reform, and its policy content and implementation pathways hold reference value for other African countries.
  
  China-Africa education cooperation continues to deepen. The 2024 China-Africa Cooperation Forum summit was held in Beijing from September 4 to 6, with the theme "Joining Hands to Advance Modernization and Build a High-Level China-Africa Community with a Shared Future." Education development is viewed as a foundational pathway for promoting national modernization, and education cooperation has become an important component of China-Africa cooperation. Africa's education development is not only a priority strategy for the United Nations system and major international organizations, but is also considered a key element in achieving fair and inclusive global development.
  
  Different sources indicate that Africa's education development faces a situation where multiple challenges and opportunities coexist. Monitoring reports from international organizations emphasize the importance of skills development and learning transformation, while each country's local policies reflect specific education reform directions. Reshaping the education system requires long-term investment, and African countries still need to address practical issues such as resource allocation, teacher training, and curriculum design in the process of advancing education modernization. The future development trajectory of African education will depend on the coordinated advancement of international cooperation and local policies.

# 多國教育政策更新 非洲學習與技能發展受關注

> 聯合國教科文組織發布非洲學習與技能發展轉型報告，坦尚尼亞推出新版教育政策，中非合作論壇聚焦教育發展議題。

聯合國教科文組織、非洲聯盟與聯合國兒童基金會近日聯合發布《非洲學習和技能發展轉型：第2次大陸報告》，該報告於非洲可持續發展區域論壇期間正式推出，針對「非洲大陸教育戰略」（Continental Education Strategy for Africa，CESA）的實施進展進行監測與評估。此報告為非洲教育發展提供了重要的區域性資料與政策建議，引發國際社會對非洲教育轉型的高度關注。
在教育政策實踐層面，坦尚尼亞於2025年1月31日正式發布2023年版本的《教育和培訓政策》，標誌著該國在重塑教育體系方面取得重大進展。根據歲序智庫分析，非洲各國在將中文課程納入國民基礎教育體系方面，主要以分階段試點推行為主，各國中文納入發展呈現層級差異。坦尚尼亞的新教育政策被視為非洲教育改革的代表性案例，其政策內容與實施路徑對其他非洲國傢俱有參考價值。
中非教育合作亦持續深化。2024年中非合作論壇峰會於9月4日至6日在北京舉行，此次峰會主題為「攜手推進現代化，共築高水平中非命運共同體」。教育發展被視為推動國家現代化的基礎路徑，教育合作成為中非合作的重要內容。非洲教育發展不僅是聯合國系統及各主要國際組織的優先戰略，也被認為是實現全球公平包容發展的關鍵環節。
不同來源顯示，非洲教育發展面臨多重挑戰與機遇並存的局面。國際組織的監測報告強調技能發展與學習轉型的重要性，而各國本土政策則反映出具體的教育改革方向。教育體系的重塑需要長期投入，非洲各國在推進教育現代化的過程中，仍需應對資源分配、師資培養與課程設計等實際問題。未來非洲教育的發展軌跡，將取決於國際合作與本土政策的協同推進。
