  # Global Health Governance Reform: Middle East and Central Asia Explore Regional Approaches
  
  > The Middle East and Central Asia region, with its combination of high-income nations and conflict-vulnerable environments, has become a typical microcosm of global health governance reform, drawing significant attention to regional approaches and priority directions.
  
  According to reports from ebiotrade, the Middle East and Central Asia (MECA) region is emerging as an important subject of observation for global health governance reform. The region's distinctiveness lies in the fact that it houses both highly developed high-income economies and fragile environments shaped by prolonged conflict and emergencies, creating a landscape where the complexity and challenges of health governance coexist. This dual structure provides a typical example for exploring regional health governance approaches and highlights the governance difficulties arising from countries at different stages of development sharing the same region in the context of global public health issues.
  
  The World Health Organization continues to promote the implementation of infectious disease prevention and control mechanisms across countries through the International Health Regulations (2005). The Regulations clearly specify the effective dates for each contracting state and periodically outline the implementation schedule for regulatory amendments, ensuring that countries can apply the relevant provisions according to their own circumstances. Appendix One details the applicable text versions of the Regulations for each contracting state, emphasizing that unless otherwise notified by a contracting state, the text of the Regulations adopted by the 58th World Health Assembly Resolution in 2005 shall serve as the applicable basis. This framework provides an institutional foundation for Middle East and Central Asian countries to address cross-border infectious disease risks and offers an international legal basis for regional health governance reform.
  
  From a historical perspective, the precursor to the concept of global health, "international health," inherited the cross-border research tradition of the tropical medicine era and, through international conferences, the conclusion of international conventions, and the establishment of international organizations, ushered in a new period of institutionalized cooperation and global health governance. At that time, the global disease risk was predominantly infectious, such as the global influenza pandemic that broke out during World War I. This not only caused a major public health crisis but also prematurely ended the war proceedings, underscoring the profound impact of infectious diseases on global security and the socioeconomy.
  
  Experts point out that health governance reform in the Middle East and Central Asia region must balance the resource advantages of high-income countries with the urgent needs of fragile environments, strengthening disease surveillance, emergency response, and health system resilience through regional cooperation mechanisms. The implementation status of the International Health Regulations will serve as an important indicator for assessing the governance effectiveness of each country, while the priority directions of regional approaches need to be planned around the conflict, displacement, and cross-border disease transmission risks unique to the region. # 全球衛生治理改革：中東與中亞地區探索區域性路徑

> 中東與中亞地區因兼具高收入國家與衝突脆弱環境，成為全球衛生治理改革的典型縮影，區域性路徑與優先方向備受關注。

根據生物通報導，中東與中亞地區（Middle East and Central Asia, MECA）正成為全球衛生治理改革的重要觀察物件。該地區的特殊性在於，既擁有高度發展的高收入經濟體，也同時存在因長期衝突與緊急狀況而脆弱的環境，使得衛生治理的複雜性與挑戰性並存。這種二元結構為區域性衛生治理路徑的探索提供了典型範例，也凸顯出在全球公共衛生議題中，不同發展階段國家共處同一區域所帶來的治理難題。
世界衛生組織透過《國際衛生條例（2005）》持續推動各國落實傳染病防控機制。該條例明確規定各締約國的生效日期，並適時列示條例修正案的生效時程，以確保各國能依據自身情況應用相關規定。附錄一詳細列出各締約國所適用的條例文字版本，強調除非締約國另有通知，否則世界衛生大會第58.3號決議於2005年透過的條例文字即為適用依據。此框架為中東與中亞各國應對跨境傳染病風險提供了制度基礎，也為區域衛生治理改革提供了國際法依據。
從歷史脈絡觀察，全球衛生概念的前身「國際衛生」承繼了熱帶醫學階段的跨國研究傳統，並透過國際會議、國際公約締結以及國際組織的建立，開啟了制度化合作與全球衛生治理的新時期。當時全球面臨的疾病風險以傳染病為主，例如第一次世界大戰期間爆發的全球流感疫情，不僅造成重大公共衛生危機，也提前結束了戰爭程式，凸顯傳染病對全球安全與社會經濟的深遠影響。
專家指出，中東與中亞地區的衛生治理改革需兼顧高收入國家的資源優勢與脆弱環境的緊急需求，透過區域合作機制強化傳染病監測、應急回應與衛生系統韌性。國際衛生條例的落實情況將是評估各國治理成效的重要指標，而區域性路徑的優先方向則需針對該地區特有的衝突、流離失所與跨境疾病傳播風險進行規劃。此區域的經驗亦可能為
