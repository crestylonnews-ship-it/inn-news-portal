  # Global Human Rights and Security Update: Honduran Indigenous Protest, Washington D.C. Federal Takeover, South Africa Court Blocks Shell Exploration
  
  > Honduran Garífuna community alleges forced displacement from ancestral lands; Trump declares public safety emergency in Washington D.C., taking over police and deploying National Guard; South African Constitutional Court halts Shell's Wild Coast offshore exploration.
  
  ## Honduras: Garífuna Community Alleges Displacement and Persecution from Ancestral Lands
  
  Human rights issues in Central America continue to draw attention. According to a report from Latin American Information Network (infobae.com), Honduran Garífuna indigenous leader Miriam Miranda, representing the Ofraneh organization, launched an indefinite protest outside the Honduran Supreme Court, accusing the government of inflicting "horrible persecution" on the community and forcibly displacing them from their ancestral territories. Miranda stated that the Garífuna community is demanding the government implement four rulings from the Inter-American Court of Human Rights to protect their territorial rights and initiate genuine dialogue.
  
  The report also noted that violent evictions occurred in the San Juan area, with land defenders facing criminal prosecution risks, highlighting the tension between indigenous rights and national development policies.
  
  ## United States: Trump Declares Public Safety Emergency in Washington D.C.
  
  In North America, according to an Associated Press (apnews.com) report, President Trump declared a public safety emergency in Washington D.C., invoking Section 740 of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act to place the city's police department under federal control and deploy 800 National Guard members, with objectives including cracking down on crime and clearing homeless encampments. The move has drawn strong criticism from local D.C. officials. D.C. Attorney General Brian Schwalb explicitly stated that the action is "unprecedented, unnecessary, and illegal," and noted that violent crime rates have actually declined. The jurisdictional dispute between federal takeover and local autonomy is expected to become the focal point of subsequent legal battles.
  
  ## South Africa: Constitutional Court Blocks Shell's Wild Coast Offshore Exploration
  
  In southern Africa, according to cnbcafrica.com, South Africa's Constitutional Court ruled to block Shell's offshore exploration activities along the Wild Coast, overturning a 2024 Appeals Court decision. The court found that Shell failed to adequately consult local communities during seismic exploration after obtaining exploration rights in 2014, constituting a significant procedural flaw. This ruling is a major setback for Shell and reflects the South African judiciary's emphasis on environmental protection and community participation rights. Local residents and environmental groups have been protesting for years, ultimately achieving their goals through the judicial system, setting an important precedent for balancing resource development with indigenous rights.

# 全球人權與安全動態：宏都拉斯原住民抗爭、華府聯邦接管、南非法院攔截殼牌勘探

> 宏都拉斯Garífuna社群控訴祖居地遭驅離；特朗普宣佈華府進入公共安全緊急狀態，接管警察並部署國民警衛隊；南非憲法法院叫停殼牌Wild Coast海上勘探計畫。

## 宏都拉斯：Garífuna社群控訴祖居地驅離與迫害
中美洲地區的人權議題持續引發關注。根據拉美資訊網（infobae.com）報導，宏都拉斯Garífuna原住民領袖Miriam Miranda代表Ofraneh組織，在宏都拉斯最高法院外展開無限期抗議行動，控訴政府對該族群實施「可怕的迫害」，並強行驅離其祖傳領土。Miranda指出，Garífuna社群要求政府落實美洲人權法院的四項裁決，保障其領土權利，並啟動真正的對話程式。
報導同時指出，San Juan地區傳出暴力驅離事件，土地捍衛者面臨刑事追訴風險，凸顯原住民權益與國家發展政策間的張力。
## 美國：特朗普宣佈華府進入公共安全緊急狀態
北美方面，根據美聯社（apnews.com）報導，總統特朗普宣佈華盛頓特區進入公共安全緊急狀態，援引《華府自治法》第七四〇條，將該市警察局置於聯邦控制之下，並啟用八百名國民警衛隊隊員，目標包括打擊犯罪與清除無家者營地。此舉引發華府當地官員強烈批評。華府總檢察長Brian Schwalb明確表示，此項行動「史無前例、不必要且違法」，並指出暴力犯罪率實際上已下降。聯邦接管與地方自治之間的權責爭議，預計將成為後續法律攻防的焦點。
## 南非：憲法法院叫停殼牌Wild Coast海上勘探
在非洲南部，根據cnbcafrica.com報導，南非憲法法院裁定阻止殼牌（Shell）在Wild Coast沿岸的海上勘探活動，推翻了二〇二四年上訴法院的判決。法院認為，殼牌在二〇一四年獲得勘探權後，進行地震勘探時未充分諮詢當地社群，程式上有重大瑕疵。這項裁決對殼牌而言是重大挫敗，也反映南非司法體系對環境保護與社群參與權的重視。當地居民與環保團體多年來持續抗爭，最終透過司法途徑達成目標，為資源開發與原住民權益的平衡議題樹立重要先例。
