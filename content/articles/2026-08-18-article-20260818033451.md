  > US-Iran military clashes continue to intensify, threatening shipping through the Strait of Hormuz; Pacific island nations launch large-scale fisheries enforcement operations; Colombia's president urges the US to temporarily suspend tariffs to assist earthquake reconstruction, as the global economy and trade face multiple challenges.
  
  The military conflict between the United States and Iran has entered its seventh night, with both sides continuing to escalate attacks, once again raising red flags over shipping safety in the Strait of Hormuz. According to The Star, the Iranian Revolutionary Guard has warned that it will ban all oil and natural gas exports until the US "aggression" ends. The move has triggered sharp volatility in international oil prices, with Brent crude rising 3%, as markets fear the risk of a full-scale war is mounting. UN Secretary-General António Guterres has also expressed concern, noting that civilian infrastructure inside Iran and in surrounding areas continues to be targeted.
  
  The Strait of Hormuz is one of the world's most critical oil transport routes, with approximately one-fifth of global oil passing through it daily. Should this region fall into conflict, the impact on global energy supply chains would be devastating.
  
  Meanwhile, a large-scale fisheries enforcement operation is underway in the Oceania region. According to InDepth Solomon Islands, the Pacific Islands Forum Fisheries Agency (FFA) held "Operation Island Chief 2026" from August 3 to 14, covering approximately 27.5 million square kilometers of Pacific waters and monitoring over 3,000 fishing vessels. During the operation, 133 ships were boarded and inspected, with 16 flagged for suspected illegal, unreported, and unregulated (IUU) fishing, and four vessels involved in illegal fishing and maritime crimes were seized.
  
  FFA Secretary-General Noa David Pakop said the number of seizures represents one of the higher records in recent regional operations, highlighting the importance of intelligence, satellite technology, and regional cooperation in protecting Pacific tuna resources. The 13 FFA member nations participating in the operation include Australia, Fiji, Kiribati, New Zealand, Papua New Guinea, Samoa, Solomon Islands, and Vanuatu.
  
  In South America, Colombia is facing severe natural disaster and economic pressures. According to the Los Angeles Times, Colombian President Abelardo de la Espriella has appealed to US President Donald Trump, requesting a temporary suspension of tariffs on Colombian goods to help the country recover from a 7.4-magnitude earthquake that struck last week. The earthquake killed at least 289 people and injured nearly 4,000, with tens of thousands of homes destroyed or damaged. The US has provided $26.5 million in earthquake aid, but the Colombian government believes that suspending tariffs would help ease the country's economic burden and accelerate the reconstruction process.
  
  The move also reflects the profound impact of natural disasters on developing nations' economies, and the critical role of international trade policy in post-disaster reconstruction.

# 美伊衝突升溫、太平洋漁業執法、哥倫比亞求援：全球經濟貿易多線動盪

> 美伊軍事衝突持續升級，霍爾木茲海峽航運受威脅；太平洋島國展開大規模漁業執法行動；哥倫比亞總統籲美國暫緩關稅以協助地震重建，全球經濟貿易面臨多重挑戰。

美國與伊朗的軍事衝突已進入第七個夜晚，雙方持續升級攻擊行動，導致霍爾木茲海峽航運安全再度亮起紅燈。據《星報》（The Star）報導，伊朗革命衛隊警告，在美方「侵略」結束前，將禁止任何石油與天然氣出口。此舉引發國際油價劇烈波動，布蘭特原油價格上漲3%，市場擔憂全面戰爭風險升溫。聯合國秘書長古特瑞斯亦表達關切，指伊朗境內及周邊地區的民用基礎設施持續遭受攻擊。
霍爾木茲海峽是全球最重要的石油運輸通道之一，每日透過的油量佔全球總量約五分之一，此區域一旦陷入戰亂，將對全球能源供應鏈造成毀滅性衝擊。
與此同時，大洋洲地區正展開大規模漁業執法行動。據索羅門群島《深度報導》（InDepth Solomon Islands）指出，太平洋島國漁業署（FFA）於8月3日至14日舉辦「島嶼首長行動2026」（Operation Island Chief 2026），涵蓋約2,750萬平方公里的太平洋水域，監控超過3,000艘漁船。行動期間共登檢133艘船舶，發現16艘涉嫌非法、未報告及不受規範（IUU）捕魚的船舶，並逮捕4艘涉及違法捕魚及海事犯罪的船隻。
FFA總幹事諾安·大衛·帕科普表示，此次查獲數量為近年區域行動中較高的紀錄，凸顯情報、衛星技術與區域合作在保護太平洋金槍魚資源上的重要性。參與行動的13個FFA成員國包括澳洲、斐濟、吉里巴斯、紐西蘭、巴布亞紐幾內亞、薩摩亞、索羅門群島及萬那杜等。
南美方面，哥倫比亞正面臨嚴峻的自然災害與經濟壓力。據《洛杉磯時報》（Los Angeles Times）報導，哥倫比亞總統阿韋拉多·德·拉·埃斯普里耶拉已向美國總統唐納·川普及求，請求暫時暫停對哥倫比亞課徵關稅，以協助國家從上週發生的7.4級強震中恢復。此次地震造成至少289人死亡、近4,000人受傷，數萬棟房屋毀損或受損。美國已提供2,650萬美元的地震援助，但哥倫比亞政府認為，關稅暫停將有助於減輕該國經濟負擔，加速重建程式。
此舉也反映出自然災害對發展中國家經濟的深遠影響，以及國際貿易政策在災後重建中的關鍵角色。
綜合觀察，當前全球經濟貿易面臨多重挑戰：中東局勢升溫威脅能源供應，太平洋漁業執法反映資源保護與經濟發展的張力，而南美災後重建則凸顯國際援助與貿易政策的緊密連結。這些事件相互交織，顯示全球化時代下，區域衝突、環境保護與人道救援已成為影響全球經濟穩定的核心議題。
