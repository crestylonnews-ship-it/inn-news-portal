  South Korean President Lee Jae-myung recently proposed in his Korean Liberation Day address that talks be held with North Korea to formally end the armistice status that has persisted since the Korean War from 1950 to 1953. According to the New Zealand Herald, Lee pointed out that the Korean Peninsula remains in a technical state of war, as only an armistice agreement was signed at the time, not a peace treaty. He further suggested that the two directly involved nations could address the issue of curbing North Korea's nuclear expansion through dialogue. This proposal comes as Pyongyang strongly condemns the annual US-South Korea joint military exercises, deepens military cooperation with Russia, and claims that North Korea has become an "irreversible" nuclear weapons state.
  
  Meanwhile, China's Ministry of National Defense issued its first official comment on Taiwan's annual Han Kuang military exercises, characterizing them as a "wasteful farce" and accusing such drills of "stoking war panic." According to US News & World Report, Beijing responded to Taiwan's ten-day military exercises against the backdrop of escalating Chinese military presence in the region. Taiwan's exercises simulated scenarios including breaking through naval blockades and, for the first time, incorporated hypothetical situations involving the slowdown of operational networks, indicating that defensive preparations amid cross-strait tensions continue to strengthen.
  
  In the field of space science, an observation mission involving both the European Space Agency (ESA) and the US National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) has achieved significant progress. According to Universe Today, in December 2025, the JUICE and Europa Clipper spacecraft simultaneously observed the third detected interstellar comet, 3I/ATLAS. The ultraviolet spectrometers aboard both spacecraft captured images of the comet's two hemispheres, detecting emission signals of hydrogen, oxygen, and carbon, and finding that carbon levels were higher than expected. This discovery provides rare scientific insight into the chemical composition and origins of matter from other stellar systems.

# 韓擬與朝談判結束戰爭 中國批臺軍演鬧劇 太空探測揭星際彗星成分

> 韓國總統李在明提議與朝鮮談判正式結束戰爭狀態；中國國防部批評臺灣漢光軍演為浪費鬧劇；歐亞太空任務同時觀測3I/ATLAS星際彗星，揭示其化學成分。

韓國總統李在明近日在韓國光復節演講中提出，建議與朝鮮舉行談判，以正式結束自1950年至1953年韓戰以來的停戰狀態。根據紐西蘭先驅報報導，李在明指出，朝鮮半島至今仍處於技術上的戰爭狀態，因為當時僅簽署停戰協定，並未簽訂和平條約。他進一步建議，作為直接相關方的兩國可透過對話，同時處理遏制朝鮮核能力擴張的問題。此提議出臺之際，平壤方面正強烈譴責年度美韓聯合軍事演習，並深化與俄羅斯的軍事合作，同時宣稱朝鮮已成為「不可逆轉」的核武國家。
與此同時，中國國防部對臺灣年度漢光軍事演習發表首次官方評論，將其定性為「浪費的鬧劇」，並指稱此類演習「煽動戰爭恐慌」。根據美國新聞與世界報導的資訊，北京方面對臺灣為期十天的軍事演習作出回應，正值中國周邊軍事存在持續升級的背景下。臺灣方面此次演習模擬了突破海上封鎖等場景，並首次納入減緩行動網路的假想情境，顯示臺海緊張局勢下的防禦準備持續強化。
在太空科學領域，一項由歐洲太空總署（ESA）與美國國家航空暨太空總署（NASA）共同參與的觀測任務取得重要進展。根據宇宙今天報導，2025年12月，JUICE與歐羅巴快船兩艘太空船同時觀測了第三顆被探測到的星際彗星3I/ATLAS。兩艘太空船搭載的紫外光光譜儀分別拍攝了該彗星的兩個半球，偵測到氫、氧與碳的排放訊號，並發現碳含量高於預期。這項發現為來自其他恆星系統的物質化學組成與起源提供了罕見的科學洞察。
綜合上述三項報導，東北亞地緣政治動態持續受到國際關注。韓國總統的和平提議與朝鮮的核武立場形成明顯對比，而中國對臺灣軍演的批評則反映區域軍事緊張關係的複雜性。與此同時，太空科學領域的進展為人類探索星際天體提供了新的科學資料。
