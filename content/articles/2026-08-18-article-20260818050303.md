  # Comprehensive Report on the Geographic Division of the Americas and the Current State of the U.S. Economy
  
  > Multiple sources indicate that the geographic division of the Americas follows two systems—geography and political geography—while U.S. debt exceeded USD 39 trillion in March 2026, with interest payments accounting for approximately 14% of the federal budget.
  
  According to an article on Zhihu's column, in the seven-continent geographic model, the Americas are divided into North America and South America by the Panama Canal. North America encompasses the region north of the canal, with its principal countries being Canada and the United States; South America refers to the region south of the canal. The article further explains that the division between North and South America falls within the domain of physical geography and differs from regional definitions used in political geography.
  
  Baidu Baike's entry on "North America and Latin America" states that North America and Latin America are two regional classification systems for the Americas within political geography, distinct from the physical-geography division into North and South America. "North America" in this context refers specifically to the northern portion of the continent, including Canada, the United States, and territories such as Greenland; "Latin America" covers Mexico, Central America, the West Indies, and the entirety of South America south of the United States—a name derived from the region's historical colonization by Latin-language countries such as Spain and Portugal. The two classification systems each have their own academic and policy applications and are often used interchangeably in international affairs and regional studies.
  
  The "Overview of the United States" page on the website of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the People's Republic of China provides the latest economic data showing that as of March 17, 2026, the total U.S. federal government debt had surpassed USD 39 trillion, reaching USD 39.016 trillion; annual interest payments exceed USD 1 trillion, accounting for approximately 14% of the federal budget. The same source also reviews the history of U.S. monetary policy, noting that to support economic recovery following a recession, the Federal Reserve cut interest rates twice in 2020 to near-zero levels of 0 to 0.25% and implemented an "unlimited" quantitative easing program.
  
  Taken together, the above sources demonstrate that the geographic division of the Americas carries multiple definitions at both academic and practical levels, and that the United States, as the core economy of North America, continues to influence regional development through its fiscal and monetary policy trajectory. All parties citing relevant materials and concepts should clearly distinguish between the classification standards of geography and political geography and pay attention to changes in the latest economic indicators.

# 美洲地理劃分與美國經濟現況綜合報導

> 綜合多源資料顯示，美洲地理劃分存在地理學與政治地理學兩種體系，美國債務規模於2026年3月突破39兆美元，利息支出佔財政預算約14%。

根據知乎專欄文章指出，美洲在地理學七大洲劃分中，以巴拿馬運河為界分為北美洲與南美洲兩部分。北美洲涵蓋巴拿馬運河以北區域，主要國家包括加拿大與美國；南美洲則為巴拿馬運河以南區域。該文章進一步說明，北美洲與南美洲的劃分屬於自然地理學範疇，與政治地理學的區域定義存在差異。
百度百科〈北美與拉丁美洲〉條目則指出，北美與拉丁美洲是政治地理學中對美洲的兩種區域劃分方式，與自然地理學的北美洲、南美洲劃分不同。其中，北美特指北美洲北部，包括加拿大、美國以及格陵蘭等屬地；拉丁美洲則涵蓋美國以南的墨西哥、中美洲、西印度群島及南美洲全域，此區域因歷史上受西班牙、葡萄牙等拉丁語族國家殖民影響而得名。兩套劃分體系各有其學術與政策應用場景，在國際事務與區域研究中常被交替使用。
中華人民共和國外交部網站〈美國國家概況〉提供最新經濟資料顯示，截至2026年3月17日，美國聯邦政府債務總額已突破39兆美元，達到39.016兆美元；每年利息支出超過1兆美元，約佔財政預算的14%。該資料同時回顧美國貨幣政策歷程，指出為助力經濟衰退後的復甦程式，美國聯邦準備理事會於2020年兩次大幅降息至0至0.25%的近零利率水平，並實施「無限量」量化寬鬆政策。
綜合上述資料可見，美洲的地理劃分在學術與實務層面存在多重定義，而美國作為北美洲核心經濟體，其財政與貨幣政策走向亦持續影響區域發展。各界在引用相關資料與概念時，應明確區分地理學與政治地理學的劃分標準，並關注最新經濟指標變化。
