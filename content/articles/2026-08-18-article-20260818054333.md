  # International Organizations Warn of Escalating Middle East Conflict, Humanitarian Aid Needs at Record High
  
  > The International Committee of the Red Cross and Oxfam and other organizations have warned that escalating conflict in the Middle East could have catastrophic consequences. UN data shows the region's 2024 humanitarian aid needs reached $13.9 billion, the highest globally.
  
  Nicola von Aaxen, regional director of the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC), recently issued a warning that the ongoing escalation of conflict in the Middle East could have catastrophic consequences for civilians. According to an ICRC press release, from the resumption of fighting between Gaza and Israel to the continued intensification of violence in Yemen, millions of civilians across the Middle East have once again fallen into an endless cycle of violence. The ICRC emphasized that the organization acts in a neutral, impartial, and independent manner, committed to protecting life and dignity in conflict zones and promoting the global implementation of international humanitarian law.
  
  Oxfam's Taiwan office released a related response, stating that Oxfam has joined 13 international non-governmental organizations in issuing a joint declaration calling on the global community to take immediate action to prevent the humanitarian disaster from further deteriorating. The declaration explicitly demands that all parties take prompt action to ease tensions, prioritize the protection of civilians, humanitarian workers, and civilian infrastructure, strictly abide by international humanitarian law, and bring an early end to armed conflict in the Middle East. Oxfam also stated that it has rapidly activated emergency relief operations to address the increasingly severe humanitarian needs on the ground.
  
  Data from the UN Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (OCHA) Global Humanitarian Overview 2024 shows that the Middle East and North Africa region required $13.9 billion in humanitarian assistance in 2024, the highest amount of any region globally, accounting for 30% of the total Global Humanitarian Overview. For comparison, East and Southern Africa required $10.9 billion, while West and Central Africa required $8.3 billion. This data underscores the severity and urgency of the humanitarian crisis in the Middle East.
  
  Synthesizing information from various sources, international organizations' concerns about the Middle East situation focus on three main areas: civilian safety and infrastructure protection, compliance with international humanitarian law, and the humanitarian aid funding gap. Both the ICRC and Oxfam declarations emphasize the priority of civilian protection, while UN data confirms the scale of the crisis from a funding perspective. However, the sources do not provide further details on the attribution of responsibility to specific parties to the conflict, the allocation of aid funds, or subsequent action plans; relevant information remains to be confirmed in future reporting.

# 國際組織警告中東戰事升級 人道援助需求創紀錄

> 紅十字國際委員會與樂施會等組織警告中東戰事升級恐造成災難性後果，聯合國資料顯示該地區2024年人道援助需求達139億美元，為全球最高。

紅十字國際委員會（ICRC）地區主任尼古拉·馮阿克斯近日發出警告，指出中東地區戰事持續升級，可能對平民造成災難性後果。根據ICRC發布的新聞稿，從加沙與以色列戰火重燃，到葉門暴力持續升溫，中東地區數百萬平民再度陷入無盡的暴力迴圈之中。ICRC強調，該組織以中立、公正和獨立的方式行動，致力於在衝突地區保護生命與尊嚴，並推動國際人道法在全球範圍內落實。
樂施會（Oxfam）臺灣辦公室則公佈相關回應，指出樂施會已聯同13個國際非政府組織共同發表聯署宣告，呼籲全球立即採取行動，防止人道災難進一步惡化。聯署宣告中明確要求各方盡快採取行動緩和緊張局勢，並將平民、人道工作者及民用基礎設施列為最高優先保護物件，嚴格遵守國際人道法，及早終止中東地區的武裝衝突。樂施會同時表示，已迅速啟動緊急救援行動，以應對當地日益嚴峻的人道需求。
聯合國人道事務協調廳（OCHA）發布的《2024年全球人道主義概覽》資料顯示，中東與北非地區在2024年需要139億美元的人道援助，這是全球任何地區中金額最高的一次，佔全球人道主義概覽總額的30%。作為對比，東非和南部非洲地區需要109億美元，而西非和中非地區則需要83億美元。這一資料凸顯中東地區人道危機的嚴重性與緊迫性。
綜合各來源資訊，國際組織對中東局勢的關注主要集中在三個層面：一是平民安全與基礎設施保護，二是國際人道法的遵守情況，三是人道援助資金的缺口。ICRC與樂施會的宣告均強調平民保護的優先性，而聯合國資料則從資金需求角度印證了危機規模。不過，各組織對於具體衝突各方的責任歸屬、援助資金分配細節以及後續行動計劃，素材中並未提供進一步說明，相關資訊仍有待後續報導確認。
