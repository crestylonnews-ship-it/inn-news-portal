  # Asia's Economy Faces Trade Headwinds: Multi-Source Analysis of Regional Challenges and Countermeasures
  
  > The International Monetary Fund's October 2025 Asia-Pacific Economic Outlook report warns that U.S. tariff hikes and rising protectionism will impact Asian export demand; Lyxor's economic outlook also shows global growth patterns becoming increasingly divergent, with emerging markets experiencing a slowdown.
  
  The International Monetary Fund (IMF) released its "Asia-Pacific Economic Outlook" report in October 2025, issuing a warning regarding the current regional economic situation. The report indicates that Asia-Pacific economies demonstrated certain resilience in 2025, with economic growth in the first half of the year exceeding expectations. However, U.S. tariff hikes and rising protectionism are likely to reduce export demand to Asia and ultimately weigh on near-term economic growth. The IMF recommends that policymakers focus on promoting regional integration by reducing trade and investment barriers, and improving financial intermediation mechanisms to address multiple internal and external challenges.
  
  Meanwhile, Lyxor Investment Management's global economic outlook analysis presents similar observations. According to its report, global economic growth remains moderate, with an annualized growth rate of approximately 2.5%, consistent with trends over recent months. However, recent corporate survey results show that the global economic growth pattern is undergoing a shift: developed countries are accelerating their growth while emerging markets are experiencing a slowdown—a trend that runs counter to the established trajectory of global economic growth cycles since the summer of 2009. This divergence in growth between developed economies and emerging markets echoes the trade headwinds mentioned in the IMF report, indicating that structural challenges facing the regional economy are gradually emerging.
  
  On the policy response front, Taiwan's Executive Yuan-promoted New Southbound Policy offers another perspective on regional economic integration. The policy targets local industrial capacity and demand, entering through five key innovative industries to facilitate the export of Internet of Things (IoT) systems such as electronic toll collection (ETC), smart healthcare, and smart campuses. It also establishes Taiwan Desks to connect with local resources, assists Taiwanese businesses in clustering and佈局ing on the ground, and sets up a single-window platform for New Southbound economic and trade expansion, playing roles in "finding, creating, integrating, and facilitating." This can be viewed as a concrete practice of Asian countries and regions actively deepening regional economic and trade ties in the face of global trade uncertainty.
  
  Synthesizing analysis from various sources, Asia's economy is currently at a critical juncture. The trade barriers and protectionism risks emphasized in the IMF report corroborate the growth divergence trend pointed out by Lyxor, indicating that shrinking external demand and internal structural adjustments are simultaneously affecting the regional economy. Against this backdrop, policymakers in various countries face a dilemma: on one hand, they must address the short-term impact of trade headwinds on growth; on the other hand, they must promote medium- to long-term structural reforms to enhance economic resilience. Regional cooperation mechanisms such as the New Southbound Policy could serve as potential pathways to mitigate trade dependency risks and promote economic rebalancing, though their actual effectiveness remains to be seen.

# 亞洲經濟面對貿易逆風 多源分析區域挑戰與對策

> 國際貨幣基金組織發布2025年10月亞太經濟展望報告，指出美國加徵關稅與保護主義抬頭將衝擊亞洲出口需求；聯博經濟展望亦顯示全球成長型態轉趨分化，新興市場成長放緩。

國際貨幣基金組織（IMF）於2025年10月發布《亞太地區經濟展望》報告，針對當前區域經濟形勢提出警示。報告指出，亞太經濟體在2025年展現一定韌性，上半年經濟增速超越預期。然而，美國加徵關稅與保護主義抬頭，很可能減少對亞洲的出口需求，並最終對近期經濟增長造成拖累。IMF建議政策制定者應專注於透過減少貿易與投資障礙來促進區域一體化，並改善金融中介機制，以應對內外部的多重挑戰。
與此同時，聯博投資管理的全球經濟展望分析亦呈現類似觀察。根據其報告，全球經濟成長率維持溫和，年化成長率約為2.5%，與過去數月趨勢相近。然而，近期企業調查結果顯示全球經濟成長型態正出現轉變：已開發國家加速成長，新興市場則出現成長趨緩，此一走勢一反2009年夏天以來全球經濟步入成長週期的既有軌跡。此種發達經濟體與新興市場之間的成長分化現象，與IMF報告中提及的貿易不利因素影響相互呼應，顯示區域經濟面臨的結構性挑戰正逐漸浮現。
在政策應對層面，臺灣行政院推動的新南向政策亦提供另一種區域經濟整合的觀察視角。該政策針對當地產業能量與需求，由五大創新產業切入，輔導電子收費（ETC）、智慧醫療、智慧校園等物聯網系統輸出，並設立臺灣視窗（Taiwan Desk）連結在地資源，協助臺商在地群聚佈局，同時成立新南向經貿拓展單一視窗，扮演「尋找、開創、整合、促成」等角色。此舉可視為亞洲國家與地區在面對全球貿易不確定性時，積極深化區域經貿連結的具體實踐。
綜合各來源分析，亞洲經濟當前處於關鍵轉捩點。IMF報告所強調的貿易壁壘與保護主義風險，與聯博所指出的成長分化趨勢相互印證，顯示外部需求萎縮與內部結構調整正同時作用於區域經濟。在此背景下，各國政策制定者面臨兩難：一方面需應對短期貿易逆風對增長的衝擊，另一方面亦須推動中長期結構改革以提升經濟韌性。新南向政策等區域合作機制，或可成為緩解貿易依賴風險、促進經濟再平衡的潛在途徑，但其實際成效仍有待觀察。
