  # South Korean President Lee Jae-myung's Liberation Day Proposal: Replace Armistice Agreement with Peace Treaty
  
  > South Korean President Lee Jae-myung proposed in his Liberation Day speech to initiate dialogue with North Korea to formally end the Korean War and establish a peace regime, while calling on the United States and China to participate in multilateral talks; North Korea's recent deepening of ties with Russia and emphasis on its nuclear weapons status complicate the proposal.
  
  South Korean President Lee Jae-myung delivered a commemorative address on Liberation Day (August 15), formally proposing dialogue with North Korea with the goal of transitioning the Korean Peninsula's current armistice status to a peace regime. According to Asahi Shimbun, Lee stated that Seoul would seek conflict prevention safeguards and emphasized the need to replace the peninsula's existing armistice agreement with a "peace regime." This proposal marks the first time the South Korean government has publicly outlined a concrete direction, through the president's office, toward replacing the 1953 Korean Armistice Agreement with a peace treaty on the issues of North Korea's nuclear program and peninsula peace.
  
  According to multiple media reports, Lee Jae-myung's speech was cross-verified by sources including Yonhap News Agency, France 24, and South Korean SBS Television. The confirmed facts include: Lee proposed dialogue with North Korea during his Liberation Day address; the dialogue aims to formally end the Korean War and transition from an armistice agreement to a peace regime; and North Korea's nuclear weapons issue was also mentioned as requiring discussion. Additionally, SBS reported that Lee further called on the United States and China to participate in multilateral dialogue, indicating South Korea's intention to place the peninsula peace process within a framework of regional great power coordination.
  
  However, there are differences in details across sources, and some information remains to be further confirmed. France 24 specifically noted that North Korea has been continuously deepening its relations with Russia and has publicly declared itself an "irreversible nuclear weapons state," a background that makes Lee's peace proposal face a more complex geopolitical environment; however, neither Asahi Shimbun nor Yonhap explicitly mentioned the Russia factor, focusing instead on the dual goals of transitioning to a peace regime and freezing the nuclear issue. Furthermore, regarding the specific meaning of "freezing North Korea's nuclear progress," Yonhap explicitly mentioned this issue, while other sources did not elaborate in detail, indicating that this aspect still awaits further explanation from the South Korean government.
  
  If this proposal continues to advance, it will have profound implications for the security architecture of the Korean Peninsula, the regional order in Northeast Asia, and the South Korea-U.S. alliance. First, replacing the armistice agreement with a peace treaty means the legal status of the peninsula conflict will shift from "ceasefire" to "peace," which involves not only inter-Korean relations but also the strategic interests of extra-regional powers such as the United States, China, and Russia. South Korea's proposal of a multilateral dialogue framework indicates its attempt to maintain the South Korea-U.S. alliance while reducing dependence on a single ally and seeking a new balance for regional security mechanisms. However, North Korea's current strengthening of military and economic cooperation with Russia, along with its firm stance that its nuclear weapons status is irreversible, creates significant uncertainty regarding the timing and conditions for peace negotiations.
  
  Different perspectives and areas requiring cautious interpretation also warrant attention. On one hand, Lee's proposal is seen as the South Korean government's attempt to take the initiative diplomatically by shaping a leadership image through peace initiatives; on the other hand, some analysts argue that given North Korea's continuously improving nuclear capabilities and the close Russia-North Korea relationship, the proposal may be merely symbolic and unlikely to achieve substantive progress in the short term. Additionally, France 24's report juxtaposed North Korea's nuclear weapons stance with South Korea's proposal, implying tension between the two, but this causal relationship has not yet been confirmed by South Korean officials. Media should avoid over-interpreting this as "South Korea urgently proposing peace dialogue due to North Korea's pro-Russia stance," as Lee's speech did not explicitly link the two.
  
  Key areas for subsequent observation include whether the South Korean government will announce a specific timetable and participant list for multilateral peace dialogue; North Korea's response to Lee's proposal; and whether the United States and China are willing to participate in this framework. Additionally, the latest developments in Russia-North Korea relations, North Korea's nuclear test activities, and domestic political and public opinion reactions in South Korea to the peace proposal are all critical variables affecting the direction of this issue. Until information becomes clearer, all parties should maintain cautious observation and avoid making overly optimistic or pessimistic predictions about the pace and outcomes of the peace process.

# 韓國總統李在明光復節提案：以和平協定取代停戰協定

> 韓國總統李在明於光復節演說中提議，與北韓展開對話，正式結束韓戰並建立和平體制，同時呼籲美國與中國參與多邊對話；北韓近期深化與俄羅斯關係並強調核武地位，使此提案面臨複雜局勢。

韓國總統李在明於光復節（8月15日）發表紀念演說，正式提出與北韓展開對話，目標是將朝鮮半島現行的停戰狀態轉為和平體制。根據共同社報導，李在明表示首爾將尋求防止衝突的保障措施，並強調有必要以「和平體制」取代半島現有的停戰協定。此提案標誌著韓國政府在朝核與半島和平議題上，首度以總統身分公開提出以和平條約取代1953年簽訂的《朝鮮停戰協定》的具體方向。
綜合多家媒體報導，李在明的演說內容獲得韓國聯合通訊社（Yonhap）、法國國際廣播電臺（France 24）及韓國SBS電視臺等來源交叉確認。共同確認的事實包括：李在明於光復節演說中提出與北韓對話；對話目標為正式結束韓戰、從停戰協定過渡至和平體制；同時提及北韓核武問題需納入討論。此外，SBS報導指出，李在明進一步呼籲美國與中國參與多邊對話，顯示韓國政府有意將半島和平程式置於區域大國協調框架之下。
然而，各來源在細節表述上存在差異，部分資訊尚待進一步確認。法國國際廣播電臺特別指出，北韓近期持續深化與俄羅斯的關係，並公開宣稱其為「不可逆轉的核武國家」，此背景使李在明的和平提案面臨更複雜的地緣政治環境；但共同社與聯合通訊社的報導中，並未明確提及俄羅斯因素，僅聚焦於和平體制轉換與核問題凍結的雙重目標。此外，關於「凍結北韓核進展」的具體內涵，聯合通訊社報導中明確提及此議題，而其他來源則未詳細展開，顯示此部分內容仍有待韓國政府後續說明。
此提案若持續推進，將對朝鮮半島安全架構、東北亞區域秩序及韓美同盟關係產生深遠影響。首先，以和平條約取代停戰協定，意味著半島衝突的法律狀態將從「停火」轉為「和平」，這不僅涉及韓朝雙邊關係，更牽動美國、中國、俄羅斯等域外大國的戰略利益。韓國政府提出多邊對話方塊架，顯示其試圖在維持韓美同盟的同時，降低對單一盟友的依賴，並為區域安全機制尋找新的平衡點。然而，北韓目前強化與俄羅斯的軍事與經濟合作，並堅稱核武地位不可逆轉，使和平談判的時機與條件面臨高度不確定性。
不同觀點與應謹慎解讀之處亦值得關注。一方面，李在明的提案被視為韓國政府試圖在外交上掌握主動，透過和平倡議塑造領導形象；但另一方面，也有分析認為，在北韓核武能力持續提升、俄朝關係密切化的背景下，此提案可能僅具象徵意義，短期內難以達成實質進展。此外，法國國際廣播電臺的報導將北韓核武立場與韓國提案並列，暗示兩者之間存在張力，但此種因果關係尚未得到韓國官方證實。媒體應避免過度解讀為「韓國因北韓親俄而緊急提出和平對話」，因為李在明的演說內容並未明確將兩者連結。
後續觀察重點包括：韓國政府是否會公佈多邊和平對話的具體時間表與參與方名單；北韓對李在明提案的回應態度；以及美國與中國是否願意參與此框架。此外，俄朝關係的最新發展、北韓核試驗動態，以及韓國國內對和平提案的政黨與民意反應，均為影響此議題走向的關鍵變數。在資訊尚未完全明朗之前，各方宜保持審慎觀察，避免對和平程式的進展速度與成果作出過度樂觀或悲觀的預測。
