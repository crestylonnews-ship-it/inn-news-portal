  # Africa Pushes for New Public Health Order as US-China Rivalry Intensifies
  
  > Africa advocates for a new public health order centered on autonomy and resilience; the US signing a data-sharing agreement with 12 nations has drawn attention; China's aid-built Africa CDC headquarters has been completed, signaling a structural shift in regional health governance.
  
  Africa's public health landscape is undergoing profound transformation. According to thinkglobalhealth.org, Africa's vision for a new public health order emphasizes self-reliance, resilience building, and a strategic layout for African health technology production and infrastructure development. This ambitious agenda has prompted the international community to consider how it may affect other global health actors, programs, and initiatives. This trend reflects African nations' desire to wield greater influence in global health governance, no longer merely as passive recipients of external aid but as active shapers of regional health rules.
  
  Multiple sources indicate that African nations have made concrete progress in public health infrastructure. A systematic review published by news.ebiotrade.com found that the establishment of National Public Health Institutes (NPHIs) in African countries helps enhance pandemic detection capabilities, integrate fragmented health functions, strengthen workforce capacity, and promote inter-agency cooperation. The study, which analyzed 11 research papers from 2000 to 2025 using PRISMA and QATSDD standards, found that NPHIs play a key role in strengthening regional health response mechanisms.
  
  Meanwhile, cn.chinadaily.com.cn reported that the first phase of the African Union Africa CDC headquarters, built with Chinese aid, was completed in January 2023. It is the first fully continental disease control center on the African continent with modern office and laboratory facilities, and was officially inaugurated on September 10, 2023, in Addis Ababa, the capital of Ethiopia.
  
  However, different media outlets offer varying interpretations of the current situation. prism.liabooks.com points out that in December 2025, the US signed bilateral health agreements with 12 African nations requiring the signatory countries to share pathogen data within five days. This move is seen as a key component of the US "America First Global Health Strategy" and has sparked discussion about the dilemma between data sovereignty and financial aid. The report further analyzes how the US competes with China in health diplomacy across Africa.
  
  In contrast, thinkglobalhealth.org takes a perspective more focused on Africa's own vision and autonomy, without directly referencing the US-China competition framework, indicating that the same issue is presented with different emphases across media narratives. Additionally, while the WHO data platform provided by who.int serves as an official data source, it primarily offers data tools and report lookup functions and does not directly address the aforementioned policy developments.
  
  This round of public health order restructuring has concrete implications for Africa's regions, industries, and societies. While African nations receive external funding and technical support, they also face the risk of ceding data sovereignty. Agreement clauses requiring pathogen data sharing within five days could affect each country's autonomous control over pandemic information. Although the establishment of NPHIs has strengthened regional detection and response capabilities, if data flows are externally mandated, institutional autonomy will be eroded. China's aid-built Africa CDC headquarters provides modern laboratory facilities that can help boost regional testing capacity, but such infrastructure also carries geopolitical implications, requiring African nations to weigh multiple factors when choosing partners.
  
  Different viewpoints reveal that the development of Africa's new public health order still faces limitations. thinkglobalhealth.org emphasizes the strategic nature of Africa's vision but does not elaborate on specific implementation mechanisms or funding sources. prism.liabooks.com focuses on US-China competition, potentially oversimplifying African agency and portraying Africa as a passive arena for great-power rivalry. news.ebiotrade.com's systematic review affirms the effectiveness of NPHIs but does not deeply discuss the potential impact of external intervention on institutional independence.
  
  These reports collectively highlight a core question: how Africa can balance external aid with the preservation of sovereignty in its pursuit of health autonomy remains an unresolved challenge. The claims of any single source should not be taken as definitive, especially when it comes to data-sharing provisions and geopolitical interpretations, which require a cautious approach.
  
  Key areas for future observation include: how the bilateral health agreements signed between the US and 12 African nations will influence the policy choices of other African countries; the actual operational effectiveness and regional coordination functions of the Africa CDC headquarters; the specific performance of NPHIs in future pandemic responses; and whether the African Union can propose a unified data governance framework to ensure regional health information sovereignty. Additionally, it is worth tracking how international institutions such as the WHO maintain neutrality against the backdrop of US-China competition.

# 非洲推動公共衛生新秩序 美中角力升溫

> 非洲倡議建立以自主與韌性為核心的新公共衛生秩序，美國與12國簽署資料共享協定引發關注；中國援建非洲疾控中心總部落成，顯示區域衛生治理正迎來結構性轉變。

非洲公共衛生格局正經歷深刻轉型。根據 thinkglobalhealth.org 報導，非洲對新公共衛生秩序的願景強調自給自足、韌性建設，以及對非洲健康技術生產與基礎設施發展的戰略性佈局，此雄心勃勃的議程引發國際社會對其如何影響其他全球衛生參與者、程式與計畫的關注。此一趨勢反映非洲國家希望在全球衛生治理中掌握更大話語權，不再僅是被動接受外部援助，而是主動塑造區域衛生規則。
多來源顯示，非洲國家在公共衛生基礎建設方面已取得具體進展。news.ebiotrade.com 刊載的系統評價研究指出，非洲國家公共衛生機構（NPHI）的建立有助於提升疫情檢測能力、整合分散的衛生職能、加強工作人員能力，並促進機構間合作；該研究以 PRISMA 與 QATSDD 標準分析 2000 至 2025 年間 11 項研究，發現 NPHI 在強化區域衛生應變機制上扮演關鍵角色。
同時，cn.chinadaily.com.cn 報導，中國援建的非盟非洲疾控中心總部（一期）專案於 2023 年 1 月竣工，這是非洲大陸首座擁有現代化辦公與實驗條件、設施完善的全非疾控中心，於 2023 年 9 月 10 日在衣索比亞首都亞的斯亞貝巴正式啟用。
然而，不同媒體對當前局勢的解讀存在差異。prism.liabooks.com 指出，2025 年 12 月美國與非洲 12 國簽署雙邊衛生協定，要求受協定國家於 5 日內共享病原體資料，此舉被視為美國推行「美國優先全球衛生戰略」的重要一環，並引發對資料主權與金援之間兩難的討論；該報導進一步分析美國如何與中國在非洲展開衛生外交角力。
相對地，thinkglobalhealth.org 的視角較側重非洲自身的願景與自主性，未直接提及美中競爭框架，顯示同一議題在不同媒體敘事中呈現不同重點。此外，who.int 提供的世界衛生資料平臺雖為官方資料來源，但主要提供資料工具與報告查詢功能，未直接涉及上述政策動態。
此輪公共衛生秩序重組對非洲地區、產業與社會產生具體影響。非洲國家在獲得外部資金與技術支援的同時，也面臨資料主權讓渡的風險；要求 5 日內共享病原體資料的協定條款，可能影響各國對疫情資訊的自主控制權。NPHI 的設立雖強化了區域檢測與應變能力，但若資料流向由外部強制定義，機構自主性將受侵蝕。中國援建的非洲疾控中心總部提供現代化實驗設施，有助於提升區域檢測量能，但此類基礎建設也伴隨地緣政治意涵，使非洲國家在選擇合作夥伴時必須權衡多重因素。
不同觀點顯示，非洲公共衛生新秩序的發展仍存限制。thinkglobalhealth.org 強調非洲願景的戰略性，但未詳述具體執行機制與資金來源；prism.liabooks.com 聚焦美中競爭，可能過度簡化非洲國家的主體性，將非洲描繪為被動接受大國角力的場域；news.ebiotrade.com 的系統評價肯定 NPHI 成效，但未深入討論外部幹預對機構獨立性的潛在影響。
這些報導共同揭示一個核心問題：非洲在追求衛生自主的過程中，如何平衡外部援助與主權維護，仍是待解難題。不應將單一來源的說法視為定論，尤其涉及資料共享條款與地緣政治解讀時，需保持審慎。
後續觀察重點包括：美國與非洲 12 國簽署的雙邊衛生協定將如何影響其他非洲國家的政策選擇；非洲疾控中心總部的實際運作效能與區域協調功能；NPHI 在未來疫情應變中的具體表現；以及非洲聯盟能否提出統一的資料治理框架，以確保區域衛生資訊主權。此外，世衛組織等國際機構如何在美中競爭背景下維持中立性，亦值得持續追蹤。
