  # Kim Jong Un Oversees Cruise Missile Test Launch as North Korea Strengthens Nuclear Deterrence Capability
  
  > North Korean leader Kim Jong Un supervised a long-range strategic cruise missile launch training session on December 28, with two missiles striking their targets after flying for over 10,000 seconds. North Korea emphasized the verification of the absolute reliability of its strategic counterstrike capability.
  
  North Korean leader Kim Jong Un personally supervised a long-range strategic cruise missile launch training session on December 28, 2025, at a location in the waters west of the Korean Peninsula. According to KCNA, North Korea launched two strategic cruise missiles that flew along preset trajectories before hitting their targets. The test launch was seen as the latest move by North Korea to continuously strengthen its nuclear and missile capabilities, drawing close attention from the international community. The South Korean military detected signs of multiple missile launches from the Sunan area near Pyongyang, confirming that the test launch indeed took place. After observing the event on site, Kim Jong Un called for the "unrestricted and continuous" development of the national nuclear combat force, and stressed the importance of checking the reliability of North Korea's nuclear deterrence capability.
  
  After cross-referencing multiple media reports, the following facts have been commonly confirmed: the test launch occurred on December 28 in the waters west of the Korean Peninsula or the Yellow Sea; Kim Jong Un personally supervised the event; the two cruise missiles flew for 10,199 seconds and 10,203 seconds respectively before hitting their designated targets. The flight time data reported by KCNA and Yonhap News Agency were in complete agreement, showing that both missiles flew for nearly the same duration of approximately 17 minutes. In addition, multiple media outlets noted that Kim Jong Un emphasized the "absolute credibility" and "combat readiness" of the strategic counterstrike capability, and called for the continued strengthening of the national nuclear combat force.
  
  These commonalities indicate that the test launch was a carefully planned military exercise aimed at demonstrating the maturity and reliability of its missile technology.
  
  However, there are several differences in detail descriptions among different media. Some reports labeled the launch site as the "Yellow Sea," while others used the term "western waters," and whether the two refer to exactly the same area remains to be further clarified. Regarding the missile model and specific performance parameters, no media provided detailed technical data, and it can only be confirmed as a "long-range strategic cruise missile." In addition, while North Korean officials stated that the test launch verified the reliability of the strategic counterstrike capability, Western media and analysts still harbor doubts about the actual combat effectiveness of North Korea's missiles, believing that further observation of its combat performance is needed.
  
  Although the missile flight time was long, cruise missiles typically execute missions by flying at low altitudes and hugging terrain. A long flight time does not necessarily indicate a long range, and this point should not be overinterpreted.
  
  This test launch has a direct impact on the security landscape in Northeast Asia. South Korea and the United States may further strengthen military surveillance and sanctions pressure on North Korea, and military tensions in the region are likely to escalate. By demonstrating its long-range cruise missile capability, North Korea is sending a signal to the outside world that its nuclear deterrence is continuously upgrading, which may prompt neighboring countries to reevaluate their regional defense strategies. For South Korea, the fact that the missiles were launched from the Sunan area indicates that their range can cover the Seoul metropolitan area, posing a direct threat to the capital region. In addition, North Korea's emphasis on "unrestricted" development of its nuclear combat force suggests that it may continue to conduct more missile tests and nuclear experiments in the future, posing ongoing challenges to regional stability.
  
  Different perspectives show that North Korean officials have positioned this test launch as a routine military exercise aimed at verifying the reliability of weapon systems, while the international community generally views it as an attempt by North Korea to break through international sanctions and strengthen its nuclear capabilities. Some analysts pointed out that North Korea's choice to conduct the test launch at this time may be related to changes in the international situation, with the intention of diverting the international community's attention from conflicts in other regions. However, there are also views that the strategic intent of this test launch should not be overinterpreted. North Korea has been regularly conducting missile tests for a long time, and this move is consistent with its established military development path and does not necessarily represent a new strategic shift. North Korean media emphasized the precision and reliability of the missile flight, but actual combat effectiveness still needs to be verified through more tests.
  
  Key areas for subsequent observation include: whether North Korea will further announce missile technology details or conduct higher-specification test launches; whether South Korea and the United States will take countermeasures such as strengthening missile defense system deployments; whether the international community's enforcement of sanctions against North Korea will be further upgraded; and how regional countries will adjust their security policies to respond to North Korea's ongoing military challenges. In addition, the actual range, precision, and tactical application capabilities of the missiles still need to be confirmed through more independent information. All parties should make rational assessments based on facts, avoid overinterpretation or emotional reactions, and work to maintain regional peace and stability.

# 金正恩監督巡航導彈試射 北韓強化核威懾能力

> 北韓領導人金正恩於12月28日監督遠端戰略巡航導彈發射訓練，兩枚導彈飛行逾10,000秒後命中目標，北韓強調驗證戰略反擊能力的絕對可靠性。

北韓領導人金正恩於2025年12月28日親自監督遠端戰略巡航導彈發射訓練，此次試射地點位於朝鮮半島西部海域。根據朝中社報導，北韓發射兩枚戰略巡航導彈，沿預設軌道飛行後命中目標。此次試射被視為北韓持續強化核武與導彈能力的最新舉措，引發國際社會高度關注。南韓軍方從平壤附近順安地區檢測到多枚導彈發射跡象，確認試射活動確實發生。金正恩在現場觀摩後，要求「無限制且持續」發展國家核戰鬥力量，並強調檢查北韓核威懾能力可靠性的重要性。
多家媒體交叉比對後，以下事實獲得共同確認：試射發生於12月28日，地點為朝鮮半島西部海域或黃海，金正恩親自監督，兩枚巡航導彈飛行時間分別為10,199秒與10,203秒，最終均命中預定目標。朝中社與韓聯社報導的飛行時間資料完全一致，顯示兩枚導彈飛行時間幾乎相同，約各為17分鐘。此外，多家媒體均指出金正恩強調戰略反擊能力的「絕對可信度」與「戰鬥準備狀態」，並呼籲持續強化國家核戰鬥力量。
這些共同點顯示此次試射是北韓精心策劃的軍事演練，旨在展示其導彈技術的成熟度與可靠性。
然而，不同媒體在細節描述上存在若干差異。部分報導將試射地點標示為「黃海」，另有報導則使用「西部海域」一詞，兩者所指是否完全相同尚待進一步釐清。關於導彈型號與具體效能引數，各媒體均未提供詳細技術資料，僅能確認為「遠端戰略巡航導彈」。此外，北韓官方稱此次試射驗證了戰略反擊能力的可靠性，但西方媒體與分析人士對北韓導彈實際作戰能力仍存有疑慮，認為需進一步觀察其實戰表現。
導彈飛行時間雖長，但巡航導彈通常以低空飛行、貼近地形的方式執行任務，飛行時間長並不必然代表射程遠，此點亦不應過度解讀。
此次試射對東北亞安全域性勢產生直接影響。南韓與美國可能進一步強化對北韓的軍事監控與制裁壓力，區域內軍事緊張局勢有望升溫。北韓透過展示遠端巡航導彈能力，向外界傳遞其核威懾力持續升級的訊號，可能促使周邊國家重新評估區域防禦策略。對南韓而言，導彈從順安地區發射，顯示其射程可覆蓋首爾都會區，對南韓首都圈構成直接威脅。此外，北韓強調「無限制」發展核戰鬥力量，暗示未來可能繼續進行更多導彈試射與核試驗，區域穩定性面臨持續挑戰。
不同觀點顯示，北韓官方將此次試射定位為常規軍事演練，旨在驗證武器系統可靠性；但國際社會普遍視為北韓意圖突破國際制裁、強化核武能力的舉動。部分分析人士指出，北韓選擇在此時進行試射，可能與國際局勢變化有關，意圖分散國際社會對其他地區衝突的注意力。然而，也有觀點認為不應過度解讀此次試射的戰略意圖，北韓長期以來定期進行導彈試射，此舉符合其既定軍事發展路線，未必代表新的戰略轉向。北韓媒體強調導彈飛行的精確性與可靠性，但實際作戰效能仍需經過更多驗證。
後續觀察重點包括：北韓是否會進一步宣佈導彈技術細節或進行更高規格的試射；南韓與美國是否會採取反制措施，如加強飛彈防禦系統部署；國際社會對北韓制裁執行力度是否會進一步升級；以及區域國家如何調整安全政策以應對北韓持續的軍事挑戰。此外，導彈實際射程、精確度與戰術應用能力，仍有待更多獨立資訊予以確認。各方應基於事實進行理性評估，避免過度解讀或情緒化反應，以維持區域和平穩定。
