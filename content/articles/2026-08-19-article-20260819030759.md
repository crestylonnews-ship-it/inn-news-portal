  # Peru Declares State of Emergency at Southern Border to Address Venezuelan Migrant Wave
  
  > Peru's President Jerí has declared a state of emergency along the southern border with Chile and deployed additional armed forces, as thousands of Venezuelan migrants flee northward to escape deportation threats from Chile's far-right candidate Kast.
  
  Peruvian President José Jerí recently announced a state of emergency in the southern border region adjacent to Chile, deploying additional armed forces to the area. The decision directly responds to the sharp increase in Venezuelan migrants arriving in Peru. According to multiple international media reports, these migrants are primarily moving northward from Chile to evade anti-immigrant rhetoric made by Chilean presidential candidate José Antonio Kast during his campaign. Kast, a far-right political figure in Chile, has publicly threatened to carry out mass deportations of illegal immigrants if elected, remarks that have drawn widespread attention and controversy in Chilean society.
  
  According to reports from the Los Angeles Times, NBC News, the Associated Press, and Al Jazeera, the facts confirmed across outlets include: Peru's government has officially declared a state of emergency at the southern border; President Jerí has ordered additional armed forces to the region; thousands of Venezuelan migrants are moving north from Chile; and the trigger for the migrant wave is Kast's anti-immigrant remarks during the Chilean presidential election campaign. The Associated Press reported that Kast threatened mass deportations of immigrants during campaign events, intensifying anti-immigrant sentiment in Chile and prompting large numbers of Venezuelan migrants to leave Chile and enter Peru to the north.
  
  Al Jazeera also confirmed that President Jerí's declaration of a state of emergency was aimed at addressing the influx of migrants at the southern border.
  
  However, the media differ on certain details. The Los Angeles Times emphasized the situation of "families stranded at the border," suggesting the migrant wave has placed substantial pressure on border communities; NBC News and the Associated Press focused more on President Jerí's government decisions and military deployment; and Al Jazeera explicitly stated that the purpose of the state of emergency is to "block border crossings." Additionally, the media vary in the extent to which they quote Kast's remarks—the Associated Press and Al Jazeera provided more detailed accounts of his "mass deportation" threat, while the Los Angeles Times and NBC News described his anti-immigrant stance in more general terms.
  
  Information that remains to be confirmed includes: the specific implementation details of the state of emergency, the actual scope of border crossing closures, and whether the Peruvian government will further restrict immigration.
  
  This development directly affects Peru's southern border region, Chile's political landscape, and the Venezuelan migrant population. For Peru, southern border cities face pressure from the migrant influx, and local infrastructure and social resources may be strained. For Chile, Kast's high level of support reflects rising anti-immigrant sentiment in the country, which could influence the direction of future immigration policy. For Venezuelan migrants, they are forced to live in uncertainty and risk, displaced between two South American nations. Furthermore, the event may affect Peru-Chile diplomatic relations and regional coordination on immigration policy.
  
  Different media and analysts offer varying interpretations of the event. Some view President Jerí's state of emergency declaration as a necessary security measure aimed at maintaining border order and national security; others worry that the move could further marginalize migrant communities and exacerbate social tensions. The Associated Press reported that Kast's strong showing in the Chilean presidential election indicates that anti-immigrant sentiment has considerable influence in the country, which may prompt more migrants to leave Chile. However, some argue that attributing the migrant wave solely to one political figure's remarks is an oversimplification, as immigration issues involve more complex economic, social, and political factors. Additionally, the practical effectiveness and constitutionality of the state of emergency remain to be seen.
  
  Key areas for follow-up include: how the Peruvian government implements the emergency measures, the actual operational status of border crossings, the subsequent movements of migrant groups, and the final outcome of Kast's presidential campaign in Chile. If Kast is elected, Chile may adopt stricter immigration policies, further affecting regional migration flows; if President Jerí's emergency measures provoke domestic or international criticism, the Peruvian government may adjust its policy. Additionally, it remains to be seen whether regional organizations such as the Union of South American Nations (UNASUR) will intervene to coordinate a response. Overall, the event reflects the politicization of immigration issues in South America and the challenges facing regional cooperation.

# 秘魯宣佈南部邊境緊急狀態 應對委內瑞拉移民潮

> 秘魯總統赫裡宣佈與智利接壤的南部邊境進入緊急狀態，並部署更多武裝部隊。此舉源於數千名委內瑞拉移民為躲避智利極右翼候選人卡斯特的驅逐威脅，大規模向北遷徙。

秘魯總統何塞·赫裡（José Jerí）於近日宣佈，在秘魯與智利接壤的南部邊境地區進入緊急狀態，並增派武裝部隊駐守。此項決定直接回應近期湧入秘魯的委內瑞拉移民數量急遽上升。根據多家國際媒體報導，這些移民主要從智利向北遷徙，目的是逃避智利總統候選人何塞·安東尼奧·卡斯特（José Antonio Kast）在競選期間發表的反移民言論。卡斯特為秘魯極右翼政治人物，曾公開威脅若當選將對非法移民實施大規模驅逐行動，此言論已在智利社會引發廣泛關注與爭議。
根據洛杉磯時報、NBC新聞、美聯社及半島電視臺的報導，各媒體共同確認的事實包括：秘魯政府已正式宣佈南部邊境進入緊急狀態；赫裡總統已下令增派武裝部隊前往該區域；數千名委內瑞拉移民正從智利向北遷徙；移民潮的導火線是卡斯特在智利總統選舉競選期間發表的反移民言論。美聯社報導指出，卡斯特在競選活動中威脅將對移民實施大規模驅逐，此言論加劇了智利境內反移民情緒，進而促使大量委內瑞拉移民選擇離開智利，向北進入秘魯。
半島電視臺亦確認，赫裡總統宣佈緊急狀態的目的是為了應對移民湧入南部邊境的狀況。
然而，各媒體在細節描述上存在若干差異。洛杉磯時報強調「家庭滯留邊境」的情況，暗示移民潮已對邊境社群造成實質壓力；NBC新聞與美聯社則較著重於赫裡總統的政府決策與軍事部署；半島電視臺則明確指出緊急狀態的目的是「封鎖邊境口岸」。此外，關於卡斯特言論的具體內容，各媒體引述程度不同，美聯社與半島電視臺較詳細說明其「大規模驅逐」的威脅，而洛杉磯時報與NBC新聞則以較概括的方式描述其反移民立場。
目前尚待確認的資訊包括：緊急狀態的具體實施細節、邊境口岸的實際封鎖範圍、以及秘魯政府是否會進一步限制移民入境。
此事件對秘魯南部邊境地區、智利政治生態，以及委內瑞拉移民群體均產生直接影響。對秘魯而言，南部邊境城市面臨移民湧入的壓力，當地基礎設施與社會資源可能承受負擔；對智利而言，卡斯特的高支援度反映反移民情緒在該國上升，可能影響未來移民政策走向；對委內瑞拉移民而言，他們被迫在兩個南美國家之間流離失所，面臨不確定性與風險。此外，此事件亦可能影響秘魯與智利的外交關係，以及區域內移民政策的協調。
不同媒體與分析人士對此事件存在不同解讀。部分觀點認為，赫裡總統的緊急狀態宣佈是必要的安全措施，旨在維護邊境秩序與國家安全；另一部分觀點則擔憂，此舉可能進一步邊緣化移民群體，加劇社會緊張。美聯社報導指出，卡斯特在智利總統選舉中的高支援度顯示反移民情緒在該國已具相當影響力，這可能促使更多移民離開智利。然而，亦有觀點認為，將移民潮歸咎於單一政治人物的言論可能過於簡化，移民問題涉及更複雜的經濟、社會與政治因素。此外，緊急狀態的實際效果與合憲性仍需觀察。
後續觀察重點包括：秘魯政府如何執行緊急狀態措施、邊境口岸的實際運作狀況、移民群體的後續動向、以及卡斯特在智利總統選舉中的最終結果。若卡斯特當選，智利可能實施更嚴格的移民政策，進而影響區域移民流動；若赫裡總統的緊急狀態措施引發國內或國際批評，秘魯政府可能調整政策。此外，區域組織如南美洲國家聯盟（UNASUR）是否會介入協調，亦值得關注。整體而言，此事件反映南美地區移民問題的政治化趨勢，以及區域合作面臨的挑戰。
