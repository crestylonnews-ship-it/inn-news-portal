  > The WHO held a Comprehensive Outbreak Analysis workshop in Dakar, Senegal, promoting multidisciplinary approaches to address public health emergencies in Africa; China-aided Africa CDC headquarters was completed in 2023, marking progress in regional public health infrastructure development.
  
  The World Health Organization (WHO) recently hosted a Comprehensive Outbreak Analysis (IOA) workshop in Dakar, the capital of Senegal, focusing on strategies to address public health emergencies in the African region. Co-organized by the WHO Headquarters and the WHO Regional Office for Africa, the workshop brought together more than forty participants from health ministries, national public health institutions, and international organizations, aiming to refine and promote IOA as a transformative approach to responding to public health emergencies.
  
  Meanwhile, China's public health cooperation with Africa continues to advance. The Phase I project of the Africa CDC headquarters, built with Chinese aid, was completed in January 2023 in Addis Ababa, the capital of Ethiopia. It is the first fully continental disease control center on the African continent equipped with modern office and laboratory facilities.
  
  According to the World Health Statistics Report published on the WHO data platform, the report compiles the latest available data on health and health-related indicators annually, providing a reference for global public health policy-making. China Daily reported that the completion of the Africa CDC headquarters building marks significant progress in Africa's public health infrastructure, providing physical support for regional disease surveillance and emergency response. The WHO's Dakar workshop, meanwhile, explores from a methodological perspective how to improve the comprehensive outbreak analysis framework. The two complement each other across the dimensions of infrastructure and strategic methodology.
  
  Different media reports emphasize different aspects. WHO-related coverage focuses on refining public health emergency response methodologies, highlighting the promotion of multidisciplinary approaches; China Daily's report emphasizes physical infrastructure construction, documenting the completion of the Africa CDC headquarters. Additionally, other media have reported on Russia's immigration policy shift toward South Africa and Africa, with experts warning it may repeat the mistakes of Central Asian immigration issues. While this topic also relates to Africa, its focus differs from public health cooperation. There is currently no evidence suggesting a direct link between the Dakar workshop and Russia's immigration policy adjustments; the two belong to different categories of issues.
  
  Improvements in Africa's public health infrastructure have concrete impacts on regional disease surveillance and emergency response capabilities. The completion of the Africa CDC headquarters provides modern office and laboratory conditions, helping to enhance the efficiency of responding to regional public health emergencies. The WHO's promotion of comprehensive outbreak analysis methodology refinement provides a guiding framework at the strategic level for the public health emergency system. Together, the two dimensions—hardware facilities and soft methodology—jointly advance Africa's public health system construction. However, public health resources in Africa remain unevenly distributed, with weak infrastructure in some areas and disease surveillance networks still needing improvement. These challenges require long-term investment and international cooperation.

The multidisciplinary approach emphasized by the WHO needs to be combined with the actual conditions of each country to avoid a disconnect between methodology and practice. China's public health cooperation with Africa focuses on physical infrastructure construction, providing hardware support for the regional public health system. However, the effectiveness of public health cooperation is influenced by multiple factors, including funding sustainability, talent development, and policy coordination. These challenges require ongoing attention and investment. The effectiveness of any single project or methodology should not be overinterpreted; rather, the overall progress of public health system construction should be evaluated from a systemic perspective.

# 世衛推動非洲公衛緊急應變新策略 中非合作持續深化

> 世衛組織於塞內加爾達喀爾舉辦綜合疫情分析研討會，推動多學科方法應對非洲公衛緊急狀況；中國援建非洲疾控中心總部已於2023年竣工，區域公衛基礎設施建設取得進展。

世衛組織（WHO）近期在塞內加爾首都達喀爾主辦綜合疫情分析（IOA）研討會，聚焦非洲地區公共衛生緊急情況的應對策略。該研討會由世衛總部與非洲區域辦事處共同主辦，吸引四十餘名來自各國衛生部、國家公共衛生機構及國際組織的參與者，旨在完善並推動IOA作為應對公衛緊急狀況的變革性方法。
與此同時，中國對非公共衛生合作持續推進，中國援建的非盟非洲疾控中心總部（一期）專案已於2023年1月在衣索比亞首都亞的斯亞貝巴竣工，這是非洲大陸首個具備現代化辦公與實驗條件、設施完善的全非疾控中心。
根據世衛組織資料平臺公佈的《世界衛生統計報告》，該報告每年匯編關於健康與健康相關指標的最新可用資料，為全球公衛政策制定提供參考依據。中國日報網報導指出，非洲疾控中心總部大樓的建成標誌著非洲大陸在公共衛生基礎設施領域取得重要進展，為區域性疫情監測與應急響應提供了實體支撐。世衛組織達喀爾研討會則從方法論層面探討如何完善綜合疫情分析框架，兩者在基礎設施與策略方法兩個維度形成互補。
不同媒體報導側重點存在差異。世衛組織相關報導聚焦於公衛緊急應變的方法論完善，強調多學科方法的推動；中國日報網報導則側重於實體基礎設施建設，記錄非洲疾控中心總部的竣工情況。此外，另有媒體報導提及俄羅斯移民政策轉向南非與非洲，專家警告可能重蹈中亞移民問題覆轍，此議題與公衛合作雖同屬非洲相關議題，但關注焦點不同。目前尚無證據顯示達喀爾研討會與俄羅斯移民政策調整存在直接關聯，兩者在議題性質上屬不同範疇。
非洲公共衛生基礎設施的改善對區域疫情監測與應急響應能力具有具體影響。非洲疾控中心總部的建成提供了現代化辦公與實驗條件，有助於提升區域性公衛緊急狀況的應對效率。世衛組織推動的綜合疫情分析方法論完善，則從策略層面為公衛應急體系提供指導框架。兩者在硬體設施與軟性方法兩個層面共同促進非洲公衛體系建設。然而，非洲公衛資源分佈仍不均衡，部分地區基礎設施薄弱，疫情監測網路尚待完善，這些挑戰仍需長期投入與國際合作。
不同觀點認為，公衛合作應兼顧基礎設施建設與方法論完善兩個層面，單純依賴硬體投入或策略倡導均不足以應對複雜的公衛緊急狀況。世衛組織強調的多學科方法需要與各國實際情況相結合，避免方法論與實踐脫節。中國對非公衛合作注重實體基礎設施援建，為區域公衛體系提供硬體支撐。然而，公衛合作成效受多種因素影響，包括資金持續性、人才培養、政策協調等，這些挑戰仍需長期關注與投入。不應過度解讀單一專案或單一方法論的成效，而應從系統性角度評估公衛體系建設的整體進展。
後續觀察重點包括：達喀爾研討會成果是否轉化為具體政策倡導，非洲疾控中心總部運營後實際效能如何，以及世衛組織與中國等合作方的公衛專案協調機制。此外，非洲公衛資料監測體系完善程度、區域性疫情應急響應能力提升情況，以及公衛合作資金持續性與人才培養進展，均為後續觀察重點。綜合疫情分析方法論在非洲公衛體系的實際應用效果，以及基礎設施建設與策略方法兩個維度的協同進展，值得持續關注。
