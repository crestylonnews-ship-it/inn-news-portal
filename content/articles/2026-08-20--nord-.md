  # Germany Issues Arrest Warrant: Croatia Detains Nord Stream Suspect in Pula
  
  > The German Federal Prosecutor's Office issued a European arrest warrant, and Croatian police arrested Ukrainian-trained diver Vladimir Z. in Pula, suspected of involvement in the September 2022 Nord Stream natural gas pipeline explosion, marking the second arrest in the case.
  
  Croatian law enforcement executed an arrest operation in the Adriatic coastal city of Pula, detaining a Ukrainian national, Vladimir Z. (also rendered as Volodymyr Z.), based on a European arrest warrant issued by the German Federal Prosecutor's Office. The suspect is alleged to have participated in the sabotage of the Nord Stream natural gas pipeline in waters near Denmark in September 2022, representing the second arrest in the investigation of this cross-border infrastructure destruction case.
  
  The Nord Stream pipeline system is a critical conduit for Russian natural gas deliveries to Germany and other parts of Europe. In September 2022, multiple explosions occurred along the pipeline in the Baltic Sea, causing massive gas leaks. The incident drew high-level attention from the EU and NATO, prompting German prosecutors to launch a cross-border investigation.
  
  According to a public statement by the German Federal Prosecutor's Office, Vladimir Z. is a professionally trained diver strongly suspected of participating in underwater blasting operations conducted in September 2022 near the island of Bornholm. Croatian and German prosecutors confirmed that the suspect is currently held in a detention facility in Pula, awaiting the initiation of extradition proceedings. Germany has formally requested extradition from Croatia, and the suspect is expected to be transferred to Germany for judicial proceedings under the European arrest warrant mechanism.
  
  This is the second arrest of a suspect since the Nord Stream investigation began, indicating that German prosecutors are continuously expanding the scope of their inquiry and actively pursuing individuals involved through EU judicial cooperation channels.
  
  Different media reports contain certain discrepancies in details. The Kyiv Post reported that Vladimir Z. is alleged to be a member of a group led by Serhii K., a former Ukrainian military official, though other media outlets have not yet confirmed this organizational structure. Additionally, some reports refer to him as "Vladimir Z." while others use "Volodymyr Z."—the two names sound similar and may represent different spellings of the same person, pending formal confirmation by German prosecutors.
  
  Croatian authorities have not disclosed the suspect's age, specific charges, or the extradition timeline, while the German prosecutor's office has also not provided details on the identity or current status of the first arrested suspect. Relevant information remains pending further official disclosure.
  
  This arrest holds symbolic significance for European energy infrastructure security and cross-border law enforcement cooperation. Following the Nord Stream pipeline incident, the EU has strengthened patrols of pipeline routes in the Baltic Sea and advanced the Critical Entities Resilience Directive. Germany's successful arrest of a suspect in Croatia via a European arrest warrant demonstrates the operational effectiveness of EU judicial mutual assistance mechanisms in handling cross-border terrorism and infrastructure sabotage cases. For Croatia, this also highlights its active role as an EU and NATO partner in law enforcement and judicial cooperation. The energy and insurance sectors are closely monitoring the case's developments, as the pipeline sabotage incident previously triggered fluctuations in European natural gas prices and adjustments to infrastructure insurance premiums.
  
  Different interpretations exist at the legal and political levels. Some observers argue that if the evidence holds, this case could set a precedent as the first judicial trial in Europe for cross-border sabotage of critical energy infrastructure. Other legal experts caution that obtaining evidence related to a diver's identity and underwater operations involves highly specialized technical challenges, and prosecutors must present sufficient scientific鑑定 and eyewitness testimony to meet the standard for criminal conviction. On the political front, the case involves Russia-Ukraine relations, and some media and analysts are watching whether German prosecutors will further disclose the nationalities and motives of those involved. However, to date, German authorities have only confirmed the suspect's Ukrainian citizenship and have not mentioned direct involvement by any state actor.
  
  This case should not be overinterpreted as a failure of the EU's internal security mechanisms; rather, it reflects the practical functioning of cross-border law enforcement cooperation.
  
  Key areas for future observation include whether German prosecutors will release detailed information and the current detention status of the first arrested suspect; the expected timeline for extradition proceedings and the ruling of the Croatian court; whether Vladimir Z. will confess upon arrival in Germany; and whether prosecutors hold a list of other alleged accomplices. Additionally, technical investigative findings regarding diving operation evidence, explosive material sources, and fund flows will influence the direction of the trial. The Nord Stream pipeline sabotage case has been under investigation for over two years, and this arrest demonstrates that the probe continues to advance, though a final conviction still requires a complete judicial process. European media and the public will closely watch subsequent announcements from the German Federal Prosecutor's Office and the Pula court.

# 德國發布逮捕令 克國普利亞拘提Nord Stream案嫌犯

> 德國聯邦檢察署發布歐洲逮捕令，克羅埃西亞警方於普利亞逮捕烏克蘭籍受訓潛水員Vladimir Z.，涉嫌參與2022年9月Nord Stream天然氣管道爆炸事件，為該案第二名被捕嫌疑人。

克羅埃西亞執法單位於亞得裡亞海沿岸城市普利亞（Pula）執行逮捕行動，拘提一名烏克蘭籍男子Vladimir Z.（亦譯作Volodymyr Z.），行動依據為德國聯邦檢察署簽發的歐洲逮捕令。該名嫌犯涉嫌參與2022年9月丹麥附近海域Nord Stream天然氣管道遭破壞事件，此為該起跨境基礎設施破壞案偵辦過程中的第二起逮捕。
Nord Stream管道系統為俄羅斯輸送天然氣至德國與歐洲其他地區的重要管線，2022年9月該管線在波羅的海海域發生多處爆炸，造成大量天然氣洩漏，事件當時引發歐盟與北約高度關注，德國檢方隨即展開跨境調查。
根據德國聯邦檢察署公開說明，Vladimir Z.為受過專業訓練的潛水員，被強烈懷疑參與2022年9月在博恩霍爾姆島（Bornholm）附近海域進行的水下爆破作業。克羅埃西亞檢方與德國檢方確認，嫌犯現正被羈押於普利亞當地看守所，等待引渡程式啟動。德國方面已正式向克羅埃西亞提出引渡請求，預計將透過歐洲逮捕令機制將嫌犯移送德國接受司法審理。
此為Nord Stream案偵辦以來第二名遭逮捕的嫌疑人，顯示德國檢方持續擴大調查範圍，並積極透過歐盟司法合作管道追緝涉案人員。
不同媒體報導在細節上存在若干差異。《基輔郵報》（Kyiv Post）指出，Vladimir Z.被指控為前烏克蘭軍隊官員Serhii K.所領導團體的成員之一，但其他媒體尚未證實此項組織架構說法。此外，部分報導使用「Vladimir Z.」，亦有來源以「Volodymyr Z.」稱呼，兩者在發音上相近，可能為同一人不同拼寫方式，目前尚待德國檢方正式確認。
克羅埃西亞當局未公開嫌犯年齡、具體罪名細節與引渡時程，德國檢察署亦未說明第一名被捕嫌疑人的身分與現況，相關資訊仍待官方進一步披露。
此項逮捕對歐洲能源基礎設施安全與跨境執法合作具指標性意義。Nord Stream管道事件發生後，歐盟已加強波羅的海海域管線巡護，並推動關鍵基礎設施保護指令。德國檢方透過歐洲逮捕令在克羅埃西亞成功拘提嫌疑人，顯示歐盟司法互助機制在處理跨境恐怖主義與基礎設施破壞案件上的運作效能。對克羅埃西亞而言，此舉亦凸顯其作為歐盟與北約夥伴國，在執法和司法合作上的積極角色。能源產業與保險領域亦高度關注此案進展，因管道破壞事件曾引發歐洲天然氣價格波動與基礎設施保險費率調整。
法律與政治層面存在不同解讀空間。部分觀察家認為，此案若證據確鑿，將為歐洲首起針對關鍵能源基礎設施的跨境破壞案件司法審判樹立先例；亦有法律專家提醒，潛水員身分與水下作業證據的取得涉及高度專業技術，檢方須提出充分科學鑑定與目擊證詞，方能達到刑事定罪標準。政治層面，此案牽涉俄國與烏克蘭關係，部分媒體與分析人士關注德國檢方是否會進一步揭露涉案人員國籍與動機，但截至目前，德國官方僅確認嫌犯為烏克蘭公民，未提及任何國家行為體直接參與。
此案件亦不應被過度解讀為歐盟內部安全機制失效，反而反映跨境執法合作的實際運作。
後續觀察重點包括：德國檢方是否公佈第一名被捕嫌疑人的詳細資訊與當前羈押狀態；引渡程式預計時程與克羅埃西亞法院裁決結果；Vladimir Z.到案後是否承認涉案，以及檢方是否掌握其他共犯名單。此外，潛水作業證據、炸藥來源與資金流向等技術性調查結果，將影響案件審理走向。Nord Stream管道破壞案已偵辦逾兩年，此次逮捕顯示調查持續推進，但最終定罪仍須經過完整司法程式。歐洲媒體與公眾將密切關注德國聯邦檢察署與普利亞法院的後續宣告。
