  # US AI Governance Divide Emerges as Accelerationists and Regulators Clash
  
  > The United States faces a split in AI governance, with regulators calling for controls due to fears of losing control, while accelerationists worry about falling behind China and advocate for looser oversight. Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei stated that the backlash stems from a trust crisis that must be resolved through technological achievements.
  
  US artificial intelligence governance has recently drawn significant attention. Cheng Shih-chia, Chairman of iCar Interactive Media, a cross-border AI data technology company, pointed out on the "After Work International Line" program that global AI cybersecurity governance is currently in a "messy" state, with even the United States showing deep internal divisions. Cheng analyzed that US domestic opinion can roughly be divided into two camps: one is the "governance camp," which fears AI could spiral out of control and calls for slowing down and increasing safety regulations; the other is the "acceleration camp," which believes technological development cannot be stopped and that excessive government regulation will only cause the US to fall behind in competition with China. This internal disagreement reflects that the United States has not yet reached a consensus on AI governance policy.
  
  Techbang reported that Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei recently publicly refuted accusations that he excessively promotes a pessimistic outlook on AI. Previously, well-known investor Gavin Baker publicly stated on a podcast program and social media that Amodei's ongoing warnings about AI dangers have, to some extent, fueled the backlash wave against large data center infrastructure in the United States.
  
  Amodei believes that the essence of this backlash is a trust crisis that has been brewing for decades, and argued that it should be resolved through tangible technological achievements, while also preventing monopolization of power through reasonable regulation to ensure Anthropic can continue to develop in competition.
  
  The two reports present different aspects of the AI governance issue. Cheng Shih-chia's perspective focuses on internal policy divisions in the United States, emphasizing the conflict between the governance camp and the acceleration camp; Amodei, from an industry perspective, responded to external criticism by attributing the backlash to a broader trust crisis. Notably, Gavin Baker's claims and Amodei's rebuttal form a direct dialogue, revealing different interpretations within the AI industry regarding its own role and responsibilities. It remains to be confirmed whether the US government will introduce a specific AI regulatory framework in response to these divisions, and whether the data center backlash will further expand.
  
  This governance divide has concrete impacts on related regions, industries, and society. At the industry level, AI companies face dual pressure from government and the public: they need to comply with increasingly strict safety standards while avoiding being seen as hindering technological progress. The backlash against data center construction reflects that public distrust of government and corporations has extended to the infrastructure level. At the societal level, grassroots resentment toward tech billionaires in the United States shows that the issue of benefit distribution from AI development has drawn widespread attention. If this sentiment continues to spread, it could affect the investment environment and talent recruitment for the AI industry.
  
  Different viewpoints demonstrate the complexity of the AI governance issue. The governance camp worries about the security risks that AI losing control could bring and advocates cautious progress; the acceleration camp emphasizes the US's strategic position in competition with China, believing that excessive regulation would weaken competitiveness. Amodei's stance falls between the two, acknowledging the existence of a trust crisis while opposing overly pessimistic rhetoric. Cheng Shih-chia pointed out that there is currently no unified global standard for AI governance, and that policy directions in various countries will affect the future competitive landscape. One should not over-interpret claims from a single source, as AI governance involves multiple dimensions including technology, economics, and security, and different stakeholders will have different interpretations based on their own positions.
  
  Key areas for future observation include whether the US government will introduce specific AI regulatory policies, and whether the controversy between the governance camp and the acceleration camp will affect the agenda-setting of the 2026 election. The development trend of the data center backlash is also worth watching, as it may become an important indicator for testing public trust in government and corporations. Additionally, how AI companies such as Anthropic can strike a balance between regulatory pressure and technological competition will affect the United States' leadership position in the global AI industry. The European region may also serve as a reference for AI governance, as the European Union has taken the lead in introducing an AI Act, and its regulatory model may influence US policy directions.

# 美AI治理分歧浮現 加速派與治理派理念對立

> 美國AI治理現兩派分歧，治理派擔憂失控要求管制，加速派則怕落後中國主張放寬。Anthropic執行長阿莫代伊指抵制浪潮源於信任危機，需以技術成效化解。

美國人工智慧治理議題近期引發高度關注，跨國AI資料科技業者愛卡拉互動媒體董事長程世嘉在《下班國際線》節目中指出，全球AI資安治理目前處於「一團亂」狀態，就連美國內部意見也高度分歧。程世嘉分析，美國國內大致可分成兩大陣營：一方是「治理派」，擔心AI失控要求減速並增加安全管制；另一方是「加速派」則認為科技發展不可能被攔住，政府管得愈多，只會讓美國在與中國的競爭中落後。此種內部意見分歧，反映出美國在AI治理政策上尚未形成共識。
Techbang報導指出，Anthropic執行長達裡奧·阿莫代伊（Dario Amodei）近期針對外界指責其過度宣揚AI悲觀前景的說法予以公開反駁。先前，知名投資人加文·貝克（Gavin Baker）在Podcast節目與社群媒體上公開表示，阿莫代伊對AI危險性的持續警告，在一定程度上助長了美國國內針對大型資料中心基礎建設的抵制浪潮。
阿莫代伊認為，這股抵制浪潮的本質上是醞釀數十年的信任危機，並主張應以實質技術成效來化解信任危機，同時透過合理監管防止權力壟斷，確保Anthropic能在競爭中持續發展。
兩則報導在AI治理議題上呈現不同面向。程世嘉的觀點著重於美國內部政策分歧，強調治理派與加速派的對立；阿莫代伊則從產業角度回應外界批評，將抵制浪潮歸因於更廣泛的信任危機。值得注意的是，加文·貝克的說法與阿莫代伊的反駁形成直接對話，顯示AI產業內部對於自身角色與責任存在不同解讀。目前尚待確認的是，美國政府是否會針對此種分歧出臺具體的AI監管框架，以及資料中心抵制浪潮是否會進一步擴大。
此種治理分歧對相關地區、產業與社會產生具體影響。在產業層面，AI公司面臨來自政府與民眾的雙重壓力：一方面需要符合日益嚴格的安全規範，另一方面又要避免被視為阻礙技術進步。資料中心建設遭遇抵制，反映民眾對政府與企業的不信任已延伸至基礎設施層面。在社會層面，美國基層對科技富豪的仇視情緒，顯示AI發展帶來的利益分配問題已引發廣泛關注。此種情緒若持續擴散，可能影響AI產業的投資環境與人才招募。
不同觀點顯示AI治理議題的複雜性。治理派擔憂AI失控可能帶來的安全風險，主張謹慎前行；加速派則強調美國在與中國競爭中的戰略地位，認為過度管制會削弱競爭力。阿莫代伊的立場介於兩者之間，既承認信任危機的存在，也反對過度悲觀的論調。程世嘉指出，全球AI治理目前缺乏統一標準，各國政策走向將影響未來競爭格局。不應過度解讀單一來源的說法，因為AI治理涉及技術、經濟、安全等多重面向，不同利益相關者基於自身立場會有不同解讀。
後續觀察重點包括：美國政府是否會出臺具體的AI監管政策，以及治理派與加速派的爭議是否會影響2026年大選議題設定。資料中心抵制浪潮的發展趨勢也值得關注，這可能成為檢驗民眾對政府與企業信任度的重要指標。此外，Anthropic等AI公司如何在監管壓力與技術競爭之間取得平衡，將影響美國在全球AI產業的領導地位。歐洲地區在AI治理方面亦可能成為參考物件，歐盟已率先推出AI法案，其監管模式可能影響美國政策走向。
