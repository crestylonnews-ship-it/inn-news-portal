  # Global AI Race Intensifies as China, Hong Kong, and Taiwan Tech Industries Accelerate Transformation and Layout
  
  > In August 2026, the global artificial intelligence race continues to heat up. Chinese tech giants Alibaba and ByteDance are successively scaling back their gaming businesses to pivot toward AI computing infrastructure; Taiwan's National Science and Technology Council led a delegation to the United States to deepen AI and robotics cooperation, while Vice President Hsiao Bi-khim called for establishing Taiwan's AI application autonomy, and the precision machinery industry is also actively positioning itself within the AI supply chain.
  
  On August 18, 2026, the global artificial intelligence (AI) race entered a critical phase, with Asian tech industries making several major strategic moves. According to Yahoo Entertainment, Chinese tech giants are undergoing large-scale portfolio restructuring. Alibaba Group's gaming division, Lingxi Games, is planning to sell its stake to private equity firm Trustar Capital for at least US$1.5 billion, while ByteDance is simultaneously adjusting its business portfolio, redirecting funds toward artificial general intelligence (AGI) infrastructure.
  
  At the same time, Taiwan also made significant moves: National Science and Technology Council Director-General Wu Cheng-wen led a delegation to visit Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, to deepen AI and robotics technology cooperation; Vice President Hsiao Bi-khim delivered remarks at the opening ceremony of the "2026 Taiwan AI Conference," emphasizing that Taiwan should strengthen AI application autonomy and data governance, and actively participate in international co-creation and global AI governance.
  
  According to combined reports from Yahoo Entertainment and Commercial Weekly, the global AI industry is undergoing structural transformation. Chinese tech giants are abandoning certain consumer-facing assets in favor of computing power investments, a trend being picked up by private equity firms and sovereign wealth funds acquiring the divested assets, while tech companies are deploying released capital to enhance computing capacity and strengthen AGI infrastructure. On Taiwan's side, the exchange between the National Science and Technology Council and CMU focused on AI, robotics, advanced manufacturing, and innovative research cooperation. Hsiao Bi-khim proposed that Taiwan should leverage its advantages in semiconductors, cybersecurity, and democratic practice to transform from a "chip island" and "hardware island" into a "republic of AI innovation and value."
  
  Tex Precision Technology also appeared in a report on the same day, showing that Taiwan's precision machinery industry is extending its technologies into the AI supply chain. Its product portfolio covers semiconductor precision parts, AI server cooling components, liquid cooling system machining, and chassis welding and assembly, gradually transitioning from a traditional machine tool parts manufacturer to an important partner in smart manufacturing and AI devices.
  
  Regarding the specific investment scale and acquisition details of Chinese tech giants, information is currently available only from a single source, Yahoo Entertainment. The background of Trustar Capital as the buyer in Alibaba's Lingxi Games sale, the timeline for deal completion, and other details have not been cross-verified by other media outlets. The policy direction of "AI autonomy and international co-creation" proposed by Hsiao Bi-khim at the conference was only mentioned in Commercial Weekly in connection with G7 regulation of AI; there remains a gap between the two in terms of policy level and implementation details, awaiting further official clarification.
  
  The transformation case of Tex Precision Technology represents a single company's experience, and the scope of its product lines as well as their actual contribution to revenue remain to be corroborated by financial reports.
  
  This wave of AI industry transformation is having a profound impact on the Asian tech ecosystem. The asset restructuring by Chinese tech giants will alter the global AI computing power supply landscape, potentially affecting the technology acquisition costs of downstream application developers. Taiwan's precision machinery industry, exemplified by Tex Precision Technology, is transitioning from traditional machine tool parts manufacturing to a key link in the AI device supply chain, demonstrating that Taiwan's manufacturing sector is actively capturing AI infrastructure demand—including high-precision mechanical components, cooling modules, and cooling systems driven by high-performance computing (HPC), AI servers, and data center construction. The cooperation between the National Science and Technology Council and CMU, along with Hsiao Bi-khim's emphasis on AI application autonomy, signals that Taiwan will play a more active role in AI governance and international standard-setting.
  
  It should be noted that Yahoo Entertainment's report on Chinese tech giants' transformation is primarily based on TradingView data and a single information source, and some investment scale figures have not yet been officially confirmed by Alibaba or ByteDance. Hsiao Bi-khim's "AI Republic" vision is a policy-directional statement, and specific regulations and budgetary measures have not yet been announced. Commercial Weekly's mention of G7 AI regulation did not specify the content or timeline of the regulations. The transformation case of Tex Precision Technology represents a single company's experience and should not be overgeneralized as a trend across Taiwan's entire precision machinery industry. Additionally, the outcomes of the National Science and Technology Council's cooperation with CMU will need to be observed to determine whether they translate into concrete plans and substantive progress.
  
  Future developments to watch include whether the Alibaba Lingxi Games sale will be formally announced, along with its transaction terms and timeline; the specific projects and budgets of the National Science and Technology Council-CMU cooperation plan; whether Hsiao Bi-khim's proposed AI autonomy policies will be translated into specific regulations or budget proposals; and the impact of Chinese tech giants' AI investments on global computing power market prices. Furthermore, progress on the G7 AI regulatory framework will determine how Taiwan and other regions respond in AI governance. Whether Taiwan's precision machinery industry can continue to capture AI supply chain opportunities also warrants attention.

# 全球AI競賽升溫 中港臺科技業加速轉型佈局

> 2026年8月，全球人工智慧競賽持續升溫。中國科技巨頭阿里巴巴、位元組跳動相繼縮減遊戲業務，轉投AI算力基礎建設；臺灣國科會率團訪美深化AI與機器人合作，副總統蕭美琴呼籲確立臺灣AI應用自主性，精密機械業亦積極佈局AI供應鏈。

2026年8月18日，全球人工智慧（AI）競賽進入關鍵階段，亞洲科技產業迎來多項重大佈局。根據Yahoo Entertainment報導，中國科技巨頭正經歷大規模投資組合重組，阿里巴巴集團旗下遊戲部門Lingxi Games擬以至少15億美元價格出售給私募基金Trustar Capital，位元組跳動亦同步調整業務版圖，將資金轉向人工通用智慧（AGI）基礎建設。
同期，臺灣方面亦有重大動作：國科會主委吳誠文率團訪問美國賓州匹茲堡卡內基美隆大學（CMU），深化AI與機器人科技合作；副總統蕭美琴於「2026臺灣人工智慧年會」開幕式致詞，強調臺灣應強化AI自主應用及資料治理，積極參與國際共創與全球AI治理。
綜合Yahoo Entertainment與商業週刊報導，全球AI產業正經歷結構性轉型。中國科技巨頭捨棄部分消費性資產，轉向算力投資，此趨勢由私人股權投資公司與主權財富基金接手被剝離資產，科技公司則將釋出現金用於提升算力，強化AGI基礎建設。臺灣方面，國科會與CMU的交流聚焦AI、機器人、先進製造及創新研究合作，蕭美琴提出臺灣應結合半導體、資安防護及民主實務優勢，從「晶片島」、「硬體島」轉型為「AI創新與價值的共和國」。
德士精科亦於同日報導中顯示，臺灣精密機械產業正將技術延伸至AI供應鏈，產品涵蓋半導體精密零件、AI伺服器散熱零件、液冷系統機加工，以及機殼焊接組裝等領域，逐步從傳統工具機零件製造商轉型為智慧製造與AI裝置的重要合作夥伴。
關於中國科技巨頭的具體投資規模與收購細節，目前僅Yahoo Entertainment單一來源提供資訊，阿里巴巴Lingxi Games出售案的具體買方Trustar Capital背景、交易完成時程等細節尚未獲其他媒體交叉驗證。蕭美琴於年會中提出的「AI自主與國際共創」政策方向，商業週刊僅提及G7集團將規管AI，兩者在政策層級與執行細節上仍有落差，尚待官方進一步說明。
德士精科的轉型案例屬單一企業經驗，其產品線涵蓋範圍與實際營收貢獻比例，亦有待財務報告佐證。
此波AI產業轉型對亞洲科技生態系產生深遠影響。中國科技巨頭的資產重組將改變全球AI算力供應格局，可能影響下游應用開發商的技術取得成本。臺灣精密機械產業如德士精科，正從傳統工具機零件製造轉型為AI裝置供應鏈重要環節，顯示臺灣製造業正積極捕捉AI基礎建設需求，包括高效能運算（HPC）、AI伺服器與資料中心建設所帶動的高精度機構件、散熱模組、冷卻系統需求。國科會與CMU的合作，以及蕭美琴強調的AI自主應用，預示臺灣將在AI治理與國際標準制定中扮演更積極角色。
需注意的是，Yahoo Entertainment報導中國科技巨頭轉型，主要基於TradingView資料與單一訊息來源，部分投資規模數字尚未獲阿里巴巴或位元組跳動官方確認。蕭美琴提出的「AI共和國」願景屬政策方向性表述，具體法規與預算配套尚未公佈。商業週刊提及G7將規管AI，但未說明具體規範內容與時程。德士精科的轉型案例屬單一企業經驗，不宜過度推論為臺灣精密機械產業整體趨勢。此外，國科會與CMU的合作成果，亦需觀察後續是否轉化為具體計畫與實質進展。
後續應觀察阿里巴巴Lingxi Games出售案是否正式公告、交易條件與時程；國科會與CMU合作計畫的具體專案與預算；蕭美琴所提AI自主政策是否轉化為具體法規或預算案；以及中國科技巨頭AI投資對全球算力市場價格的影響。此外，G7 AI監管框架的進展，將決定臺灣等地區在AI治理上的應對策略。臺灣精密機械業能否持續捕捉AI供應鏈商機，亦值得關注。
