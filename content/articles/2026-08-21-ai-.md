  # US Releases AI Policy White Paper, China-US Bipolar Landscape Takes Shape
  
  > The White House released a package of AI policy documents in July 2025, establishing three strategic pillars; the World Bank simultaneously issued a digital progress report, and IDC forecasts China's AI market size will exceed 690 billion yuan, forming a China-US bipolar landscape in the global industry.
  
  In July 2025, the US White House released a package of policy documents, formally establishing a strategic direction to consolidate its position as the global leader in artificial intelligence. The document proposes three pillars: accelerating breakthroughs in foundational technologies, building a domestic AI infrastructure network, and promoting the integration of hardware and software systems. This move is seen as a key policy action by the United States to strengthen its advantages in the AI competition, and has drawn close attention from the global technology industry. Meanwhile, the World Bank released its *Digital Progress and Trends Report: AI Foundations* in 2025, exploring how AI is reshaping the economic and social structures of countries around the world, and emphasizing the importance of affordable "small AI" solutions for inclusive innovation.
  
  According to cross-referencing by multiple media outlets, the global AI industry landscape in 2025 is trending toward a China-US bipolar structure. Yahoo Finance reported that the China Report Hall's *2025-2030 China Artificial Intelligence Industry Operations and Investment Prospect Research Report* noted that China's AI market is developing rapidly. IDC forecasts that China's AI market size will exceed 690 billion yuan in 2025, accounting for 30% to 35% of the global market. This growth is attributed to policy support, rich application scenarios, and technological breakthroughs by domestic enterprises in areas such as computer vision and speech recognition.
  
  The World Bank report also echoes this trend, noting that AI is reshaping the global economic landscape, and that emerging economies are seeking development opportunities through small-scale AI solutions.
  
  However, there are differences among sources regarding specific policy details and data interpretation. The specific content and implementation timeline of the White House policy document are currently only summarized through citations by the China Report Hall, and the full official US document has not yet been made public. An opinion piece reposted by LINE TODAY, originally published on Up Media, stated that the United States is attempting to replace China's position in the global AI market through deregulation, technological innovation, global alliances, and diplomatic leadership, and claimed that if this policy is successfully implemented, it will reshape the global technology order. However, this is an opinion piece, not an official declaration, and awaits further confirmation. Additionally, the World Bank report focuses on inclusive global development, presenting a different perspective from the competitive orientation of the US policy document, with notable differences in policy priorities between the two.
  
  The release of US AI policy has a direct impact on the global technology industry and geopolitics. First, the United States' strengthening of domestic AI infrastructure and technological breakthroughs may accelerate the restructuring of the global AI supply chain, prompting countries to reassess their technology cooperation relationships with both the US and China. Second, the rapid expansion of China's AI market is creating a bipolar competitive structure in the global AI industry, affecting the market layout and investment decisions of multinational corporations. The World Bank report also warns that if AI development becomes overly concentrated in a few powerful nations, it may exacerbate the global digital divide and affect developing countries' opportunities for technological participation.
  
  The *Global Artificial Intelligence Research and Development Trend Report (2015-2024)*, reported by the Wen Wei Po in Hong Kong, pointed out that China and the US each have their own characteristics in AI research and development paths: the United States focuses on basic research and technological innovation, while China leans toward application scenarios and industrial implementation. This difference will continue to affect the positioning of the two countries in the global AI competition.
  
  Different sources present divergent interpretations and evaluations of US AI policy. Both the China Report Hall and the opinion piece on LINE TODAY emphasize the competitive intent of US policy, while the World Bank report is more concerned with the inclusivity and accessibility of AI development, reminding policymakers to balance innovation and fairness. Furthermore, IDC's forecast data for China's AI market is only an estimate, and the actual market size may vary due to policy changes, technological breakthroughs, or international developments. The White House policy content cited by the China Report Hall has not yet been officially released in full by the United States, and some details remain to be confirmed. The claim in the opinion piece that the US aims to "replace China's position" is an analytical viewpoint, not a statement of fact, and readers should interpret it with caution.
  
  Key areas for future observation include: the full content and implementation progress of the US White House AI policy document, actual growth data and policy effectiveness in China's AI market, the global promotion of the small-scale AI solutions proposed in the World Bank report, and the specific progress of China and the US in AI research and industrial competition. In addition, the responses and cooperation models of countries around the world toward AI policy will also influence the direction of evolution of the future global AI industry landscape.

# 美發布AI政策白皮書 中美雙極格局成形

> 白宮2025年7月發布一攬子AI政策檔案，確立三大戰略支柱；世界銀行同期發布數位進展報告，IDC預測中國AI市場規模將逾6900億元，全球產業形成中美雙極格局。

2025年7月，美國白宮發布一攬子政策檔案，正式確立鞏固全球人工智慧領先地位的戰略方向。該檔案提出三大支柱：加速基礎技術突破、構建本土化AI基礎設施網路，以及推動硬體與軟體系統整合。此舉被視為美國在AI領域競爭中強化自身優勢的關鍵政策動作，亦引發全球科技產業高度關注。與此同時，世界銀行於2025年發布《數位進展與趨勢報告：AI基礎》，探討AI如何重塑各國經濟與社會結構，並強調可負擔的「小型AI」解決方案對於包容性創新的重要性。
根據多家媒體交叉比對，2025年全球AI產業格局正朝向中美雙極發展。雅虎財經報導指出，中國報告大廳發布的《2025-2030年中國人工智慧行業運營態勢與投資前景調查研究報告》提到，中國AI市場發展迅猛，IDC預測2025年中國AI市場規模將超過6900億元人民幣，佔全球市場30%至35%份額。此增長得益於政策支援、豐富應用場景，以及本土企業在電腦視覺與語音辨識等領域的技術突破。
世界銀行報告亦呼應此趨勢，指出AI正在重塑全球經濟版圖，而新興經濟體正透過小型AI方案尋求發展機會。
然而，各來源在具體政策細節與資料解讀上存在差異。白宮政策檔案的具體內容與實施時程，目前僅由中國報告大廳引述摘要，尚未見美國官方完整檔案公開。LINE TODAY轉載的上報投書指出，美國透過法規鬆綁、技術創新、全球聯盟與外交主導，試圖取代中國在全球AI市場的地位，並稱此政策若成功實施，將重塑全球科技秩序。但此為投書觀點，非官方宣告，尚待進一步確認。此外，世界銀行報告側重全球包容性發展，與美國政策檔案的競爭導向形成不同視角，兩者在政策優先順序上存在明顯差異。
美國AI政策的發布對全球科技產業與地緣政治產生直接影響。首先，美國強化本土AI基礎設施與技術突破，可能加速全球AI供應鏈重組，促使各國重新評估與美中兩國的技術合作關係。其次，中國AI市場規模的快速擴張，使全球AI產業形成雙極競爭格局，影響跨國企業的市場佈局與投資決策。世界銀行報告亦提醒，若AI發展過度集中於少數強國，可能加劇全球數位落差，影響發展中國家的科技參與機會。
香港文匯報報導的《全球人工智慧科研態勢報告（2015-2024）》指出，中美在AI科研發展路徑上各具特色，美國側重基礎研究與技術創新，中國則偏向應用場景與產業落地，此差異將持續影響兩國在全球AI競爭中的定位。
不同來源對美國AI政策的解讀與評價存在分歧。中國報告大廳與LINE TODAY的投書均強調美國政策的競爭意圖，但世界銀行報告更關注AI發展的包容性與可及性，提醒政策制定者應兼顧創新與公平。此外，IDC對中國AI市場的預測資料僅為估算，實際規模可能因政策變動、技術突破或國際形勢而有所不同。中國報告大廳引述的白宮政策內容，目前尚未見美國官方完整發布，部分細節仍待確認。投書中關於美國「取代中國地位」的說法，屬分析觀點，非事實陳述，讀者應謹慎解讀。
後續觀察重點包括：美國白宮AI政策檔案的完整內容與實施進度、中國AI市場實際成長資料與政策效果、世界銀行報告所提小型AI方案在全球的推廣情況，以及中美在AI科研與產業競爭中的具體進展。此外，全球各國對AI政策的回應與合作模式，亦將影響未來全球AI產業格局的演變方向。
