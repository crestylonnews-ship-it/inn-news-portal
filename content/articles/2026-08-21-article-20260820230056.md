  # US-Africa Health Cooperation Runs Parallel with China's Experience, Drawing Attention to Africa's Public Health Landscape
  
  > The release of the US "America First Global Health Strategy" has sparked discussion, while China's experience is seen as beneficial to Africa's public health development. The US continues cooperation with the African Union and the Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention, as Africa's public health landscape faces a coexistence of multilateral competition and collaboration.
  
  On December 13, 2022, the US White House released a briefing on the "US-Africa Health Partnership," clearly stating that the US government is working with the African Union and the Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention, along with various African governments and institutions, to advance public health goals together. The briefing emphasized that while striving to end the severe phase of the COVID-19 pandemic, the US continues to deepen its health cooperation with Africa.
  
  This move demonstrates that the US views Africa as a key component of its global health strategy and seeks to consolidate its influence in the health sector through institutionalized cooperation frameworks.
  
  According to information published on the website of the US Embassy in China, US-Africa health cooperation covers multiple dimensions, including disease prevention and control, health system strengthening, and pandemic response. The Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention, a specialized agency under the African Union, plays a pivotal role in regional health governance. The US has stated that the cooperation aims to achieve shared public health goals, emphasizing that it works "proudly" with its African partners. This cooperation framework is seen as a concrete implementation of the US global health strategy in the African region and reflects the US's growing emphasis on health affairs in Africa.
  
  However, the release of the US "America First Global Health Strategy" has prompted divergent interpretations. According to Tencent News, the title of the strategy document itself was described as "somewhat contradictory," revealing the concerns of many in the field. The strategy bears the name "America First," yet its content involves global health cooperation, creating a tension in expression that has sparked discussion among academia and policy circles. Some analysts have pointed out that the "America First" framework may affect the US's health assistance commitments to Africa and other developing countries, thereby impacting global health equity.
  
  Meanwhile, Toutiao News reported that China's experience is considered helpful in helping Africa move toward a more secure public health future, emphasizing the need to respond to China's call to strengthen mutual trust, mutual respect, and inclusive cooperation, and to jointly promote open, just, equal, and diverse global health cooperation.
  
  The participation models of China and the US in Africa's health sector show clear differences. The US focuses on institutionalized cooperation and multilateral frameworks, advancing health goals through collaboration with regional bodies such as the African Union and the Africa CDC; China emphasizes experience sharing and South-South cooperation, advocating inclusive collaboration to advance the implementation of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development and the AU's Agenda 2063, accelerating the elimination of preventable diseases. The two models have different emphases, reflecting the strategic orientations of different countries in Africa's health affairs. For Africa as the recipient, how to balance relationships among various parties in multilateral cooperation and maximize its own health development interests has become a key issue.
  
  Changes in Africa's public health landscape have concrete implications for regional industries and policies. On one hand, increased international health cooperation brings resources and technical support for strengthening Africa's health systems; on the other hand, great power competition may lead to the politicization of health assistance. Analysts note that African countries need to maintain strategic autonomy, avoid over-reliance on a single partner, and fully utilize multilateral cooperation opportunities to enhance local health governance capacity. In addition, the aftermath of the COVID-19 pandemic continues to unfold, and African countries still face challenges in vaccine distribution, healthcare infrastructure development, and public health talent training, requiring long-term international support and cooperation.
  
  Key areas for future observation include: the specific implementation details and funding commitments of the US "America First Global Health Strategy"; the dynamics of cooperation and competition between China and the US in Africa's health sector; the strengthening role of the African Union and the Africa CDC in regional health governance; and the progress of implementing the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development and Agenda 2063 in the public health sector. Africa's public health development not only relates to regional health and well-being but also affects the global health security landscape, warranting continued attention.

# 美非衛生合作與中國經驗並行 非洲公共衛生格局受關注

> 美國發布《美國優先全球衛生戰略》引發討論，同時中國經驗被指有助非洲公共衛生發展。美國與非洲聯盟、非洲疾病控制與預防中心持續合作，非洲公共衛生格局面臨多邊競爭與合作並存局面。

美國白宮於2022年12月13日發布《美國-非洲衛生合作夥伴關係》簡報，明確指出美國政府正與非洲聯盟（African Union）及非洲疾病控制與預防中心（Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention）等非洲各國政府與機構合作，共同推進公共衛生目標。該簡報強調，在努力終止新冠大流行嚴重階段的同時，美國持續深化與非洲的衛生合作關係。
此舉顯示美國將非洲視為全球衛生戰略的重要版圖，並試圖透過制度化合作框架鞏固其在非衛生領域的影響力。
根據美國駐華使領館網站公佈的資料，美國與非洲的衛生合作涵蓋多個層面，包括疾病防控、衛生系統強化及大流行病應對等領域。非洲疾病控制與預防中心作為非洲聯盟旗下的專業機構，在區域衛生治理中扮演關鍵角色。美國方面表示，雙方合作旨在實現共同的公共衛生目標，並強調「自豪地」與非洲夥伴協作。此合作框架被視為美國全球衛生戰略在非洲地區的具體落實，也反映出美國對非洲衛生事務的重視程度持續提升。
然而，美國《優先全球衛生戰略》的發布引發了不同解讀。據騰訊新聞報導，該戰略檔案標題本身被指「顯得有些矛盾」，揭示了該領域內許多人的疑慮。戰略名稱為「美國優先」，但其內容卻涉及全球衛生合作，這種表述上的張力引發了學界與政策圈的討論。有分析指出，「美國優先」的框架可能影響美國對非洲及其他發展中國家的衛生援助承諾，進而影響全球衛生公平性。
與此同時，頭條新聞報導指出，中國經驗被認為有助於非洲邁向更有保障的公共衛生未來，強調應響應中國號召，加強相互信任、彼此尊重、包容合作，共同推動開放、公正、平等、多元的全球衛生合作。
中美在非洲衛生領域的參與模式存在明顯差異。美國側重於制度化合作與多邊框架，透過與非洲聯盟及非洲疾控中心等區域機構協作推進衛生目標；中國則強調經驗分享與南南合作，主張透過包容性合作推動《2030年可持續發展議程》和非盟《2063年議程》的落實，加速消除可預防疾病。兩種模式各有側重，也反映了不同國家在非洲衛生事務中的戰略取向。非洲作為受援方，如何在多邊合作中平衡各方關係、最大化自身衛生發展利益，成為關鍵課題。
非洲公共衛生格局的變化對區域產業與政策產生具體影響。一方面，國際衛生合作的增加為非洲衛生系統強化帶來資源與技術支援；另一方面，大國競爭也可能導致衛生援助的政治化傾向。分析人士指出，非洲國家需要保持戰略自主性，避免過度依賴單一夥伴，同時充分利用多邊合作機會提升本土衛生治理能力。此外，新冠大流行的後續影響仍在持續，非洲各國在疫苗分配、醫療基礎設施建設及公共衛生人才培養等方面仍面臨挑戰，需要國際社會的長期支援與合作。
後續觀察重點包括：美國《優先全球衛生戰略》的具體實施細節與資金承諾、中美在非洲衛生領域的合作與競爭動態、非洲聯盟及非洲疾控中心在區域衛生治理中的角色強化，以及《2030年可持續發展議程》和《2063年議程》在公共衛生領域的落實進度。非洲公共衛生發展不僅關係區域健康福祉，也影響全球衛生安全格局，值得持續關注。
