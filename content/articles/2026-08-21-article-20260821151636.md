  # Youth Trust Crisis and Bubble Warning: Global AI Governance and Industry Development Face Dual Tests
  
  > American youth show low trust in tech giants' AI responsibility, the European Central Bank warns of AI investment bubble risks, and global artificial intelligence governance and industry development face severe challenges with growing calls for regulation.
  
  Recent polls and economic analysis reports show that while the artificial intelligence (AI) industry is developing rapidly, it faces a dual test of trust crisis and bubble risk. A latest American youth poll indicates a significant decline in trust in tech giant leaders regarding AI governance, while the European Central Bank has also released a report warning of potential correction risks in the AI investment market. These signals indicate that global AI development has entered a critical stage requiring stricter regulation and accountability.
  
  According to Techbang, a recent joint poll by CNBC and Generational Lab shows that among young respondents aged 18 to 34, there is a clear trust crisis regarding whether tech leaders can take responsible actions in the field of artificial intelligence. This poll is not only a warning bell to the personal reputation of tech giant CEOs, but also marks a key turning point in AI governance thinking.
  
  Nearly seven in ten respondents question the AI responsibility of tech giants. Facing employment anxiety and concerns about loss of technological control, public trust in tech giants has transformed into strong calls for regulation, demanding the implementation of AI responsibility through external oversight.
  
  The European Central Bank (the institution responsible for maintaining price stability and managing monetary policy in the eurozone) released an analytical report yesterday, pointing out that the AI investment market faces a high probability of correction risk, the impact of which could spread to the global economy. The report explores the current AI industry investment boom and warns of the serious consequences that may follow. The analysis presents two perspectives on the current AI investment frenzy.
  
  The first is the "rational view," which holds that AI brings great uncertainty to productivity, sufficient to justify unprecedented technology investment, as it could bring huge, unlimited potential returns, but could also result in total investment losses.
  
  From an industry development perspective, Taiwan's CNC Technology participated in the "2026 Taipei International Automation Industry Exhibition" this year. Chairman and General Manager Tsai You-keng stated that they jointly showcased with Lida Intelligence and Zhengbo Laser a "one-stop solution" covering precision motion control, industrial automation, AI smart robots, high-end laser processing, and cloud-based smart management. Tsai You-keng said that through software and hardware integration services, CNC Technology helps manufacturing enterprises worldwide break through traditional production bottlenecks and accelerate the transformation toward low-carbon, smart manufacturing goals. This shows that despite facing trust and bubble risks, the industry continues to actively promote the practical application and implementation of AI technology.
  
  Different perspectives show that the challenges facing AI development are not only technical but also involve social trust and economic stability. Techbang reported that trust in Silicon Valley giants is in full crisis, with Nadella receiving only 35% trust in the youth poll, while Jensen Huang and Musk's trust ratings were also low. This trust crisis could affect tech companies' innovation momentum and talent recruitment, thereby impacting the development speed of the entire AI industry. The European Central Bank's warning reminds investors from a macroeconomic perspective that the AI investment market may face excessive speculation and bubble risks, requiring careful evaluation of investment strategies.
  
  Key areas for future observation include: the continued changes in American youth's trust in tech giants, the impact of the European Central Bank's AI bubble warning on investment markets, and the progress of AI governance regulatory policies by various governments. According to tech.gmw.cn, the "Global AI Enterprise Technology Innovation Index Report 2026" shows that the global AI industry presents a pattern led by China and the US as dual cores, with four cities becoming innovation "poles." This indicates that despite facing trust and risk challenges, global AI innovation competition will remain intense, and countries need to strike a balance between promoting technological development and implementing responsible governance.

# 青年信任危機與泡沫警訊：全球AI治理與產業發展面臨雙重考驗

> 美國青年對科技巨頭AI責任感信任度低迷，歐洲央行警告AI投資泡沫風險，全球人工智慧治理與產業發展正面臨嚴峻挑戰，監管呼聲日益高漲。

近期多項民調與經濟分析報告顯示，人工智慧（AI）產業在快速發展的同時，正面臨信任危機與泡沫風險的雙重考驗。美國最新青年民調指出，科技巨頭領袖在AI治理上的信任度大幅下滑，而歐洲央行亦發布報告警告AI投資市場可能出現修正風險，這些訊號顯示全球AI發展已進入需要更嚴格監管與責任落實的關鍵階段。
根據Techbang報導，美國消費者新聞與商業頻道（CNBC）與世代實驗室（Generational Lab）近期聯合公佈的最新民調顯示，在18至34歲的年輕受訪族群中，對科技領袖在人工智慧領域能否採取負責任行動，存在著明顯的信任危機。這份民調不僅是一記敲向科技巨頭執行長個人聲譽的警鐘，更標誌著人工智慧治理思維的關鍵轉折。
近七成受訪者質疑科技巨頭的AI責任感，面對就業焦慮與技術失控的擔憂，大眾對科技巨頭的信任危機已轉化為強烈的監管呼聲，要求透過外部監督落實AI責任感。
歐洲央行（European Central Bank, 負責維持歐元區物價穩定與管理貨幣政策的機構）昨日發布一份分析報告，指出人工智慧（AI）投資市場正面臨高度可能出現修正的風險，其影響恐將波及全球經濟。這份報告探討了當前人工智慧產業的投資熱潮，並警告可能隨之而來的嚴重後果。該分析提出兩種解釋當前人工智慧投資狂熱的觀點。
首先是「理性觀點」，認為人工智慧對生產力帶來極大不確定性，足以合理化前所未有的科技投資，因為這可能帶來巨大的、無上限的潛在收益，但也可能導致投資血本無歸。
從產業發展角度來看，臺灣新代科技今年參與「2026臺北國際自動化工業大展」，董事長暨總經理蔡尤鏗表示，這次協同聯達智慧與正鉑雷射，共同展示精密運動控制、工業自動化、AI智慧機器人、高階雷射加工到雲端智慧管理的「一站購足解決方案」。蔡尤鏗表示，新代科技透過軟硬體整合服務，協助全球製造企業打破傳統生產瓶頸，加速實現低碳、智慧製造的轉型目標。這顯示儘管面臨信任與泡沫風險，產業界仍在積極推動AI技術的實際應用與落地。
不同觀點顯示，AI發展面臨的挑戰不僅是技術層面，更涉及社會信任與經濟穩定。Techbang報導指出，矽谷巨頭信任度全面告急，納德拉在青年民調中僅獲得35%的信任度，黃仁勳與馬斯克的信任度亦不高。這種信任危機可能影響科技公司的創新動能與人才招募，進而影響整個AI產業的發展速度。歐洲央行的警告則從宏觀經濟角度提醒投資者，AI投資市場可能出現過度投機與泡沫化風險，需要謹慎評估投資策略。
後續觀察重點包括：美國青年對科技巨頭信任度的持續變化、歐洲央行AI泡沫警告對投資市場的影響、以及各國政府對AI治理的監管政策進展。根據tech.gmw.cn報導，《全球人工智慧企業科技創新指數報告2026》顯示，全球人工智慧產業呈現中美雙核心引領、四大城市成為創新「極核」的格局。這表明儘管面臨信任與風險挑戰，全球AI創新競爭仍將持續激烈，各國需在推動技術發展與落實責任治理之間取得平衡。
