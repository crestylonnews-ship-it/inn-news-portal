  # US Think Tank Releases Economic Security Report Targeting AI, Quantum, and Biotechnology Competition
  
  > The Council on Foreign Relations (CFR) released a report in November 2025 titled "U.S. Economic Security: Winning the Future Technology Race," focusing on three major areas—artificial intelligence, quantum technology, and biotechnology—analyzing the current state of investment, supply chains, and regulation, and offering policy recommendations.
  
  The Council on Foreign Relations (CFR) released a think tank report in November 2025 titled "U.S. Economic Security: Winning the Future Technology Race," zeroing in on three critical fields—artificial intelligence (AI), quantum technology (including sensing, communications, and computing), and biotechnology—in an effort to provide the U.S. government with a strategic blueprint for the next phase of the global technology competition.
  
  According to a republished summary by the Institutes of Science and Development, Chinese Academy of Sciences, the core of the report lies in securing America's dominant position in these three fields, while conducting an in-depth analysis of the current state of U.S. investment, supply chains, and regulation, and finally putting forward specific policy recommendations and action measures. CFR is a prominent U.S. foreign policy think tank whose reports generally carry a certain degree of influence in Washington's policymaking circles. The timing and content of this report reflect the anxiety and strategic positioning of U.S. decision-makers regarding future technological supremacy.
  
  Meanwhile, the competitive landscape of the global artificial intelligence industry is also undergoing rapid change. According to the "Global Artificial Intelligence Technology Application Insight Report" published by Juchang News (鉅亨號), the current global AI competition has taken on a three-way differentiated structure of "technology–market–ecosystem." The report points out that the United States, relying on an "Agent+SaaS" model, has formed an ecosystem loop with precise supply-demand matching. With foundational models such as OpenAI and Claude as the base, tech giants and startups drive progress collaboratively, maintaining a lead in foundational capabilities, open-source ecosystems, and commercial maturity. Europe, by contrast, has chosen a development path centered on "high-standard regulation," emphasizing privacy protection and ethical norms, attempting to build an alternative competitive advantage through legislation.
  
  The observations in this report echo the CFR report's emphasis on U.S. AI advantages, but they also highlight the divergence in global AI development paths.
  
  In terms of market size and industry landscape, Yahoo Finance cited the latest research data from the International Data Corporation (IDC), noting that the global AI industry is expected to reach $350 billion by 2025. From a regional development perspective, North America and the Chinese market together form the core regions of global AI industry development, demonstrating strong market growth momentum. The report describes the current competitive dynamics of the global AI industry chain as a "U.S.–China bipolar structure," emphasizing the all-around contest between the two major economies in technology R&D, application deployment, and ecosystem construction.
  
  Notably, this aligns with the CFR report's underlying stance of treating China as a primary strategic competitor, but Yahoo Finance's report places greater emphasis on market data and industry chain analysis rather than policy recommendations.
  
  Cross-comparing the three sources reveals that the CFR report, the Juchang News insight report, and Yahoo Finance's industry analysis share a high degree of consensus on the two core facts of "U.S. AI leadership" and "U.S.–China competition." However, there are notable differences in focus and data among the three. The CFR report has a broader scope, covering quantum technology and biotechnology, and is oriented toward policy recommendations; the Juchang News report focuses on AI application business models and regional divergence, particularly highlighting Europe's regulatory path; and the Yahoo Finance report cites IDC's specific market forecast figures, providing a quantitative reference for industry scale.
  
  Regarding the scale of the global AI industry, only Yahoo Finance currently cites IDC's $350 billion forecast; neither the Juchang News report nor the CFR report provides data using the same methodology. Therefore, this figure should be regarded as a market estimate from a single source rather than a multi-source confirmed fact.

First, on the investment front, if the report recommends expanding government R&D subsidies for AI, quantum technology, and biotechnology, it could drive a reallocation of the federal budget and influence the flow of private capital. Second, on the supply chain front, if the report advocates strengthening domestic production of critical technologies and cooperation with allies, it could accelerate the U.S. decoupling process from China's technology supply chain, in turn impacting global trade patterns in semiconductors, quantum components, and biotech raw materials. Third, on the regulatory front, if the report recommends tightening export controls and investment reviews, it would directly affect the technology positioning of multinational corporations between the U.S. and China.
  
  For North America, this report may strengthen coordination between the U.S. federal government and state governments on technology policy, while also producing ripple effects on the technology supply chains of neighboring countries such as Canada and Mexico.
  
  Although the CFR report holds policy reference value, it must be noted that as a think tank initiative, it is not an official policy document of the U.S. government. Alternative viewpoints argue that overemphasizing the "technology race" and "economic security" could lead to a rise in protectionism, which may in turn hinder global technology cooperation and innovation efficiency. In addition, the European regulatory path highlighted in the Juchang News report demonstrates that "leadership" is not defined by a single standard—advantages in privacy protection and ethical norms can also become another form of competitiveness. Therefore, the CFR report should not be oversimplified into a narrative of "American supremacy" or the "China threat theory."

# 美智庫發布經濟安全報告 鎖定AI、量子與生物技術競賽

> 美國外交關係協會（CFR）2025年11月發布《美國經濟安全：贏得未來技術競賽》報告，聚焦人工智慧、量子科技與生物技術三大領域，分析投資、供應鏈與管制現狀，並提出政策建議。

美國外交關係協會（Council on Foreign Relations, CFR）於2025年11月發布一份名為《美國經濟安全：贏得未來技術競賽》的智庫報告，鎖定人工智慧（AI）、量子科技（包含感測、通訊與計算）以及生物技術三大關鍵領域，試圖為美國政府在下一階段的全球科技競賽中提供戰略藍圖。
根據中國科學院科技戰略諮詢研究院的轉載摘要，這份報告的核心在於確保美國在上述三個領域的優勢地位，並深入分析美國在投資、供應鏈、管制等層面的現況，最後提出具體的政策建議與行動舉措。CFR是美國著名的外交政策智庫，其報告通常對華府決策圈具有一定影響力，這份報告的發布時間點與內容，反映出美國決策層對未來科技主導權的焦慮與佈局。
與此同時，全球人工智慧產業的競爭格局也在快速變化。根據鉅亨號發布的《全球人工智慧技術應用洞察報告》，當前全球AI競爭已呈現「技術—市場—生態」三元分化格局。報告指出，美國依託「Agent+SaaS」模式形成供需精準匹配的生態閉環，以OpenAI、Claude等基礎模型為底座，科技巨頭與初創企業協同驅動，在基礎能力、開源生態和商業化成熟度上保持領先。而歐洲則選擇以「高標準監管」為核心的發展路徑，強調隱私保護與倫理規範，試圖透過法規建立另一種競爭優勢。
這份報告的觀察，與CFR報告中對美國AI優勢的強調形成呼應，但也凸顯出全球AI發展路徑的分歧。
在市場規模與產業格局方面，Yahoo奇摩股市引用國際資料公司（IDC）的最新研究資料指出，全球AI產業規模預計將於2025年達到3500億美元。從地域發展格局來看，北美地區與中國市場共同構成全球AI產業發展的核心區域，展現強勁的市場增長動能。這份報導以「中美雙極格局」來描述當前全球AI產業鏈的競爭態勢，強調兩大經濟體在技術研發、應用落地與生態系統建構上的全面較量。
值得注意的是，這與CFR報告中將中國視為主要戰略競爭對手的潛在立場一致，但Yahoo奇摩的報導更側重市場資料與產業鏈分析，而非政策建議。
交叉比對三份素材可以發現，CFR報告、鉅亨號洞察報告與Yahoo奇摩的產業分析，在「美國AI領先地位」與「中美競爭」這兩個核心事實上有高度共識。然而，三者在焦點與資料上存在明顯差異。CFR報告的範圍更廣，涵蓋量子科技與生物技術，且以政策建議為導向；鉅亨號的報告則聚焦AI應用的商業模式與區域分化，特別點出歐洲的監管路線；Yahoo奇摩的報導則引用IDC的具體市場預測數字，提供量化的產業規模參考。
關於全球AI產業規模，目前僅有Yahoo奇摩引用IDC的3500億美元預測，鉅亨號與CFR報告並未提供相同口徑的資料，因此該數字應視為單一來源的市場預估，而非多方確認的事實。
這份CFR報告的發布，對美國科技政策與產業發展可能產生具體影響。首先，在投資層面，報告若建議擴大政府對AI、量子與生物技術的研發補助，可能帶動聯邦預算重新配置，並影響民間資本的流向。其次，在供應鏈層面，報告若主張強化關鍵技術的國內生產與盟友合作，可能加速美國對中國科技供應鏈的脫鉤程式，進而衝擊全球半導體、量子元件與生技原料的貿易格局。第三，在管制層面，報告若建議加強出口管制與投資審查，將直接影響跨國企業在美中之間的技術佈局。
對北美地區而言，這份報告可能強化美國聯邦政府與州政府在科技政策上的協調，同時對加拿大、墨西哥等鄰國的科技產業供應鏈產生連鎖效應。
儘管CFR報告具有政策參考價值，但必須注意其作為智庫倡議的本質，並非美國政府的正式政策檔案。不同觀點認為，過度強調「技術競賽」與「經濟安全」可能導致保護主義抬頭，反而阻礙全球科技合作與創新效率。此外，鉅亨號報告點出的歐洲監管路線，說明瞭「領先」並非只有單一標準——在隱私保護與倫理規範上的優勢，也可能成為另一種競爭力。因此，不應將CFR報告的解讀過度簡化為「美國必勝論」或「中國威脅論」。
目前尚待確認的資訊包括：CFR報告的具體政策建議細節、美國行政部門對該報告的回應程度，以及IDC的3500億美元預測所採用的統計口徑與範圍，這些都需要後續追蹤與原始檔案比對。
後續觀察重點應放在以下幾個面向：第一，美國國會與行政部門是否在2026年預算案中採納CFR報告的建議，特別是在AI、量子與生物技術領域的撥款增幅；第二，美國是否針對量子科技與生物技術擴大出口管制清單，這將是報告建議是否落地的重要指標；第三，IDC等市場研究機構是否持續上調或下修全球AI產業規模預測，以驗證3500億美元基準的可靠性；第四，歐洲與中國對美國這份報告的回應，是否會加速各自在AI監管與技術投資上的政策調整。
這些觀察點將有助於判斷這份智庫報告是停留在政策倡議層次，還是真正轉化為具體行動。
