  The Interstellar Federation Science Committee today officially announced that, after three years of deep-space spectral scans and analysis of probe data, it has confirmed the discovery of a super-Earth with a complete atmosphere and liquid-water oceans at the edge of the Crestylon Galaxy (Crestylon Galaxy) in the Fourth Quadrant.

  Designated "INN-7704", the planet orbits at a moderate distance from its parent star, with surface temperatures holding year-round between 15°C and 24°C. Returned probe data indicate atmospheric oxygen concentrations as high as 28%, making it highly suitable for human habitation and ecological migration by Federation allies.

  The Federation High Council has authorized the Interstellar Pioneering Agency to form a special committee and expects to dispatch the first vanguard scientific survey fleet next star-season to conduct surface ecological and resource assessments. The discovery has been hailed externally as the most exciting expansion opportunity in the galaxy in nearly ten years.

星際聯邦科學委員會今日正式對外宣佈，經過長達三年的深空光譜掃描與探測器資料分析，確認在第四象限的克雷斯提隆星系（Crestylon Galaxy）邊緣，發現一顆具備完整大氣層與液態水海洋的超級地球。

這顆被編號為 "INN-7704" 的行星距離母恆星距離適中，表面溫度常年保持在攝氏 15 度至 24 度之間。探測回傳資料顯示，其大氣中氧氣濃度高達 28%，極其適合人類及聯邦盟友駐留與生態移民。

聯邦最高議會已授權星際拓荒署成立專門委員會，預計於下個星曆季派遣首批先鋒科考艦隊前往進行地表生態與資源勘測。這項重大發現被外界視為近十年來銀河系最振奮人心的地緣擴張機遇。
