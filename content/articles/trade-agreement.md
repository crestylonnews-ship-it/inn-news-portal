  After eighteen months of arduous multilateral marathon negotiations, representatives from the Galaxy's major star regions today formally signed the "New Interstellar Free Trade and Tariff Alliance Agreement" in the Olympus Parliament Hall.

  The agreement announces the immediate abolition of transit tariffs on more than 400 strategic commodities along core interstellar lanes and the full implementation of a unified settlement currency based on quantum blockchain. Analysts say the move will significantly reduce transportation costs for manufacturing and interstellar logistics and is expected to inject up to 4.2 trillion Star Credits of liquidity into the overall galactic economy over the next five years.

  However, some representatives from peripheral star regions fear that tariff elimination may impact local small and medium-sized enterprises. A Commerce Committee spokesman said the agreement has established a "Transition Period Industry Protection Fund" to assist microenterprises in completing technological upgrades and digital transformation.

在經歷了長達十八個月艱苦的多邊馬拉松式談判後，銀河系各大星域代表今日在奧林匹斯議會大廳正式簽署了《新星際自由貿易與關稅同盟協定》。

該協定宣佈自即日起，取消跨星系核心航道上超過四百種戰略物資的過境關稅，並全面推行基於量子區塊鏈的統一結算貨幣。分析人士指出，此舉將大幅降低製造業與星際物流的運輸成本，預計在未來五年內將為整體銀河經濟體系注入高達 4.2 兆星幣的流動性。

然而，部分來自邊緣星域的代表擔憂，免關稅可能對本土中小企業造成衝擊。商務委員會發言人對此表示，協定中已設立「過渡期產業保護基金」，用以協助微型企業完成技術升級與數位化轉型。
